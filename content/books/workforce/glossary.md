Payroll borrows its words from tax law, wage-hour law, benefits law and a century of service bureau operations, so the same word shifts with context: a liability is an amount a pay run owes a tax agency on one screen and a balance sheet line on a provider's 10-K on another, an employer is a legal entity with an EIN to the IRS and a company with one contract to a provider's sales team, a deposit is a federal tax payment through EFTPS and a direct deposit of net pay into a worker's bank, and wages are a different number for income tax, Social Security, unemployment and a garnishment's disposable earnings. Entries are grouped by the part of the volume that explains them, alphabetized within each group, and each is one line and a link to the chapter that owns the idea; a word with two meanings appears once per meaning, qualified. Terms the [Payments](/books/payments/glossary), [Banking & Lending](/books/banking-and-lending/glossary), [Health Tech](/books/health-tech/glossary) and [Investing & Digital Assets](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/glossary) glossaries already define keep their definitions. The volume's own names for the core records (company, employer, tax jurisdiction, work location, tax registration, person, worker, employment, job assignment, pay rate, pay schedule, pay period, tax election, worker tax location, bank account, pay distribution, time entry, timesheet, pay item type, earning code, deduction code, taxability rule, deduction election, pay run, pay statement, pay statement line, accumulator, liability, remittance, money movement, filing, garnishment order, leave ledger, leave case, benefit enrollment, life event, document, notice, audit event) are the ones in [The Core Data Model](/books/workforce/workforce-business#the-core-data-model).

## The Business

- **85% rule (CPEO):** a worker is a CPEO's work site employee only at a site where at least 85% of the people performing services for the customer are covered by the CPEO contract ([PEOs & Co-Employment](/books/workforce/peos-and-co-employment#worksite-employees-and-the-85-rule)).
- **Accumulator:** a quarter-to-date or year-to-date total per worker, employer and key (a pay item, a tax, a jurisdiction), derived from paid statement lines and never the source of truth ([The Workforce Business](/books/workforce/workforce-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **Administrative services only (ASO):** a PEO vendor's payroll and HR service without co-employment; the client stays the only employer and files under its own EIN ([Who Runs Payroll: Agents & Liability](/books/workforce/who-runs-payroll#aso-and-eor)).
- **Agent's List:** the list a reporting agent submits to the IRS of every taxpayer it will file or deposit for, each with its own Form 8655 ([Who Runs Payroll: Agents & Liability](/books/workforce/who-runs-payroll#reporting-agent)).
- **Aggregate return:** one Form 941 or 940 filed under a Section 3504 agent's, designated payer's or CPEO's own EIN for many clients, with Schedule R allocating it ([Who Runs Payroll: Agents & Liability](/books/workforce/who-runs-payroll#the-irs-s-five-arrangements)).
- **Certified professional employer organization (CPEO):** a PEO certified by the IRS that is treated as the employer of its worksite employees for federal employment taxes and files aggregate returns under its own EIN ([Who Runs Payroll: Agents & Liability](/books/workforce/who-runs-payroll#certified-professional-employer-organization)).
- **Client service agreement:** the written co-employment contract between a PEO and a client that allocates every employer duty between them ([PEOs & Co-Employment](/books/workforce/peos-and-co-employment#the-client-service-agreement)).
- **Coemployer:** a PEO or a client that is party to a co-employment relationship, each an employer of the same worker for different purposes ([PEOs & Co-Employment](/books/workforce/peos-and-co-employment#the-client-service-agreement)).
- **Company:** the provider's customer, one account and one contract, with one or more employers under it ([The Workforce Business](/books/workforce/workforce-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **CPEO bond:** the surety bond a CPEO keeps for each April-to-March period, the greater of 5% of its prior-year section 3511 liability (capped at $1 million) or $50,000 ([PEOs & Co-Employment](/books/workforce/peos-and-co-employment#cpeo-certification)).
- **Deduction election:** a worker's deduction, as an amount or percent with any goal, limit and arrears rule and the enrollment or order behind it, effective-dated ([The Workforce Business](/books/workforce/workforce-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **Designated payer:** a third party, typically a PEO without IRS certification, that pays a client's workers under a service agreement claiming to be their employer or co-employer, files under its own EIN and is jointly liable with the client (Treas. Reg. 31.3504-2) ([Who Runs Payroll: Agents & Liability](/books/workforce/who-runs-payroll#designated-payer-the-non-certified-peo)).
- **Duty allocation:** the line in a client service agreement that assigns one employer duty (wages, a tax, workers' compensation, a plan, hiring and firing) to the PEO, the client or both ([PEOs & Co-Employment](/books/workforce/peos-and-co-employment#the-client-service-agreement)).
- **Employer:** a legal entity with an EIN that employs people and files returns; a company with three EINs is three employers ([The Workforce Business](/books/workforce/workforce-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **Employment:** one stint of a worker at an employer, from hire to termination; a rehire is a new employment on the same worker ([The Workforce Business](/books/workforce/workforce-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **Filing arrangement:** which IRS arrangement files an employer's federal employment tax returns for a period (in-house, payroll service provider, reporting agent, Section 3504 agent, designated payer or CPEO), deciding the EIN, signer, deposit schedule and liability ([Who Runs Payroll: Agents & Liability](/books/workforce/who-runs-payroll#the-irs-s-five-arrangements)).
- **Float:** interest a provider earns on client funds between collecting a payroll and paying it out, the average balance times the yield ([How Payroll Providers Make Money](/books/workforce/revenue-models#interest-on-client-funds)).
- **Float revenue:** Dayforce's name for investment income earned on customer funds before they are remitted ([How Payroll Providers Make Money](/books/workforce/revenue-models#interest-on-client-funds)).
- **Form 2678:** the employer's and agent's application for IRS approval of a Section 3504 agent ([Who Runs Payroll: Agents & Liability](/books/workforce/who-runs-payroll#section-3504-agent)).
- **Form 8655:** the reporting agent authorization that lets an agent sign and file returns, make deposits and receive notices under a client's EIN ([Who Runs Payroll: Agents & Liability](/books/workforce/who-runs-payroll#reporting-agent)).
- **Form 8821:** a tax information authorization that lets a designee inspect and receive a taxpayer's information, without representing it or signing for it ([Who Runs Payroll: Agents & Liability](/books/workforce/who-runs-payroll#seeing-and-speaking-for-the-account-forms-8821-and-2848)).
- **Form 8973:** a CPEO's notice to the IRS, within 30 days, that a service contract with a customer started or ended ([Who Runs Payroll: Agents & Liability](/books/workforce/who-runs-payroll#certified-professional-employer-organization)).
- **Form M-1:** the annual report a MEWA's administrator files with the DOL by March 1, with registration 30 days before operating in a state ([PEOs & Co-Employment](/books/workforce/peos-and-co-employment#master-health-plans-as-mewas)).
- **Gross billings:** everything a PEO bills its clients, wages, payroll taxes, benefits, workers' compensation and its fee, of which only the markup over payroll cost is revenue ([How Payroll Providers Make Money](/books/workforce/revenue-models#gross-billings-and-net-revenue)).
- **Insurance service revenues:** TriNet's revenue line for insurance-related billings and fees collected from PEO clients and withheld from worksite employees, reported with insurance costs as an expense ([How Payroll Providers Make Money](/books/workforce/revenue-models#gross-billings-and-net-revenue)).
- **Job assignment:** what an employment is over time: title, department, manager, work location, FLSA status, pay basis and standard hours, effective-dated ([The Workforce Business](/books/workforce/workforce-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **Liability (payroll):** an amount a pay run owes a third party (an agency, carrier, plan, order payee or employee), with its due date computed from a rule ([The Workforce Business](/books/workforce/workforce-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **Markup (PEO):** the part of a PEO's gross billings above worksite employees' payroll cost, covering estimated taxes, benefits, workers' compensation and the service fee; Insperity's revenue consists solely of it ([How Payroll Providers Make Money](/books/workforce/revenue-models#insperity-worked-per-worksite-employee)).
- **Master plan:** a benefit plan sponsored by a PEO that its clients' worksite employees join, a MEWA under ERISA when it covers two or more employers ([PEOs & Co-Employment](/books/workforce/peos-and-co-employment#master-health-plans-as-mewas)).
- **Money movement:** one movement of funds on one rail (a client debit, a net pay credit, a tax payment, a garnishment, a benefit remittance, a refund), with its status, return code and trace number ([The Workforce Business](/books/workforce/workforce-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **Multiple employer welfare arrangement (MEWA):** a plan or arrangement providing welfare benefits to the employees of two or more employers, which includes a PEO health plan covering two or more clients ([PEOs & Co-Employment](/books/workforce/peos-and-co-employment#master-health-plans-as-mewas)).
- **Pay item type:** an entry in the volume's catalog of what a pay item is (regular pay, overtime, tips, imputed income, a pre-tax deferral, a garnishment), to which every company code maps and from which taxability is decided ([The Workforce Business](/books/workforce/workforce-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **Pay run:** one run of one employer's payroll for one period and type (regular, off-cycle, bonus, correction, void, reversal), from draft to paid ([The Workforce Business](/books/workforce/workforce-business#the-life-of-a-pay-run)).
- **Pay statement:** one worker's statement for one pay run, with gross, net and its lines; never edited once paid, only reversed or adjusted by a new statement ([The Workforce Business](/books/workforce/workforce-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **Pay statement line:** one earning, deduction, employee tax, employer tax, reimbursement or memo on a pay statement, with its hours, rate, amount and subject and taxable wages ([The Workforce Business](/books/workforce/workforce-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **Payroll reporting agent (California):** an agent registered with the EDD that files returns under each client's employer account, keeping a DE 972 memorandum on file in place of a power of attorney per client ([Who Runs Payroll: Agents & Liability](/books/workforce/who-runs-payroll#state-equivalents)).
- **Payroll service provider (PSP):** a third party that prepares payroll, returns and deposits under the client's EIN without an IRS authorization form and without liability for the client's taxes ([Who Runs Payroll: Agents & Liability](/books/workforce/who-runs-payroll#payroll-service-provider)).
- **Pays per control:** ADP's measure of employees paid per client, the per-worker meter on its existing base ([How Payroll Providers Make Money](/books/workforce/revenue-models#modules-and-attach)).
- **Per employee per month (PEPM):** pricing by the number of workers each month, the base of most payroll and HR software fees ([How Payroll Providers Make Money](/books/workforce/revenue-models#per-employee-per-month)).
- **Principal versus agent:** the ASC 606 judgment of whether an entity controls a good or service before it reaches the customer and reports the gross amount, or arranges for another party to provide it and reports its fee; for a PEO it decides which parts of gross billings are revenue ([How Payroll Providers Make Money](/books/workforce/revenue-models#gross-billings-and-net-revenue); for goods, [Commerce Accounting](/books/commerce/commerce-accounting#revenue-for-goods)).
- **Professional employer organization (PEO):** a firm that co-employs a client's workers, running payroll, benefits and HR under its own plans and allocating employer duties by contract ([PEOs & Co-Employment](/books/workforce/peos-and-co-employment#the-client-service-agreement)).
- **Quarterly assertion (CPEO):** a responsible individual's statement under penalties of perjury, with a CPA's examination-level attestation, that a CPEO withheld and deposited federal employment taxes and kept positive working capital, due by the end of the second month after each quarter ([PEOs & Co-Employment](/books/workforce/peos-and-co-employment#cpeo-certification)).
- **Remittance:** a payment to an agency, carrier, plan or order payee that satisfies one or more liabilities, with its method, confirmation number and settlement date ([The Workforce Business](/books/workforce/workforce-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **Reporting agent:** a payroll service provider authorized on Form 8655 to sign and e-file a client's employment tax returns and deposit through EFTPS under the client's EIN, with the client still liable ([Who Runs Payroll: Agents & Liability](/books/workforce/who-runs-payroll#reporting-agent)).
- **Revenue retention:** the share of revenue a provider keeps from existing clients year over year, distinct from client retention, the share of clients kept ([The Payroll & HR Landscape](/books/workforce/workforce-landscape#what-gets-counted)).
- **Schedule R:** the allocation schedule an aggregate filer attaches to Form 941, 943 or 940, splitting wages and taxes among the employers it filed for ([Who Runs Payroll: Agents & Liability](/books/workforce/who-runs-payroll#section-3504-agent)).
- **Section 3504 agent:** an agent approved on Form 2678 that files aggregate returns and deposits under its own EIN for the employers it serves, jointly liable with them ([Who Runs Payroll: Agents & Liability](/books/workforce/who-runs-payroll#section-3504-agent)).
- **Tax registration:** an employer's account with one jurisdiction for one tax, with its account number, rate, deposit frequency, dates and status ([The Workforce Business](/books/workforce/workforce-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **Taxability rule:** whether a pay item type is subject to, exempt from or only reported for one tax in one jurisdiction, effective-dated; the heart of gross-to-net ([The Workforce Business](/books/workforce/workforce-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **Trust fund taxes:** income tax withheld and the employee's share of Social Security and Medicare, held by the employer for the government ([Who Runs Payroll: Agents & Liability](/books/workforce/who-runs-payroll#who-stays-liable-worked)).
- **Wage base restart:** a worker's Social Security and FUTA wage bases starting again at zero when wages move to a new employer without a successor rule, such as a PEO without certification ([PEOs & Co-Employment](/books/workforce/peos-and-co-employment#wage-base-restarts)).
- **Worker:** a person working for one employer, as an employee or a contractor ([The Workforce Business](/books/workforce/workforce-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **Worksite employee (WSE, WSEE):** a worker co-employed by a PEO at a client's worksite, the unit PEOs count and price; TriNet counts co-employees and others receiving PEO services, Insperity averages those paid per month ([PEOs & Co-Employment](/books/workforce/peos-and-co-employment#worksite-employees-and-the-85-rule)).
- **Zero-margin benefits pass-through:** benefit costs a PEO bills its clients at cost and reports in revenue and in cost at the same amount, earning nothing on them, as ADP reports for ADP TotalSource ([How Payroll Providers Make Money](/books/workforce/revenue-models#gross-billings-and-net-revenue)).

## Gross to Net

- **8 and 80 system:** the FLSA section 7(j) option for hospitals and residential care establishments to pay overtime after 8 hours in a day and 80 in a fixed 14-day period instead of 40 in a workweek ([Payroll by Industry](/books/workforce/payroll-by-industry#healthcare)).
- **Aggregate procedure:** withholding on supplemental wages by adding them to a regular period's wages, figuring tax on the total and subtracting what was withheld from the regular wages ([Supplemental Wages & Imputed Income](/books/workforce/supplemental-wages-and-imputed-income#flat-and-aggregate-worked)).
- **Allocated tips:** tips a large food or beverage establishment assigns to employees when reported tips fall short of 8% of qualifying sales, reported in W-2 box 8 and never withheld on ([Tips & the 2025 Tax Law](/books/workforce/tips-and-the-2025-deductions#allocated-tips-and-form-8027)).
- **Arrears (deduction):** the part of a deduction a short paycheck couldn't take, kept as a balance and recovered on later statements by the election's rule ([Gross-to-Net, Step by Step](/books/workforce/gross-to-net#negative-net-and-arrears)).
- **Back pay:** wages paid late for past work, including retroactive increases, taxed as ordinary wages in the year paid ([Retro Pay, Voids & Corrections](/books/workforce/retro-pay-voids-and-corrections#retroactive-rate-changes)).
- **Cafeteria plan (Section 125):** a written plan letting employees choose between cash and qualified benefits paid with pre-tax salary reductions, exempt from income tax, Social Security, Medicare and FUTA for the benefits chosen ([Pre-Tax & Post-Tax Deductions](/books/workforce/deductions#section-125-cafeteria-plans)).
- **Cash tips (payroll):** tips the employee already holds, taxed through payroll but excluded from net pay; for the section 224 deduction, "cash tips" also includes charged tips ([Tips & the 2025 Tax Law](/books/workforce/tips-and-the-2025-deductions#cash-and-charged-tips)).
- **Certified payroll:** the weekly payroll report, with a signed Statement of Compliance, that a Davis-Bacon contractor submits for each covered project, optionally on Form WH-347 ([Payroll by Industry](/books/workforce/payroll-by-industry#construction-and-certified-payroll)).
- **Charged tips:** tips paid by card or through an employer-run arrangement and paid to the employee on the paycheck ([Tips & the 2025 Tax Law](/books/workforce/tips-and-the-2025-deductions#cash-and-charged-tips)).
- **Computational bridge:** Pub 15-T's optional conversion of a 2019-or-earlier W-4 into 2020-or-later fields, so one withholding path handles both ([Federal Withholding & the W-4](/books/workforce/federal-withholding#the-form-w-4-since-2020)).
- **Flat rate (supplemental):** 22% federal income tax withholding on separately paid supplemental wages, allowed when income tax was withheld from regular wages in the current or prior year ([Supplemental Wages & Imputed Income](/books/workforce/supplemental-wages-and-imputed-income#flat-and-aggregate-worked)).
- **Gross-up:** solving for the gross payment that leaves a stated net after taxes and deductions, run on an off-cycle statement ([Gross-to-Net, Step by Step](/books/workforce/gross-to-net#gross-up-and-net-to-gross)).
- **Group-term life imputed income:** the Table 2-2 cost of employer group-term life coverage over $50,000, less after-tax employee payments, added to wages and reported with W-2 code C ([Supplemental Wages & Imputed Income](/books/workforce/supplemental-wages-and-imputed-income#group-term-life-over-50-000)).
- **Housing allowance (clergy):** an amount designated in advance as a minister's housing allowance, excluded from income tax wages but included for self-employment tax ([Payroll by Industry](/books/workforce/payroll-by-industry#nonprofits-and-churches)).
- **Imputed income:** the taxable value of a noncash benefit, added to tax bases on the statement without adding to net pay ([Supplemental Wages & Imputed Income](/books/workforce/supplemental-wages-and-imputed-income#imputed-income)).
- **Lock-in letter:** an IRS notice to an employer setting an employee's permitted filing status and withholding, which W-4s that lower withholding can't override ([Federal Withholding & the W-4](/books/workforce/federal-withholding#lock-in-letters)).
- **Manual check:** a payment made outside the pay run and recorded afterward as a statement with its taxes and deductions ([Retro Pay, Voids & Corrections](/books/workforce/retro-pay-voids-and-corrections#manual-checks)).
- **Nonresident alien wage addition:** the Pub 15-T amount added to a nonresident alien's wages only to figure income tax withholding, never reported on the W-2 ([Federal Withholding & the W-4](/books/workforce/federal-withholding#nonresident-aliens)).
- **Off-cycle run:** a pay run outside the regular schedule, such as a bonus, final pay or correction, with its own withholding and deduction choices ([Retro Pay, Voids & Corrections](/books/workforce/retro-pay-voids-and-corrections#off-cycle-runs)).
- **Overpayment:** wages paid that the worker wasn't owed, recovered net in the same year and gross in a later one, under federal and state limits on deductions ([Retro Pay, Voids & Corrections](/books/workforce/retro-pay-voids-and-corrections#overpayments-and-recovery)).
- **Percentage method:** Pub 15-T's annualize, look up and de-annualize withholding computation (Worksheet 1A for automated systems) ([Federal Withholding & the W-4](/books/workforce/federal-withholding#worksheet-1a-worked)).
- **Qualified overtime compensation:** the premium portion of overtime required by FLSA section 7, deductible under section 225 for 2025 to 2028 and reported in W-2 box 12 with code TT from 2026 ([Tips & the 2025 Tax Law](/books/workforce/tips-and-the-2025-deductions#the-qualified-overtime-deduction)).
- **Qualified tips:** voluntary cash or charged tips in an occupation that customarily received tips by December 31, 2024, deductible under section 224 up to $25,000 for 2025 to 2028 ([Tips & the 2025 Tax Law](/books/workforce/tips-and-the-2025-deductions#the-qualified-tips-deduction)).
- **Retro pay:** the difference owed when a rate change takes effect for periods already paid, recomputed by work period and including overtime on the new regular rate ([Retro Pay, Voids & Corrections](/books/workforce/retro-pay-voids-and-corrections#retroactive-rate-changes)).
- **Section 45B credit:** the employer's business credit for Social Security and Medicare paid on tips above the amount needed to reach the minimum wage, for food or beverage and, from 2025, beauty services ([Tips & the 2025 Tax Law](/books/workforce/tips-and-the-2025-deductions#the-section-45b-credit)).
- **Service charge:** a mandatory charge on a bill, such as an automatic gratuity, which is regular wages when distributed to staff and never a tip ([Tips & the 2025 Tax Law](/books/workforce/tips-and-the-2025-deductions#tips-and-service-charges)).
- **Special accounting rule:** the option to treat noncash fringe benefits provided in November and December as paid in the next year ([Supplemental Wages & Imputed Income](/books/workforce/supplemental-wages-and-imputed-income#imputed-income)).
- **Subject wages and taxable wages:** the wages a tax applies to in a period, and the part of them under what is left of the tax's wage base for the year ([Gross-to-Net, Step by Step](/books/workforce/gross-to-net#wage-bases-and-caps)).
- **Supplemental wages:** pay outside regular wages, such as bonuses, commissions, severance, back pay and retroactive increases, with their own withholding methods and the 37% rate over $1 million ([Supplemental Wages & Imputed Income](/books/workforce/supplemental-wages-and-imputed-income#what-counts-as-supplemental)).
- **Taxability matrix:** the table of each pay item type's treatment (subject, exempt or reported only) for each tax and jurisdiction, effective-dated ([Gross-to-Net, Step by Step](/books/workforce/gross-to-net#the-taxability-matrix)).
- **Treasury Tipped Occupation Code (TTOC):** the three-digit code for a tipped occupation reported in W-2 box 14b from 2026, up to two per W-2, with 000 for tips from a nonqualifying occupation ([Tips & the 2025 Tax Law](/books/workforce/tips-and-the-2025-deductions#what-employers-report)).
- **Void:** a statement that negates a paid statement line for line, usually because the payment never reached the worker ([Retro Pay, Voids & Corrections](/books/workforce/retro-pay-voids-and-corrections#voids-reversals-and-adjustments)).
- **Wage bracket method:** Pub 15-T's lookup tables for manual payroll, generally for wages under $100,000 a year ([Federal Withholding & the W-4](/books/workforce/federal-withholding#the-wage-bracket-method)).

## Payroll Tax

- **Accuracy of deposits rule:** the safe harbor that excuses a federal deposit shortfall of no more than the greater of $100 or 2% of the deposit, if made up by the makeup date ([Deposits, Returns & the Tax Calendar](/books/workforce/deposits-and-returns#paying-with-the-return-and-the-accuracy-of-deposits-rule)).
- **Act 32:** Pennsylvania's law requiring employers with Pennsylvania worksites to withhold and remit local earned income tax by tax collection district, using PSD codes ([State & Local Payroll Taxes](/books/workforce/state-and-local-tax#local-income-taxes)).
- **Additional Medicare Tax:** the 0.9% employee-only Medicare tax an employer withholds on wages it pays an employee in excess of $200,000 in a calendar year ([FICA, FUTA & SUTA](/books/workforce/fica-futa-and-suta#additional-medicare-tax)).
- **Automatic Exemption from Penalty (AEP):** the IRS program replacing first-time abatement requests from summer 2026, under which an eligible taxpayer's penalty is not assessed ([Tax Notices, Amendments & Agency Accounts](/books/workforce/tax-notices-and-agency-accounts#penalty-relief)).
- **Averaged failure-to-deposit penalty:** a 2% to 10% penalty the IRS may assess by spreading a quarter's liability evenly when the monthly record or Schedule B is missing or wrong ([Deposits, Returns & the Tax Calendar](/books/workforce/deposits-and-returns#the-returns)).
- **Bona fide employer office:** a home office that meets New York's test (the primary factor, or four secondary and three other factors), so telecommuting days there count as days worked outside New York ([Multi-State, Reciprocity & Remote Work](/books/workforce/multi-state-and-remote-work#the-convenience-of-the-employer-rule)).
- **Business Services Online (BSO):** the Social Security Administration's portal for filing W-2s and W-2cs, uploading EFW2 files and checking submission status ([Year-End, W-2s & Wage Reporting](/books/workforce/year-end-and-w2s#efw2-and-bso)).
- **Calculation layer:** the tax engine that turns one payment's wages, elections, addresses and year-to-date totals into tax amounts, statelessly and by pay date ([Tax Engines & Filing Services](/books/workforce/tax-engines-and-filing#the-calculation-layer)).
- **Combined Annual Wage Reporting (CAWR):** the IRS and SSA program that compares income tax withheld and Social Security and Medicare wages on Forms 94X with the W-2 totals sent to SSA ([Year-End, W-2s & Wage Reporting](/books/workforce/year-end-and-w2s#reconciling-w-2s-to-the-941s)).
- **Convenience of the employer rule:** New York's rule that a nonresident's days worked at home for their own convenience count as New York days when their assigned office is in New York ([Multi-State, Reciprocity & Remote Work](/books/workforce/multi-state-and-remote-work#the-convenience-of-the-employer-rule)).
- **CP136:** the IRS notice that an employer's deposit schedule for the next year may differ, computed from its lookback period ([Tax Notices, Amendments & Agency Accounts](/books/workforce/tax-notices-and-agency-accounts#irs-notices)).
- **CP207:** the IRS notice that a Schedule B or monthly liability record has missing or incorrect information, with 45 days to correct it ([Tax Notices, Amendments & Agency Accounts](/books/workforce/tax-notices-and-agency-accounts#irs-notices)).
- **Credit reduction state:** a state with federal unemployment loans outstanding on two or more consecutive January 1s and not repaid by November 10, whose employers lose part of the 5.4% FUTA credit ([FICA, FUTA & SUTA](/books/workforce/fica-futa-and-suta#futa)).
- **Deposit designation:** an employer's instruction, within 90 days of a penalty notice, on how its deposits apply to liabilities so the failure-to-deposit penalty is smallest ([Tax Notices, Amendments & Agency Accounts](/books/workforce/tax-notices-and-agency-accounts#penalty-relief)).
- **Deposit period:** the span over which federal tax liabilities accumulate toward one deposit: a calendar month for monthly depositors, Wednesday to Friday or Saturday to Tuesday for semiweekly ones ([Deposits, Returns & the Tax Calendar](/books/workforce/deposits-and-returns#when-deposits-are-due)).
- **EFTPS:** the Treasury's Electronic Federal Tax Payment System, through which employers and their providers make federal tax deposits, and where an employer can verify its provider's deposits with its own PIN ([Deposits, Returns & the Tax Calendar](/books/workforce/deposits-and-returns#how-deposits-are-made-eftps)).
- **EFW2:** the Social Security Administration's electronic format for filing W-2 Copy A (Publication 42-007), with EFW2C for W-2c ([Year-End, W-2s & Wage Reporting](/books/workforce/year-end-and-w2s#efw2-and-bso)).
- **Experience rating:** setting each employer's state unemployment rate from its own history of contributions, benefits charged and payroll, as federal law requires ([FICA, FUTA & SUTA](/books/workforce/fica-futa-and-suta#suta-state-unemployment-insurance)).
- **Failure-to-deposit penalty:** the IRS penalty on a late or improper deposit, 2%, 5%, 10% or 15% by how late it is ([Deposits, Returns & the Tax Calendar](/books/workforce/deposits-and-returns#failure-to-deposit-penalties)).
- **File-and-remit layer:** the filing service that turns a period's paychecks into deposits, returns, wage reports, amendments and agency correspondence for each employer ([Tax Engines & Filing Services](/books/workforce/tax-engines-and-filing#the-file-and-remit-layer)).
- **First-time abatement (FTA):** the IRS administrative waiver of failure-to-file, failure-to-pay and failure-to-deposit penalties for taxpayers with three clean prior years ([Tax Notices, Amendments & Agency Accounts](/books/workforce/tax-notices-and-agency-accounts#penalty-relief)).
- **Form 940:** the employer's annual FUTA return, with Schedule A for multi-state employers and credit reduction states ([FICA, FUTA & SUTA](/books/workforce/fica-futa-and-suta#futa)).
- **Form 941-X:** the adjusted employment tax return filed separately for each Form 941 being corrected, as an interest-free adjustment or a claim for refund ([Tax Notices, Amendments & Agency Accounts](/books/workforce/tax-notices-and-agency-accounts#form-941-x-and-the-interest-free-adjustment)).
- **Form 944:** the annual employment tax return the IRS lets its smallest employers file instead of quarterly Forms 941, only by written notice ([Deposits, Returns & the Tax Calendar](/books/workforce/deposits-and-returns#the-returns)).
- **Fractions-of-cents adjustment:** the Form 941 line 7 difference between employee FICA withheld payment by payment and the quarter's wages times the rates ([FICA, FUTA & SUTA](/books/workforce/fica-futa-and-suta#social-security-and-medicare)).
- **FUTA credit:** the credit of up to 5.4% against the 6.0% federal unemployment tax for state contributions paid in full and on time, leaving 0.6% ([FICA, FUTA & SUTA](/books/workforce/fica-futa-and-suta#futa)).
- **Interest-free adjustment:** a Form 941-X correction of underreported tax, filed and paid by the due date of the return for the quarter the error was discovered ([Tax Notices, Amendments & Agency Accounts](/books/workforce/tax-notices-and-agency-accounts#form-941-x-and-the-interest-free-adjustment)).
- **Letter 99C:** the IRS letter telling an employer that its employment tax returns and W-2s disagree after CAWR review, with 45 days to respond ([Tax Notices, Amendments & Agency Accounts](/books/workforce/tax-notices-and-agency-accounts#irs-notices)).
- **Localization of work:** the ordered state unemployment tests (localized service, base of operations, direction and control, residence) that assign a multi-state worker's wages to one state ([FICA, FUTA & SUTA](/books/workforce/fica-futa-and-suta#which-state-gets-the-unemployment-wages)).
- **Location code:** a tax engine's code for the set of taxing jurisdictions an address falls in, computed when the address changes and passed to every calculation ([Tax Engines & Filing Services](/books/workforce/tax-engines-and-filing#location-codes-and-the-address-problem)).
- **Lookback period:** for Form 941 filers, the four quarters from July 1 two years back to June 30 of the prior year, whose total taxes set the deposit schedule ([Deposits, Returns & the Tax Calendar](/books/workforce/deposits-and-returns#the-deposit-schedule)).
- **Makeup date:** the date by which a deposit shortfall under the accuracy of deposits rule must be deposited: the return due date for monthly depositors, the first Wednesday or Friday on or after the 15th of the next month for semiweekly ones ([Deposits, Returns & the Tax Calendar](/books/workforce/deposits-and-returns#paying-with-the-return-and-the-accuracy-of-deposits-rule)).
- **Mobile workforce threshold:** a state's or city's number of days (or dollars) of work below which a nonresident isn't withheld for, such as Indiana's 30 days or Ohio municipalities' 20 ([Multi-State, Reciprocity & Remote Work](/books/workforce/multi-state-and-remote-work#mobile-workforce-thresholds)).
- **Monthly depositor:** an employer that reported $50,000 or less of taxes in its lookback period and deposits by the 15th of the following month ([Deposits, Returns & the Tax Calendar](/books/workforce/deposits-and-returns#the-deposit-schedule)).
- **Next-day deposit rule:** the rule that $100,000 or more of taxes accumulated on any day must be deposited by the next business day, making a monthly depositor semiweekly ([Deposits, Returns & the Tax Calendar](/books/workforce/deposits-and-returns#the-100-000-next-day-rule)).
- **Political Subdivision (PSD) code:** Pennsylvania's code for a municipality and school district pair, used to determine local earned income tax ([State & Local Payroll Taxes](/books/workforce/state-and-local-tax#local-income-taxes)).
- **Reciprocity agreement:** an agreement between two states not to tax each other's residents on wages, effective for an employee once they file the work state's certificate ([Multi-State, Reciprocity & Remote Work](/books/workforce/multi-state-and-remote-work#reciprocity-agreements)).
- **Reserve ratio:** the most common experience rating formula, contributions minus benefits charged divided by payroll; the higher the ratio, the lower the unemployment rate ([FICA, FUTA & SUTA](/books/workforce/fica-futa-and-suta#suta-state-unemployment-insurance)).
- **Schedule B (Form 941):** the report of tax liability by day that semiweekly depositors file with Form 941 ([Deposits, Returns & the Tax Calendar](/books/workforce/deposits-and-returns#the-returns)).
- **Semiweekly depositor:** an employer above $50,000 in its lookback period, depositing taxes on Wednesday to Friday paydays by the following Wednesday and on Saturday to Tuesday paydays by the following Friday ([Deposits, Returns & the Tax Calendar](/books/workforce/deposits-and-returns#when-deposits-are-due)).
- **Social Security wage base:** the most wages per employee per employer per year subject to Social Security tax: $184,500 for 2026 and $192,300 announced for 2027 ([FICA, FUTA & SUTA](/books/workforce/fica-futa-and-suta#social-security-and-medicare)).
- **State unemployment insurance (SUI, SUTA):** the employer contributions, and in a few states employee contributions, that fund state unemployment benefits and earn the FUTA credit ([FICA, FUTA & SUTA](/books/workforce/fica-futa-and-suta#suta-state-unemployment-insurance)).
- **Successor employer:** an employer whose predecessor's wages count toward the year's wage bases (Social Security, the Additional Medicare threshold, FUTA and state unemployment) when the rules are met: one that acquires substantially all of a trade or business, or a CPEO and its customer under IRC 3511(b) ([FICA, FUTA & SUTA](/books/workforce/fica-futa-and-suta#successor-employers-and-wage-base-transfers)).
- **SUTA dumping:** shifting payroll to an entity with a lower unemployment rate to escape a bad experience rating, which 42 USC 503(k) requires states to prevent and penalize ([FICA, FUTA & SUTA](/books/workforce/fica-futa-and-suta#successor-employers-and-wage-base-transfers)).
- **Trust fund recovery penalty:** a penalty of 100% of unpaid trust fund taxes, assessable against the people responsible for paying them over who acted willfully ([Deposits, Returns & the Tax Calendar](/books/workforce/deposits-and-returns#failure-to-deposit-penalties)).
- **Voluntary contribution:** an extra state unemployment payment, made early in the rate year, that lowers the employer's assigned rate in states that allow it and is never refunded ([FICA, FUTA & SUTA](/books/workforce/fica-futa-and-suta#suta-state-unemployment-insurance)).
- **W-2c and W-3c:** the corrected wage statement and its transmittal, showing previously reported and correct amounts for each changed box ([Year-End, W-2s & Wage Reporting](/books/workforce/year-end-and-w2s#corrections-w-2c-and-w-3c)).
- **W-3:** the transmittal that totals an employer's W-2s for the Social Security Administration and must agree with its Forms 941 ([Year-End, W-2s & Wage Reporting](/books/workforce/year-end-and-w2s#the-w-3-and-filing-with-ssa)).

## Moving the Money

- **Administrative wage garnishment (AWG):** an order from a federal agency or the Department of Education, not a court, to withhold up to 15% of disposable pay for a non-tax debt or defaulted federal student loan, behind support orders ([Garnishments & Levies](/books/workforce/garnishments-and-levies#federal-agency-and-student-loan-garnishment)).
- **Approval deadline:** the last moment an employer can approve a payroll and still make its check date, computed from the funding speed, the banking calendar and the provider's cutoff ([Funding a Payroll](/books/workforce/funding-a-payroll#check-date-math-and-holidays)).
- **Cash requirement:** everything a provider will pay out on an employer's behalf for one pay run (net pay it sends, taxes and garnishments it remits), collected by the client debit; not gross pay ([Funding a Payroll](/books/workforce/funding-a-payroll#what-a-pay-run-collects)).
- **Client debit:** the ACH debit (or wire) that collects a pay run's cash requirement from the employer's bank account before the provider pays out ([Funding a Payroll](/books/workforce/funding-a-payroll#debit-timing-four-day-to-same-day)).
- **Client funds obligations:** the balance sheet liability for money a provider holds to pay clients' employees, agencies and other payees, carried at face while the funds held for them are carried at fair value ([Client Funds & Impound Accounts](/books/workforce/client-funds-and-impounds#client-funds-obligations-on-the-balance-sheet)).
- **Consumer Credit Protection Act (CCPA) Title III:** the federal wage garnishment law: disposable earnings, the 25% and 30-times limits for ordinary debts, 50 to 65% for support, and no discharge for one garnishment ([Garnishments & Levies](/books/workforce/garnishments-and-levies#disposable-earnings-and-the-ccpa-limits)).
- **Disposable earnings:** earnings left after deductions required by law (taxes, the employee's Social Security and Medicare, required state contributions and retirement); voluntary deductions are not subtracted ([Garnishments & Levies](/books/workforce/garnishments-and-levies#disposable-earnings-and-the-ccpa-limits)).
- **e-IWO:** the federal child support office's electronic exchange through which employers receive income withholding orders and send acknowledgments ([Child Support Withholding](/books/workforce/child-support-withholding#the-income-withholding-order)).
- **Exempt amount (levy):** the take-home pay an IRS wage levy leaves the employee each pay period, by filing status, pay period and dependents from Publication 1494 ([Garnishments & Levies](/books/workforce/garnishments-and-levies#irs-levies)).
- **Funding speed:** the number of banking days between the client debit and the check date (four-day, two-day, next-day, same-day), a credit tier the provider assigns and can take away ([Funding a Payroll](/books/workforce/funding-a-payroll#debit-timing-four-day-to-same-day)).
- **Funding strike:** a record of a failed payroll funding against an employer, counted toward lengthening its funding speed or removing it from the platform ([When the Debit Fails](/books/workforce/failed-debits-and-recovery#recovery-from-the-employer)).
- **Grantor trust (client funds):** the trust structure some providers use to hold client payroll money, with a provider-owned, regulated trust bank as sole trustee ([Client Funds & Impound Accounts](/books/workforce/client-funds-and-impounds#trust-accounts-and-trust-banks)).
- **Impound:** collecting a client's taxes and net pay ahead of the dates they are owed and holding them in a client trust account until paid ([Client Funds & Impound Accounts](/books/workforce/client-funds-and-impounds#what-is-impounded-and-for-how-long)).
- **Income Withholding for Support (IWO):** the federal standard form (OMB 0970-0154) that orders an employer to withhold child support and says how much, to whom and under which state's limits ([Child Support Withholding](/books/workforce/child-support-withholding#the-income-withholding-order)).
- **Laddering:** investing client funds in slices maturing period by period, so a fixed share is reinvested at current rates and yield lags rate changes ([Client Funds & Impound Accounts](/books/workforce/client-funds-and-impounds#investment-policy-and-ladders)).
- **National Medical Support Notice (NMSN):** the notice requiring an employer to forward a child's health coverage order to its plan within 20 business days and withhold the employee's premium ([Child Support Withholding](/books/workforce/child-support-withholding#medical-support-the-nmsn)).
- **Net pay split:** an ordered list of a worker's net pay destinations, each a fixed amount or percentage, with one remainder account, fixed at approval ([Direct Deposit, Pay Cards & Checks](/books/workforce/paying-employees#splits-worked)).
- **Payroll card account:** a prepaid account established through an employer to receive wages, covered by Regulation E's prepaid rule and offered only as one choice among ways to be paid ([Direct Deposit, Pay Cards & Checks](/books/workforce/paying-employees#payroll-cards-under-regulation-e)).
- **Recovery case:** the record of an outstanding balance from a failed client debit, with its retries, block on the next payroll and collection, until resolved by final money or a decision about the loss ([When the Debit Fails](/books/workforce/failed-debits-and-recovery#how-the-platforms-do-it)).
- **Reverse wire:** a debit initiated by an embedded payroll provider against a partner platform's bank account to fund its customers' payroll obligations ([Funding a Payroll](/books/workforce/funding-a-payroll#wire-prefunding)).
- **State disbursement unit (SDU):** the state office that receives withheld child support from employers, due within 7 business days of payday, and pays it to families ([Child Support Withholding](/books/workforce/child-support-withholding#remitting-to-the-state-disbursement-unit)).
- **Unclaimed wages:** pay a worker never received (an uncashed check, a returned deposit, a pay card balance) that becomes reportable to a state after its dormancy period ([Direct Deposit, Pay Cards & Checks](/books/workforce/paying-employees#uncashed-paychecks-and-unclaimed-wages)).

## Time and Wage-Hour

- **Break attestation:** the worker's own answer at clock-out about whether a required meal or rest break was provided, recorded on their device and used to decide whether a California premium is owed ([Breaks, Premiums & Attestations](/books/workforce/breaks-and-premiums#attestations-and-waivers)).
- **Buddy punching:** one worker clocking in or out for another, countered by identification at the clock (PIN, badge, photo, biometric) and by checking punches against the schedule ([Clocks, Kiosks, Geofences & Biometrics](/books/workforce/time-clocks-and-biometrics#buddy-punching)).
- **Cash wage (tipped):** the wage an employer pays a tipped employee in cash before counting tips; at least $2.13 an hour under the FLSA, higher or the full minimum in many states ([Tipped Employees](/books/workforce/tipped-employees#the-tip-credit)).
- **Clopening:** a closing shift followed by an opening shift with too short a rest between them; fair workweek laws in Oregon and Seattle require 10 hours or pay time and a half for hours inside the window ([Fair Workweek & Scheduling Laws](/books/workforce/scheduling-laws#oregon-and-seattle)).
- **Daily overtime:** overtime owed for hours over a daily threshold (8 in California, Alaska and, for lower-paid employees, Nevada), in addition to weekly overtime ([Overtime & the Regular Rate](/books/workforce/overtime-and-the-regular-rate#state-daily-and-seventh-day-overtime)).
- **De minimis rule:** the federal rule that insubstantial periods of a few seconds or minutes that can't practically be recorded may be disregarded, never fixed or regularly required time; California doesn't apply it to regular off-the-clock minutes ([The Workweek & the Time Record](/books/workforce/time-and-attendance#hours-worked)).
- **Discretionary bonus:** a bonus whose payment and amount the employer decides at or near the end of the period with no prior promise, excludable from the regular rate under FLSA section 7(e)(3) ([Overtime & the Regular Rate](/books/workforce/overtime-and-the-regular-rate#the-regular-rate)).
- **Dual jobs:** employment in a tipped and a non-tipped occupation by the same employee; no tip credit may be taken for hours in the non-tipped job ([Tipped Employees](/books/workforce/tipped-employees#dual-jobs-and-the-80-20-history)).
- **Fair workweek law:** a state or city law requiring advance schedules, good-faith hour estimates, rest between shifts and predictability pay for late changes, for covered retail, food service and hospitality employers ([Fair Workweek & Scheduling Laws](/books/workforce/scheduling-laws#what-the-laws-regulate)).
- **Final pay:** the wages due when employment ends, payable on a deadline set by state law by separation type and notice, from immediately in California to the end of the pay period in Washington ([Pay Frequency, Pay Statements & Final Pay](/books/workforce/pay-frequency-statements-and-final-pay#final-pay)).
- **Fluctuating workweek:** paying a nonexempt employee a fixed salary for all hours in weeks that vary, plus half the week's regular rate for each overtime hour, under 29 CFR 778.114 ([Overtime & the Regular Rate](/books/workforce/overtime-and-the-regular-rate#flsa-overtime)).
- **Geofence:** a boundary around a work location used to flag or block mobile punches made outside it ([Clocks, Kiosks, Geofences & Biometrics](/books/workforce/time-clocks-and-biometrics#mobile-and-geofenced-punches)).
- **Good-faith estimate:** a written estimate given at hire under fair workweek laws of the hours a worker can expect (median monthly hours in Oregon) and whether on-call shifts are expected ([Fair Workweek & Scheduling Laws](/books/workforce/scheduling-laws#what-the-laws-regulate)).
- **Highly compensated employee (HCE, FLSA):** an employee with total annual compensation of at least $107,432, including $684 a week in salary, exempt from overtime if they regularly perform one exempt duty ([Overtime & the Regular Rate](/books/workforce/overtime-and-the-regular-rate#exemptions-and-the-salary-level)).
- **Hours worked:** the time an employer must pay for under wage-hour law, including work suffered or permitted, waiting when engaged to wait, short rest breaks and some travel ([The Workweek & the Time Record](/books/workforce/time-and-attendance#hours-worked)).
- **Illinois Biometric Information Privacy Act (BIPA):** the Illinois law requiring written notice, a written release and a public retention policy before collecting biometric identifiers, with $1,000 or $5,000 in liquidated damages per violation ([Clocks, Kiosks, Geofences & Biometrics](/books/workforce/time-clocks-and-biometrics#biometric-clocks-and-illinois-bipa)).
- **Major fraction:** in California rest period rules, any part of a four-hour work period over two hours, which earns another ten-minute rest period ([Breaks, Premiums & Attestations](/books/workforce/breaks-and-premiums#california-rest-periods)).
- **Manual worker (New York):** a worker in New York whose physical labor makes them subject to weekly pay within seven days of the week's end under Labor Law 191 ([Pay Frequency, Pay Statements & Final Pay](/books/workforce/pay-frequency-statements-and-final-pay#pay-frequency)).
- **Meal and rest premium (California):** one additional hour of pay at the regular rate of compensation for each workday a compliant meal, rest or recovery period isn't provided, paid as wages under Labor Code 226.7 ([Breaks, Premiums & Attestations](/books/workforce/breaks-and-premiums#the-premium)).
- **Minimum wage adjustment:** the make-up earning paid when a workweek's creditable wages fall short of the applicable minimum for its hours ([Minimum Wage as Data](/books/workforce/minimum-wage-as-data#the-minimum-wage-check)).
- **Predictability pay:** premium pay a fair workweek law requires for employer changes to a posted schedule inside the notice period, excluded from the FLSA regular rate ([Fair Workweek & Scheduling Laws](/books/workforce/scheduling-laws#premiums-as-earning-codes)).
- **PUMP Act:** the 2022 law extending FLSA break time and private space for nursing employees to express milk for up to one year after a child's birth ([Breaks, Premiums & Attestations](/books/workforce/breaks-and-premiums#lactation-breaks)).
- **Raw punch:** a clock event exactly as captured, kept beside any rounded or edited time so pay can be recomputed ([The Workweek & the Time Record](/books/workforce/time-and-attendance#raw-punches-and-rounding)).
- **Regular rate:** all compensation for a workweek except the FLSA section 7(e) exclusions, divided by all hours worked; overtime is owed at one and a half times it ([Overtime & the Regular Rate](/books/workforce/overtime-and-the-regular-rate#the-regular-rate)).
- **Reporting time pay:** California's pay of half the scheduled day, between 2 and 4 hours, for an employee who reports for a shift and is given less than half of it ([Fair Workweek & Scheduling Laws](/books/workforce/scheduling-laws#premiums-as-earning-codes)).
- **Retro overtime:** extra overtime premium owed for past workweeks when a bonus or commission earned in them is later apportioned back, under 29 CFR 778.209 ([Overtime & the Regular Rate](/books/workforce/overtime-and-the-regular-rate#bonuses-and-retro-overtime)).
- **Rounding (time clock):** recording punches to the nearest 5, 6 or 15 minutes, allowed federally only if it doesn't underpay over time and barred for meal punches in California ([The Workweek & the Time Record](/books/workforce/time-and-attendance#raw-punches-and-rounding)).
- **Salary level (exempt):** the minimum salary for the FLSA white-collar exemptions, $684 a week in the regulations as of November 2026, and higher under state laws such as California's two times the state minimum wage ([Overtime & the Regular Rate](/books/workforce/overtime-and-the-regular-rate#exemptions-and-the-salary-level)).
- **Seventh-day overtime:** California's time and a half for the first 8 hours and double time beyond 8 on the seventh consecutive day of work in a workweek ([Overtime & the Regular Rate](/books/workforce/overtime-and-the-regular-rate#state-daily-and-seventh-day-overtime)).
- **Tip credit:** the part of the minimum wage an employer may satisfy with an employee's tips, up to $5.12 an hour under the FLSA and limited to tips actually received, with notice; banned in several states ([Tipped Employees](/books/workforce/tipped-employees#the-tip-credit)).
- **Tip pool:** a mandatory sharing of tips among employees; with a tip credit only among customarily tipped employees (traditional), without one possibly including others paid the full minimum (nontraditional), never managers or the employer ([Tipped Employees](/books/workforce/tipped-employees#tip-pools-and-the-manager-rule)).
- **Tipped employee:** an employee in an occupation in which they customarily and regularly receive more than $30 a month in tips under the FLSA ([Tipped Employees](/books/workforce/tipped-employees#who-is-a-tipped-employee)).
- **Voluntary standby list:** under Oregon's predictive scheduling law, workers who agree in writing to be offered extra hours, which earn no change premium when accepted ([Fair Workweek & Scheduling Laws](/books/workforce/scheduling-laws#oregon-and-seattle)).
- **Wage statement:** the itemized statement given with each payment of wages, with contents set by state law such as California Labor Code 226(a) and New York Labor Law 195(3) ([Pay Frequency, Pay Statements & Final Pay](/books/workforce/pay-frequency-statements-and-final-pay#pay-statements)).
- **Waiting time penalty:** California's penalty for willfully late final pay, the employee's daily wages for each day late up to 30 days, under Labor Code 203 ([Pay Frequency, Pay Statements & Final Pay](/books/workforce/pay-frequency-statements-and-final-pay#waiting-time-penalties)).
- **Weighted average regular rate:** the regular rate of an employee who works at two or more rates in a workweek, total earnings divided by total hours ([Overtime & the Regular Rate](/books/workforce/overtime-and-the-regular-rate#two-rates-in-one-week)).
- **Workweek:** the fixed, regularly recurring 168-hour period an employer sets for each employee, the unit for FLSA overtime and minimum wage ([The Workweek & the Time Record](/books/workforce/time-and-attendance#the-workweek)).

## Hiring and HR Records

- **Adverse action (employment):** a decision not to hire, promote or retain a person based in whole or in part on a consumer report, which requires a pre-adverse action notice before it and an adverse action notice after it ([Background Checks, Fair Chance & Drug Testing](/books/workforce/background-checks-and-fair-chance#the-fcra-workflow)).
- **Alternative procedure (Form I-9):** DHS's optional remote examination of I-9 documents by live video, available since August 1, 2023 to employers in good standing in E-Verify, with copies of the documents kept front and back ([Onboarding: I-9, E-Verify & New Hire Reporting](/books/workforce/onboarding-i9-and-new-hires#the-remote-alternative-procedure)).
- **Applicant tracking system (ATS):** the system that records requisitions, postings, candidates, applications, interviews, offers and the hire hand-off to the HRIS ([Recruiting, ATS & AI in Hiring](/books/workforce/recruiting-and-hiring#how-the-platforms-do-it)).
- **Authorized representative (Form I-9):** a person who completes Section 2 on the employer's behalf by examining documents in person, for whose actions the employer remains liable ([Onboarding: I-9, E-Verify & New Hire Reporting](/books/workforce/onboarding-i9-and-new-hires#how-the-platforms-do-it)).
- **Automated employment decision tool (AEDT):** New York City's term for software used to screen, score or rank candidates for hiring or promotion, usable only after a bias audit within one year, a public summary and notice 10 business days before use ([Recruiting, ATS & AI in Hiring](/books/workforce/recruiting-and-hiring#ai-in-hiring)).
- **Bias audit:** an independent analysis of an automated hiring tool's selection or scoring rates by sex, race and ethnicity, required annually by New York City's Local Law 144 ([Recruiting, ATS & AI in Hiring](/books/workforce/recruiting-and-hiring#ai-in-hiring)).
- **Conditional offer:** an offer of employment made before a background check, which fair chance laws require before any conviction history inquiry ([Background Checks, Fair Chance & Drug Testing](/books/workforce/background-checks-and-fair-chance#fair-chance-sequencing)).
- **Consumer reporting agency (CRA), employment:** a background screening provider whose report on a candidate or employee is a consumer report under the FCRA ([Background Checks, Fair Chance & Drug Testing](/books/workforce/background-checks-and-fair-chance#how-the-platforms-do-it)).
- **Continuous monitoring:** recurring checks during employment for new criminal or driving records, each hit a new consumer report subject to the same authorization and adverse action rules ([Background Checks, Fair Chance & Drug Testing](/books/workforce/background-checks-and-fair-chance#continuous-monitoring-and-rechecks)).
- **Correction (HRIS):** replacing a value that was wrong for its effective range, keeping the wrong value in history with when it was recorded, as distinct from a backdated change ([The HRIS as Record of Truth](/books/workforce/hris-record-of-truth#effective-dating-and-future-dated-changes)).
- **Disposition reason:** the recorded reason an application ended (hired, rejected, withdrawn, closed), the field an employer relies on when asked why a person wasn't hired ([Recruiting, ATS & AI in Hiring](/books/workforce/recruiting-and-hiring#requisitions-postings-and-applications)).
- **E-Verify:** the DHS and SSA system that confirms the employment eligibility of a new hire from the I-9, with a case due by the third business day after work for pay begins ([Onboarding: I-9, E-Verify & New Hire Reporting](/books/workforce/onboarding-i9-and-new-hires#e-verify)).
- **EEO-1 Component 1:** the annual EEOC report of workforce demographics by job category, sex and race or ethnicity, from private employers with 100 or more employees and certain federal contractors with 50 or more, proposed for rescission in July 2026 ([Pay Transparency, Pay Equity & EEO-1](/books/workforce/pay-transparency-and-equity#eeo-1-component-1)).
- **Employment loss (WARN):** a termination other than for cause, a voluntary departure or retirement, a layoff over 6 months, or an hours cut of more than 50% in each month of a 6-month period ([Terminations, Offboarding & WARN](/books/workforce/terminations-and-warn#warn)).
- **Fair chance law:** a state or local law (ban-the-box) that delays conviction history inquiries until a conditional offer and requires an individualized assessment and a chance to respond before withdrawing it ([Background Checks, Fair Chance & Drug Testing](/books/workforce/background-checks-and-fair-chance#fair-chance-sequencing)).
- **Final Nonconfirmation:** the E-Verify result that work authorization couldn't be confirmed after the employee contacted DHS or SSA, the time ran out, or the employee chose not to take action ([Onboarding: I-9, E-Verify & New Hire Reporting](/books/workforce/onboarding-i9-and-new-hires#e-verify)).
- **Form I-9:** the USCIS form, current edition 01/20/25, on which a new employee attests to work authorization by the first day and the employer examines documents within three business days ([Onboarding: I-9, E-Verify & New Hire Reporting](/books/workforce/onboarding-i9-and-new-hires#form-i-9)).
- **Further Action Notice:** the E-Verify notice an employer reviews in private with an employee whose case returned a mismatch, recording whether they will take action ([Onboarding: I-9, E-Verify & New Hire Reporting](/books/workforce/onboarding-i9-and-new-hires#e-verify)).
- **Future-dated change:** an HRIS change recorded now with an effective range that starts later, which downstream systems must hear about when it is recorded and when it takes effect ([The HRIS as Record of Truth](/books/workforce/hris-record-of-truth#effective-dating-and-future-dated-changes)).
- **Gender pay gap report:** the EU Directive's periodic report of pay differences by sex, due from employers with 250 or more workers by June 7, 2027 and annually after ([Pay Transparency, Pay Equity & EEO-1](/books/workforce/pay-transparency-and-equity#the-eu-pay-transparency-directive)).
- **HRIS (human resources information system):** the employer's record of who works there, in what job, at what pay and reporting to whom, as of each date, and the source of joiner, mover and leaver events ([The HRIS as Record of Truth](/books/workforce/hris-record-of-truth)).
- **Impact ratio:** a group's selection rate divided by the rate of the group with the highest rate; under four-fifths it is generally regarded as evidence of adverse impact ([Recruiting, ATS & AI in Hiring](/books/workforce/recruiting-and-hiring#worked-numbers-an-impact-ratio)).
- **Individualized assessment (employment):** the employer's review of a conviction against the nature and gravity of the offense, the time passed and the job's duties before withdrawing an offer under a fair chance law ([Background Checks, Fair Chance & Drug Testing](/books/workforce/background-checks-and-fair-chance#fair-chance-sequencing)).
- **Investigative consumer report:** a consumer report built from personal interviews about character, reputation, personal characteristics and mode of living, with its own disclosure within three days of the request ([Background Checks, Fair Chance & Drug Testing](/books/workforce/background-checks-and-fair-chance#the-fcra-workflow)).
- **Joiner, mover, leaver (HRIS):** the hire, transfer and termination events an HRIS emits to payroll, benefits and identity, each with an effective date ([The HRIS as Record of Truth](/books/workforce/hris-record-of-truth#joiner-mover-and-leaver-events)).
- **Joint pay assessment:** the EU Directive's review with workers' representatives when a category shows a gender pay gap of at least 5% that is neither justified by objective, gender-neutral criteria nor remedied within six months ([Pay Transparency, Pay Equity & EEO-1](/books/workforce/pay-transparency-and-equity#the-eu-pay-transparency-directive)).
- **Mass layoff (WARN):** a reduction in force, not from a plant closing, with employment losses at a single site in 30 days for at least 33% and 50 full-time employees, or for 500 ([Terminations, Offboarding & WARN](/books/workforce/terminations-and-warn#warn)).
- **Mini-WARN law:** a state plant closing and layoff notice law with lower thresholds or longer notice than federal WARN, such as California's, New York's and New Jersey's ([Terminations, Offboarding & WARN](/books/workforce/terminations-and-warn#state-mini-warn-laws)).
- **New hire report:** the employer's report of a newly hired employee to the State Directory of New Hires within 20 days, used to match child support cases ([Onboarding: I-9, E-Verify & New Hire Reporting](/books/workforce/onboarding-i9-and-new-hires#state-new-hire-reporting)).
- **Older Workers Benefit Protection Act (OWBPA):** the ADEA's conditions for a knowing and voluntary release of age claims, including 21 days to consider (45 in a group program) and 7 days to revoke ([Terminations, Offboarding & WARN](/books/workforce/terminations-and-warn#severance-and-releases-in-payroll)).
- **Pay data report (California):** the annual report private employers with 100 or more payroll employees file with the Civil Rights Department by the second Wednesday of May, counting employees by job category, pay band, race or ethnicity and sex ([Pay Transparency, Pay Equity & EEO-1](/books/workforce/pay-transparency-and-equity#pay-data-reporting)).
- **Pay scale:** California's term for the range required in a job posting, "a good faith estimate of the salary or hourly wage range that the employer reasonably expects to pay" ([Pay Transparency, Pay Equity & EEO-1](/books/workforce/pay-transparency-and-equity#salary-range-posting-laws)).
- **Personnel file access law:** a state law giving employees, and often former employees, the right to inspect and copy their personnel records within a deadline ([Employee Records, Privacy & Retention](/books/workforce/employee-records-and-privacy#personnel-file-access)).
- **Plant closing (WARN):** the shutdown of a single site, or of facilities or operating units within one, causing employment losses for 50 or more full-time employees in any 30-day period ([Terminations, Offboarding & WARN](/books/workforce/terminations-and-warn#warn)).
- **Position management:** modeling the organization as seats that exist with or without an incumbent, each with a job code, org unit, FTE and budget ([The HRIS as Record of Truth](/books/workforce/hris-record-of-truth#positions-and-org-structures)).
- **Pre-adverse action notice:** the copy of the consumer report and the FCRA Summary of Rights an employer gives before acting on the report, so the person can dispute errors ([Background Checks, Fair Chance & Drug Testing](/books/workforce/background-checks-and-fair-chance#the-fcra-workflow)).
- **Requisition:** an approved request to hire, with the job, position, pay range, openings and hiring team ([Recruiting, ATS & AI in Hiring](/books/workforce/recruiting-and-hiring#requisitions-postings-and-applications)).
- **Rescind (HRIS):** an event that cancels a hire or termination that never took effect, pointing at the event it reverses so each consumer can undo what it did ([The HRIS as Record of Truth](/books/workforce/hris-record-of-truth#joiner-mover-and-leaver-events)).
- **Retention schedule:** the rules that set how long each record type is kept, from which event, with the latest applicable date winning ([Employee Records, Privacy & Retention](/books/workforce/employee-records-and-privacy#retention-schedules-by-record)).
- **Reverification (Form I-9):** confirming continued work authorization in Supplement B when a noncitizen's employment authorization expires; never required for U.S. citizens or noncitizen nationals ([Onboarding: I-9, E-Verify & New Hire Reporting](/books/workforce/onboarding-i9-and-new-hires#reverification-and-rehires)).
- **Salary history ban:** a law barring employers from asking applicants about prior pay ([Pay Transparency, Pay Equity & EEO-1](/books/workforce/pay-transparency-and-equity#salary-history-bans)).
- **Separation:** the record of a terminated employment, with its termination date, last day worked, reason and the fan-out of tasks it generates ([Terminations, Offboarding & WARN](/books/workforce/terminations-and-warn#the-separation-workflow)).
- **SIDES (State Information Data Exchange System):** the system run with the Department of Labor through which employers, TPAs and PEOs answer state unemployment claim requests electronically ([Terminations, Offboarding & WARN](/books/workforce/terminations-and-warn#how-the-platforms-do-it)).
- **Standalone disclosure:** the FCRA's written notice that a consumer report may be obtained for employment, in a document that consists solely of the disclosure ([Background Checks, Fair Chance & Drug Testing](/books/workforce/background-checks-and-fair-chance#the-fcra-workflow)).
- **Tentative Nonconfirmation (mismatch):** the E-Verify result that the I-9 information didn't match DHS or SSA records, during which the employer may take no adverse action ([Onboarding: I-9, E-Verify & New Hire Reporting](/books/workforce/onboarding-i9-and-new-hires#e-verify)).
- **Truncated TIN:** an SSN or other TIN with its first five digits replaced by Xs or asterisks, permitted on statements furnished after December 31, 2020 but not on returns filed with the IRS or SSA ([Employee Records, Privacy & Retention](/books/workforce/employee-records-and-privacy#ssns-on-statements-w-2s-and-screens)).
- **Voluntary self-identification:** an applicant's or employee's optional answers on race, ethnicity, sex, veteran and disability status, stored apart from hiring decisions ([Recruiting, ATS & AI in Hiring](/books/workforce/recruiting-and-hiring#eeo-self-identification)).
- **WARN Act:** the federal law requiring 60 days' written notice of a plant closing or mass layoff from employers with 100 or more full-time employees ([Terminations, Offboarding & WARN](/books/workforce/terminations-and-warn#warn)).

## Leave

- **Designation notice (FMLA):** the employer's written notice, due within five business days after it has enough information, that leave is or isn't designated FMLA leave, with the amount counted if known ([FMLA Administration](/books/workforce/fmla#notices-and-their-deadlines)).
- **Eligibility notice (FMLA):** the employer's notice, due within five business days after an employee requests leave or the employer learns it may qualify, stating whether the employee is eligible and, if not, why ([FMLA Administration](/books/workforce/fmla#notices-and-their-deadlines)).
- **Employer pick-up (PFML):** an employer's voluntary payment of the employee's share of a state paid leave contribution, which is additional wages to the employee under Rev. Rul. 2025-4 ([State Paid Family & Medical Leave](/books/workforce/state-paid-leave#federal-tax-treatment-rev-rul-2025-4)).
- **Equivalent plan:** an employer's own paid leave plan, approved by the state, that replaces contributions to a state paid family and medical leave program, as in Oregon ([State Paid Family & Medical Leave](/books/workforce/state-paid-leave#private-and-equivalent-plans)).
- **Frontloading:** granting a year's sick leave or PTO at the start of the year instead of accruing it hour by hour, which changes carryover and payout rules in some states ([Sick Leave & PTO Engines](/books/workforce/sick-leave-and-pto#frontloading-versus-accrual)).
- **Intermittent leave:** FMLA leave taken in separate blocks for one qualifying reason, counted in the payroll's smallest leave increment, never more than one hour ([FMLA Administration](/books/workforce/fmla#intermittent-and-reduced-schedule-leave)).
- **Leave year method (FMLA):** the employer's choice of the 12-month period for the 12-week entitlement: calendar year, fixed year, forward from first use, or rolling backward ([FMLA Administration](/books/workforce/fmla#the-entitlement-and-the-leave-year)).
- **Military caregiver leave:** up to 26 workweeks of FMLA leave in a single 12-month period, measured forward, to care for a covered servicemember or veteran with a serious injury or illness ([FMLA Administration](/books/workforce/fmla#military-leave)).
- **Paid family and medical leave (PFML):** a state program that pays a weekly benefit during family or medical leave from a fund financed by payroll contributions, withheld and reported by the employer ([State Paid Family & Medical Leave](/books/workforce/state-paid-leave#the-programs-at-a-glance)).
- **Qualifying exigency leave:** FMLA leave for needs arising from a family member's military duty, within the 12-week entitlement ([FMLA Administration](/books/workforce/fmla#military-leave)).
- **State Disability Insurance (SDI):** California's payroll-funded disability and Paid Family Leave program, withheld from employees at 1.3% with no wage limit for 2026 ([State Paid Family & Medical Leave](/books/workforce/state-paid-leave#program-by-program)).
- **Substitution of paid leave:** running accrued paid leave concurrently with unpaid FMLA leave, at the employee's election or the employer's requirement ([FMLA Administration](/books/workforce/fmla#the-ada-paid-leave-and-state-leave)).
- **Vacation as wages (California):** California's rule that vacation vests as labor is performed, can't be forfeited and is paid at the final rate at separation ([Sick Leave & PTO Engines](/books/workforce/sick-leave-and-pto#california-vacation-as-wages)).
- **Voluntary Plan (California):** a private plan approved to replace SDI for an employer's workforce, which must give every SDI benefit and at least one better ([State Paid Family & Medical Leave](/books/workforce/state-paid-leave#private-and-equivalent-plans)).

## Benefits Administration

- **834:** the HIPAA benefit enrollment and maintenance transaction (ASC X12N 005010X220A1) a plan sponsor or its platform sends a carrier to add, change, terminate or verify members ([Carrier Connections & the 834](/books/workforce/carrier-connections#the-834-in-one-page)).
- **999:** the implementation acknowledgment saying an X12 file was or wasn't syntactically valid ([Carrier Connections & the 834](/books/workforce/carrier-connections#acknowledgments)).
- **Administrative period (ACA):** an optional gap of up to 90 days between a measurement period and its stability period, used to compute status and run enrollment ([The ACA Employer Mandate & 1095-C](/books/workforce/aca-employer-mandate#who-is-full-time)).
- **Affordability safe harbor:** a test of the employee's share of the lowest-cost self-only minimum value plan against W-2 box 1 wages, the rate of pay or the poverty line, which protects the employer from the 4980H(b) payment (9.96% for 2026) ([The ACA Employer Mandate & 1095-C](/books/workforce/aca-employer-mandate#affordability-and-minimum-value)).
- **Applicable large employer (ALE):** an employer, counted with its controlled group, that averaged at least 50 full-time employees including full-time equivalents in the prior calendar year, and so is subject to section 4980H and 1094-C and 1095-C reporting ([The ACA Employer Mandate & 1095-C](/books/workforce/aca-employer-mandate#who-is-covered-applicable-large-employer-status)).
- **Applicable premium (COBRA):** the plan's cost of coverage for similarly situated active participants, fixed before each 12-month determination period, on which the 102% (or 150%) COBRA premium is computed ([COBRA & State Continuation](/books/workforce/cobra#elections-and-premiums)).
- **Average enrollee premium:** a small group composite rate computed from per-member ACA rates and fixed for the plan year ([Benefits Administration Platforms](/books/workforce/benefits-platforms#the-data-model-plans-rates-and-eligibility)).
- **Broker of record:** the insurance agency that places and services an employer's coverage for a line, and often the channel through which the employer gets its benefits administration platform ([Benefits Administration Platforms](/books/workforce/benefits-platforms#brokers-as-a-channel)).
- **Change file:** an 834 carrying only the members that changed, identified by BGN08 code 2 ([Carrier Connections & the 834](/books/workforce/carrier-connections#change-files-and-full-files)).
- **Change in status:** an event (marriage, divorce, birth, a change in employment status, a dependent gaining or losing eligibility, a move) on which a cafeteria plan may permit a consistent mid-year election change under Treas. Reg. 1.125-4 ([Enrollment & Life Events](/books/workforce/enrollment-and-life-events#the-three-windows)).
- **Companion guide:** a carrier's or exchange's document stating which parts and values of the 834 implementation guide it uses ([Carrier Connections & the 834](/books/workforce/carrier-connections#the-834-in-one-page)).
- **Comparability (HSA):** the rule that employer HSA contributions made outside a cafeteria plan be the same amount or percentage of the deductible for comparable employees with the same category of coverage ([HSAs, FSAs & HRAs](/books/workforce/hsa-fsa-and-hras#payroll-contributions-seed-money-and-comparability)).
- **Consistency rule:** the requirement that a mid-year cafeteria plan election change be on account of and correspond with the change in status that permitted it ([Enrollment & Life Events](/books/workforce/enrollment-and-life-events#the-three-windows)).
- **Coverage tier:** the set of people an election covers (employee only, employee plus spouse, employee plus children, family), the unit most employer plans price by ([Enrollment & Life Events](/books/workforce/enrollment-and-life-events#coverage-tiers-and-rates)).
- **Decision support:** tools that help employees compare plans and estimate total cost during enrollment, reading the same plan and rate data the election saves ([Benefits Administration Platforms](/books/workforce/benefits-platforms#decision-support)).
- **Election notice (COBRA):** the notice offering continuation coverage, due 14 days after the administrator learns of the qualifying event, or 44 days after the event when the employer is the administrator ([COBRA & State Continuation](/books/workforce/cobra#the-notices-and-their-clocks)).
- **Employer shared responsibility payment:** the section 4980H assessable payment an ALE owes when a full-time employee receives a premium tax credit; $3,340 a year per full-time employee (less 30) under 4980H(a) and $5,010 per credit recipient under 4980H(b) for 2026 ([The ACA Employer Mandate & 1095-C](/books/workforce/aca-employer-mandate#the-two-penalties-worked)).
- **Evidence of insurability (EOI):** individual underwriting a group life or disability carrier requires for coverage above the guaranteed issue amount, for late entrants or for increases; the excess is pending until approved ([Enrollment & Life Events](/books/workforce/enrollment-and-life-events#evidence-of-insurability)).
- **Form 1094-C:** an ALE member's transmittal to the IRS with monthly offer indicators and full-time and total employee counts ([The ACA Employer Mandate & 1095-C](/books/workforce/aca-employer-mandate#forms-1094-c-and-1095-c)).
- **Form 1095-C:** the statement an ALE files and furnishes for each full-time employee, with monthly offer (line 14), lowest-cost contribution (line 15) and safe harbor (line 16) codes ([The ACA Employer Mandate & 1095-C](/books/workforce/aca-employer-mandate#forms-1094-c-and-1095-c)).
- **Full file (Verify):** an 834 listing all current members, identified by BGN08 code 4, used periodically to confirm the sender's and carrier's records agree ([Carrier Connections & the 834](/books/workforce/carrier-connections#change-files-and-full-files)).
- **Full-time employee (ACA):** an employee with at least 30 hours of service a week on average, or 130 hours in a calendar month ([The ACA Employer Mandate & 1095-C](/books/workforce/aca-employer-mandate#who-is-full-time)).
- **Full-time equivalent (FTE):** the hours of employees who aren't full-time, capped at 120 each per month and divided by 120, counted only to determine ALE status ([The ACA Employer Mandate & 1095-C](/books/workforce/aca-employer-mandate#who-is-covered-applicable-large-employer-status)).
- **Grace period (health FSA):** up to two and a half months after the plan year during which new expenses can be paid from the prior year's balance; a plan can't also have a carryover ([HSAs, FSAs & HRAs](/books/workforce/hsa-fsa-and-hras#health-fsas-uniform-coverage-carryover-and-grace)).
- **Guaranteed issue:** the amount of group life or disability coverage an eligible employee gets without evidence of insurability ([Enrollment & Life Events](/books/workforce/enrollment-and-life-events#evidence-of-insurability)).
- **Health FSA carryover:** a cafeteria plan feature letting up to $680 of unused 2026 health FSA money carry into the next plan year ([HSAs, FSAs & HRAs](/books/workforce/hsa-fsa-and-hras#health-fsas-uniform-coverage-carryover-and-grace)).
- **High deductible health plan (HDHP):** a health plan meeting the year's minimum deductible and out-of-pocket maximum ($1,700 and $8,500 self-only for 2026), required for HSA eligibility; bronze and catastrophic exchange plans qualify from 2026 ([HSAs, FSAs & HRAs](/books/workforce/hsa-fsa-and-hras#hsa-eligibility-and-the-hdhp)).
- **HIPAA special enrollment:** a group health plan's required opportunity, of at least 30 days (60 for Medicaid and CHIP), to enroll after loss of other coverage, marriage, birth, adoption or placement, with coverage from the birth or adoption date or the first of the month after the request ([Enrollment & Life Events](/books/workforce/enrollment-and-life-events#hipaa-special-enrollment)).
- **Hour of service:** each hour an employee is paid, or entitled to payment, for work, plus paid leave hours, the unit of ACA full-time and ALE counts ([The ACA Employer Mandate & 1095-C](/books/workforce/aca-employer-mandate#who-is-covered-applicable-large-employer-status)).
- **Individual coverage HRA (ICHRA):** an employer-funded HRA that reimburses individual market or Medicare coverage, offered by employee class with minimum class sizes, a 3:1 age ratio and a 90-day notice ([HSAs, FSAs & HRAs](/books/workforce/hsa-fsa-and-hras#ichra)).
- **Initial measurement period:** a 3- to 12-month look-back period for a new variable hour, seasonal or part-time employee, which with its administrative period must end by the last day of the first calendar month after the first anniversary ([The ACA Employer Mandate & 1095-C](/books/workforce/aca-employer-mandate#who-is-full-time)).
- **Last-month rule:** the HSA rule treating someone eligible on December 1 as eligible all year, subject to a testing period through the next year ([HSAs, FSAs & HRAs](/books/workforce/hsa-fsa-and-hras#the-hsa-limit-month-by-month)).
- **Letter 226-J:** the IRS's initial letter proposing an employer shared responsibility payment, answered on Form 14764 with corrections on Form 14765 within at least 90 days ([The ACA Employer Mandate & 1095-C](/books/workforce/aca-employer-mandate#letter-226-j)).
- **List bill:** a carrier's periodic invoice computed from its census, with a line per covered employee or a total by tier, which the employer reconciles to enrollment and deductions ([Carrier Connections & the 834](/books/workforce/carrier-connections#reconciling-carrier-invoices)).
- **Look-back measurement method:** determining ACA full-time status from hours over a past measurement period of up to 12 months, held for a stability period of at least six months ([The ACA Employer Mandate & 1095-C](/books/workforce/aca-employer-mandate#who-is-full-time)).
- **Maintenance type code:** the 834 INS03 value saying what a member record does: 021 addition, 001 change, 024 cancellation or termination, 025 reinstatement, 030 audit or compare ([Carrier Connections & the 834](/books/workforce/carrier-connections#the-834-in-one-page)).
- **Match true-up:** a year-end employer contribution that pays the difference between a plan's annual match formula and the per-payroll matches actually made ([401(k) Deferrals & Remittance](/books/workforce/retirement-plan-deferrals#matches-and-true-ups)).
- **Mini-COBRA (state continuation):** a state law giving continuation rights under insured plans, usually for employers below COBRA's 20-employee threshold or as an extension of it, with its own duration, premium cap and administrator ([COBRA & State Continuation](/books/workforce/cobra#state-continuation)).
- **Plan compensation:** the plan document's definition of pay on which deferral percentages and matches are computed, capped at $360,000 for 2026 ([401(k) Deferrals & Remittance](/books/workforce/retirement-plan-deferrals#plan-compensation)).
- **Qualified beneficiary:** a covered employee, spouse or dependent child who loses group health coverage because of a qualifying event and has an independent right to elect COBRA ([COBRA & State Continuation](/books/workforce/cobra#qualifying-events-and-beneficiaries)).
- **Qualified small employer HRA (QSEHRA):** an HRA for employers that aren't ALEs and offer no group health plan, limited to $6,450 self-only and $13,100 family for 2026, with a 90-day notice and W-2 code FF ([HSAs, FSAs & HRAs](/books/workforce/hsa-fsa-and-hras#qsehra)).
- **Qualifying event (COBRA):** an event that causes loss of group health coverage: termination other than for gross misconduct, reduced hours, Medicare entitlement, divorce or legal separation, death, or a child's loss of dependent status ([COBRA & State Continuation](/books/workforce/cobra#qualifying-events-and-beneficiaries)).
- **Recordkeeper file:** the per-payroll record of contributions by source, loans and census data payroll sends a 401(k) recordkeeper with the deposit, totaling to the remittance ([401(k) Deferrals & Remittance](/books/workforce/retirement-plan-deferrals#the-payroll-to-recordkeeper-file)).
- **Roth catch-up rule:** SECURE 2.0 section 603 as implemented by TD 10033: catch-up contributions of a participant whose prior-year FICA wages from the employer exceeded the threshold ($150,000 of 2025 wages for 2026) must be Roth ([401(k) Deferrals & Remittance](/books/workforce/retirement-plan-deferrals#the-roth-catch-up-rule)).
- **Self-Correction Component (SCC):** the 2025 VFCP option to self-correct late participant contributions with lost earnings of $1,000 or less, remitted within 180 days, by online notice ([401(k) Deferrals & Remittance](/books/workforce/retirement-plan-deferrals#late-deposits-and-vfcp)).
- **Small plan safe harbor (deposits):** the DOL rule that participant contributions to a plan with fewer than 100 participants are timely if deposited by the 7th business day after withholding ([401(k) Deferrals & Remittance](/books/workforce/retirement-plan-deferrals#remittance-timing)).
- **Stability period:** the period during which an employee's ACA status from a look-back measurement holds regardless of current hours, at least six months and no shorter than the measurement period ([The ACA Employer Mandate & 1095-C](/books/workforce/aca-employer-mandate#who-is-full-time)).
- **Uniform coverage:** the health FSA rule that the full annual election is available from the start of the coverage period, regardless of contributions so far ([HSAs, FSAs & HRAs](/books/workforce/hsa-fsa-and-hras#health-fsas-uniform-coverage-carryover-and-grace)).
- **Voluntary Fiduciary Correction Program (VFCP):** the DOL program for correcting fiduciary breaches such as late deposits of participant contributions, with excise tax relief under PTE 2002-51 ([401(k) Deferrals & Remittance](/books/workforce/retirement-plan-deferrals#late-deposits-and-vfcp)).
- **Waiting period:** the time before a new employee's group health coverage starts, which may not exceed 90 calendar days ([Enrollment & Life Events](/books/workforce/enrollment-and-life-events#the-three-windows)).

## Contractors and Global

- **ABC test:** a state classification test that presumes employment unless the hiring entity proves the worker is free from control, works outside the usual course of its business, and is customarily engaged in an independent trade ([Worker Classification](/books/workforce/worker-classification#abc-tests)).
- **Aggregator (global payroll):** a provider that collects payroll inputs centrally, sends them to in-country partners to calculate and file, and returns normalized gross-to-net results ([International Payroll](/books/workforce/global-payroll#aggregators-and-in-country-partners)).
- **B-notice:** the notice a payer sends a payee listed on an IRS CP2100 or CP2100A for a name and TIN mismatch, within 15 business days, before backup withholding begins ([Contractors & 1099s](/books/workforce/contractors-and-1099s#b-notices-and-their-clocks)).
- **Backup withholding:** federal income tax withheld at 24% from reportable payments to a payee without a correct, certified TIN, reported on Form 945 ([Contractors & 1099s](/books/workforce/contractors-and-1099s#backup-withholding)).
- **Combined Federal/State Filing (CF/SF):** the IRS program that forwards information returns, including Form 1099-NEC, to participating states ([Contractors & 1099s](/books/workforce/contractors-and-1099s#state-filing)).
- **Contractor of record (COR):** a provider that contracts with and pays an independent contractor on a client's behalf and takes on classification review ([Employer of Record & Global Employment](/books/workforce/employer-of-record#contractor-of-record)).
- **CP2100 and CP2100A:** IRS notices listing a payer's information returns with missing or mismatched name and TIN combinations, CP2100 for 50 or more and CP2100A for fewer ([Contractors & 1099s](/books/workforce/contractors-and-1099s#b-notices-and-their-clocks)).
- **Economic reality test:** the FLSA test of whether a worker is economically dependent on a business, applied through six factors in the 2024 rule and subject to a 2026 proposal ([Worker Classification](/books/workforce/worker-classification#the-dol-economic-reality-test)).
- **Employer of record (EOR):** a provider that is the legal employer of a worker in a country where the client has no entity, while the client directs the work ([Employer of Record & Global Employment](/books/workforce/employer-of-record#eor-mechanics)).
- **Form 1099-NEC:** the information return for nonemployee compensation, at $2,000 or more for 2026, due to recipients and the IRS by January 31 ([Contractors & 1099s](/books/workforce/contractors-and-1099s#forms-1099-nec-and-1099-misc)).
- **Form SS-8:** the form a business or worker files to ask the IRS to determine a worker's status for federal employment taxes ([Worker Classification](/books/workforce/worker-classification#the-irs-common-law-test)).
- **Form W-9:** the form on which a US payee certifies its name, TIN and backup withholding status to a payer ([Contractors & 1099s](/books/workforce/contractors-and-1099s#the-w-9)).
- **Full Payment Submission (FPS):** the UK Real Time Information report of each payment to employees, sent to HMRC on or before payday ([International Payroll](/books/workforce/global-payroll#united-kingdom)).
- **G2N report:** a gross-to-net payroll result per worker per country, the unit a global payroll employer receives, reconciles and books ([International Payroll](/books/workforce/global-payroll#how-the-platforms-do-it)).
- **IRIS:** the IRS Information Returns Intake System, the only e-file system for information returns once FIRE closes after November 19, 2026 ([Contractors & 1099s](/books/workforce/contractors-and-1099s#iris-and-the-end-of-fire)).
- **Permanent establishment (PE):** a company's taxable presence in a country under a tax treaty, through a fixed place of business or a dependent agent who concludes contracts, which an EOR doesn't remove ([Employer of Record & Global Employment](/books/workforce/employer-of-record#permanent-establishment-risk)).
- **Section 3509 rates:** the reduced federal employment tax rates (1.5% of wages and 20% of employee FICA, doubled without information returns) for an employer that misclassified an employee ([Worker Classification](/books/workforce/worker-classification#the-cost-of-misclassification)).
- **Section 530 relief:** protection from federal employment tax assessments for a business that consistently treated workers as contractors with a reasonable basis ([Worker Classification](/books/workforce/worker-classification#section-530-relief)).
- **TIN Matching:** the IRS service that lets payers check a payee's name and TIN combination before filing information returns ([Contractors & 1099s](/books/workforce/contractors-and-1099s#tin-matching)).
- **Voluntary Classification Settlement Program (VCSP):** the IRS program for prospectively reclassifying workers as employees for 10% of one year's section 3509 liability, filed on Form 8952 ([Worker Classification](/books/workforce/worker-classification#the-voluntary-classification-settlement-program)).

## Running the Platform

- **Account change hold:** a hold on a newly changed deposit or funding account, near a check date or on a first deposit, released only after out-of-band verification; the run pays held destinations by a fallback ([Payroll Fraud & Security](/books/workforce/payroll-fraud-and-security#how-to-build-it)).
- **Alternate procedure (Rev. Proc. 2004-53):** by agreement, the successor in an acquisition furnishes one W-2 per acquired employee that includes the predecessor's wages and withholding, and both employers file Schedule D (Form 941) ([Changing Providers Mid-Year](/books/workforce/changing-providers#rev-proc-2004-53-standard-and-alternate-procedures)).
- **Assisted connection:** a unified API connection in which the vendor operates a third-party administrator account in the employer's payroll system, refreshing data weekly and entering writes by hand within days ([Unified HR & Payroll APIs](/books/workforce/unified-hr-apis#automated-and-assisted-connections)).
- **Automated connection:** a unified API connection authorized by OAuth, credentials or an API token that syncs on a schedule (Finch: every 24 hours) and submits writes directly ([Unified HR & Payroll APIs](/books/workforce/unified-hr-apis#automated-and-assisted-connections)).
- **Business email compromise (BEC):** a scam that compromises or spoofs email and other communications to trick a business into an unauthorized transfer or disclosure; in payroll, a request to change an employee's bank account or to send W-2s ([Payroll Fraud & Security](/books/workforce/payroll-fraud-and-security#direct-deposit-change-fraud-and-business-email-compromise)).
- **Calculation snapshot:** the complete, hashed input document a pay statement's calculation read (assignments, rates, hours, elections, year-to-date, rule versions), stored so the statement can be replayed exactly ([Payroll Platform Architecture](/books/workforce/payroll-architecture#the-pay-run-engine)).
- **Client retention:** the share of a period's beginning clients still clients at its end; differs from revenue retention, which counts dollars ([Payroll & HR Metrics](/books/workforce/workforce-metrics#how-the-companies-report)).
- **Complementary user entity control (CUEC):** a control a service organization's SOC 1 report assumes the client operates, such as approving payroll registers, reviewing changes and user access, and verifying tax deposits ([Controls, Audits & SOC 1](/books/workforce/controls-audits-and-soc1#complementary-user-entity-controls)).
- **Correction rate:** correction, void and reversal statements as a share of all statements in a period, split by cause ([Payroll & HR Metrics](/books/workforce/workforce-metrics#operational-metrics)).
- **Crosswalk (migration):** the table mapping a previous provider's employee IDs and codes to the new system's records, kept as data beyond the migration ([Changing Providers Mid-Year](/books/workforce/changing-providers#data-mapping)).
- **Deduction write-back:** a change to a payroll deduction (a 401(k) deferral, a premium) sent into the employer's payroll system by a recordkeeper or benefits platform through an API, effective for the pay period it reaches before the close ([Unified HR & Payroll APIs](/books/workforce/unified-hr-apis#deduction-write-back)).
- **Embedded payroll:** a payroll provider's engine, money movement and tax filing sold as APIs and UI components to a software platform that offers payroll to its own customers under its own brand ([Embedded Payroll](/books/workforce/embedded-payroll)).
- **Ghost employee:** a worker on the payroll who doesn't work for the employer, invented or a former employee never terminated, whose pay goes to someone else ([Payroll Fraud & Security](/books/workforce/payroll-fraud-and-security#ghost-employees-and-time-theft)).
- **Historical payroll:** a previous provider's payroll loaded per check date as a paid run with no money movement, so year-to-date, quarter totals and Schedule B come from rows ([Changing Providers Mid-Year](/books/workforce/changing-providers#how-to-build-it)).
- **Insurance cost ratio (ICR):** TriNet's ratio of insurance costs to insurance service revenues ([Payroll & HR Metrics](/books/workforce/workforce-metrics#how-the-companies-report)).
- **Master file review:** a periodic confirmation by managers of every active worker on the payroll, the control that finds ghost employees ([Payroll Fraud & Security](/books/workforce/payroll-fraud-and-security#ghost-employees-and-time-theft)).
- **On-time pay rate:** the share of expected payments available to workers by the check date; reported as provider-attributable (excluding employer and worker-data causes) and worker-experienced (all causes) ([Payroll & HR Metrics](/books/workforce/workforce-metrics#operational-metrics)).
- **Onboard status:** an embedded payroll partner's per-company readiness, such as Check's `completed`, `needs_attention` and `blocking`, by requirement category ([Embedded Payroll](/books/workforce/embedded-payroll#onboarding-as-a-state-machine)).
- **Parallel run:** a pay period computed on both the old and new payroll system and compared worker by worker before go-live, with differences classified and a go rule set in advance ([Changing Providers Mid-Year](/books/workforce/changing-providers#parallel-runs)).
- **Payroll blocker:** a named condition that stops a company from running payroll, such as a missing bank verification, missing state tax setup or an open recovery case ([Embedded Payroll](/books/workforce/embedded-payroll#onboarding-as-a-state-machine)).
- **Payroll diversion:** redirecting an employee's direct deposit to an account a criminal controls, through a phished login or an impersonated request ([Payroll Fraud & Security](/books/workforce/payroll-fraud-and-security#direct-deposit-change-fraud-and-business-email-compromise)).
- **Platform (embedded payroll):** the software company that embeds a payroll partner, owns the customer relationship and the inputs, and carries the losses its contract assigns ([Embedded Payroll](/books/workforce/embedded-payroll#partner-liability-and-risk)).
- **Reopen deadline:** on Check, the time until which an approved payroll can return to draft: the later of 11am ET on the approval deadline day or one hour after approval, capped at the approval deadline ([Embedded Payroll](/books/workforce/embedded-payroll#the-payroll-state-machine)).
- **Replay (payroll):** recomputing a paid statement from its stored snapshot and comparing to the cent, which proves the engine read nothing outside its inputs ([Payroll Platform Architecture](/books/workforce/payroll-architecture#the-pay-run-engine)).
- **Revenues per WSEE per month:** Insperity's gross billings per worksite employee per month less payroll costs per worksite employee per month ([Payroll & HR Metrics](/books/workforce/workforce-metrics#revenue-per-worker-per-month)).
- **Rule version:** a published edition of a rule set (rates, taxability, limits, engine code), recorded on every calculation that read it; a correction is a new version ([Payroll Platform Architecture](/books/workforce/payroll-architecture#versioned-rules)).
- **Schedule D (Form 941):** the IRS schedule that explains differences between Forms 941 and W-2 caused by acquisitions, statutory mergers and consolidations; filed under the alternate procedure, not the standard ([Changing Providers Mid-Year](/books/workforce/changing-providers#rev-proc-2004-53-standard-and-alternate-procedures)).
- **Separations rate (JOLTS):** separations from the payroll in a month divided by employment, times 100; split into quits, layoffs and discharges, and other separations ([Payroll & HR Metrics](/books/workforce/workforce-metrics#hr-metrics-employers-buy-software-for)).
- **SOC 1:** an examination of controls at a service organization relevant to its user entities' internal control over financial reporting, used by those entities' auditors ([Controls, Audits & SOC 1](/books/workforce/controls-audits-and-soc1#what-a-payroll-soc-1-covers)).
- **Standard procedure (Rev. Proc. 2004-53):** each employer in an acquisition reports and furnishes W-2s for the wages it paid; no Schedule D ([Changing Providers Mid-Year](/books/workforce/changing-providers#rev-proc-2004-53-standard-and-alternate-procedures)).
- **Subservice organization:** a vendor of a service organization (a bank, a tax engine, a host) whose controls matter to the service organization's SOC 1 objectives ([Controls, Audits & SOC 1](/books/workforce/controls-audits-and-soc1#complementary-user-entity-controls)).
- **Tax notice rate:** agency notices received per 1,000 employer-quarters, split by cause ([Payroll & HR Metrics](/books/workforce/workforce-metrics#operational-metrics)).
- **Time to hire:** days between two hiring endpoints the platform states (application or requisition to accepted offer or start); no official definition ([Payroll & HR Metrics](/books/workforce/workforce-metrics#hr-metrics-employers-buy-software-for)).
- **Unified HR and payroll API:** one API over many employers' payroll and HRIS systems, normalizing their data into common models and, for some products, writing deductions back ([Unified HR & Payroll APIs](/books/workforce/unified-hr-apis)).
- **Variance review:** comparing a payroll register with prior runs by total and by worker, with thresholds that force an explanation before approval ([Controls, Audits & SOC 1](/books/workforce/controls-audits-and-soc1#worked-numbers-a-variance-review)).
- **W-2 phishing:** a spoofed email, usually appearing to come from an executive, asking payroll or HR for every employee's W-2; the IRS asks victims to report to `dataloss@irs.gov` ([Payroll Fraud & Security](/books/workforce/payroll-fraud-and-security#w-2-phishing)).
- **Year-to-date load:** the batch of a previous provider's or predecessor's history loaded before a new system's first live payroll, reconciled to the source's registers and returns ([Changing Providers Mid-Year](/books/workforce/changing-providers#year-to-date-loading-and-quarter-boundaries)).
