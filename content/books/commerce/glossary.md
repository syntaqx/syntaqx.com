Commerce borrows its words from restaurants, grocery, logistics, payments and consumer law, and the same word shifts with context: a fee is a charge to the customer in one chapter and a commission to the merchant in another, a batch is a courier's trip with several orders and a retailer's file of price changes, and "available" means in stock to a store and likely to be found to a model. Entries are grouped by the part of the volume that explains them, alphabetized within each group, and each is one line and a link to the chapter that owns the idea; a word with two meanings appears once per meaning, qualified. The volume's own names for the core records (merchant, brand, location, product, item, location item, modifier group, price list, customer, company, cart, order, order line, order charge, fulfillment, shipment, inventory level, return, subscription, listing, supplier, purchase order, document) are the ones in [The Core Data Model](/books/commerce/commerce-business#the-core-data-model).

## The Business

- **1P (first party, vendor):** a retailer buying a brand's goods on a purchase order and reselling them as the seller of record, as Amazon does for items it sells from its own inventory ([Business Models & Unit Economics](/books/commerce/business-models#retailer-1p)).
- **3P (third party):** a seller listing its own goods on someone else's marketplace and paying fees on each sale; 61% of Amazon's paid units in the fourth quarter of 2025 ([Business Models & Unit Economics](/books/commerce/business-models#marketplace-seller-3p)).
- **Available (inventory):** on hand less committed at a location, less safety stock and reservations; computed, never stored ([The Commerce Business](/books/commerce/commerce-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **Brand:** a name products are sold under; a merchant can own several ([The Commerce Business](/books/commerce/commerce-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **Bundle component:** an item inside a bundle or kit, with its quantity, so the bundle sells as one line and ships as its parts ([The Commerce Business](/books/commerce/commerce-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **CAC (customer acquisition cost):** marketing spend in a period over new customers acquired in it, blended or by attributed channel, measured by cohort ([Business Models & Unit Economics](/books/commerce/business-models#the-unit-economics)).
- **Cart:** a purchase being built, with lines at the prices shown, repriced at checkout and converted to an order ([The Commerce Business](/books/commerce/commerce-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **Channel:** where an order came from (the merchant's own site or app, a marketplace, a retailer's purchase order, the point of sale, a delivery app, the phone); a property of the order, never of the merchant ([The Commerce Business](/books/commerce/commerce-business#channels)).
- **Channel partner:** the marketplace, retailer or service behind a channel, such as Amazon, Best Buy or DoorDash, whose order ID or PO number is unique per partner ([The Commerce Business](/books/commerce/commerce-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **Committed (inventory):** units sold at a location and not yet shipped ([The Commerce Business](/books/commerce/commerce-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **Company:** a business buyer (a retailer, a contractor, a school) with contacts who buy for it and, often, its own price list ([The Commerce Business](/books/commerce/commerce-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **Composable commerce:** joining a headless platform and separate best-of-breed services (search, content, promotions, tax) through APIs behind a storefront the merchant writes ([The Commerce Landscape](/books/commerce/commerce-landscape#platforms-by-class)).
- **Connected system:** an external platform, marketplace, POS, OMS, WMS, ERP or app account a merchant connects, which external IDs and system-of-record rules point at ([The Commerce Landscape](/books/commerce/commerce-landscape#how-to-build-it)).
- **Contribution margin:** what an order leaves after every cost that scales with it (goods, freight and duty, fulfillment, payments, marketplace fees, deductions, returns), kept before and after acquisition spend ([Business Models & Unit Economics](/books/commerce/business-models#the-unit-economics)).
- **Document:** an invoice, credit memo, packing slip, ship notice or EDI transaction, kept as exchanged and linked to its order, purchase order or fulfillment ([The Commerce Business](/books/commerce/commerce-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **Drop-ship:** selling goods a supplier ships directly to the customer, so the seller holds no inventory but remains the merchant of record ([Business Models & Unit Economics](/books/commerce/business-models#drop-ship)).
- **DTC (direct to consumer):** a brand selling its own products on its own site or app, keeping the full price and paying for traffic, payments, fulfillment and service ([Business Models & Unit Economics](/books/commerce/business-models#direct-to-consumer-dtc)).
- **External reference:** a POS, ERP or partner ID stored on an item or order, keyed by connected system, so the same record can be matched across systems and duplicate injections caught ([The Commerce Business](/books/commerce/commerce-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **Fulfillment:** a decision to send some of an order's lines from one location by one provider (delivery, pickup, dine-in, shipping or digital), kept separate from the order so splits and providers can change ([The Commerce Business](/books/commerce/commerce-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **GMV (gross merchandise value):** the total value of goods sold through a platform or marketplace, each company on its own definition; Shopify reported $378.4 billion for 2025 ([The Commerce Landscape](/books/commerce/commerce-landscape#how-big-it-is)).
- **Headless commerce:** a platform that keeps catalog, cart, orders and checkout behind APIs, with the storefront a separate application the merchant writes ([The Commerce Landscape](/books/commerce/commerce-landscape#platforms-by-class)).
- **Hosted SaaS platform:** a commerce platform the vendor runs (servers, upgrades, checkout, PCI validation), configured in an admin and extended by apps ([Choosing & Hosting a Store](/books/commerce/choosing-a-platform#four-ways-to-run-a-store)).
- **Inventory level:** an item's on-hand and committed counts at one location ([The Commerce Business](/books/commerce/commerce-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **Item:** something sold and counted, defined once with its SKU, barcode and tax category: a variant, a dish, a modifier option, a bundle or a digital good ([The Commerce Business](/books/commerce/commerce-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **Listing:** an item offered on an external channel under that channel's ID (an ASIN, a Walmart item ID), with the channel's status ([The Commerce Business](/books/commerce/commerce-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **Location:** a place that sells, stocks or makes goods (a store, a warehouse, a 3PL's or marketplace's fulfillment center, an online store, a kitchen, a virtual brand, a dark store), where hours, stock, prices, fee caps and tax resolve ([The Commerce Business](/books/commerce/commerce-business#who-s-who)).
- **Location item:** an item as sold at one location, with its in-store price and availability ([The Commerce Business](/books/commerce/commerce-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **LTV (lifetime value):** the contribution a customer generates over the time they keep buying, measured by cohort rather than projected from an average ([Business Models & Unit Economics](/books/commerce/business-models#the-unit-economics)).
- **Merchant:** the business that sells (a brand, a retailer, a distributor, a restaurant group, a grocer, or a franchisee under it), distinct from its locations ([The Commerce Business](/books/commerce/commerce-business#who-s-who)).
- **Merchant of record:** the party that charges the buyer, refunds and absorbs chargebacks and, as a result, owes the tax: the brand on its own site, usually the marketplace on a 3P sale ([The Commerce Business](/books/commerce/commerce-business#who-s-who)).
- **Modifier group:** a choice attached to an item, such as "choose a cheese", with minimum and maximum selections; its options are items, so they have prices, stock and their own groups ([The Commerce Business](/books/commerce/commerce-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **Omnichannel brand:** a brand selling through several models at once (its own site, marketplaces, wholesale to retailers and carriers, stores), with margin computed per channel ([Business Models & Unit Economics](/books/commerce/business-models#omnichannel-brands)).
- **Order charge:** any non-goods line on an order (shipping, a fee, tax, duty, tip, discount, commission, referral fee or retailer deduction) with its payer, payee and the rule that produced it ([The Commerce Business](/books/commerce/commerce-business#how-the-money-splits)).
- **Order line:** an item ordered, with its name, SKU and unit price snapshotted as sold and modifiers as child lines ([The Commerce Business](/books/commerce/commerce-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **Payback:** the months until a cohort's cumulative contribution repays its acquisition cost ([Business Models & Unit Economics](/books/commerce/business-models#the-unit-economics)).
- **Price list:** prices for a market, currency, customer group, company or channel, with quantity breaks and a validity range ([The Commerce Business](/books/commerce/commerce-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **Print on demand:** drop-ship in which the supplier prints the seller's design on a blank product after each order, at a base cost per order ([Business Models & Unit Economics](/books/commerce/business-models#print-on-demand)).
- **Product:** what a shopper picks before choosing options, with its title and handle; its variants are items ([The Commerce Business](/books/commerce/commerce-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **Purchase order (outbound):** what a merchant ordered from its supplier; a retailer's PO received by a brand is, to the brand, an order ([The Commerce Business](/books/commerce/commerce-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **Referral fee:** a marketplace's percentage of each sale charged to the seller, such as Amazon's and Walmart's 15% in most categories ([The Commerce Business](/books/commerce/commerce-business#a-marketplace-3p-sale)).
- **Return:** goods coming back on an order, with lines, reasons, a method (mail, drop-off, in store or returnless) and each unit's disposition ([The Commerce Business](/books/commerce/commerce-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **SAQ A, A-EP and D:** the PCI DSS self-assessments an e-commerce merchant validates against, decided by whether its payment page is fully the provider's (A), built on its own page with the provider's script or direct post (A-EP), or receives card data (D) ([Choosing & Hosting a Store](/books/commerce/choosing-a-platform#pci-dss-scope)).
- **Shipment:** a parcel or load in a carrier's hands with its tracking number; one fulfillment can be several shipments ([The Commerce Business](/books/commerce/commerce-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **Subscription:** a promise to place orders on a schedule against a stored payment method, the same shape as a membership or a service agreement ([The Commerce Business](/books/commerce/commerce-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **Supplier:** a manufacturer, distributor, drop-ship or print-on-demand partner the merchant buys from ([The Commerce Business](/books/commerce/commerce-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **System of record:** the one system whose copy of an object (items, costs, orders) wins when systems disagree, recorded per connected system ([The Commerce Landscape](/books/commerce/commerce-landscape#oms-wms-and-erp)).
- **Virtual brand:** a delivery-only menu cooked in another restaurant's kitchen, modeled as a location of its own merchant at the kitchen's address ([The Commerce Business](/books/commerce/commerce-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **Wholesale:** selling cases or pallets to retailers, distributors or carriers at a wholesale price on payment terms, net of deductions ([Business Models & Unit Economics](/books/commerce/business-models#wholesale)).

## Catalog and Pricing

- **Attribute:** a typed product fact (text, number with a unit, select, boolean, media) defined once and valued per product or item, optionally per locale and channel ([Product Information & Content](/books/commerce/product-information#attribute-models)).
- **Breakage:** the part of gift card balances that will never be redeemed, recognized as revenue under ASC 606 in proportion to redemptions or when remote, never by changing a customer's balance ([Promotions, Discounts & Gift Cards](/books/commerce/promotions-and-gift-cards#breakage)).
- **Bundle (kit):** several items sold as one line, with availability from its components and its price allocated to them; a prebuilt kit is stocked as its own unit ([Products, Variants & Bundles](/books/commerce/products-and-variants#bundles-and-kits)).
- **Collection:** a merchandised set of products with its own page, manual or rule-based, with stored membership ([Site Search & Merchandising](/books/commerce/site-search-and-merchandising#collections-and-category-pages)).
- **Compare-at price:** the struck-through "was" price beside a sale price; a legal claim about past prices, computed from price history or not shown ([Pricing & Price Lists](/books/commerce/pricing#list-sale-and-compare-at-prices)).
- **Completeness:** the share of a category's required attributes an item has for a channel and locale, used to gate publishing ([Product Information & Content](/books/commerce/product-information#what-a-pim-is-for)).
- **Discount allocation:** an order-level discount split across the lines it applied to, so tax, refunds and margin work line by line ([Promotions, Discounts & Gift Cards](/books/commerce/promotions-and-gift-cards#allocating-discounts-to-lines)).
- **Facet:** a filter beside search or collection results with counts per value, built from typed attributes per category ([Site Search & Merchandising](/books/commerce/site-search-and-merchandising#facets-and-filters)).
- **GDSN (Global Data Synchronisation Network):** GS1's network of certified data pools through which brands publish item data once and retailers subscribe ([Product Information & Content](/books/commerce/product-information#channel-content-and-syndication)).
- **Gift card:** a closed-loop stored-value balance the merchant owes the holder: a liability until redeemed, covered by Regulation E 1005.20 and state law ([Promotions, Discounts & Gift Cards](/books/commerce/promotions-and-gift-cards#gift-cards-and-store-credit)).
- **GS1 Company Prefix:** the number licensed by a GS1 member organization that begins a brand's GTINs and identifies it as the brand owner ([Products, Variants & Bundles](/books/commerce/products-and-variants#identifiers-gtin-upc-and-gs1)).
- **GTIN (Global Trade Item Number):** GS1's 8, 12, 13 or 14 digit identifier for a trade item, assigned by the brand owner and stored as 14 digits; the U.P.C. is a GTIN-12 ([Products, Variants & Bundles](/books/commerce/products-and-variants#identifiers-gtin-upc-and-gs1)).
- **MAP (minimum advertised price):** a brand's limit on the price resellers advertise, not the price they sell at, judged under the rule of reason and best run as a unilateral published policy ([Pricing & Price Lists](/books/commerce/pricing#map-and-resale-price-maintenance)).
- **Merchandising rule:** a time-bound, owned override of ranking for a query or collection: pin, hide, boost, bury, filter, redirect or banner ([Site Search & Merchandising](/books/commerce/site-search-and-merchandising#merchandising-rules)).
- **Modifier option (catalog):** a choice that changes price or personalization but not which unit is picked, so it carries no SKU or stock of its own ([Products, Variants & Bundles](/books/commerce/products-and-variants#how-the-platforms-do-it)).
- **MSRP:** manufacturer's suggested retail price; a suggestion, which a reseller is free not to follow ([Pricing & Price Lists](/books/commerce/pricing#list-sale-and-compare-at-prices)).
- **Option:** an axis a product varies on (size, color, fit), with a controlled vocabulary of values that its items each take one of ([Products, Variants & Bundles](/books/commerce/products-and-variants#options-and-the-variant-explosion)).
- **Pack size:** an inner pack, case or pallet of an item's eaches, with its own GTIN and dimensions; stock is counted in eaches ([Products, Variants & Bundles](/books/commerce/products-and-variants#units-and-pack-sizes)).
- **PIM (product information management):** the system of record for descriptive product data (attributes, copy, media and translations), with completeness per channel and publishing ([Product Information & Content](/books/commerce/product-information#what-a-pim-is-for)).
- **Price history:** the append-only record of every price that took effect, by list, item, quantity and currency, from which reference price, MAP and pricing law questions are answered ([Pricing & Price Lists](/books/commerce/pricing#price-history)).
- **Price list assignment:** which buyers a price list applies to (market, channel, group, company, membership) and whether the most specific or the lowest price wins ([Pricing & Price Lists](/books/commerce/pricing#price-lists-price-books-and-b2b-prices)).
- **Promotion:** a rule that changes a price after the shopper has chosen: who, what, when and how much, with a class, rank and stacking rules ([Promotions, Discounts & Gift Cards](/books/commerce/promotions-and-gift-cards#discount-engines)).
- **Reference price:** a past or comparison price shown beside the current one, lawful only on the basis each market requires, such as the EU's lowest price in the prior 30 days ([Pricing & Price Lists](/books/commerce/pricing#reference-prices-and-price-comparisons)).
- **Resale price maintenance:** an agreement fixing a reseller's price or minimum price, judged under the rule of reason federally since Leegin and treated as an unreasonable restraint in some states ([Pricing & Price Lists](/books/commerce/pricing#map-and-resale-price-maintenance)).
- **SKU (stock-keeping unit):** the merchant's own code for one thing it counts, unique within its catalog, distinct from the global GTIN ([Products, Variants & Bundles](/books/commerce/products-and-variants#products-and-skus)).
- **Stacking:** the rules for which discounts combine on one order: global or class exclusivity, explicit combinations, best deal or full stacking ([Promotions, Discounts & Gift Cards](/books/commerce/promotions-and-gift-cards#stacking-and-exclusivity)).
- **Store credit:** a balance owed to a known customer, usually from a return, kept on the same ledger as gift cards with its own rules ([Promotions, Discounts & Gift Cards](/books/commerce/promotions-and-gift-cards#store-credit)).
- **Surveillance pricing:** setting an individual's price from personal data, studied by the FTC and restricted or required to be disclosed by state laws from 2025 ([Pricing & Price Lists](/books/commerce/pricing#dynamic-and-personalized-pricing)).
- **Synonym (search):** an equivalent or one-way mapping between query terms, applied at search time and kept as owned, versioned data ([Site Search & Merchandising](/books/commerce/site-search-and-merchandising#synonyms-spelling-and-query-understanding)).
- **Taxonomy:** a category tree with attributes attached; the merchant's own is kept separate from Google's, Shopify's, GS1 GPC and each marketplace's, and mapped to them ([Product Information & Content](/books/commerce/product-information#taxonomies)).
- **Unit price:** the price per unit of measure (per ounce, per 100 grams, per count), computed from price and declared net quantity, required by EU law and by states adopting NIST Handbook 130 ([Pricing & Price Lists](/books/commerce/pricing#unit-pricing-and-price-display-law)).
- **Variant:** the volume's item when it belongs to a product: one combination of option values, with its own SKU, barcode, stock and cost ([Products, Variants & Bundles](/books/commerce/products-and-variants#options-and-the-variant-explosion)).

## Storefront and Checkout

- **Accelerated checkout:** a wallet or network (Shop Pay, Apple Pay, Google Pay, PayPal, Link, Amazon Pay) that supplies the email, address and payment credential in one step, after which shipping and tax must be recomputed for the address it returns ([Cart & Checkout](/books/commerce/cart-and-checkout#accelerated-checkouts)).
- **Authorization window:** how long a hold can be captured, the shorter of the network's limit and the processor's; stored as `capture_before` and watched by the fulfillment queue ([Taking Payment in Commerce](/books/commerce/taking-payment#authorization-windows)).
- **Capture at fulfillment:** charging the authorized card when goods ship rather than at checkout, so cancellations are voids and the customer pays for what they receive ([Taking Payment in Commerce](/books/commerce/taking-payment#capture-policies)).
- **Certified service provider (CSP):** a tax engine certified under Streamlined Sales Tax whose services member states pay for on behalf of qualifying remote sellers ([Sales Tax, VAT & Duties](/books/commerce/sales-tax-vat-and-duties#streamlined-sales-tax)).
- **Chargeback guarantee:** a fraud vendor's promise to reimburse fraud chargebacks on orders it approved, conditioned on shipping to the screened address ([Taking Payment in Commerce](/books/commerce/taking-payment#fraud-screening-and-chargeback-guarantees)).
- **Checkout:** a versioned, expiring attempt to turn a cart into an order, holding contact, addresses, shipping quotes and the idempotency key the order and authorization share ([Cart & Checkout](/books/commerce/cart-and-checkout#how-to-build-it)).
- **Checkout extensibility:** a hosted checkout extended through UI extensions in named targets, functions at defined decision points and sandboxed pixels, in place of template edits ([Cart & Checkout](/books/commerce/cart-and-checkout#extending-a-hosted-checkout)).
- **DAP and DDP:** Incoterms rules under which the buyer (Delivered at Place) or the seller (Delivered Duty Paid) clears and pays import duty and tax ([Sales Tax, VAT & Duties](/books/commerce/sales-tax-vat-and-duties#duties-de-minimis-ddp-and-dap)).
- **Delay notice:** the Mail Order Rule's request for a buyer's consent to a later shipping date, with a right to cancel; silence counts only for a first notice with a definite date within 30 days ([Consumer Protection Online](/books/commerce/online-consumer-protection#shipping-promises-the-mail-order-rule)).
- **Economic nexus:** a state's power to require sales tax collection once a remote seller's sales into it cross a threshold, most often $100,000 a year ([Sales Tax, VAT & Duties](/books/commerce/sales-tax-vat-and-duties#nexus-after-wayfair)).
- **Exemption certificate:** the buyer's signed claim that a sale is exempt (resale, government, nonprofit), accepted in good faith and kept as a document ([Sales Tax, VAT & Duties](/books/commerce/sales-tax-vat-and-duties#exemption-certificates)).
- **Faceted navigation:** filter links on collection and search pages that create near-endless URL combinations, kept out of the crawl except for chosen indexable categories ([Storefronts](/books/commerce/storefronts#variant-faceted-and-paginated-urls)).
- **Guest checkout:** buying without an account; the guest is still a customer record matched by email ([Cart & Checkout](/books/commerce/cart-and-checkout#guest-checkout)).
- **Headless storefront:** the merchant's own web application rendering pages from a platform's storefront API, usually handing checkout back to the platform ([Storefronts](/books/commerce/storefronts#themes-and-headless)).
- **IOSS (Import One Stop Shop):** the EU scheme for charging import VAT at checkout on consignments of €150 or less, reported monthly ([Sales Tax, VAT & Duties](/books/commerce/sales-tax-vat-and-duties#eu-vat-oss-and-ioss)).
- **Legal guarantee (EU):** the seller's minimum two-year liability for defects in goods, whatever any commercial warranty says ([Consumer Protection Online](/books/commerce/online-consumer-protection#warranties)).
- **Mail Order Rule:** the FTC rule requiring a reasonable basis for shipping promises, shipment within the stated time or 30 days, delay notices and prompt refunds ([Consumer Protection Online](/books/commerce/online-consumer-protection#shipping-promises-the-mail-order-rule)).
- **Marketing consent:** a per-channel, append-only record of what a customer agreed to receive, with wording, source and time ([Customers, Accounts & Privacy](/books/commerce/customers-and-accounts#marketing-consent)).
- **Merchant listing (structured data):** Google's Product and Offer markup for pages where the merchant sells the product, requiring a name, image, price above zero and currency ([Storefronts](/books/commerce/storefronts#structured-data)).
- **Multicapture:** capturing one authorization in several pieces, one per shipment, with the final capture releasing the rest ([Taking Payment in Commerce](/books/commerce/taking-payment#split-shipments-and-partial-capture)).
- **OSS (One Stop Shop):** the EU's single VAT return for intra-EU distance sales (Union scheme) and non-EU sellers' services (non-Union scheme), filed quarterly ([Sales Tax, VAT & Duties](/books/commerce/sales-tax-vat-and-duties#eu-vat-oss-and-ioss)).
- **Redaction:** deleting a customer's personal fields while keeping the order records that tax and accounting require ([Customers, Accounts & Privacy](/books/commerce/customers-and-accounts#deletion-against-retention)).
- **Remitter:** who pays a tax line to the authority (the merchant, a marketplace, a merchant of record or nobody), recorded on each tax charge ([Sales Tax, VAT & Duties](/books/commerce/sales-tax-vat-and-duties#how-to-build-it)).
- **Right of withdrawal:** the EU and UK consumer's right to cancel a distance purchase within 14 days of delivery for any reason, with a refund within 14 days ([Consumer Protection Online](/books/commerce/online-consumer-protection#the-eu-right-of-withdrawal)).
- **Shipping promise:** any shipping time a shopper was shown, stored with its deadline and basis because the Mail Order Rule makes it binding ([Consumer Protection Online](/books/commerce/online-consumer-protection#how-to-build-it)).
- **Shipping quote:** an option shown at checkout with its price, promised arrival and expiry, the selected one being what the order charges and promises ([Cart & Checkout](/books/commerce/cart-and-checkout#shipping-options)).
- **Storefront:** a site or app presenting an online location's catalog, by theme or headless, with its domain, locale, price list and live release ([Storefronts](/books/commerce/storefronts#how-to-build-it)).
- **Streamlined Sales Tax (SST):** the multistate agreement on common definitions, sourcing, an exemption form and state-paid service providers, with 23 full members and one associate ([Sales Tax, VAT & Duties](/books/commerce/sales-tax-vat-and-duties#streamlined-sales-tax)).
- **Theme:** a template package the platform renders and hosts (Liquid on Shopify, Stencil on Commerce.com), arranged by merchants in an editor ([Storefronts](/books/commerce/storefronts#themes-and-headless)).
- **URL redirect:** a permanent mapping from a path a storefront once served to its replacement, written whenever a handle changes and kept forever ([Storefronts](/books/commerce/storefronts#how-to-build-it)).

## Orders, Inventory and Fulfillment

- **3PL (third-party logistics provider):** a company that runs warehouses for brands, receiving, storing, picking, packing and shipping their stock for fees per receipt, unit stored and order, as ShipBob and ShipMonk do ([Warehouses, 3PLs & Fulfillment Networks](/books/commerce/warehouses-and-3pls#3pls)).
- **940 and 945:** the X12 Warehouse Shipping Order a brand sends a warehouse and the Warehouse Shipping Advice the warehouse sends back when it ships ([Warehouses, 3PLs & Fulfillment Networks](/books/commerce/warehouses-and-3pls#integrating-with-a-3pl)).
- **Advance exchange:** shipping the replacement before the original comes back, with a hold or authorization in case it never does ([Returns & Exchanges](/books/commerce/returns-and-exchanges#exchanges-and-store-credit)).
- **Advance ship notice (ASN):** a notice of what an inbound truck, pallet or carton contains, sent ahead so the receiver can check it; ShipBob's version is a warehouse receiving order ([Warehouses, 3PLs & Fulfillment Networks](/books/commerce/warehouses-and-3pls#receiving-and-inbound); for purchase orders, [Purchasing & Replenishment](/books/commerce/purchasing-and-replenishment#receiving-and-asns)).
- **Available to promise (ATP):** availability projected by date from on-hand stock, expected receipts and known demand, as NetSuite computes it; the number a backorder or delivery date depends on ([Inventory](/books/commerce/inventory#available-to-promise)).
- **Billable weight:** the greater of a package's actual weight and its dimensional weight, each rounded up, which carriers price on ([Shipping & Carriers](/books/commerce/shipping-and-carriers#rate-shopping)).
- **Blind count:** a cycle count in which the counter isn't shown the expected quantity ([Inventory](/books/commerce/inventory#cycle-counts-and-shrink)).
- **BOPIS (buy online, pick up in store):** an online order fulfilled from a store's shelf and collected at a counter or locker, modeled as a pickup fulfillment at the store ([Omnichannel Fulfillment](/books/commerce/omnichannel-fulfillment#the-options)).
- **BORIS (buy online, return in store):** returning an online order at a store counter under the order's return policy, Home Depot's name for it ([Omnichannel Fulfillment](/books/commerce/omnichannel-fulfillment#returns-in-store)).
- **Box-free return:** handing an unpackaged item and a code to a drop-off counter that consolidates many returns into one shipment, as Happy Returns and Amazon's partner stores do ([Returns & Exchanges](/books/commerce/returns-and-exchanges#return-networks)).
- **Bracketing:** buying several sizes or colors intending to return all but one; 51% of Gen Z shoppers said they do it in NRF's 2024 report ([Returns & Exchanges](/books/commerce/returns-and-exchanges#return-fraud-and-abuse)).
- **Cartonization:** choosing the smallest box that fits an order's items, because carriers bill dimensional weight ([Warehouses, 3PLs & Fulfillment Networks](/books/commerce/warehouses-and-3pls#inside-a-warehouse)).
- **Consolidated Screening List:** the US government's combined export screening lists from Commerce, State and the Treasury, used to screen buyers before an export ([Cross-Border Commerce](/books/commerce/cross-border-commerce#export-controls)).
- **Croston's method:** a forecast for intermittent demand that smooths non-zero demand sizes and the gaps between them separately; widely used, known to be biased ([Purchasing & Replenishment](/books/commerce/purchasing-and-replenishment#forecasting)).
- **Curbside (Drive Up):** pickup with the handoff at the customer's car after they check in with a parking spot or vehicle description ([Omnichannel Fulfillment](/books/commerce/omnichannel-fulfillment#curbside)).
- **Cycle count:** counting a slice of bins or items every day (by ABC class, trigger or sweep) instead of closing the building for a full physical inventory ([Inventory](/books/commerce/inventory#cycle-counts-and-shrink)).
- **DAP (Delivered at Place):** the Incoterm under which the seller delivers to the destination and the buyer clears import and pays duty and tax, typically at the door ([Cross-Border Commerce](/books/commerce/cross-border-commerce#incoterms-dap-and-ddp)).
- **DDP (Delivered Duty Paid):** the Incoterm under which the seller also clears import and pays the duty and tax, so the shopper pays nothing on delivery ([Cross-Border Commerce](/books/commerce/cross-border-commerce#incoterms-dap-and-ddp)).
- **De minimis (customs):** a value below which imports enter without duty; the US suspended its $800 exemption for all countries from August 29, 2025 and repeals it by statute on July 1, 2027 ([Cross-Border Commerce](/books/commerce/cross-border-commerce#customs-entry-and-duties)).
- **Dimensional weight:** length times width times height, divided by a carrier's divisor (139 for USPS, UPS and FedEx as of October 2026, USPS only above one cubic foot) ([Shipping & Carriers](/books/commerce/shipping-and-carriers#dimensional-weight)).
- **Disposition:** what happens to a returned unit, set at inspection: restock, refurbish, return to vendor, liquidate, donate, recycle or destroy ([Returns & Exchanges](/books/commerce/returns-and-exchanges#disposition)).
- **Distributed order management (DOM):** sourcing each order's lines across many locations (warehouses, stores, 3PLs, marketplace networks, suppliers) by rules and costs ([Order Management](/books/commerce/order-management#sourcing-and-distributed-order-management)).
- **Dock-to-stock time:** the time from stock arriving at a warehouse to being available to sell ([Warehouses, 3PLs & Fulfillment Networks](/books/commerce/warehouses-and-3pls#slas-and-what-they-measure)).
- **EAR99:** the designation for items subject to the US Export Administration Regulations but not on the Commerce Control List; most consumer goods ([Cross-Border Commerce](/books/commerce/cross-border-commerce#export-controls)).
- **EEI (Electronic Export Information):** the US export filing in the Automated Export System, required over $2,500 per Schedule B code or for licensed items ([Cross-Border Commerce](/books/commerce/cross-border-commerce#export-controls)).
- **Endless aisle:** an order taken in a store for something the store doesn't have, fulfilled from a warehouse, another store or a supplier ([Omnichannel Fulfillment](/books/commerce/omnichannel-fulfillment#endless-aisle)).
- **EOQ (economic order quantity):** the order size that balances ordering cost against holding cost: the square root of 2 x annual demand x cost per order over the yearly holding cost per unit ([Purchasing & Replenishment](/books/commerce/purchasing-and-replenishment#reorder-points-and-safety-stock)).
- **Fill rate (supplier):** units received over units ordered, a supplier score computed from receipts ([Purchasing & Replenishment](/books/commerce/purchasing-and-replenishment#suppliers-and-what-you-buy-from-them)).
- **Fulfillment order:** Shopify's unit of fulfillment work: some of an order's lines assigned to one location, with its own state (open, scheduled, on hold, in progress, closed, cancelled) ([Order Management](/books/commerce/order-management#how-the-platforms-do-it)).
- **Fulfillment request:** asking a warehouse or 3PL to ship a fulfillment, which it accepts or rejects; after acceptance, cancellation is a request it may refuse ([Order Management](/books/commerce/order-management#how-to-build-it)).
- **Hold (order):** a stop on an order or fulfillment without cancelling it, for payment, fraud, address, stock, an upsell window, an exchange, a schedule or a compliance check ([Order Management](/books/commerce/order-management#holds)).
- **HS code:** the six-digit Harmonized System classification shared by more than 200 countries, extended nationally (10-digit HTSUS and Schedule B in the US, 8-digit CN in the EU) ([Cross-Border Commerce](/books/commerce/cross-border-commerce#hs-codes-and-customs-data)).
- **IMpb (Intelligent Mail package barcode):** the GS1-128-based barcode USPS requires on commercial packages, with piece-level data sent electronically ([Shipping & Carriers](/books/commerce/shipping-and-carriers#labels)).
- **IOSS (Import One-Stop Shop):** the EU scheme since 1 July 2021 under which a seller or marketplace collects VAT at checkout on consignments up to €150 ([Cross-Border Commerce](/books/commerce/cross-border-commerce#customs-entry-and-duties)).
- **Landed cost:** a unit's supplier price plus freight, insurance, duty, brokerage and handling, allocated to received units by quantity, value, weight or volume ([Purchasing & Replenishment](/books/commerce/purchasing-and-replenishment#landed-cost)).
- **Lead time (purchasing):** the time from placing a purchase order to the goods arriving at the dock, including the supplier's confirmation, production, transit and customs; its variance matters as much as its average ([Purchasing & Replenishment](/books/commerce/purchasing-and-replenishment#lead-times)).
- **License plate number (LPN):** an ID on a pallet, carton or tote so a warehouse can move its contents as one thing ([Warehouses, 3PLs & Fulfillment Networks](/books/commerce/warehouses-and-3pls#inside-a-warehouse)).
- **Market:** a set of countries sold to the same way: currency, price list, tax-inclusive display, payment methods, duties policy and seller of record ([Cross-Border Commerce](/books/commerce/cross-border-commerce#markets)).
- **Merchant-of-record service:** a provider (Global-e, Reach, Shopify Managed Markets through Global-e) that buys from the brand and resells to the shopper abroad, taking on tax, duty, payments and local consumer duties for a fee ([Cross-Border Commerce](/books/commerce/cross-border-commerce#the-merchant-of-record)).
- **Min/max:** a reorder policy that orders up to a maximum when projected stock falls below a minimum ([Purchasing & Replenishment](/books/commerce/purchasing-and-replenishment#reorder-points-and-safety-stock)).
- **MOQ (minimum order quantity):** the smallest quantity a supplier will sell or make in one order; with case packs and order multiples it rounds every replenishment quantity ([Purchasing & Replenishment](/books/commerce/purchasing-and-replenishment#moqs-case-packs-and-order-multiples)).
- **Multi-Channel Fulfillment (MCF):** Amazon shipping a seller's orders from other channels out of the seller's FBA inventory, optionally in unbranded boxes ([Warehouses, 3PLs & Fulfillment Networks](/books/commerce/warehouses-and-3pls#amazon-multi-channel-fulfillment-and-fba)).
- **Order edit:** a staged change to an order (lines, quantities, shipping, discounts) committed with its inventory, tax and payment consequences; Shopify allows edits to unfulfilled lines only ([Order Management](/books/commerce/order-management#edits-and-cancellations)).
- **Oversell:** an order for stock that doesn't exist, from concurrency, sync lag or a deliberate policy to keep selling ([Inventory](/books/commerce/inventory#overselling)).
- **Phantom stock:** units the inventory record says are on hand that aren't on the shelf; a public study found 65% of nearly 370,000 store records inaccurate ([Omnichannel Fulfillment](/books/commerce/omnichannel-fulfillment#store-inventory-accuracy)).
- **Rate shopping:** choosing, per package, the cheapest carrier, service and account whose expected delivery meets the promise ([Shipping & Carriers](/books/commerce/shipping-and-carriers#rate-shopping)).
- **Reorder point:** the projected stock level that triggers a replenishment order: demand during the lead time plus safety stock ([Purchasing & Replenishment](/books/commerce/purchasing-and-replenishment#reorder-points-and-safety-stock)).
- **Reservation (inventory):** stock set aside with an owner and an expiry before it's sold, for a checkout, a quote, a drop or a store hold ([Inventory](/books/commerce/inventory#reservations)).
- **Restocking fee:** a percentage of the price kept from a refund, recorded as its own line with its rule ([Returns & Exchanges](/books/commerce/returns-and-exchanges#return-fees-and-restocking)).
- **Return to vendor (RTV):** sending goods back to a supplier under its authorization for a credit memo ([Purchasing & Replenishment](/books/commerce/purchasing-and-replenishment#returns-to-vendor)).
- **Returnless refund (keep it):** refunding without asking for the item back, by rule up to a value, when return costs exceed the item's recovery value ([Returns & Exchanges](/books/commerce/returns-and-exchanges#returnless-refunds)).
- **Right of withdrawal:** the EU and UK consumer's right to withdraw from a distance contract within 14 days without a reason, refunded within 14 days ([Returns & Exchanges](/books/commerce/returns-and-exchanges#return-policies-and-the-law)).
- **RMA (return merchandise authorization):** an approved return request with a number the warehouse scans to match the package to the order lines ([Returns & Exchanges](/books/commerce/returns-and-exchanges#the-return-lifecycle)).
- **Safety stock (selling buffer):** units withheld from a channel so lag and count errors don't oversell; distinct from replenishment safety stock, which covers demand during supplier lead time ([Inventory](/books/commerce/inventory#safety-stock-and-channel-buffers)).
- **Safety stock:** stock held beyond expected lead-time demand to cover variation in demand and lead time at a target service level ([Purchasing & Replenishment](/books/commerce/purchasing-and-replenishment#reorder-points-and-safety-stock)).
- **Ship-from-store:** a store picking, packing and shipping an online order with a carrier label, as a shipping fulfillment from the store location ([Omnichannel Fulfillment](/books/commerce/omnichannel-fulfillment#ship-from-store)).
- **Short pick:** a picker finding fewer units than the record says; treated as a count that corrects the store's inventory ([Omnichannel Fulfillment](/books/commerce/omnichannel-fulfillment#store-inventory-accuracy)).
- **Shrink:** the difference between recorded and actual stock that no movement explains; 1.6% of US retail sales in fiscal 2022 by NRF's survey ([Inventory](/books/commerce/inventory#cycle-counts-and-shrink)).
- **Sourcing (order routing):** choosing which location and provider ship each line: filter to feasible locations, then rank by splits, distance, cost and the promise ([Order Management](/books/commerce/order-management#sourcing-and-distributed-order-management)).
- **Split shipment:** an order sent as several fulfillments or packages, each with its own label, cost and capture ([Order Management](/books/commerce/order-management#splits-and-merges)).
- **SSCC (Serial Shipping Container Code):** GS1's 18-digit ID for a carton or pallet, printed as a GS1-128 barcode and linked to the ASN ([Warehouses, 3PLs & Fulfillment Networks](/books/commerce/warehouses-and-3pls#receiving-and-inbound)).
- **Store buffer:** units kept back from a store's online availability for walk-ins and count error, per item and store ([Omnichannel Fulfillment](/books/commerce/omnichannel-fulfillment#store-inventory-accuracy)).
- **Three-way match:** paying a supplier invoice only where the purchase order, the receipt and the invoice agree within tolerance ([Purchasing & Replenishment](/books/commerce/purchasing-and-replenishment#purchase-orders)).
- **Tracking event:** a carrier scan, stored raw and mapped to a small normalized set (pre-transit, in transit, out for delivery, delivered, exception, return to sender, failure) ([Shipping & Carriers](/books/commerce/shipping-and-carriers#tracking-events)).
- **Unified commerce:** one back end for orders, inventory and customers across every channel, as opposed to separate systems synced together ([Omnichannel Fulfillment](/books/commerce/omnichannel-fulfillment#unified-commerce)).
- **Wardrobing:** using an item and returning it as unused, such as an outfit after an event ([Returns & Exchanges](/books/commerce/returns-and-exchanges#return-fraud-and-abuse)).
- **WMS (warehouse management system):** software that directs receiving, putaway, picking, packing and shipping inside a building ([Warehouses, 3PLs & Fulfillment Networks](/books/commerce/warehouses-and-3pls#inside-a-warehouse)).
- **Zone (carrier):** a distance band from origin to destination that carriers price by; USPS uses zones 1 to 9 ([Shipping & Carriers](/books/commerce/shipping-and-carriers#rate-shopping)).

## Subscriptions and Memberships

- **Account updater:** a network service that refreshes stored card numbers and expiries when cards are reissued, run before each renewal batch ([Stored Cards & Recurring Billing](/books/payments/stored-credentials#keeping-the-card-alive); for subscriptions, [Subscription Commerce](/books/commerce/subscription-commerce#dunning-and-account-updaters)).
- **Anchor (subscription):** the day a subscription's cycle aligns to, such as the 15th of each month; without one, the cycle follows the first successful charge ([Subscription Commerce](/books/commerce/subscription-commerce#schedules-and-anchors)).
- **Automatic renewal law (ARL):** a state statute governing disclosure, consent, reminders and cancellation for subscriptions that renew unless cancelled, California's being the most detailed ([Auto-Renewal & Cancellation Law](/books/commerce/auto-renewal-law#state-laws)).
- **Billing attempt:** one try at charging a renewal, keyed by renewal and attempt number so a rerun can't charge twice ([Subscription Commerce](/books/commerce/subscription-commerce#renewal-billing-and-stored-credentials)).
- **Breakage (points):** points expected never to be redeemed, recognized in proportion to redemptions when the seller expects to be entitled to it ([Memberships & Loyalty](/books/commerce/memberships-and-loyalty#breakage)).
- **Build-a-box:** a subscription whose contents the customer picks within rules each cycle, read at cutoff ([Subscription Commerce](/books/commerce/subscription-commerce#kinds-of-product-subscription)).
- **Cancellation flow:** the screens between a cancel request and its confirmation, including reasons and save offers, configured per jurisdiction and versioned ([Auto-Renewal & Cancellation Law](/books/commerce/auto-renewal-law#cancellation-flows-in-subscription-apps)).
- **Charge lead:** the days a renewal is charged before its order ships, which moves the customer's deadline to change it and the anchor for reminders ([Subscription Commerce](/books/commerce/subscription-commerce#schedules-and-anchors)).
- **Click to cancel:** California's required online cancellation link or button, which must be shown alongside any online save offer ([Auto-Renewal & Cancellation Law](/books/commerce/auto-renewal-law#california)).
- **Continuous service:** California's term for a subscription that continues until the consumer cancels, as opposed to a renewal at the end of a definite term ([Auto-Renewal & Cancellation Law](/books/commerce/auto-renewal-law#california)).
- **Curated box:** a subscription whose contents the merchant chooses each cycle ([Subscription Commerce](/books/commerce/subscription-commerce#kinds-of-product-subscription)).
- **Cutoff:** a buffer before the anchor after which a new or changed subscription misses the current cycle ([Subscription Commerce](/books/commerce/subscription-commerce#schedules-and-anchors)).
- **Dunning:** the sequence of retries and customer messages after a recurring payment fails; for goods, the renewal order waits until it succeeds ([Stored Cards & Recurring Billing](/books/payments/stored-credentials#dunning); [Subscription Commerce](/books/commerce/subscription-commerce#dunning-and-account-updaters)).
- **Involuntary churn:** subscribers lost to failed payment rather than cancellation, reported apart because its fix is billing ([Subscription Commerce](/books/commerce/subscription-commerce#churn-economics)).
- **Loyalty ledger:** the append-only record of points earned, vested, redeemed, reversed and expired, from which balances and the liability are computed ([Memberships & Loyalty](/books/commerce/memberships-and-loyalty#points-programs)).
- **Material right:** an option, such as accumulated reward points, that gives a customer something they wouldn't get without the purchase; part of the price is deferred and recognized when the points are redeemed or expire ([Memberships & Loyalty](/books/commerce/memberships-and-loyalty#points-as-material-rights)).
- **Need-timed subscription:** a subscription whose dates follow an outside rule (region and season) rather than consumption, as Pestie's reapplication schedule does ([Subscription Commerce](/books/commerce/subscription-commerce#kinds-of-product-subscription)).
- **Paid membership:** a fee for access or benefits renewed on a term, such as Costco, Prime or Walmart+; the fee is deferred and recognized over the term ([Memberships & Loyalty](/books/commerce/memberships-and-loyalty#paid-memberships)).
- **Prepaid plan:** one charge for several scheduled shipments, held as a liability and recognized as each ships ([Subscription Commerce](/books/commerce/subscription-commerce#prepaid-plans-and-their-accounting)).
- **Replenishment subscription:** a subscription to a product used up at a steady rate, whose interval is the consumption rate ([Subscription Commerce](/books/commerce/subscription-commerce#kinds-of-product-subscription)).
- **ROSCA (Restore Online Shoppers' Confidence Act):** the US federal law (15 U.S.C. 8403) requiring clear disclosure of material terms before billing information is collected, express informed consent and simple mechanisms to stop recurring charges ([Auto-Renewal & Cancellation Law](/books/commerce/auto-renewal-law#rosca-and-what-the-ftc-enforces)).
- **Save offer:** a pause, skip, swap or discount offered during cancellation, lawful only on conditions such as a visible cancel option or the consumer's permission ([Auto-Renewal & Cancellation Law](/books/commerce/auto-renewal-law#cancellation-flows-in-subscription-apps)).
- **Selling plan:** Shopify's name for a subscription offer on a product (interval, anchor, cutoff, pricing), grouped into selling plan groups such as subscribe-and-save or prepaid ([Subscription Commerce](/books/commerce/subscription-commerce#how-the-platforms-do-it)).
- **Skip:** a subscriber's choice to miss one cycle, recorded as a skipped renewal rather than an absence ([Subscription Commerce](/books/commerce/subscription-commerce#skip-pause-swap-and-upgrade)).
- **Subscribe-and-save:** a discount off the one-time price for choosing a subscription, set by the plan's pricing policy ([Subscription Commerce](/books/commerce/subscription-commerce#kinds-of-product-subscription)).
- **Swap:** changing a subscription line's variant (flavor, size) from the next renewal ([Subscription Commerce](/books/commerce/subscription-commerce#skip-pause-swap-and-upgrade)).
- **Tier (loyalty):** a status level earned by qualifying activity in a window, with its own benefits and a requalification date ([Memberships & Loyalty](/books/commerce/memberships-and-loyalty#tiers)).
- **Unconditional gift (goods):** the rule in California, North Dakota and Oregon that goods sent under an automatic renewal without the required consent are the consumer's to keep, free ([Auto-Renewal & Cancellation Law](/books/commerce/auto-renewal-law#the-rules-at-a-glance)).
- **Withdrawal (EU and UK):** the consumer's 14-day right to cancel a distance contract without reason, from first delivery for regular deliveries of goods, distinct from cancelling future renewals ([Auto-Renewal & Cancellation Law](/books/commerce/auto-renewal-law#eu-and-uk)).

## Marketplaces

- **A-to-z Guarantee claim:** an Amazon buyer's claim against a third-party seller; one Amazon doesn't deny counts toward the Order Defect Rate and may be refunded from the seller's account ([Selling on Amazon](/books/commerce/selling-on-amazon#returns)).
- **Account Health Rating (AHR):** Amazon's 0 to 1,000 score of a seller's policy compliance, Healthy from 200, with deactivation possible below 100 ([Selling on Amazon](/books/commerce/selling-on-amazon#account-health)).
- **ACoS and ROAS:** advertising cost of sales (ad spend over attributed sales) and its inverse, return on ad spend; both inherit the network's attribution model ([Retail Media & Marketplace Ads](/books/commerce/retail-media#measurement)).
- **ASIN (Amazon Standard Identification Number):** Amazon's identifier for a product's detail page, shared by every seller who offers it ([Selling on Amazon](/books/commerce/selling-on-amazon#how-the-platforms-do-it)).
- **Clean room:** a network-hosted environment where an advertiser joins its pseudonymized data with the network's and gets only aggregated results back, such as Amazon Marketing Cloud ([Retail Media & Marketplace Ads](/books/commerce/retail-media#measurement)).
- **Cost-per-sale ad:** an ad charged only when an attributed item sells, as a percentage of the sale (eBay's general Promoted Listings, Etsy's Offsite Ads), modeled as a charge on the order ([Retail Media & Marketplace Ads](/books/commerce/retail-media#marketplace-ads-elsewhere)).
- **Deferred transaction (DD+7):** marketplace proceeds held until a set time after delivery, seven days on Amazon, before they can be paid out ([Selling on Amazon](/books/commerce/selling-on-amazon#settlements-and-the-money)).
- **Featured Offer (Buy Box):** the one offer on a shared Amazon product page shown with Add to Cart and Buy Now, chosen on price, delivery speed and performance ([Selling on Amazon](/books/commerce/selling-on-amazon#the-featured-offer)).
- **Gated category:** a category or brand a marketplace seller must be approved for before listing, often with supplier invoices as evidence ([Selling on Amazon](/books/commerce/selling-on-amazon#category-and-brand-gating)).
- **Incrementality:** the sales an ad caused that wouldn't have happened without it, measured with a holdout rather than by attribution ([Retail Media & Marketplace Ads](/books/commerce/retail-media#measurement)).
- **Managed marketplace model:** a platform that takes suppliers' goods and sets the retail price and logistics itself, as Temu's original model is described; wholesale to the supplier ([Walmart, eBay, Etsy, TikTok Shop & Others](/books/commerce/other-marketplaces#temu-shein-and-the-managed-model)).
- **Offer-only listing (match):** a listing that offers the seller's price and stock on a product the channel already has, without sending product content ([Multichannel Listing & Feeds](/books/commerce/multichannel-listing#the-listing-life-cycle)).
- **Order Defect Rate (ODR):** Amazon's share of a seller's orders over 60 days with negative feedback, an A-to-z claim or a chargeback; the target is under 1% ([Selling on Amazon](/books/commerce/selling-on-amazon#account-health)).
- **Product facts and sales terms:** the shared description of a product (title, brand, attributes, images) and one seller's offer on it (price, quantity, condition, fulfillment), synced separately ([Multichannel Listing & Feeds](/books/commerce/multichannel-listing#the-listing-life-cycle)).
- **Product type (channel):** a channel's schema for a kind of product, listing the attributes it requires and allows, such as Amazon's product type definitions and Walmart's item spec ([Multichannel Listing & Feeds](/books/commerce/multichannel-listing#amazon-listings-items-and-product-type-schemas)).
- **Reseller (marketplace):** a partner that buys a brand's goods and sells them on marketplaces as the seller, as Pattern does, making the channel a wholesale customer for the brand ([Multichannel Listing & Feeds](/books/commerce/multichannel-listing#resellers-and-agencies)).
- **Restricted Data Token (RDT):** an SP-API token required to call operations that return Amazon customers' personal data ([Selling on Amazon](/books/commerce/selling-on-amazon#customer-data)).
- **Retail media:** advertising a retailer or marketplace sells on its own shelves and with its shoppers' data, on site, off site and in stores ([Retail Media & Marketplace Ads](/books/commerce/retail-media)).
- **Seller Fulfilled Prime (SFP):** Amazon's program for sellers who ship Prime-badged orders from their own warehouse after a 30-day trial ([Selling on Amazon](/books/commerce/selling-on-amazon#seller-fulfilled-prime)).
- **Settlement report:** a marketplace's statement for a payout period, one line per transaction and amount type, reconciled to orders and the deposit ([Selling on Amazon](/books/commerce/selling-on-amazon#settlements-and-the-money)).
- **SP-API (Selling Partner API):** Amazon's REST APIs for sellers and vendors, authorized by OAuth through Login with Amazon and limited by roles ([Selling on Amazon](/books/commerce/selling-on-amazon#the-selling-partner-api)).
- **Sponsored Products:** cost-per-click ads for individual listings in a marketplace's search results and product pages; on Amazon the product must be eligible for the Featured Offer ([Retail Media & Marketplace Ads](/books/commerce/retail-media#amazon-ads)).
- **Suppressed listing:** a listing that was live and is hidden by the channel for an issue, as opposed to a submission rejected before it took effect ([Multichannel Listing & Feeds](/books/commerce/multichannel-listing#errors-and-suppression)).
- **TACoS (total advertising cost of sales):** ad spend over all sales of the advertised items, attributed or not, checkable against the order book ([Retail Media & Marketplace Ads](/books/commerce/retail-media#measurement)).
- **Valid tracking rate:** the share of seller-fulfilled shipments with a tracking number the carrier recognizes; above 95% on Amazon, 99% on Walmart ([Walmart, eBay, Etsy, TikTok Shop & Others](/books/commerce/other-marketplaces#performance-standards-compared)).
- **Vendor (1P) purchase order:** Amazon's order to a vendor, acknowledged line by line as accepted, backordered or rejected, then shipped and invoiced ([Selling on Amazon](/books/commerce/selling-on-amazon#1p-vendor-central)).

## Wholesale and B2B

- **2/10 net 30:** terms giving 2% off if paid within 10 days, otherwise the full amount due in 30 ([B2B Commerce](/books/commerce/b2b-commerce#net-terms-and-credit)).
- **810, 850, 855, 856, 860:** the X12 Invoice, Purchase Order, Purchase Order Acknowledgment, Ship Notice/Manifest and buyer-initiated Purchase Order Change Request ([EDI](/books/commerce/edi#the-transactions)).
- **820 (remittance advice):** the X12 Payment Order/Remittance Advice a retailer sends with payment, listing deductions by code ([EDI](/books/commerce/edi#the-transactions)).
- **846 and 852:** the X12 Inventory Inquiry/Advice and Product Activity Data, carrying stock and sell-through between partners ([EDI](/books/commerce/edi#the-transactions)).
- **997 and 999:** X12 acknowledgments that a functional group was received and parsed; the 999 adds errors against the implementation guide ([EDI](/books/commerce/edi#envelopes-and-acknowledgments)).
- **Approval rule:** a company's condition (amount, category, budget) that holds a buyer's cart for an approver before it becomes an order ([B2B Commerce](/books/commerce/b2b-commerce#approval-flows)).
- **AS2:** point-to-point EDI transport over HTTP with signing, encryption and a signed receipt (MDN), defined in RFC 4130 ([EDI](/books/commerce/edi#transport-vans-as2-sftp-and-apis)).
- **Company location:** a buyer's branch or site where orders ship and terms, catalogs and approvals resolve ([B2B Commerce](/books/commerce/b2b-commerce#company-accounts-and-buyer-roles)).
- **Compliance chargeback:** a fee a retailer deducts for breaking its supplier rules, such as an unscannable label or a late ASN, per carton, per occurrence or as a percent of cost ([Wholesale & Retail Distribution](/books/commerce/wholesale-and-retail-distribution#compliance-chargebacks)).
- **Contract catalog:** the items a company may see and the prices it pays, assigned to the company or a location with precedence and validity ([B2B Commerce](/books/commerce/b2b-commerce#contract-catalogs-and-price-lists)).
- **Co-op advertising:** marketing money a brand accrues as a percentage of a retailer's purchases, spent on advertising its products with proof of performance ([Wholesale & Retail Distribution](/books/commerce/wholesale-and-retail-distribution#co-op-mdf-and-promotional-allowances)).
- **cXML:** the XML protocol for punchout (setup request, order message) and purchase orders between procurement systems and suppliers ([B2B Commerce](/books/commerce/b2b-commerce#punchout)).
- **Deduction:** any amount a retailer subtracts from a payment, coded on the remittance and accepted or disputed inside the retailer's window ([Wholesale & Retail Distribution](/books/commerce/wholesale-and-retail-distribution#deductions-and-disputes)).
- **Drop-ship vendor (DSV):** a brand that ships a retailer's online orders directly to the retailer's customers, usually on the retailer's packing slip and carrier account ([Drop Ship & Print on Demand](/books/commerce/drop-ship-and-print-on-demand#retailer-drop-ship-programs)).
- **EDI (electronic data interchange):** the exchange of business documents in a standard format, X12 in North America and EDIFACT elsewhere ([EDI](/books/commerce/edi)).
- **GS1-128 label:** the carton or pallet label carrying the SSCC and shipment data as GS1-128 barcodes, built from the same pack record as the ASN ([EDI](/books/commerce/edi#gs1-128-labels-and-sscc)).
- **Interchange control number:** the nine-digit ISA13 sequence a sender assigns each X12 interchange, used to catch duplicates ([EDI](/books/commerce/edi#envelopes-and-acknowledgments)).
- **MABD (must-arrive-by date):** the date a retailer's PO must arrive at its distribution center, around which on-time windows are measured ([Wholesale & Retail Distribution](/books/commerce/wholesale-and-retail-distribution#otif-programs)).
- **MDF (market development funds):** a discretionary budget a brand allocates to a retailer or partner for approved marketing programs ([Wholesale & Retail Distribution](/books/commerce/wholesale-and-retail-distribution#co-op-mdf-and-promotional-allowances)).
- **Negotiable quote:** a quote whose lines and prices a buyer and rep revise in versions until one is accepted and converted to an order ([B2B Commerce](/books/commerce/b2b-commerce#quotes)).
- **Net terms:** an invoice due a set number of days after issue, such as net 30; in B2B, a payment method checked against a credit limit ([B2B Commerce](/books/commerce/b2b-commerce#net-terms-and-credit); [Checkout, Invoices & Payment Links](/books/payments/checkout-and-invoicing#b2b-receivables)).
- **OCI (Open Catalog Interface):** SAP's punchout protocol, returning the cart as `NEW_ITEM` form fields to a `HOOK_URL` ([B2B Commerce](/books/commerce/b2b-commerce#punchout)).
- **OTIF (on-time in-full):** a retailer's measure of whether a vendor delivered the whole order inside its window, fined as a percentage of cost on failing cases ([Wholesale & Retail Distribution](/books/commerce/wholesale-and-retail-distribution#otif-programs)).
- **Partner map:** one trading partner's requirements for one EDI document and direction (version, segments, codes, transport, acknowledgment), versioned and tested ([EDI](/books/commerce/edi#partner-testing)).
- **Print file:** a design's artwork for one placement, versioned with a hash and rights evidence, snapshotted on the order lines printed from it ([Drop Ship & Print on Demand](/books/commerce/drop-ship-and-print-on-demand#print-on-demand)).
- **Print on demand (POD):** drop shipping in which the provider prints the seller's design on a blank product after each order ([Drop Ship & Print on Demand](/books/commerce/drop-ship-and-print-on-demand#print-on-demand)).
- **Punchout:** a buyer leaving its procurement system for the supplier's catalog, whose cart returns as a requisition; the purchase order follows separately ([B2B Commerce](/books/commerce/b2b-commerce#punchout)).
- **Robinson-Patman Act:** the US law requiring promotional allowances and services to be offered to competing customers on proportionally equal terms ([Wholesale & Retail Distribution](/books/commerce/wholesale-and-retail-distribution#co-op-mdf-and-promotional-allowances)).
- **Routing guide:** a retailer's rules for shipping to it (carriers, routing requests, pallets, labels, paperwork), versioned and enforced with chargebacks ([Wholesale & Retail Distribution](/books/commerce/wholesale-and-retail-distribution#routing-guides)).
- **Seller of record:** the party the customer bought from, who owns the shipping promise, returns, tax and product claims whoever ships ([Drop Ship & Print on Demand](/books/commerce/drop-ship-and-print-on-demand#liability)).
- **Sell-through:** units a retailer sold to shoppers over units it received, from its POS data or EDI 852 ([Wholesale & Retail Distribution](/books/commerce/wholesale-and-retail-distribution#sell-through-and-pos-data)).
- **Supplier route:** the rule that a supplier fulfills an item, with its cost, production and handling days, regions and priority ([Drop Ship & Print on Demand](/books/commerce/drop-ship-and-print-on-demand#how-to-build-it)).
- **Trading partner:** a retailer, carrier or distributor a brand exchanges documents with under an agreement, routing guide and EDI maps ([Wholesale & Retail Distribution](/books/commerce/wholesale-and-retail-distribution#how-to-build-it)).
- **VAN (value-added network):** a mailbox network that routes EDI interchanges between partners, traditionally billed per kilocharacter ([EDI](/books/commerce/edi#transport-vans-as2-sftp-and-apis)).
- **Vendor agreement:** the contract a retailer signs with a vendor, incorporating its supplier manual and routing guide, which every deduction cites ([Wholesale & Retail Distribution](/books/commerce/wholesale-and-retail-distribution#becoming-a-vendor)).

## Point of Sale and Stores

- **Blind close:** counting a cash drawer without being shown the expected amount, then revealing the over or short ([Store Operations](/books/commerce/store-operations#cash-management-and-end-of-day)).
- **Check (restaurant):** what one guest or group pays for an order; an order can have several, split by item, seat or evenly ([Restaurants & Food Service](/books/commerce/restaurants#tables-seats-and-checks)).
- **Coursing:** grouping a table's items into courses that the kitchen receives as separate tickets and fires in turn ([Restaurants & Food Service](/books/commerce/restaurants#coursing-and-firing)).
- **Drop:** excess cash moved from a drawer to the safe during a shift, counted and recorded on both sides ([Store Operations](/books/commerce/store-operations#cash-management-and-end-of-day)).
- **Expediter (expo):** the kitchen station between front and back of house that sees every item on an order and releases it when all stations are done ([Restaurants & Food Service](/books/commerce/restaurants#the-kitchen-tickets-stations-and-the-kds)).
- **Fire by prep time:** staggering when each item starts cooking by its prep time so a table's items finish together ([Restaurants & Food Service](/books/commerce/restaurants#coursing-and-firing)).
- **Float:** the counted cash a drawer opens with ([Store Operations](/books/commerce/store-operations#cash-management-and-end-of-day)).
- **KDS (kitchen display system):** screens that replace printed kitchen tickets; cooks bump items when done and the times are recorded ([Restaurants & Food Service](/books/commerce/restaurants#the-kitchen-tickets-stations-and-the-kds)).
- **Manager approval:** a second staff member's PIN on someone else's void, refund or override, recorded with both names ([Store Operations](/books/commerce/store-operations#staff-roles-and-permissions)).
- **No-sale:** opening the cash drawer without a sale; sometimes needed and watched in aggregate ([Store Operations](/books/commerce/store-operations#cash-management-and-end-of-day)).
- **Offline mode (POS):** taking sales and stored card payments without a connection, within vendor limits and at the merchant's risk ([Point of Sale](/books/commerce/point-of-sale#offline-mode-and-its-limits)).
- **Organized retail crime (ORC):** theft by groups who steal goods to resell them, often online ([Store Operations](/books/commerce/store-operations#organized-retail-crime-and-the-law)).
- **Over/short:** counted cash minus expected cash at a drawer's close ([Store Operations](/books/commerce/store-operations#cash-management-and-end-of-day)).
- **Paid in / paid out:** cash added to or removed from a drawer for something other than a sale, each with a reason ([Point of Sale](/books/commerce/point-of-sale#registers-and-cash)).
- **Register session:** one drawer from its opening float to its closing count ([Store Operations](/books/commerce/store-operations#how-to-build-it)).
- **Sales receipt:** the customer's record of a sale, printed or digital, rendered from the order; distinct from a goods receipt ([Point of Sale](/books/commerce/point-of-sale#receipts-and-privacy)).
- **Service charge:** a mandatory charge on a restaurant bill; the employer's revenue, and wages if distributed, never a tip ([Restaurants & Food Service](/books/commerce/restaurants#tips-service-charges-and-the-law)).
- **Shrink event:** a known loss (theft, damage, expiry, vendor shortage) recorded when it happens, with retail and cost value ([Store Operations](/books/commerce/store-operations#shrink-and-loss-prevention)).
- **Tender:** one way a sale was paid (card, cash, gift card, store credit, EBT, house account); a sale can have several ([Point of Sale](/books/commerce/point-of-sale#tenders)).
- **Tip (restaurant):** a voluntary payment from the guest that belongs to the employee; the employer may not keep any of it ([Restaurants & Food Service](/books/commerce/restaurants#tips-service-charges-and-the-law)).
- **Tip credit:** counting tips toward a tipped employee's federal minimum wage, at most $5.12 an hour over a $2.13 cash wage ([Restaurants & Food Service](/books/commerce/restaurants#tips-service-charges-and-the-law)).
- **Tip pool:** tips gathered and divided among eligible staff by a formula; never shared with managers ([Restaurants & Food Service](/books/commerce/restaurants#tip-pools)).
- **Void:** cancelling a line or sale before it closes; nothing was sold, unlike a return ([Point of Sale](/books/commerce/point-of-sale#the-sale-at-the-register)).
- **X report / Z report:** a mid-shift snapshot of a register session, and the end-of-day close that locks the day ([Store Operations](/books/commerce/store-operations#cash-management-and-end-of-day)).

## On-Demand Delivery

- **86 (to 86 an item):** restaurant slang for marking an item unavailable because it ran out; every platform has a separate, fast endpoint or webhook for it ([Merchants, Menus & Catalogs](/books/commerce/merchants-menus-and-catalogs#availability)).
- **Acceptance deadline:** the time by which a merchant must accept or cancel an order before it escalates; DoorDash holds integration partners to merchant cancel and order failure rates below 1% ([Orders, Dispatch & Batching](/books/commerce/orders-dispatch-and-batching#the-order-lifecycle)).
- **All-in price:** a price that includes every mandatory fee; required for advertised delivery service prices in California and handled by fee disclosure safe harbors in Minnesota and Colorado ([Fees, Commissions & the Law](/books/commerce/delivery-fees-and-the-law#consumer-fees-and-all-in-prices)).
- **Basic and enhanced service fees:** New York City's categories for non-delivery merchant fees, capped at 5% and 20%, the enhanced fee allowed only if a basic plan is offered ([Fees, Commissions & the Law](/books/commerce/delivery-fees-and-the-law#city-commission-caps)).
- **Batching:** giving one courier two or more orders on one trip; a trade-off between courier time per order and the later customer's wait, best set as an explicit, versioned weight ([Orders, Dispatch & Batching](/books/commerce/orders-dispatch-and-batching#assignment-and-batching)).
- **Benefit application:** the record that a membership offset a fee on an order, kept beside the fee itself so savings and program cost are sums ([Subscriptions & Loyalty](/books/commerce/delivery-subscriptions#the-benefit-order-by-order)).
- **Busy mode:** a merchant setting that adds prep time to incoming orders during a rush; Uber suggests 10 to 20 minutes ([Orders, Dispatch & Batching](/books/commerce/orders-dispatch-and-batching#prep-time-and-etas)).
- **Commission:** the merchant's fee to a marketplace, a percentage of the order by plan, such as DoorDash's 15%, 25% or 30% ([Fees, Commissions & the Law](/books/commerce/delivery-fees-and-the-law#merchant-commission-tiers)).
- **Commission cap:** a city limit on what a delivery platform may charge a restaurant per order, such as New York City's 15% delivery fee cap and Portland's 15% and 4% ([Fees, Commissions & the Law](/books/commerce/delivery-fees-and-the-law#city-commission-caps)).
- **Core delivery service:** San Francisco's term for delivery without advertising, search placement, consulting or card processing, which must be offered at 15% or less for the cap not to apply ([Fees, Commissions & the Law](/books/commerce/delivery-fees-and-the-law#city-commission-caps)).
- **Courier imminent:** Uber Direct's flag that the courier is one minute from pickup or dropoff ([Orders, Dispatch & Batching](/books/commerce/orders-dispatch-and-batching#the-handoff)).
- **Delivery offer:** an assignment proposed to a contractor courier, with pay, distance and an expiry; declines and timeouts return the delivery to the pool ([Orders, Dispatch & Batching](/books/commerce/orders-dispatch-and-batching#assignment-and-batching)).
- **Error charge:** the share of a customer refund passed to the merchant for a kitchen or store error, such as DoorDash's 25% to 100% of the item price plus tax, disputable within a window ([Refunds, Missing Items & Fraud](/books/commerce/refunds-and-delivery-fraud#merchant-error-charges-and-disputes)).
- **ETA (estimated time of delivery):** the arrival time shown to the customer, a sum of predictions for acceptance, prep, courier arrival and travel, recomputed as the order progresses ([Orders, Dispatch & Batching](/books/commerce/orders-dispatch-and-batching#prep-time-and-etas)).
- **First-party ordering:** a merchant taking orders on its own site or app, often through a vendor like Olo, with delivery by a logistics network ([The Delivery Landscape](/books/commerce/delivery-landscape#business-models)).
- **Found rate:** the share of ordered grocery items a shopper finds; the signal behind predicted availability ([Merchants, Menus & Catalogs](/books/commerce/merchants-menus-and-catalogs#grocery-catalogs)).
- **Global matching:** assigning couriers by optimizing over all open orders and couriers together, instead of greedily per order as it arrives ([Orders, Dispatch & Batching](/books/commerce/orders-dispatch-and-batching#assignment-and-batching)).
- **GOV and GTV:** order value measures: DoorDash's Marketplace GOV ($102.0 billion in 2025) and Instacart's GTV ($37.2 billion), defined differently, so ratios built on them differ ([The Delivery Landscape](/books/commerce/delivery-landscape#take-rates-and-unit-economics)).
- **Greedy matching:** assigning the best available courier to each order as it arrives, without considering other open orders ([Orders, Dispatch & Batching](/books/commerce/orders-dispatch-and-batching#assignment-and-batching)).
- **Inventory file (full, delta, reinventory):** a retailer's catalog upload; in full and delta files a missing row leaves an item unchanged, in a reinventory file it marks the item unavailable ([Merchants, Menus & Catalogs](/books/commerce/merchants-menus-and-catalogs#grocery-catalogs)).
- **Listing agreement:** the merchant's written consent to be listed, required before listing in California and New York City ([Merchants, Menus & Catalogs](/books/commerce/merchants-menus-and-catalogs#onboarding-merchants)).
- **Logistics only (white-label delivery):** delivery of orders taken on a merchant's own channels, such as DoorDash Drive and Uber Direct, priced per delivery ([The Delivery Landscape](/books/commerce/delivery-landscape#business-models)).
- **Marketplace:** an app listing many merchants that takes the order, charges the customer and the merchant, and usually delivers with its own couriers ([The Delivery Landscape](/books/commerce/delivery-landscape#business-models)).
- **Markup:** a higher in-app price than in-store, set by the merchant; California bars the platform from charging more than the restaurant's price ([Merchants, Menus & Catalogs](/books/commerce/merchants-menus-and-catalogs#in-app-prices-and-markups)).
- **Membership:** a monthly or yearly program (DashPass, Uber One, Instacart+, Grubhub+) that waives or reduces fees on eligible orders over a minimum ([Subscriptions & Loyalty](/books/commerce/delivery-subscriptions#how-the-platforms-do-it)).
- **Menu publication:** a record of the menu payload sent to a channel, with its hash and time, answering what a customer saw ([Merchants, Menus & Catalogs](/books/commerce/merchants-menus-and-catalogs#the-source-of-truth)).
- **Net Revenue Margin:** DoorDash's revenue as a percentage of Marketplace GOV, 13.4% in 2025 ([The Delivery Landscape](/books/commerce/delivery-landscape#take-rates-and-unit-economics)).
- **Override (menu):** a price, quantity or suspension that differs when an item appears in a particular menu, item or modifier group ([Merchants, Menus & Catalogs](/books/commerce/merchants-menus-and-catalogs#menus-modifiers-and-items)).
- **Owned inventory:** a model where the seller stocks its own micro-fulfillment centers and is the merchant, as Gopuff describes ([The Delivery Landscape](/books/commerce/delivery-landscape#business-models)).
- **Pre-modifier:** a modifier of a modifier ("no", "extra", "on the side"); among the fields Toast's DoorDash integration doesn't sync ([Merchants, Menus & Catalogs](/books/commerce/merchants-menus-and-catalogs#menus-modifiers-and-items)).
- **Prep time:** the time from acceptance to ready; nobody records it directly, so platforms start from the merchant's default and infer the rest ([Orders, Dispatch & Batching](/books/commerce/orders-dispatch-and-batching#prep-time-and-etas)).
- **Promotion abuse:** using many accounts to claim per-person offers; enforced by linking devices, cards, addresses and phones at redemption ([Refunds, Missing Items & Fraud](/books/commerce/refunds-and-delivery-fraud#promotion-abuse)).
- **Refund abuse:** false missing-item or non-delivery reports, alone or through paid refund services; tied with promotion abuse as the top consumer-side fraud in one vendor's 2025 data ([Refunds, Missing Items & Fraud](/books/commerce/refunds-and-delivery-fraud#customer-refund-abuse)).
- **Return delivery:** a second fulfillment that brings an undeliverable order back; Uber Direct gives it its own ID prefixed `ret_` ([Orders, Dispatch & Batching](/books/commerce/orders-dispatch-and-batching#the-handoff)).
- **Self-delivery:** a marketplace listing whose orders the merchant's own drivers deliver, at a lower commission ([The Delivery Landscape](/books/commerce/delivery-landscape#business-models)).
- **Shop and deliver:** a shopper picks the order in a retailer's store, handles replacements and pays, then delivers, as on Instacart and Shipt ([Orders, Dispatch & Batching](/books/commerce/orders-dispatch-and-batching#shop-and-deliver)).
- **Store pause:** a temporary deactivation of a location on a channel; DoorDash treats every POS-initiated deactivation as temporary, defaulting to 14 days ([Merchants, Menus & Catalogs](/books/commerce/merchants-menus-and-catalogs#availability)).
- **Substitution:** a replacement for an item the shopper couldn't find, chosen by preference or approval and recorded as a link from the original line ([Merchants, Menus & Catalogs](/books/commerce/merchants-menus-and-catalogs#substitutions)).
- **Tip:** the customer's payment to the courier; paid in full as its own line and never used to offset guaranteed pay ([Fees, Commissions & the Law](/books/commerce/delivery-fees-and-the-law#tips)).
- **Transaction fee:** New York City's capped charge to restaurants for processing, 3% unless it equals the processor's charge to the platform ([Fees, Commissions & the Law](/books/commerce/delivery-fees-and-the-law#city-commission-caps)).
- **Trial conversion:** a free membership period that becomes paid unless cancelled, the point state notice rules target ([Subscriptions & Loyalty](/books/commerce/delivery-subscriptions#cancellation-law)).
- **Unassignment:** a courier dropping a delivery before pickup, which returns it to waiting; DoorDash's `created` state can recur ([Orders, Dispatch & Batching](/books/commerce/orders-dispatch-and-batching#the-order-lifecycle)).

## Regulated Goods

- **25(b) (minimum-risk pesticide):** a product with only EPA-listed low-risk ingredients and a conforming label, exempt from federal registration but not necessarily from state registration ([Pesticides, Chemicals & Hazmat](/books/commerce/pesticides-chemicals-and-hazmat#minimum-risk-products-and-treated-articles)).
- **Adult signature:** a carrier delivery service that requires a recipient 21 or older with ID; required for alcohol and tobacco shipments ([Alcohol, Tobacco & Age-Restricted Goods](/books/commerce/age-restricted-goods#age-verification-at-checkout-and-at-delivery)).
- **Buyers Guide:** the FTC-required window sticker on a used vehicle stating its warranty terms, part of the sale contract ([Automotive Retail](/books/commerce/automotive-retail#disclosures-on-the-vehicle)).
- **CARS Rule:** the FTC's 2023 auto dealer rule on offering prices and add-on consent, vacated by the Fifth Circuit in January 2025 ([Automotive Retail](/books/commerce/automotive-retail#the-cars-rule-and-what-replaced-it)).
- **Children's Product Certificate (CPC):** the importer's or manufacturer's certificate that a children's product passed third-party testing to every applicable rule ([Product Safety, Compliance & Recalls](/books/commerce/product-safety-and-recalls#certificates-and-efiling)).
- **Dealer management system (DMS):** a car dealer's system of record for vehicles, deals, F&I, accounting, service and parts ([Automotive Retail](/books/commerce/automotive-retail#dealer-management-systems)).
- **Dealer reserve:** a dealer's share of the difference between a lender's buy rate and the contract rate on a loan it arranged ([Automotive Retail](/books/commerce/automotive-retail#f-i-financing-leases-and-add-ons)).
- **eFiling (CPSC):** filing certificate data electronically with CBP at import, required from July 8, 2026 for most products ([Product Safety, Compliance & Recalls](/books/commerce/product-safety-and-recalls#certificates-and-efiling)).
- **EPA registration number:** the number on a registered pesticide's label identifying the product, distinct from the establishment number of the factory ([Pesticides, Chemicals & Hazmat](/books/commerce/pesticides-chemicals-and-hazmat#fifra-for-sellers)).
- **F&I (finance and insurance):** the dealership office that arranges financing or a lease and sells add-on products ([Automotive Retail](/books/commerce/automotive-retail#f-i-financing-leases-and-add-ons)).
- **FFL transfer:** shipping a firearm bought online to a federal firearms licensee in the buyer's state, who runs the background check and hands it over ([Alcohol, Tobacco & Age-Restricted Goods](/books/commerce/age-restricted-goods#firearms-and-ammunition)).
- **Limited quantity:** small inner containers of certain hazardous materials shipped under relaxed DOT rules with a dedicated mark ([Pesticides, Chemicals & Hazmat](/books/commerce/pesticides-chemicals-and-hazmat#hazmat-shipping)).
- **Monroney label:** the federally required window sticker on a new vehicle with its price, equipment and safety ratings ([Automotive Retail](/books/commerce/automotive-retail#disclosures-on-the-vehicle)).
- **PACT Act:** the federal law requiring online sellers of cigarettes and vapes to register, report every shipment monthly to each state, verify age and use adult-signature delivery ([Alcohol, Tobacco & Age-Restricted Goods](/books/commerce/age-restricted-goods#tobacco-and-vapes)).
- **Package tag (cannabis):** the state-issued, often RFID, identifier on each cannabis package that every movement and sale is reported against ([Cannabis & Hemp](/books/commerce/cannabis-and-hemp#seed-to-sale-tracking)).
- **Proposition 65 warning:** California's required warning before significant exposure to a listed chemical, shown online before purchase ([Pesticides, Chemicals & Hazmat](/books/commerce/pesticides-chemicals-and-hazmat#california-proposition-65)).
- **Purchase limit (cannabis):** the most a customer may buy per day or transaction by category, computed from each product's equivalent weight ([Cannabis & Hemp](/books/commerce/cannabis-and-hemp#the-dispensary-point-of-sale)).
- **Recall:** a corrective action to stop sale, notify buyers and remedy a hazardous product, accounted for unit by unit ([Product Safety, Compliance & Recalls](/books/commerce/product-safety-and-recalls#recalls)).
- **Restricted use pesticide (RUP):** a pesticide that may be sold only to or for use by certified applicators ([Pesticides, Chemicals & Hazmat](/books/commerce/pesticides-chemicals-and-hazmat#fifra-for-sellers)).
- **Safety data sheet (SDS):** the 16-section hazard document for a chemical, which distributors pass to business customers ([Pesticides, Chemicals & Hazmat](/books/commerce/pesticides-chemicals-and-hazmat#safety-data-sheets)).
- **Section 15(b) report:** the duty of anyone in a product's chain to tell CPSC within 24 hours of information suggesting a substantial hazard ([Product Safety, Compliance & Recalls](/books/commerce/product-safety-and-recalls#reporting-section-15-b)).
- **Seed-to-sale tracking:** a state system that follows every cannabis plant and package from planting to sale, such as Metrc ([Cannabis & Hemp](/books/commerce/cannabis-and-hemp#seed-to-sale-tracking)).
- **Three-tier system:** the post-Prohibition structure separating alcohol producers, wholesalers and retailers ([Alcohol, Tobacco & Age-Restricted Goods](/books/commerce/age-restricted-goods#alcohol-the-three-tier-system)).
- **UN3480 / UN3481:** the UN numbers for lithium-ion batteries shipped alone (including power banks) and packed with or contained in equipment ([Pesticides, Chemicals & Hazmat](/books/commerce/pesticides-chemicals-and-hazmat#lithium-batteries)).
