Banking borrows its words from accounting, law and a century of bank operations, and the same word shifts with context: a hold is a card authorization on one screen and a garnishment freeze on another, a balance is ledger, available or collected depending on who asks, a program is a fintech's product to a sponsor bank and a card network's portfolio to an issuer, and "margin" means one thing in a bank's NIM and another in a credit union's report to NCUA. Entries are grouped by the part of the volume that explains them, alphabetized within each group, and each is one line and a link to the chapter that owns the idea; a word with two meanings appears once per meaning, qualified. Terms the [Payments](/books/payments/glossary) glossary already defines keep its definition. The volume's own names for the core records (institution, program, party, party identity, product, account, account party, GL account, journal entry, transaction, hold, balance snapshot, interest accrual, fee assessment, statement, disclosure, application, credit decision, loan, payment schedule, loan payment, payment allocation, collateral, case) are the ones in [The Core Data Model](/books/banking-and-lending/banking-business#the-core-data-model).

## The Business

- **Account:** one deposit, loan or line contract with one institution, under one product version and in at most one program ([The Banking & Lending Business](/books/banking-and-lending/banking-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **Account party:** a party's role on an account (owner, joint owner, authorized signer, beneficiary, guarantor, co-borrower), effective-dated, deciding insurance, authority and liability ([The Banking & Lending Business](/books/banking-and-lending/banking-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **Asset-liability management (ALM):** measuring and steering how a bank's earnings and economic value respond to interest rates, through repricing, behavioral assumptions and hedges ([Bank Economics & Balance Sheet Risk](/books/banking-and-lending/bank-economics#interest-rate-risk-and-alm)).
- **Available balance:** ledger balance less open holds, plus any funds the institution makes available early; what the customer can spend now ([The Banking & Lending Business](/books/banking-and-lending/banking-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **Balance snapshot:** an account's ledger, available and collected balances frozen for one business date, which statements, interest and overdraft decisions read ([The Banking & Lending Business](/books/banking-and-lending/banking-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **Bank holding company:** a company that controls a bank, supervised by the Federal Reserve whatever the bank's charter ([Charters & Regulators](/books/banking-and-lending/charters-and-regulators#holding-companies)).
- **Bridge bank:** a temporary national bank the FDIC charters through the OCC to run a failed bank while it arranges a final sale; two years, extendable three times by a year ([When Banks Fail](/books/banking-and-lending/bank-failures#how-the-fdic-resolves-a-bank)).
- **Call report:** the quarterly report of condition and income every insured bank files (FFIEC 031, 041 or 051), due 30 days after quarter end and public ([Exams, Ratings & Call Reports](/books/banking-and-lending/exams-and-supervision#call-reports)).
- **CAMELS:** the supervisory rating of capital, asset quality, management, earnings, liquidity and sensitivity to market risk, each and a composite from 1 (best) to 5; confidential ([Exams, Ratings & Call Reports](/books/banking-and-lending/exams-and-supervision#camels)).
- **Case:** a dispute, error claim, fraud claim, legal process, complaint or investigation, with the time notice arrived, the rule's deadline and the outcome ([The Banking & Lending Business](/books/banking-and-lending/banking-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **Change in control:** acquiring the power to direct a bank or to vote 25% or more of a class of its voting securities, which needs 60 days' prior notice to its regulator ([Starting a Bank](/books/banking-and-lending/starting-a-bank#buying-a-bank-instead)).
- **Collected balance:** the part of an account's balance that has actually cleared ([The Banking & Lending Business](/books/banking-and-lending/banking-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **Community bank:** the FDIC's category for smaller, relationship-focused banks; 3,818 of 4,238 insured institutions at June 30, 2026 ([The Banking Landscape](/books/banking-and-lending/banking-landscape#banks-by-size)).
- **Community bank leverage ratio (CBLR):** the optional simple capital framework for qualifying banks under $10 billion; a leverage ratio above 8% counts as well capitalized ([Bank Economics & Balance Sheet Risk](/books/banking-and-lending/bank-economics#capital-in-brief)).
- **Community Reinvestment Act (CRA):** the law requiring regulators to rate how each bank meets its community's credit needs and weigh it on applications ([Exams, Ratings & Call Reports](/books/banking-and-lending/exams-and-supervision#cra)).
- **Confidential supervisory information (CSI):** examination reports, ratings and findings, which remain the agency's property and can't be disclosed without its permission ([Exams, Ratings & Call Reports](/books/banking-and-lending/exams-and-supervision#confidential-supervisory-information)).
- **Consent order:** a public enforcement order a bank agrees to, with articles and deadlines, enforceable under 12 USC 1818 until terminated ([Exams, Ratings & Call Reports](/books/banking-and-lending/exams-and-supervision#enforcement)).
- **Credit decision:** the record of an approval, counteroffer or decline with its frozen inputs, policy and model versions, outcome and principal reasons ([The Banking & Lending Business](/books/banking-and-lending/banking-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **Credit union:** a member-owned cooperative depository chartered by NCUA or a state and insured by the NCUSIF; 4,214 federally insured at June 30, 2026 ([The Banking Landscape](/books/banking-and-lending/banking-landscape#credit-unions)).
- **De novo bank:** a newly chartered bank, held to its approved business plan and an 8% tier 1 leverage floor for its first three years ([Starting a Bank](/books/banking-and-lending/starting-a-bank#the-path)).
- **Deposit beta:** the share of a change in the policy rate that a bank passes on to its depositors' rates ([The Banking & Lending Business](/books/banking-and-lending/banking-business#the-spread)).
- **Disclosure:** a record of what was shown or sent to a customer: the template version, the rendered document, the channel, the time and the E-SIGN consent relied on ([The Banking & Lending Business](/books/banking-and-lending/banking-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **Economic value of equity (EVE):** the present value of a bank's assets minus its liabilities under a rate scenario, the long-run view of interest rate risk ([Bank Economics & Balance Sheet Risk](/books/banking-and-lending/bank-economics#interest-rate-risk-and-alm)).
- **Efficiency ratio:** noninterest expense over revenue (taxable-equivalent net interest income plus noninterest income); lower is better ([Bank Economics & Balance Sheet Risk](/books/banking-and-lending/bank-economics#the-efficiency-ratio)).
- **Exempt issuer:** a debit card issuer with less than $10 billion in assets with its affiliates, outside Regulation II's interchange cap ([The Banking & Lending Business](/books/banking-and-lending/banking-business#interchange)).
- **FDIC certificate number:** the FDIC's identifier for an insured institution, the key to its public data ([The Banking Landscape](/books/banking-and-lending/banking-landscape#how-to-build-it)).
- **Field of membership:** the definition of who may join a credit union, checked as an account opening rule ([The Banking Landscape](/books/banking-and-lending/banking-landscape#credit-unions)).
- **Gain on sale:** the difference between what an investor pays for a loan and its cost to originate, booked when the loan is sold ([The Banking & Lending Business](/books/banking-and-lending/banking-business#gain-on-sale)).
- **GL account:** an account in the institution's chart of accounts, with its type, normal side and call report line ([The Banking & Lending Business](/books/banking-and-lending/banking-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **Hold (deposit account):** an amount set aside from an account's available balance without changing its ledger balance, for a card authorization, deposit availability, legal process, fraud or collateral, with an expiry ([The Banking & Lending Business](/books/banking-and-lending/banking-business#the-life-of-a-deposit)).
- **Independent mortgage company:** a nonbank mortgage lender; 63.1% of home-purchase loans in 2023 HMDA data ([The Banking Landscape](/books/banking-and-lending/banking-landscape#nonbank-lenders)).
- **Industrial loan company (ILC):** a state-chartered, FDIC-insured industrial bank whose commercial parent can avoid bank holding company status ([Charters & Regulators](/books/banking-and-lending/charters-and-regulators#industrial-loan-companies)).
- **Institution:** a bank or credit union, with its charter type, primary regulator, FDIC certificate or NCUA charter number and RSSD ID ([The Banking & Lending Business](/books/banking-and-lending/banking-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **Institution event:** a dated merger, acquisition, failure, charter conversion, name change or core conversion, with the routing numbers it moved and its source ([The Banking Landscape](/books/banking-and-lending/banking-landscape#how-to-build-it)).
- **Interchange:** the fee the acquirer pays the issuer on every card transaction, set by the networks' tables ([The Banking & Lending Business](/books/banking-and-lending/banking-business#interchange)).
- **Interest accrual:** a day's interest on an account, computed at full precision before it posts as a journal entry ([The Banking & Lending Business](/books/banking-and-lending/banking-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **Journal entry:** one balanced money movement in the institution's books, with lines that sum to zero, a business date, a source and an idempotency key ([The Banking & Lending Business](/books/banking-and-lending/banking-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **Least-cost test:** the FDIC's duty to resolve a failed bank by the method least costly to the Deposit Insurance Fund ([When Banks Fail](/books/banking-and-lending/bank-failures#the-least-cost-test-and-the-systemic-risk-exception)).
- **Ledger balance:** the sum of an account's posted transactions ([The Banking & Lending Business](/books/banking-and-lending/banking-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **Line:** an account with a limit the customer can draw and repay, such as a credit card, a HELOC or a business line of credit ([The Banking & Lending Business](/books/banking-and-lending/banking-business#who-s-who)).
- **Liquidity coverage ratio (LCR):** high-quality liquid assets over 30-day net cash outflows, at least 1.0 for the largest banks ([Bank Economics & Balance Sheet Risk](/books/banking-and-lending/bank-economics#liquidity)).
- **Loan:** the lending terms that extend a loan or line account: principal, rate, term, amortization method, owner and servicer ([The Banking & Lending Business](/books/banking-and-lending/banking-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **Matter requiring attention (MRA):** a written supervisory finding management must remediate by a date; since November 2, 2026 limited by the OCC and FDIC to material financial risk and substantive violations ([Exams, Ratings & Call Reports](/books/banking-and-lending/exams-and-supervision#findings-mras-and-violations)).
- **Middleware (BaaS platform):** software between fintech programs and banks that provides accounts, cards, payments and a subledger as an API ([The Banking & Lending Business](/books/banking-and-lending/banking-business#who-s-who)).
- **Mortgage servicing right (MSR):** the right to service a mortgage and collect its servicing fee, an asset that can be bought and sold ([The Banking & Lending Business](/books/banking-and-lending/banking-business#servicing)).
- **National bank:** a bank chartered and supervised by the OCC, a Federal Reserve member, with National Bank Act preemption ([Charters & Regulators](/books/banking-and-lending/charters-and-regulators#national-banks-and-the-occ)).
- **National trust bank:** a national bank limited to fiduciary and related activities, with no insured deposits ([Charters & Regulators](/books/banking-and-lending/charters-and-regulators#trust-banks-and-novel-charters)).
- **Neobank:** a consumer fintech offering bank accounts in its own brand, either as a program manager on sponsor banks or with its own charter ([The Banking Landscape](/books/banking-and-lending/banking-landscape#neobanks)).
- **Net interest income:** interest earned on loans and securities less interest paid on deposits and borrowings ([The Banking & Lending Business](/books/banking-and-lending/banking-business#the-spread)).
- **Net interest margin (NIM):** net interest income over average earning assets; 3.32% for FDIC-insured institutions in the second quarter of 2026 ([The Banking & Lending Business](/books/banking-and-lending/banking-business#the-spread)).
- **Ownership category:** the FDIC's classification of how a deposit is held (single, joint, retirement, trust, employee benefit, business, government), each insured separately ([The Banking & Lending Business](/books/banking-and-lending/banking-business#the-life-of-a-deposit)).
- **Party:** a person or business, independent of any account ([The Banking & Lending Business](/books/banking-and-lending/banking-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **Party identity:** a verified identifier for a party, with the method, the vendor's result and when it was verified ([The Banking & Lending Business](/books/banking-and-lending/banking-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **Preemption:** federal banking law displacing a state law that discriminates against or prevents or significantly interferes with a national bank's powers ([Charters & Regulators](/books/banking-and-lending/charters-and-regulators#preemption)).
- **Preliminary conditional approval:** the OCC's first-step charter approval, after which capital must be raised within 12 months and the bank opened within 18 ([Starting a Bank](/books/banking-and-lending/starting-a-bank#the-path)).
- **Primary federal regulator:** the agency that supervises a bank day to day: the OCC for national banks, the Federal Reserve for state member banks, the FDIC for state nonmember banks ([Charters & Regulators](/books/banking-and-lending/charters-and-regulators#the-charter-map)).
- **Problem Bank List:** the FDIC's count of banks rated CAMELS composite 4 or 5; 47 at June 30, 2026 ([Exams, Ratings & Call Reports](/books/banking-and-lending/exams-and-supervision#camels)).
- **Product version:** one immutable set of a product's rates, fees, limits and terms; an account stays on its version until moved with notice ([The Banking & Lending Business](/books/banking-and-lending/banking-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **Program:** a fintech's product on an institution's charter, with its program manager, any middleware and its product scope ([The Banking & Lending Business](/books/banking-and-lending/banking-business#who-s-who)).
- **Program manager (banking program):** the company that designs, markets and services a deposit, card or lending product on a sponsor bank's charter ([The Banking & Lending Business](/books/banking-and-lending/banking-business#who-s-who)).
- **Prompt corrective action:** the capital categories, from well capitalized to critically undercapitalized, that trigger mandatory supervisory action ([Bank Economics & Balance Sheet Risk](/books/banking-and-lending/bank-economics#capital-in-brief)).
- **Provisional hold (failure):** a hold a large bank must be able to place automatically on uninsured balances on the day after it fails, under 12 CFR 360.9 ([When Banks Fail](/books/banking-and-lending/bank-failures#the-receivership-weekend-in-systems)).
- **Purchase and assumption (P&A):** the usual resolution, in which a healthy bank assumes a failed bank's deposits and buys some or all of its assets ([When Banks Fail](/books/banking-and-lending/bank-failures#how-the-fdic-resolves-a-bank)).
- **Receiver's certificate:** an uninsured depositor's claim on a failed bank's receivership, paid as its assets are sold ([When Banks Fail](/books/banking-and-lending/bank-failures#how-the-fdic-resolves-a-bank)).
- **Regulation II:** the Federal Reserve's rule capping debit interchange for issuers of $10 billion or more at 21 cents plus 0.05% plus a fraud-prevention adjustment of up to 1 cent; its 21-cent standard was held invalid by the Eighth Circuit on October 26, 2026, pending the mandate ([The Banking & Lending Business](/books/banking-and-lending/banking-business#interchange)).
- **RSSD ID:** the Federal Reserve's identifier for a financial institution, used across its releases and structure data ([The Banking Landscape](/books/banking-and-lending/banking-landscape#how-to-build-it)).
- **Servicer:** the company that bills, collects and reports a loan, which may not own it ([The Banking & Lending Business](/books/banking-and-lending/banking-business#servicing)).
- **Significant deviation:** a change from a de novo bank's approved business plan (new products, partners, markets, growth well off projection) that needs prior regulatory non-objection for three years ([Starting a Bank](/books/banking-and-lending/starting-a-bank#capital-liquidity-and-the-three-year-plan)).
- **Special assessment:** a charge on banks to recover Deposit Insurance Fund losses from a systemic risk exception; 3.36 basis points a quarter on uninsured deposits above $5 billion after the 2023 failures ([When Banks Fail](/books/banking-and-lending/bank-failures#the-special-assessment)).
- **Sponsor bank (deposits and lending):** a bank that holds the accounts and issues the cards and loans of a fintech program, and carries the program's regulatory obligations ([The Banking Landscape](/books/banking-and-lending/banking-landscape#sponsor-banks-and-baas)).
- **State member bank:** a state-chartered bank that has joined the Federal Reserve System, supervised by the Fed and its state ([Charters & Regulators](/books/banking-and-lending/charters-and-regulators#state-banks-member-and-nonmember)).
- **State nonmember bank:** a state-chartered bank outside the Federal Reserve System, supervised by the FDIC and its state ([Charters & Regulators](/books/banking-and-lending/charters-and-regulators#state-banks-member-and-nonmember)).
- **Systemic risk exception:** the only exception to the least-cost test, needing two-thirds of the FDIC and Fed boards and the Treasury Secretary; used for SVB and Signature in March 2023 ([When Banks Fail](/books/banking-and-lending/bank-failures#the-least-cost-test-and-the-systemic-risk-exception)).
- **Taxable-equivalent (FTE) basis:** net interest income grossed up for tax-exempt income so yields compare with taxable assets ([Bank Economics & Balance Sheet Risk](/books/banking-and-lending/bank-economics#net-interest-margin)).
- **Technology provider:** a core, digital banking, origination, card processing or BaaS vendor, recorded with which institutions run it and when ([The Banking Landscape](/books/banking-and-lending/banking-landscape#how-to-build-it)).
- **Transaction:** the customer-visible activity row on an account, linked to the journal entry that is its double-entry truth ([The Banking & Lending Business](/books/banking-and-lending/banking-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **Uniform Bank Performance Report (UBPR):** the FFIEC's public report of a bank's ratios against its peer group, built from call reports ([Exams, Ratings & Call Reports](/books/banking-and-lending/exams-and-supervision#reading-the-public-data)).
- **Unsafe or unsound practice:** conduct contrary to prudent operation likely to materially harm a bank's financial condition or risk loss to the Deposit Insurance Fund, the basis for orders and, since November 2026, MRAs ([Exams, Ratings & Call Reports](/books/banking-and-lending/exams-and-supervision#findings-mras-and-violations)).
- **Well capitalized:** the top capital category: total risk-based 10%, tier 1 risk-based 8%, CET1 6.5% and leverage 5%, and under no order setting a specific level ([Bank Economics & Balance Sheet Risk](/books/banking-and-lending/bank-economics#capital-in-brief)).

## The Core

- **APY earned:** the yield an account actually earned over a statement period, from the interest paid and the average daily balance, shown on the periodic statement ([Interest, APY & Statements](/books/banking-and-lending/interest-and-statements#apy-earned-the-statement-formula)).
- **Average daily balance method:** interest computed by applying a periodic rate to the average of the period's daily balances; with the daily balance method, one of the two Regulation DD allows ([Interest, APY & Statements](/books/banking-and-lending/interest-and-statements#balance-methods)).
- **Bank Service Company Act:** the statute making services a bank buys by contract, such as a hosted core, examinable as if the bank performed them itself (12 USC 1867) ([Core Banking Systems](/books/banking-and-lending/core-banking-systems#hosted-in-house-and-cloud-native)).
- **Batch core:** a core that memo posts during the day and hard posts, accrues and rolls the business date in a nightly batch ([Core Banking Systems](/books/banking-and-lending/core-banking-systems#batch-cores-and-real-time-cores)).
- **Block (deposit account):** a reduction of available balance that lasts until someone releases it, as for legal process or fraud, as opposed to a hold that expires ([Posting, Holds & Balances](/books/banking-and-lending/posting-holds-and-balances#holds-and-blocks)).
- **Booking date, value date:** when an entry posts to the books, and the date it counts from for interest, fees and availability; a backdated correction has a past value date and today's booking date ([Posting, Holds & Balances](/books/banking-and-lending/posting-holds-and-balances#effective-dates-and-backdating)).
- **Change in terms:** a change to a disclosed account term; adverse changes to consumer deposit terms need 30 days' notice (Reg DD) and significant changes to open-end credit 45 (Reg Z) ([Products & Product Configuration](/books/banking-and-lending/product-configuration#changing-terms-on-existing-accounts)).
- **Coexistence:** running two cores at once during a progressive migration, with each account's system of record held as data and every integration routing by it ([Core Modernization & Conversions](/books/banking-and-lending/core-modernization#coexistence-and-the-system-of-record)).
- **Compounding:** interest earning interest, at crediting or daily on accrued interest; the disclosed APY must reflect the frequency the account uses ([Interest, APY & Statements](/books/banking-and-lending/interest-and-statements#compounding)).
- **Conversion (core):** moving an institution's accounts, history and obligations in flight from one core to another, usually over a weekend after rehearsals ([Core Modernization & Conversions](/books/banking-and-lending/core-modernization#conversion-weekends)).
- **Core banking system:** the system of record for an institution's accounts, postings and general ledger, usually a vendor's product hosted by the vendor ([The Banking Landscape](/books/banking-and-lending/banking-landscape#how-the-platforms-do-it)).
- **Crediting (interest):** moving accrued interest into the account's balance, where it becomes spendable and, for tax, paid; rounding happens here, once ([Interest, APY & Statements](/books/banking-and-lending/interest-and-statements#accrual-is-daily)).
- **Day count convention:** the rule that turns an annual rate into a daily one: Actual/365, Actual/360, 30/360 or Actual/Actual ([Interest, APY & Statements](/books/banking-and-lending/interest-and-statements#day-count-conventions)).
- **End of day (EOD):** the ordered sequence that closes a business date: hard posting, item fees, accrual, date-driven events, snapshots, GL proofs, files out and the date roll ([Posting, Holds & Balances](/books/banking-and-lending/posting-holds-and-balances#end-of-day-and-the-business-date)).
- **Float:** deposited items credited to the ledger but not yet collected from the paying bank; ledger balance minus float is the collected balance ([Posting, Holds & Balances](/books/banking-and-lending/posting-holds-and-balances#three-balances)).
- **GL mapping:** the configuration that says which general ledger accounts each kind of activity on each product version debits and credits ([Core Banking Systems](/books/banking-and-lending/core-banking-systems#the-general-ledger-and-its-subledgers)).
- **Grandfathering:** leaving existing accounts on an older product version when the product changes ([Products & Product Configuration](/books/banking-and-lending/product-configuration#grandfathering)).
- **Hard posting:** recording a transaction in the ledger with its journal entry, final except by reversal ([Posting, Holds & Balances](/books/banking-and-lending/posting-holds-and-balances#memo-posting-and-hard-posting)).
- **Memo posting:** recording an item that changes available balance before it reaches the ledger, such as a card authorization or a deposit under review ([Posting, Holds & Balances](/books/banking-and-lending/posting-holds-and-balances#memo-posting-and-hard-posting)).
- **Posting order:** the order a core posts a day's items in, which decides how many overdraw an account near zero; high-to-low ordering was the subject of the overdraft litigation ([Posting, Holds & Balances](/books/banking-and-lending/posting-holds-and-balances#posting-order)).
- **Product factory:** the part of a core that defines products: rates, tiers, fees, limits, maturity, dormancy and GL mapping ([Products & Product Configuration](/books/banking-and-lending/product-configuration#the-product-factory)).
- **Progressive migration:** replacing a core in slices (by product, by component, or greenfield beside it) rather than in one big-bang conversion ([Core Modernization & Conversions](/books/banking-and-lending/core-modernization#big-bang-or-progressive)).
- **Real-time core:** a core that hard posts each transaction as it happens, keeping end of day for accrual, fees and statements ([Core Banking Systems](/books/banking-and-lending/core-banking-systems#batch-cores-and-real-time-cores)).
- **Subledger proof:** the daily check that the sum of account balances on a product equals its GL control line ([Core Banking Systems](/books/banking-and-lending/core-banking-systems#how-to-build-it)).
- **Subledger:** per-account detail beneath the general ledger, which must sum to the GL's balances every day ([The Banking & Lending Business](/books/banking-and-lending/banking-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **Tiering method A, B:** Regulation DD's two tiered-rate shapes: the whole balance earns its tier's rate (A), or each slice earns its own tier's rate (B) ([Interest, APY & Statements](/books/banking-and-lending/interest-and-statements#balance-methods)).

## Deposits

- **APSN (authorize positive, settle negative):** charging an overdraft fee when a card purchase approved against a positive available balance settles against a negative one ([Overdraft, NSF & Fees](/books/banking-and-lending/overdraft-and-fees#which-balance-decides)).
- **APY (annual percentage yield):** the yearly yield on a deposit including compounding, computed by Regulation DD's formula from interest over principal and days in the term ([The Banking & Lending Business](/books/banking-and-lending/banking-business#the-life-of-a-deposit)).
- **Banking day, business day:** the part of a business day a bank office is open for substantially all its business, when a deposit is received; and any weekday that isn't a federal holiday, which availability deadlines count ([Funds Availability](/books/banking-and-lending/funds-availability#what-the-schedule-covers)).
- **Certificate of deposit (CD, share certificate):** a time deposit with a maturity of at least seven days, a disclosed early withdrawal penalty and a renewal policy ([Deposit Accounts & Truth in Savings](/books/banking-and-lending/deposit-accounts#the-four-shapes)).
- **Debanking (politicized or unlawful):** restricting or adversely changing a customer's accounts or services for political or religious beliefs or politically disfavored lawful business, as defined by Executive Order 14331 ([Dormancy, Escheat & Closing Accounts](/books/banking-and-lending/dormancy-and-escheat#closing-accounts)).
- **Deposit insurance:** FDIC coverage of $250,000 per depositor, per insured bank, per ownership category ([Deposit Insurance](/books/banking-and-lending/deposit-insurance)).
- **Dormant account:** an institution's own status for an account without owner activity for a period it chooses, separate from the state's abandonment clock ([Dormancy, Escheat & Closing Accounts](/books/banking-and-lending/dormancy-and-escheat#dormant-is-not-abandoned)).
- **Due diligence letter (unclaimed property):** the notice a holder must send an apparent owner before reporting property to the state, by each state's window and threshold ([Dormancy, Escheat & Closing Accounts](/books/banking-and-lending/dormancy-and-escheat#due-diligence-and-reporting)).
- **Early withdrawal penalty:** what a time deposit charges for withdrawing before maturity, usually days of interest on the amount withdrawn; at least seven days' simple interest within six days of deposit ([Deposit Accounts & Truth in Savings](/books/banking-and-lending/deposit-accounts#worked-numbers-an-early-withdrawal)).
- **Error (Regulation E):** an unauthorized or incorrect transfer, an omission from a statement, a bookkeeping error, an ATM shortfall, an unidentified transfer, or a request for required documentation ([Errors & Disputes on Deposit Accounts](/books/banking-and-lending/deposit-errors-and-disputes#what-counts-as-an-error)).
- **Escheatment:** turning unclaimed money over to the state after a dormancy period ([Dormancy, Escheat & Closing Accounts](/books/banking-and-lending/dormancy-and-escheat#due-diligence-and-reporting)).
- **Exception hold:** holding a deposit beyond Regulation CC's schedule under one of six exceptions (new account, large deposit, redeposit, repeated overdrafts, reasonable cause, emergency), with a notice ([Funds Availability](/books/banking-and-lending/funds-availability#exception-holds)).
- **Expedited recredit:** Regulation CC's claim for a consumer improperly charged for a substitute check: up to $2,500 by the 10th business day if undecided, the rest by the 45th calendar day ([Errors & Disputes on Deposit Accounts](/books/banking-and-lending/deposit-errors-and-disputes#regulation-cc-and-check-claims)).
- **Financial institution data match (FIDM):** the quarterly match of bank accounts against parents owing past-due child support that state agencies run under federal law ([Levies, Garnishments & Legal Process](/books/banking-and-lending/legal-process#child-support)).
- **Garnishment:** court process by which a judgment creditor freezes and takes a debtor's funds held by a bank, subject to state exemptions and federal benefit protection ([Levies, Garnishments & Legal Process](/books/banking-and-lending/legal-process#what-arrives)).
- **Grace period (time deposit):** the days after a CD matures and renews during which the depositor can withdraw or change terms without penalty, commonly ten ([Deposit Accounts & Truth in Savings](/books/banking-and-lending/deposit-accounts#maturity-and-renewal)).
- **IRS levy (Form 668-A):** the IRS's seizure of funds on deposit when served, which the bank surrenders after a 21-day hold unless released ([Levies, Garnishments & Legal Process](/books/banking-and-lending/legal-process#irs-levies)).
- **Lookback period (31 CFR 212):** the two months ending the day before a garnishment account review, whose federal benefit deposits set the protected amount ([Levies, Garnishments & Legal Process](/books/banking-and-lending/legal-process#protected-federal-benefits-31-cfr-212)).
- **Money market deposit account (MMDA):** a savings deposit with tiered rates and limited check writing; a deposit, unlike a money market fund ([Deposit Accounts & Truth in Savings](/books/banking-and-lending/deposit-accounts#the-four-shapes)).
- **Notice of Right to Garnish Federal Benefits:** the notice the United States or a state child support agency attaches to an order so the bank follows customary procedures instead of the Part 212 review ([Levies, Garnishments & Legal Process](/books/banking-and-lending/legal-process#protected-federal-benefits-31-cfr-212)).
- **NSF (nonsufficient funds) fee, returned item fee:** a fee for refusing to pay a check or ACH debit and returning it, dropped by many large banks since 2022 ([Overdraft, NSF & Fees](/books/banking-and-lending/overdraft-and-fees#how-the-platforms-do-it)).
- **Overdraft (discretionary):** a bank paying an item the balance doesn't cover, at its discretion, for a per-item fee that isn't a finance charge unless agreed in writing ([Overdraft, NSF & Fees](/books/banking-and-lending/overdraft-and-fees#the-product-lines)).
- **Overdraft opt-in:** the consumer's affirmative consent, after the Model Form A-9 notice, without which no fee may be charged for an ATM or one-time debit card overdraft ([Overdraft, NSF & Fees](/books/banking-and-lending/overdraft-and-fees#the-opt-in)).
- **Ownership category:** the legal capacity an account is held in (single, joint, retirement, trust, employee benefit, business, government), each insured separately ([Deposit Insurance](/books/banking-and-lending/deposit-insurance#the-ownership-categories)).
- **Part 370:** the FDIC rule requiring banks with 2 million or more deposit accounts to compute every account's insured amount within 24 hours of failure ([Deposit Insurance](/books/banking-and-lending/deposit-insurance#how-a-bank-computes-insured-amounts)).
- **Pass-through insurance:** FDIC coverage that reaches each owner of a pooled custodial account, if the records allow it ([Deposit Insurance](/books/banking-and-lending/deposit-insurance#pass-through-coverage)).
- **POD (payable on death) account:** an informal revocable trust account whose named beneficiaries take the balance at the owner's death, insured in the trust category ([Deposit Insurance](/books/banking-and-lending/deposit-insurance#the-2024-trust-rule)).
- **Presumed abandoned:** the state-law status of property with no qualifying owner activity for the statutory period, commonly three years for deposits, after which it is reported and delivered to the state ([Dormancy, Escheat & Closing Accounts](/books/banking-and-lending/dormancy-and-escheat#periods-and-what-counts-as-activity)).
- **Priority rules (escheat):** Texas v. New Jersey's order of claims: the state of the owner's last known address on the holder's books, otherwise the holder's state of incorporation ([Dormancy, Escheat & Closing Accounts](/books/banking-and-lending/dormancy-and-escheat#which-state-gets-it)).
- **Protected amount (31 CFR 212):** the lesser of federal benefit deposits in the lookback period and the balance at review, which a bank must leave available when garnishment arrives ([Levies, Garnishments & Legal Process](/books/banking-and-lending/legal-process#protected-federal-benefits-31-cfr-212)).
- **Provisional credit:** money an institution credits a consumer during a Regulation E investigation, reversible with notice if no error is found ([Errors & Disputes on Deposit Accounts](/books/banking-and-lending/deposit-errors-and-disputes#the-notice-and-the-clock)).
- **Reasonable period:** the extension Regulation CC presumes reasonable when an exception applies, up to one business day for on-us checks and five for other checks ([Funds Availability](/books/banking-and-lending/funds-availability#exception-holds)).
- **Reclamation (federal benefits):** a paying agency's recovery of benefit payments deposited after a beneficiary's death, under 31 CFR 210 ([Dormancy, Escheat & Closing Accounts](/books/banking-and-lending/dormancy-and-escheat#deceased-owners)).
- **Regulation CC:** US funds availability and check collection rules ([Funds Availability](/books/banking-and-lending/funds-availability)).
- **Regulation D:** the Federal Reserve rule defining transaction accounts, savings deposits and time deposits, without the six-transfer limit since April 2020 ([Deposit Accounts & Truth in Savings](/books/banking-and-lending/deposit-accounts#regulation-d-after-2020)).
- **Regulation DD (Truth in Savings):** the consumer deposit disclosure rule: account disclosures, change and maturity notices, statements and advertising; Part 707 for credit unions ([Deposit Accounts & Truth in Savings](/books/banking-and-lending/deposit-accounts#truth-in-savings)).
- **Re-presentment:** a payee presenting a returned check or ACH debit again, which can draw a fresh NSF fee unless the bank links it to the original ([Overdraft, NSF & Fees](/books/banking-and-lending/overdraft-and-fees#re-presentment)).
- **Right to Financial Privacy Act (RFPA):** the federal law limiting how federal agencies obtain an individual's bank records: authorization, subpoena or summons, warrant or formal request, with a certificate of compliance ([Levies, Garnishments & Legal Process](/books/banking-and-lending/legal-process#records-subpoenas-and-the-right-to-financial-privacy-act)).
- **Trust rule (2024):** the FDIC formula insuring trust accounts at $250,000 per eligible beneficiary per owner, up to five ($1,250,000), from April 1, 2024; credit unions from December 1, 2026 ([Deposit Insurance](/books/banking-and-lending/deposit-insurance#the-2024-trust-rule)).

## Financial Crime

- **314(a), 314(b):** the PATRIOT Act channels by which FinCEN relays law enforcement's requests for a bank to search its accounts and transactions for named subjects, and by which institutions that have filed a notice share information with each other under a safe harbor ([BSA/AML Programs](/books/banking-and-lending/bsa-aml#314-a-and-314-b)).
- **50 Percent Rule:** OFAC's rule that an entity owned 50% or more, directly or indirectly and in the aggregate, by blocked persons is itself blocked, listed or not ([Sanctions Screening](/books/banking-and-lending/sanctions-screening#the-50-percent-rule)).
- **Account takeover (ATO):** a criminal gaining control of a customer's account through stolen or socially engineered credentials and moving its money; an unauthorized transfer under Regulation E ([Account Takeover & Scams](/books/banking-and-lending/account-takeover-and-scams#account-takeover)).
- **AML program (five pillars):** a bank's written anti-money laundering program: internal controls, independent testing, a designated BSA officer, training and risk-based customer due diligence ([BSA/AML Programs](/books/banking-and-lending/bsa-aml#the-program)).
- **APP fraud:** authorized push payment fraud, where a payer is tricked into sending money themselves ([Account Takeover & Scams](/books/banking-and-lending/account-takeover-and-scams#unauthorized-or-authorized)).
- **Beneficial owner:** an individual who owns 25% or more of a business, or the one person who controls it, identified under FinCEN's customer due diligence rule ([KYC & Account Opening](/books/banking-and-lending/kyc-and-account-opening#customer-due-diligence-and-beneficial-owners)).
- **Blocked account:** a separate, interest-bearing account into which a bank moves property it has blocked under OFAC sanctions, until a license releases it ([Sanctions Screening](/books/banking-and-lending/sanctions-screening#blocking-rejecting-and-reporting)).
- **Checking account report:** a consumer report on a person's past deposit accounts, such as closures for cause, from ChexSystems or Early Warning, used at account opening and subject to FCRA adverse action duties ([KYC & Account Opening](/books/banking-and-lending/kyc-and-account-opening#deposit-screening-chexsystems-and-early-warning)).
- **CIP (Customer Identification Program):** the rule that a bank obtain at least a name, date of birth, address and identification number before opening an account, and verify them ([KYC & Account Opening](/books/banking-and-lending/kyc-and-account-opening#the-cip-rule)).
- **Continuing activity SAR:** a follow-up SAR on suspicious activity that continues after an initial filing; under FinCEN's guidance, after a 90-day review and by day 120 after the prior SAR, though a separate review isn't required ([BSA/AML Programs](/books/banking-and-lending/bsa-aml#suspicious-activity-reports)).
- **CTR (currency transaction report):** the report a bank files within 15 days for currency transactions of more than $10,000 in a business day by or for one person, aggregated across branches ([BSA/AML Programs](/books/banking-and-lending/bsa-aml#currency-transaction-reports-and-structuring)).
- **CTR exemption:** a bank's designation of a customer (another bank, a government agency, a listed company, an eligible business or payroll customer) whose currency transactions need no CTR, filed on FinCEN Form 110 and reviewed annually ([BSA/AML Programs](/books/banking-and-lending/bsa-aml#currency-transaction-reports-and-structuring)).
- **Customer due diligence (CDD):** understanding the nature and purpose of a customer relationship, identifying a business's beneficial owners, and monitoring and updating customer information on a risk basis ([KYC & Account Opening](/books/banking-and-lending/kyc-and-account-opening#customer-due-diligence-and-beneficial-owners)).
- **Customer risk profile:** the stored, versioned assessment of a customer's money laundering risk and expected activity that monitoring compares against ([BSA/AML Programs](/books/banking-and-lending/bsa-aml#the-customer-risk-profile)).
- **eCBSV:** the Social Security Administration's electronic Consent Based SSN Verification, confirming whether a name, SSN and date of birth match its records, with the consumer's consent ([KYC & Account Opening](/books/banking-and-lending/kyc-and-account-opening#verifying-identity-in-software)).
- **Elder financial exploitation (EFE):** the illegal or improper use of an older adult's funds, property or assets, reported on SARs with FinCEN's key term ([Account Takeover & Scams](/books/banking-and-lending/account-takeover-and-scams#elder-financial-exploitation)).
- **Initial detection:** the point at which a bank's review determines activity is suspicious, which starts the 30-day SAR clock; not the moment an alert fires ([BSA/AML Programs](/books/banking-and-lending/bsa-aml#suspicious-activity-reports)).
- **KYB (know your business):** verifying a business customer as an entity and the people who own, control and sign for it ([KYC & Account Opening](/books/banking-and-lending/kyc-and-account-opening#kyb-business-accounts)).
- **Legal entity customer:** a corporation, LLC, general partnership or similar entity opening an account, whose beneficial owners a bank must identify and verify, with sixteen excluded categories ([KYC & Account Opening](/books/banking-and-lending/kyc-and-account-opening#customer-due-diligence-and-beneficial-owners)).
- **Money mule:** an account, or the person behind it, that receives and passes on stolen or laundered funds, opened with a stolen or synthetic identity or by a recruited or paid owner ([Account Takeover & Scams](/books/banking-and-lending/account-takeover-and-scams#mules-and-the-receiving-side)).
- **OFAC, SDN list:** the Treasury office that runs US sanctions, and its list of blocked persons ([Sanctions Screening](/books/banking-and-lending/sanctions-screening#lists-and-list-management)).
- **Rejected transaction (sanctions):** a payment a bank refuses because a sanctions program prohibits it although no blocked person has an interest to block, reported to OFAC within 10 business days ([Sanctions Screening](/books/banking-and-lending/sanctions-screening#blocking-rejecting-and-reporting)).
- **SAR (suspicious activity report):** the confidential report a bank files within 30 days of determining that a transaction of $5,000 or more may involve crime, BSA evasion or no lawful purpose ([BSA/AML Programs](/books/banking-and-lending/bsa-aml#suspicious-activity-reports)).
- **Senior Safe Act:** the federal law giving trained bank employees and their institution immunity for good-faith reports of suspected exploitation of people 65 and older ([Account Takeover & Scams](/books/banking-and-lending/account-takeover-and-scams#elder-financial-exploitation)).
- **Structuring:** conducting currency transactions in any amount or pattern to evade CTR reporting, a crime whether or not the money is clean ([BSA/AML Programs](/books/banking-and-lending/bsa-aml#currency-transaction-reports-and-structuring)).
- **Synthetic identity fraud:** using a combination of real and invented personal information to fabricate a person or entity, typically to build credit and then default ([KYC & Account Opening](/books/banking-and-lending/kyc-and-account-opening#synthetic-identities)).
- **Travel rule:** the requirement that banks keep and pass along originator and beneficiary details on funds transfers of $3,000 or more ([BSA/AML Programs](/books/banking-and-lending/bsa-aml#funds-transfers-and-the-travel-rule)).
- **Trusted contact:** a person a customer names whom the bank may contact about suspected exploitation, without authority over the account ([Account Takeover & Scams](/books/banking-and-lending/account-takeover-and-scams#elder-financial-exploitation)).
- **Unauthorized electronic fund transfer:** under Regulation E, a transfer initiated by someone other than the consumer without actual authority and from which the consumer receives no benefit, including with credentials obtained by fraud ([Account Takeover & Scams](/books/banking-and-lending/account-takeover-and-scams#unauthorized-or-authorized)).
- **Weak AKA:** a broad or generic alias OFAC marks on its lists as likely to produce false hits, which it doesn't expect institutions to screen ([Sanctions Screening](/books/banking-and-lending/sanctions-screening#lists-and-list-management)).

## Fintech and BaaS

- **AISP, ASPSP:** an account information service provider, which reads a customer's payment accounts with consent under PSD2, and the account servicing payment service provider, the customer's bank, which must let it ([Open Banking in the UK & EU](/books/banking-and-lending/open-banking-uk-and-eu#account-information-under-psd2)).
- **Authorization disclosure:** under the 1033 rule as written, the signed notice naming the third party, bank, aggregator, product, data categories, duration and revocation method that a third party needs before collecting data ([Open Banking in the US](/books/banking-and-lending/open-banking#the-1033-rule)).
- **Bank-direct:** a BaaS model in which the bank exposes its own core and ledger through an API, with no middleware between it and the programs ([Banking as a Service](/books/banking-and-lending/banking-as-a-service#three-shapes)).
- **Compliance task matrix:** the record, per program, of which party performs each obligation the bank owns, under which procedure, with what evidence ([Banking as a Service](/books/banking-and-lending/banking-as-a-service#who-owns-which-obligation)).
- **Covered EWA:** earned wage access within accrued wages from payroll data, repaid by payroll deduction, without recourse, collection or credit reporting, and without credit underwriting; not credit under the CFPB's December 2025 advisory opinion ([Earned Wage Access](/books/banking-and-lending/earned-wage-access#the-federal-arc)).
- **Credit builder loan:** an installment loan whose proceeds are held in a locked CD or savings account until it is repaid, reported to the bureaus monthly ([Credit Builders & Secured Products](/books/banking-and-lending/credit-builders#credit-builder-loans)).
- **Credit enhancement:** a program partner's contractual promise to indemnify or reimburse the sponsor bank's credit and fraud losses on loans it originates ([Banking as a Service](/books/banking-and-lending/banking-as-a-service#reserves-and-credit-enhancement)).
- **Critical activity:** under the 2023 interagency guidance, a third-party activity whose failure could cause significant risk, customer impact or harm to the bank's condition or operations ([Sponsor Bank Oversight](/books/banking-and-lending/sponsor-bank-oversight#the-2023-interagency-guidance)).
- **Custodial deposit account with transactional features:** the FDIC proposal's term for a pooled account held for many beneficial owners who can direct transfers from it ([Program Ledgers & the Synapse Lesson](/books/banking-and-lending/program-ledgers#what-the-custodial-proposal-would-require)).
- **Data aggregator:** a company that connects to many institutions and delivers account data to the third parties that retain it ([Open Banking in the US](/books/banking-and-lending/open-banking#how-the-platforms-do-it)).
- **Data provider (1033):** a Regulation E financial institution, Regulation Z card issuer or other holder of covered account data that must share it at the consumer's request under the 1033 rule as written ([Open Banking in the US](/books/banking-and-lending/open-banking#the-1033-rule)).
- **Developer interface:** a data provider's API for authorized third parties, with a 99.5% monthly floor of proper responses under the 1033 rule as written ([Open Banking in the US](/books/banking-and-lending/open-banking#worked-numbers-the-99-5-floor)).
- **Direct-to-consumer (D2C) EWA:** earned wage access contracted with the worker alone, typically estimating wages from bank data and recovering by debiting the worker's account ([Earned Wage Access](/books/banking-and-lending/earned-wage-access#two-models)).
- **Earned wage access (EWA):** access to wages already earned before payday, recovered from the next paycheck ([Earned Wage Access](/books/banking-and-lending/earned-wage-access)).
- **Employer-integrated EWA:** earned wage access through the employer's payroll, computing accrued wages from payroll data and recovering through payroll; the CFPB's "employer-partnered" ([Earned Wage Access](/books/banking-and-lending/earned-wage-access#two-models)).
- **Expedite fee:** a fee for instant delivery of EWA funds when a free, slower option exists; not a finance charge in the normal course under the 2025 advisory opinion ([Earned Wage Access](/books/banking-and-lending/earned-wage-access#the-federal-arc)).
- **FBO account (for-benefit-of):** a bank account in the provider's name holding money for its merchants ([Treasury & Bank Operations](/books/payments/treasury#fbo-accounts-and-pass-through-insurance)).
- **FDX (Financial Data Exchange):** the industry body and API standard for US consumer-permissioned data sharing, recognized by the CFPB as a standard setter ([Open Banking in the US](/books/banking-and-lending/open-banking#from-screen-scraping-to-apis)).
- **FIDA:** the EU's proposed Financial Data Access regulation, extending data sharing beyond payment accounts; not adopted as of October 2026 ([Open Banking in the UK & EU](/books/banking-and-lending/open-banking-uk-and-eu#psd3-the-psr-and-fida)).
- **Graduation (secured card):** moving a secured card to an unsecured line after a review, with the deposit returned ([Credit Builders & Secured Products](/books/banking-and-lending/credit-builders#secured-cards)).
- **In-transit item:** an amount posted on one side of a program reconciliation that will clear on the other on a known date, such as an ACH credit awaiting the next sweep ([Program Ledgers & the Synapse Lesson](/books/banking-and-lending/program-ledgers#daily-reconciliation)).
- **Lookback:** a review an order requires of past activity, such as every customer onboarded or every dispute handled since a date ([Sponsor Bank Oversight](/books/banking-and-lending/sponsor-bank-oversight#what-an-order-does-to-a-program)).
- **Omnibus account:** one account at a bank holding many owners' money in aggregate, with a subledger saying whose each dollar is ([Program Ledgers & the Synapse Lesson](/books/banking-and-lending/program-ledgers#the-accounts-on-the-bank-s-core)).
- **Open Banking Standard:** the UK's common open banking API and customer experience specification, maintained by Open Banking Limited under the CMA Order ([Open Banking in the UK & EU](/books/banking-and-lending/open-banking-uk-and-eu#the-uk-standard)).
- **Pass-through insurance:** FDIC coverage that reaches each owner of a pooled custodial account, if the records allow it ([Treasury & Bank Operations](/books/payments/treasury#fbo-accounts-and-pass-through-insurance)).
- **Payroll process deduction:** EWA recovery through instructions the payroll processor acts on, rather than a debit from the worker's account after payday ([Earned Wage Access](/books/banking-and-lending/earned-wage-access#the-federal-arc)).
- **Per-owner balance file:** the daily file of each beneficial owner's balance in an omnibus account, reconciled to the account's balance at the bank ([Program Ledgers & the Synapse Lesson](/books/banking-and-lending/program-ledgers#how-to-build-it)).
- **Program reserve:** cash a program posts at its sponsor bank against fraud, negative balances and losses, at a level the bank sets ([Banking as a Service](/books/banking-and-lending/banking-as-a-service#reserves-and-credit-enhancement)).
- **Reconfirmation (UK AIS):** the AISP's duty to reconfirm a customer's explicit consent at least every 90 days, in place of reauthentication at the bank ([Open Banking in the UK & EU](/books/banking-and-lending/open-banking-uk-and-eu#reauthentication-90-days-then-two-answers)).
- **Regulation II (Durbin):** the Federal Reserve's cap on debit interchange for issuers with $10 billion or more in assets, tested at each year end ([Neobanks & Consumer Fintech](/books/banking-and-lending/neobanks#the-durbin-line)).
- **Screen scraping:** accessing accounts by logging in with the customer's banking credentials and reading the pages, which API access replaces ([Open Banking in the US](/books/banking-and-lending/open-banking#from-screen-scraping-to-apis)).
- **Section 1033:** the Dodd-Frank provision giving consumers a right to their account data, implemented by a 2024 CFPB rule enjoined since October 29, 2025 ([Open Banking in the US](/books/banking-and-lending/open-banking#the-injunction-and-the-rewrite)).
- **Secured card:** a credit card whose line is backed by a deposit the cardholder pledges ([Credit Builders & Secured Products](/books/banking-and-lending/credit-builders#secured-cards)).
- **Share-secured loan:** a credit union loan against shares the member already holds, pledged until repaid ([Credit Builders & Secured Products](/books/banking-and-lending/credit-builders#credit-builder-loans)).
- **Sponsor migration:** moving a program's accounts from one bank to another, with new account numbers, cards and agreements ([Banking as a Service](/books/banking-and-lending/banking-as-a-service#moving-between-sponsor-banks)).
- **Third-party risk management (TPRM):** a bank's oversight of its vendors and partners across planning, due diligence, contracting, monitoring and termination ([Sponsor Bank Oversight](/books/banking-and-lending/sponsor-bank-oversight#the-guidance)).
- **Tokenized account number (TAN):** an account number issued per app that works for ACH and RTP only while consent lasts, returning R04 after revocation ([Open Banking in the US](/books/banking-and-lending/open-banking#tokenized-account-numbers)).

## Lending Products

- **Ability to repay (ATR):** the duty to determine in good faith, from eight verified factors, that a borrower can repay a closed-end dwelling-secured loan ([Mortgages & Home Equity](/books/banking-and-lending/mortgages#ability-to-repay-and-qualified-mortgages)).
- **Amortization (level payment):** repaying a loan in equal periodic payments whose interest share falls and principal share rises over the term ([The Banking & Lending Business](/books/banking-and-lending/banking-business#the-life-of-a-loan)).
- **Amortized cost:** measuring a loan at its principal adjusted for deferred fees and costs, with a CECL allowance for expected losses booked at origination ([The Lending Business](/books/banking-and-lending/lending-business#amortized-cost-or-fair-value)).
- **Average daily balance:** the sum of a card balance at the end of each day in the cycle divided by the days in the cycle, to which the daily periodic rate applies ([Credit Cards as Credit](/books/banking-and-lending/credit-cards#interest-calculation-methods)).
- **Average prime offer rate (APOR):** the weekly benchmark rate a mortgage's APR is compared against to decide whether it's higher-priced or within the General QM price limit ([Mortgages & Home Equity](/books/banking-and-lending/mortgages#ability-to-repay-and-qualified-mortgages)).
- **Balance segment:** a part of a card balance with its own rate and rules: purchases, cash advances, balance transfers, promotional, deferred interest, penalty ([Credit Cards as Credit](/books/banking-and-lending/credit-cards#balance-segments-and-aprs)).
- **BNPL (buy now, pay later):** a lender paying the merchant up front and collecting from the customer in installments ([Buy Now, Pay Later](/books/banking-and-lending/buy-now-pay-later#the-products)).
- **Buy rate (indirect auto):** the risk-based rate at which a lender will buy a dealer's retail installment contract; not the Payments volume's partner buy rate ([Auto Lending](/books/banking-and-lending/auto-lending#direct-and-indirect-lending)).
- **Captive finance company:** a manufacturer's own lender, which carries the parent's subsidized rates, leases and dealer floorplan ([Auto Lending](/books/banking-and-lending/auto-lending#captive-finance)).
- **Charge card:** a card whose balance is due in full each cycle, so it carries no revolving balance or interest ([Credit Cards as Credit](/books/banking-and-lending/credit-cards#how-the-platforms-do-it)).
- **Conforming loan limit:** the largest loan Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac may buy, set yearly by FHFA by county; $832,750 for most one-unit homes in 2026 ([Mortgages & Home Equity](/books/banking-and-lending/mortgages#loan-programs)).
- **Correspondent lending:** a smaller lender closing and funding a mortgage in its own name and selling it to a larger lender that aggregates loans for the agencies ([Mortgages & Home Equity](/books/banking-and-lending/mortgages#origination-channels-retail-broker-correspondent)).
- **Cost of funds:** what the money a lender lends costs it: deposit rates for a bank, facility and securitization spreads for a nonbank ([The Lending Business](/books/banking-and-lending/lending-business#cost-of-funds)).
- **Daily simple interest:** interest accrued each day on the outstanding principal, so early payments save interest and late ones cost it ([Personal & Installment Loans](/books/banking-and-lending/installment-loans#how-interest-accrues)).
- **Dealer reserve (participation):** the lender's payment to a dealer for a contract, usually a share of the interest from marking the contract rate above the buy rate ([Auto Lending](/books/banking-and-lending/auto-lending#dealer-markup-and-participation)).
- **Deferred interest:** a promotion under which interest accrues but is waived if the balance is paid in full by the end of the period, and charged if not; not a grace period ([Credit Cards as Credit](/books/banking-and-lending/credit-cards#balance-segments-and-aprs)).
- **Deferred Payment Credit (DPC):** the UK's regulated form of interest-free BNPL, repayable in 12 or fewer instalments within 12 months, regulated from July 15, 2026 ([Buy Now, Pay Later](/books/banking-and-lending/buy-now-pay-later#the-uk-deferred-payment-credit)).
- **Direct Loan:** a federal student loan made by the Department of Education: Subsidized, Unsubsidized, PLUS or Consolidation ([Student Loans](/books/banking-and-lending/student-loans#federal-loans)).
- **Double-cycle billing:** charging interest on the previous cycle's balance after a grace period is lost; barred by Regulation Z ([Credit Cards as Credit](/books/banking-and-lending/credit-cards#grace-periods)).
- **Draw period:** the years in which a HELOC borrower may borrow against the line, often paying interest only, before the repayment period begins ([Mortgages & Home Equity](/books/banking-and-lending/mortgages#home-equity-helocs-and-home-equity-loans)).
- **Fair value (loans):** measuring a loan each period at what it would sell for, with expected losses in the price and changes in earnings ([The Lending Business](/books/banking-and-lending/lending-business#amortized-cost-or-fair-value)).
- **FHA loan:** a mortgage insured by the Federal Housing Administration, with a 3.5% minimum down payment and upfront and annual mortgage insurance premiums ([Mortgages & Home Equity](/books/banking-and-lending/mortgages#loan-programs)).
- **GAP (guaranteed asset protection):** insurance or a debt cancellation waiver that covers what's owed beyond a totaled or stolen vehicle's value, refundable when it stops providing a benefit ([Auto Lending](/books/banking-and-lending/auto-lending#gap-and-add-on-products)).
- **Grace period:** the time after a card statement during which new purchases accrue no interest if the statement balance is paid in full ([Credit Cards as Credit](/books/banking-and-lending/credit-cards#grace-periods)).
- **HELOC (home equity line of credit):** an open-end line secured by the borrower's home, with a draw period and a repayment period ([Mortgages & Home Equity](/books/banking-and-lending/mortgages#home-equity-helocs-and-home-equity-loans)).
- **Higher-priced mortgage loan:** a first-lien mortgage whose APR is 1.5 points or more over APOR (more for jumbo and subordinate liens), which loses the QM safe harbor and needs an escrow account ([Mortgages & Home Equity](/books/banking-and-lending/mortgages#ability-to-repay-and-qualified-mortgages)).
- **Holder Rule:** the FTC rule requiring consumer credit contracts from a sale to carry a notice that any holder takes them subject to the buyer's claims against the seller ([Auto Lending](/books/banking-and-lending/auto-lending#direct-and-indirect-lending)).
- **Income-Based Repayment (IBR):** a federal student loan plan with payments of 10% or 15% of income above 150% of the poverty line, capped at the standard payment ([Student Loans](/books/banking-and-lending/student-loans#the-2025-26-changes)).
- **Indirect lending:** lending through a dealer, which submits the application, writes the contract as original creditor and assigns it to the lender ([Auto Lending](/books/banking-and-lending/auto-lending#direct-and-indirect-lending)).
- **Interest rate lock commitment (IRLC):** a lender's commitment to a mortgage rate and price for a period, carried as a derivative and hedged by pull-through ([Mortgages & Home Equity](/books/banking-and-lending/mortgages#rate-locks)).
- **Jumbo loan:** a mortgage above the conforming loan limit, held by the lender or sold to private investors ([Mortgages & Home Equity](/books/banking-and-lending/mortgages#loan-programs)).
- **Minimum payment warning:** the card statement box showing how long and how much it takes to repay paying only the minimum, and the 36-month alternative ([Credit Cards as Credit](/books/banking-and-lending/credit-cards#minimum-payments)).
- **Money factor:** a lessor's rent charge per dollar of capitalized cost plus residual, per month; a lease rate that may not be called an APR ([Auto Lending](/books/banking-and-lending/auto-lending#leases)).
- **Origination fee:** a fee charged at funding, usually deducted from the proceeds, which is a finance charge and raises the APR above the interest rate ([Personal & Installment Loans](/books/banking-and-lending/installment-loans#origination-fees-and-the-apr)).
- **Pay-in-4:** an interest-free BNPL loan in up to four payments, the first at checkout and the rest every two weeks ([Buy Now, Pay Later](/books/banking-and-lending/buy-now-pay-later#the-products)).
- **Payday alternative loan (PAL):** a federal credit union's small-dollar loan under NCUA rules: PAL I up to $1,000 over 6 months, PAL II up to $2,000 over 12 ([Personal & Installment Loans](/books/banking-and-lending/installment-loans#small-dollar-and-payday-loans)).
- **Penalty APR:** a higher rate a card issuer may apply to an existing balance only after a payment is more than 60 days late, reversed after six on-time payments ([Credit Cards as Credit](/books/banking-and-lending/credit-cards#the-card-act-rules)).
- **Private mortgage insurance (PMI):** insurance protecting the lender on a conventional loan with less than 20% down, cancellable at 80% and ending at 78% of original value on the schedule ([Mortgages & Home Equity](/books/banking-and-lending/mortgages#loan-programs)).
- **Public Service Loan Forgiveness (PSLF):** cancellation of the remaining Direct Loan balance after 120 qualifying payments while working for a qualifying public service employer ([Student Loans](/books/banking-and-lending/student-loans#pslf-and-other-forgiveness)).
- **Pull-through:** the share of mortgage rate locks expected to close, used to weight the pipeline's rate exposure for hedging ([Mortgages & Home Equity](/books/banking-and-lending/mortgages#rate-locks)).
- **Qualified mortgage (QM):** a mortgage meeting the product, points and fees and price limits of Regulation Z, presumed to satisfy ability to repay ([Mortgages & Home Equity](/books/banking-and-lending/mortgages#ability-to-repay-and-qualified-mortgages)).
- **Repayment Assistance Plan (RAP):** the federal income-driven plan from July 1, 2026: 1% to 10% of AGI, $50 off per dependent, interest waiver, principal match, forgiveness after 360 payments ([Student Loans](/books/banking-and-lending/student-loans#the-2025-26-changes)).
- **Residual value:** a leased vehicle's estimated value at the end of the term, which the lessor carries the risk of under a closed-end lease ([Auto Lending](/books/banking-and-lending/auto-lending#leases)).
- **Retail installment contract (RIC):** a credit sale contract a dealer writes with the buyer as original creditor and then assigns to a lender ([Auto Lending](/books/banking-and-lending/auto-lending#direct-and-indirect-lending)).
- **Right of rescission:** a borrower's right to cancel a home-secured loan, other than a purchase loan, until midnight of the third business day after closing, notice and disclosures ([Mortgages & Home Equity](/books/banking-and-lending/mortgages#home-equity-helocs-and-home-equity-loans)).
- **Rule of 78s:** a rebate method that front-loads earned interest on a precomputed loan; barred for consumer terms over 61 months ([Personal & Installment Loans](/books/banking-and-lending/installment-loans#how-interest-accrues)).
- **Self-certification form:** the Department of Education form a private student loan borrower signs before consummation, showing cost of attendance and aid received ([Student Loans](/books/banking-and-lending/student-loans#private-loans-and-their-disclosures)).
- **Student loan refinancing:** a private loan that pays off federal or private student loans, giving up federal plans, forgiveness and discharge rights on federal ones ([Student Loans](/books/banking-and-lending/student-loans#refinancing)).
- **Subvention:** a manufacturer's payment to its captive to offer below-market loan rates or lease terms ([Auto Lending](/books/banking-and-lending/auto-lending#captive-finance)).
- **Tiered Standard plan:** the federal fixed-payment plan for loans made from July 1, 2026, over 10 to 25 years by total balance ([Student Loans](/books/banking-and-lending/student-loans#the-2025-26-changes)).
- **VA funding fee:** the one-time fee on a VA-guaranteed mortgage instead of mortgage insurance, by down payment and use, waived for disabled veterans ([Mortgages & Home Equity](/books/banking-and-lending/mortgages#loan-programs)).
- **Vintage:** the month or quarter a loan was originated, the cohort its losses are read by ([The Lending Business](/books/banking-and-lending/lending-business#credit-losses)).
- **Wholesale (broker) lending:** a lender underwriting and funding mortgages that independent brokers originate ([Mortgages & Home Equity](/books/banking-and-lending/mortgages#origination-channels-retail-broker-correspondent)).
- **Yield (loan):** what a loan portfolio earns as a rate on its average balance, interest plus fees that are part of the cost of credit ([The Lending Business](/books/banking-and-lending/lending-business#yield)).

## Business Lending

- **Account debtor:** the business's customer who owes a receivable; once notified of an assignment it can discharge the debt only by paying the assignee ([Small Business Lending](/books/banking-and-lending/small-business-lending#invoice-factoring-and-financing)).
- **Administrative agent:** the bank that runs a syndicated loan for the lenders: receives payments, distributes shares, computes interest and fees, sends notices and records assignments ([Commercial Lending](/books/banking-and-lending/commercial-lending#participations-syndication-and-the-lending-limit)).
- **Asset-based lending (ABL):** a revolving line whose availability is set each period by eligible receivables and inventory times advance rates, monitored by certificates, field exams and cash dominion ([Commercial Lending](/books/banking-and-lending/commercial-lending#borrowing-bases-and-asset-based-lending)).
- **Blanket lien:** a security interest in all of a business's personal property, perfected by one UCC-1 describing all assets ([Small Business Lending](/books/banking-and-lending/small-business-lending#ucc-filings)).
- **Borrowing base:** eligible collateral times its advance rates, less reserves: what an asset-based borrower may draw this period ([Commercial Lending](/books/banking-and-lending/commercial-lending#borrowing-bases-and-asset-based-lending)).
- **Classified asset:** a loan rated substandard, doubtful or loss under the common regulatory definitions ([Commercial Lending](/books/banking-and-lending/commercial-lending#risk-ratings-and-credit-review)).
- **Confession of judgment:** a signed admission letting a creditor enter judgment without a lawsuit; restricted in New York since 2019 and void under Virginia's and Texas's sales-based financing laws ([Small Business Lending](/books/banking-and-lending/small-business-lending#merchant-cash-advances)).
- **Continuation statement:** a filing that extends a UCC-1 for another five years, allowed only in the six months before it lapses ([Small Business Lending](/books/banking-and-lending/small-business-lending#ucc-filings)).
- **Covenant:** a promise in a credit agreement tested on a schedule for the life of the loan: financial, reporting, negative or affirmative ([Commercial Lending](/books/banking-and-lending/commercial-lending#covenants)).
- **Cross-aging:** making all of a customer's receivables ineligible for a borrowing base when any, or a set share, of them are past due ([Commercial Lending](/books/banking-and-lending/commercial-lending#borrowing-bases-and-asset-based-lending)).
- **Debt service coverage ratio (DSCR):** net operating income divided by annual debt service; the core test for income-producing real estate ([Commercial Lending](/books/banking-and-lending/commercial-lending#underwriting-from-the-spread)).
- **Debt yield:** net operating income divided by the loan amount, a leverage measure independent of rate and amortization ([Commercial Lending](/books/banking-and-lending/commercial-lending#underwriting-from-the-spread)).
- **Dilution:** credits that reduce receivables (returns, allowances, disputes, bad debts), usually expected at 5% or less in asset-based lending ([Commercial Lending](/books/banking-and-lending/commercial-lending#borrowing-bases-and-asset-based-lending)).
- **Equipment finance agreement (EFA):** a loan under which the borrower owns the equipment from the start and grants a security interest in it ([Small Business Lending](/books/banking-and-lending/small-business-lending#equipment-finance)).
- **Estimated APR (commercial financing):** an APR on a sales-based financing computed from projected sales, required by New York (historical or opt-in method), California and, from 2027, Vermont ([Embedded Lending](/books/banking-and-lending/embedded-lending#state-commercial-financing-disclosure-laws)).
- **Factor rate:** an MCA's price as a multiple of the amount advanced (1.35 means $1.35 delivered per $1), which says nothing about cost until there's a timeline ([Small Business Lending](/books/banking-and-lending/small-business-lending#merchant-cash-advances)).
- **Factoring:** buying a business's invoices at a discount, with an advance, a reserve and a fee; with or without recourse, with or without notice to the customer ([Small Business Lending](/books/banking-and-lending/small-business-lending#invoice-factoring-and-financing)).
- **Financing statement (UCC-1):** the public filing that perfects a security interest in personal property, made in the debtor's state of organization and effective for five years ([Small Business Lending](/books/banking-and-lending/small-business-lending#ucc-filings)).
- **Legal lending limit:** a national bank's cap on loans to one borrower: 15% of unimpaired capital and surplus, plus 10% fully secured by readily marketable collateral ([Commercial Lending](/books/banking-and-lending/commercial-lending#participations-syndication-and-the-lending-limit)).
- **Merchant cash advance (MCA):** a purchase of a business's future receivables, repaid as a share of its sales, which the provider treats as not a loan ([Small Business Lending](/books/banking-and-lending/small-business-lending#merchant-cash-advances)).
- **Participation:** an undivided share of a loan the lead bank made and keeps servicing, sold to other banks ([Commercial Lending](/books/banking-and-lending/commercial-lending#participations-syndication-and-the-lending-limit)).
- **Personal guarantee:** an owner's promise to pay a business's debt: unlimited, limited, or only for the validity of what the business represented ([Small Business Lending](/books/banking-and-lending/small-business-lending#personal-guarantees-and-who-else-is-liable)).
- **Purchase-money security interest (PMSI):** a lien securing the price of the collateral itself, which beats an earlier blanket lien on equipment if perfected within 20 days of possession ([Small Business Lending](/books/banking-and-lending/small-business-lending#equipment-finance)).
- **Reconciliation (MCA):** adjusting an MCA's estimated daily debit to the specified percentage of actual receipts; a reconciliation that never works is a sign of a disguised loan ([Small Business Lending](/books/banking-and-lending/small-business-lending#merchant-cash-advances)).
- **Repayment rate:** the percentage of each transaction a payment platform withholds from payouts toward a loan or advance ([Embedded Lending](/books/banking-and-lending/embedded-lending#withholding-from-payouts)).
- **Revenue-based financing (RBF):** an advance repaid as a fixed share of monthly revenue until a capped multiple is paid ([Small Business Lending](/books/banking-and-lending/small-business-lending#revenue-based-financing)).
- **Risk rating:** a bank's grade of a loan's credit risk on its own scale, with special mention, substandard, doubtful and loss as the common regulatory grades ([Commercial Lending](/books/banking-and-lending/commercial-lending#risk-ratings-and-credit-review)).
- **SBA 504:** SBA's fixed-asset program: a bank's first-lien loan, a Certified Development Company's second-lien loan funded by a guaranteed debenture, and at least 10% from the borrower ([Small Business Lending](/books/banking-and-lending/small-business-lending#sba-7-a-and-504)).
- **SBA 7(a):** SBA's main guarantee program for loans up to $5 million made by banks and other lenders, guaranteed 50% to 90% by program ([Small Business Lending](/books/banking-and-lending/small-business-lending#sba-7-a-and-504)).
- **Shared National Credit (SNC):** a commitment of $100 million or more shared by multiple regulated institutions, reviewed jointly by the Federal Reserve, FDIC and OCC ([Commercial Lending](/books/banking-and-lending/commercial-lending#participations-syndication-and-the-lending-limit)).
- **Special mention:** a loan with potential weaknesses that deserve management's close attention, below pass but not classified ([Commercial Lending](/books/banking-and-lending/commercial-lending#risk-ratings-and-credit-review)).
- **Spreading:** entering a borrower's statements and tax returns into a standard template so ratios and covenants can be computed and compared ([Commercial Lending](/books/banking-and-lending/commercial-lending#underwriting-from-the-spread)).
- **Springing covenant:** a covenant tested only when a condition is met, such as excess availability falling below a threshold ([Commercial Lending](/books/banking-and-lending/commercial-lending#covenants)).
- **Stacking:** a business taking several merchant cash advances on the same receivables, visible in its UCC filings ([Small Business Lending](/books/banking-and-lending/small-business-lending#ucc-filings)).
- **Substandard:** a loan inadequately protected by the obligor's paying capacity or the collateral, with a well-defined weakness ([Commercial Lending](/books/banking-and-lending/commercial-lending#risk-ratings-and-credit-review)).
- **Syndicated loan:** a credit with several lenders in one agreement, each a lender of record for its share, run by an administrative agent ([Commercial Lending](/books/banking-and-lending/commercial-lending#participations-syndication-and-the-lending-limit)).
- **True lease:** a lease where the lessor keeps the residual and the lessee can't end up owning for nominal consideration; otherwise the lease is a security interest under UCC 1-203 ([Small Business Lending](/books/banking-and-lending/small-business-lending#equipment-finance)).
- **Withholding (repayment):** a platform keeping part of a merchant's payout and applying it to the merchant's loan or advance, posted as a loan payment ([Embedded Lending](/books/banking-and-lending/embedded-lending#withholding-from-payouts)).

## Origination and Underwriting

- **Ability to repay (ATR):** the Regulation Z requirement to determine, on eight factors verified from third-party records, that a mortgage borrower can repay before consummation ([Underwriting & Decision Engines](/books/banking-and-lending/decision-engines#ability-to-repay-and-the-qualified-mortgage)).
- **Adverse action:** a decline, or a counteroffer the applicant doesn't accept, which requires a notice with the principal reasons; also an unfavorable change to an existing account for credit reasons ([Adverse Action & Explaining Decisions](/books/banking-and-lending/adverse-action#when-a-notice-is-owed)).
- **Amount financed:** the credit the borrower actually gets the use of: principal less prepaid finance charges, the base the APR is measured against ([APR, Finance Charges & Rate Caps](/books/banking-and-lending/apr-and-rate-caps#the-finance-charge)).
- **Annual percentage rate (APR):** the finance charge as a yearly rate on the amount financed, computed by Appendix J's actuarial method and accurate within 1/8 or 1/4 of a point ([APR, Finance Charges & Rate Caps](/books/banking-and-lending/apr-and-rate-caps#the-actuarial-method)).
- **Application (Regulation B):** an oral or written request for credit under the creditor's procedures; a prequalification becomes one if the creditor evaluates the consumer's data and declines ([Loan Origination](/books/banking-and-lending/loan-origination#what-counts-as-an-application)).
- **Application (TRID):** for most mortgages, receipt of six items: name, income, SSN, property address, estimated value and loan amount, which starts the Loan Estimate clock ([Loan Origination](/books/banking-and-lending/loan-origination#what-counts-as-an-application)).
- **Automated underwriting system (AUS):** an agency's decision service called from the LOS, such as Fannie Mae's Desktop Underwriter or Freddie Mac's Loan Product Advisor, returning a recommendation and conditions ([Loan Origination](/books/banking-and-lending/loan-origination#how-the-platforms-do-it)).
- **Average prime offer rate (APOR):** the benchmark rate for a comparable transaction, as of the date the rate is set, that higher-priced, QM and HMDA rate spread tests compare a mortgage's APR against ([APR, Finance Charges & Rate Caps](/books/banking-and-lending/apr-and-rate-caps#tests-that-read-the-apr)).
- **Cash-flow underwriting:** deciding credit from a borrower's bank account transactions (income, bills, balances, overdrafts) instead of or beside the bureau file ([Credit Data & Scores](/books/banking-and-lending/credit-data-and-scores#cash-flow-and-alternative-data)).
- **Champion-challenger:** deciding a random share of live applications with a new strategy to compare it with the current one; its declines are real adverse actions ([Underwriting & Decision Engines](/books/banking-and-lending/decision-engines#champion-challenger-and-shadow-testing)).
- **Changed circumstance (TRID):** one of six listed reasons that lets a lender revise the Loan Estimate's tolerance baseline, if the revision goes out within three business days ([TRID & Mortgage Disclosures](/books/banking-and-lending/mortgage-disclosures#changed-circumstances-and-revised-estimates)).
- **Closing Disclosure:** the TRID form the borrower must receive three business days before consummation, restarting that wait for an inaccurate APR, a product change or an added prepayment penalty ([TRID & Mortgage Disclosures](/books/banking-and-lending/mortgage-disclosures#the-closing-disclosure)).
- **Completed application:** an application for which the creditor has all the information it normally uses to decide; Reg B's 30-day notice clock runs from it ([Loan Origination](/books/banking-and-lending/loan-origination#what-counts-as-an-application)).
- **Condition (underwriting):** something that must be provided or true before a milestone (approval, documents, closing, funding), with an owner and a signed clearance ([Loan Origination](/books/banking-and-lending/loan-origination#conditions)).
- **Control (transferable record):** the electronic substitute for possession of a note: a system that reliably establishes who the authoritative copy was issued or transferred to, making that person the holder ([Closing, eNotes & MERS](/books/banking-and-lending/closing-and-enotes#control-e-sign-and-ueta)).
- **Counteroffer:** approving different terms than requested; adverse action under Reg B if the applicant doesn't accept, with a notice due within 90 days ([Underwriting & Decision Engines](/books/banking-and-lending/decision-engines#counteroffers)).
- **Credit score disclosure exception:** giving every applicant their score and its context in place of risk-based pricing notices (12 CFR 1022.74(d) and (e)) ([Adverse Action & Explaining Decisions](/books/banking-and-lending/adverse-action#what-the-fcra-adds)).
- **Cure (TRID):** a refund of charges above a tolerance limit, made within 60 days after consummation with a corrected Closing Disclosure ([TRID & Mortgage Disclosures](/books/banking-and-lending/mortgage-disclosures#cures)).
- **Debt-to-income ratio (DTI):** total monthly debt payments including the proposed one, divided by gross monthly income; Fannie Mae allows up to 50% through DU ([Underwriting & Decision Engines](/books/banking-and-lending/decision-engines#debt-to-income-and-residual-income)).
- **DIDMCA opt-out:** a state's election under section 525 that state-chartered banks' exported rates don't apply to loans made in it; Colorado, Iowa and Oregon have one ([APR, Finance Charges & Rate Caps](/books/banking-and-lending/apr-and-rate-caps#opt-outs-and-the-colorado-case)).
- **Disparate impact (lending):** liability for a neutral criterion's discriminatory effect; removed from Regulation B in 2026 but still argued under the Fair Housing Act and state laws ([Fair Lending, HMDA & 1071](/books/banking-and-lending/fair-lending#disparate-impact-and-the-2026-reg-b-rule)).
- **Empirically derived scoring system:** Reg B's term for a credit scoring system built and periodically revalidated on applicant data with accepted statistical methods; anything else is judgmental ([Underwriting & Decision Engines](/books/banking-and-lending/decision-engines#scoring-and-judgmental-systems-under-reg-b)).
- **eNote:** a promissory note created, signed and stored electronically as a MISMO SMART Doc, sealed with a hash and registered on the MERS eRegistry ([Closing, eNotes & MERS](/books/banking-and-lending/closing-and-enotes#enotes-and-smart-docs)).
- **eRegistry (MERS):** the system of record for who is Controller and Location of each eNote's authoritative copy; a sale is a transfer of control on it ([Closing, eNotes & MERS](/books/banking-and-lending/closing-and-enotes#the-mers-system-and-the-eregistry)).
- **Finance charge:** the dollar cost of consumer credit: charges the consumer pays that the creditor imposes incident to the credit, with listed exclusions ([APR, Finance Charges & Rate Caps](/books/banking-and-lending/apr-and-rate-caps#the-finance-charge)).
- **Firewall (Section 1071):** the statutory limit on underwriters' access to an applicant's women-owned, minority-owned and principal-owner demographic answers ([Fair Lending, HMDA & 1071](/books/banking-and-lending/fair-lending#demographic-data-and-the-firewall)).
- **Firm offer of credit:** the offer a prescreened solicitation must make; the only basis for using a consumer report on someone who didn't apply ([Credit Data & Scores](/books/banking-and-lending/credit-data-and-scores#permissible-purpose)).
- **General QM:** the qualified mortgage defined by product limits, points and fees caps, verified DTI or residual income and an APR within a threshold over APOR ([Underwriting & Decision Engines](/books/banking-and-lending/decision-engines#ability-to-repay-and-the-qualified-mortgage)).
- **Hard and soft inquiry:** a pull for a credit application that other lenders see and scores count, versus one for prequalification, account review or prescreening that scores don't count ([Credit Data & Scores](/books/banking-and-lending/credit-data-and-scores#permissible-purpose)).
- **HMDA LAR:** the loan/application register: one row per covered mortgage application with up to 38 data points, recorded quarterly and filed by March 1 ([Fair Lending, HMDA & 1071](/books/banking-and-lending/fair-lending#hmda-who-reports-and-what)).
- **Judgmental system:** under Reg B, any way of evaluating applicants other than an empirically derived scoring system, including an underwriter's review ([Underwriting & Decision Engines](/books/banking-and-lending/decision-engines#scoring-and-judgmental-systems-under-reg-b)).
- **Key factors:** up to four factors (five with inquiries) that most hurt a credit score, disclosed with the score under the FCRA; not the same as ECOA reasons ([Adverse Action & Explaining Decisions](/books/banking-and-lending/adverse-action#what-the-fcra-adds)).
- **Knockout rule:** a policy rule that declines regardless of score, such as state eligibility or a maximum DTI, run before any model ([Underwriting & Decision Engines](/books/banking-and-lending/decision-engines#policy-then-score-then-price)).
- **Loan Estimate:** the TRID form due three business days after a six-field application and at least seven business days before consummation, whose figures set the tolerance baseline ([TRID & Mortgage Disclosures](/books/banking-and-lending/mortgage-disclosures#the-loan-estimate)).
- **Loan origination system (LOS):** the lender's system of record for an application: workflow, verifications, underwriting calls, disclosures and handoff to closing and servicing ([Loan Origination](/books/banking-and-lending/loan-origination#point-of-sale-and-the-los)).
- **Military annual percentage rate (MAPR):** the Military Lending Act's all-in rate, capped at 36%, which counts credit insurance, ancillary products and fees Regulation Z excludes ([APR, Finance Charges & Rate Caps](/books/banking-and-lending/apr-and-rate-caps#the-military-lending-act)).
- **MIN (Mortgage Identification Number):** the 18-digit number that identifies a loan on the MERS System and eRegistry for its life ([Closing, eNotes & MERS](/books/banking-and-lending/closing-and-enotes#the-mers-system-and-the-eregistry)).
- **MISMO:** the mortgage industry's data standards body, publishing the Reference Model, the ULAD dataset and SMART Doc ([Loan Origination](/books/banking-and-lending/loan-origination#mismo-the-urla-and-mortgage-data)).
- **Model inventory:** the list of every model with its owner, use, tier, versions, validations and findings; a model not in it isn't governed ([Model Risk Management](/books/banking-and-lending/model-risk#what-counts-as-a-model)).
- **Model risk guidance (SR 26-2, OCC Bulletin 2026-13):** the US banking agencies' April 2026 model risk management guidance, superseding SR 11-7 and leaving generative and agentic AI out of scope ([Model Risk Management](/books/banking-and-lending/model-risk#the-guidance-in-2026)).
- **Notice of incompleteness:** the written notice naming what an incomplete application lacks and a deadline to provide it, due within 30 days if the creditor doesn't decide ([Loan Origination](/books/banking-and-lending/loan-origination#incomplete-applications)).
- **Override (credit):** a person changing the engine's outcome, high-side (approving a decline) or low-side (declining an approval), with a reason and an authority ([Underwriting & Decision Engines](/books/banking-and-lending/decision-engines#manual-review-referrals-and-overrides)).
- **Permissible purpose (credit):** the FCRA basis for pulling a consumer report: a credit transaction, the consumer's written instructions, account review or a prescreened firm offer ([Credit Data & Scores](/books/banking-and-lending/credit-data-and-scores#permissible-purpose)).
- **Point of sale (POS, origination):** the borrower-facing application: forms, consent, document upload and data connections, feeding the LOS ([Loan Origination](/books/banking-and-lending/loan-origination#point-of-sale-and-the-los)).
- **Population stability index (PSI):** a drift measure comparing the share of a population in each score band now with the share at development ([Model Risk Management](/books/banking-and-lending/model-risk#monitoring-and-drift)).
- **Prequalification:** an estimate of terms, usually on a soft inquiry, that promises nothing; not an application unless the lender evaluates and declines ([Loan Origination](/books/banking-and-lending/loan-origination#what-counts-as-an-application)).
- **Prescreening:** selecting consumers from a bureau's files for a firm offer of credit, honoring opt-outs and limited list contents ([Credit Data & Scores](/books/banking-and-lending/credit-data-and-scores#permissible-purpose)).
- **Principal reasons:** the specific factors that drove an adverse action, stated in the ECOA notice; "insufficient score" or "internal standards" don't qualify ([Adverse Action & Explaining Decisions](/books/banking-and-lending/adverse-action#what-the-ecoa-notice-says)).
- **Rate exportation:** a bank charging borrowers in any state the rate its home state allows, under the National Bank Act for national banks and DIDMCA section 521 for state banks ([APR, Finance Charges & Rate Caps](/books/banking-and-lending/apr-and-rate-caps#state-usury-and-rate-exportation)).
- **Reason family:** a group of correlated model features ranked together so that one underlying cause yields one adverse action reason ([Adverse Action & Explaining Decisions](/books/banking-and-lending/adverse-action#reasons-from-machine-learning-models)).
- **Redlining:** refusing or discouraging lending in neighborhoods because of their racial or ethnic composition ([Fair Lending, HMDA & 1071](/books/banking-and-lending/fair-lending#redlining-enforcement)).
- **Remote online notarization (RON):** notarization with the signer and notary on audio-video, authorized by state law, with identity proofing and a retained recording ([Closing, eNotes & MERS](/books/banking-and-lending/closing-and-enotes#notarization-in-person-electronic-and-remote)).
- **Representative credit score:** the score a mortgage is decided on: the middle of three (or lower of two) per borrower, then the lowest borrower's ([Credit Data & Scores](/books/banking-and-lending/credit-data-and-scores#single-bureau-tri-merge-and-the-representative-score)).
- **Residual income:** what a borrower has left each month after debts, used beside or instead of DTI ([Underwriting & Decision Engines](/books/banking-and-lending/decision-engines#debt-to-income-and-residual-income)).
- **Right of rescission:** a consumer's right to cancel a non-purchase loan secured by a principal dwelling until midnight of the third business day, which holds funding ([Closing, eNotes & MERS](/books/banking-and-lending/closing-and-enotes#rescission-and-funding)).
- **Risk-based pricing notice:** the FCRA notice owed when a consumer report leads to terms materially less favorable than most consumers get ([Adverse Action & Explaining Decisions](/books/banking-and-lending/adverse-action#what-the-fcra-adds)).
- **Section 1071:** ECOA's small business lending data collection; the revised rule applies from January 1, 2028 to lenders with 1,000 originations in each of two years ([Fair Lending, HMDA & 1071](/books/banking-and-lending/fair-lending#section-1071)).
- **SMART Doc:** MISMO's eNote format (Securable, Manageable, Archivable, Retrievable, Transferable) binding data, view and signatures in one tamper-evident document ([Closing, eNotes & MERS](/books/banking-and-lending/closing-and-enotes#enotes-and-smart-docs)).
- **Special purpose credit program:** a written-plan program extending credit to a class that would otherwise be denied, which for-profit lenders may no longer define by race, color, national origin or sex ([Fair Lending, HMDA & 1071](/books/banking-and-lending/fair-lending#discouragement-and-special-purpose-credit-programs)).
- **Tolerance (TRID):** how far a closing charge may exceed the Loan Estimate: zero for lender and affiliate fees, 10% in aggregate for listed third-party services and recording, unlimited for the rest ([TRID & Mortgage Disclosures](/books/banking-and-lending/mortgage-disclosures#tolerances)).
- **Trended data:** the history of balances and payments behind a tradeline (typically 24 months), used by FICO 10T and VantageScore 4.0 ([Credit Data & Scores](/books/banking-and-lending/credit-data-and-scores#the-bureaus-and-what-a-file-holds)).
- **Tri-merge:** a combined report and score from all three nationwide agencies, required for GSE mortgages ([Credit Data & Scores](/books/banking-and-lending/credit-data-and-scores#single-bureau-tri-merge-and-the-representative-score)).
- **True lender:** the doctrine that treats a bank partner's nonbank as the real lender when it holds the economic substance, applying state caps and licensing to it ([APR, Finance Charges & Rate Caps](/books/banking-and-lending/apr-and-rate-caps#true-lender)).
- **Uniform Residential Loan Application (URLA):** Fannie Mae Form 1003 and Freddie Mac Form 65, redesigned and mandatory for GSE loans from March 1, 2021, exchanged as ULAD ([Loan Origination](/books/banking-and-lending/loan-origination#mismo-the-urla-and-mortgage-data)).
- **Valid when made:** the OCC and FDIC rule that interest permissible when a bank made a loan stays permissible after the loan is sold, their answer to Madden ([APR, Finance Charges & Rate Caps](/books/banking-and-lending/apr-and-rate-caps#madden-and-valid-when-made)).
- **Verification of employment, income or assets (VOE, VOI, VOA):** confirming a borrower's statements from the source (payroll records, IRS transcripts, bank data) rather than documents ([Loan Origination](/books/banking-and-lending/loan-origination#documents-and-verification-services)).

## Servicing and Collections

- **ACDV (automated consumer dispute verification):** the electronic form a bureau sends a furnisher through e-OSCAR for a disputed tradeline, with one or two of 29 reason codes and up to 255 characters of text ([Furnishing Credit Data](/books/banking-and-lending/credit-reporting#disputes)).
- **Allowance for credit losses:** a contra-asset reducing loans at amortized cost to what the lender expects to collect, sized for lifetime expected losses under CECL ([Credit Losses & CECL](/books/banking-and-lending/credit-losses-and-cecl#the-allowance-under-asc-326)).
- **Automatic stay:** the bankruptcy rule that halts lawsuits, collection, repossession and setoff against a debtor from the moment a petition is filed ([Collections & Recovery](/books/banking-and-lending/collections#stays-bankruptcy-and-the-scra)).
- **Boarding:** setting up a loan on the servicing system from origination or a prior servicer, reconciled to the cent before the first statement ([Loan Servicing](/books/banking-and-lending/loan-servicing#boarding)).
- **CECL (current expected credit loss):** the ASC 326 model that reserves for a loan's lifetime expected losses from the day it is booked, adjusted each period for conditions and forecasts ([Credit Losses & CECL](/books/banking-and-lending/credit-losses-and-cecl#the-allowance-under-asc-326)).
- **Charge-off:** removing a loan the lender doesn't expect to collect from its books against the allowance, for banks at 120 days past due for closed-end retail loans and 180 for open-end, with later collections booked as recoveries ([Collections & Recovery](/books/banking-and-lending/collections#charge-off-timing)).
- **Date of first delinquency:** the month and year the delinquency before a charge-off or collection began, which starts the seven-year reporting limit after 180 days and doesn't reset on sale ([Furnishing Credit Data](/books/banking-and-lending/credit-reporting#worked-numbers-the-date-of-first-delinquency)).
- **Days past due (DPD):** how late a loan is, counted from the due date of its oldest unpaid installment ([Collections & Recovery](/books/banking-and-lending/collections#delinquency-buckets)).
- **Debt collector (FDCPA):** a person whose principal business is collecting debts or who regularly collects debts owed to another; not a creditor collecting its own debts in its own name ([Collections & Recovery](/books/banking-and-lending/collections#who-the-law-covers)).
- **Debt sale:** selling charged-off accounts to a buyer, with account-level documents and exclusions for bankruptcy, litigation, fraud and deceased borrowers ([Collections & Recovery](/books/banking-and-lending/collections#placement-debt-sale-and-recoveries)).
- **Delinquency bucket:** a range of days past due (30 to 59, 60 to 89 and so on) that drives collection treatment, reporting and loss estimates ([Collections & Recovery](/books/banking-and-lending/collections#delinquency-buckets)).
- **Direct dispute:** a consumer's dispute of furnished information sent to the furnisher rather than a bureau, investigated within the bureau's period ([Furnishing Credit Data](/books/banking-and-lending/credit-reporting#disputes)).
- **Dual tracking:** pursuing foreclosure while a borrower's complete loss mitigation application is pending, which Regulation X bars ([Mortgage Servicing, Escrow & Loss Mitigation](/books/banking-and-lending/mortgage-servicing#loss-mitigation)).
- **e-OSCAR:** the dispute network built by Equifax, Experian, Innovis and TransUnion through which bureaus send disputes to furnishers ([Furnishing Credit Data](/books/banking-and-lending/credit-reporting#how-the-platforms-do-it)).
- **Early intervention:** Regulation X's required live contact by day 36 and written notice by day 45 of a mortgage delinquency ([Mortgage Servicing, Escrow & Loss Mitigation](/books/banking-and-lending/mortgage-servicing#early-intervention-and-continuity-of-contact)).
- **Escrow analysis:** the annual projection of a mortgage escrow account that sets the monthly escrow payment and finds any surplus, shortage or deficiency ([Mortgage Servicing, Escrow & Loss Mitigation](/books/banking-and-lending/mortgage-servicing#escrow)).
- **Escrow cushion:** the reserve a servicer may hold in escrow above projected disbursements, at most one-sixth of the year's total ([Mortgage Servicing, Escrow & Loss Mitigation](/books/banking-and-lending/mortgage-servicing#escrow)).
- **Forbearance:** a period of reduced or suspended payments, resolved at its end by repayment, deferral or modification ([Mortgage Servicing, Escrow & Loss Mitigation](/books/banking-and-lending/mortgage-servicing#forbearance)).
- **Force-placed insurance:** hazard insurance a servicer buys when the borrower's lapses, chargeable only after notices at least 45 and 15 days ahead ([Mortgage Servicing, Escrow & Loss Mitigation](/books/banking-and-lending/mortgage-servicing#force-placed-insurance)).
- **Furnisher:** a lender or other company that reports account information to a consumer reporting agency, with FCRA duties of accuracy, correction and dispute investigation ([Furnishing Credit Data](/books/banking-and-lending/credit-reporting#the-furnisher-s-duties)).
- **Loss mitigation:** a servicer's evaluation of a delinquent mortgage borrower for the alternatives to foreclosure the loan's owner allows, on Regulation X's deadlines ([Mortgage Servicing, Escrow & Loss Mitigation](/books/banking-and-lending/mortgage-servicing#loss-mitigation)).
- **Metro 2:** the CDIA's standard format for furnishing tradelines: a header, a base segment per account with 24 months of history, supplementary segments and a trailer ([Furnishing Credit Data](/books/banking-and-lending/credit-reporting#metro-2)).
- **Mortgage servicing right (MSR):** the right to service a mortgage and collect its fee, carried as an asset whose value moves with prepayment expectations ([Mortgage Servicing, Escrow & Loss Mitigation](/books/banking-and-lending/mortgage-servicing#mortgage-servicing-rights)).
- **Payment allocation:** how one loan payment was applied to fees, interest, principal and escrow under the rule in force that day, stored rather than recomputed ([Loan Servicing](/books/banking-and-lending/loan-servicing#payment-application)).
- **Payoff quote:** a computed record of the amount that pays a loan in full through a good-through date, with a per diem after it ([Loan Servicing](/books/banking-and-lending/loan-servicing#payoff-quotes)).
- **Per diem:** a loan's interest for one day at its current principal, used to extend a payoff quote ([Loan Servicing](/books/banking-and-lending/loan-servicing#worked-numbers-daily-simple-interest-allocation)).
- **Provision for credit losses:** the expense that builds or trues up the allowance each period, including the day-one expected loss on new loans ([Credit Losses & CECL](/books/banking-and-lending/credit-losses-and-cecl#charge-offs-and-recoveries-in-the-ledger)).
- **Re-aging:** returning a delinquent open-end account to current after qualifying payments; for banks at most once in 12 months and twice in five years ([Collections & Recovery](/books/banking-and-lending/collections#hardship-programs)).
- **Reasonable and supportable forecast:** the period over which a CECL estimate uses forecasts of economic conditions before reverting to historical loss information ([Credit Losses & CECL](/books/banking-and-lending/credit-losses-and-cecl#forecasts-and-reversion)).
- **Recovery:** money collected on a loan after it was charged off, credited to the allowance ([Credit Losses & CECL](/books/banking-and-lending/credit-losses-and-cecl#charge-offs-and-recoveries-in-the-ledger)).
- **Roll rate:** the share of balances in one delinquency bucket that move to the next in a month, used to manage collections and forecast losses ([Credit Losses & CECL](/books/banking-and-lending/credit-losses-and-cecl#methods)).
- **Servicing transfer:** moving a loan's servicing to a new servicer; for mortgages with notices 15 days either side and a 60-day window in which a payment to the old servicer isn't late ([Loan Servicing](/books/banking-and-lending/loan-servicing#servicing-transfers)).
- **Seven-in-seven (7-in-7):** Regulation F's presumption that calling a person about a debt more than seven times in seven days, or within seven days after a conversation, is harassment ([Collections & Recovery](/books/banking-and-lending/collections#contact-strategy)).
- **Small servicer:** a mortgage servicer of 5,000 or fewer loans that it or an affiliate owns or originated, exempt from much of Regulation X's servicing subpart ([Mortgage Servicing, Escrow & Loss Mitigation](/books/banking-and-lending/mortgage-servicing#the-servicer-s-obligations)).
- **Subservicer:** a company that does the servicing work on loans whose servicing right another company owns ([Loan Servicing](/books/banking-and-lending/loan-servicing#how-the-platforms-do-it)).
- **Suspense (unapplied funds):** where a servicer holds a partial payment until it adds up to a full periodic payment, shown on the statement ([Loan Servicing](/books/banking-and-lending/loan-servicing#payment-application)).
- **Time-barred debt:** a debt past its statute of limitations, on which a debt collector may not sue or threaten to sue ([Collections & Recovery](/books/banking-and-lending/collections#time-barred-debt)).
- **Validation notice:** the notice a debt collector gives within five days of first contact itemizing the debt and setting the 30-day period to dispute it ([Collections & Recovery](/books/banking-and-lending/collections#validation-notices-and-disputes)).
- **Vintage analysis:** tracking and estimating losses by origination cohort, applied to each cohort's remaining balance by age ([Credit Losses & CECL](/books/banking-and-lending/credit-losses-and-cecl#methods)).
- **WARM (weighted-average remaining maturity):** a CECL loss-rate method multiplying an average annual loss rate by a pool's remaining life ([Credit Losses & CECL](/books/banking-and-lending/credit-losses-and-cecl#methods)).

## Funding and Licensing

- **Advance (servicer):** a servicer's payment to investors of principal and interest a borrower didn't pay, or of taxes, insurance and property costs, recovered later ([The Secondary Mortgage Market](/books/banking-and-lending/secondary-mortgage-market#servicing-rights-and-advances)).
- **Advance rate:** the share of a pledged loan pool's eligible balance a warehouse lender will lend against; the rest is the haircut ([Funding the Book](/books/banking-and-lending/funding-the-book#warehouse-lines)).
- **Bank partnership (lending):** a nonbank's lending program in which a bank makes the loans under its charter and sells the loans or their economics to the nonbank or its investors ([Lender Licensing & Bank Partnerships](/books/banking-and-lending/lender-licensing#partnership-or-charter)).
- **Borrowing base (warehouse):** eligible pledged balance times the advance rate: the most a lender may draw on a warehouse line ([Funding the Book](/books/banking-and-lending/funding-the-book#worked-numbers-a-borrowing-base)).
- **Cash window:** a GSE's purchase of individual conforming loans for cash, which it later pools into its own securities ([The Secondary Mortgage Market](/books/banking-and-lending/secondary-mortgage-market#cash-window-or-swap)).
- **Concentration limit:** a funder's cap on the share of a pool in one state, grade, term or merchant, with the excess treated as ineligible ([Funding the Book](/books/banking-and-lending/funding-the-book#warehouse-lines)).
- **Conforming loan:** a conventional mortgage that meets Fannie Mae's or Freddie Mac's guides and the loan limit FHFA sets each year ([The Secondary Mortgage Market](/books/banking-and-lending/secondary-mortgage-market#the-three-agencies)).
- **Early amortization:** a facility or securitization's switch from revolving to repaying investors from collections when a performance trigger is breached ([Funding the Book](/books/banking-and-lending/funding-the-book#warehouse-lines)).
- **Excess servicing:** the part of a mortgage's servicing strip above the minimum servicing fee, which can be sold separately from the servicing right ([The Secondary Mortgage Market](/books/banking-and-lending/secondary-mortgage-market#worked-numbers-g-fee-and-servicing-strip)).
- **Extinguishment:** Ginnie Mae's seizure of a defaulted issuer's servicing, under which the issuer forfeits its right to recover advances ([The Secondary Mortgage Market](/books/banking-and-lending/secondary-mortgage-market#servicing-rights-and-advances)).
- **Forward flow:** an agreement under which an investor buys a lender's loans as they are made, on agreed criteria ([Funding the Book](/books/banking-and-lending/funding-the-book#forward-flow-and-whole-loan-sales)).
- **Funding allocation:** the recorded decision of which capital source (balance sheet, warehouse, buyer, trust) holds a loan, effective-dated ([Funding the Book](/books/banking-and-lending/funding-the-book#allocation-at-origination)).
- **Ginnie Mae:** the government corporation that guarantees timely payment on securities issuers create from FHA, VA and USDA loans, backed by the full faith and credit of the United States ([The Secondary Mortgage Market](/books/banking-and-lending/secondary-mortgage-market#the-three-agencies)).
- **Guarantee fee (g-fee):** what Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac charge for credit risk: an ongoing strip of the note rate plus upfront loan-level price adjustments ([The Secondary Mortgage Market](/books/banking-and-lending/secondary-mortgage-market#guarantee-fees)).
- **Haircut:** the part of a pledged loan's balance a warehouse lender won't lend against, funded by the lender's own equity ([Funding the Book](/books/banking-and-lending/funding-the-book#warehouse-lines)).
- **Loan-level price adjustment (LLPA):** an upfront GSE fee by credit score, loan-to-value and other attributes, the main form of risk-based pricing in the g-fee ([The Secondary Mortgage Market](/books/banking-and-lending/secondary-mortgage-market#guarantee-fees)).
- **NMLS:** the Nationwide Multistate Licensing System, where nonbank lenders, servicers, collectors, money transmitters and mortgage loan originators hold and renew state licenses ([Lender Licensing & Bank Partnerships](/books/banking-and-lending/lender-licensing#nmls-and-state-licenses)).
- **Participation interest:** an undivided percentage share of a loan's payments and losses sold by the lender that keeps legal title ([Funding the Book](/books/banking-and-lending/funding-the-book#participations)).
- **Risk retention:** the requirement that a securitization's sponsor keep at least 5% of the credit risk as a vertical or horizontal residual interest ([Funding the Book](/books/banking-and-lending/funding-the-book#securitization)).
- **SAFE Act:** the federal law requiring every mortgage loan originator to be state-licensed or federally registered through NMLS with a unique identifier ([Lender Licensing & Bank Partnerships](/books/banking-and-lending/lender-licensing#nmls-and-state-licenses)).
- **Scheduled/scheduled remittance:** remitting scheduled interest net of servicing and scheduled principal to an investor whether or not the borrower paid; the only type for Fannie Mae MBS ([The Secondary Mortgage Market](/books/banking-and-lending/secondary-mortgage-market#cash-window-or-swap)).
- **Securitization:** selling a loan pool to a bankruptcy-remote trust that issues tranched notes paid by a waterfall, with the residual absorbing losses first ([Funding the Book](/books/banking-and-lending/funding-the-book#securitization)).
- **Swap (agency):** delivering a pool of loans to Fannie Mae or Freddie Mac in exchange for the mortgage-backed security backed by them ([The Secondary Mortgage Market](/books/banking-and-lending/secondary-mortgage-market#cash-window-or-swap)).
- **TCCA fee:** the 10 basis points of every GSE guarantee fee paid to the Treasury since December 2012, extended to 2032 ([The Secondary Mortgage Market](/books/banking-and-lending/secondary-mortgage-market#guarantee-fees)).
- **UMBS:** the Uniform Mortgage-Backed Security Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac have both issued since June 3, 2019, with a 55-day payment delay ([The Secondary Mortgage Market](/books/banking-and-lending/secondary-mortgage-market#umbs)).
- **Warehouse line:** a revolving credit facility secured by the loans it funds, drawn at an advance rate until the loans are sold or securitized ([Funding the Book](/books/banking-and-lending/funding-the-book#warehouse-lines)).

## Business Banking

- **Account analysis:** the monthly statement that prices a business account's services and offsets them with an earnings credit on its balances ([Treasury Management](/books/banking-and-lending/treasury-management#account-analysis-and-the-earnings-credit)).
- **ACH debit block, ACH positive pay:** a bank service that refuses every ACH debit to an account, and one that admits only authorized originators and presents the rest for a pay or return decision ([Treasury Management](/books/banking-and-lending/treasury-management#payables-fraud-controls)).
- **Agent institution:** a bank that places its customers' deposits through a reciprocal network and receives matching deposits back, eligible for the brokered deposit exception up to its cap ([Sweeps, Reciprocal Deposits & Yield](/books/banking-and-lending/sweeps-and-yield#reciprocal-deposits-and-the-brokered-deposit-rule)).
- **Balance required:** the investable balance whose earnings credit would exactly pay a month's service charges at the account's ECR ([Treasury Management](/books/banking-and-lending/treasury-management#worked-numbers-an-analysis-statement)).
- **Balance-based and fee-based charges:** service charges an earnings credit may offset, and those it may not ([Treasury Management](/books/banking-and-lending/treasury-management#account-analysis-and-the-earnings-credit)).
- **Controlled disbursement:** an account whose checks are presented early in the day so the bank can report, and the company fund, that day's total ([Treasury Management](/books/banking-and-lending/treasury-management#liquidity-zbas-target-balances-and-sweeps)).
- **Deposit sweep:** spreading one customer's balance across many insured program banks, at most $250,000 at each, to multiply its deposit insurance ([Sweeps, Reciprocal Deposits & Yield](/books/banking-and-lending/sweeps-and-yield#how-a-deposit-sweep-works)).
- **Earnings credit rate (ECR):** the rate a bank applies to a business account's investable balance to compute the credit that offsets its service charges ([Treasury Management](/books/banking-and-lending/treasury-management#account-analysis-and-the-earnings-credit)).
- **Exception default (positive pay):** the pay or return decision a bank applies to an exception the client doesn't decide by the cutoff ([Treasury Management](/books/banking-and-lending/treasury-management#exceptions-deadlines-and-defaults)).
- **Exclusion (sweep):** a bank a customer bars from receiving its swept deposits, usually because it already holds deposits there ([Sweeps, Reciprocal Deposits & Yield](/books/banking-and-lending/sweeps-and-yield#how-a-deposit-sweep-works)).
- **Investment sweep:** moving a balance overnight or on instruction into a money market fund or Treasury bills, out of deposits and out of deposit insurance ([Sweeps, Reciprocal Deposits & Yield](/books/banking-and-lending/sweeps-and-yield#money-market-funds-and-treasury-products)).
- **Not-a-bank disclosure:** a program manager's statement that it is a technology company, not an insured bank, naming the partner banks that provide the accounts ([The Banking Landscape](/books/banking-and-lending/banking-landscape#sponsor-banks-and-baas)).
- **One-way placement:** deposits a bank places at network banks without receiving matching deposits back ([Sweeps, Reciprocal Deposits & Yield](/books/banking-and-lending/sweeps-and-yield#reciprocal-deposits-and-the-brokered-deposit-rule)).
- **Payee positive pay:** positive pay that also matches the payee name on each presented check against the issue file ([Treasury Management](/books/banking-and-lending/treasury-management#payables-fraud-controls)).
- **Platform user:** a login acting for a business with scoped, effective-dated grants, distinct from the account parties the bank verified ([Business Accounts & Startup Banking](/books/banking-and-lending/business-accounts#users-roles-and-approvals)).
- **Program bank:** an insured bank that receives deposits through a sweep program, from a published list that changes ([Sweeps, Reciprocal Deposits & Yield](/books/banking-and-lending/sweeps-and-yield#how-a-deposit-sweep-works)).
- **Reciprocal deposits:** deposits a bank receives through a placement network in the same aggregate amount and maturity as those it places, excepted from brokered up to a cap rewritten in 2026 ([Sweeps, Reciprocal Deposits & Yield](/books/banking-and-lending/sweeps-and-yield#reciprocal-deposits-and-the-brokered-deposit-rule)).
- **Reverse positive pay:** a service in which the bank sends the client the checks presented and the client decides which to pay ([Treasury Management](/books/banking-and-lending/treasury-management#payables-fraud-controls)).
- **Security procedure (UCC 4A):** an agreed method of verifying payment orders; a commercially reasonable one followed in good faith binds the customer to orders it didn't send ([Business Accounts & Startup Banking](/books/banking-and-lending/business-accounts#wires-and-security-procedures)).
- **Settlement window (sweep):** the time between a deposit or withdrawal at the sponsor bank and the next placement or recall, when balances there can exceed the insurance limit ([Sweeps, Reciprocal Deposits & Yield](/books/banking-and-lending/sweeps-and-yield#the-settlement-window)).
- **Sub-account:** one of several accounts a business holds under one relationship, used as an envelope for payroll, taxes or projects; insured together with the rest at the same bank ([Business Accounts & Startup Banking](/books/banking-and-lending/business-accounts#sub-accounts)).
- **Target balance account:** an account the bank tops up or sweeps down to a set balance at end of day ([Treasury Management](/books/banking-and-lending/treasury-management#liquidity-zbas-target-balances-and-sweeps)).
- **Zero balance account (ZBA):** a sub-account brought to zero at end of day against a concentration account ([Treasury Management](/books/banking-and-lending/treasury-management#liquidity-zbas-target-balances-and-sweeps)).

## Spend Management

- **Accountable plan:** an employer's reimbursement or allowance arrangement that meets the business connection, substantiation and return-of-excess requirements of 26 CFR 1.62-2, so its payments aren't wages ([Reimbursements, Mileage & Per Diem](/books/banking-and-lending/reimbursements-and-per-diem#the-accountable-plan)).
- **Accrual (month-end):** a journal entry dated the last day of a period for expense incurred but not yet posted, such as received hotel stays still pending on the card, reversed on the first day of the next period ([Accounting Sync & the Close](/books/banking-and-lending/accounting-sync#accruals-and-the-close)).
- **Centrally billed account (CBA):** a commercial card account billed to the company in one statement for all its cards; GSA SmartPay's purchase and fleet accounts ([Corporate Cards & Underwriting](/books/banking-and-lending/corporate-cards#liability-and-billing-models)).
- **Combined liability:** a corporate card on which the company and the employee are jointly and severally liable, except for the employee's personal charges ([Corporate Cards & Underwriting](/books/banking-and-lending/corporate-cards#liability-and-billing-models)).
- **Corporate liability:** a corporate card on which the company alone owes all charges, billed to the company ([Corporate Cards & Underwriting](/books/banking-and-lending/corporate-cards#liability-and-billing-models)).
- **Daily terms (1-day terms):** charge card terms under which each day's settled spend is pulled from the business's account that day, with the limit near its balance ([Corporate Cards & Underwriting](/books/banking-and-lending/corporate-cards#daily-weekly-or-monthly)).
- **Deemed substantiated:** the amount of a mileage or per diem allowance the IRS treats as substantiated without receipts: the lesser of the allowance and the federal rate, given time, place and business purpose ([Reimbursements, Mileage & Per Diem](/books/banking-and-lending/reimbursements-and-per-diem#per-diem)).
- **Deposit account control agreement (DACA):** an agreement among a debtor, a secured party and the depositary bank that the bank will follow the secured party's instructions, giving it control of the account under UCC 9-104 ([Corporate Cards & Underwriting](/books/banking-and-lending/corporate-cards#security-interests-setoff-and-guarantees)).
- **Dimension (accounting):** a tag on a GL line beyond the account, such as department, class, location, project or a custom segment, whose availability differs by ERP ([Accounting Sync & the Close](/books/banking-and-lending/accounting-sync#gl-coding-and-dimensions)).
- **Duty of care (travel):** an employer's responsibility for employees traveling for work: knowing where they are, warning them, reaching them in an emergency and getting them home ([Business Travel & Duty of Care](/books/banking-and-lending/business-travel#duty-of-care)).
- **Dynamic limit:** a corporate card limit recomputed as balances, burn or revenue change, applied without a request; a credit-based cut to one account is adverse action ([Corporate Cards & Underwriting](/books/banking-and-lending/corporate-cards#limits-from-cash)).
- **Expense reimbursement scheme:** an employee fraud claiming fictitious or inflated business expenses: mischaracterized, overstated, fictitious or multiple reimbursements ([Receipts, Expense Policy & Audit](/books/banking-and-lending/expense-management#expense-fraud)).
- **FAVR allowance:** a fixed and variable rate mileage allowance combining periodic fixed payments and per-mile payments computed from local costs of a standard automobile, under Rev. Proc. 2019-46 ([Reimbursements, Mileage & Per Diem](/books/banking-and-lending/reimbursements-and-per-diem#mileage)).
- **Fixed date method:** the 1.62-2(g) safe harbor for a reasonable period: advances within 30 days, substantiation within 60 days and return of excess within 120 days of the expense ([Reimbursements, Mileage & Per Diem](/books/banking-and-lending/reimbursements-and-per-diem#the-accountable-plan)).
- **Fleet card:** a commercial card for fuel and vehicle maintenance that captures driver, vehicle and odometer at the pump, often on a closed network ([Corporate Cards & Underwriting](/books/banking-and-lending/corporate-cards#commercial-card-types)).
- **Fund (spend):** an instance of a spend program scoped to a person, team or cycle, usually with its own virtual card ([Virtual Cards & Spend Controls](/books/banking-and-lending/card-controls#spend-programs-and-requests)).
- **GDS (global distribution system):** a network through which airlines, hotels and car companies distribute inventory to agencies, earning booking fees from suppliers and paying agencies incentives ([Business Travel & Duty of Care](/books/banking-and-lending/business-travel#gds-and-ndc-in-brief)).
- **GSA SmartPay:** the US government's commercial card program, with purchase, travel, fleet and integrated accounts; $39.4 billion of spend in fiscal 2025 ([Corporate Cards & Underwriting](/books/banking-and-lending/corporate-cards#commercial-card-types)).
- **Hard budget:** a budget that declines any authorization that would take its available amount below zero, counting holds and cleared spend ([Virtual Cards & Spend Controls](/books/banking-and-lending/card-controls#budgets-hard-and-soft)).
- **High-low method:** the IRS per diem method with one rate for listed high-cost localities and one for the rest of CONUS, $329 and $230 from October 1, 2026, used for an employee for the whole calendar year ([Reimbursements, Mileage & Per Diem](/books/banking-and-lending/reimbursements-and-per-diem#per-diem)).
- **Individually billed account (IBA):** a commercial card account billed to the cardholder, who is reimbursed through expense reports; GSA SmartPay's usual travel account ([Corporate Cards & Underwriting](/books/banking-and-lending/corporate-cards#liability-and-billing-models)).
- **Lodge account (central travel account):** a company card account with no plastic, to which its travel management company charges bookings, billed to the company monthly; American Express calls it a Business Travel Account ([Corporate Cards & Underwriting](/books/banking-and-lending/corporate-cards#commercial-card-types)).
- **Match tolerance:** the percentage or absolute difference in quantity, price or amount within which a bill still matches its PO or receipt ([AP, Bill Pay & Procurement](/books/banking-and-lending/accounts-payable#two-and-three-way-match)).
- **NDC (New Distribution Capability):** IATA's XML standard, under Resolution 787, for airlines to sell their own offers and orders to any channel by API instead of through GDS fares ([Business Travel & Duty of Care](/books/banking-and-lending/business-travel#gds-and-ndc-in-brief)).
- **Non-PO invoice:** a bill approved on the invoice alone, with no purchase order to match, such as a utility or a monthly service invoice ([AP, Bill Pay & Procurement](/books/banking-and-lending/accounts-payable#procure-to-pay)).
- **Nonaccountable plan:** an arrangement, or the part of one, that fails the accountable plan requirements, so its payments are wages reported on the W-2 and subject to withholding ([Reimbursements, Mileage & Per Diem](/books/banking-and-lending/reimbursements-and-per-diem#the-accountable-plan)).
- **ONE Order:** IATA's standard, under Resolution 797, for a single airline order record that would replace the PNR, e-ticket and EMD ([Business Travel & Duty of Care](/books/banking-and-lending/business-travel#gds-and-ndc-in-brief)).
- **Participation (card receivable):** a program manager's purchase of an issuing bank's card receivables, up to 100%, which moves the credit risk and funding to the program ([Corporate Cards & Underwriting](/books/banking-and-lending/corporate-cards#who-owns-the-receivable)).
- **Payment-release approval:** a second approval, after a bill is approved, that releases it for payment on a date and method ([AP, Bill Pay & Procurement](/books/banking-and-lending/accounts-payable#procure-to-pay)).
- **Per diem allowance:** a fixed daily amount for lodging, meals and incidentals, or M&IE only, paid instead of reimbursing receipts and substantiated up to the federal rate ([Reimbursements, Mileage & Per Diem](/books/banking-and-lending/reimbursements-and-per-diem#per-diem)).
- **Price variance (AP):** the difference between an invoice's unit price and the PO's, times the quantity, routed to the buyer; distinct from the quantity variance routed to receiving ([AP, Bill Pay & Procurement](/books/banking-and-lending/accounts-payable#worked-numbers-a-price-variance)).
- **Principal or agent (interchange):** the accounting judgment that decides whether a program reports interchange gross or net of the issuing bank's share ([The Spend Management Business](/books/banking-and-lending/spend-management#commercial-interchange)).
- **Purchasing card (P-card):** a centrally billed commercial card for goods and services a company buys often, controlled by merchant category and paired with Level 3 data ([Corporate Cards & Underwriting](/books/banking-and-lending/corporate-cards#commercial-card-types)).
- **Receipt matching:** linking a receipt to a card transaction by merchant, amount and date, with tolerances and review bands ([Receipts, Expense Policy & Audit](/books/banking-and-lending/expense-management#matching)).
- **Rewards (commercial card):** cash back or points paid to the customer out of interchange, about half of it at the platforms that disclose the ratio ([The Spend Management Business](/books/banking-and-lending/spend-management#rewards-as-a-share-of-interchange)).
- **Soft budget:** a budget that approves authorizations past its amount, up to a stated buffer, and flags the overrun ([Virtual Cards & Spend Controls](/books/banking-and-lending/card-controls#budgets-hard-and-soft)).
- **Spend management platform:** software that bundles corporate cards, spend controls, expense and bill pay, often with business accounts, on partner banks' charters ([The Banking Landscape](/books/banking-and-lending/banking-landscape#who-owns-whom-recent-moves)).
- **Spend program:** a versioned template of limits, categories and permitted spend that the cards issued under it inherit ([Virtual Cards & Spend Controls](/books/banking-and-lending/card-controls#spend-programs-and-requests)).
- **Split transaction:** two or more purchases just under an approval or receipt threshold, made close together to evade it ([Receipts, Expense Policy & Audit](/books/banking-and-lending/expense-management#expense-fraud)).
- **Standard mileage rate:** the IRS cents-per-mile business rate, 72.5 cents for January to June 2026 and 76 cents from July 1, 2026, applied by the date of the trip ([Reimbursements, Mileage & Per Diem](/books/banking-and-lending/reimbursements-and-per-diem#mileage)).
- **Substantiation:** the records a business expense needs under 26 CFR 1.274-5: amount, time, place and business purpose, with receipts for lodging and at $75 or more ([Receipts, Expense Policy & Audit](/books/banking-and-lending/expense-management#the-irs-receipt-rules)).
- **Three-way match (AP):** comparing an invoice line with its PO line and its receipt, and paying only what all three support within tolerances; not the Payments reconciliation sense ([AP, Bill Pay & Procurement](/books/banking-and-lending/accounts-payable#two-and-three-way-match)).
- **Travel management company (TMC):** an agency that books and services a company's travel under its policy and negotiated rates, earning client fees, supplier commissions and GDS incentives ([Business Travel & Duty of Care](/books/banking-and-lending/business-travel#travel-management-companies)).
- **Two-way match:** comparing an invoice with its purchase order before payment, without a receipt ([AP, Bill Pay & Procurement](/books/banking-and-lending/accounts-payable#two-and-three-way-match)).
- **Vendor card:** a virtual card issued for one supplier or subscription, with the limit on the card ([Virtual Cards & Spend Controls](/books/banking-and-lending/card-controls#cards-per-person-vendor-or-purpose)).

## Running the Platform

- **Business date:** the institution's processing date a posting counts for, which differs from the calendar date after a cutoff or on a holiday ([The Banking & Lending Business](/books/banking-and-lending/banking-business#the-core-data-model)).
