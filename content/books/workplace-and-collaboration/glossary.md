Workplace software borrows its words from consumer chat, document editing, IT administration, project management, records law and law practice, and the same word shifts with context: a member is a person inside one workspace while an account is the person everywhere, a board is a Kanban view of work items in one part and an infinite canvas in another, a hold suspends deletion for a lawsuit and a screen keeps a lawyer away from a matter, and "external" means a guest in one chapter and another organization's federated users in the next. Entries are grouped by the part of the volume that explains them, alphabetized within each group, and each is one line and a link to the chapter that owns the idea; a word with two meanings appears once per meaning, qualified. The volume's own names for the core records (organization, workspace, domain, account, member, group, container, item, item version, grant, share link, plan, subscription, seat assignment, app, app installation, API token, audit event, retention policy, legal hold, hold custodian) are the ones in [The Core Data Model](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/workplace-business#the-core-data-model).

## The Business

- **Account:** A person's identity (one email, one login), either personal or managed by an organization ([The Workplace Software Business](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/workplace-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **Add-on:** A separately priced capability attached to a plan, such as an AI assistant or customer-managed keys ([Plans, Pricing & Seats](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/plans-pricing-and-seats#how-to-build-it)).
- **AI credits:** A per-seat or pooled allowance of AI usage included in a plan, consumed by members and paid for by the organization ([Business Models & Unit Economics](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/business-models#usage-and-ai-credits)).
- **Annual recurring revenue (ARR):** The annualized run rate of a customer's active recurring contracts at a point in time ([Business Models & Unit Economics](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/business-models#per-seat-saas)).
- **Best-of-breed:** A standalone product chosen as the best in one category, run beside a suite ([The Collaboration Landscape](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/workplace-landscape#suites-against-best-of-breed)).
- **Bottom-up adoption:** Growth in which end users adopt a product first and the organization buys later ([Business Models & Unit Economics](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/business-models#bottom-up-and-top-down)).
- **CAC payback:** The months of a customer's gross margin needed to repay the cost of acquiring it ([Business Models & Unit Economics](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/business-models#bottom-up-and-top-down)).
- **Commitments (EU):** Remedies a company offers to close a Commission antitrust case, made binding without a finding of infringement ([Suites, Bundling & Antitrust](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/suites-bundling-and-antitrust#the-commitments)).
- **Container:** A place content lives (channel, space, project, board, folder, drive, canvas, meeting series) that carries visibility and policy ([The Workplace Software Business](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/workplace-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **Controller and processor:** The customer organization decides how its content is used (controller); the vendor processes it on the customer's instructions (processor) ([The Workplace Software Business](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/workplace-business#the-inversion)).
- **Discount symmetry:** The Teams commitment that discounts on suites with Teams can't exceed those on suites without it ([Suites, Bundling & Antitrust](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/suites-bundling-and-antitrust#the-commitments)).
- **Fair billing:** Slack's policy of billing only members active in the last 28 days and crediting the rest by the day ([Plans, Pricing & Seats](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/plans-pricing-and-seats#what-counts-as-a-seat)).
- **Feature gate:** A plan entitlement that turns a limit, feature or admin control on or off by tier ([Plans, Pricing & Seats](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/plans-pricing-and-seats#tier-ladders-and-feature-gates)).
- **Freemium:** A free plan with limits designed to be outgrown, as the first step of product-led growth ([Business Models & Unit Economics](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/business-models#freemium-and-product-led-growth)).
- **Gross retention:** Net retention with expansion capped at zero, isolating churn and contraction ([Business Models & Unit Economics](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/business-models#retention-and-expansion)).
- **Inversion of duties:** The workplace pattern in which the customer organization, not the vendor, moderates, retains, holds and answers legal requests ([The Workplace Software Business](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/workplace-business#the-inversion)).
- **Item:** One piece of content of any kind (message, page, file, work item, comment, design, recording) ([The Workplace Software Business](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/workplace-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **Land and expand:** Selling a small first deal to one team, then growing seats, products and editions inside the customer ([Business Models & Unit Economics](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/business-models#freemium-and-product-led-growth)).
- **Listed Providers:** The clouds (Microsoft, Alibaba, Amazon, Google) on which Microsoft licenses without mobility rights can't be redeployed ([Suites, Bundling & Antitrust](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/suites-bundling-and-antitrust#licensing-restrictions-on-clouds)).
- **Member:** An account inside one workspace, with a kind (full, guest, external, bot, service), a role and a status ([The Workplace Software Business](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/workplace-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **Net dollar retention (NDR):** A cohort's recurring revenue now divided by the same customers' revenue a year earlier, including expansion, contraction and churn ([Business Models & Unit Economics](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/business-models#retention-and-expansion)).
- **New commerce experience (NCE):** Microsoft's CSP purchasing platform with monthly, annual and three-year terms and a seven-day reduction window ([Plans, Pricing & Seats](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/plans-pricing-and-seats#what-counts-as-a-seat)).
- **Organization:** The customer: the billing entity that owns domains, contracts and org-wide policy ([The Workplace Software Business](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/workplace-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **Product retirement:** A dated end of sale or end of life with notice, read-only, deletion and export dates ([The Collaboration Landscape](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/workplace-landscape#how-to-build-it)).
- **Product-led growth (PLG):** A go-to-market in which the product, not a salesperson, makes the first sale ([Business Models & Unit Economics](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/business-models#freemium-and-product-led-growth)).
- **Remaining performance obligations (RPO):** Contracted revenue not yet recognized ([Business Models & Unit Economics](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/business-models#per-seat-saas)).
- **Seat:** A license for one user for one period, defined differently by each vendor ([Plans, Pricing & Seats](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/plans-pricing-and-seats#what-counts-as-a-seat)).
- **Seat type:** A per-role seat price on the same plan, such as Figma's Full, Dev and Collab seats ([Plans, Pricing & Seats](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/plans-pricing-and-seats#what-counts-as-a-seat)).
- **SMS (strategic market status):** The UK CMA's designation under the DMCC Act 2024 that allows conduct requirements on a firm ([Suites, Bundling & Antitrust](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/suites-bundling-and-antitrust#the-uk-case-business-software-and-copilot)).
- **SSO tax:** The price difference between a base paid tier and the tier that includes single sign-on ([Plans, Pricing & Seats](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/plans-pricing-and-seats#the-sso-tax)).
- **Suite:** Many products sold under one contract, identity, admin console and price ([Suites, Bundling & Antitrust](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/suites-bundling-and-antitrust#why-suites-win-procurement)).
- **Switch window:** A dated opportunity for a customer to move to a suite without a product, required by the Teams commitments ([Suites, Bundling & Antitrust](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/suites-bundling-and-antitrust#how-to-build-it)).
- **Tying:** Making one product available only with another, an abuse when the seller is dominant ([Suites, Bundling & Antitrust](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/suites-bundling-and-antitrust#the-teams-case)).
- **User tier:** Atlassian's annual pricing band by number of users, fixed for the term ([Plans, Pricing & Seats](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/plans-pricing-and-seats#what-counts-as-a-seat)).
- **Workspace:** A tenant unit inside an organization (Slack workspace, Atlassian site, Notion workspace), where region and most settings live ([The Workplace Software Business](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/workplace-business#the-core-data-model)).

## Tenancy and Identity

- **Access review:** A periodic check, often scoped to guests or external users, of whether each person still needs the access they hold ([Guests & Cross-Org Collaboration](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/guests-and-external-collaboration#three-models-for-an-outsider)).
- **Administrative unit:** A Microsoft Entra container that scopes an admin role to a subset of users, groups or devices, for regional or departmental admins ([Admin Consoles & Policies](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/admin-consoles-and-policies#microsoft-entra-id)).
- **Advanced Data Residency:** A Microsoft 365 add-on that commits data at rest to a single-country local region geography and extends residency to more services, covering all paid licenses in the tenant ([Data Residency & Sovereignty](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/data-residency-and-sovereignty#microsoft-s-eu-data-boundary-multi-geo-and-advanced-data-residency)).
- **Anyone-with-the-link sharing:** A share link that grants access to whoever holds the URL, without signing in, acting as a bearer credential ([Permissions & Sharing](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/permissions-and-sharing#link-sharing)).
- **Approved domains:** A Slack setting that lets anyone with an email address on listed domains join a workspace without an invitation ([Tenants, Workspaces & Organizations](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/tenants-and-workspaces#finding-and-joining-a-workspace)).
- **B2B direct connect:** A Microsoft Entra mutual trust between two tenants that lets each side's users join Teams shared channels with their home accounts, without guest objects ([Guests & Cross-Org Collaboration](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/guests-and-external-collaboration#trust-between-two-admins)).
- **Break-glass account:** An emergency admin account that can sign in when the identity provider or normal admin paths fail, kept few, protected by a phishing-resistant factor and alarmed ([SSO, Domains & Managed Accounts](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/sso-and-managed-accounts#who-can-still-sign-in-when-the-identity-provider-is-down)).
- **Claiming accounts:** Moving accounts on a verified domain from personal control to an organization's management, as a step separate from verifying the domain ([SSO, Domains & Managed Accounts](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/sso-and-managed-accounts#managed-versus-personal-accounts)).
- **CLOUD Act:** The US law (18 U.S.C. 2713) requiring providers to disclose data in their possession, custody or control regardless of where it is stored ([Data Residency & Sovereignty](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/data-residency-and-sovereignty#sovereignty-beyond-location)).
- **Conditional Access:** Microsoft Entra's policy engine that evaluates user, location, device, app and risk signals at sign-in and blocks or grants access with requirements such as MFA ([Admin Consoles & Policies](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/admin-consoles-and-policies#signals-and-conditions)).
- **Conflicting account:** A personal Google account created with a company address that collides with the managed account an admin wants to create ([SSO, Domains & Managed Accounts](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/sso-and-managed-accounts#managed-versus-personal-accounts)).
- **Context-aware access:** Google Workspace policies that limit app access by IP range, device state, operating system and geographic origin ([Admin Consoles & Policies](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/admin-consoles-and-policies#signals-and-conditions)).
- **Cross-tenant access settings:** Microsoft Entra's inbound, outbound and trust settings that govern how users of another tenant may collaborate with yours ([Guests & Cross-Org Collaboration](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/guests-and-external-collaboration#trust-between-two-admins)).
- **Data Privacy Framework:** The EU-US framework whose July 10, 2023 adequacy decision allows personal data transfers to US organizations, upheld by the General Court in Latombe in September 2025 ([Data Residency & Sovereignty](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/data-residency-and-sovereignty#the-transfer-law)).
- **Data residency:** A vendor commitment to store specified categories of customer data at rest in a chosen region, with other categories explicitly excluded ([Data Residency & Sovereignty](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/data-residency-and-sovereignty#what-residency-covers)).
- **Deactivation:** Reversibly stopping a member's access (sessions, tokens, channels) while keeping their account, roles and authored content ([Provisioning, SCIM & Offboarding](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/provisioning-and-scim#deactivation-versus-deletion)).
- **Domain capture:** Figma's automatic addition of people with a company email, existing accounts and new sign-ups, to the organization as members ([Tenants, Workspaces & Organizations](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/tenants-and-workspaces#notion-and-figma-organizations-over-workspaces-and-teams)).
- **Domain verification:** Proving control of an email domain, usually with a DNS TXT record, as the root of trust for claiming and capturing accounts ([SSO, Domains & Managed Accounts](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/sso-and-managed-accounts#proving-the-domain)).
- **EU Data Boundary:** Microsoft's commitment, completed February 2025, to store and process customer data, pseudonymized personal data and support data for its enterprise cloud services in the EU and EFTA ([Data Residency & Sovereignty](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/data-residency-and-sovereignty#microsoft-s-eu-data-boundary-multi-geo-and-advanced-data-residency)).
- **External access:** Teams federation that lets users find, chat with and call people in other organizations without guest accounts ([Guests & Cross-Org Collaboration](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/guests-and-external-collaboration#three-models-for-an-outsider)).
- **External user:** In Atlassian, any account the organization doesn't manage, including unclaimed accounts on its own domain ([Guests & Cross-Org Collaboration](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/guests-and-external-collaboration#sharing-files-and-pages-with-outsiders)).
- **Guest:** An outsider given an account or access inside the host's tenant under the host's rules, often limited to one container and set to expire ([Guests & Cross-Org Collaboration](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/guests-and-external-collaboration#three-models-for-an-outsider)).
- **Joiner-mover-leaver:** The HR-driven identity life cycle of hires, transfers and terminations that provisioning carries from the HR system to every app ([Provisioning, SCIM & Offboarding](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/provisioning-and-scim#joiner-mover-leaver)).
- **Just-in-time provisioning:** Creating an account the first time a person signs in through SSO, which admits people but never removes them ([SSO, Domains & Managed Accounts](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/sso-and-managed-accounts#enforcing-single-sign-on)).
- **Managed account:** An account an organization controls (edits, deactivates, applies sign-in policy to) after verifying its domain and claiming it ([SSO, Domains & Managed Accounts](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/sso-and-managed-accounts#managed-versus-personal-accounts)).
- **Multi-Geo:** A Microsoft 365 add-on that stores each user's data in a chosen geography within one tenant, through a preferred data location ([Data Residency & Sovereignty](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/data-residency-and-sovereignty#microsoft-s-eu-data-boundary-multi-geo-and-advanced-data-residency)).
- **Multitenant organization:** A Microsoft Entra grouping of up to 100 tenants one company owns, joined by each tenant's admin as equals ([Tenants, Workspaces & Organizations](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/tenants-and-workspaces#microsoft-365-one-tenant-and-then-several)).
- **Organizational unit:** A node in Google Workspace's settings tree; each user belongs to one, and settings inherit from parent to child unless overridden ([Tenants, Workspaces & Organizations](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/tenants-and-workspaces#google-workspace-one-customer-organizational-units)).
- **Privileged Identity Management:** Microsoft Entra's just-in-time elevation, where admins hold eligible roles and activate them for a limited time, optionally with approval ([Admin Consoles & Policies](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/admin-consoles-and-policies#just-in-time-elevation)).
- **Profile source:** In Okta, the one system authoritative for a user's identity attributes, such as an HR system ([Provisioning, SCIM & Offboarding](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/provisioning-and-scim#okta)).
- **Relationship-based access control (ReBAC):** Authorization stored as relationship tuples between users and objects, with inheritance expressed as schema rules, as in Zanzibar, OpenFGA and SpiceDB ([Permissions & Sharing](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/permissions-and-sharing#zanzibar-and-relationship-based-access-control)).
- **Schrems II:** The July 16, 2020 Court of Justice judgment (C-311/18) that invalidated the EU-US Privacy Shield and kept standard contractual clauses valid with verification duties ([Data Residency & Sovereignty](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/data-residency-and-sovereignty#the-transfer-law)).
- **SCIM:** The IETF standard (RFC 7643 and 7644) through which identity providers create, update, deactivate and group users in apps ([Provisioning, SCIM & Offboarding](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/provisioning-and-scim#scim-2-0)).
- **Security defaults:** Microsoft Entra's free baseline that requires MFA registration, MFA for admins and blocks legacy authentication ([SSO, Domains & Managed Accounts](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/sso-and-managed-accounts#mfa-policy)).
- **Shared channel:** One channel hosted by two or more organizations at once, such as Slack Connect channels and Teams shared channels ([Guests & Cross-Org Collaboration](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/guests-and-external-collaboration#three-models-for-an-outsider)).
- **Slack Connect:** Slack's shared channels and DMs between organizations, with each organization's settings governing its own members' messages ([Guests & Cross-Org Collaboration](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/guests-and-external-collaboration#who-owns-shared-content)).
- **System role:** A Slack admin role carved out for one job, such as Legal Holds Admin, Export Admin or Users Admin ([Admin Consoles & Policies](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/admin-consoles-and-policies#slack)).
- **Tenant isolation:** Guaranteeing one customer's data can't reach another's, across primary stores, indexes, caches and exports ([Tenants, Workspaces & Organizations](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/tenants-and-workspaces#tenant-isolation)).
- **Zookie:** Zanzibar's opaque consistency token encoding a timestamp, stored with a content version so later permission checks for it run on a snapshot at least that fresh ([Permissions & Sharing](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/permissions-and-sharing#zanzibar-and-relationship-based-access-control)).

## Chat and Messaging

- **Availability view:** A string from Microsoft Graph's getSchedule with one digit per time slot (free, tentative, busy, out of office, working elsewhere) describing a person's or room's free/busy ([Email & Calendar](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/email-and-calendar#free-busy-and-availability)).
- **Broadcast mention:** A mention such as `@channel`, `@here` or `@everyone` that notifies every member (or every active member) of a container, gated by permissions and size thresholds ([Notifications, Presence & Attention](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/notifications-and-attention#channel-wide-mentions)).
- **CalDAV:** The IETF protocol (RFC 4791) for accessing and managing iCalendar data over WebDAV, extended by RFC 6638 for server-side scheduling ([Email & Calendar](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/email-and-calendar#caldav-and-implicit-scheduling)).
- **Canvas:** A formatted document embedded in a chat channel or DM, with its own edit and comment permissions ([Channels, Threads & Direct Messages](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/workplace-chat#canvases-lists-and-huddles)).
- **Cell (availability zone):** A siloed copy of a service stack in one availability zone that receives and sends traffic only within the zone, so the zone can be drained without user-visible errors ([Chat at Scale](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/chat-architecture#cells-and-draining)).
- **Channel server:** In Slack's architecture, a stateful in-memory service that owns a consistent-hashed set of channels and fans their messages out to gateway servers ([Chat at Scale](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/chat-architecture#the-write-path-at-slack)).
- **Compliance copy:** A copy of a chat message stored for search, hold and export separately from the chat service's primary store, as Teams does in participants' and group mailboxes ([Chat at Scale](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/chat-architecture#teams-a-chat-service-and-a-record-in-every-mailbox)).
- **Constructive knowledge:** Under the FLSA, what an employer should have known about unscheduled work through reasonable diligence, which makes that work compensable ([Frontline & Deskless Workers](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/frontline-workers#after-hours-messages-and-wage-hour-law)).
- **Do not disturb:** A member state that suppresses notification banners and sounds while still delivering messages, with bounded breakthrough for urgent senders or a priority list ([Notifications, Presence & Attention](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/notifications-and-attention#do-not-disturb-schedules-and-breakthrough)).
- **Free/busy:** Time ranges and a busy status for a calendar, shared without event details, used to find meeting times ([Email & Calendar](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/email-and-calendar#free-busy-and-availability)).
- **Frontline Member:** Slack's restricted, lower-priced account type for deskless employees, limited to channels admins add them to and billed on everyone added ([Frontline & Deskless Workers](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/frontline-workers#restricted-seats)).
- **Frontline worker:** An employee who works mostly away from a desk, on phones or shared devices, often in shifts and often paid hourly ([Frontline & Deskless Workers](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/frontline-workers)).
- **Group DM:** A direct message conversation among a small fixed set of people, without a name or owner, such as Slack's DMs of up to nine people ([Channels, Threads & Direct Messages](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/workplace-chat#direct-messages-and-group-chats)).
- **Huddle:** Slack's informal audio and video call started in a channel or DM, with its own notes thread and canvas ([Channels, Threads & Direct Messages](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/workplace-chat#canvases-lists-and-huddles)).
- **iCalendar:** The IETF data format (RFC 5545) for events, to-dos, free/busy and time zones, the interchange format for meeting invitations ([Email & Calendar](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/email-and-calendar#the-calendar-standards)).
- **ID-only push notification:** A push that carries only a message id, with the app fetching content from the server on receipt, keeping content out of Apple's and Google's push services ([Notifications, Presence & Attention](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/notifications-and-attention#push-through-someone-else-s-pipe)).
- **iMIP:** The binding (RFC 6047) that carries iTIP scheduling messages in email, with rules for authenticating organizers and attendees ([Email & Calendar](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/email-and-calendar#itip-the-organizer-owns-the-event)).
- **Implicit scheduling:** CalDAV scheduling in which the server sends invitations, replies and cancellations when a client saves an event ([Email & Calendar](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/email-and-calendar#caldav-and-implicit-scheduling)).
- **In-line thread:** A side conversation branching from one message in a space, as in Google Chat since 2022, notifying participants and followers ([Channels, Threads & Direct Messages](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/workplace-chat#in-line-threads-in-google-chat)).
- **iTIP:** The scheduling protocol (RFC 5546) defining methods such as REQUEST, REPLY, CANCEL and COUNTER and how updates are ordered by UID, SEQUENCE and DTSTAMP ([Email & Calendar](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/email-and-calendar#itip-the-organizer-owns-the-event)).
- **JSCalendar:** A JSON data model for calendar data (RFC 8984), compatible with iCalendar and intended to succeed it over time ([Email & Calendar](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/email-and-calendar#jscalendar-and-the-json-future)).
- **Messaging policy:** A tenant-level policy, assignable to users or groups, that decides who can edit or delete sent messages, use broadcast mentions and see read receipts ([Channels, Threads & Direct Messages](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/workplace-chat#editing-and-deleting-the-admin-s-decision)).
- **Predictive scheduling law:** A law requiring advance publication of work schedules, rest between shifts and premium pay for late changes, such as Oregon's 14-day notice rule ([Frontline & Deskless Workers](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/frontline-workers#predictive-scheduling-laws)).
- **Presence:** A derived signal of a member's availability (available, busy, away, do not disturb, offline) computed from activity, app state and the calendar across devices ([Notifications, Presence & Attention](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/notifications-and-attention#presence)).
- **Presence session:** One client's or app's contribution to a user's presence, which expires unless refreshed and is aggregated with the others ([Notifications, Presence & Attention](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/notifications-and-attention#presence)).
- **Priority access:** A Teams list of people whose messages and calls break through a user's do-not-disturb state ([Notifications, Presence & Attention](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/notifications-and-attention#do-not-disturb-schedules-and-breakthrough)).
- **Push-to-talk:** Walkie-talkie style voice over Wi-Fi or cellular data on phones, using chat channels as talk groups, as in Teams Walkie Talkie ([Frontline & Deskless Workers](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/frontline-workers#push-to-talk)).
- **Read marker:** The server-side record of the last message a member has read in a channel or followed thread, from which unread and badge counts are derived ([Chat at Scale](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/chat-architecture#ordering-unread-state-and-badges)).
- **Resource mailbox:** An Exchange mailbox for a room or piece of equipment whose calendar processing rules accept, decline or route bookings ([Email & Calendar](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/email-and-calendar#rooms-and-resources)).
- **Right to disconnect:** A legal rule on work contact outside working hours, ranging from a written-policy duty (Ontario) to a right to refuse unreasonable contact (Australia) ([Notifications, Presence & Attention](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/notifications-and-attention#the-right-to-disconnect)).
- **Scheduling link:** A booking page that publishes a host's availability from connected calendars and creates the event when a guest picks a slot ([Email & Calendar](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/email-and-calendar#scheduling-links)).
- **Shared device mode:** A Microsoft Entra feature that lets frontline workers sign in once to all apps on a shared phone and sign out of all of them at shift end ([Frontline & Deskless Workers](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/frontline-workers#identity-without-a-desk)).
- **Shift-based tag:** A Teams mention tag tied to the Shifts schedule that notifies only the people on shift when it's used ([Frontline & Deskless Workers](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/frontline-workers#reaching-whoever-is-on-shift)).
- **Task publishing:** Publishing a task list from headquarters to many location teams, with assignment by local managers and completion reporting by location ([Frontline & Deskless Workers](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/frontline-workers#publishing-tasks-to-locations)).
- **Thread:** A set of replies attached to a parent message, followed by participants and shown apart from the channel's main stream ([Channels, Threads & Direct Messages](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/workplace-chat#channels-with-reply-threads)).
- **Topic:** In Zulip, a label every channel message carries that groups it into a conversation, used instead of reply threads ([Channels, Threads & Direct Messages](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/workplace-chat#topics-in-zulip)).
- **Unified Grid:** Slack's 2023 to 2024 re-architecture that gave Enterprise Grid users one organization-wide view across workspaces ([Chat at Scale](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/chat-architecture#unified-grid-from-workspace-to-organization)).
- **Urgent message:** A Teams message marked urgent that re-notifies the recipient every 2 minutes for 20 minutes until read ([Notifications, Presence & Attention](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/notifications-and-attention#senders-signals)).
- **VAVAILABILITY:** The iCalendar component (RFC 7953) for publishing when a user can be booked, such as recurring working hours ([Email & Calendar](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/email-and-calendar#free-busy-and-availability)).

## Meetings and Voice

- **Ask mode:** a workplace check-in setting in which a user's automatically detected work location isn't shared until the user opts in ([Rooms, Devices & the Hybrid Office](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/rooms-and-the-hybrid-office#work-location-and-presence)).
- **Async video:** a recorded screen-and-camera message shared as a link and watched later, replacing a live meeting ([Async Video & Screen Recording](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/async-video)).
- **Auto attendant:** a voice application that answers a business number and routes callers through a menu ([Business Phone & Contact Center](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/business-phone-and-contact-center#what-a-business-phone-system-is)).
- **Auto-release:** freeing a booked room automatically when nobody checks in within a set window, or when all but one invitee declines ([Rooms, Devices & the Hybrid Office](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/rooms-and-the-hybrid-office#booking-rooms-and-desks)).
- **Breakout room:** a sub-session split off from a meeting that participants move into and back from without rejoining ([Meetings & Video](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/meetings-and-video#breakout-rooms)).
- **CCaaS (contact center as a service):** a cloud contact center with queues, routing, IVR, agent tooling and quality management ([Business Phone & Contact Center](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/business-phone-and-contact-center#contact-centers)).
- **Cloud Video Interop (CVI):** Microsoft-qualified third-party gateways that let SIP and H.323 room systems join Teams meetings ([Meetings & Video](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/meetings-and-video#interop-with-rooms-and-other-platforms)).
- **Compliance recording:** automatic, policy-driven recording of a user's calls and meetings, owned by the company, for regulatory capture ([Recordings, Transcripts & AI Notes](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/recordings-transcripts-and-ai-notes#convenience-and-compliance-recording)).
- **Congestion control:** the sender-side algorithm that estimates available bandwidth from packet delay and loss and sets the media bitrate ([Meetings & Video](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/meetings-and-video#simulcast-svc-and-bandwidth-adaptation)).
- **Convenience recording:** an ad hoc recording a user starts and manages, stored with the meeting's files ([Recordings, Transcripts & AI Notes](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/recordings-transcripts-and-ai-notes#convenience-and-compliance-recording)).
- **Direct Guest Join:** a room system joining another vendor's meeting through that vendor's web client as a guest over WebRTC ([Rooms, Devices & the Hybrid Office](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/rooms-and-the-hybrid-office#joining-across-vendors)).
- **Direct Routing:** connecting Teams Phone to any carrier through a certified session border controller the customer or a partner manages ([Business Phone & Contact Center](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/business-phone-and-contact-center#connecting-to-the-phone-network)).
- **Dynamic emergency calling:** sending a 911 call with a location resolved from the client's current network connection rather than a fixed registered address ([Business Phone & Contact Center](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/business-phone-and-contact-center#emergency-calling-on-the-customer-s-network-map)).
- **eCDN (enterprise content delivery network):** caches or peer-to-peer delivery inside a customer's network that keep a broadcast event from pulling one stream per viewer through the internet edge ([Meetings & Video](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/meetings-and-video#large-meetings-webinars-and-town-halls)).
- **End-to-end encryption (E2EE):** encryption with keys held only on participants' devices, so the service forwards media it can't read and can't record, transcribe or summarize ([Meetings & Video](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/meetings-and-video#encryption)).
- **Explicit recording consent:** a policy that requires each participant to agree before they're recorded or transcribed ([Recordings, Transcripts & AI Notes](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/recordings-transcripts-and-ai-notes#consent-law)).
- **Firm order commitment (FOC):** the losing carrier's confirmation of the date it will release ported numbers ([Business Phone & Contact Center](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/business-phone-and-contact-center#numbers-and-porting)).
- **Ghost meeting:** a recurring room booking nobody attends, holding the room until released ([Rooms, Devices & the Hybrid Office](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/rooms-and-the-hybrid-office#booking-rooms-and-desks)).
- **Inform mode:** a workplace check-in setting in which automatic work location is shared unless the user opts out ([Rooms, Devices & the Hybrid Office](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/rooms-and-the-hybrid-office#work-location-and-presence)).
- **Lobby (waiting room):** where people who don't bypass admission wait until an organizer admits them to a meeting ([Meetings & Video](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/meetings-and-video#meeting-security)).
- **Location Information Service (LIS):** the customer-maintained map from access points, switch ports, switches and subnets to emergency addresses ([Business Phone & Contact Center](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/business-phone-and-contact-center#emergency-calling-on-the-customer-s-network-map)).
- **Messaging Layer Security (MLS):** the IETF group key agreement protocol (RFC 9420) used for end-to-end encrypted meetings ([Meetings & Video](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/meetings-and-video#encryption)).
- **Notetaker bot:** an app that joins meetings as a participant, often from a user's calendar, to record, transcribe and summarize them ([Recordings, Transcripts & AI Notes](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/recordings-transcripts-and-ai-notes#notetaker-bots)).
- **Operator Connect:** connecting Teams Phone to a certified carrier that has already integrated with Microsoft, enabled from the admin center ([Business Phone & Contact Center](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/business-phone-and-contact-center#connecting-to-the-phone-network)).
- **Resource account:** the mailbox-enabled identity a room or shared device signs in as, instead of a person ([Rooms, Devices & the Hybrid Office](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/rooms-and-the-hybrid-office#rooms-as-accounts)).
- **Selective forwarding unit (SFU):** a media server that forwards each sender's streams to the receivers that need them without decoding them ([Meetings & Video](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/meetings-and-video#topologies-why-everyone-runs-an-sfu)).
- **SFrame:** per-frame end-to-end media encryption (RFC 9605) that leaves routing data visible to an SFU ([Meetings & Video](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/meetings-and-video#encryption)).
- **Simulcast:** a sender encoding the same source at several qualities so an SFU can forward each receiver the one it can take ([Meetings & Video](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/meetings-and-video#simulcast-svc-and-bandwidth-adaptation)).
- **Speaker identification:** attributing speech to enrolled people using voice profiles, a form of biometric processing ([Recordings, Transcripts & AI Notes](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/recordings-transcripts-and-ai-notes#transcripts-captions-and-speaker-identification)).
- **SVC (scalable video coding):** one video stream encoded in layers that an SFU can strip for weaker receivers ([Meetings & Video](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/meetings-and-video#simulcast-svc-and-bandwidth-adaptation)).
- **Town hall:** a broadcast-style event in which attendees watch with a delay and interact through Q&A rather than microphones and cameras ([Meetings & Video](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/meetings-and-video#large-meetings-webinars-and-town-halls)).
- **UCaaS (unified communications as a service):** the cloud business phone system sold with chat and meetings, priced per seat ([Business Phone & Contact Center](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/business-phone-and-contact-center)).
- **Viewership retention:** views at each point of a video as a percentage of its most-watched point ([Async Video & Screen Recording](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/async-video#viewer-analytics-and-privacy)).
- **Workplace check-in:** automatic setting of a user's actual work location from corporate Wi-Fi or a desk peripheral, off by default and without history ([Rooms, Devices & the Hybrid Office](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/rooms-and-the-hybrid-office#work-location-and-presence)).

## Docs, Wikis and Files

- **Advanced electronic signature (AdES):** An eIDAS signature uniquely linked to and capable of identifying the signatory, under the signatory's control, that makes later changes detectable ([E-Signatures & Agreements](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/e-signatures-and-agreements#eidas-three-levels-and-qualified-providers)).
- **Atlassian Document Format (ADF):** The JSON document schema behind Confluence and Jira content, with a `doc` root, block and inline nodes, marks and attrs ([Docs & Wikis](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/docs-and-wikis#structured-documents-confluence-google-docs-and-loop)).
- **Audience targeting:** Showing intranet content only to members of chosen groups, a placement filter that is not a permission ([Intranets & Knowledge Bases](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/intranets-and-knowledge#targeting-is-not-permission)).
- **Awareness (presence):** Ephemeral co-editing state such as cursors and selections, kept outside the document and expired by silence ([Real-Time Co-Editing](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/real-time-collaboration#presence-and-cursors)).
- **Block (docs):** A unit of content with an ID, a type, properties, an ordered list of children and a parent pointer, from which a page is built ([Docs & Wikis](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/docs-and-wikis#blocks-notion-s-model)).
- **Blocklist:** The ordered list of content hashes of a file's fixed-size blocks, which identifies a file version in block-level sync ([Files, Sync & Storage](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/files-and-sync#block-level-sync)).
- **Certificate of completion:** The provider-generated record of an e-signature envelope's history, stored with the signed documents as evidence ([E-Signatures & Agreements](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/e-signatures-and-agreements#the-certificate-of-completion)).
- **Conflicted copy:** A sibling file a sync service creates when two edits to the same file can't be reconciled, so neither write is lost ([Files, Sync & Storage](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/files-and-sync#conflicts)).
- **Connector data policy:** A tenant rule grouping low-code connectors as Business, Non-Business or Blocked so data can't flow between groups in one app or flow ([No-Code Databases & Internal Tools](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/no-code-databases-and-internal-tools#governance-and-shadow-apps)).
- **Contract lifecycle management (CLM):** Software covering an agreement from request and drafting through negotiation, signature, obligations and renewal ([E-Signatures & Agreements](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/e-signatures-and-agreements#contract-lifecycle-management)).
- **CRDT:** A conflict-free replicated data type, whose concurrent operations commute so replicas converge without a central order ([Real-Time Co-Editing](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/real-time-collaboration#crdts)).
- **Delegation (low-code):** Translating an app's query into a query the data source runs, instead of fetching a capped number of rows and filtering locally ([No-Code Databases & Internal Tools](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/no-code-databases-and-internal-tools#formulas-and-computed-fields)).
- **Envelope:** An e-signature package of documents, ordered recipients and fields, with a status from created through completed, declined or voided ([E-Signatures & Agreements](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/e-signatures-and-agreements#the-envelope)).
- **Fractional indexing:** Ordering siblings by position strings that always leave room between neighbors, so inserts and moves never renumber ([Real-Time Co-Editing](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/real-time-collaboration#server-authority-figma-s-approach)).
- **Home site:** The SharePoint site set as an organization's intranet landing page and the basis of the SharePoint app in Teams ([Intranets & Knowledge Bases](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/intranets-and-knowledge#the-suite-intranet-sharepoint)).
- **Hub site:** A SharePoint site that groups related sites with shared navigation, recommended over nested subsites ([Intranets & Knowledge Bases](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/intranets-and-knowledge#the-suite-intranet-sharepoint)).
- **Live doc:** A Confluence content type that saves every change for all viewers in real time with no publish step ([Docs & Wikis](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/docs-and-wikis#structured-documents-confluence-google-docs-and-loop)).
- **Local undo:** Multiplayer undo that reverts only the current user's changes, merged against everything since ([Real-Time Co-Editing](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/real-time-collaboration#undo-in-multiplayer)).
- **Local-first software:** Software that keeps the primary copy of data on the user's device and syncs it, usually with CRDTs ([Real-Time Co-Editing](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/real-time-collaboration#crdts)).
- **Loop component:** A live Microsoft 365 content fragment backed by a `.loop` file whose storage location depends on where it was created ([Docs & Wikis](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/docs-and-wikis#structured-documents-confluence-google-docs-and-loop)).
- **Managed Environment:** A Power Platform environment with premium governance features such as sharing limits, usage insights and environment data policies ([No-Code Databases & Internal Tools](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/no-code-databases-and-internal-tools#governance-and-shadow-apps)).
- **Operational transformation (OT):** Adjusting each concurrent edit operation against the ones it crossed so all editors converge, usually through a central server ([Real-Time Co-Editing](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/real-time-collaboration#operational-transformation)).
- **Page owner:** The person responsible for a page, separate from its creator and transferable, reassigned when the owner leaves ([Docs & Wikis](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/docs-and-wikis#staleness-and-ownership)).
- **Page restriction:** A per-page limit on viewing or editing that can only narrow the container's access; view restrictions inherit down the tree ([Docs & Wikis](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/docs-and-wikis#containers-trees-and-restrictions)).
- **Pooled storage:** Storage sold per seat but consumed as one organization-wide allowance ([Files, Sync & Storage](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/files-and-sync#quotas-and-pooled-storage)).
- **Publish to the web:** Making a document or site readable outside the organization, ideally behind an admin switch ([Docs & Wikis](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/docs-and-wikis#publishing-to-the-web)).
- **Qualified electronic signature (QES):** An eIDAS advanced signature made on a qualified signature creation device with a qualified certificate, equivalent to a handwritten signature across the EU ([E-Signatures & Agreements](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/e-signatures-and-agreements#eidas-three-levels-and-qualified-providers)).
- **Qualified trust service provider (QTSP):** A provider audited and granted qualified status by a national authority, listed on an EU trusted list ([E-Signatures & Agreements](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/e-signatures-and-agreements#eidas-three-levels-and-qualified-providers)).
- **Remote online notarization (RON):** Notarization of an electronic document by a commissioned notary for a signer on a recorded video call ([E-Signatures & Agreements](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/e-signatures-and-agreements#remote-online-notarization)).
- **Rewind (point-in-time restore):** Undoing all file changes in an account or folder back to a chosen moment, the main ransomware recovery tool ([Files, Sync & Storage](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/files-and-sync#versions-deletes-and-ransomware)).
- **Shadow app:** A business application built by employees in a no-code or low-code tool without IT's involvement or visibility ([No-Code Databases & Internal Tools](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/no-code-databases-and-internal-tools#governance-and-shadow-apps)).
- **Shared drive:** A file container owned by a team or the organization rather than a member, so files outlive any one account ([Files, Sync & Storage](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/files-and-sync#shared-drives-and-who-owns-the-file)).
- **Signing group:** A shared e-signature recipient, any one member of which may sign for the organization ([E-Signatures & Agreements](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/e-signatures-and-agreements#the-sender-s-account)).
- **Synced tree:** A sync engine's record of the last state known to match on both client and server, used as a merge base ([Files, Sync & Storage](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/files-and-sync#the-sync-engine)).
- **Verification (knowledge):** A named verifier's confirmation that content is current, which expires after an interval and demotes the content ([Intranets & Knowledge Bases](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/intranets-and-knowledge#verification-and-expiry)).
- **Version trimming:** Deleting file versions beyond a library's count or age limit, which retention policies and holds override ([Files, Sync & Storage](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/files-and-sync#versions-deletes-and-ransomware)).

## Work Management

- **Automation flow:** a customer-built rule of trigger, conditions, branches and actions that changes work items after an event, metered by plan ([Automation Rules](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/automation-rules#triggers-conditions-and-actions)).
- **Burndown chart:** remaining sprint work plotted against time in the board's estimation statistic, with scope added mid-sprint shown as scope change ([Boards, Sprints & Cycles](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/boards-sprints-and-cycles#the-standard-charts)).
- **Change advisory board (CAB):** the recurring group that reviews and authorizes normal change requests before they are implemented ([IT & Service Management](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/service-management#problems-and-changes)).
- **Closing keyword:** a word such as "fixes" or "closes" before an issue reference in a pull request or commit that closes the issue when the change merges to the default branch ([Development Collaboration](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/development-collaboration#linking-work-to-code)).
- **CMDB:** the configuration management database of configuration items and their relationships, used for impact analysis on incidents and changes ([IT & Service Management](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/service-management#the-cmdb-and-assets)).
- **CODEOWNERS:** a repository file mapping paths to the people or teams whose review is required for changes to them, where the last matching pattern wins ([Development Collaboration](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/development-collaboration#code-owners)).
- **Company-managed space:** a Jira space configured by Jira admins through shared schemes, so one workflow or field change applies to every space using it ([Workflows & Status Machines](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/workflows-and-states#team-managed-and-company-managed)).
- **Cumulative flow diagram:** an area chart of items in each status over time, where a widening band marks a bottleneck ([Boards, Sprints & Cycles](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/boards-sprints-and-cycles#the-standard-charts)).
- **Custom field:** a customer-defined attribute on work items, with contexts, options and per-tenant limits, and costs in validation, indexing and export ([Work Items, Hierarchies & Fields](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/work-items-and-fields#custom-fields)).
- **Cycle (Linear):** an automatic, repeating time-box of one to eight weeks whose unfinished issues roll over by default ([Boards, Sprints & Cycles](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/boards-sprints-and-cycles#linear-cycles)).
- **Cycle time:** the elapsed time between when a work item started and when it finished ([Boards, Sprints & Cycles](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/boards-sprints-and-cycles#flow-metrics-and-their-misuse)).
- **Delivery plan:** an Azure Boards timeline of up to 20 team backlogs with cross-team dependencies drawn between items ([Portfolios, Roadmaps & Goals](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/portfolios-roadmaps-and-goals#plans-and-portfolios)).
- **Deployment gating:** a pipeline pausing until the linked change request is approved in the service desk ([IT & Service Management](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/service-management#problems-and-changes)).
- **Development panel:** the section of a work item showing linked branches, commits, pull requests, builds and deployments, inferred from the item key in their text ([Development Collaboration](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/development-collaboration#linking-work-to-code)).
- **Flow actor:** the identity an automation rule acts as, such as "Automation for Jira" by default in Jira ([Automation Rules](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/automation-rules#the-rule-actor)).
- **Goal:** an outcome that several projects contribute to, with sub-goals, key results and an on track, at risk or off track status ([Portfolios, Roadmaps & Goals](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/portfolios-roadmaps-and-goals#projects-initiatives-and-goals)).
- **Impact and urgency matrix:** the lookup that computes an incident's priority from how much it affects and how soon it hurts ([IT & Service Management](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/service-management#incidents-and-priority)).
- **Initiative (Linear):** a grouping of projects around a company objective, with sub-initiatives and status updates ([Portfolios, Roadmaps & Goals](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/portfolios-roadmaps-and-goals#projects-initiatives-and-goals)).
- **InnerSource:** applying open source development practices inside a company, so any team can read and propose changes to another team's code ([Development Collaboration](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/development-collaboration#inner-source)).
- **JQL:** Jira Query Language, a field-operator-value language with keywords and functions that returns work items and is not SQL ([Queries, Views & Dashboards](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/queries-views-and-dashboards#jql)).
- **Loop detection:** an automation engine's limit on how many times rules may trigger themselves or each other in succession before the chain is stopped ([Automation Rules](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/automation-rules#loops-and-runaway-rules)).
- **Major incident:** an incident causing significant business disruption, run through its own process, queue and manager ([IT & Service Management](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/service-management#incidents-and-priority)).
- **Merge queue:** a mechanism that merges approved pull requests in order, testing each on top of those ahead of it ([Development Collaboration](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/development-collaboration#merge-queues)).
- **Permission-trimmed query:** a query whose results, counts and rollups include only what the viewer may see ([Queries, Views & Dashboards](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/queries-views-and-dashboards#permission-trimmed-queries)).
- **Plan (Jira Plans):** a sandboxed view over boards, spaces and filters for scheduling, capacity and dependencies across teams, formerly Advanced Roadmaps ([Portfolios, Roadmaps & Goals](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/portfolios-roadmaps-and-goals#plans-and-portfolios)).
- **Post function:** a step that runs after a workflow transition, such as setting a field or firing an event ([Workflows & Status Machines](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/workflows-and-states#conditions-validators-and-post-functions)).
- **Request type:** a service desk form with its own fields, workflow and audience that employees use to ask for something ([IT & Service Management](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/service-management#requests-and-queues)).
- **Resolution:** the field recording how a work item ended (done, won't do, duplicate), kept separate from its status ([Workflows & Status Machines](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/workflows-and-states#resolutions)).
- **Saved filter:** a stored query with an owner and an audience, shared without widening anyone's access to the items it returns ([Queries, Views & Dashboards](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/queries-views-and-dashboards#saved-filters-and-views)).
- **SLA clock:** a per-ticket timer that starts, pauses and stops on conditions, runs on a business calendar, and records whether its goal was met ([IT & Service Management](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/service-management#sla-clocks)).
- **Smart Commit:** a command in a commit message (comment, log time or transition) that acts on the Jira work item whose key it names ([Development Collaboration](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/development-collaboration#linking-work-to-code)).
- **Sprint:** a fixed time-box of one month or less with a goal, whose scope and estimates are recorded at start and close ([Boards, Sprints & Cycles](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/boards-sprints-and-cycles#sprints)).
- **State category:** a fixed product-level class (such as proposed, in progress, resolved, completed) that every customer-named status maps to ([Workflows & Status Machines](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/workflows-and-states#statuses-and-categories)).
- **Status update:** an owner's dated note on a project or goal with a health value, collected on a cadence ([Portfolios, Roadmaps & Goals](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/portfolios-roadmaps-and-goals#status-updates)).
- **Team-managed space:** a Jira space set up and maintained by its own team, with a self-contained workflow ([Workflows & Status Machines](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/workflows-and-states#team-managed-and-company-managed)).
- **Transition:** a permitted move between two statuses, with conditions, validators and post functions attached ([Workflows & Status Machines](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/workflows-and-states#transitions)).
- **Triage:** a team inbox where work arriving from outside the team is accepted, declined, merged as a duplicate or snoozed ([Boards, Sprints & Cycles](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/boards-sprints-and-cycles#triage)).
- **Velocity:** the sum of estimates a team completes per sprint, useful for its own capacity and misleading as a performance measure ([Boards, Sprints & Cycles](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/boards-sprints-and-cycles#estimates-and-velocity)).
- **WIP limit:** an advisory maximum (and sometimes minimum) on items in a board column ([Boards, Sprints & Cycles](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/boards-sprints-and-cycles#wip-limits)).
- **WIQL:** Azure Boards' Work Item Query Language, with SELECT, FROM, WHERE, ORDER BY and an ASOF clause for historical queries ([Queries, Views & Dashboards](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/queries-views-and-dashboards#azure-boards-wiql)).

- **Branch (design file):** A separate working copy of a design file or library that reaches the main file only by a reviewed merge ([Design Tools](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/design-tools#files-versions-and-branches)).
- **Brand kit:** Canva's set of approved colors, fonts and logos applied through templates and approvals, its design system for non-designers ([Design Tools](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/design-tools#sketch-penpot-adobe-xd-and-canva)).
- **Code Connect:** Figma's mapping from a design component to the real code component, so Dev Mode shows the codebase's import and usage ([Design Systems & Handoff](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/design-systems-and-handoff#dev-mode-and-handoff)).
- **Component property:** A named, typed way an instance may vary from its master (boolean, instance swap, text, variant, slot) without detaching ([Design Systems & Handoff](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/design-systems-and-handoff#components-variants-and-properties)).
- **Culling:** Skipping the drawing of canvas objects whose bounds fall outside the viewport while keeping them selectable and exportable ([Whiteboards & Diagrams](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/whiteboards-and-diagrams#the-infinite-canvas)).
- **Design Tokens Format Module:** The W3C Design Tokens Community Group's JSON format for tokens, stable since version 2025.10 ([Design Systems & Handoff](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/design-systems-and-handoff#variables-and-tokens)).
- **Detach:** Breaking an instance's link to its library component, after which it no longer receives updates; the main health signal for a design system ([Design Systems & Handoff](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/design-systems-and-handoff#libraries-and-publishing)).
- **Dev Mode:** Figma's inspection mode for engineers, with code, applied variables, measurements, annotations and ready-for-dev status ([Design Systems & Handoff](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/design-systems-and-handoff#dev-mode-and-handoff)).
- **Dev seat:** A Figma seat with full Dev Mode access but no design editing, priced for engineers ([Design Tools](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/design-tools#seats-admins-and-who-decides)).
- **Diagram-as-code:** Writing a diagram as text (Mermaid, PlantUML) rendered by a layout engine, so it lives and diffs with the code ([Whiteboards & Diagrams](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/whiteboards-and-diagrams#diagrams-and-diagram-as-code)).
- **Editable-board limit:** The free-tier cap on how many boards can be edited at once (three on Miro and Mural) ([Whiteboards & Diagrams](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/whiteboards-and-diagrams#plans-limits-and-the-admin)).
- **Facilitator lock:** A whiteboard control that stops participants from moving a board's structure during a session ([Whiteboards & Diagrams](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/whiteboards-and-diagrams#templates-and-facilitation)).
- **Fractional index:** A sibling-order key between two neighbors' keys, so an insert or move changes one object only ([Design Tools](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/design-tools#multiplayer)).
- **Library (design):** A design file whose components, styles and variables are published for other files to use and update from ([Design Systems & Handoff](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/design-systems-and-handoff#libraries-and-publishing)).
- **Library update:** A published change that each consuming file reviews and accepts when it chooses; it is never pushed automatically ([Design Systems & Handoff](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/design-systems-and-handoff#libraries-and-publishing)).
- **Mermaid:** A text syntax and JavaScript renderer for flowcharts, sequence, class, state and other diagrams ([Whiteboards & Diagrams](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/whiteboards-and-diagrams#diagrams-and-diagram-as-code)).
- **Mode (variables):** One of several values a variable holds for a context such as light and dark or a brand ([Design Systems & Handoff](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/design-systems-and-handoff#variables-and-tokens)).
- **Multiplayer:** Figma's term for live co-editing through a server process that owns each open document ([Design Tools](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/design-tools#multiplayer)).
- **Page space:** A canvas's own coordinate system, in which object positions don't change when a viewer pans or zooms ([Whiteboards & Diagrams](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/whiteboards-and-diagrams#the-infinite-canvas)).
- **PlantUML:** A text-to-diagram tool for UML and other notations that uses Graphviz for layout ([Whiteboards & Diagrams](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/whiteboards-and-diagrams#diagrams-and-diagram-as-code)).
- **Presence (canvas):** Live cursors, selections and similar state that sync to collaborators but are never saved ([Whiteboards & Diagrams](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/whiteboards-and-diagrams#object-models)).
- **Ready for dev:** A status a designer sets on a frame or component to tell engineers it can be built, with changes tracked from that version ([Design Systems & Handoff](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/design-systems-and-handoff#dev-mode-and-handoff)).
- **Resolver Module:** The W3C design tokens companion spec that resolves token sets and modifiers into one context, such as a dark theme ([Design Systems & Handoff](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/design-systems-and-handoff#variables-and-tokens)).
- **Style Dictionary:** An open-source build tool that turns token files into platform code (CSS, iOS, Android) and reads the W3C format ([Design Systems & Handoff](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/design-systems-and-handoff#variables-and-tokens)).
- **Variable (Figma):** A named, typed value (color, number, string, boolean, timing, easing) bound to layer properties, Figma's form of a design token ([Design Systems & Handoff](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/design-systems-and-handoff#variables-and-tokens)).
- **Variable scoping:** Restricting a variable to the properties it may apply to, such as spacing tokens to gaps and padding ([Design Systems & Handoff](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/design-systems-and-handoff#variables-and-tokens)).
- **Version history (design file):** Automatic checkpoints plus named versions of a design file, restorable without losing later work ([Design Tools](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/design-tools#files-versions-and-branches)).

## Search, Integrations and AI

- **ACL sync:** Copying a source system's permissions into a search index, on a schedule or by events ([Enterprise Search](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/enterprise-search#acl-sync-and-its-latency)).
- **Admin consent:** an administrator's approval of an app's or client's permissions on behalf of the whole tenant, required for tenant-wide scopes ([Apps & Marketplaces](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/apps-and-marketplaces#admin-approval-and-consent)).
- **Agent identity:** a principal type for an AI agent, distinct from users and app registrations, that makes its actions attributable and revocable, as in Microsoft Entra Agent ID ([AI Agents, Connectors & MCP](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/ai-agents-and-mcp#agent-identity)).
- **Agent sponsor:** the person accountable for an agent, recorded with its identity and asked to re-justify it ([AI Agents, Connectors & MCP](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/ai-agents-and-mcp#agent-identity)).
- **API controls:** Google Workspace's per-app access levels (trusted, specific data, limited, blocked) and restricted services ([Apps & Marketplaces](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/apps-and-marketplaces#admin-approval-and-consent)).
- **App installation:** one app installed into one workspace or tenant, with its own granted scopes and tokens ([Apps & Marketplaces](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/apps-and-marketplaces#what-an-app-is)).
- **Autoreplay:** Zapier's automatic replay of errored runs on paid plans ([Integration Platforms & Workflow Builders](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/integration-platforms#failure-handling)).
- **CASA:** the App Defense Alliance's Cloud Application Security Assessment, required annually by Google for apps using restricted scopes ([Apps & Marketplaces](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/apps-and-marketplaces#marketplace-review)).
- **Change notification:** a Microsoft Graph delivery telling a subscriber a resource changed, basic (ID only) or rich (with encrypted data) ([APIs, Events & Rate Limits](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/apis-webhooks-and-rate-limits#microsoft-graph-change-notifications)).
- **Client ID Metadata Document:** an HTTPS URL used as an OAuth client ID that points to the client's name and redirect URIs, the registration MCP prefers when a client isn't pre-registered ([AI Agents, Connectors & MCP](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/ai-agents-and-mcp#mcp-authorization)).
- **Connect:** Atlassian's older app framework in which apps run on the developer's servers, being retired in favor of Forge ([Apps & Marketplaces](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/apps-and-marketplaces#hosting-forge-and-the-connect-retirement)).
- **Connection (integration):** a saved credential an integration platform holds to one external account, usually an employee's OAuth grant ([Integration Platforms & Workflow Builders](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/integration-platforms#credential-custody)).
- **Connector data groups:** Power Platform's Business, Non-business and Blocked classes; connectors in different groups can't be combined in one flow ([Integration Platforms & Workflow Builders](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/integration-platforms#governance)).
- **Copilot connector:** Microsoft's connector (formerly Graph connector) that indexes or federates external content for Microsoft Search and Copilot ([Enterprise Search](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/enterprise-search#how-the-platforms-do-it)).
- **Data access governance reports:** SharePoint Advanced Management reports on permission state, broad sharing and sensitivity labels, used for oversharing remediation ([AI Assistants Over Company Data](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/ai-assistants#oversharing)).
- **Delta query:** a Microsoft Graph request that returns only changes since a stored token ([APIs, Events & Rate Limits](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/apis-webhooks-and-rate-limits#request-apis)).
- **Deny precedence:** The rule that a deny entry in an ACL overrides any grant to the same person ([Enterprise Search](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/enterprise-search#the-acl-model)).
- **Document-level security:** Restricting which indexed documents a query can return by the searcher's identity and groups ([Enterprise Search](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/enterprise-search#permission-trimming)).
- **Early binding:** Applying permissions as a filter inside the search query, so counts, facets and pages are correct ([Enterprise Search](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/enterprise-search#permission-trimming)).
- **Embedded iPaaS:** an integration platform a software vendor ships inside its own product under its own brand ([Integration Platforms & Workflow Builders](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/integration-platforms#unified-apis-and-embedded-integration)).
- **External group:** A group from a non-directory source synced as its own object and referenced by item ACLs instead of being expanded ([Enterprise Search](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/enterprise-search#the-acl-model)).
- **Federated search:** Querying each source's own search API at query time and merging the results, with no index of the content ([Enterprise Search](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/enterprise-search#index-or-federate)).
- **Forge:** Atlassian's hosted serverless app platform, with scopes and egress declared in a manifest ([Apps & Marketplaces](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/apps-and-marketplaces#hosting-forge-and-the-connect-retirement)).
- **Grounding:** retrieving tenant content as the asking user and giving it to the model to answer from, with citations ([AI Assistants Over Company Data](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/ai-assistants#how-grounding-works)).
- **Group intersection:** An ACL entry that grants access only to people in all of several groups at once ([Enterprise Search](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/enterprise-search#the-acl-model)).
- **Identity mapping:** Linking each source system's user ids to one directory identity so ACLs can be evaluated ([Enterprise Search](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/enterprise-search#identity-mapping)).
- **Indexed search:** Copying content and permissions into a central index ahead of queries ([Enterprise Search](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/enterprise-search#index-or-federate)).
- **iPaaS:** integration platform as a service, such as Zapier, Make, Workato or Power Automate ([Integration Platforms & Workflow Builders](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/integration-platforms#how-the-platforms-do-it)).
- **Late binding:** Checking permissions on results after the query runs, a costlier fallback for rules a filter can't express ([Enterprise Search](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/enterprise-search#permission-trimming)).
- **Lifecycle notification:** a Graph warning that a subscription or its token is expiring or that an admin revoked the app's access ([APIs, Events & Rate Limits](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/apis-webhooks-and-rate-limits#microsoft-graph-change-notifications)).
- **MCP server:** a vendor-hosted endpoint that lets outside AI clients act in a workspace with the user's permissions ([AI Agents, Connectors & MCP](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/ai-agents-and-mcp#how-the-platforms-do-it)).
- **Model Context Protocol (MCP):** an open protocol for connecting model-driven clients to servers that expose tools, resources and prompts ([AI Agents, Connectors & MCP](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/ai-agents-and-mcp#the-model-context-protocol)).
- **Oversharing:** Content whose permissions are broader than intended, exposed when search or AI makes it findable ([Enterprise Search](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/enterprise-search#oversharing-search-makes-permissions-visible)).
- **Permission trimming:** Showing a searcher only results the source system would let them open ([Enterprise Search](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/enterprise-search#permission-trimming)).
- **Points-based rate limit:** Atlassian's hourly quota in which each request costs points by the objects it touches ([APIs, Events & Rate Limits](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/apis-webhooks-and-rate-limits#rate-limits)).
- **Polling trigger:** an integration trigger that asks the source for new records on a schedule ([Integration Platforms & Workflow Builders](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/integration-platforms#triggers-and-polling)).
- **Power Platform request:** the unit Power Automate meters per user or flow per 24 hours, counting every action including failures and retries ([Integration Platforms & Workflow Builders](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/integration-platforms#billing-units)).
- **Principal set:** The searcher's identity, groups and applicable everyone principals, computed once per query and used as the filter ([Enterprise Search](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/enterprise-search#how-to-build-it)).
- **Prompt injection via shared content:** instructions planted in channels, documents, issues or email that an agent reads and follows ([AI Agents, Connectors & MCP](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/ai-agents-and-mcp#prompt-injection-through-shared-content)).
- **Reciprocal rank fusion:** Combining ranked lists by summing 1/(k + rank) per document, used to merge keyword and vector results ([Enterprise Search](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/enterprise-search#ranking-with-organizational-signals)).
- **Restricted Content Discovery:** A SharePoint site setting that keeps content out of org-wide search and Copilot without changing permissions ([Enterprise Search](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/enterprise-search#oversharing-search-makes-permissions-visible)).
- **Revenue share:** the marketplace operator's cut of paid app sales it bills, such as Atlassian's Forge and Connect rates ([Apps & Marketplaces](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/apps-and-marketplaces#revenue-share-and-billing)).
- **Runs on Atlassian:** Atlassian's badge for Forge apps that use only Atlassian-hosted compute and storage, match residency and let admins control egress ([Apps & Marketplaces](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/apps-and-marketplaces#hosting-forge-and-the-connect-retirement)).
- **Semantic index:** Microsoft's organization-wide index over SharePoint and connector content that grounds Copilot, trimmed to each user's access ([Enterprise Search](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/enterprise-search#oversharing-search-makes-permissions-visible)).
- **Socket Mode:** Slack's delivery of events and interactions over a WebSocket instead of a public Request URL ([APIs, Events & Rate Limits](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/apis-webhooks-and-rate-limits#slack-events-and-socket-mode)).
- **Step-up authorization:** requesting broader OAuth scopes only when a privileged tool is first used, after an `insufficient_scope` challenge ([AI Agents, Connectors & MCP](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/ai-agents-and-mcp#mcp-authorization)).
- **Teamwork Graph:** Atlassian's graph of work objects from its own and connected apps, fed by Forge connectors with permissions ([Enterprise Search](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/enterprise-search#how-the-platforms-do-it)).
- **Token passthrough:** an MCP server forwarding a client's token to an upstream API, forbidden by the specification ([AI Agents, Connectors & MCP](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/ai-agents-and-mcp#mcp-authorization)).
- **Unified API:** one schema per software category (HR, CRM, ATS) over many vendors' APIs, such as Merge ([Integration Platforms & Workflow Builders](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/integration-platforms#unified-apis-and-embedded-integration)).
- **Unlisted app:** a Slack app distributed commercially outside the Marketplace, subject to lower history limits since May 2025 for new installs and since March 3, 2026 for existing ones ([APIs, Events & Rate Limits](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/apis-webhooks-and-rate-limits#the-2025-slack-changes)).
- **Watch channel:** Google Drive's push subscription on a file or the changes feed, expiring in at most a day or a week ([APIs, Events & Rate Limits](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/apis-webhooks-and-rate-limits#google-workspace-push)).
- **XPIA:** cross-prompt injection attack, Microsoft's term for instructions arriving through retrieved content, flagged in Copilot audit records ([AI Agents, Connectors & MCP](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/ai-agents-and-mcp#prompt-injection-through-shared-content)).

## Security and Compliance

- **Approved channels register:** A firm's dated list of communication channels and devices approved for business, which the off-channel cases and MiFID II's device register require ([Regulated Communications & Archiving](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/regulated-communications#the-off-channel-sweep)).
- **Archive connection:** An archiver's admin-approved, workspace-wide feed of messages, edits, deletions, files and recordings, the most privileged app grant in a tenant ([Regulated Communications & Archiving](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/regulated-communications#capture-what-an-archive-needs-from-the-tool)).
- **Audit Logs API:** Slack's Enterprise-only, read-only API at `api.slack.com/audit/v1/` that returns organization audit events to an app the Org Owner installs with the `auditlogs:read` scope ([Audit Logs](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/audit-logs#how-the-platforms-do-it)).
- **Audit (Premium):** Microsoft Purview's higher audit tier, with longer default retention, custom audit retention policies, intelligent insights such as `MailItemsAccessed` properties, and more API bandwidth ([Audit Logs](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/audit-logs#how-the-platforms-do-it)).
- **Bossware:** Dedicated employee monitoring software that scores keyboard and mouse activity, times apps and sites, and takes screenshots or webcam captures ([Employee Monitoring & Privacy](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/employee-monitoring-and-privacy#bossware)).
- **C5:** The German BSI's Cloud Computing Compliance Criteria Catalogue, an audited baseline that also requires disclosure of jurisdiction and data locations ([Certifications & Procurement Assurance](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/compliance-certifications#international-programs)).
- **CAIQ:** The Cloud Security Alliance's yes-or-no questionnaire mapped to its Cloud Controls Matrix, completed for the STAR Registry ([Certifications & Procurement Assurance](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/compliance-certifications#trust-centers-and-questionnaires)).
- **CASB:** A cloud access security broker, a service that discovers the cloud apps an organization's people use and applies data security controls to connected SaaS apps ([DLP & Information Protection](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/dlp-and-information-protection#how-the-platforms-do-it)).
- **CJIS Security Addendum:** The FBI-approved agreement every private contractor processing criminal justice information signs, in place of a certification ([Certifications & Procurement Assurance](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/compliance-certifications#programs-without-a-certificate)).
- **Client-side encryption (CSE):** Google Workspace encryption in which content is encrypted on the user's device with keys from a customer-chosen key service, so Google stores only ciphertext ([Encryption & Customer-Managed Keys](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/encryption-and-customer-keys#what-client-side-and-double-key-encryption-give-up)).
- **Communication Compliance:** Microsoft Purview's supervision tool that applies policies and classifiers to Teams, email, Copilot and imported messages for designated reviewers ([Regulated Communications & Archiving](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/regulated-communications#supervision-and-lexicon-review)).
- **Complementary user entity controls:** Controls a SOC 2 report says the customer must run, in workplace products mostly admin console settings ([Certifications & Procurement Assurance](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/compliance-certifications#what-a-buyer-asks-for)).
- **Content access request:** A reasoned, approved and expiring request to reach a named person's or private container's content, the control works agreements and DPIAs ask for ([Employee Monitoring & Privacy](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/employee-monitoring-and-privacy#product-patterns-that-hold-up)).
- **Crypto-shredding:** Making data unreadable by destroying the keys that encrypt it, used by customer-key products as the proof of deletion at exit ([Encryption & Customer-Managed Keys](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/encryption-and-customer-keys#a-credible-exit)).
- **Custodian:** A person whose data a legal hold preserves, typically a key player in the dispute ([Retention & Legal Hold](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/retention-and-legal-hold#scope-custodians-containers-and-queries)).
- **Customer Key:** Microsoft Purview's customer-managed root key service for Exchange, SharePoint, OneDrive, Teams and Copilot data, using two Azure Key Vault keys and a Microsoft-protected availability key ([Encryption & Customer-Managed Keys](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/encryption-and-customer-keys#how-the-platforms-do-it)).
- **Customer-managed key (CMK):** A root key held in the customer's own cloud KMS that wraps the vendor's data keys, giving the customer a revocation switch and a key-use log; the name Atlassian and Zoom use for their schemes ([Encryption & Customer-Managed Keys](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/encryption-and-customer-keys#what-customer-managed-keys-buy)).
- **Data loss prevention (DLP):** Policies that detect sensitive content or labels in messages, files and shares and warn, block, hide or quarantine them ([DLP & Information Protection](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/dlp-and-information-protection#where-the-check-runs)).
- **Device code phishing:** Tricking a victim into entering an attacker-generated device sign-in code on a genuine sign-in page, which hands the attacker tokens ([Threats & Incidents](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/threats-and-incidents#phishing-for-tokens)).
- **Discovery API:** Slack's Enterprise API for eDiscovery and DLP partners, returning messages, files, canvases and preserved edits and deletions ([eDiscovery & Export](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/ediscovery-and-export#exports-and-apis)).
- **Double Key Encryption (DKE):** Microsoft encryption that needs both a customer-held key and an Azure key to open content, at the cost of search, eDiscovery, co-authoring and Copilot ([Encryption & Customer-Managed Keys](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/encryption-and-customer-keys#what-client-side-and-double-key-encryption-give-up)).
- **EDRM:** The Electronic Discovery Reference Model, the industry's map of discovery stages, revised as EDRM 2.0 in September 2026 ([eDiscovery & Export](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/ediscovery-and-export#the-edrm)).
- **Enterprise Key Management (EKM):** Slack's add-on that encrypts messages, files and the search index with keys in the customer's AWS KMS ([Encryption & Customer-Managed Keys](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/encryption-and-customer-keys#how-the-platforms-do-it)).
- **GCC High:** Microsoft's US government environment for defense contractors and ITAR or CUI data, assessed at FIPS 199 High, with US-screened staff and federation only with itself ([Certifications & Procurement Assurance](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/compliance-certifications#government-clouds)).
- **GovSlack:** Slack's separate US government environment, with its own SOC reports and FedRAMP High authorization ([Certifications & Procurement Assurance](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/compliance-certifications#government-clouds)).
- **HAR file:** A browser recording of network traffic, often uploaded to support, which can contain live session tokens ([Threats & Incidents](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/threats-and-incidents#phishing-for-tokens)).
- **Illicit consent grant:** An attacker's app that a user or admin is tricked into authorizing through a genuine consent screen ([Threats & Incidents](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/threats-and-incidents#oauth-consent-and-app-abuse)).
- **Insider risk management:** Correlating signals such as downloads, DLP matches and HR events about one person into reviewable cases, pseudonymized by default ([DLP & Information Protection](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/dlp-and-information-protection#insider-risk)).
- **IRAP:** Australia's program of ASD-endorsed assessors who assess cloud services against the Information Security Manual, up to PROTECTED ([Certifications & Procurement Assurance](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/compliance-certifications#international-programs)).
- **ISO/IEC 42001:** The management system standard for organizations that develop, provide or use AI systems ([Certifications & Procurement Assurance](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/compliance-certifications#the-iso-family-now-including-privacy-and-ai)).
- **Legal hold:** An instruction that suspends deletion of specified people's or places' content, overriding retention policies, until released ([Retention & Legal Hold](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/retention-and-legal-hold#legal-hold-mechanics)).
- **Lexicon review:** Supervision that flags messages matching keyword lists and patterns for a principal's review ([Regulated Communications & Archiving](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/regulated-communications#supervision-and-lexicon-review)).
- **Litigation hold notice:** The communication telling custodians what to preserve, with acknowledgments and periodic reminders ([Retention & Legal Hold](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/retention-and-legal-hold#notices-reminders-and-release)).
- **MailItemsAccessed:** The Microsoft 365 audit event recording access to mailbox items, whose unexpected AppID revealed the 2023 Storm-0558 intrusion ([Audit Logs](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/audit-logs#the-2023-lesson-logging-as-a-security-baseline)).
- **Modern attachment:** A file shared in a message by link rather than as an attached copy, which keeps changing after the message is sent ([eDiscovery & Export](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/ediscovery-and-export#links-and-modern-attachments)).
- **Off-channel communications:** Business conducted on texting or messaging apps a regulated firm doesn't capture, the subject of the SEC and CFTC sweep from 2021 ([Regulated Communications & Archiving](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/regulated-communications#the-off-channel-sweep)).
- **Policy tip:** The in-app notice DLP shows a user whose action matched a policy, often with an option to override with a justification ([DLP & Information Protection](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/dlp-and-information-protection#false-positives-and-user-friction)).
- **Preservation Lock:** Microsoft's lock that makes a retention policy impossible to disable or weaken, even by global admins ([Retention & Legal Hold](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/retention-and-legal-hold#preservation-lock)).
- **Principles of retention:** Microsoft's published precedence rules: retention over deletion, longest retention, explicit over implicit, shortest deletion ([Retention & Legal Hold](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/retention-and-legal-hold#which-rule-wins)).
- **Privilege log:** The description of withheld privileged material that Rule 26(b)(5)(A) requires, detailed enough for the other side to assess the claim ([eDiscovery & Export](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/ediscovery-and-export#privilege-and-clawback)).
- **Retention label:** A Purview label applied to an individual item that sets how long it is kept, traveling with the item, unlike a location-wide retention policy ([Retention & Legal Hold](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/retention-and-legal-hold#which-rule-wins)).
- **Retention policy:** A customer rule for how long content in a location is kept and what happens when the time ends ([Retention & Legal Hold](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/retention-and-legal-hold#how-the-platforms-do-it)).
- **Review set:** A Purview eDiscovery (Premium) collection of search results copied for review, threading, analytics and export ([eDiscovery & Export](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/ediscovery-and-export#how-the-platforms-do-it)).
- **RSMF:** The Relativity Short Message Format, an email-wrapped zip with a JSON manifest that carries chat conversations into review platforms ([eDiscovery & Export](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/ediscovery-and-export#threading-chat-for-review-rsmf)).
- **Rule 17a-4:** The SEC rule requiring broker-dealers to keep business communications three years, on an audit-trail or non-rewriteable system ([Regulated Communications & Archiving](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/regulated-communications#the-us-recordkeeping-rules)).
- **Rule 37(e):** The federal civil rule on lost electronically stored information: measures to cure prejudice, and adverse inferences or dismissal only on intent to deprive ([Retention & Legal Hold](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/retention-and-legal-hold#rule-37-e-and-spoliation)).
- **Rule 502(d) order:** A federal court order that disclosure in the case doesn't waive privilege, binding in other proceedings ([eDiscovery & Export](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/ediscovery-and-export#privilege-and-clawback)).
- **SCuBA:** CISA's secure configuration baselines for Microsoft 365 and Google Workspace, with the ScubaGear and ScubaGoggles assessment tools ([Threats & Incidents](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/threats-and-incidents#detection)).
- **Sensitivity label:** A clear-text, persistent classification on a file, email, meeting or container that can trigger encryption, markings and sharing limits ([DLP & Information Protection](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/dlp-and-information-protection#sensitivity-labels)).
- **Session binding:** Tying a session or token to a device or network so a copied token fails elsewhere ([Threats & Incidents](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/threats-and-incidents#phishing-for-tokens)).
- **SIEM export:** Streaming or pulling a vendor's audit events into the customer's security information and event management system, the long-term record ([Audit Logs](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/audit-logs#retention-and-the-customer-s-copy)).
- **Simulation mode:** Running a DLP policy without enforcing its actions so its matches can be tuned before it blocks anything ([DLP & Information Protection](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/dlp-and-information-protection#false-positives-and-user-friction)).
- **Spoliation:** The destruction or loss of evidence a party had a duty to preserve ([Retention & Legal Hold](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/retention-and-legal-hold#rule-37-e-and-spoliation)).
- **SubstrateHolds:** The hidden mailbox folder where Microsoft keeps Teams messages that are edited, deleted or expired until retention allows permanent deletion ([Retention & Legal Hold](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/retention-and-legal-hold#deletion-is-a-pipeline)).
- **Technical device (works council):** Under German law, any tool objectively suitable to monitor behavior or performance, whose introduction needs works council agreement ([Employee Monitoring & Privacy](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/employee-monitoring-and-privacy#works-councils-and-microsoft-365)).
- **Token theft:** Replaying a session or refresh token issued to a user who already passed MFA ([Threats & Incidents](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/threats-and-incidents#phishing-for-tokens)).
- **Tombstone:** A placeholder that hides a message or file flagged by DLP until an admin reviews it ([DLP & Information Protection](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/dlp-and-information-protection#where-the-check-runs)).
- **Trust center:** A vendor's page for certifications, policies and subprocessors, with gated reports released under NDA ([Certifications & Procurement Assurance](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/compliance-certifications#trust-centers-and-questionnaires)).
- **Undertaking (17a-4):** A broker-dealer's filing, signed by a designated executive officer or third party, promising to furnish records to the SEC ([Regulated Communications & Archiving](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/regulated-communications#how-the-records-must-be-kept)).
- **Vault:** Google Workspace's retention, hold, search and export tool ([Retention & Legal Hold](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/retention-and-legal-hold#how-the-platforms-do-it)).

## Law and Professional Services

- **Block billing:** combining several tasks in one time entry with a single total, which e-billing audits flag or reject ([Time, Billing & E-Billing](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/time-billing-and-ledes#time-entry)).
- **Client trust account:** a bank account holding client and third-party money separate from the firm's own, as Model Rule 1.15 requires ([Trust Accounting & IOLTA](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/trust-accounting-iolta#whose-money-it-is)).
- **Collection rate:** cash collected divided by the value billed ([Time, Billing & E-Billing](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/time-billing-and-ledes#realization-and-collection)).
- **Commingling:** mixing the firm's own money with client money in trust, including leaving earned fees there ([Trust Accounting & IOLTA](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/trust-accounting-iolta#whose-money-it-is)).
- **Conflict check:** a recorded search of the firm's parties and matters for current, former and prospective client conflicts before a matter opens or a party is added ([Matters, Intake & Conflicts](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/matters-intake-and-conflicts#conflict-search)).
- **Contingent fee:** a fee that is a percentage of the recovery, which Model Rule 1.5(c) requires in a writing signed by the client ([Time, Billing & E-Billing](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/time-billing-and-ledes#rates-and-fee-arrangements)).
- **CTAPP:** California's Client Trust Account Protection Program, requiring yearly trust account registration, certification and self-assessment ([Trust Accounting & IOLTA](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/trust-accounting-iolta#state-rules)).
- **Document management system (DMS):** a matter-organized repository for a firm's documents and filed email, with profiles, versions and wall-aware security ([Legal Documents, DMS & AI](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/legal-documents-and-ai#the-matter-workspace)).
- **E-billing:** submitting invoices to a client's platform as LEDES files, where they are audited against the client's billing guidelines ([Time, Billing & E-Billing](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/time-billing-and-ledes#e-billing-and-outside-counsel-guidelines)).
- **Email filing:** moving relevant email from personal mailboxes into the matter workspace, deduplicated and confirmed ([Legal Documents, DMS & AI](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/legal-documents-and-ai#email-filing)).
- **Engagement letter:** the document setting the scope of a representation and the basis or rate of the fee ([Matters, Intake & Conflicts](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/matters-intake-and-conflicts#engagement-letters)).
- **Ethical wall:** a screen that keeps named people out of a matter (or limits it to a named team) across every system, including search and AI ([Matters, Intake & Conflicts](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/matters-intake-and-conflicts#ethical-walls)).
- **Formal Opinion 512:** the ABA's July 2024 opinion applying competence, confidentiality, communication, supervision and fee rules to generative AI ([Legal Documents, DMS & AI](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/legal-documents-and-ai#generative-ai)).
- **Imputation:** the rule that one lawyer's conflict is attributed to every lawyer in the firm, under Model Rule 1.10 ([Matters, Intake & Conflicts](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/matters-intake-and-conflicts#the-conflict-rules)).
- **IOLTA:** Interest on Lawyers' Trust Accounts, pooled trust accounts for nominal or short-term client funds whose interest funds civil legal aid ([Trust Accounting & IOLTA](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/trust-accounting-iolta#iolta)).
- **LEDES:** the Legal Electronic Data Exchange Standard formats for invoices, budgets and timekeeper data, maintained by the LEDES Oversight Committee ([Time, Billing & E-Billing](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/time-billing-and-ledes#ledes)).
- **LEDES 1998B:** the 24-field pipe-delimited invoice format most US e-billing runs on ([Time, Billing & E-Billing](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/time-billing-and-ledes#ledes-1998b)).
- **Matter:** one engagement for one client, the unit of conflicts, time, documents, trust money and retention ([Law Firms & Professional Services](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/law-firms-and-professional-services#the-matter-centric-model)).
- **Nominal or short-term funds:** client money that cannot earn the client net interest, which goes into IOLTA rather than a separate interest-bearing account ([Trust Accounting & IOLTA](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/trust-accounting-iolta#iolta)).
- **Outside counsel guidelines:** a corporate client's rules for its law firms on billing, staffing, expenses and security ([Time, Billing & E-Billing](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/time-billing-and-ledes#e-billing-and-outside-counsel-guidelines)).
- **Pre-bill:** the draft invoice a responsible lawyer reviews, edits and approves before it goes to the client ([Time, Billing & E-Billing](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/time-billing-and-ledes#pre-bills)).
- **Privilege designation:** document metadata recording attorney-client privilege or work product status, used in review and production ([Legal Documents, DMS & AI](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/legal-documents-and-ai#privilege)).
- **Professional services automation (PSA):** software for agencies and consultancies that plans resources and tracks time and project margin ([Law Firms & Professional Services](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/law-firms-and-professional-services#professional-services-beyond-law)).
- **Prospective client:** a person who consults a lawyer about a possible representation and is protected by Model Rule 1.18 even if no engagement follows ([Matters, Intake & Conflicts](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/matters-intake-and-conflicts#intake-and-prospective-clients)).
- **Realization rate:** billed value divided by the standard value of recorded time ([Time, Billing & E-Billing](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/time-billing-and-ledes#realization-and-collection)).
- **Screened:** isolated from any participation in a matter through timely procedures, as defined in Model Rule 1.0(k) ([Matters, Intake & Conflicts](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/matters-intake-and-conflicts#the-conflict-rules)).
- **Self-learning tool:** in Opinion 512's sense, a GAI tool that uses inputs to improve outputs for others, which requires client informed consent before client information goes in ([Legal Documents, DMS & AI](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/legal-documents-and-ai#generative-ai)).
- **Three-way reconciliation:** proving that the adjusted bank balance, the trust book balance and the total of client ledgers agree, with no negative ledger ([Trust Accounting & IOLTA](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/trust-accounting-iolta#three-way-reconciliation)).
- **Timekeeper:** a person whose time is billed, with a LEDES classification and dated rates, separate from a platform account ([Law Firms & Professional Services](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/law-firms-and-professional-services#how-to-build-it)).
- **UTBMS:** the Uniform Task-Based Management System of task, activity and expense codes used on legal invoices ([Time, Billing & E-Billing](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/time-billing-and-ledes#utbms-codes)).
- **Utilization:** billable hours recorded divided by available working hours ([Time, Billing & E-Billing](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/time-billing-and-ledes#realization-and-collection)).

## Running the Platform

- **Accessibility conformance report (ACR):** A completed VPAT with documented test results, rating each criterion as Supports, Partially Supports, Does Not Support or Not Applicable ([Accessibility & Localization](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/accessibility-and-localization#vpats-and-conformance-reports)).
- **Adoption Score:** Microsoft's organization-level score of how a tenant uses Microsoft 365, in equally weighted categories over a 28-day window ([Workplace Metrics](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/workplace-metrics#adoption-reports-for-the-customer)).
- **App protection policy (MAM):** Intune policy that protects corporate data inside a managed app, with or without device enrollment, including selective wipe ([Desktop & Mobile Clients](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/desktop-and-mobile-clients#mobile-managed-apps-on-unmanaged-phones)).
- **Blocking floor:** The client version below which a vendor refuses to serve a desktop or mobile client, such as Teams' page for clients more than three months old ([Desktop & Mobile Clients](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/desktop-and-mobile-clients#auto-update-and-version-floors)).
- **CART:** Communication Access Real-time Translation, human-generated live captions brought into a meeting by a captioner ([Accessibility & Localization](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/accessibility-and-localization#captions-transcripts-and-interpreters-in-meetings)).
- **Cell (tenant placement):** A slice of the system (servers, caches, databases) serving a fixed set of tenants or a zone, so that a failure stays inside it ([Building It Yourself](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/collaboration-architecture#cells)).
- **Client boot:** The call a chat client makes on connect to load channels, preferences and unread state, and the critical path in both Slack incident reviews ([Reliability, Outages & SLAs](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/reliability-and-outages#what-the-incident-reviews-teach)).
- **Cloud Solution Provider (CSP):** Microsoft's reseller program, with indirect resellers buying through distributors and direct-bill partners buying from Microsoft ([Billing, Seats & Resellers](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/billing-and-seat-management#resellers-and-the-microsoft-csp-channel)).
- **Colo (Figma):** A group of related tables that share a shard key and physical layout so joins and transactions within one key still work ([Building It Yourself](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/collaboration-architecture#how-the-platforms-do-it)).
- **Co-terming:** Aligning the end dates of a customer's subscriptions so they renew together, usually by prorating add-ons to the existing term ([Billing, Seats & Resellers](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/billing-and-seat-management#co-terming)).
- **Domain claiming:** Verifying a corporate email domain and taking control of the accounts on it as managed accounts ([From Self-Serve to Enterprise](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/selling-to-enterprises#domain-capture-and-admin-claiming)).
- **Drain:** Shifting traffic away from a zone or cell quickly and incrementally, as Slack does by reweighting Envoy clusters ([Building It Yourself](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/collaboration-architecture#cells)).
- **Expansion signal:** Product usage or account metadata (domain spread, plan limits, governance interest) that suggests an account is ready for a sales conversation ([From Self-Serve to Enterprise](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/selling-to-enterprises#expansion-signals-and-sales-assist)).
- **Fair Billing Policy:** Slack's rule that members inactive for more than 28 days stop billing, with prorated credits ([Billing, Seats & Resellers](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/billing-and-seat-management#how-the-platforms-do-it)).
- **Large-customer count:** The number of customers above a revenue line (such as $100,000 ARR), the common public proxy for enterprise success ([Workplace Metrics](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/workplace-metrics#how-the-platforms-do-it)).
- **License reclaim:** Finding seats with no recent activity and removing or reassigning them ([Workplace Metrics](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/workplace-metrics#license-utilization-and-reclaim)).
- **Logical shard:** A partition of data (often a schema) mapped onto a smaller number of physical databases, so capacity changes are remaps ([Building It Yourself](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/collaboration-architecture#logical-shards-and-moving-tenants)).
- **Maximum Quantity Billing:** Atlassian's monthly billing on the highest seat count reached during the cycle ([Billing, Seats & Resellers](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/billing-and-seat-management#counting-seats)).
- **Migration mode:** A state of a destination container that accepts back-dated, attributed imports and is explicitly completed, as in the Teams import API ([Migrations, Imports & Switching](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/migrations-and-switching#importers-and-migration-mode)).
- **Post-incident review (PIR):** The vendor's published account of an incident's cause, timeline and fixes; Microsoft commits to a preliminary PIR within 48 hours for broad incidents ([Reliability, Outages & SLAs](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/reliability-and-outages#status-service-health-and-the-admin)).
- **Private offer:** A negotiated price for one customer transacted through a cloud marketplace, with listing fees by contract value ([Billing, Seats & Resellers](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/billing-and-seat-management#cloud-marketplaces)).
- **Proration:** Charging or crediting a seat change for the fraction of the period remaining ([Billing, Seats & Resellers](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/billing-and-seat-management#proration)).
- **Scatter query:** A query without the shard key that must ask every shard, cheap while a cache hides it and dangerous when it doesn't ([Building It Yourself](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/collaboration-architecture#choosing-the-shard-key)).
- **Seat ledger:** The append-only record of seat-count changes and their causes from which billable quantities are derived ([Billing, Seats & Resellers](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/billing-and-seat-management#how-to-build-it)).
- **Seat utilization:** Active members as a share of paid seats on a subscription ([Workplace Metrics](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/workplace-metrics#product-metrics-inside-the-vendor)).
- **Service health:** A tenant-scoped incident and advisory feed for admins, alongside the public status page ([Reliability, Outages & SLAs](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/reliability-and-outages#status-service-health-and-the-admin)).
- **Shared container host:** The one tenant that stores a container shared across organizations, which other organizations read through ([Building It Yourself](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/collaboration-architecture#how-to-build-it)).
- **Siloing:** Making a service take traffic only from its own zone and call only servers there, so a zone can be drained ([Building It Yourself](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/collaboration-architecture#cells)).
- **Soft delete (tenant):** Marking a workspace deleted with a delay and purge date so it can be restored, the main change after Atlassian's 2022 outage ([Reliability, Outages & SLAs](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/reliability-and-outages#atlassian-april-2022-deleting-tenants-is-the-dangerous-operation)).
- **Switching (EU Data Act):** A customer's move between data processing services, with contract clocks and, from January 12, 2027, no switching charges ([Migrations, Imports & Switching](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/migrations-and-switching#the-eu-data-act-switching-as-a-duty)).
- **Tenant-to-tenant migration:** Moving users and content between two tenants of the same product after a merger or divestiture ([Migrations, Imports & Switching](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/migrations-and-switching#tenant-to-tenant-mergers-and-divestitures)).
- **True-up:** A periodic count of seats used above a committed quantity, invoiced as overage ([Billing, Seats & Resellers](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/billing-and-seat-management#true-ups-and-tiers)).
- **User-minutes:** Microsoft's SLA unit for collaboration services: incident minutes multiplied by users affected ([Reliability, Outages & SLAs](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/reliability-and-outages#how-the-platforms-do-it)).
- **VPAT:** ITI's Voluntary Product Accessibility Template, in 508, EU, WCAG and INT editions ([Accessibility & Localization](/books/workplace-and-collaboration/accessibility-and-localization#vpats-and-conformance-reports)).
