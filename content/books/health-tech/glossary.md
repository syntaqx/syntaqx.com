Health care borrows its words from medicine, insurance, law and a dozen standards bodies, and the same word shifts with context: a claim is a bill to a payer and an assertion in a FHIR resource, an encounter is a visit to a clinician and a row a billing system can't post without, and a patient is a person to a physician and an animal to a veterinarian (whose client is the owner). Entries are grouped by the part of the volume that explains them, alphabetized within each group, and each is one line and a link to the chapter that owns the idea. The volume's own names for the core records are the ones in [The Core Data Model](/books/health-tech/health-tech-business#the-core-data-model).

## The Business

- **Accounting of disclosures:** the list a patient may request of who outside the practice received their information for purposes other than treatment, payment and operations, over the past six years, answered within 60 days; the `disclosures` table ([The Health Tech Business](/books/health-tech/health-tech-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **Allowed amount:** what a payer's contract or fee schedule says a service is worth; the payer and the patient split it, and the rest of the charge is written off ([The Health Tech Business](/books/health-tech/health-tech-business#fee-for-service-through-insurance)).
- **Appointment:** a booking for a patient at a time with a practitioner and resources; the plan, as distinct from the encounter that happened ([The Health Tech Business](/books/health-tech/health-tech-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **BAA (business associate agreement):** the contract HIPAA requires before PHI is shared with a business associate, listing permitted uses, safeguards, breach reporting, subcontractor terms and return or destruction at termination ([Selling Software into Health Care](/books/health-tech/vendor-compliance-posture#what-a-baa-must-say)).
- **Business associate:** a person or company that creates, receives, maintains or transmits PHI on behalf of a covered entity, including its subcontractors that do the same ([Selling Software into Health Care](/books/health-tech/vendor-compliance-posture#being-a-business-associate)).
- **Capitation:** a fixed payment per member per month to a provider or plan, whether or not the member receives care ([The Health Tech Business](/books/health-tech/health-tech-business#value-based-payment-and-capitation)).
- **Charge:** a billable line from a procedure at the practice's fee, billed to a payer or the guarantor ([The Health Tech Business](/books/health-tech/health-tech-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **Claim:** a bill to one payer for one patient's services under one coverage, sent as an X12 837 ([The Health Tech Business](/books/health-tech/health-tech-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **Clearinghouse:** a company that routes and checks claims, eligibility checks, claim status and remittances between providers and payers ([The Health Tech Landscape](/books/health-tech/health-tech-landscape#revenue-cycle-and-clearinghouses)).
- **Collection ratio:** collections over net production for a period; how much of what the practice earned it actually received ([The Practice Business](/books/health-tech/practice-business#production-and-collections)).
- **Collections:** payments received from patients and payers, summed from the ledger ([The Practice Business](/books/health-tech/practice-business#production-and-collections)).
- **Contractual adjustment:** the write-off of the difference between a practice's charge and a payer's allowed amount, agreed by contract (group code CO) ([The Health Tech Business](/books/health-tech/health-tech-business#fee-for-service-through-insurance)).
- **Corporate practice doctrine:** state law barring lay corporations from practicing medicine, dentistry or veterinary medicine or employing licensees to do it, the reason MSOs and DSOs exist ([The Practice Business](/books/health-tech/practice-business#corporate-practice-doctrines)).
- **Coverage:** a patient's insurance under a plan, with member ID, subscriber, relationship and order (primary, secondary) ([The Health Tech Business](/books/health-tech/health-tech-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **Covered entity:** a health plan, a clearinghouse, or a provider that conducts HIPAA standard transactions such as claims electronically ([The Health Tech Business](/books/health-tech/health-tech-business#who-s-who)).
- **DSO (dental service organization):** a company that provides business services to dental practices under management agreements, with licensed dentists owning the clinical entities ([The Practice Business](/books/health-tech/practice-business#dsos-and-msos)).
- **Encounter:** a visit where care was delivered, in person or virtual; what claims and the clinical record run on ([The Health Tech Business](/books/health-tech/health-tech-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **Fee schedule:** a set of fees by procedure code: the practice's own office fees, or a payer's contracted or published amounts ([The Practice Business](/books/health-tech/practice-business#how-to-build-it)).
- **Guarantor:** the person or organization responsible for a patient's bill: the patient, a parent, a spouse, or for a veterinarian the client ([The Health Tech Business](/books/health-tech/health-tech-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **HBNR (Health Breach Notification Rule):** the FTC rule (16 CFR Part 318) requiring vendors of personal health records outside HIPAA to notify people, the FTC and sometimes the media of breaches, including unauthorized sharing ([Selling Software into Health Care](/books/health-tech/vendor-compliance-posture#the-ftc-health-breach-notification-rule)).
- **HITRUST:** a security assessment and certification framework common in health care, in e1, i1 and r2 tiers ([Selling Software into Health Care](/books/health-tech/vendor-compliance-posture#reports-and-certifications)).
- **Ledger entry:** an append-only posting of a charge, payment, adjustment, write-off, refund or transfer to a guarantor's account; balances are sums of entries ([The Health Tech Business](/books/health-tech/health-tech-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **MSO (management services organization):** a company providing the business side of a medical practice under a management agreement while licensees own the practice ([The Practice Business](/books/health-tech/practice-business#dsos-and-msos)).
- **NPI (National Provider Identifier):** the 10-digit identifier with a check digit that HIPAA assigns to health care providers, type 1 for individuals and type 2 for organizations ([The Health Tech Business](/books/health-tech/health-tech-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **Patient:** the person receiving care, or for a veterinarian the animal ([The Health Tech Business](/books/health-tech/health-tech-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **Patient responsibility:** the part of an allowed amount the patient owes under the plan design (deductible, copay, coinsurance), group code PR on the remittance ([The Health Tech Business](/books/health-tech/health-tech-business#fee-for-service-through-insurance)).
- **Payer:** an insurer, government program or plan administrator that pays claims ([The Health Tech Business](/books/health-tech/health-tech-business#who-s-who)).
- **Percentage of collections:** a revenue cycle vendor's fee computed as a share of the payments it collects for the customer ([Business Models for Health Software](/books/health-tech/health-tech-business-models#percentage-of-collections-and-outsourced-rcm)).
- **PHI (protected health information):** individually identifiable health information held by a covered entity or business associate ([Selling Software into Health Care](/books/health-tech/vendor-compliance-posture#being-a-business-associate)).
- **PMPM (per member per month):** a fee or payment computed on the number of covered members each month, as in enterprise telehealth access fees and capitation ([Business Models for Health Software](/books/health-tech/health-tech-business-models#enterprise-and-consumer-telehealth)).
- **Practitioner:** a licensed person who delivers care; the NPI belongs to them ([The Health Tech Business](/books/health-tech/health-tech-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **Practitioner role:** a practitioner at an organization and location, with specialty and taxonomy code; claims name the rendering role ([The Health Tech Business](/books/health-tech/health-tech-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **Procedure:** a service planned or performed, with its CPT, CDT, HCPCS or local code; clinical, as distinct from its charge ([The Health Tech Business](/books/health-tech/health-tech-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **Production:** the practice's fees for completed procedures in a period, gross or net of PPO write-offs and adjustments ([The Practice Business](/books/health-tech/practice-business#production-and-collections)).
- **QHIN (Qualified Health Information Network):** a network designated to exchange records under TEFCA ([The Health Tech Landscape](/books/health-tech/health-tech-landscape#networks-and-exchange)).
- **Recall:** the recurring visit a patient is due for (a cleaning, an annual exam, a vaccine), with a due date computed from the last completed procedure ([The Practice Business](/books/health-tech/practice-business#recall-is-the-revenue-loop)).
- **Remittance:** a payer's payment and explanation across claims, sent as an X12 835 ([The Health Tech Business](/books/health-tech/health-tech-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **Scheduled production:** the fees of treatment-planned procedures attached to scheduled appointments; what the schedule is worth if everyone shows ([The Practice Business](/books/health-tech/practice-business#production-and-collections)).
- **Self-funded plan:** an employer health plan where the employer bears the claims cost and an insurer or TPA administers it under ERISA ([The Health Tech Business](/books/health-tech/health-tech-business#fee-for-service-through-insurance)).
- **Subscriber:** the person who holds the insurance policy a patient is covered under ([The Health Tech Business](/books/health-tech/health-tech-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **Taxonomy code:** a ten-character NUCC code describing a provider's type and specialty ([The Health Tech Business](/books/health-tech/health-tech-business#the-core-data-model)).

## Records and Interoperability

- **A40 (merge patient):** the HL7 v2 ADT event telling a receiver that two patient identifiers now refer to one patient ([HL7 v2 & Interfaces](/books/health-tech/hl7-v2#the-messages-that-run-a-practice)).
- **ACK (acknowledgment):** the HL7 v2 reply that takes custody of a message: AA, AE or AR in original mode, CA, CE or CR for an enhanced-mode commit ([HL7 v2 & Interfaces](/books/health-tech/hl7-v2#acknowledgments)).
- **Addendum:** information added to a signed note under a new date and signature, leaving the original unchanged ([EHRs, PMSs & the Clinical Record](/books/health-tech/ehr-and-pms-data#notes-and-signatures)).
- **ADT (admission, discharge and transfer):** the HL7 v2 patient administration feed carrying registrations, visits, demographics updates and merges ([HL7 v2 & Interfaces](/books/health-tech/hl7-v2#the-messages-that-run-a-practice)).
- **Amendment (HIPAA):** a patient's request to change information in the designated record set, decided within 60 days with one 30-day extension ([EHRs, PMSs & the Clinical Record](/books/health-tech/ehr-and-pms-data#amendments)).
- **Backend Services (SMART):** server-to-server authorization with a signed JWT and system scopes, no user involved ([SMART on FHIR & App Platforms](/books/health-tech/smart-on-fhir#backend-services)).
- **Bridge (dental imaging):** a link that launches imaging software on the same patient from the practice management system, by command line or file ([C-CDA, Documents & Images](/books/health-tech/documents-and-ccda#dental-imaging-and-bridges)).
- **Bulk Data ($export):** FHIR's asynchronous export of many patients' data as NDJSON files, by patient, group or system ([FHIR](/books/health-tech/fhir#bulk-data-export)).
- **C-CDA (Consolidated Clinical Document Architecture):** the US library of CDA XML templates for clinical documents such as the CCD, referral note and discharge summary ([C-CDA, Documents & Images](/books/health-tech/documents-and-ccda#c-cda)).
- **CapabilityStatement:** the FHIR resource a server returns at `/metadata` listing the resources, interactions, searches and guides it supports ([FHIR](/books/health-tech/fhir#resources-and-the-rest-api)).
- **CCD (Continuity of Care Document):** the C-CDA summary of a patient's current state, the document networks return when asked for the record ([C-CDA, Documents & Images](/books/health-tech/documents-and-ccda#c-cda)).
- **CDS Hooks:** an HL7 standard for an EHR to call outside decision support at workflow points such as opening a chart or signing an order, returning cards ([FHIR](/books/health-tech/fhir#cds-hooks)).
- **CDT (Code on Dental Procedures and Nomenclature):** the ADA's copyrighted, yearly-updated HIPAA code set for dental services ([Terminologies & Code Sets](/books/health-tech/terminology-and-coding#cdt)).
- **Chart status (dental):** the state of a charted procedure: treatment planned, complete, existing current or other provider, referred out, or a condition ([EHRs, PMSs & the Clinical Record](/books/health-tech/ehr-and-pms-data#dental-teeth-surfaces-and-status)).
- **CommonWell:** an EHR vendor alliance running a master person index and record locator service, and a designated QHIN ([Networks & Exchange](/books/health-tech/health-information-exchange#kinds-of-networks)).
- **CPT (Current Procedural Terminology):** the AMA's copyrighted code set for physician and professional services, HCPCS Level I ([Terminologies & Code Sets](/books/health-tech/terminology-and-coding#cpt)).
- **Credential service provider (CSP):** a service that proofs a person's identity and issues a credential, such as an IAL2 claims token for TEFCA individual access ([Patient Matching & Identity](/books/health-tech/patient-matching-and-identity#identity-proofing-and-ial2)).
- **CVX:** CDC's code set for vaccines administered, paired with MVX manufacturer codes ([Terminologies & Code Sets](/books/health-tech/terminology-and-coding#the-clinical-code-sets)).
- **Designated record set:** the medical and billing records, and any records used to make decisions about individuals, that HIPAA's access and amendment rights reach ([EHRs, PMSs & the Clinical Record](/books/health-tech/ehr-and-pms-data#the-legal-medical-record)).
- **DFT (detail financial transaction):** the HL7 v2 message that posts charges, payments and adjustments to a billing system ([HL7 v2 & Interfaces](/books/health-tech/hl7-v2#the-messages-that-run-a-practice)).
- **DICOM (Digital Imaging and Communications in Medicine):** the international standard for medical images and their metadata, ISO 12052 ([C-CDA, Documents & Images](/books/health-tech/documents-and-ccda#dicom-pacs-and-dicomweb)).
- **DICOMweb:** DICOM's RESTful services: QIDO-RS to query, WADO-RS to retrieve, STOW-RS to store and UPS-RS for worklists ([C-CDA, Documents & Images](/books/health-tech/documents-and-ccda#dicom-pacs-and-dicomweb)).
- **Direct (secure messaging):** S/MIME-encrypted email between providers' Direct addresses through HISPs, the certified push for referrals ([C-CDA, Documents & Images](/books/health-tech/documents-and-ccda#direct-secure-messaging)).
- **Duplicate (patient):** one person with two records in the same system, fixed by a merge ([Patient Matching & Identity](/books/health-tech/patient-matching-and-identity#duplicates-overlays-and-overlaps)).
- **eHealth Exchange:** a network of networks whose participants, federal agencies among them, sign the DURSA; a designated QHIN ([Networks & Exchange](/books/health-tech/health-information-exchange#kinds-of-networks)).
- **EHR (electronic health record):** the clinical half of a practice's software: problems, medications, allergies, notes, orders and results ([EHRs, PMSs & the Clinical Record](/books/health-tech/ehr-and-pms-data#ehr-pms-or-both)).
- **EHR launch:** a SMART launch from inside the EHR, which passes `iss` and `launch` so the app opens in the clinician's current context ([SMART on FHIR & App Platforms](/books/health-tech/smart-on-fhir#the-two-launches)).
- **EMPI (enterprise master patient index):** the registry linking every local identifier for a patient across an organization's systems to one identity ([Patient Matching & Identity](/books/health-tech/patient-matching-and-identity#the-master-patient-index)).
- **Exchange purpose:** the reason asserted for a network query (treatment, payment, operations, public health, government benefits, individual access), which decides who must answer ([Networks & Exchange](/books/health-tech/health-information-exchange#tefca-as-of-october-2026)).
- **FDI/ISO notation:** two-digit tooth numbering whose first digit is the quadrant, 11 to 48 for permanent teeth and 51 to 85 for primary ([EHRs, PMSs & the Clinical Record](/books/health-tech/ehr-and-pms-data#dental-teeth-surfaces-and-status)).
- **FHIR (Fast Healthcare Interoperability Resources):** HL7's REST standard for health data as typed JSON resources; R4 is the US production version ([FHIR](/books/health-tech/fhir)).
- **HCPCS Level II:** CMS's alphanumeric codes for supplies, equipment, drugs and services outside CPT, updated quarterly ([Terminologies & Code Sets](/books/health-tech/terminology-and-coding#hcpcs-level-ii)).
- **HIE (health information exchange):** a regional or state organization, or the practice generally, of sharing records between organizations ([Networks & Exchange](/books/health-tech/health-information-exchange#kinds-of-networks)).
- **HISP (health information service provider):** the service that sends and receives Direct messages and manages certificates for its users ([C-CDA, Documents & Images](/books/health-tech/documents-and-ccda#direct-secure-messaging)).
- **HL7 v2:** the pipe-delimited event messaging standard that runs most hospital interfaces, used by 95% of US health care organizations in 2024 ([HL7 v2 & Interfaces](/books/health-tech/hl7-v2)).
- **IAL2 (identity assurance level 2):** NIST's remote or on-site identity proofing with stronger evidence and verification, the bar for TEFCA individual access ([Patient Matching & Identity](/books/health-tech/patient-matching-and-identity#identity-proofing-and-ial2)).
- **ICD-10-CM:** the US diagnosis code set maintained by CDC's NCHS, released by fiscal year from October 1 with April updates ([Terminologies & Code Sets](/books/health-tech/terminology-and-coding#icd-10-cm-and-icd-10-pcs)).
- **ICD-10-PCS:** CMS's procedure code set for hospital inpatients ([Terminologies & Code Sets](/books/health-tech/terminology-and-coding#icd-10-cm-and-icd-10-pcs)).
- **Implementation guide (IG):** a package of FHIR profiles, searches and examples that narrows the base standard for a use case, such as US Core ([FHIR](/books/health-tech/fhir#profiles-us-core-and-implementation-guides)).
- **Individual access services (IAS):** TEFCA's exchange purpose letting a person's approved app retrieve their own records after IAL2 proofing ([Networks & Exchange](/books/health-tech/health-information-exchange#individual-access-services)).
- **Interface engine:** middleware that receives, transforms, routes and stores interface messages between systems, such as Rhapsody, Mirth Connect, Iguana or Cloverleaf ([HL7 v2 & Interfaces](/books/health-tech/hl7-v2#interface-engines)).
- **LOINC:** the code system for lab tests, observations and document types ([Terminologies & Code Sets](/books/health-tech/terminology-and-coding#the-clinical-code-sets)).
- **MBI (Medicare Beneficiary Identifier):** Medicare's 11-character random identifier that replaced SSN-based claim numbers ([Patient Matching & Identity](/books/health-tech/patient-matching-and-identity#no-national-identifier)).
- **MLLP (Minimal Lower Layer Protocol):** the TCP framing for HL7 v2: a 0x0B start byte, then 0x1C and 0x0D at the end, with no security of its own ([HL7 v2 & Interfaces](/books/health-tech/hl7-v2#mllp-the-transport)).
- **MPI (master patient index):** the registry of every patient an organization knows, which matches, links and merges them ([Patient Matching & Identity](/books/health-tech/patient-matching-and-identity#the-master-patient-index)).
- **NDC (National Drug Code):** FDA's three-segment drug package identifier, moving to a uniform 12-digit format effective March 7, 2033 ([Terminologies & Code Sets](/books/health-tech/terminology-and-coding#ndc)).
- **Odontogram:** the tooth chart on which dental findings and procedures are recorded by tooth and surface ([EHRs, PMSs & the Clinical Record](/books/health-tech/ehr-and-pms-data#dental-teeth-surfaces-and-status)).
- **ORU (observation result):** the HL7 v2 message carrying lab and other results as OBR and OBX segments ([HL7 v2 & Interfaces](/books/health-tech/hl7-v2#the-messages-that-run-a-practice)).
- **Overlay:** two people's information on one record, the dangerous matching error, fixed by an unmerge and a clinical review ([Patient Matching & Identity](/books/health-tech/patient-matching-and-identity#duplicates-overlays-and-overlaps)).
- **PACS (picture archiving and communication system):** the server that stores medical images and serves them to viewers ([C-CDA, Documents & Images](/books/health-tech/documents-and-ccda#dicom-pacs-and-dicomweb)).
- **Patient discovery:** the first step of a network query, sending demographics so each responder can match and return its local identifier ([Networks & Exchange](/books/health-tech/health-information-exchange#query-push-and-notify)).
- **Plan of care (therapy):** a therapy plan with diagnosis, goals and treatment frequency and duration, which Medicare requires certified and recertified at least every 90 days ([EHRs, PMSs & the Clinical Record](/books/health-tech/ehr-and-pms-data#physical-therapy-the-plan-of-care)).
- **PMS (practice management system):** the business half of a practice's software: schedule, demographics, insurance, charges, claims and the ledger ([EHRs, PMSs & the Clinical Record](/books/health-tech/ehr-and-pms-data#ehr-pms-or-both)).
- **Problem list:** a patient's coded current and past conditions, distinct from the diagnoses billed for one encounter ([EHRs, PMSs & the Clinical Record](/books/health-tech/ehr-and-pms-data#what-the-clinical-record-holds)).
- **Profile (FHIR):** a set of constraints on a resource (required and must-support elements, bound value sets, extensions) ([FHIR](/books/health-tech/fhir#profiles-us-core-and-implementation-guides)).
- **QHIN (Qualified Health Information Network):** a network designated by the RCE to exchange under TEFCA's Common Agreement; 11 as of October 2026 ([Networks & Exchange](/books/health-tech/health-information-exchange#tefca-as-of-october-2026)).
- **RCE (Recognized Coordinating Entity):** The Sequoia Project, which designates QHINs and maintains TEFCA's SOPs under contract with ASTP/ONC ([Networks & Exchange](/books/health-tech/health-information-exchange#tefca-as-of-october-2026)).
- **Record locator service:** a network service that says which members hold records for a person ([Networks & Exchange](/books/health-tech/health-information-exchange#kinds-of-networks)).
- **Referential matching:** patient matching that consults third-party identity data from outside health care to link records ([Patient Matching & Identity](/books/health-tech/patient-matching-and-identity#matching-methods)).
- **RxNorm:** NLM's normalized names for clinical drugs, each with an RXCUI, linking prescriptions to NDCs and commercial drug databases ([Terminologies & Code Sets](/books/health-tech/terminology-and-coding#the-clinical-code-sets)).
- **SIU (scheduling information unsolicited):** the HL7 v2 appointment notifications, S12 booked through S26 no-show ([HL7 v2 & Interfaces](/books/health-tech/hl7-v2#the-messages-that-run-a-practice)).
- **SMART App Launch:** the OAuth 2.0 profile for FHIR apps, covering launch context, v2 scopes, PKCE and discovery; 2.0.0 is the certification baseline ([SMART on FHIR & App Platforms](/books/health-tech/smart-on-fhir)).
- **SNOMED CT:** the clinical terminology for findings, disorders and procedures, distributed in the US by NLM in March and September editions ([Terminologies & Code Sets](/books/health-tech/terminology-and-coding#the-clinical-code-sets)).
- **SOAP note:** the note structure of subjective, objective, assessment and plan, used across medicine, dentistry, therapy and veterinary care ([EHRs, PMSs & the Clinical Record](/books/health-tech/ehr-and-pms-data#notes-and-signatures)).
- **Standalone launch:** a SMART launch outside the EHR, in which the app requests context through scopes such as `launch/patient` ([SMART on FHIR & App Platforms](/books/health-tech/smart-on-fhir#the-two-launches)).
- **Subscription (FHIR):** a client's request to be notified of events on a topic, by rest-hook or websocket, ideally with id-only payloads ([FHIR](/books/health-tech/fhir#subscriptions-being-told)).
- **Taxonomy code:** the NUCC's ten-character code for a provider's type and specialty, used in HIPAA transactions and on the NPI application ([Terminologies & Code Sets](/books/health-tech/terminology-and-coding#the-clinical-code-sets)).
- **TEFCA (Trusted Exchange Framework and Common Agreement):** the federal framework connecting networks through QHINs under one agreement, live since December 2023 ([Networks & Exchange](/books/health-tech/health-information-exchange#tefca-as-of-october-2026)).
- **Universal tooth numbering:** the US system numbering permanent teeth 1 to 32 and primary teeth A to T ([EHRs, PMSs & the Clinical Record](/books/health-tech/ehr-and-pms-data#dental-teeth-surfaces-and-status)).
- **US Core:** the US national FHIR implementation guide expressing USCDI; 6.1.0 was the certification baseline by December 31, 2025 ([FHIR](/books/health-tech/fhir#profiles-us-core-and-implementation-guides)).
- **USCDI (United States Core Data for Interoperability):** ASTP/ONC's versioned list of data classes certified health IT must exchange; v3 is the certification baseline ([EHRs, PMSs & the Clinical Record](/books/health-tech/ehr-and-pms-data#what-the-clinical-record-holds)).
- **Value set:** a purpose-built list of codes from one or more code systems, with a definition and dated expansions ([Terminologies & Code Sets](/books/health-tech/terminology-and-coding#value-sets)).
- **VSAC (Value Set Authority Center):** NLM's repository of public value sets, including CMS's eCQM value sets ([Terminologies & Code Sets](/books/health-tech/terminology-and-coding#value-sets)).
- **X12:** the standards body and format for HIPAA's administrative transactions: 837 claims, 835 remittances, 270/271 eligibility, 276/277 claim status, 278 authorizations ([The X12 Transaction Family](/books/health-tech/revenue-cycle#the-x12-transaction-family)).
- **Z segment:** a locally defined HL7 v2 segment, where each interface keeps the fields the standard doesn't have ([HL7 v2 & Interfaces](/books/health-tech/hl7-v2#anatomy-of-a-message)).

## Rules of the Record

- **Authorization (HIPAA):** a patient's signed permission for a use or disclosure HIPAA doesn't otherwise allow, with required elements and statements, an expiration, and revocable in writing ([Consent, Authorization & Proxies](/books/health-tech/consent-and-authorization#authorization-under-hipaa)).
- **Break-the-glass:** an explicit, time-limited, reviewed grant of emergency access to one patient's record, satisfying the Security Rule's required emergency access procedure ([Security for Health Data](/books/health-tech/health-records-security#access-control-and-break-the-glass)).
- **CHPL (Certified Health IT Product List):** ONC's public list of certified health IT products with their criteria, real world testing and attestations ([Cures Act, Information Blocking & Certification](/books/health-tech/information-blocking-and-certification#the-certification-program)).
- **Consumer health data:** personal information that identifies a consumer's past, present or future health status, regulated outside HIPAA by Washington, Nevada and Connecticut laws ([State Health Privacy & Sensitive Data](/books/health-tech/state-health-privacy#consumer-health-data-laws)).
- **Cyber device:** a medical device with sponsor-validated software that can connect to the internet and could be vulnerable to cybersecurity threats, subject to FD&C Act section 524B ([Security for Health Data](/books/health-tech/health-records-security#medical-device-security)).
- **Designated record set:** the medical and billing records a provider keeps, and any records used to make decisions about individuals; the scope of the right of access ([HIPAA in Practice](/books/health-tech/hipaa-in-practice#the-right-of-access)).
- **EHI (electronic health information):** electronic PHI that would be in a designated record set, excluding psychotherapy notes and litigation material; what information blocking protects ([Cures Act, Information Blocking & Certification](/books/health-tech/information-blocking-and-certification#what-counts-as-ehi)).
- **Emergency access procedure:** the Security Rule's required way to obtain necessary ePHI in an emergency ([Security for Health Data](/books/health-tech/health-records-security#access-control-and-break-the-glass)).
- **HICP (Health Industry Cybersecurity Practices):** HHS 405(d)'s voluntary sector guidance, five threats and ten practices in small and medium-to-large volumes ([Security for Health Data](/books/health-tech/health-records-security#hhs-405-d-hicp-and-the-performance-goals)).
- **HPH CPGs (Healthcare and Public Health Cybersecurity Performance Goals):** HHS's ten essential and ten enhanced voluntary security goals for the sector ([Security for Health Data](/books/health-tech/health-records-security#hhs-405-d-hicp-and-the-performance-goals)).
- **HTI rules (Health Data, Technology, and Interoperability):** ONC's numbered rulemakings for certification and information blocking, HTI-1 (2024) through HTI-5 (final October 2026) ([Cures Act, Information Blocking & Certification](/books/health-tech/information-blocking-and-certification#the-hti-rules)).
- **Information blocking:** a practice by a provider, certified developer or HIN likely to interfere with access, exchange or use of EHI, unless an exception applies ([Cures Act, Information Blocking & Certification](/books/health-tech/information-blocking-and-certification#the-actors)).
- **Information blocking exception:** one of ten regulatory safe harbors in 45 CFR Part 171, such as preventing harm, privacy, infeasibility and manner ([Cures Act, Information Blocking & Certification](/books/health-tech/information-blocking-and-certification#the-ten-exceptions)).
- **Marketing (HIPAA):** a communication encouraging purchase or use of a product or service, needing an authorization unless an exclusion such as treatment or refill reminders applies without third-party pay ([HIPAA in Practice](/books/health-tech/hipaa-in-practice#marketing)).
- **MHMDA (My Health My Data Act):** Washington's consumer health data law, with separate consent to collect and share, a signed authorization to sell and a geofence ban ([State Health Privacy & Sensitive Data](/books/health-tech/state-health-privacy#consumer-health-data-laws)).
- **Minimum necessary:** the HIPAA standard limiting PHI used or disclosed to what the purpose needs, with exceptions such as treatment disclosures ([HIPAA in Practice](/books/health-tech/hipaa-in-practice#tpo-minimum-necessary-and-incidental-disclosures)).
- **NPP (notice of privacy practices):** the notice every covered provider gives patients, with a good-faith written acknowledgment; updated by February 16, 2026 ([HIPAA in Practice](/books/health-tech/hipaa-in-practice#the-notice-of-privacy-practices)).
- **Part 2 (42 CFR Part 2):** federal confidentiality rules for substance use disorder treatment records from federally assisted programs, aligned with HIPAA by the 2024 rule ([State Health Privacy & Sensitive Data](/books/health-tech/state-health-privacy#part-2-substance-use-disorder-records)).
- **Personal representative:** someone with legal authority to make health care decisions for an individual, treated as the individual under HIPAA ([Consent, Authorization & Proxies](/books/health-tech/consent-and-authorization#minors-proxies-and-personal-representatives)).
- **Psychotherapy notes:** a mental health professional's separately kept notes of counseling session contents, needing an authorization for most uses and outside the right of access ([State Health Privacy & Sensitive Data](/books/health-tech/state-health-privacy#genetic-mental-health-and-other-sensitive-categories)).
- **Right of access:** a patient's right to a copy of their designated record set within 30 days (one 30-day extension), in the format asked for if readily producible, at a cost-based fee ([HIPAA in Practice](/books/health-tech/hipaa-in-practice#the-right-of-access)).
- **Sensitive services (California):** gender-affirming care, abortion and contraception, whose records California requires EHR businesses to segregate and shield from out-of-state access ([State Health Privacy & Sensitive Data](/books/health-tech/state-health-privacy#california-cmia-and-sensitive-services)).
- **TPO (treatment, payment and health care operations):** the uses and disclosures HIPAA permits without authorization ([HIPAA in Practice](/books/health-tech/hipaa-in-practice#tpo-minimum-necessary-and-incidental-disclosures)).
- **USCDI (United States Core Data for Interoperability):** the versioned floor of data elements certified APIs must support; v3 is the certification baseline from January 1, 2026 ([Cures Act, Information Blocking & Certification](/books/health-tech/information-blocking-and-certification#uscdi)).
- **Wall of shame:** the industry's name for OCR's public breach portal listing every breach of 500 or more individuals ([HIPAA in Practice](/books/health-tech/hipaa-in-practice#breach-notification-and-the-wall-of-shame)).

## Revenue Cycle

- **270/271:** the X12 eligibility inquiry and response: is a member covered, and what does the plan pay for a service type ([Eligibility & Benefits](/books/health-tech/eligibility-and-benefits#the-270-and-271)).
- **276/277:** the X12 claim status inquiry and response, with category codes for received, pending, information requested and finalized ([Denials, Appeals & Claim Status](/books/health-tech/denials-and-appeals#claim-status-276-and-277)).
- **277CA:** the claim acknowledgment saying a clearinghouse or payer accepted a claim into adjudication or rejected it ([Claims](/books/health-tech/claims#submission-and-acknowledgments)).
- **278:** the X12 prior authorization request and response, HIPAA's adopted standard, now giving way to FHIR APIs ([Prior Authorization](/books/health-tech/prior-authorization#the-278-and-the-fax)).
- **835 (ERA, electronic remittance advice):** the X12 payment and remittance advice: what a payer paid, adjusted and assigned to the patient, line by line ([Remittance & Posting](/books/health-tech/remittance-and-payment-posting#the-835)).
- **837P, 837I, 837D:** the X12 professional, institutional and dental claims, version 5010 under HIPAA ([Claims](/books/health-tech/claims#the-837-claim)).
- **999:** the implementation acknowledgment saying an X12 file was or wasn't syntactically valid ([Claims](/books/health-tech/claims#submission-and-acknowledgments)).
- **ABN (advance beneficiary notice, CMS-R-131):** the notice a traditional Medicare patient signs before a service Medicare is expected to deny, so the provider may bill them ([Coding, Documentation & Compliance](/books/health-tech/coding-and-documentation#medical-necessity-ncds-and-lcds)).
- **ACO (accountable care organization):** providers jointly accountable for the cost and quality of an assigned Medicare population, sharing in savings ([Value-Based Care & Quality](/books/health-tech/value-based-care#acos-the-shared-savings-program-reach-and-lead)).
- **Advanced APM (alternative payment model):** a risk-bearing payment model whose qualifying participants are paid from a higher conversion factor from 2026 ([Value-Based Care & Quality](/books/health-tech/value-based-care#alternative-payment-models)).
- **Anti-Kickback Statute (AKS):** the federal crime of paying or receiving anything of value for referrals of federal program business, with regulatory safe harbors ([Coding, Documentation & Compliance](/books/health-tech/coding-and-documentation#anti-kickback-and-stark-for-software)).
- **Attribution:** a program's assignment of patients to the clinician or organization accountable for them, per period ([Value-Based Care & Quality](/books/health-tech/value-based-care#what-the-software-must-capture)).
- **CARC (claim adjustment reason code):** the code saying why a dollar on a remittance wasn't paid, such as 45 for a charge over the fee schedule ([Remittance & Posting](/books/health-tech/remittance-and-payment-posting#carc-rarc-and-group-codes)).
- **CCD+:** the Nacha corporate credit with one addenda record that HIPAA requires for health care EFT, carrying the 835's trace number ([Remittance & Posting](/books/health-tech/remittance-and-payment-posting#eft-and-reassociation)).
- **CDI (clinical documentation integrity):** reviewing records and sending non-leading queries so documentation supports accurate codes ([Coding, Documentation & Compliance](/books/health-tech/coding-and-documentation#documentation-integrity)).
- **Clean claim rate:** claims that pass edits with no manual intervention over claims accepted into the claims tool, in HFMA's definition ([The Revenue Cycle](/books/health-tech/revenue-cycle#the-kpis)).
- **COB (coordination of benefits):** the rules deciding which of a patient's plans pays first, including the birthday rule for children ([Eligibility & Benefits](/books/health-tech/eligibility-and-benefits#coordination-of-benefits)).
- **CRD, DTR, PAS (Da Vinci):** HL7 FHIR guides for discovering whether authorization is required, completing the payer's documentation, and submitting the request ([Prior Authorization](/books/health-tech/prior-authorization#da-vinci-crd-dtr-and-pas)).
- **Crossover:** Medicare forwarding a processed claim to a supplemental payer or Medicaid automatically ([Remittance & Posting](/books/health-tech/remittance-and-payment-posting#secondary-claims)).
- **Days in A/R:** net accounts receivable over average daily net patient service revenue; how long money sits between service and cash ([The Revenue Cycle](/books/health-tech/revenue-cycle#the-kpis)).
- **Denial:** an adjudicated claim line the payer refused to pay, appealed or corrected; distinct from a rejection ([Denials, Appeals & Claim Status](/books/health-tech/denials-and-appeals#denial-categories)).
- **Downgrade (alternate benefit):** a dental plan paying for a procedure at the rate of a cheaper alternative, such as a composite filling at the amalgam rate ([Eligibility & Benefits](/books/health-tech/eligibility-and-benefits#dental-benefits)).
- **E/M (evaluation and management):** visit codes whose level is chosen by medical decision making or total time since 2021 ([Coding, Documentation & Compliance](/books/health-tech/coding-and-documentation#evaluation-and-management)).
- **eCQM (electronic clinical quality measure):** a measure specified to be computed from structured EHR data, published on the eCQI Resource Center ([Value-Based Care & Quality](/books/health-tech/value-based-care#hedis-and-ecqms)).
- **False Claims Act:** federal liability, per claim and trebled, for knowingly submitting or causing false claims, mostly enforced through whistleblower suits ([Coding, Documentation & Compliance](/books/health-tech/coding-and-documentation#the-false-claims-act)).
- **Frequency limitation:** a dental or medical plan's cap on how often a procedure is covered, per year, per period or per tooth ([Eligibility & Benefits](/books/health-tech/eligibility-and-benefits#dental-benefits)).
- **Gold carding:** a state-law exemption from prior authorization for a provider with a high approval rate for a service ([Prior Authorization](/books/health-tech/prior-authorization#industry-commitments-and-gold-carding)).
- **Good faith estimate:** the itemized estimate the No Surprises Act requires for uninsured and self-pay patients within 1 or 3 business days ([Patient Statements, Estimates & Collections](/books/health-tech/patient-financial-experience#the-no-surprises-act)).
- **Group code:** the remittance code saying who bears an adjustment: CO contractual, PR patient, OA other, PI payer-initiated ([Remittance & Posting](/books/health-tech/remittance-and-payment-posting#carc-rarc-and-group-codes)).
- **HCC (hierarchical condition category):** a group of diagnoses that raises a Medicare Advantage risk score ([Value-Based Care & Quality](/books/health-tech/value-based-care#medicare-advantage-and-risk-adjustment)).
- **HEDIS:** NCQA's measure set for health plans, more than 90 measures that commercial and MA contracts pay practices on ([Value-Based Care & Quality](/books/health-tech/value-based-care#hedis-and-ecqms)).
- **LCD (local coverage determination):** a Medicare contractor's coverage policy for its jurisdiction where no national policy exists ([Coding, Documentation & Compliance](/books/health-tech/coding-and-documentation#medical-necessity-ncds-and-lcds)).
- **Medical debt reporting laws:** state laws barring or limiting credit reporting of medical debt, in 15 states and DC as of September 2026 ([Patient Statements, Estimates & Collections](/books/health-tech/patient-financial-experience#medical-debt-and-credit-reporting)).
- **MIPS (Merit-based Incentive Payment System):** Medicare's scoring of clinicians on quality, cost, improvement activities and interoperability, adjusting Part B payments ([Value-Based Care & Quality](/books/health-tech/value-based-care#mips-and-the-quality-payment-program)).
- **Modifier:** a two-character code on a claim line changing what a procedure code means, such as 25 or 59 ([Claims](/books/health-tech/claims#modifiers)).
- **MSSP (Medicare Shared Savings Program):** Medicare's permanent ACO program ([Value-Based Care & Quality](/books/health-tech/value-based-care#acos-the-shared-savings-program-reach-and-lead)).
- **MUE (medically unlikely edit):** CMS's cap on the units of a code reported for one patient on one day ([Claims](/books/health-tech/claims#scrubbing-and-edits)).
- **NCCI (National Correct Coding Initiative):** CMS's quarterly code pair (PTP) and unit (MUE) edits ([Claims](/books/health-tech/claims#scrubbing-and-edits)).
- **NCD (national coverage determination):** CMS's nationwide Medicare coverage policy for a service ([Coding, Documentation & Compliance](/books/health-tech/coding-and-documentation#medical-necessity-ncds-and-lcds)).
- **Operating rules (CAQH CORE):** HIPAA-adopted rules for how parties behave around the standard transactions: real-time eligibility, consistent codes, EFT and ERA matching ([The Revenue Cycle](/books/health-tech/revenue-cycle#operating-rules-and-connectivity)).
- **Patient control number:** the practice's own claim identifier (CLM01), echoed on every acknowledgment and remittance ([Claims](/books/health-tech/claims#from-charges-to-a-claim)).
- **Payer ID:** a payer's routing identifier at a particular clearinghouse; it differs between clearinghouses ([Clearinghouses](/books/health-tech/clearinghouses#payer-ids-and-connectivity)).
- **Predetermination:** a dental pre-treatment estimate from the payer; an estimate, not a permission ([Claims](/books/health-tech/claims#dental-claims)).
- **Prior authorization:** the payer's permission, before a service, that it will consider the service necessary and covered ([Prior Authorization](/books/health-tech/prior-authorization#when-authorization-is-required)).
- **RAC (recovery audit contractor):** Medicare's post-payment auditors, contracted by region ([Denials, Appeals & Claim Status](/books/health-tech/denials-and-appeals#audits-and-recoupments)).
- **RADV (risk adjustment data validation):** CMS's audit of the diagnoses behind Medicare Advantage payments ([Value-Based Care & Quality](/books/health-tech/value-based-care#medicare-advantage-and-risk-adjustment)).
- **RARC (remittance advice remark code):** a code adding detail to an adjustment, such as what was missing ([Remittance & Posting](/books/health-tech/remittance-and-payment-posting#carc-rarc-and-group-codes)).
- **Reassociation:** matching an EFT deposit to its 835 by the TRN trace number and amount ([Remittance & Posting](/books/health-tech/remittance-and-payment-posting#eft-and-reassociation)).
- **Recoupment:** a payer taking back an overpayment on an older claim by reducing a later payment ([Remittance & Posting](/books/health-tech/remittance-and-payment-posting#provider-level-adjustments-and-recoupments)).
- **Rejection:** a claim refused before adjudication, by syntax, clearinghouse or payer edits; fixed and resent, not appealed ([Claims](/books/health-tech/claims#submission-and-acknowledgments)).
- **Revenue cycle:** the chain from registration and eligibility through coding, claims, remittance, denials and patient collection ([The Revenue Cycle](/books/health-tech/revenue-cycle#the-stages)).
- **Service type code:** the X12 code naming what an eligibility inquiry asks about, such as 30 for plan coverage or 35 for dental care ([Eligibility & Benefits](/books/health-tech/eligibility-and-benefits#service-type-codes)).
- **Stark (physician self-referral law):** the strict liability ban on physicians referring Medicare patients to entities they have financial relationships with, unless an exception applies ([Coding, Documentation & Compliance](/books/health-tech/coding-and-documentation#anti-kickback-and-stark-for-software)).
- **Timely filing:** a payer's deadline from the date of service to receive a claim; one calendar year for Medicare ([Claims](/books/health-tech/claims#timely-filing)).
- **Upcoding:** billing a higher-paying code, or adding diagnoses, beyond what the documentation supports ([Coding, Documentation & Compliance](/books/health-tech/coding-and-documentation#the-false-claims-act)).
- **Virtual card payment:** a payer paying a provider with a single-use card the practice runs at its own processing cost; providers may insist on EFT instead ([Remittance & Posting](/books/health-tech/remittance-and-payment-posting#virtual-card-payments)).

## Practices and Patient Engagement

- **8-minute rule:** Medicare's counting of timed therapy codes in 15-minute units from total timed minutes, with 8 minutes the threshold for one unit ([Optometry, PT & Other Specialties](/books/health-tech/specialty-practices#the-8-minute-rule)).
- **Alternate benefit (downgrade):** a dental plan paying for a cheaper procedure than the one performed, such as a tooth-colored filling paid as amalgam, leaving the difference to the patient ([Dental Practice](/books/health-tech/dental-practice#insurance-estimation)).
- **Ambient scribe:** AI that records a clinical visit and drafts a note for the clinician to review and sign ([Practice Analytics & AI Front Desk](/books/health-tech/practice-analytics-and-ai#ambient-scribes)).
- **API Exchange (Dentrix):** Henry Schein One's program for third-party access to Dentrix and Dentrix Ascend, with a security review, an agreement and a program fee ([Integrating with Practice Management Systems](/books/health-tech/pms-integration#henry-schein-one-dentrix-and-ascend)).
- **ASAP list:** patients already scheduled who would take an earlier time, texted when a slot opens ([Scheduling & Online Booking](/books/health-tech/scheduling-and-online-booking#waitlists-and-asap-lists)).
- **Blockout:** time reserved on an operatory or provider schedule for particular appointment types, bookable only by them ([Scheduling & Online Booking](/books/health-tech/scheduling-and-online-booking#blocks-and-ideal-days)).
- **Call pop:** the card that appears when the phone rings, matching the caller's number to patients in the PMS and showing the household, next appointment, recall and balance ([Phones for Practices](/books/health-tech/practice-phones#call-pop-and-patient-context)).
- **Card on file consent:** a guarantor's permission to charge a stored card for balances after insurance, with a cap, an expiry and a notice before each charge ([Payments in the Practice](/books/health-tech/patient-payments#card-on-file-and-the-preauthorized-health-care-transaction)).
- **Case acceptance:** treatment accepted over treatment presented, by value or count; the number dental growth depends on ([Dental Practice](/books/health-tech/dental-practice#treatment-plans-and-case-acceptance)).
- **CDT (Code on Dental Procedures and Nomenclature):** the ADA's licensed dental procedure code set, revised each year by its Code Maintenance Committee ([Dental Practice](/books/health-tech/dental-practice#cdt-and-the-tooth)).
- **Concierge fee:** an annual fee a practice charges for services Medicare and insurers don't cover, such as extra access, while still billing them for covered visits ([In-Office Membership Plans & Cash Pay](/books/health-tech/membership-plans#concierge-fees-and-medicare)).
- **Confidential communications request:** a patient's instruction under 45 CFR 164.522(b) to be contacted by other means or at another place, which every sending system must honor ([Two-Way Texting & Messaging](/books/health-tech/patient-texting#patient-preference-and-confidential-communications)).
- **Confirmation of prescription release:** the signed acknowledgment or delivery evidence an eye care prescriber keeps for at least three years to show it released an eyeglass or contact lens prescription ([Optometry, PT & Other Specialties](/books/health-tech/specialty-practices#the-eyeglass-rule)).
- **Confirmation status:** the appointment's state after a confirmation request (sent, confirmed, not accepted), written back to the PMS for the front desk ([Reminders, Confirmations & Recall](/books/health-tech/reminders-and-recall#confirmations-and-reminder-cadences)).
- **Consumer Review Fairness Act:** the 2016 federal law voiding form-contract clauses that restrict or penalize consumer reviews ([Reviews & Reputation](/books/health-tech/reviews-and-reputation#gag-clauses-and-the-consumer-review-fairness-act)).
- **Deferred interest:** a financing promotion that charges interest from the purchase date on the whole amount if any balance remains when the promotional period ends ([Payments in the Practice](/books/health-tech/patient-payments#patient-financing)).
- **Direct primary care (DPC):** primary care for a fixed periodic fee with no insurance billing; HSA-compatible from 2026 within the IRS caps ([In-Office Membership Plans & Cash Pay](/books/health-tech/membership-plans#direct-primary-care-hsas-and-the-2026-change)).
- **Discount medical plan organization:** a business that sells access to discounts from providers, regulated by many states; membership platforms may administer plans as one ([In-Office Membership Plans & Cash Pay](/books/health-tech/membership-plans#is-it-insurance)).
- **Dispatchable location:** the street address plus suite, floor or room sent with a 911 call from a multi-line phone system ([Phones for Practices](/books/health-tech/practice-phones#911-from-the-practice)).
- **eConnector:** Open Dental's always-on Windows service on the office server that relays its cloud features and Remote API ([Integrating with Practice Management Systems](/books/health-tech/pms-integration#open-dental)).
- **Frequency limit:** a dental plan's cap on how often a procedure is paid, per benefit year or rolling period, which needs the patient's history to apply ([Dental Practice](/books/health-tech/dental-practice#insurance-estimation)).
- **Health care exemption (TCPA):** 47 CFR 64.1200(a)(9)(iv), letting health care providers send certain free informational calls and texts without prior consent, limited to listed purposes, 160 characters and one a day, three a week ([Two-Way Texting & Messaging](/books/health-tech/patient-texting#the-tcpa-healthcare-exemption)).
- **Imaging bridge:** an integration where the dental PMS launches an imaging program with the patient's context while images stay in the imaging system ([Dental Practice](/books/health-tech/dental-practice#imaging-and-bridges)).
- **In-office membership plan:** a practice's own subscription covering listed preventive services plus a discount on others, sold mainly to uninsured patients ([In-Office Membership Plans & Cash Pay](/books/health-tech/membership-plans#four-kinds-of-plan)).
- **Intake packet:** the set of forms sent to a patient before a visit, chosen by appointment type, patient status and location ([Digital Intake, Forms & Consent](/books/health-tech/digital-intake-and-forms#the-intake-packet)).
- **KX modifier threshold:** the annual Medicare outpatient therapy amount above which claims must carry the KX modifier attesting medical necessity; $2,480 for PT and SLP combined in 2026 ([Optometry, PT & Other Specialties](/books/health-tech/specialty-practices#thresholds)).
- **Lab case:** a crown, denture, aligner or other appliance made by a dental lab, tracked from prescription to the seat appointment ([Dental Practice](/books/health-tech/dental-practice#dental-lab-cases)).
- **Late cancellation:** a cancellation inside the practice's policy window, too late to refill the slot ([Reminders, Confirmations & Recall](/books/health-tech/reminders-and-recall#no-shows-economics-and-measurement)).
- **Missed-call text-back:** an automatic text to a caller nobody answered, opening a text conversation ([Phones for Practices](/books/health-tech/practice-phones#missed-calls-and-text-back)).
- **MLTS (multi-line telephone system):** a phone system serving an office's many lines, bound by Kari's Law and dispatchable location rules ([Phones for Practices](/books/health-tech/practice-phones#911-from-the-practice)).
- **Morning huddle:** the daily team review of the schedule annotated with unscheduled treatment, recall, balances and risks ([Practice Analytics & AI Front Desk](/books/health-tech/practice-analytics-and-ai#dashboards-and-the-morning-huddle)).
- **No-show rate:** appointments the patient neither attended nor cancelled, over appointments that reached their start time ([Reminders, Confirmations & Recall](/books/health-tech/reminders-and-recall#no-shows-economics-and-measurement)).
- **Operatory:** a dental treatment room with one chair, a column on the schedule; a schedule of resource kind `operatory` in the core model ([Scheduling & Online Booking](/books/health-tech/scheduling-and-online-booking#the-practice-schedule)).
- **Passive verification:** the Contact Lens Rule's mechanism letting a seller fill a prescription the prescriber doesn't answer within eight business hours ([Optometry, PT & Other Specialties](/books/health-tech/specialty-practices#the-contact-lens-rule)).
- **Patient owner (vet):** a person or organization with a role on an animal patient (owner, co-owner, authorized agent, emergency contact), with ownership dated ([Veterinary Practice](/books/health-tech/veterinary-practice#the-client-and-the-animal)).
- **Pattern (appointment):** an appointment's time units, each marked as needing the provider or only the chair, so one provider can work several operatories at once ([Scheduling & Online Booking](/books/health-tech/scheduling-and-online-booking#the-practice-schedule)).
- **Payer virtual card:** a single-use card number a health plan pays a provider with, carrying a card fee; providers can require EFT instead ([Payments in the Practice](/books/health-tech/patient-payments#payer-virtual-cards)).
- **Perio chart:** a dated set of periodontal measurements (probing depths, bleeding, recession, mobility, furcation) per tooth and site ([Dental Practice](/books/health-tech/dental-practice#periodontal-charting)).
- **PIMS (practice information management system):** veterinary practice management software, such as Cornerstone, ezyVet or AVImark ([Veterinary Practice](/books/health-tech/veterinary-practice#how-the-platforms-do-it)).
- **Plan of care certification:** a physician's or qualifying practitioner's signature on an outpatient therapy plan, renewed at least every 90 days for Medicare ([Optometry, PT & Other Specialties](/books/health-tech/specialty-practices#plan-of-care-and-certification)).
- **Planned appointment:** diagnosed treatment the patient hasn't booked, tracked for follow-up as unscheduled treatment ([Reminders, Confirmations & Recall](/books/health-tech/reminders-and-recall#reactivation-and-unscheduled-treatment)).
- **Practice entity:** the licensed professional organization that owns a practice and is the covered entity, distinct from the group that manages it and the brand it uses ([Multi-Location Groups & Call Centers](/books/health-tech/multi-location-and-call-centers#why-groups-are-shaped-the-way-they-are)).
- **Practice number:** a phone number with a meaning (location, brand, campaign or central line) that decides greeting, first answer and booking scope ([Multi-Location Groups & Call Centers](/books/health-tech/multi-location-and-call-centers#brand-numbers-and-location-numbers)).
- **Preauthorized health care transaction:** Visa's transaction type for charging a patient's card for the balance after insurance adjudicates, under a consent of up to a year ([Payments in the Practice](/books/health-tech/patient-payments#card-on-file-and-the-preauthorized-health-care-transaction)).
- **Predetermination:** a claim submitted before dental treatment asking what the plan would pay; an estimate, not a guarantee ([Dental Practice](/books/health-tech/dental-practice#predeterminations)).
- **Private feedback:** a channel for a patient to tell the practice about a visit privately; offered beside the public review link, not instead of it ([Reviews & Reputation](/books/health-tech/reviews-and-reputation#gating-and-private-feedback)).
- **Reactivation:** outreach to patients with no recent visit and no appointment, recall's last step ([Reminders, Confirmations & Recall](/books/health-tech/reminders-and-recall#reactivation-and-unscheduled-treatment)).
- **Recall type:** a kind of recurring visit (prophy, perio, rabies, annual eye exam) with a default interval and the procedures that reset it ([Reminders, Confirmations & Recall](/books/health-tech/reminders-and-recall#hygiene-recall)).
- **Review gating:** asking patients to rate a visit first and sending only happy ones to public review sites; prohibited by Google's policy ([Reviews & Reputation](/books/health-tech/reviews-and-reputation#gating-and-private-feedback)).
- **Sync agent:** a Windows service installed on a practice's server that reads the PMS database and talks out to a vendor's cloud ([Integrating with Practice Management Systems](/books/health-tech/pms-integration#the-sync-agent)).
- **Text-to-pay:** a payment link sent by SMS or email for a balance, copay or deposit, opening a hosted page ([Payments in the Practice](/books/health-tech/patient-payments#text-to-pay)).
- **Unified API:** one vendor API that reads and writes many PMSs, such as NexHealth's or Sikka's ([Integrating with Practice Management Systems](/books/health-tech/pms-integration#unified-apis-and-middlemen)).
- **Unscheduled treatment:** accepted or presented treatment with no appointment; the list engagement campaigns work ([Dental Practice](/books/health-tech/dental-practice#treatment-plans-and-case-acceptance)).
- **VCPR (veterinarian-client-patient relationship):** the relationship that permits a veterinarian to prescribe, defined federally and by each state, increasingly allowed to start by telehealth ([Veterinary Practice](/books/health-tech/veterinary-practice#the-vcpr-and-telemedicine)).
- **Waitlist:** people without an appointment who want one, notified when a slot opens ([Scheduling & Online Booking](/books/health-tech/scheduling-and-online-booking#waitlists-and-asap-lists)).
- **Wellness program (pet):** a subscription for preventive pet care that isn't insurance, as the NAIC Pet Insurance Model Act defines it ([In-Office Membership Plans & Cash Pay](/books/health-tech/membership-plans#vet-wellness-plans)).
- **Write-back:** a change an integrator asks the PMS to make (a booking, a confirmation, a form, a payment), applied asynchronously and confirmed later ([Integrating with Practice Management Systems](/books/health-tech/pms-integration#writing-back)).

## Care Delivery

- **340B:** the federal program requiring manufacturers to sell covered outpatient drugs to qualifying hospitals and clinics at or below a ceiling price, with bans on duplicate Medicaid discounts and diversion ([Hospitals & Health Systems](/books/health-tech/hospitals-and-health-systems#340b)).
- **Accession number:** the performing lab's or imaging center's own number for an order once it receives the specimen or performs the study ([Labs, Imaging & Diagnostics](/books/health-tech/labs-and-diagnostics#the-order-to-result-loop)).
- **ADT (admit, discharge, transfer):** the stream of registration and patient movement events a hospital's departmental systems subscribe to, usually HL7 v2 ([Hospitals & Health Systems](/books/health-tech/hospitals-and-health-systems#adt-the-hospital-s-event-stream)).
- **Asynchronous care (store and forward):** a questionnaire, photo or message reviewed by a clinician later rather than in a live visit ([Telehealth](/books/health-tech/telehealth#modalities-and-what-each-records)).
- **Audio-only telehealth:** a phone call accepted as a visit under a payer's telehealth rules ([Telehealth](/books/health-tech/telehealth#modalities-and-what-each-records)).
- **CCM (chronic care management):** monthly Medicare-paid care coordination between visits, billed by staff or practitioner time against a consented care plan ([Referrals & Care Coordination](/books/health-tech/referrals-and-care-coordination#care-management-programs)).
- **CDS (clinical decision support):** software that gives a clinician a recommendation, alert or score; excluded from the device definition only when it meets section 520(o)(1)(E)'s criteria ([Digital Health Apps & Devices](/books/health-tech/digital-health-and-devices#clinical-decision-support)).
- **CLIA (Clinical Laboratory Improvement Amendments):** the federal certification every lab testing human specimens needs, from a certificate of waiver to accreditation, with rules on report contents and critical values ([Labs, Imaging & Diagnostics](/books/health-tech/labs-and-diagnostics#clia)).
- **Closed-loop referral:** a referral tracked from order through scheduling and visit until the consult report comes back to the sender ([Referrals & Care Coordination](/books/health-tech/referrals-and-care-coordination#the-referral-loop)).
- **Compendium:** a lab's catalog of orderable tests with codes, specimen requirements and ask-at-order-entry questions, versioned per lab ([Labs, Imaging & Diagnostics](/books/health-tech/labs-and-diagnostics#how-labs-connect)).
- **Compounding (503A, 503B):** a pharmacy (503A) or registered outsourcing facility (503B) preparing a drug rather than dispensing a manufactured product ([E-Prescribing & Pharmacy](/books/health-tech/e-prescribing#compounding-and-the-glp-1-era)).
- **Critical value:** a result indicating an imminently life-threatening condition, which the lab must report immediately and the practice must route to a clinician who acts ([Labs, Imaging & Diagnostics](/books/health-tech/labs-and-diagnostics#critical-values)).
- **Digital therapeutic:** software that is itself the treatment, often an FDA-authorized prescription device ([Digital Health Apps & Devices](/books/health-tech/digital-health-and-devices#digital-therapeutics-and-how-they-get-paid)).
- **Direct secure messaging:** certificate-secured email for health care between Direct addresses through accredited HISPs ([Referrals & Care Coordination](/books/health-tech/referrals-and-care-coordination#direct-messaging)).
- **DTC telehealth (direct to consumer):** cash-pay, usually subscription treatment sold online, with physician-owned medical groups and often the company's own pharmacy ([Telehealth](/books/health-tech/telehealth#direct-to-consumer-telehealth)).
- **E-visit:** a patient-initiated portal exchange billed as online evaluation and management by cumulative time over 7 days (99421 to 99423) ([Patient Portals & Patient Access Apps](/books/health-tech/patient-portals-and-access#portal-messaging-and-e-visits)).
- **EMTALA (Emergency Medical Treatment and Labor Act):** the law requiring hospital EDs to screen and stabilize anyone who comes, regardless of ability to pay, without delaying for payment or insurance questions ([Hospitals & Health Systems](/books/health-tech/hospitals-and-health-systems#emtala)).
- **EPCS (electronic prescribing of controlled substances):** DEA's rules for e-prescribing Schedules II to V: identity proofing, two-factor signing, two-person access control and audited applications; mandatory for Part D at 70% ([E-Prescribing & Pharmacy](/books/health-tech/e-prescribing#epcs-controlled-substances)).
- **General wellness product:** a low-risk product for maintaining a healthy lifestyle that FDA doesn't regulate as a device, under its January 2026 guidance ([Digital Health Apps & Devices](/books/health-tech/digital-health-and-devices#wellness-or-medical-device)).
- **HBNR (Health Breach Notification Rule):** the FTC rule requiring health apps and personal health record vendors outside HIPAA to notify people of breaches, including unauthorized disclosures ([Digital Health Apps & Devices](/books/health-tech/digital-health-and-devices#the-ftc-health-breach-notification-rule)).
- **Hospital at home:** inpatient-level care delivered in the patient's home under Medicare's Acute Hospital Care at Home waiver, extended to September 30, 2030 ([Hospitals & Health Systems](/books/health-tech/hospitals-and-health-systems#inpatient-ambulatory-and-hospital-at-home)).
- **IMLC (Interstate Medical Licensure Compact):** an expedited pathway for physicians to separate full licenses in member states ([Telehealth](/books/health-tech/telehealth#licensure-and-the-compacts)).
- **Information blocking (results release):** under the Cures Act, delaying results or notes to patients without an exception, such as a blanket hold for clinician review ([Labs, Imaging & Diagnostics](/books/health-tech/labs-and-diagnostics#results-release-under-cures)).
- **NCPDP SCRIPT:** the standard for prescription messages between prescribers and pharmacies; versions 2017071 and 2023011 coexist until January 1, 2028 ([E-Prescribing & Pharmacy](/books/health-tech/e-prescribing#the-script-transactions)).
- **NCPDP Telecommunication Standard:** the HIPAA standard for real-time pharmacy claims, version D.0 moving to F6 on August 14, 2027 ([E-Prescribing & Pharmacy](/books/health-tech/e-prescribing#pharmacy-systems-and-claims)).
- **NLC (Nurse Licensure Compact):** one multistate nursing license with the privilege to practice in member states ([Telehealth](/books/health-tech/telehealth#licensure-and-the-compacts)).
- **Observation status:** an outpatient stay in a hospital bed, paid under the outpatient system even overnight ([Hospitals & Health Systems](/books/health-tech/hospitals-and-health-systems#inpatient-ambulatory-and-hospital-at-home)).
- **Patient Access API:** the FHIR API CMS requires impacted payers to offer members for claims, clinical and, from 2027, prior authorization data ([Patient Portals & Patient Access Apps](/books/health-tech/patient-portals-and-access#the-payer-patient-access-api)).
- **PCCP (predetermined change control plan):** FDA authorization in advance for planned changes to an AI-enabled device ([Digital Health Apps & Devices](/books/health-tech/digital-health-and-devices#fda-s-rules-for-software-that-is-a-device)).
- **PDMP (prescription drug monitoring program):** a state database of controlled-substance dispensing that prescribers and pharmacists query ([E-Prescribing & Pharmacy](/books/health-tech/e-prescribing#prescription-drug-monitoring-programs)).
- **Placer and filler numbers:** the ordering system's and the performing system's identifiers for the same order ([Labs, Imaging & Diagnostics](/books/health-tech/labs-and-diagnostics#the-order-to-result-loop)).
- **Price transparency file:** a hospital's machine-readable file of gross, cash and negotiated charges in CMS's template, attested from 2026 ([Hospitals & Health Systems](/books/health-tech/hospitals-and-health-systems#hospital-price-transparency)).
- **Proxy access:** one person using a portal for another, by their own login and a scoped grant ([Patient Portals & Patient Access Apps](/books/health-tech/patient-portals-and-access#proxy-access)).
- **PSYPACT:** the compact giving psychologists authority for interjurisdictional telepsychology (APIT) and a temporary authorization to practice (TAP) ([Telehealth](/books/health-tech/telehealth#licensure-and-the-compacts)).
- **RPM and RTM (remote physiologic and therapeutic monitoring):** monthly Medicare services built from device data days and management time, not telehealth visits ([Telehealth](/books/health-tech/telehealth#remote-patient-and-therapeutic-monitoring)).
- **Ryan Haight Act:** the 2008 law requiring an in-person evaluation before prescribing controlled substances over the internet, waived by DEA's telemedicine flexibilities through December 31, 2026 ([E-Prescribing & Pharmacy](/books/health-tech/e-prescribing#dea-telemedicine-prescribing-status)).
- **SaMD (software as a medical device):** software intended to diagnose, treat, mitigate or prevent disease, regulated by FDA as a device ([Digital Health Apps & Devices](/books/health-tech/digital-health-and-devices#fda-s-rules-for-software-that-is-a-device)).
- **Surescripts:** the national network carrying e-prescriptions, medication history and benefit checks between prescribers and pharmacies ([E-Prescribing & Pharmacy](/books/health-tech/e-prescribing#how-the-platforms-do-it)).
- **TCM (transitional care management):** post-discharge care paid once per transition, with a contact within 2 business days and a visit within 7 or 14 days ([Referrals & Care Coordination](/books/health-tech/referrals-and-care-coordination#care-management-programs)).
- **Two-midnight rule:** Medicare's benchmark that inpatient admission is generally appropriate when the physician expects care to cross two midnights ([Hospitals & Health Systems](/books/health-tech/hospitals-and-health-systems#inpatient-ambulatory-and-hospital-at-home)).

## Payers

- **Accumulator:** a member's running total toward a deductible, out-of-pocket maximum, visit limit or dental maximum for a benefit period, kept as a ledger of entries per claim line ([Inside a Payer](/books/health-tech/payer-operations#accumulators)).
- **Accumulator adjustment program:** a plan or PBM rule that manufacturer copay assistance does not count toward the member's deductible or out-of-pocket maximum ([Pharmacy Benefits & PBMs](/books/health-tech/pharmacy-benefits#copay-assistance-and-accumulators)).
- **Adjudication:** the payer's processing of a claim through edits, eligibility, network, benefits, coding, pricing and cost-sharing to a paid or denied result; reprocessing adds a new adjudication ([Inside a Payer](/books/health-tech/payer-operations#claims-adjudication)).
- **Auto-adjudication:** a claim adjudicated with no human touch; the core admin vendors' headline metric ([Inside a Payer](/books/health-tech/payer-operations#claims-adjudication)).
- **CAQH ProView:** the shared credentialing profile a practitioner enters once and shares with the plans they designate; CAQH rebranded as DataSpring in 2026 ([Credentialing, Enrollment & Provider Data](/books/health-tech/credentialing-and-enrollment#how-the-platforms-do-it)).
- **Coinsurance:** the member's percentage share of the allowed amount after the deductible ([Payers & Plans](/books/health-tech/payers-and-plans#plan-design)).
- **Copay:** a fixed amount the member pays per service type, often outside the deductible ([Payers & Plans](/books/health-tech/payers-and-plans#plan-design)).
- **Credentialing:** a payer's verification of a practitioner's licenses, training, history and sanctions from primary sources, with a committee decision and recredentialing on a cycle ([Credentialing, Enrollment & Provider Data](/books/health-tech/credentialing-and-enrollment#credentialing)).
- **Deductible:** what a member pays each benefit period before the plan pays, individual or family, embedded or aggregate ([Payers & Plans](/books/health-tech/payers-and-plans#plan-design)).
- **ERISA:** the federal law governing private employer benefit plans; it preempts state laws relating to self-funded plans while saving state insurance regulation of insurers ([Payers & Plans](/books/health-tech/payers-and-plans#the-kinds-of-payer)).
- **Formulary:** a plan's list of covered drugs, in tiers, with prior authorization, step therapy and quantity limits, versioned through the year ([Pharmacy Benefits & PBMs](/books/health-tech/pharmacy-benefits#formularies-and-drug-utilization-management)).
- **LEIE (List of Excluded Individuals/Entities):** the HHS OIG list of parties excluded from federal health care programs, whose items and services federal programs won't pay for ([Credentialing, Enrollment & Provider Data](/books/health-tech/credentialing-and-enrollment#credentialing)).
- **MAC (Medicare Administrative Contractor):** the regional contractor that processes Medicare Part A and B claims for a jurisdiction, or DME claims ([Payers & Plans](/books/health-tech/payers-and-plans#the-kinds-of-payer)).
- **Medical loss ratio:** the share of premium an insurer must spend on medical care (80% or 85% by market) or rebate ([Payers & Plans](/books/health-tech/payers-and-plans#plan-design)).
- **Medicare Advantage:** private plans paid per member by CMS that replace traditional Medicare Parts A and B for members who choose them; 55% of eligible beneficiaries in 2026 ([Payers & Plans](/books/health-tech/payers-and-plans#the-kinds-of-payer)).
- **Network:** the set of providers contracted to accept a payer's rates for a plan; participation is per practitioner, location and date ([Payers & Plans](/books/health-tech/payers-and-plans#networks)).
- **Out-of-pocket maximum:** the most a member pays in a benefit period for in-network covered care; $10,600 individual and $21,200 family for 2026 Marketplace plans ([Payers & Plans](/books/health-tech/payers-and-plans#plan-design)).
- **Payer enrollment:** loading a practitioner into a payer's system to be paid under a TIN at a location in a network from an effective date ([Credentialing, Enrollment & Provider Data](/books/health-tech/credentialing-and-enrollment#payer-enrollment)).
- **PBM (pharmacy benefit manager):** the company that runs a plan's drug benefit: pharmacy network, formulary, rebates and real-time pharmacy claims ([Pharmacy Benefits & PBMs](/books/health-tech/pharmacy-benefits#the-parties-and-the-money)).
- **PECOS:** CMS's online Medicare enrollment system for providers and suppliers ([Credentialing, Enrollment & Provider Data](/books/health-tech/credentialing-and-enrollment#medicare-enrollment)).
- **Provider Access API:** the CMS-0057-F FHIR API giving in-network providers with a treatment relationship a member's claims, clinical and prior authorization data, required from January 1, 2027 ([Inside a Payer](/books/health-tech/payer-operations#payer-apis)).
- **Provider directory:** a plan's public list of in-network providers, verified at least every 90 days and updated within 2 business days under the No Surprises Act ([Credentialing, Enrollment & Provider Data](/books/health-tech/credentialing-and-enrollment#provider-directories)).
- **Real-time benefit check:** a query at prescribing time for a drug's coverage, prior authorization need and estimated cost-share, with alternatives, on NCPDP RTPB ([Pharmacy Benefits & PBMs](/books/health-tech/pharmacy-benefits#real-time-benefit-checks)).
- **Rebate:** a manufacturer's payment to a PBM for formulary position, arriving months after the claim ([Pharmacy Benefits & PBMs](/books/health-tech/pharmacy-benefits#the-parties-and-the-money)).
- **Self-funded plan:** an employer plan that pays claims from the employer's own money, administered by an insurer or TPA; 67% of covered workers in 2025 ([Payers & Plans](/books/health-tech/payers-and-plans#the-kinds-of-payer)).
- **Spread pricing:** a PBM charging the plan more for a claim than it pays the pharmacy and keeping the difference ([Pharmacy Benefits & PBMs](/books/health-tech/pharmacy-benefits#the-parties-and-the-money)).
- **TiC (Transparency in Coverage) files:** the machine-readable in-network rate and out-of-network allowed amount files plans must post publicly since July 1, 2022, revised by an October 2026 final rule ([Inside a Payer](/books/health-tech/payer-operations#transparency-in-coverage-files)).
- **TPA (third-party administrator):** a company that processes claims for a self-funded plan without bearing the risk ([Payers & Plans](/books/health-tech/payers-and-plans#the-kinds-of-payer)).
- **Utilization management:** a payer's review of medical necessity before, during or after care: prior authorization, concurrent and retrospective review ([Inside a Payer](/books/health-tech/payer-operations#utilization-management)).

## Pharma and Clinical Trials

- **ADaM (Analysis Data Model):** the CDISC standard for analysis datasets derived from SDTM, anchored by the subject-level ADSL ([Trial Systems & 21 CFR Part 11](/books/health-tech/trial-systems-and-part-11#data-standards-cdash-sdtm-adam-and-define-xml)).
- **AEMS (FDA Adverse Event Monitoring System):** FDA's adverse event database, formerly FAERS, with a public dashboard and quarterly data files ([Pharmacovigilance & Regulatory Submissions](/books/health-tech/pharmacovigilance-and-regulatory#e2b-r3-and-fda-s-adverse-event-system)).
- **Applicable clinical trial:** a trial that must be registered on ClinicalTrials.gov and report results under FDAAA 801 and 42 CFR Part 11 ([Clinical Trials](/books/health-tech/clinical-trials#clinicaltrials-gov)).
- **CDASH:** the CDISC standard for collecting trial data on case report forms, traceable to SDTM ([Trial Systems & 21 CFR Part 11](/books/health-tech/trial-systems-and-part-11#data-standards-cdash-sdtm-adam-and-define-xml)).
- **Copay card:** manufacturer-funded assistance paying a commercially insured patient's cost-share, excluded for federal program beneficiaries under the Anti-Kickback Statute ([Pharma Commercial & Patient Support](/books/health-tech/pharma-commercial#copay-cards-patient-assistance-and-the-anti-kickback-statute)).
- **CRO (contract research organization):** a company a sponsor hires to run trial obligations, subject to the same FDA actions for obligations transferred in writing ([Clinical Trials](/books/health-tech/clinical-trials#sponsors-cros-sites-and-investigators)).
- **CTMS (clinical trial management system):** the system for study and site milestones, enrollment, monitoring and site payments ([Trial Systems & 21 CFR Part 11](/books/health-tech/trial-systems-and-part-11#the-systems)).
- **Day zero:** the first receipt by anyone in a company or its agents of a valid adverse event report, which starts every reporting clock ([Pharmacovigilance & Regulatory Submissions](/books/health-tech/pharmacovigilance-and-regulatory#safety-reporting-clocks)).
- **Decentralized elements:** trial activities performed remotely through telehealth, home visits and local providers, under FDA's September 2024 guidance ([Clinical Trials](/books/health-tech/clinical-trials#econsent-and-decentralized-elements)).
- **E2B(R3):** the ICH message standard for individual case safety reports, required for FDA postmarketing reports from October 1, 2026 ([Pharmacovigilance & Regulatory Submissions](/books/health-tech/pharmacovigilance-and-regulatory#e2b-r3-and-fda-s-adverse-event-system)).
- **eCTD (electronic common technical document):** the dossier format for regulatory submissions, sent as lifecycle sequences; FDA accepts v3.2.2 and v4.0 ([Pharmacovigilance & Regulatory Submissions](/books/health-tech/pharmacovigilance-and-regulatory#ectd-submissions-and-esg-nextgen)).
- **EDC (electronic data capture):** the system where sites enter trial data into electronic case report forms, with audit trails and investigator signatures ([Trial Systems & 21 CFR Part 11](/books/health-tech/trial-systems-and-part-11#the-systems)).
- **eTMF (electronic trial master file):** the repository of a trial's essential documents, organized by the TMF Reference Model ([Trial Systems & 21 CFR Part 11](/books/health-tech/trial-systems-and-part-11#the-systems)).
- **Form FDA 1572:** the investigator statement a sponsor must obtain before an investigator participates, with the investigator's commitments, facilities and IRB ([Clinical Trials](/books/health-tech/clinical-trials#sponsors-cros-sites-and-investigators)).
- **Hub services:** manufacturer-funded support taking a specialty prescription to first fill: benefits investigation, prior authorization help, assistance enrollment and pharmacy referral ([Pharma Commercial & Patient Support](/books/health-tech/pharma-commercial#hub-services-and-patient-support)).
- **ICSR (individual case safety report):** one adverse event case as reported to a regulator, versioned with each follow-up ([Pharmacovigilance & Regulatory Submissions](/books/health-tech/pharmacovigilance-and-regulatory#inside-a-safety-database)).
- **IND (investigational new drug application):** the FDA filing that allows an unapproved drug to be tested in people, in effect 30 days after receipt unless on clinical hold ([Clinical Trials](/books/health-tech/clinical-trials#phases-and-the-ind)).
- **IRB (institutional review board):** the committee of at least five members that approves and oversees research and its consent forms ([Clinical Trials](/books/health-tech/clinical-trials#sponsors-cros-sites-and-investigators)).
- **Open Payments:** CMS's public database of manufacturer payments and transfers of value to physicians, certain other practitioners and teaching hospitals, under the Sunshine Act ([Pharma Commercial & Patient Support](/books/health-tech/pharma-commercial#open-payments)).
- **Part 11:** 21 CFR Part 11, FDA's rule on electronic records and signatures: validation, audit trails, access and signature controls ([Trial Systems & 21 CFR Part 11](/books/health-tech/trial-systems-and-part-11#21-cfr-part-11)).
- **PDMA (Prescription Drug Marketing Act):** the law behind FDA's sample rules: written requests, receipts, annual inventory, loss reporting and lot tracking ([Pharma Commercial & Patient Support](/books/health-tech/pharma-commercial#samples-under-the-pdma)).
- **REMS (Risk Evaluation and Mitigation Strategy):** an FDA-required drug safety program that can require certified prescribers and pharmacies, safe-use evidence, monitoring or a registry ([Pharmacovigilance & Regulatory Submissions](/books/health-tech/pharmacovigilance-and-regulatory#rems-labeling-and-product-identifiers)).
- **RTSM (randomization and trial supply management):** the system that randomizes subjects to blinded arms and manages drug supply; also called IRT ([Trial Systems & 21 CFR Part 11](/books/health-tech/trial-systems-and-part-11#the-systems)).
- **SDTM (Study Data Tabulation Model):** the CDISC standard for organizing trial data in domains for submission, required by FDA and PMDA ([Trial Systems & 21 CFR Part 11](/books/health-tech/trial-systems-and-part-11#data-standards-cdash-sdtm-adam-and-define-xml)).
- **Sponsor:** the company, institution or agency that takes responsibility for a trial and files the IND ([Clinical Trials](/books/health-tech/clinical-trials#sponsors-cros-sites-and-investigators)).
- **Validation:** documented evidence that a regulated system does what it is intended to do, now done risk-based under GAMP 5 and computer software assurance ([Trial Systems & 21 CFR Part 11](/books/health-tech/trial-systems-and-part-11#validation)).

## Beyond the US

- **C5 attestation:** the German BSI cloud security attestation that statutory health insurance actors need for cloud processing of health data, Type 2 from July 1, 2025 ([The EU](/books/health-tech/eu-health-data#national-regimes-germany-and-france)).
- **Caldicott Principles:** the National Data Guardian's eight principles for using confidential information in UK health and care ([Canada & the UK](/books/health-tech/canada-and-uk#uk-data-protection-and-confidentiality)).

- **DSPT (Data Security and Protection Toolkit):** the self-assessment every organisation with access to NHS patient data and systems must complete ([Canada & the UK](/books/health-tech/canada-and-uk#uk-supplier-assurance)).
- **EHDS (European Health Data Space):** the EU regulation in force since March 26, 2025 giving patients rights over electronic health data, regulating EHR systems and governing secondary use, applying in waves to 2035 ([The EU](/books/health-tech/eu-health-data#the-european-health-data-space)).
- **EUDAMED:** the EU medical device database, four of whose modules became mandatory on May 28, 2026 ([The EU](/books/health-tech/eu-health-data#mdr-and-ivdr-for-software)).
- **HDS (hébergeur de données de santé):** the French certification required to host personal health data for others ([The EU](/books/health-tech/eu-health-data#national-regimes-germany-and-france)).
- **Health data access body:** an EHDS authority that issues permits for secondary use of health data in secure processing environments ([The EU](/books/health-tech/eu-health-data#the-european-health-data-space)).
- **MDR (Medical Device Regulation):** Regulation (EU) 2017/745, which decides when software is a medical device and what conformity it needs ([The EU](/books/health-tech/eu-health-data#mdr-and-ivdr-for-software)).
- **MyHealth@EU:** the EU cross-border infrastructure exchanging Patient Summaries and ePrescriptions between member states ([The EU](/books/health-tech/eu-health-data#how-the-platforms-do-it)).
- **NHS login:** the NHS's OpenID Connect identity service for patient-facing apps, with P0, P5 and P9 verification levels ([Canada & the UK](/books/health-tech/canada-and-uk#uk-how-the-nhs-is-organised)).
- **OHIP (Ontario Health Insurance Plan):** Ontario's public health insurance plan, billed by fee code from the Schedule of Benefits ([Canada & the UK](/books/health-tech/canada-and-uk#canada-provincial-plans)).
- **PHIPA:** Ontario's Personal Health Information Protection Act, governing health information custodians and their agents, deemed substantially similar to PIPEDA ([Canada & the UK](/books/health-tech/canada-and-uk#canada-privacy-law)).
- **PIPEDA:** Canada's federal private-sector privacy law for personal information in commercial activities ([Canada & the UK](/books/health-tech/canada-and-uk#canada-privacy-law)).

## Running the Platform

- **A/R tie-out:** the conversion check that the new system's converted balances, credits included, equal the old system's accounts receivable report at the final conversion ([Data Migration & Conversions](/books/health-tech/data-migration-and-conversions#balances-and-claims-in-flight)).
- **AB 3030:** the 2024 California law requiring a disclaimer and a path to a human on generative AI patient communications about clinical information, unless a licensed provider reviewed them ([AI in Health Tech](/books/health-tech/ai-in-health-tech#patient-facing-ai)).
- **Ambient scribe:** software that records a visit, transcribes it and drafts a note for the clinician to edit and sign ([AI in Health Tech](/books/health-tech/ai-in-health-tech#ambient-scribes)).
- **BCA (Business Continuity Access):** Epic's downtime toolset, a read-only copy of the chart and printed downtime reports from designated PCs ([Operations, Support & Downtime](/books/health-tech/health-tech-operations#how-the-platforms-do-it)).
- **Clean claim rate (CL-1):** HFMA's key: claims that pass edits without manual intervention over claims accepted for billing ([Health Tech Metrics](/books/health-tech/health-tech-metrics#revenue-cycle-metrics)).
- **Conversion:** moving a practice's data from one practice management system or EHR to another, by test conversion, mapping and a final conversion ([Data Migration & Conversions](/books/health-tech/data-migration-and-conversions#the-conversion-process)).
- **Data use agreement (DUA):** the contract a limited data set requires, binding the recipient to permitted uses and to not identify or contact anyone ([De-identification, Research & Data Use](/books/health-tech/de-identification-and-data-use#limited-data-sets-and-data-use-agreements)).
- **Days in A/R, net (FM-1):** HFMA's key: net patient receivables over average daily net patient service revenue for the last three months ([Health Tech Metrics](/books/health-tech/health-tech-metrics#revenue-cycle-metrics)).
- **Decision support intervention (DSI):** ONC's term for EHR features that support clinical decisions, evidence-based or predictive; HTI-5 removed the source attribute requirements for them ([AI in Health Tech](/books/health-tech/ai-in-health-tech#decision-support-transparency-and-nondiscrimination)).
- **Designated record set:** the medical and billing records a provider maintains about individuals, which access and amendment rights reach, including after a system switch ([Data Migration & Conversions](/books/health-tech/data-migration-and-conversions#exports-the-law-requires)).
- **Downtime kit:** what a practice works from when its systems are down: printed schedules and reports, contact lists and paper forms ([Operations, Support & Downtime](/books/health-tech/health-tech-operations#downtime-from-the-practice-s-side)).
- **EHI export:** ONC's certification criterion § 170.315(b)(10), single-patient and population export of all electronic health information a product stores ([Data Migration & Conversions](/books/health-tech/data-migration-and-conversions#exports-the-law-requires)).
- **Expert Determination:** HIPAA de-identification by a qualified expert who finds a very small risk of identification by the anticipated recipient and documents why ([De-identification, Research & Data Use](/books/health-tech/de-identification-and-data-use#the-two-methods-as-ocr-reads-them)).
- **FHIR facade:** a FHIR API published over a product's own relational model, with stable IDs, real versions and writes through the domain ([Health Tech Architecture](/books/health-tech/health-tech-architecture#a-fhir-facade-over-your-model)).
- **GRR (gross revenue retention):** a cohort's recurring revenue a year later capped at its starting value, over its starting revenue; excludes expansion ([Health Tech Metrics](/books/health-tech/health-tech-metrics#vendor-metrics)).
- **Inbound message:** an HL7, FHIR, X12 or vendor message stored as received, unique on the sender's ID, before it's parsed and applied ([Health Tech Architecture](/books/health-tech/health-tech-architecture#the-sync-and-integration-layer)).
- **Limited data set:** PHI with 16 kinds of direct identifiers removed but dates, town, state and ZIP kept, usable for research, public health or operations under a DUA ([De-identification, Research & Data Use](/books/health-tech/de-identification-and-data-use#limited-data-sets-and-data-use-agreements)).
- **Location (vendor metric):** a physical office under active subscription; a multi-office customer counts once per location ([Health Tech Metrics](/books/health-tech/health-tech-metrics#how-the-companies-report)).
- **MAP Keys:** HFMA's 29 standard revenue cycle KPIs in five groups ([Health Tech Metrics](/books/health-tech/health-tech-metrics#revenue-cycle-metrics)).
- **NRR (net revenue retention):** a cohort's recurring revenue a year later, expansion included, over its starting revenue; new customers excluded ([Health Tech Metrics](/books/health-tech/health-tech-metrics#vendor-metrics)).
- **PCCP (predetermined change control plan):** a plan, authorized with a device, describing changes that can later be made without a new submission ([AI in Health Tech](/books/health-tech/ai-in-health-tech#clinical-decision-support-and-the-fda)).
- **Practice notice:** a recorded notice to a practice about maintenance, an outage or a security incident, with its deadline and recipients ([Operations, Support & Downtime](/books/health-tech/health-tech-operations#how-to-build-it)).
- **Privacy-preserving record linkage:** joining records about the same person across sources by keyed tokens made where the data is held ([De-identification, Research & Data Use](/books/health-tech/de-identification-and-data-use#tokenization-and-linkage)).
- **Safe Harbor:** HIPAA de-identification by removing 18 kinds of identifiers and having no actual knowledge the rest identifies anyone ([De-identification, Research & Data Use](/books/health-tech/de-identification-and-data-use#the-two-methods-as-ocr-reads-them)).
- **Sale of PHI:** a disclosure for remuneration from or for the recipient, prohibited without authorization except in listed cases ([De-identification, Research & Data Use](/books/health-tech/de-identification-and-data-use#selling-data-and-secondary-use)).
- **Sensitivity label:** a record-level marking (Part 2, reproductive, mental health) checked on every read and outbound path ([Health Tech Architecture](/books/health-tech/health-tech-architecture#segmenting-phi)).
- **Source attributes:** model-card information about predictive decision support that HTI-1 required certified EHRs to expose and HTI-5 removed ([AI in Health Tech](/books/health-tech/ai-in-health-tech#decision-support-transparency-and-nondiscrimination)).
- **Support session:** a time-boxed, reasoned, logged delegation of a staff member into a practice's data, scoped to a ticket and a patient ([Operations, Support & Downtime](/books/health-tech/health-tech-operations#support-access-to-phi)).
- **Sync agent:** software on a practice's server that reads from and writes to its practice management system for a cloud platform ([Health Tech Architecture](/books/health-tech/health-tech-architecture#the-sync-and-integration-layer)).
- **Test conversion:** a full trial conversion into a test database that the practice reviews before the final ([Data Migration & Conversions](/books/health-tech/data-migration-and-conversions#the-conversion-process)).
