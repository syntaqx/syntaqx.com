Advertising borrows its words from media buying, auctions, statistics and privacy law, and the same word shifts with context: an impression is an event to an ad server and a billable unit to a contract, a seat is a buyer's account at an exchange, and "net" means after the platform's fee to a publisher and after the agency's commission to a media buyer. Entries are grouped by the part of the volume that explains them, alphabetized within each group, and each is one line and a link to the chapter that owns the idea; a word with two meanings appears once per meaning, qualified. The volume's own names for the core records (organization, advertiser, ad account, property, ad unit, seller account, buyer seat, insertion order, campaign, line item, creative, deal, ad request, auction, bid, ad event, conversion, delivery day, invoice) are the ones in [The Core Data Model](/books/advertising/advertising-business#the-core-data-model).

## The Business

- **Ad account:** an advertiser's account on one buying platform, in one currency and time zone, operated by the advertiser or an agency ([The Advertising Business](/books/advertising/advertising-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **Ad event:** an append-only delivery record (impression, viewable impression, click, video quartile, completion, reward), timestamped at the event and flagged for invalid traffic ([The Advertising Business](/books/advertising/advertising-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **Ad network:** a company that aggregates many publishers' inventory and sells it as one, such as AdSense or AdMob ([The Advertising Business](/books/advertising/advertising-business#who-s-who)).
- **Ad request:** one request for ads from a property and ad unit, carrying device, coarse location and the privacy signals ([The Advertising Business](/books/advertising/advertising-business#the-life-of-an-impression)).
- **Ad unit:** a placement on a property, with its sizes, formats and position; hierarchical ([The Advertising Business](/books/advertising/advertising-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **Advertiser:** a brand being advertised, the party whose money it is, owned by an organization ([The Advertising Business](/books/advertising/advertising-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **Agency:** a company that plans and buys media for advertisers and operates their ad accounts, usually inside a holding company ([The Advertising Business](/books/advertising/advertising-business#who-s-who)).
- **Agency agreement:** the advertiser's contract with its agency, setting scope, compensation, principal buying, rebate treatment and audit rights ([Agencies & Media Buying](/books/advertising/agencies-and-media-buying#how-agencies-are-paid)).
- **Agent for a disclosed principal:** the default basis on which an agency buys media in the advertiser's name, passing the cost through ([Agencies & Media Buying](/books/advertising/agencies-and-media-buying#agent-and-principal)).
- **Auction:** one competition for one impression opportunity, whoever runs it (an exchange, a publisher ad server, a walled garden), with a type, a floor, a winner and a clearing price ([The Advertising Business](/books/advertising/advertising-business#the-open-web-and-the-walled-gardens)).
- **Bid:** a buyer's offer in an auction, with its seat, deal, price, creative, outcome and loss reason ([The Advertising Business](/books/advertising/advertising-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **Billable count:** one party's daily roll-up of delivery after invalid-traffic filtering, which an invoice line is priced on ([The Advertising Business](/books/advertising/advertising-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **Buyer seat:** a buyer's account at an exchange or SSP, for a DSP, an agency or an advertiser ([The Advertising Business](/books/advertising/advertising-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **Campaign:** a buy with a goal and a budget, under an ad account and optionally an insertion order ([The Advertising Business](/books/advertising/advertising-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **Commerce media:** advertising sold by retailers and other commerce platforms using their shopper data; $63.4 billion of US internet advertising in 2025 by IAB's count ([The Advertising Business](/books/advertising/advertising-business#the-open-web-and-the-walled-gardens)).
- **Contribution ex-TAC:** revenue minus traffic acquisition costs, the non-GAAP measure networks and SSPs report because so much of their gross revenue passes through to publishers ([The Advertising Landscape](/books/advertising/advertising-landscape#reading-advertising-revenue)).
- **Conversion:** an outcome (a purchase, install or lead) reported to or observed by a platform, with its source and the attribution claim on it ([The Advertising Business](/books/advertising/advertising-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **Creative:** an ad, with its format, size or duration, assets, landing URL and review status ([The Advertising Business](/books/advertising/advertising-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **Delivery day:** the `delivery_daily` row: one source's billable counts and cost for a line item or deal on one day ([The Advertising Business](/books/advertising/advertising-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **Direct revenue:** ad revenue the publisher's own team sells under an IO or programmatic guaranteed deal, keeping the whole media amount ([Publishers & Yield](/books/advertising/publishers-and-yield#direct-and-indirect-revenue)).
- **eCPM (effective CPM):** revenue (seller side) or cost (buyer side) per thousand impressions, whatever pricing model the buyer used; the comparison metric across models ([Publishers & Yield](/books/advertising/publishers-and-yield#the-yield-metrics)).
- **Fill rate:** filled ad requests divided by ad requests ([Publishers & Yield](/books/advertising/publishers-and-yield#the-yield-metrics)).
- **Finalized earnings:** a network's month-end settled publisher earnings after its close, which can differ from the daily estimates once invalid traffic is removed ([Publishers & Yield](/books/advertising/publishers-and-yield#payment-thresholds-and-deductions)).
- **Holding company:** a group that owns many advertising and media agencies (Dentsu, Havas, Omnicom, Publicis, WPP) and pools their buying ([The Advertising Landscape](/books/advertising/advertising-landscape#holding-companies)).
- **Hop:** one intermediary node in a supply path or SupplyChain object, each with its own fee and log ([Take Rates & Supply Quality](/books/advertising/take-rates-and-supply-quality#supply-path-optimization)).
- **House ad:** the publisher's own promotion in an ad slot, served when no paid demand fills it and earning nothing ([Publishers & Yield](/books/advertising/publishers-and-yield#from-sponsorship-to-house)).
- **IAB/PwC Internet Advertising Revenue Report:** the annual survey-based estimate of US internet advertising revenue, by format and cross-cutting cut, released each April ([The Advertising Landscape](/books/advertising/advertising-landscape#how-big-the-market-is)).
- **Impression RPM:** earnings per thousand ad impressions, close to eCPM, which falls when low-value slots are added ([Publishers & Yield](/books/advertising/publishers-and-yield#the-yield-metrics)).
- **Indirect revenue:** ad revenue sold through an intermediary's auction or network, received net of its fees ([Publishers & Yield](/books/advertising/publishers-and-yield#direct-and-indirect-revenue)).
- **In-house agency:** an advertiser's own team doing work an outside agency would otherwise do, sometimes including media buying ([Agencies & Media Buying](/books/advertising/agencies-and-media-buying#in-housing)).
- **Insertion order (IO):** the contract for a direct buy between an advertiser or agency and a seller, with deliverables, prices, dates, budget, the standard terms it incorporates and the controlling measurement ([The Advertising Business](/books/advertising/advertising-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **Invoice line:** one amount on an invoice or publisher statement (media, a fee, a revenue share, a credit), with the delivery source and agreement it was priced on ([The Advertising Business](/books/advertising/advertising-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **K2 report:** the ANA-commissioned K2 Intelligence study (2016) that found non-transparent agency practices, including rebates and principal transactions, pervasive in its sample ([Agencies & Media Buying](/books/advertising/agencies-and-media-buying#rebates-and-transparency)).
- **Labor-based fee:** agency compensation computed from hours or staff time at agreed rates ([Agencies & Media Buying](/books/advertising/agencies-and-media-buying#how-agencies-are-paid)).
- **Line item:** the unit of delivery (an ad group, ad set or line item), with its dates, budget, priority, price, targeting, pacing and frequency caps ([The Advertising Business](/books/advertising/advertising-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **Made-for-advertising (MFA) site:** a site built to sell ad impressions bought with cheap paid traffic, marked by high ad density, auto-refresh and templated content, under the ANA, 4A's, WFA and ISBA definition ([Take Rates & Supply Quality](/books/advertising/take-rates-and-supply-quality#made-for-advertising-sites)).
- **Managed ad network:** a network that runs a publisher's ad stack and sells its inventory for a share of revenue, such as Mediavine or Raptive ([Publishers & Yield](/books/advertising/publishers-and-yield#self-managed-or-managed)).
- **Match rate (supply chain audit):** the share of one party's impressions that can be matched to another party's log in a supply chain study ([Take Rates & Supply Quality](/books/advertising/take-rates-and-supply-quality#isba-and-pwc-uk-2020-and-2023)).
- **Media commission:** agency compensation computed as a percentage of media spend on an agreed base ([Agencies & Media Buying](/books/advertising/agencies-and-media-buying#how-agencies-are-paid)).
- **Media plan:** the agency's allocation of a budget to channels, vendors and placements with planned cost, quantity and KPI, before anything is bought ([Agencies & Media Buying](/books/advertising/agencies-and-media-buying#the-agency-s-job)).
- **Micros:** integer millionths of a currency unit, the ad tech convention for prices and bids ([The Advertising Business](/books/advertising/advertising-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **Net revenue (agency):** an agency group's revenue less pass-through costs, mainly media and production billed on to clients ([The Advertising Landscape](/books/advertising/advertising-landscape#reading-advertising-revenue)).
- **Open web:** advertising split across interoperating companies (publisher ad servers, exchanges, DSPs, verification) that talk through standards, as opposed to a walled garden ([The Advertising Business](/books/advertising/advertising-business#the-open-web-and-the-walled-gardens)).
- **Organization:** any company in the chain, holding one or more roles (advertiser, agency, publisher, network, exchange, DSP, measurement vendor) ([The Advertising Business](/books/advertising/advertising-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **Page RPM:** estimated earnings per thousand page views, the publisher's outcome metric ([Publishers & Yield](/books/advertising/publishers-and-yield#the-yield-metrics)).
- **Principal media:** inventory an agency buys for its own account and resells to clients at its own price, rather than buying as their agent ([Agencies & Media Buying](/books/advertising/agencies-and-media-buying#agent-and-principal)).
- **Programmatic:** buying and selling impressions through automated auctions and deals; $162.4 billion of US internet advertising in 2025 by IAB's count ([The Advertising Business](/books/advertising/advertising-business#an-open-auction-programmatic-impression)).
- **Property:** a publisher's site, app, CTV app, podcast, channel, screen network or retail surface ([The Advertising Business](/books/advertising/advertising-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **Publisher:** the owner of an audience and its ad space ([The Advertising Business](/books/advertising/advertising-business#who-s-who)).
- **Rebate (agency volume bonus):** cash or free media a seller gives an agency in return for the spend placed with it ([Agencies & Media Buying](/books/advertising/agencies-and-media-buying#rebates-and-transparency)).
- **Remnant inventory:** impressions left after direct and programmatic sales, sold to networks or as backfill ([Publishers & Yield](/books/advertising/publishers-and-yield#inventory-types)).
- **Revenue share basis:** what a network's percentage applies to, such as gross spend or spend net of the buy-side fee ([Publishers & Yield](/books/advertising/publishers-and-yield#networks-and-revenue-share)).
- **RFP (request for proposals):** the agency's request to sellers for packages and rates against an audience, dates, formats and budget ([Agencies & Media Buying](/books/advertising/agencies-and-media-buying#the-agency-s-job)).
- **Seller account:** a seller ID at an SSP or exchange, the sellers.json row, for a publisher or an intermediary ([The Advertising Business](/books/advertising/advertising-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **Sell-through rate:** reserved impressions as a share of forecasted impressions, reserved meaning booked to guaranteed (standard or sponsorship) line items ([Publishers & Yield](/books/advertising/publishers-and-yield#sell-through)).
- **Supply path:** the ordered chain of seller nodes (ad system and seller ID) an impression passed through from the inventory owner to the buyer ([Take Rates & Supply Quality](/books/advertising/take-rates-and-supply-quality#supply-path-optimization)).
- **Supply path optimization (SPO):** a buyer choosing which exchanges and sellers to buy each publisher's inventory through, preferring fewer hops, lower fees and better quality and dropping duplicate paths ([Take Rates & Supply Quality](/books/advertising/take-rates-and-supply-quality#supply-path-optimization)).
- **Take rate:** the share of a transaction an intermediary keeps ([Take Rates & Supply Quality](/books/advertising/take-rates-and-supply-quality#what-the-studies-measured)).
- **Trading desk:** the programmatic buying unit of an agency or holding company that runs DSP seats, deals and data for clients ([Agencies & Media Buying](/books/advertising/agencies-and-media-buying#trading-desks)).
- **Traffic acquisition cost (TAC):** what a platform pays the publishers and distribution partners whose sites or apps carried its ads; 20.3% of Alphabet's advertising revenues in 2025 ([The Advertising Business](/books/advertising/advertising-business#a-self-serve-search-or-social-click)).
- **TrueAdSpend and TrueCPM:** the ANA benchmark's share of programmatic spend that reaches fraud-free, measurable, viewable impressions on sites that aren't made for advertising, and the cost per thousand of those qualified impressions ([Take Rates & Supply Quality](/books/advertising/take-rates-and-supply-quality#ana-and-tag-trustnet-benchmark-2025-onward)).
- **Unknown delta:** advertiser spend a supply-chain study can't trace to a fee or to the publisher; 15% in ISBA's 2020 study and 3% in its second ([The Advertising Business](/books/advertising/advertising-business#an-open-auction-programmatic-impression)).
- **Walled garden:** a platform that owns the audience, the inventory, the auction, the ad server and the measurement in one company ([The Advertising Business](/books/advertising/advertising-business#the-open-web-and-the-walled-gardens)).

## Ad Serving and Creative

- **Acceptable Ads:** eyeo-administered criteria for ads that participating ad blockers allow by default ([Ad Tags, Rendering & Page Performance](/books/advertising/ad-tags-and-rendering#ad-blocking-and-acceptable-ads)).
- **Account hierarchy:** the platform's nesting of a buy, as campaign, ad group and ad; campaign, ad set and ad; or order, line item and creative ([Campaigns & Line Items](/books/advertising/campaigns-and-line-items#the-hierarchy)).
- **Ad Experience Report:** Google's report of Better Ads Standards violations per site; a site failing for more than 30 days has all its ads removed in Chrome ([Ad Tags, Rendering & Page Performance](/books/advertising/ad-tags-and-rendering#chrome-s-ad-filtering)).
- **Ad refresh:** replacing a slot's ad without a page reload, declared to the ad server ([Ad Tags, Rendering & Page Performance](/books/advertising/ad-tags-and-rendering#refresh)).
- **Ad server:** the system that decides which ad serves and records it; a publisher ad server ranks line items and demand, an advertiser ad server hosts and tracks creatives ([Ad Servers](/books/advertising/ad-servers#publisher-and-advertiser-ad-servers)).
- **Advertiser ad server (third-party ad server, 3PAS):** the advertiser's system that hosts its creatives, serves or tracks them wherever they were bought and counts them independently; under IAB/4A's terms usually the controlling count ([Ad Servers](/books/advertising/ad-servers#publisher-and-advertiser-ad-servers)).
- **Allocation plan:** a compact offline plan (an order and serving rate per contract) that an ad server uses to split impressions among guaranteed contracts ([Forecasting & Avails](/books/advertising/forecasting-and-avails#allocation-plans)).
- **Avails:** future impressions matching a line item's targeting that can be booked without hurting existing reservations ([Forecasting & Avails](/books/advertising/forecasting-and-avails)).
- **Average daily budget:** a daily amount enforced over a longer period, allowing days above it: Google Ads up to twice on a day and 30.4 times in a month, Meta up to 75% over on a day and 7 times in a week ([Campaigns & Line Items](/books/advertising/campaigns-and-line-items#budgets)).
- **Beacon:** a tracking request a counter treats as evidence an event happened, often a 1x1 pixel ([Counting Impressions & Clicks](/books/advertising/counting-impressions-and-clicks#beacons-and-pixels)).
- **Begin to render (BTR):** counting an impression once the creative has loaded and started painting ([Counting Impressions & Clicks](/books/advertising/counting-impressions-and-clicks#begin-to-render)).
- **Better Ads Standards:** the Coalition for Better Ads' list of ad experiences (pop-ups, autoplay video with sound, dense ads and others) that consumer research found most annoying ([Ad Tags, Rendering & Page Performance](/books/advertising/ad-tags-and-rendering#chrome-s-ad-filtering)).
- **Billing close:** the cutoff after which late beacons no longer change a period's billable counts ([Counting Impressions & Clicks](/books/advertising/counting-impressions-and-clicks#late-data-and-the-billing-close)).
- **Bonus impressions:** delivery over the booked goal; with a third-party ad server, capped at 10% without the agency's consent under the IAB/4A's terms ([Pacing & Frequency Capping](/books/advertising/pacing-and-frequency#over-and-under-delivery)).
- **Cache buster:** a random value per serve (`ord=`, `%%CACHEBUSTER%%`) that stops cached responses hiding impressions ([Creative Production, Review & DCO](/books/advertising/creative-production-and-dco#click-trackers-and-macros)).
- **Click macro:** a placeholder (`%%CLICK_URL_UNESC%%`) that the serving ad server replaces with its click-counting URL ([Creative Production, Review & DCO](/books/advertising/creative-production-and-dco#click-trackers-and-macros)).
- **Client-initiated counting:** counting from a request the device sends after render, never at decision or insertion time ([Counting Impressions & Clicks](/books/advertising/counting-impressions-and-clicks#client-initiated-counting)).
- **Competitive exclusion:** a label rule keeping competing advertisers' ads off the same page or pod ([Ad Servers](/books/advertising/ad-servers#eligibility-filters)).
- **Contending line item:** a booked line item competing for the same forecast impressions ([Forecasting & Avails](/books/advertising/forecasting-and-avails#contention)).
- **Count on download (COD):** counting when the ad starts downloading; Google Ad Manager drops it for display on February 17, 2027 ([Counting Impressions & Clicks](/books/advertising/counting-impressions-and-clicks#how-ad-servers-count)).
- **Creative rotation:** the rule for choosing among a winning line item's creatives: even, optimized, weighted or sequential ([Ad Servers](/books/advertising/ad-servers#creative-selection)).
- **Cumulative Layout Shift (CLS):** the Core Web Vitals metric for unexpected movement of page content, which ad slots without reserved space cause ([Ad Tags, Rendering & Page Performance](/books/advertising/ad-tags-and-rendering#layout-shift)).
- **Deduplication (events):** discarding repeat beacons for the same impression or click by its ID, so retries and double fires count once ([Counting Impressions & Clicks](/books/advertising/counting-impressions-and-clicks#deduplication)).
- **Delivery goal:** a quantity promised or capped on a line item (impressions, share of voice), which drives pacing and make-goods ([Campaigns & Line Items](/books/advertising/campaigns-and-line-items#objectives-and-goals)).
- **Delivery grace period:** an extension of a line item's end date during which it serves as fast as possible to finish its goal ([Pacing & Frequency Capping](/books/advertising/pacing-and-frequency#the-end-of-the-flight)).
- **Delivery speed (even, frontloaded, ASAP):** the setting for how fast a line item delivers, from spread across the day's traffic to as fast as possible ([Pacing & Frequency Capping](/books/advertising/pacing-and-frequency#delivery-speed)).
- **Dynamic allocation:** an ad server letting guaranteed line items compete with a temporary CPM, so other demand wins only when it pays more than the guarantee needs ([Ad Servers](/books/advertising/ad-servers#guaranteed-demand-against-the-auction)).
- **Dynamic creative optimization (DCO):** a template filled per impression from a feed row chosen by rules or a model ([Creative Production, Review & DCO](/books/advertising/creative-production-and-dco#dynamic-creative-optimization)).
- **Effective targeting:** a line item's own targeting ANDed with the constraints enforced from its ancestors ([Campaigns & Line Items](/books/advertising/campaigns-and-line-items#targeting-as-data)).
- **Flex ad:** an ad defined by an aspect ratio and a size range rather than fixed pixels, carried in OpenRTB as wratio, hratio and wmin ([Ad Formats & Standards](/books/advertising/ad-formats#flexible-sizes)).
- **Flight:** the period a line item may deliver, in the account's or contract's time zone, optionally split into segments ([Campaigns & Line Items](/books/advertising/campaigns-and-line-items#flighting-and-time-zones)).
- **Frequency cap:** a limit on how many times one person is shown a line item, campaign or creative in a period ([Pacing & Frequency Capping](/books/advertising/pacing-and-frequency#frequency-capping)).
- **Heavy Ad Intervention:** Chrome unloading an ad with no user interaction that uses more than 60 seconds of CPU in total, 15 seconds in any 30, or 4 MB of network ([Ad Tags, Rendering & Page Performance](/books/advertising/ad-tags-and-rendering#heavy-ad-intervention)).
- **High Water Mark (HWM):** an allocation algorithm that serves the scarcest contracts first, each at a rate sized to its demand ([Forecasting & Avails](/books/advertising/forecasting-and-avails#allocation-plans)).
- **IAB New Ad Portfolio:** IAB Tech Lab's set of ad formats defined by aspect ratio and flexible sizing, replacing the fixed-size standard units ([Ad Formats & Standards](/books/advertising/ad-formats#the-iab-new-ad-portfolio)).
- **Impression:** an ad counted as served; under MRC and IAB guidelines, counted when it begins to render ([Counting Impressions & Clicks](/books/advertising/counting-impressions-and-clicks#begin-to-render)).
- **Impression staleness window:** the longest time from an impression to a click for the click to count ([Counting Impressions & Clicks](/books/advertising/counting-impressions-and-clicks#click-measurement-and-validation)).
- **Initial load and subload:** creative weight loaded before the host page's load event, and weight loaded after it without user action ([Ad Formats & Standards](/books/advertising/ad-formats#load-limits)).
- **Interstitial:** a full-screen ad, flagged `imp.instl` in OpenRTB, limited mainly by rules on when it may appear ([Ad Formats & Standards](/books/advertising/ad-formats#interstitials-and-their-rules)).
- **Key-value:** a publisher-defined targeting attribute the page or app sends with the request (section=sports), predefined or dynamic ([Campaigns & Line Items](/books/advertising/campaigns-and-line-items#targeting-as-data)).
- **LEAN:** IAB's principles for lightweight, encrypted, AdChoices-supported, non-invasive ads ([Ad Formats & Standards](/books/advertising/ad-formats#lean)).
- **Make-good:** extra delivery owed for a guaranteed shortfall, booked as a linked zero-rate line item, or replaced by a credit ([Pacing & Frequency Capping](/books/advertising/pacing-and-frequency#make-goods)).
- **Matched, available and possible units:** impressions that fit the targeting; those bookable without affecting reserved line items; and the maximum, which may displace same and lower priority line items ([Forecasting & Avails](/books/advertising/forecasting-and-avails#what-a-forecast-reports)).
- **Measured click and received click:** a click counted on the publisher's side, versus one recorded on arrival on the advertiser's side ([Counting Impressions & Clicks](/books/advertising/counting-impressions-and-clicks#click-measurement-and-validation)).
- **MRAID:** IAB Tech Lab's API for rich media ads in app containers; 3.0 is current ([Ad Formats & Standards](/books/advertising/ad-formats#rich-media-and-mraid)).
- **Native ad:** an ad delivered as assets (title, image, data) that the publisher renders in its own style, under OpenRTB Native 1.2 ([Ad Formats & Standards](/books/advertising/ad-formats#native-ads)).
- **Opportunity cost (temporary CPM, pCPM):** the per-request price a guaranteed line item ranks at against auction demand, set by how far it is from its goal; it ranks the line item but never bills ([Ad Servers](/books/advertising/ad-servers#guaranteed-demand-against-the-auction)).
- **Overbooking:** booking beyond what is available, which puts equal and lower priority delivery at risk ([Forecasting & Avails](/books/advertising/forecasting-and-avails#overbooking)).
- **Pacing:** delivering a goal or spending a budget at the planned rate across a flight ([Pacing & Frequency Capping](/books/advertising/pacing-and-frequency)).
- **Pause ad:** a CTV ad shown when the viewer pauses content ([Ad Formats & Standards](/books/advertising/ad-formats#video-and-ctv-formats)).
- **Performance goal:** a target ratio (CPA, ROAS, viewability) that drives optimization and is never guaranteed ([Campaigns & Line Items](/books/advertising/campaigns-and-line-items#objectives-and-goals)).
- **Priority tier (line item priority):** the class a line item competes in (sponsorship, standard or guaranteed, price-competing, house), separate from any vendor's priority number and its direction ([Ad Servers](/books/advertising/ad-servers#priority-classes)).
- **Probabilistic throttling:** pacing by deciding with a probability whether a line item takes part in a request, separate from its bid ([Pacing & Frequency Capping](/books/advertising/pacing-and-frequency#pacing-controllers)).
- **Publisher ad server:** the system that decides which line item or bid fills a publisher's ad unit on each request and records the decision ([Ad Servers](/books/advertising/ad-servers#publisher-and-advertiser-ad-servers)).
- **Repeat-click refractory period:** the minimum time between clicks on one impression for a second click to count ([Counting Impressions & Clicks](/books/advertising/counting-impressions-and-clicks#click-measurement-and-validation)).
- **Reservation (hold):** inventory set aside for a proposal or line item before or after signature, so later bookings see it as taken ([Forecasting & Avails](/books/advertising/forecasting-and-avails#reservations-and-holds-before-signature)).
- **Responsive search ad:** a search ad assembled by the platform from up to 15 headlines and 4 descriptions ([Creative Production, Review & DCO](/books/advertising/creative-production-and-dco#specs-and-trafficking)).
- **Roadblock:** two or more creatives from one line item served together on a page, or not at all ([Ad Servers](/books/advertising/ad-servers#creative-selection)).
- **SafeFrame:** IAB Tech Lab's cross-origin iframe container for ads, with an API for geometry and expansion ([Ad Tags, Rendering & Page Performance](/books/advertising/ad-tags-and-rendering#iframes-and-safeframe)).
- **Shared budget:** one budget that several campaigns or line items draw on, such as Google's and Microsoft's shared budgets, Meta's campaign budget or DV360's insertion order budget ([Campaigns & Line Items](/books/advertising/campaigns-and-line-items#budgets)).
- **Single Request Architecture (SRA):** requesting all slots on a page in one ad request, so roadblocks and competitive exclusions hold ([Ad Tags, Rendering & Page Performance](/books/advertising/ad-tags-and-rendering#the-tag-lifecycle)).
- **Targeting expression:** a line item's targeting stored as a typed tree of criteria (dimensions and key-values with includes and excludes), usually an OR of ANDs ([Campaigns & Line Items](/books/advertising/campaigns-and-line-items#targeting-as-data)).
- **Third-party tag:** creative markup that calls another ad server to serve and count the real creative ([Creative Production, Review & DCO](/books/advertising/creative-production-and-dco#specs-and-trafficking)).
- **Tracked ad (MRC):** an ad for which measurement started, rendered or not; not an impression ([Counting Impressions & Clicks](/books/advertising/counting-impressions-and-clicks#tracked-ads)).
- **Tracking ad (Campaign Manager):** an advertiser ad server entry that logs impressions and clicks on an ad served by someone else ([Creative Production, Review & DCO](/books/advertising/creative-production-and-dco#specs-and-trafficking)).
- **Traffic shape:** the expected share of a day's requests in each interval, which an even plan follows ([Pacing & Frequency Capping](/books/advertising/pacing-and-frequency#the-plan-traffic-shape)).
- **Trafficking:** setting up creatives on line items with their tags, trackers, landing URLs and dates, so delivery can start ([Creative Production, Review & DCO](/books/advertising/creative-production-and-dco#specs-and-trafficking)).
- **Yield group:** a set of outside demand partners (server-side bidders or mediation networks) admitted into a publisher ad server's decision for given inventory and format ([Ad Servers](/books/advertising/ad-servers#mediating-demand)).

## Programmatic

- **Ad exchange:** the marketplace that receives an impression opportunity, sends bid requests to connected bidders, runs an auction among their bids and returns the winner ([SSPs & Ad Exchanges](/books/advertising/ssps-and-exchanges#the-exchange-auction)).
- **AdCOM:** IAB Tech Lab's object model for ads, placements, users and devices, written for OpenRTB 3.0 and whose lists OpenRTB 2.6 references ([OpenRTB](/books/advertising/openrtb#versions-and-status)).
- **ads.cert:** IAB Tech Lab's cryptographic protocols for ad systems, whose version 2.0 offers Call Signs (keys in DNS) and Authenticated Connections (signed server-to-server requests) ([ads.txt, sellers.json & SupplyChain](/books/advertising/ads-txt-sellers-json-and-schain#ads-cert)).
- **ads.txt:** a file on a publisher's domain listing the seller accounts authorized to sell its inventory, each as DIRECT or RESELLER ([ads.txt, sellers.json & SupplyChain](/books/advertising/ads-txt-sellers-json-and-schain#ads-txt)).
- **Analytics adapter:** a Prebid.js module that subscribes to the wrapper's auction, bid, timeout and render events and sends them to a collector, the only record of losing and timed-out header bids ([Header Bidding & Prebid](/books/advertising/header-bidding-and-prebid#user-ids-consent-and-analytics)).
- **app-ads.txt:** the ads.txt extension for apps, posted on the developer domain taken from the app's store listing ([ads.txt, sellers.json & SupplyChain](/books/advertising/ads-txt-sellers-json-and-schain#app-ads-txt)).
- **Auction package:** a seller-built bundle of inventory, sometimes with data, sold to buyers as a deal ID ([Deals, PMPs & Curation](/books/advertising/deals-and-curation#curation)).
- **Bid caching:** reusing an unused header bid from an earlier auction on the same page while its TTL lasts, off by default in Prebid.js ([Header Bidding & Prebid](/books/advertising/header-bidding-and-prebid#bid-caching-and-rendering)).
- **Bid request:** the OpenRTB message an exchange POSTs to a bidder describing one or more impressions, with floors, deals, context, device, user and consent signals ([How Programmatic Works](/books/advertising/how-programmatic-works#what-a-bid-request-looks-like)).
- **Bid response:** the bidder's answer to a bid request, with seat bids and bids, or HTTP 204 for no bid ([OpenRTB](/books/advertising/openrtb#the-bid-response)).
- **Bid shading:** lowering a bid in a first-price auction toward the lowest price that still wins often enough, keeping surplus for the buyer ([Demand-Side Platforms](/books/advertising/demand-side-platforms#value-and-bid-shading)).
- **Bidder:** the service that receives bid requests, values each impression for its campaigns and answers with a bid or no bid ([How Programmatic Works](/books/advertising/how-programmatic-works#the-parties-and-what-each-runs)).
- **Bidder adapter:** the module an exchange writes so Prebid can build its requests, parse its bids and register its user syncs ([Header Bidding & Prebid](/books/advertising/header-bidding-and-prebid#writing-a-bidder-adapter)).
- **Bidder timeout:** the time a wrapper waits for bids before it sends what it has to the ad server; late bids lose ([Header Bidding & Prebid](/books/advertising/header-bidding-and-prebid#timeouts)).
- **Billing notice (burl):** the URL an exchange calls when a winning bid becomes billable under the exchange's own policy ([OpenRTB](/books/advertising/openrtb#notices-and-macros)).
- **Blocks (ad quality):** a seller's rules keeping advertiser domains, categories, creative attributes or buyers out of its auctions ([SSPs & Ad Exchanges](/books/advertising/ssps-and-exchanges#floors-and-blocks)).
- **buyers.json:** a file in which buying systems declare the buyers they act for, the buy-side mirror of sellers.json ([ads.txt, sellers.json & SupplyChain](/books/advertising/ads-txt-sellers-json-and-schain#buyers-json-and-the-demandchain-object)).
- **Callout:** one bid request sent to one bidder for one auction, recorded with its deadline, status and latency; the unit of RTB traffic and cost ([How Programmatic Works](/books/advertising/how-programmatic-works#the-bid-request-lifecycle)).
- **Client-side header bidding:** header bidding run in the reader's browser, each exchange called from the page with its own cookies ([Header Bidding & Prebid](/books/advertising/header-bidding-and-prebid#client-side-server-side-and-in-app)).
- **Cookie sync (cookie matching):** a pixel or redirect exchange that lets two ad systems pair their own IDs for the same browser, so one can send the other's ID in later bid requests ([How Programmatic Works](/books/advertising/how-programmatic-works#cookie-syncing-and-matching-identifiers)).
- **Curated Audiences:** IAB Tech Lab's specification for sending seller-side audience segments in bid requests without cross-site identifiers, renamed from Seller Defined Audiences in December 2024 ([Deals, PMPs & Curation](/books/advertising/deals-and-curation#curation)).
- **Curation:** packaging inventory from many sellers with a third party's data and selection into a deal sold to buyers ([Deals, PMPs & Curation](/books/advertising/deals-and-curation#curation)).
- **Curator:** the party (a data company, retailer, trading desk or the buyer itself) that builds and sells a curated deal and takes a fee from it ([Deals, PMPs & Curation](/books/advertising/deals-and-curation#curation)).
- **Curator fee:** the margin a curator takes from a curated transaction, charged only when the curated bid wins on the platforms that document it ([Deals, PMPs & Curation](/books/advertising/deals-and-curation#curator-fees-and-fee-transparency)).
- **Deal:** a private marketplace, preferred or programmatic guaranteed arrangement between a seller and a buyer, carried in bid requests by its deal ID ([Deals, PMPs & Curation](/books/advertising/deals-and-curation#the-transaction-types)).
- **Deal bid rate and deal win rate:** a deal's bids divided by the bid requests that carried it, and its winning bids divided by its bids ([Deals, PMPs & Curation](/books/advertising/deals-and-curation#why-a-deal-doesn-t-spend)).
- **Deal ID:** the string a seller system attaches to bid requests in `pmp.deals[]` and a buyer's bid repeats in `dealid`, unique only within the issuing system ([Deals, PMPs & Curation](/books/advertising/deals-and-curation#deal-ids-in-openrtb)).
- **Deals API:** IAB Tech Lab's API, v1.0 final in February 2026, for a seller system to push a deal's terms and status to buying platforms ([Deals, PMPs & Curation](/books/advertising/deals-and-curation#setting-up-a-deal)).
- **DemandChain object:** the bid response object listing every party involved in buying the creative, the buy-side mirror of the SupplyChain object ([ads.txt, sellers.json & SupplyChain](/books/advertising/ads-txt-sellers-json-and-schain#buyers-json-and-the-demandchain-object)).
- **DIRECT and RESELLER:** the ads.txt relationship values for an account the publisher controls and one it has authorized another company to control and resell ([ads.txt, sellers.json & SupplyChain](/books/advertising/ads-txt-sellers-json-and-schain#ads-txt)).
- **DSP (demand-side platform):** a platform that bids on impressions across many exchanges for advertisers and agencies, with seats, budgets, targeting and reporting ([Demand-Side Platforms](/books/advertising/demand-side-platforms)).
- **Extension (ext):** an object-level OpenRTB field for data outside the specification, whose contents each exchange publishes to its bidders ([OpenRTB](/books/advertising/openrtb#extensions)).
- **Fan-out:** sending one impression's bid request to many bidders at once under one deadline and taking what arrives in time ([How Programmatic Works](/books/advertising/how-programmatic-works#the-fan-out-in-code)).
- **First Look:** an ad server giving its affiliated exchange the first chance at an impression ahead of other exchanges, found in the US court record and barred for indirect transactions by the 2026 remedies as reported ([Header Bidding & Prebid](/books/advertising/header-bidding-and-prebid#how-the-platforms-do-it)).
- **Header auction:** the auction a header bidding wrapper runs among exchanges before the ad server decides, whose winner still has to win in the ad server ([Header Bidding & Prebid](/books/advertising/header-bidding-and-prebid#from-waterfalls-to-header-auctions)).
- **Header bidding:** asking several exchanges for bids before the publisher ad server decides, through Prebid or a server-to-server service, and passing the best bids in as key-values ([Header Bidding & Prebid](/books/advertising/header-bidding-and-prebid#from-waterfalls-to-header-auctions)).
- **INVENTORYPARTNERDOMAIN:** the ads.txt variable that points to a content partner's ads.txt to authorize its sellers, followed one hop ([ads.txt, sellers.json & SupplyChain](/books/advertising/ads-txt-sellers-json-and-schain#ads-txt)).
- **Last Look:** an ad server letting its affiliated exchange see the winning header bid and outbid it ([Header Bidding & Prebid](/books/advertising/header-bidding-and-prebid#how-the-platforms-do-it)).
- **Loss notice (lurl):** the URL an exchange calls when a bid is known to have lost, often with a reason code ([OpenRTB](/books/advertising/openrtb#notices-and-macros)).
- **Loss reason code:** a code sent with a loss notice saying why a bid lost, from the OpenRTB list (below 500) or the exchange's own (500 and up) ([OpenRTB](/books/advertising/openrtb#no-bid-and-loss-reasons)).
- **Macro (OpenRTB):** a placeholder such as `${AUCTION_PRICE}` or `${AUCTION_LOSS}` in a notice URL or markup that the exchange replaces with a value before calling it ([OpenRTB](/books/advertising/openrtb#notices-and-macros)).
- **MANAGERDOMAIN:** the ads.txt 1.1 variable naming a publisher's primary or exclusive monetization partner, optionally per country ([ads.txt, sellers.json & SupplyChain](/books/advertising/ads-txt-sellers-json-and-schain#ads-txt)).
- **Minimum to win:** the lowest bid that would have won an auction, passed to bidders by some exchanges through `${AUCTION_MIN_TO_WIN}` ([OpenRTB](/books/advertising/openrtb#notices-and-macros)).
- **Net bidding:** applying fees before the auction so every bid competes net of what is taken from it, which the MRC encourages ([Deals, PMPs & Curation](/books/advertising/deals-and-curation#curator-fees-and-fee-transparency)).
- **No-bid reason (nbr):** a code in a bid response, or on a no-bid, giving why the bidder did not bid ([OpenRTB](/books/advertising/openrtb#no-bid-and-loss-reasons)).
- **Non-participating publisher:** a publisher with no ads.txt or app-ads.txt file, which buyers treat separately from an unauthorized seller ([ads.txt, sellers.json & SupplyChain](/books/advertising/ads-txt-sellers-json-and-schain#what-a-dsp-checks)).
- **Open auction:** programmatic buying open to any buyer seat at the exchange's floor, with no deal ([Deals, PMPs & Curation](/books/advertising/deals-and-curation#the-transaction-types)).
- **Open Bidding:** Google Ad Manager's server-side alternative to header bidding, where third-party exchanges bid inside the ad server's own auction ([Header Bidding & Prebid](/books/advertising/header-bidding-and-prebid#how-the-platforms-do-it)).
- **OpenRTB:** IAB Tech Lab's protocol for bid requests and responses between exchanges and bidders; 2.6 is the maintained version and 3.0 is not adopted ([OpenRTB](/books/advertising/openrtb#versions-and-status)).
- **OWNERDOMAIN:** the ads.txt 1.1 variable naming the business that owns the site or app, matching its sellers.json domain ([ads.txt, sellers.json & SupplyChain](/books/advertising/ads-txt-sellers-json-and-schain#ads-txt)).
- **Payment node (hp):** a SupplyChain node where the ad system pays the seller (`hp = 1`), as against a non-payment node (`hp = 0`) added in schain 1.1 ([ads.txt, sellers.json & SupplyChain](/books/advertising/ads-txt-sellers-json-and-schain#the-supplychain-object)).
- **Placeholder record:** the ads.txt line `placeholder.example.com, placeholder, DIRECT, placeholder`, which says a site authorizes no sellers ([ads.txt, sellers.json & SupplyChain](/books/advertising/ads-txt-sellers-json-and-schain#ads-txt)).
- **Prebid Cache:** the store where Prebid keeps video and app bids' markup so the ad server's creative can fetch it by key ([Header Bidding & Prebid](/books/advertising/header-bidding-and-prebid#bid-caching-and-rendering)).
- **Prebid Server:** the open-source server for server-to-server header bidding, in Go and Java, used for web, app, AMP, CTV, DOOH and audio ([Header Bidding & Prebid](/books/advertising/header-bidding-and-prebid#client-side-server-side-and-in-app)).
- **Prebid.js:** the open-source JavaScript header bidding wrapper that runs in the browser, the core of the Prebid suite ([Header Bidding & Prebid](/books/advertising/header-bidding-and-prebid#configuring-prebid-js)).
- **Preferred deal:** a non-guaranteed deal at a fixed price that gives one buyer the first chance to buy, signaled with `deal.at = 3` ([Deals, PMPs & Curation](/books/advertising/deals-and-curation#the-transaction-types)).
- **Pretargeting (declared filters):** a bidder's declared filters at an exchange, so it receives only bid requests matching its criteria ([SSPs & Ad Exchanges](/books/advertising/ssps-and-exchanges#traffic-shaping)).
- **Price bucket (hb_pb):** the value a header bid's CPM is rounded down to so an ad server line item at that price can match it ([Header Bidding & Prebid](/books/advertising/header-bidding-and-prebid#price-granularity-and-targeting-keys)).
- **Price granularity:** the table of price ranges and increments that turns header bids into price buckets, which sets how many line items a publisher needs ([Header Bidding & Prebid](/books/advertising/header-bidding-and-prebid#price-granularity-and-targeting-keys)).
- **Private auction (PMP):** an auction restricted to invited buyer seats with a deal floor, signaled with `pmp.deals[]` and `wseat` ([Deals, PMPs & Curation](/books/advertising/deals-and-curation#the-transaction-types)).
- **Programmatic guaranteed (PG):** a deal with a fixed price and reserved volume that runs through programmatic pipes and books like an IO ([Deals, PMPs & Curation](/books/advertising/deals-and-curation#the-transaction-types)).
- **Project Poirot:** the DV360 bid-shading program the US court found lowered Google's bids on rival exchanges ([The Google Ad Tech Cases](/books/advertising/google-ad-tech-cases#the-us-case)).
- **Real-time bidding (RTB):** selling one impression at a time through an auction among bidders who answer a bid request within a deadline ([How Programmatic Works](/books/advertising/how-programmatic-works#the-parties-and-what-each-runs)).
- **Second Price Plus:** OpenRTB's name for auction type 2, in which the winner pays the second-highest price plus a small increment ([OpenRTB](/books/advertising/openrtb#auction-types)).
- **Seller type:** the sellers.json field saying whether a seller account is the PUBLISHER, an INTERMEDIARY or BOTH ([ads.txt, sellers.json & SupplyChain](/books/advertising/ads-txt-sellers-json-and-schain#sellers-json)).
- **sellers.json:** a file on an ad system's domain listing its seller IDs, each as publisher, intermediary or both ([ads.txt, sellers.json & SupplyChain](/books/advertising/ads-txt-sellers-json-and-schain#sellers-json)).
- **Send all bids:** the Prebid setting that sends every bidder's best bid to the ad server under bidder-suffixed keys, not only the top bid ([Header Bidding & Prebid](/books/advertising/header-bidding-and-prebid#price-granularity-and-targeting-keys)).
- **Server-side header bidding:** header bidding run from a server that fans out to exchanges, cutting page weight at the cost of the browser's cookies ([Header Bidding & Prebid](/books/advertising/header-bidding-and-prebid#client-side-server-side-and-in-app)).
- **SSP (supply-side platform):** a platform that runs auctions for publishers' impressions across many buyers; with an exchange, the supply side ([SSPs & Ad Exchanges](/books/advertising/ssps-and-exchanges#what-an-ssp-runs)).
- **Supply chain complete:** the schain flag saying the chain lists every node back to the inventory owner ([ads.txt, sellers.json & SupplyChain](/books/advertising/ads-txt-sellers-json-and-schain#the-supplychain-object)).
- **SupplyChain object (schain):** the list of every seller that handled a bid request, back to the inventory owner, carried in OpenRTB's source object ([ads.txt, sellers.json & SupplyChain](/books/advertising/ads-txt-sellers-json-and-schain#the-supplychain-object)).
- **Tmax:** the OpenRTB request field giving the bidder's deadline in milliseconds, network latency included ([OpenRTB](/books/advertising/openrtb#the-bid-request)).
- **Trading location:** a region an exchange sends bid requests from, near which bidders run endpoints to keep latency low ([How Programmatic Works](/books/advertising/how-programmatic-works#the-latency-budget)).
- **Traffic shaping:** an exchange choosing, per request, which bidders to call, by declared filters, capacity and predicted bid likelihood, with an exploration share ([SSPs & Ad Exchanges](/books/advertising/ssps-and-exchanges#traffic-shaping)).
- **Transaction ID (source.tid):** an ID shared by every system handling one impression opportunity, used to join multi-hop paths and spot duplicate offers ([How Programmatic Works](/books/advertising/how-programmatic-works#who-sees-what)).
- **Transparent Ad Marketplace (TAM):** Amazon Publisher Services' server-to-server header bidding marketplace, with a service fee charged to bidders ([Header Bidding & Prebid](/books/advertising/header-bidding-and-prebid#client-side-server-side-and-in-app)).
- **Tying (AdX and DFP):** the US court's finding that Google tied its exchange to its publisher ad server, one basis of the ad tech liability ruling ([The Google Ad Tech Cases](/books/advertising/google-ad-tech-cases#the-us-case)).
- **Unified Pricing Rules (UPR):** Google Ad Manager's 2019 floor rules that removed per-exchange floors, found by the US court not to create a level playing field and barred for indirect transactions by the 2026 remedies as reported ([The Google Ad Tech Cases](/books/advertising/google-ad-tech-cases#the-us-case)).
- **User ID module:** the Prebid module that runs identity providers' submodules on the page and passes their IDs to every bidder as extended IDs, gated on consent ([Header Bidding & Prebid](/books/advertising/header-bidding-and-prebid#user-ids-consent-and-analytics)).
- **Win notice (nurl):** the URL an exchange may call when a bid wins, which OpenRTB says doesn't by itself mean a delivered or billable ad ([OpenRTB](/books/advertising/openrtb#notices-and-macros)).
- **Wrapper (header bidding):** the code (Prebid.js, an APS tag, a server) that runs a header auction and hands its bids to the ad server ([Header Bidding & Prebid](/books/advertising/header-bidding-and-prebid#from-waterfalls-to-header-auctions)).

## Auctions and Pricing

- **Automated bidding (auction-time bidding):** the platform setting the bid in each auction toward an advertiser's goal and budget ([Automated Bidding](/books/advertising/automated-bidding)).
- **Bid cap:** a strategy that never bids above a set amount in any single auction ([Automated Bidding](/books/advertising/automated-bidding#the-strategies)).
- **Bid multiplier:** the single factor an autobidder applies to every opportunity's value, learned from tracking the budget and the target ([Automated Bidding](/books/advertising/automated-bidding#the-bidding-problem)).
- **Billing event:** the counted event a line item is charged for, separate from its optimization goal ([Pricing Models](/books/advertising/pricing-models#billing-events)).
- **Budget optimization:** the platform moving one budget among campaigns or ad sets toward the ones with the best marginal results ([Automated Bidding](/books/advertising/automated-bidding#budget-optimization)).
- **Clearing price:** what the winner of an auction pays, before downstream fees ([Auction Mechanics](/books/advertising/auction-mechanics#second-price-and-first-price)).
- **Cost cap:** Meta's and LinkedIn's average cost-per-result goal, not guaranteed ([Automated Bidding](/books/advertising/automated-bidding#the-strategies)).
- **Cost per point (CPP):** the price of one gross rating point (1% of the target audience) in TV buying ([Pricing Models](/books/advertising/pricing-models#the-models)).
- **Cost per sale (CPS):** a percentage of an attributed sale, charged per order ([Pricing Models](/books/advertising/pricing-models#the-models)).
- **CPA, CPL, CPI:** cost per acquisition, per lead and per install ([Pricing Models](/books/advertising/pricing-models#the-models)).
- **CPC:** cost per click ([Pricing Models](/books/advertising/pricing-models#the-models)).
- **CPM:** cost per thousand impressions (mille), the unit most display, video and programmatic prices are quoted in ([Pricing Models](/books/advertising/pricing-models#the-models)).
- **CPV and CPCV:** cost per view, under the platform's own definition of a view, and cost per completed view ([Pricing Models](/books/advertising/pricing-models#the-models)).
- **Data exclusion:** telling the bidder to ignore conversion data from a period when tracking was broken ([Automated Bidding](/books/advertising/automated-bidding#seasonality-and-data-exclusions)).
- **Dynamic floor:** a floor set per request or segment by a model predicting the clearing price ([Floors & Yield Optimization](/books/advertising/floors-and-yield-optimization#hard-soft-and-dynamic-floors)).
- **Effective bid:** a nominal bid converted into the auction's ranking unit and multiplied by quality or relevance factors ([Auction Mechanics](/books/advertising/auction-mechanics#quality-weighted-ranking)).
- **First-price auction:** a sealed-bid auction where the winner pays its own bid, so bidders shade below value; the open-web default since Google Ad Manager's 2019 move ([Auction Mechanics](/books/advertising/auction-mechanics#second-price-and-first-price)).
- **Floor basis (gross or net):** whether a floor is compared with the price the buyer pays or with the seller's share after the exchange's fee ([Floors & Yield Optimization](/books/advertising/floors-and-yield-optimization#floors-across-the-chain)).
- **Generalized second-price auction (GSP):** the multi-slot search auction that ranks ads by quality-weighted bid and charges each the minimum to keep its position; truthful bidding is not an equilibrium ([Auction Mechanics](/books/advertising/auction-mechanics#the-generalized-second-price-auction)).
- **Hard reserve (hard floor):** a minimum a bid must exceed to compete ([Auction Mechanics](/books/advertising/auction-mechanics#reserve-prices)).
- **Learning period:** the time after a strategy is created or materially changed while it recalibrates and performance varies ([Automated Bidding](/books/advertising/automated-bidding#learning-periods)).
- **Modified second-price auction:** the MRC's term for search, social and retail auctions whose winner pays an amount set by an auctioneer-specific formula ([Auction Mechanics](/books/advertising/auction-mechanics#how-the-platforms-do-it)).
- **MRC Auction Transparency Standards:** the MRC's January 2026 standards requiring any ad auctioneer to disclose and report its auction type, price rules, bid factors, reserves and changes ([Auction Mechanics](/books/advertising/auction-mechanics#the-mrc-auction-transparency-standards)).
- **Optimization goal:** the event a delivery system predicts and maximizes for a line item ([Pricing Models](/books/advertising/pricing-models#billing-events)).
- **Pricing rule:** a floor applied to requests that match its targeting (Google Ad Manager's term) ([Floors & Yield Optimization](/books/advertising/floors-and-yield-optimization#rule-based-floors)).
- **Rate card:** a seller's dated list prices per placement, format and period ([Pricing Models](/books/advertising/pricing-models#rate-cards)).
- **Reserve price (floor):** the price below which an auctioneer won't sell an impression ([Auction Mechanics](/books/advertising/auction-mechanics#reserve-prices)).
- **Seasonality adjustment:** a scheduled expected change in conversion rate for a short event, given to the bidder ([Automated Bidding](/books/advertising/automated-bidding#seasonality-and-data-exclusions)).
- **Second-price auction:** a sealed-bid auction where the highest bidder wins and pays the second-highest bid or that plus an increment; bidding true value is a dominant strategy ([Auction Mechanics](/books/advertising/auction-mechanics#second-price-and-first-price)).
- **Share of voice:** a buy for a fixed share of a placement's impressions over a period ([Pricing Models](/books/advertising/pricing-models#flat-fees-sponsorships-and-share-of-voice)).
- **Skip rate:** the share of auctions a floors module runs without floors, as a control for testing ([Floors & Yield Optimization](/books/advertising/floors-and-yield-optimization#floor-experiments)).
- **Smart Bidding:** Google's automated strategies: Target CPA, Target ROAS, Maximize conversions and Maximize conversion value ([Automated Bidding](/books/advertising/automated-bidding#how-the-platforms-do-it)).
- **Soft reserve (soft floor):** in a second-price auction, a minimum the winner pays when the runner-up is below it, while bids between the hard and soft reserves pay their own bid ([Auction Mechanics](/books/advertising/auction-mechanics#reserve-prices)).
- **Sponsorship:** a flat-fee or share-of-voice buy of a placement or section for a period, usually at the highest priority ([Pricing Models](/books/advertising/pricing-models#flat-fees-sponsorships-and-share-of-voice)).
- **Target CPA (tCPA):** a strategy aiming for an average cost per conversion at a target ([Automated Bidding](/books/advertising/automated-bidding#the-strategies)).
- **Target CPM (floor):** Ad Manager's floor type that adjusts the floor per request to reach an average ([Floors & Yield Optimization](/books/advertising/floors-and-yield-optimization#hard-soft-and-dynamic-floors)).
- **Target ROAS (tROAS):** a strategy aiming for an average conversion value per unit of spend at a target ([Automated Bidding](/books/advertising/automated-bidding#value-bidding)).
- **Total value (Meta):** Meta's ranking score, the bid times the estimated action rate plus an ad quality score ([Auction Mechanics](/books/advertising/auction-mechanics#quality-weighted-ranking)).
- **Unified auction:** one decision in which all non-guaranteed sources (header bids, exchanges, remnant line items) compete on the same basis ([Auction Mechanics](/books/advertising/auction-mechanics#waterfalls-and-unified-auctions)).
- **Value bidding:** bidding on the predicted value of a conversion rather than its count, from values the advertiser reports ([Automated Bidding](/books/advertising/automated-bidding#value-bidding)).
- **VCG (Vickrey-Clarke-Groves):** a multi-slot mechanism charging each winner the value its presence costs the other bidders; truthful bidding is dominant ([Auction Mechanics](/books/advertising/auction-mechanics#vcg)).
- **vCPM:** cost per thousand viewable impressions, under a named viewability standard ([Pricing Models](/books/advertising/pricing-models#the-models)).
- **Waterfall:** calling demand sources one at a time in a fixed order by expected price until one fills, the older alternative to a unified auction ([Auction Mechanics](/books/advertising/auction-mechanics#waterfalls-and-unified-auctions)).
- **Yield management:** maximizing a publisher's revenue across guaranteed, deal and auction demand ([Floors & Yield Optimization](/books/advertising/floors-and-yield-optimization#yield-management)).

## Identity and Audiences

- **AdAttributionKit:** Apple's successor to SKAdNetwork (iOS 17.4), covering alternative marketplaces and re-engagement, with no ATT requirement ([Mobile Ad IDs, ATT & SKAdNetwork](/books/advertising/mobile-ad-ids-and-att#skadnetwork-and-adattributionkit)).
- **ADMaP:** IAB Tech Lab's attribution data matching protocol, using private set intersection and trusted execution environments ([Data Clean Rooms](/books/advertising/data-clean-rooms#iab-tech-lab-pair-admap-and-the-end-of-opja)).
- **Agent type (atype):** AdCOM's classification of an identifier as a browser or device ID (1), an in-app device ID (2) or a person-based ID (3) ([Identifiers After Cookies](/books/advertising/identifiers-after-cookies#extended-identifiers-in-openrtb)).
- **Android Advertising ID:** Google Play's resettable advertising identifier, returned as zeros after the user deletes it ([Mobile Ad IDs, ATT & SKAdNetwork](/books/advertising/mobile-ad-ids-and-att#android-advertising-id-app-set-id-and-install-referrer)).
- **App Set ID:** Android's identifier scoped to one developer's apps, recommended for analytics and fraud prevention instead of the Advertising ID ([Mobile Ad IDs, ATT & SKAdNetwork](/books/advertising/mobile-ad-ids-and-att#android-advertising-id-app-set-id-and-install-referrer)).
- **App Tracking Transparency (ATT):** Apple's permission prompt an app must show before tracking a user across other companies' apps and websites, since iOS 14.5 ([Mobile Ad IDs, ATT & SKAdNetwork](/books/advertising/mobile-ad-ids-and-att#idfa-and-app-tracking-transparency)).
- **Audience Taxonomy:** IAB Tech Lab's vocabulary for audience segments, labeling each as demographic, interest or purchase intent ([Targeting & Taxonomies](/books/advertising/targeting-and-taxonomies#the-iab-tech-lab-taxonomies)).
- **Bounce tracking:** gaining first-party storage by redirecting a navigation through a tracker's site; browsers delete such sites' state when the user never interacted with them ([Third-Party Cookies & Browser Privacy](/books/advertising/cookies-and-browser-privacy#what-browsers-do-to-trackers)).
- **cattax:** the OpenRTB field naming the taxonomy of a request's category codes, defaulting to Content Taxonomy 1.0 when absent ([Targeting & Taxonomies](/books/advertising/targeting-and-taxonomies#the-iab-tech-lab-taxonomies)).
- **CHIPS (partitioned cookies):** a cookie set with the `Partitioned` attribute, keyed to both its setter and the top-level site, so it is readable only on the site where it was set ([Third-Party Cookies & Browser Privacy](/books/advertising/cookies-and-browser-privacy#what-still-works-for-legitimate-cross-site-state)).
- **Content Taxonomy:** IAB Tech Lab's vocabulary of what content is about; 3.0 broke compatibility with 2.x and 3.1 is current ([Targeting & Taxonomies](/books/advertising/targeting-and-taxonomies#the-iab-tech-lab-taxonomies)).
- **Contextual targeting:** choosing ads by what the page, app or content is about rather than by who the viewer is ([Targeting & Taxonomies](/books/advertising/targeting-and-taxonomies#contextual-targeting)).
- **Conversion value:** the advertised app's 6-bit fine value (0 to 63) or coarse value (low, medium, high) reported in a postback ([Mobile Ad IDs, ATT & SKAdNetwork](/books/advertising/mobile-ad-ids-and-att#skadnetwork-and-adattributionkit)).
- **Customer data platform (CDP):** software that keeps a persistent, unified customer record available to other systems, used to define and send audiences ([First-Party Data & Audiences](/books/advertising/first-party-data-and-audiences#customer-data-platforms-and-onboarding)).
- **Customer match:** uploading hashed customer identifiers to an ad platform to target or exclude matching users, as Google Customer Match, Meta custom audiences, Microsoft customer match and LinkedIn Matched Audiences do ([First-Party Data & Audiences](/books/advertising/first-party-data-and-audiences#customer-list-uploads)).
- **Data broker:** a business that knowingly collects and sells to third parties the personal information of consumers it has no direct relationship with (California's definition) ([Data Brokers & Third-Party Data](/books/advertising/data-brokers#registration-and-deletion-laws)).
- **Data clean room:** an environment where parties run agreed computations over each other's data with controls on joins and outputs, so neither sees the other's records ([Data Clean Rooms](/books/advertising/data-clean-rooms#what-a-clean-room-is-and-the-models)).
- **Data fee:** what a buyer pays a data provider for a segment used on an impression, as a CPM or a percent of media under the platform's rules ([Data Brokers & Third-Party Data](/books/advertising/data-brokers#how-data-fees-are-charged)).
- **Data Transparency Standard (Data Label):** IAB Tech Lab's minimum disclosure fields for audience segments, with an audited compliance program ([Data Brokers & Third-Party Data](/books/advertising/data-brokers#what-a-segment-label-should-say)).
- **Daypart:** a schedule of days and hours when a line item may serve, evaluated in a stated time zone ([Targeting & Taxonomies](/books/advertising/targeting-and-taxonomies#dayparting)).
- **Delete Act:** California's law requiring data brokers to register every year and to process deletion requests through DROP ([Data Brokers & Third-Party Data](/books/advertising/data-brokers#registration-and-deletion-laws)).
- **Designated Market Area (DMA):** Nielsen's US television and radio markets; OpenRTB's `metro` carries Google's similar but not identical codes ([Targeting & Taxonomies](/books/advertising/targeting-and-taxonomies#geography)).
- **Device storage disclosure:** the TCF JSON file in which a vendor declares each cookie or storage item it uses, with type, lifetime, domains and purposes ([Third-Party Cookies & Browser Privacy](/books/advertising/cookies-and-browser-privacy#declaring-what-is-stored)).
- **Differential privacy:** adding calibrated noise to results so any one person's data changes them only within a bound, each query spending part of a privacy budget ([Data Clean Rooms](/books/advertising/data-clean-rooms#thresholds-and-differential-privacy-in-practice)).
- **DROP (Delete Request and Opt-out Platform):** California's platform, live January 1, 2026, through which one consumer request reaches every registered data broker, who must process it on a 45-day cycle ([Data Brokers & Third-Party Data](/books/advertising/data-brokers#registration-and-deletion-laws)).
- **EUID (European Unified ID):** UID2's European counterpart in a separate namespace, with a GDPR and TCF consent basis ([Identifiers After Cookies](/books/advertising/identifiers-after-cookies#uid2-and-euid)).
- **Extended identifiers (EIDs):** OpenRTB's `user.eids` array of identifiers from named sources, each with an agent type, match method, inserter and matcher ([Identifiers After Cookies](/books/advertising/identifiers-after-cookies#extended-identifiers-in-openrtb)).
- **First-party data:** data a business collects about its own customers and visitors from its own sites, apps, stores and CRM ([First-Party Data & Audiences](/books/advertising/first-party-data-and-audiences#kinds-of-first-party-audience)).
- **Identity graph:** a vendor's linked set of identifiers (emails, cookies, device IDs, households) believed to belong to one person or household ([Identifiers After Cookies](/books/advertising/identifiers-after-cookies#other-email-based-and-graph-ids)).
- **IDFA:** Apple's identifier for advertisers, available to an app only after the user authorizes tracking under ATT ([Mobile Ad IDs, ATT & SKAdNetwork](/books/advertising/mobile-ad-ids-and-att#idfa-and-app-tracking-transparency)).
- **IFA type (ifa_type):** the label saying which platform's namespace a CTV or mobile identifier for advertising belongs to (idfa, aaid, rida, tifa, vida and others) ([Mobile Ad IDs, ATT & SKAdNetwork](/books/advertising/mobile-ad-ids-and-att#ctv-identifiers)).
- **Intelligent Tracking Prevention (ITP):** Safari's tracking protections: third-party cookies blocked by default, caps on script-written storage and on cookies set after link decoration, partitioned third-party storage ([Third-Party Cookies & Browser Privacy](/books/advertising/cookies-and-browser-privacy#how-the-browsers-got-here)).
- **Link decoration:** click IDs or other identifiers appended to a landing URL, whose script-set cookies Safari caps ([Third-Party Cookies & Browser Privacy](/books/advertising/cookies-and-browser-privacy#what-browsers-do-to-trackers)).
- **Location type:** AdCOM's source of a request's location: GPS or location services (1), IP address (2) or user provided (3) ([Targeting & Taxonomies](/books/advertising/targeting-and-taxonomies#geography)).
- **Lookalike audience:** an audience a platform models as similar to a seed audience, sized as a share of a market, whose members the advertiser never sees ([First-Party Data & Audiences](/books/advertising/first-party-data-and-audiences#lookalikes-and-modeled-audiences)).
- **Match method (mm):** AdCOM's code for how an identifier was matched: cookie sync, authenticated, observed, inference or vendor-specific ([Identifiers After Cookies](/books/advertising/identifiers-after-cookies#extended-identifiers-in-openrtb)).
- **Match rate (identity):** the share of one party's records or users that a partner can match to its own identifiers ([Identifiers After Cookies](/books/advertising/identifiers-after-cookies#match-rates)).
- **Membership duration:** how long a person stays in an audience after qualifying, capped by each platform (540 days for Google Customer Match) ([First-Party Data & Audiences](/books/advertising/first-party-data-and-audiences#customer-list-uploads)).
- **Minimum aggregation threshold:** the minimum number of distinct people behind each clean room output row ([Data Clean Rooms](/books/advertising/data-clean-rooms#thresholds-and-differential-privacy-in-practice)).
- **Normalization and hashing:** lowercasing, trimming and formatting an email or phone the way the receiving platform specifies before SHA-256, so the same person hashes the same everywhere ([Identifiers After Cookies](/books/advertising/identifiers-after-cookies#normalization-and-hashing)).
- **Onboarding:** matching offline or CRM identifiers to an identity graph and delivering the resulting IDs to ad platforms ([First-Party Data & Audiences](/books/advertising/first-party-data-and-audiences#customer-data-platforms-and-onboarding)).
- **PADFAA:** the federal Protecting Americans' Data from Foreign Adversaries Act, barring data brokers from providing Americans' sensitive data to foreign adversaries, enforced by the FTC ([Data Brokers & Third-Party Data](/books/advertising/data-brokers#federal-rules-on-foreign-adversaries)).
- **PAIR (Publisher Advertiser Identity Reconciliation):** IAB Tech Lab's protocol, originally from Google, for matching advertiser and publisher first-party data across clean rooms with commutative encryption; it replaced OPJA ([Data Clean Rooms](/books/advertising/data-clean-rooms#iab-tech-lab-pair-admap-and-the-end-of-opja)).
- **Play Install Referrer:** the Android API that gives an installed app its referrer URL and its click and install timestamps ([Mobile Ad IDs, ATT & SKAdNetwork](/books/advertising/mobile-ad-ids-and-att#android-advertising-id-app-set-id-and-install-referrer)).
- **Postback data tier (crowd anonymity tier):** Apple's anonymity level for an install, which decides how many source identifier digits and how fine a conversion value a postback carries ([Mobile Ad IDs, ATT & SKAdNetwork](/books/advertising/mobile-ad-ids-and-att#skadnetwork-and-adattributionkit)).
- **Postback (SKAdNetwork, AdAttributionKit):** the signed, delayed attribution report the device sends to the winning ad network (and runners-up), with a source identifier and conversion value ([Mobile Ad IDs, ATT & SKAdNetwork](/books/advertising/mobile-ad-ids-and-att#skadnetwork-and-adattributionkit)).
- **PPID (publisher provided identifier):** a publisher's own hashed or encrypted user ID sent to its ad server for frequency capping and audiences within its own inventory ([Identifiers After Cookies](/books/advertising/identifiers-after-cookies#publisher-provided-ids)).
- **Privacy budget:** the total privacy loss (epsilon) a collaboration or dataset allows across queries before it can no longer be queried ([Data Clean Rooms](/books/advertising/data-clean-rooms#thresholds-and-differential-privacy-in-practice)).
- **Privacy Sandbox:** Google's set of browser and Android APIs meant to replace cross-site tracking; most were retired on October 17, 2025, while Chrome kept third-party cookies ([Third-Party Cookies & Browser Privacy](/books/advertising/cookies-and-browser-privacy#the-privacy-sandbox-and-what-is-left-of-it)).
- **RampID:** LiveRamp's people-based identifier, in maintained, derived, placeholder and household forms ([Identifiers After Cookies](/books/advertising/identifiers-after-cookies#other-email-based-and-graph-ids)).
- **Retargeting:** showing ads to people who visited a site or used an app, from the advertiser's pixel or SDK audiences ([First-Party Data & Audiences](/books/advertising/first-party-data-and-audiences#website-and-app-retargeting)).
- **segtax:** the OpenRTB community extension in a Data object's `ext` naming the taxonomy of its segment IDs; without it, segment IDs are proprietary ([Targeting & Taxonomies](/books/advertising/targeting-and-taxonomies#the-iab-tech-lab-taxonomies)).
- **SharedID:** Prebid's first-party UUID cookie identifier for a publisher's users ([Identifiers After Cookies](/books/advertising/identifiers-after-cookies#publisher-provided-ids)).
- **SKAdNetwork (SKAN):** Apple's aggregated, privacy-preserving install attribution; version 4 sends up to three postbacks in three conversion windows ([Mobile Ad IDs, ATT & SKAdNetwork](/books/advertising/mobile-ad-ids-and-att#skadnetwork-and-adattributionkit)).
- **Source identifier:** the 2- to 4-digit hierarchical campaign code an ad network signs into an impression, trimmed from the left at lower tiers ([Mobile Ad IDs, ATT & SKAdNetwork](/books/advertising/mobile-ad-ids-and-att#skadnetwork-and-adattributionkit)).
- **Storage Access API:** a browser API that lets a cross-site iframe ask, after a user gesture, for its unpartitioned cookies on a top-level site; grants lapse after about 30 days ([Third-Party Cookies & Browser Privacy](/books/advertising/cookies-and-browser-privacy#what-still-works-for-legitimate-cross-site-state)).
- **Third-party cookie:** a cookie set by a domain other than the site in the address bar, the old basis of cross-site tracking and cookie syncing ([Third-Party Cookies & Browser Privacy](/books/advertising/cookies-and-browser-privacy#how-the-browsers-got-here)).
- **Third-party data:** audience data a buyer gets from a company other than itself or the publisher, usually a data provider's segments sold through a DSP or marketplace ([Data Brokers & Third-Party Data](/books/advertising/data-brokers#how-third-party-data-reaches-a-buyer)).
- **Total Cookie Protection:** Firefox's default since June 2022 of a separate cookie jar per top-level site, which partitions third-party cookies ([Third-Party Cookies & Browser Privacy](/books/advertising/cookies-and-browser-privacy#how-the-browsers-got-here)).
- **UID2 (Unified ID 2.0):** a deterministic identifier derived from a normalized, hashed email or phone; raw UID2s stay with advertisers and DSPs, while encrypted UID2 tokens travel in bid requests and DSPs decrypt them at bid time ([Identifiers After Cookies](/books/advertising/identifiers-after-cookies#uid2-and-euid)).

## Measurement

- **Accreditation:** the MRC's decision, after a CPA audit and audit committee review, that a product's metric in named environments meets its standards; scoped, dated and suspendable ([Measurement Standards & the MRC](/books/advertising/measurement-standards-and-the-mrc#how-an-audit-works)).
- **Adstock:** the carryover of advertising's effect into later periods, decaying ([Marketing Mix Modeling](/books/advertising/marketing-mix-modeling#the-model)).
- **Attention:** a measure of whether an ad was seen or heard by a person and how deeply, accrued on viewable, SIVT-filtered impressions under the IAB and MRC guidelines ([Viewability & Attention](/books/advertising/viewability-and-attention#attention)).
- **Attribution window:** the longest time from a click, engaged view or view to a conversion for that interaction to get credit ([Attribution](/books/advertising/attribution#windows)).
- **Big Data + Panel:** a hybrid that calibrates big data tuning to a panel and projects persons to the full population ([Audience & Cross-Media Measurement](/books/advertising/audience-measurement#panels-big-data-and-hybrids)).
- **Click flooding (click spamming):** sending masses of fake clicks so one is the last click before organic installs ([Mobile Measurement Partners](/books/advertising/mobile-measurement-partners#fraud)).
- **Click-to-install time (CTIT):** the time from a click to the install it is credited with, whose distribution exposes click flooding and injection ([Mobile Measurement Partners](/books/advertising/mobile-measurement-partners#fraud)).
- **Conversion action:** the advertiser's definition of what a platform counts as a conversion: source, category, counting, value and bidding role ([Attribution](/books/advertising/attribution#conversion-actions)).
- **Conversion lift study:** a platform experiment that holds out a random control group from an advertiser's ads and compares conversions ([Incrementality & Lift](/books/advertising/incrementality-and-lift#user-holdouts-psas-and-why-they-break)).
- **Conversions API:** a server-to-server feed of conversions to an ad platform, with hashed customer details and an event ID shared with the browser pixel for deduplication ([Attribution](/books/advertising/attribution#receiving-conversions)).
- **Currency:** the audience measurement a buy transacts, guarantees and reconciles on ([Audience & Cross-Media Measurement](/books/advertising/audience-measurement#currencies-and-the-jic)).
- **Data-driven attribution (DDA):** a platform model that splits credit by comparing converting and non-converting paths in the advertiser's own data ([Attribution](/books/advertising/attribution#models)).
- **Duration weighted viewable impression (DWVI):** viewable duration divided by creative length, capped at completion, reported beside unweighted metrics in cross-media video ([Viewability & Attention](/books/advertising/viewability-and-attention#cross-media-100-and-duration)).
- **Engaged view:** a video watched for a qualifying duration without a click, attributed under its own window ([Attribution](/books/advertising/attribution#windows)).
- **Engage-through attribution:** Meta's 2026 name for conversions credited to non-link clicks (likes, shares, saves) and engaged views ([Attribution](/books/advertising/attribution#windows)).
- **Geo experiment:** a lift test that randomizes regions rather than users to treatment or control ([Incrementality & Lift](/books/advertising/incrementality-and-lift#geo-experiments)).
- **Ghost ads:** logging, for control users, the impressions the advertiser's ad would have won and serving the next-best ad, so exposed and would-be-exposed users can be compared ([Incrementality & Lift](/books/advertising/incrementality-and-lift#ghost-ads)).
- **Gross rating points (GRPs) and TRPs:** summed rating points across a schedule, counting duplication (reach times frequency); TRPs against the target demographic ([Audience & Cross-Media Measurement](/books/advertising/audience-measurement#the-metrics)).
- **iCPA and iROAS:** spend divided by incremental conversions, and incremental conversion value divided by spend ([Incrementality & Lift](/books/advertising/incrementality-and-lift#what-is-being-estimated)).
- **Incrementality:** the outcomes an ad caused: the difference between what happened with it and what would have happened without it ([Incrementality & Lift](/books/advertising/incrementality-and-lift)).
- **Intent-to-treat (ITT):** comparing everyone assigned to treatment with everyone assigned to control, exposed or not ([Incrementality & Lift](/books/advertising/incrementality-and-lift#what-is-being-estimated)).
- **Joint Industry Committee (JIC):** the US buyer and seller forum that certifies national TV measurement providers as currencies for fixed terms ([Audience & Cross-Media Measurement](/books/advertising/audience-measurement#currencies-and-the-jic)).
- **Last-click attribution:** giving all the credit for a conversion to the last eligible click before it ([Attribution](/books/advertising/attribution#models)).
- **Lookback window (MMP):** the period before an install in which a click or impression can be credited, set per network and method ([Mobile Measurement Partners](/books/advertising/mobile-measurement-partners#attribution-methods-and-windows)).
- **Marginal ROI (mROI):** the return on the next unit of spend in a channel at current spend, the slope of its response curve ([Marketing Mix Modeling](/books/advertising/marketing-mix-modeling#the-model)).
- **Marketing mix model (MMM):** a regression of an outcome on channel spend or exposure over time, with adstock, saturation and controls, estimating each channel's contribution from aggregate data ([Marketing Mix Modeling](/books/advertising/marketing-mix-modeling)).
- **Measured rate:** viewable plus non-viewable impressions over rendered served impressions, the share of impressions a measurer could classify ([Viewability & Attention](/books/advertising/viewability-and-attention#measured-rate-and-viewable-rate)).
- **Media Rating Council (MRC):** the US industry self-regulatory body that audits and accredits measurement products against published standards ([Measurement Standards & the MRC](/books/advertising/measurement-standards-and-the-mrc#what-the-mrc-is)).
- **Meridian and Robyn:** Google's Bayesian and Meta's regularized-regression open-source MMM libraries ([Marketing Mix Modeling](/books/advertising/marketing-mix-modeling#how-the-platforms-do-it)).
- **MMM calibration:** tying a mix model to experiments, as ROI priors in a Bayesian model or as an optimization objective (Robyn's MAPE.LIFT) ([Marketing Mix Modeling](/books/advertising/marketing-mix-modeling#calibration-with-experiments)).
- **Mobile measurement partner (MMP):** a neutral client SDK and service, such as AppsFlyer or Adjust, that attributes installs and in-app events across ad networks, sends postbacks and reports cost and ROAS ([Mobile Measurement Partners](/books/advertising/mobile-measurement-partners)).
- **Modeled conversion:** a conversion a platform estimates rather than observes, as under consent mode when cookies are denied ([Attribution](/books/advertising/attribution#modeled-conversions)).
- **Open Measurement SDK (OM SDK, OMID):** the IAB Tech Lab SDK and interface through which apps and players expose ad signals, so verification vendors measure through one integration ([Measurement Standards & the MRC](/books/advertising/measurement-standards-and-the-mrc#how-measurement-reaches-an-impression)).
- **Outcomes and Data Quality Standard:** the MRC's standard for attribution, lift and MMM, separating direct from indirect sales measures and experiments from observational methods ([Measurement Standards & the MRC](/books/advertising/measurement-standards-and-the-mrc#outcomes-and-data-quality)).
- **Panel:** a recruited sample of households or people, metered and weighted to represent a population, the direct source of persons and demographics ([Audience & Cross-Media Measurement](/books/advertising/audience-measurement#panels-big-data-and-hybrids)).
- **Platform-reported conversions:** the conversions an ad platform credits to itself under its own windows and models, stored beside the advertiser's own count, not instead of it ([Attribution](/books/advertising/attribution#platform-reported-and-advertiser-side-attribution)).
- **Postback (MMP to network):** a server-to-server notice from the MMP to a network of an attributed install or in-app event ([Mobile Measurement Partners](/books/advertising/mobile-measurement-partners#postbacks-to-networks)).
- **PSA test:** a lift test that shows the control group a public service announcement in place of the ad, biased under optimized delivery ([Incrementality & Lift](/books/advertising/incrementality-and-lift#user-holdouts-psas-and-why-they-break)).
- **Rating:** an audience as a percentage of a universe; one rating point is 1% ([Audience & Cross-Media Measurement](/books/advertising/audience-measurement#the-metrics)).
- **Reattribution and re-engagement:** crediting a lapsed user's reinstall, or a reopened installed app, to a retargeting network after an inactivity period ([Mobile Measurement Partners](/books/advertising/mobile-measurement-partners#attribution-methods-and-windows)).
- **Rejected install:** an install or engagement an MMP refused to attribute, with a reason, reported to the network ([Mobile Measurement Partners](/books/advertising/mobile-measurement-partners#fraud)).
- **Saturation (Hill curve):** diminishing returns to spend in a channel, often modeled with a Hill function ([Marketing Mix Modeling](/books/advertising/marketing-mix-modeling#the-model)).
- **Self-attributing network (SAN):** a network that doesn't accept tracking links and instead tells the MMP whether it claims an install ([Mobile Measurement Partners](/books/advertising/mobile-measurement-partners#self-attributing-networks)).
- **Synthetic control:** a counterfactual built as a weighted combination of untreated units that tracked the treated ones before the test ([Incrementality & Lift](/books/advertising/incrementality-and-lift#geo-experiments)).
- **Tracking link:** an MMP's click or impression URL for one network and campaign ([Mobile Measurement Partners](/books/advertising/mobile-measurement-partners#the-architecture)).
- **Treatment-on-the-treated (TOT):** comparing exposed treatment users with the control users who would have been exposed ([Incrementality & Lift](/books/advertising/incrementality-and-lift#what-is-being-estimated)).
- **Undetermined impression:** a served impression whose viewability the measurer could not establish, reported as its own bucket ([Viewability & Attention](/books/advertising/viewability-and-attention#measured-rate-and-viewable-rate)).
- **Universe estimate:** the population of a market and demographic that ratings divide by ([Audience & Cross-Media Measurement](/books/advertising/audience-measurement#universe-estimates)).
- **Verification vendor:** an independent company that measures viewability, invalid traffic and brand safety on impressions for buyers or sellers ([Measurement Standards & the MRC](/books/advertising/measurement-standards-and-the-mrc#how-measurement-reaches-an-impression)).
- **Viewable impression:** an impression with at least 50% of its pixels in view for one continuous second (two seconds of play for video), under the MRC's guidelines ([Viewability & Attention](/books/advertising/viewability-and-attention#the-viewable-impression)).
- **Viewable rate (viewability rate):** viewable impressions over measured (viewable plus non-viewable) impressions, under a named standard ([Viewability & Attention](/books/advertising/viewability-and-attention#measured-rate-and-viewable-rate)).
- **View-through conversion:** a conversion credited to an ad that was shown but not interacted with ([Attribution](/books/advertising/attribution#view-through)).

## Fraud and Brand Safety

- **Ad policy:** a platform's rules on what may be advertised, how, to whom and where, applied to ads, destinations and accounts ([Ad Review, Policies & Malvertising](/books/advertising/ad-review-and-malvertising#policy-as-data)).
- **Ad review:** deciding whether an ad, its assets and its destination comply with policy before and after it serves ([Ad Review, Policies & Malvertising](/books/advertising/ad-review-and-malvertising#review-in-the-pipeline)).
- **Ads Transparency Center and Ad Library:** a platform's public, searchable record of ads that ran and who ran them ([Ad Review, Policies & Malvertising](/books/advertising/ad-review-and-malvertising#transparency-centers)).
- **Advertiser verification:** a platform's check of an advertiser's identity or business operations, by program and jurisdiction, often shown in ad disclosures and transparency centers ([Ad Review, Policies & Malvertising](/books/advertising/ad-review-and-malvertising#advertiser-verification-and-account-enforcement)).
- **App spoofing:** declaring a valuable app's bundle ID in a bid request from a different app or a server; in CTV, also declaring a non-CTV app as CTV ([Invalid Traffic & Ad Fraud](/books/advertising/invalid-traffic-and-ad-fraud#the-schemes)).
- **Blocklist and inclusion list:** a list of domains, apps, channels or URLs not to buy (blocklist), or the only ones to buy (inclusion list or allowlist) ([Brand Safety & Suitability](/books/advertising/brand-safety-and-suitability#blocklists-inclusion-lists-and-keywords)).
- **Botnet:** a network of hijacked home computers or phones running hidden browsers or apps that load and click ads, classed as SIVT ([Invalid Traffic & Ad Fraud](/books/advertising/invalid-traffic-and-ad-fraud#the-schemes)).
- **Brand safety:** the floor of content no advertiser should fund at all, such as illegal content and terrorist promotion ([Brand Safety & Suitability](/books/advertising/brand-safety-and-suitability#safety-suitability-and-the-floor)).
- **Brand Safety Floor:** the baseline content categories excluded from monetization by default, an industry floor the MRC's 2018 supplement describes and GARM later maintained ([Brand Safety & Suitability](/books/advertising/brand-safety-and-suitability#safety-suitability-and-the-floor)).
- **Brand suitability:** the advertiser-chosen band above the floor: which sensitive content, at which risk tier, a given brand accepts ([Brand Safety & Suitability](/books/advertising/brand-safety-and-suitability#safety-suitability-and-the-floor)).
- **Clawback:** taking back money already paid to a seller or publisher for traffic later found invalid ([Invalid Traffic & Ad Fraud](/books/advertising/invalid-traffic-and-ad-fraud#refunds-credits-and-clawbacks)).
- **Click injection:** Android fraud in which a junk app fires a click just before another app's install completes, stealing attribution for an organic install ([Invalid Traffic & Ad Fraud](/books/advertising/invalid-traffic-and-ad-fraud#the-schemes)).
- **Cloaking:** showing an ad reviewer a compliant page and real users a different, violating one ([Ad Review, Policies & Malvertising](/books/advertising/ad-review-and-malvertising#cloaking-and-evasion)).
- **Content-level verification:** brand-safety classification of the specific page, video or post, which the MRC distinguishes from property-level classification of a whole site or app ([Brand Safety & Suitability](/books/advertising/brand-safety-and-suitability#content-level-verification-the-mrc-supplement)).
- **Device attestation:** a cryptographic token (via Privacy Pass in the Open Measurement SDK) proving an impression came from a real app on a real device ([Invalid Traffic & Ad Fraud](/books/advertising/invalid-traffic-and-ad-fraud#tag-certified-against-fraud)).
- **Domain spoofing:** selling impressions as if they came from a different, usually more valuable, site; the MRC calls it domain misrepresentation and classes it as SIVT ([Invalid Traffic & Ad Fraud](/books/advertising/invalid-traffic-and-ad-fraud#the-schemes)).
- **Editorial adjacency guidelines:** categories an IO says an advertiser's ads must not appear next to, with a make-good or credit as the remedy under the IAB/4A's terms ([Brand Safety & Suitability](/books/advertising/brand-safety-and-suitability#the-contract-remedy)).
- **GARM (Global Alliance for Responsible Media):** the WFA initiative, launched in 2019, that maintained the Brand Safety Floor and suitability framework until it was discontinued on August 9, 2024 ([Brand Safety & Suitability](/books/advertising/brand-safety-and-suitability#garm-s-end-and-the-ftc-orders)).
- **General invalid traffic (GIVT):** invalid traffic the MRC says routine filtering finds with lists or standard parameter checks: data-center traffic, declared bots, non-browser user agents, prefetch ([Invalid Traffic & Ad Fraud](/books/advertising/invalid-traffic-and-ad-fraud#givt-and-sivt)).
- **Impersonation ad:** an ad pretending to be a business, government agency or public figure, often with a lookalike domain ([Ad Review, Policies & Malvertising](/books/advertising/ad-review-and-malvertising#scams-and-impersonation)).
- **Invalid traffic (IVT):** impressions, clicks or conversions that don't come from a real person with a real chance of seeing the ad, whether innocent (crawlers, prefetch) or fraudulent ([Invalid Traffic & Ad Fraud](/books/advertising/invalid-traffic-and-ad-fraud#givt-and-sivt)).
- **IVT credit:** money returned to a buyer for invalid traffic found after billing, as a credit line on a later invoice rather than an edit to the issued one ([Invalid Traffic & Ad Fraud](/books/advertising/invalid-traffic-and-ad-fraud#refunds-credits-and-clawbacks)).
- **Keyword blocking:** avoiding pages or content whose text matches listed keywords, the coarsest of the list controls ([Brand Safety & Suitability](/books/advertising/brand-safety-and-suitability#blocklists-inclusion-lists-and-keywords)).
- **Malvertising:** malware or phishing delivered through ads, either through a malicious destination or through a creative or redirect chain that hijacks the page ([Ad Review, Policies & Malvertising](/books/advertising/ad-review-and-malvertising#malvertising)).
- **Payment ID:** TAG's follow-the-money system that intermediaries must implement on OpenRTB transactions, so buyers can avoid untrustworthy parties in the supply chain ([Invalid Traffic & Ad Fraud](/books/advertising/invalid-traffic-and-ad-fraud#tag-certified-against-fraud)).
- **Post-bid blocking:** replacing a won impression's creative with a blank or house ad when the verification tag finds the actual page unsafe; the impression is usually still paid for ([Brand Safety & Suitability](/books/advertising/brand-safety-and-suitability#pre-bid-post-bid-and-reporting)).
- **Pre-bid avoidance:** declining to bid on a request whose declared site, app or content is outside the advertiser's brand-safety policy ([Brand Safety & Suitability](/books/advertising/brand-safety-and-suitability#pre-bid-post-bid-and-reporting)).
- **SDK spoofing:** fake installs and in-app events sent to attribution providers as if from their SDK, often through malicious code in another app on real devices ([Invalid Traffic & Ad Fraud](/books/advertising/invalid-traffic-and-ad-fraud#the-schemes)).
- **Sophisticated invalid traffic (SIVT):** invalid traffic the MRC says needs advanced analytics, multi-point corroboration or human review to find: hijacked devices, emulators, click farms, falsified events, spoofing, stacked ads, adware ([Invalid Traffic & Ad Fraud](/books/advertising/invalid-traffic-and-ad-fraud#givt-and-sivt)).
- **SSAI spoofing:** a server pretending to be many CTV devices by sending server-side ad requests and tracking calls with fabricated device data ([Invalid Traffic & Ad Fraud](/books/advertising/invalid-traffic-and-ad-fraud#the-schemes)).
- **Strike:** an account-level penalty for a repeated policy violation that escalates through holds to suspension within a window ([Ad Review, Policies & Malvertising](/books/advertising/ad-review-and-malvertising#advertiser-verification-and-account-enforcement)).
- **TAG Certified Against Fraud:** TAG's certification requiring, among other things, filtering of all monetizable transactions for GIVT and SIVT, Payment ID for intermediaries, ads.txt honored and device headers on SSAI tracking ([Invalid Traffic & Ad Fraud](/books/advertising/invalid-traffic-and-ad-fraud#tag-certified-against-fraud)).
- **Trustworthy Accountability Group (TAG):** the industry body that runs certification programs against fraud, malware and piracy in the ad supply chain ([Invalid Traffic & Ad Fraud](/books/advertising/invalid-traffic-and-ad-fraud#tag-certified-against-fraud)).

## Walled Gardens

- **Actual CPC:** the amount charged for a click: the minimum needed to clear the thresholds and the ad below ([Search Advertising](/books/advertising/search-advertising#ad-rank-and-the-price-of-a-click)).
- **Ad load:** the share of feed slots that carry ads, set by spacing, caps and comparison with organic content ([Social Advertising](/books/advertising/social-advertising#ad-load-and-placement-in-feeds)).
- **Ad Rank:** the score that orders ads in a search auction, from the bid, auction-time quality, thresholds, competitiveness, context and the expected impact of assets ([Search Advertising](/books/advertising/search-advertising#ad-rank-and-the-price-of-a-click)).
- **Ad Rank threshold:** the minimum Ad Rank to show at all or in a given position ([Search Advertising](/books/advertising/search-advertising#ad-rank-and-the-price-of-a-click)).
- **Advantage+ (Meta):** Meta's automated campaigns and settings, including Advantage+ sales campaigns, which replaced Advantage+ shopping campaigns ([Social Advertising](/books/advertising/social-advertising#automated-campaigns)).
- **AI Max for Search:** Google's extension of Search campaigns with query matching beyond keywords, text customization and URL expansion ([Search Advertising](/books/advertising/search-advertising#performance-max-ai-max-and-the-end-of-dynamic-search-ads)).
- **Apple Ads:** Apple's ad platform (formerly Apple Search Ads), selling App Store search results and other placements, with clicks priced as cost per tap (CPT) ([Search Advertising](/books/advertising/search-advertising#app-store-search-apple-ads)).
- **Assets (search):** headlines, descriptions, sitelinks, images and other parts the platform combines into a search ad per auction ([Search Advertising](/books/advertising/search-advertising#assets-and-ad-formats)).
- **Campaign objective:** the outcome a social campaign is built for (awareness, traffic, leads, sales, app promotion), which limits its optimization goals and billing events ([Social Advertising](/books/advertising/social-advertising#objectives-optimization-goals-and-billing-events)).
- **Change history:** the record of every configuration change with its actor and client ([Self-Serve Ad Platforms](/books/advertising/self-serve-ad-platforms#change-history)).
- **Closed-loop measurement:** a network attributing sales it observes in its own transactions to its own ads ([Retail Media Networks](/books/advertising/retail-media-networks#closed-loop-measurement)).
- **Delivery system:** a social platform's pipeline that retrieves, ranks and auctions ads for each feed slot, predicting each ad's action rate for each person ([Social Advertising](/books/advertising/social-advertising#the-delivery-system)).
- **Halo sales:** attributed sales of an advertiser's other products, not the advertised item ([Retail Media Networks](/books/advertising/retail-media-networks#closed-loop-measurement)).
- **In-store media:** a retailer's screens, audio and shelf placements in physical stores, sold beside its digital inventory ([Retail Media Networks](/books/advertising/retail-media-networks#in-store-media)).
- **Keyword:** a word or phrase an advertiser bids on so its ads can show for related searches ([Search Advertising](/books/advertising/search-advertising#keywords-and-match-types)).
- **Manager account:** an account that manages other accounts on a platform (Google Ads' term), the agency structure ([Self-Serve Ad Platforms](/books/advertising/self-serve-ad-platforms#accounts-roles-and-agency-access)).
- **Match type:** the rule for which searches a keyword can match: broad, phrase or exact, now defined by meaning ([Search Advertising](/books/advertising/search-advertising#keywords-and-match-types)).
- **Negative keyword:** a term that keeps ads out of searches containing it ([Search Advertising](/books/advertising/search-advertising#keywords-and-match-types)).
- **Offsite extension:** a retail network carrying its shopper audiences into other platforms' inventory ([Retail Media Networks](/books/advertising/retail-media-networks#offsite-extension)).
- **Partial failure:** an API mode that commits the successful operations in a batch and returns errors for the failed ones ([Self-Serve Ad Platforms](/books/advertising/self-serve-ad-platforms#the-api)).
- **Performance Max:** Google's goal-based campaign type serving across all Google inventory from asset groups and search themes ([Search Advertising](/books/advertising/search-advertising#performance-max-ai-max-and-the-end-of-dynamic-search-ads)).
- **Quality Score:** Google Ads' 1 to 10 keyword diagnostic; not an input in the auction ([Search Advertising](/books/advertising/search-advertising#quality-score-as-a-diagnostic)).
- **Restatement window:** the period during which a platform's reported numbers may still change ([Self-Serve Ad Platforms](/books/advertising/self-serve-ad-platforms#reporting)).
- **Retail media network as a service:** a provider running a retailer's ad network on its own platform, such as Carrot Ads or Criteo ([Retail Media Networks](/books/advertising/retail-media-networks#build-or-rent)).
- **Retail media network (RMN):** a retailer selling ads on its sites, apps, stores and partners' inventory using shopper data, with sales as the measured outcome ([Retail Media Networks](/books/advertising/retail-media-networks)).
- **Search terms report:** the actual queries that triggered ads, with their metrics and matched keywords ([Search Advertising](/books/advertising/search-advertising#search-terms-and-negatives)).
- **Search theme:** a Performance Max input steering query matching, with the same priority as phrase and broad match keywords ([Search Advertising](/books/advertising/search-advertising#performance-max-ai-max-and-the-end-of-dynamic-search-ads)).
- **Self-serve ad platform:** a buying tool (an Ads Manager) where advertisers create accounts, campaigns and creatives, pay and report without a sales contract ([Self-Serve Ad Platforms](/books/advertising/self-serve-ad-platforms#onboarding-and-advertiser-verification)).
- **Sponsored brand:** an onsite retail ad for a brand rather than one product, priced by click, viewable impression or reserved share of voice (Amazon's Sponsored Brands) ([Retail Media Networks](/books/advertising/retail-media-networks#onsite-display-and-brand-formats)).
- **Sponsored product:** a retailer's onsite cost-per-click ad that promotes a buyable offer among product results ([Retail Media Networks](/books/advertising/retail-media-networks#onsite-sponsored-products)).

## Channels

- **Ad buffet:** extra ads returned with a pod to replace pod ads that fail to play (Google Ad Manager's term) ([Server-Side Ad Insertion & Ad Pods](/books/advertising/server-side-ad-insertion#filling-a-pod)).
- **Ad pod:** a break of several ads played back to back, requested in OpenRTB 2.6 with a total duration and slot count; structured, dynamic or hybrid ([Server-Side Ad Insertion & Ad Pods](/books/advertising/server-side-ad-insertion#ad-pods-in-openrtb)).
- **Adapter (mediation):** the code that connects a mediation SDK to one network's SDK, versioned against both ([In-App Advertising & Mediation](/books/advertising/in-app-advertising#the-in-app-stack)).
- **Addressable TV:** sending different ads to different households in the same linear spot position, through set-top boxes, smart TVs or streamed linear ([Linear TV, Upfronts & Addressable TV](/books/advertising/linear-tv-and-upfronts#addressable-tv)).
- **Ad-ID:** the US ad registry's creative identification code, run by a company of the 4A's and the ANA ([Linear TV, Upfronts & Addressable TV](/books/advertising/linear-tv-and-upfronts#traffic-and-the-as-run-log)).
- **As-run log:** the record of what actually aired: each spot's time, break, program, length, creative ID and status ([Linear TV, Upfronts & Addressable TV](/books/advertising/linear-tv-and-upfronts#traffic-and-the-as-run-log)).
- **Audience deficiency units (ADUs):** additional spots a TV seller provides to make up a ratings guarantee shortfall ([Linear TV, Upfronts & Addressable TV](/books/advertising/linear-tv-and-upfronts#guarantees-and-make-goods)).
- **Automatic content recognition (ACR):** a smart TV's identification of what is on screen by matching audio or video fingerprints, used for targeting, attribution and TV measurement ([Connected TV & Streaming](/books/advertising/connected-tv#automatic-content-recognition)).
- **Baked-in ad:** an ad recorded into a podcast episode's audio, heard by everyone who downloads the episode ([Audio & Podcasts](/books/advertising/audio-and-podcasts#podcast-ad-models)).
- **Competitive separation:** keeping competing advertisers, the same advertiser or the same creative out of one pod or break ([Server-Side Ad Insertion & Ad Pods](/books/advertising/server-side-ad-insertion#competitive-separation-and-deduplication)).
- **Connected TV (CTV):** video delivered over the internet and watched on a television, the MRC's device-based definition, as distinct from the broader content term OTT ([Connected TV & Streaming](/books/advertising/connected-tv#the-supply-chain)).
- **Digital out-of-home (DOOH):** advertising on digital screens in public places, sold by share of loop or by individual plays ([Digital Out-of-Home](/books/advertising/digital-out-of-home)).
- **Dynamic ad insertion (podcasts):** stitching ads into a podcast file at download time, chosen for that request ([Audio & Podcasts](/books/advertising/audio-and-podcasts#podcast-ad-models)).
- **FAST:** free ad-supported streaming TV: scheduled linear channels delivered over the internet, often assembled from on-demand libraries ([Connected TV & Streaming](/books/advertising/connected-tv#the-supply-chain)).
- **Host-read ad:** an ad read by the podcast's host, baked-in or dynamically inserted, and subject to endorsement disclosure rules ([Audio & Podcasts](/books/advertising/audio-and-podcasts#podcast-ad-models)).
- **Impression multiplier:** the estimated number of impressions one DOOH play produces, carried in OpenRTB's `qty` object and used to price the play ([Digital Out-of-Home](/books/advertising/digital-out-of-home#the-programmatic-request)).
- **Impression-level revenue:** the value of each in-app impression as the mediation SDK reports it, in micros with a precision such as exact or estimated ([In-App Advertising & Mediation](/books/advertising/in-app-advertising#impression-level-revenue)).
- **In-app bidding:** a real-time auction among ad networks for an in-app impression, run by the mediation platform's server from bid signals the SDK collects ([In-App Advertising & Mediation](/books/advertising/in-app-advertising#waterfalls-and-in-app-bidding)).
- **InLine:** a VAST response that contains the ad itself: media files, tracking and creative details ([VAST & Video Ads](/books/advertising/vast-and-video-ads#vast-in-one-page)).
- **Intrinsic in-game ad:** an ad rendered as part of a game world, such as a billboard texture, measured under the IAB and MRC Intrinsic In-Game guidelines ([Ads in Games](/books/advertising/ads-in-games#intrinsic-in-game-advertising)).
- **Inventory share:** the portion of an app's ad breaks a TV platform sells itself under its distribution agreement ([Connected TV & Streaming](/books/advertising/connected-tv#how-the-money-moves)).
- **Likelihood to see (LTS):** the MRC's OOH impression level that adjusts viewable impressions for evidence the ad was noticed ([Digital Out-of-Home](/books/advertising/digital-out-of-home#how-buyers-count-it)).
- **Loop:** a fixed cycle of content and ad positions that repeats on a DOOH screen ([Digital Out-of-Home](/books/advertising/digital-out-of-home#screens-venues-and-loops)).
- **Mediation:** software in an app that chooses among several ad networks for each ad request, by bidding, a waterfall or both ([In-App Advertising & Mediation](/books/advertising/in-app-advertising#the-in-app-stack)).
- **Mezzanine file:** the high-quality source video in VAST that server-side insertion transcodes to match the content stream; never played directly ([VAST & Video Ads](/books/advertising/vast-and-video-ads#renditions-and-transcoding)).
- **Offerwall:** a list of tasks that each pay an in-app reward when a conversion is confirmed ([Ads in Games](/books/advertising/ads-in-games#offerwalls)).
- **OpenOOH venue taxonomy:** the industry list of DOOH venue types, with nested parent, child and grandchild IDs ([Digital Out-of-Home](/books/advertising/digital-out-of-home#screens-venues-and-loops)).
- **OTT (over the top):** video content delivered over the internet rather than by cable, satellite or broadcast, on any device ([Connected TV & Streaming](/books/advertising/connected-tv#the-supply-chain)).
- **Playable ad:** an interactive HTML5 demo of a game, usually ending in an install button ([Ads in Games](/books/advertising/ads-in-games#playables)).
- **Podcast consumer:** IAB Tech Lab's v2.3 term (formerly listener) for a unique IP address and user agent combination over a stated period ([Audio & Podcasts](/books/advertising/audio-and-podcasts#counting-podcast-downloads)).
- **Podcast download:** under IAB Tech Lab's v2.3 guidelines, a unique episode request that delivers the episode, counting partial downloads of at least the header plus one minute ([Audio & Podcasts](/books/advertising/audio-and-podcasts#counting-podcast-downloads)).
- **Proof of play:** evidence, usually from the screen player's logs, that a DOOH ad played as sold ([Digital Out-of-Home](/books/advertising/digital-out-of-home#proof-of-play)).
- **Quartile events:** the VAST tracking events fired at a video ad's start, first quartile, midpoint, third quartile and completion ([VAST & Video Ads](/books/advertising/vast-and-video-ads#tracking-events)).
- **Ratings guarantee:** a seller's promise of an audience in a demographic on a named currency, settled with additional airtime if delivery falls short ([Linear TV, Upfronts & Addressable TV](/books/advertising/linear-tv-and-upfronts#guarantees-and-make-goods)).
- **Rewarded ad:** a video or playable an app user chooses to watch for an in-app reward, granted only on the network's signed server-side callback ([Ads in Games](/books/advertising/ads-in-games#rewarded-ads)).
- **Rewarded interstitial:** a rewarded ad shown at a transition without an opt-in prompt, after an intro screen with a skip option ([In-App Advertising & Mediation](/books/advertising/in-app-advertising#formats)).
- **Scatter:** TV time bought close to when it airs, at prices that can differ from upfront pricing ([Linear TV, Upfronts & Addressable TV](/books/advertising/linear-tv-and-upfronts#the-markets)).
- **Server-guided ad insertion (SGAI):** a model in which a shared, cacheable manifest marks breaks as HLS interstitials and each player fetches its own ads ([Server-Side Ad Insertion & Ad Pods](/books/advertising/server-side-ad-insertion#server-guided-ad-insertion)).
- **Server-side ad insertion (SSAI):** stitching ads into a video or audio stream at a server, so the player plays content and ads as one stream and the server fires tracking on the device's behalf ([Server-Side Ad Insertion & Ad Pods](/books/advertising/server-side-ad-insertion#how-ssai-works)).
- **Server-side verification (SSV) callback:** a network's signed server-to-server call to a game's server saying a reward was earned ([Ads in Games](/books/advertising/ads-in-games#rewarded-ads)).
- **SIMID:** Secure Interactive Media Interface Definition, the sandboxed interactive layer that replaced VPAID's interactivity ([VAST & Video Ads](/books/advertising/vast-and-video-ads#from-vpaid-to-simid-and-omid)).
- **TV off:** a CTV device playing content and ads while the television it drives is switched off; events confirmed in that state are not counted as impressions ([Connected TV & Streaming](/books/advertising/connected-tv#measuring-ctv)).
- **Universal Ad ID:** VAST's creative identifier with its registry (such as Ad-ID), the same across every system that handles the creative ([VAST & Video Ads](/books/advertising/vast-and-video-ads#vast-in-one-page)).
- **Upfront:** the forward market in which advertisers commit to a season's TV and streaming time, usually with ratings guarantees ([Linear TV, Upfronts & Addressable TV](/books/advertising/linear-tv-and-upfronts#the-markets)).
- **VAST:** IAB Tech Lab's Video Ad Serving Template, the XML response that tells a player which media to play and which tracking to fire; 4.3 is the latest published version ([VAST & Video Ads](/books/advertising/vast-and-video-ads#vast-in-one-page)).
- **VAST error code:** the numbered reason a player reports through the Error URL when an ad fails, such as a wrapper limit or an unsupported media file ([VAST & Video Ads](/books/advertising/vast-and-video-ads#error-codes)).
- **VMAP:** Video Multiple Ad Playlist, the IAB Tech Lab format describing when ad breaks occur in a piece of content ([VAST & Video Ads](/books/advertising/vast-and-video-ads#vmap)).
- **VPAID:** the deprecated Video Player-Ad Interface Definition, which ran ad code inside the player, replaced by SIMID and OMID ([VAST & Video Ads](/books/advertising/vast-and-video-ads#from-vpaid-to-simid-and-omid)).
- **Wrapper (VAST):** a VAST response that points to another VAST document and adds its own tracking; chains should be limited to five wrappers ([VAST & Video Ads](/books/advertising/vast-and-video-ads#wrappers-and-chains)).

## Privacy and Law

- **Ad repository:** a public, searchable record of the ads a very large online platform served, kept until one year after an ad's last impression (DSA Article 39) ([DSA, DMA & Political Ads](/books/advertising/dsa-dma-and-political-ads#ad-repositories)).
- **Adapted disclaimer:** an FEC-permitted short "paid for by" statement with an indicator and a one-action path to the full disclaimer, for internet ads where a full one won't fit ([DSA, DMA & Political Ads](/books/advertising/dsa-dma-and-political-ads#us-political-ad-law)).
- **Ads mode:** this volume's single per-request field (personalized, non-personalized, limited) derived once from every consent and legal signal and read by every downstream system ([Consent Management & the TCF](/books/advertising/consent-and-the-tcf#the-consent-signal-model)).
- **Age treatment:** Google's ad SDK setting (child, teen, unspecified) that replaced TFCD and TFUA and applies ad serving protections ([Children, Teens & Advertising](/books/advertising/children-and-teens#signals-on-the-request)).
- **Article 5(3):** the ePrivacy Directive rule requiring consent for storing or reading information on a user's device unless strictly necessary or for transmission ([GDPR & ePrivacy for Ads](/books/advertising/gdpr-and-eprivacy-for-ads#article-5-3-device-access)).
- **Audience composition standard:** an industry or state rule that an ad run only where a minimum share of the audience is of legal age, as the alcohol codes and California cannabis rules require ([Advertising Content Law](/books/advertising/advertising-content-law#regulated-categories)).
- **CCPA and CPRA:** California's consumer privacy law as amended by the CPRA, which gives opt-outs of sale and of sharing for cross-context behavioral advertising, enforced by the CPPA and the Attorney General ([US State Privacy Law for Advertising](/books/advertising/us-state-privacy-law#the-state-laws-in-force)).
- **Child-directed property:** a site, app or content item whose primary audience is children under 13, on which every request is treated as a child's under COPPA ([Children, Teens & Advertising](/books/advertising/children-and-teens#child-directed-and-mixed-audience-properties)).
- **CJEU C-604/22 (IAB Europe):** the EU Court of Justice's 2024 ruling that a TC string can be personal data and that IAB Europe can be a joint controller for the framework ([Consent Management & the TCF](/books/advertising/consent-and-the-tcf#the-tcf-in-court)).
- **Clear and conspicuous:** the FTC's standard for a disclosure that consumers will notice and understand, in the same format as the claim it qualifies ([Advertising Content Law](/books/advertising/advertising-content-law#the-ftc-act)).
- **Consent management platform (CMP):** the banner, settings and script that ask for, store and expose a visitor's privacy choices to ad tags; registered with IAB Europe for the TCF ([Consent Management & the TCF](/books/advertising/consent-and-the-tcf#what-a-cmp-does)).
- **Consent mode:** Google's (and Microsoft's) tag-level consent signals (`ad_storage`, `ad_user_data`, `ad_personalization`, `analytics_storage`) for advertiser-side tags ([Consent Management & the TCF](/books/advertising/consent-and-the-tcf#google-s-certified-cmps-and-consent-mode)).
- **COPPA:** the US rule for online services directed to children under 13 or with actual knowledge of one, which needs verifiable parental consent before persistent identifiers are used for behavioral ads ([Children, Teens & Advertising](/books/advertising/children-and-teens#coppa-for-ad-systems)).
- **COPPA 2025 amendments:** the FTC's revised COPPA Rule, with compliance from April 22, 2026, adding separate parental consent for disclosing children's data to third parties such as ad networks ([Children, Teens & Advertising](/books/advertising/children-and-teens#the-2025-amendments)).
- **COPPA flag (regs.coppa):** the OpenRTB field marking a request as subject to COPPA, which strips identifiers and personalization downstream ([Children, Teens & Advertising](/books/advertising/children-and-teens#signals-on-the-request)).
- **Cross-context behavioral advertising:** targeting ads using a person's activity across businesses, sites, apps or services other than the one they intentionally interact with ([US State Privacy Law for Advertising](/books/advertising/us-state-privacy-law#sale-sharing-and-targeted-advertising)).
- **Data (Use and Access) Act (DUAA):** the UK's 2025 law that rewrote the PECR device-access rule with exceptions (strictly necessary, statistical, appearance and function) and a power to add more, so far without an advertising exception ([GDPR & ePrivacy for Ads](/books/advertising/gdpr-and-eprivacy-for-ads#the-uk-after-the-duaa)).
- **Delivery skew:** an ad reaching a demographically different audience than its targeting allowed, because the optimizer chose who saw it ([Sensitive Data & Discriminatory Targeting](/books/advertising/sensitive-data-and-discriminatory-targeting#targeting-versus-delivery)).
- **Digital Markets Act (DMA):** the EU law on gatekeepers whose Article 5(2) needs a user's consent to combine personal data across a gatekeeper's services for ads ([DSA, DMA & Political Ads](/books/advertising/dsa-dma-and-political-ads#the-dma-and-cross-service-data)).
- **Digital Services Act (DSA):** the EU law whose Article 26 requires per-ad disclosures and bars profiling ads on special categories, Article 28 bars profiling ads to minors and Article 39 requires ad repositories ([DSA, DMA & Political Ads](/books/advertising/dsa-dma-and-political-ads#the-dsa-at-the-ad)).
- **disclosedVendors:** the TC string segment, mandatory from TCF v2.3 (February 28, 2026), marking which vendors were shown to the user ([Consent Management & the TCF](/books/advertising/consent-and-the-tcf#versions-and-deadlines)).
- **Establishment claim:** a claim that states a level of proof ("clinically proven"), which must be substantiated at that level ([Advertising Content Law](/books/advertising/advertising-content-law#the-ftc-act)).
- **EU political advertising regulation:** Regulation (EU) 2024/900 on the transparency and targeting of political advertising, applying from October 10, 2025 ([DSA, DMA & Political Ads](/books/advertising/dsa-dma-and-political-ads#eu-political-advertising)).
- **Geofence ban:** a state rule forbidding virtual boundaries within a set distance of sensitive facilities to track, collect from or advertise to people there ([Sensitive Data & Discriminatory Targeting](/books/advertising/sensitive-data-and-discriminatory-targeting#location-and-sensitive-places)).
- **Global Privacy Control (GPC):** a browser signal (`Sec-GPC: 1`, `navigator.globalPrivacyControl`) that California requires businesses to honor as an opt-out of sale and sharing ([GPP, GPC & US Opt-Outs](/books/advertising/gpp-gpc-and-us-opt-outs#global-privacy-control)).
- **Global Privacy Platform (GPP):** IAB Tech Lab's container string for privacy signals from many jurisdictions, a header plus tilde-separated sections, carried in `regs.gpp` ([GPP, GPC & US Opt-Outs](/books/advertising/gpp-gpc-and-us-opt-outs#the-gpp-string)).
- **Global Vendor List (GVL):** IAB Europe's published list of registered vendors with the purposes, legal bases, features and retention each declares, versioned weekly ([Consent Management & the TCF](/books/advertising/consent-and-the-tcf#purposes-features-and-legal-bases)).
- **gpp_sid:** the OpenRTB field listing which GPP section IDs govern the transaction ([GPP, GPC & US Opt-Outs](/books/advertising/gpp-gpc-and-us-opt-outs#the-gpp-string)).
- **Joint controller:** one of two or more controllers that jointly determine the purposes and means of processing (GDPR Article 26) ([GDPR & ePrivacy for Ads](/books/advertising/gdpr-and-eprivacy-for-ads#controllers-in-the-ad-chain)).
- **Lanham Act (false advertising):** the federal law under which a competitor can sue over false or misleading claims about its own or a rival's products ([Advertising Content Law](/books/advertising/advertising-content-law#competitors-and-self-regulation)).
- **Legitimate interest assessment:** the documented three-part test (interest, necessity, balancing) behind processing on legitimate interest ([GDPR & ePrivacy for Ads](/books/advertising/gdpr-and-eprivacy-for-ads#lawful-bases-for-advertising)).
- **Limited ads:** Google's third tier of ads, served without device storage, identifiers or personalization when consent for storage is missing or the string is invalid ([Consent Management & the TCF](/books/advertising/consent-and-the-tcf#google-s-certified-cmps-and-consent-mode)).
- **Limited Data Use (LDU):** Meta's data processing option under which it processes data from listed US states as a service provider or processor ([GPP, GPC & US Opt-Outs](/books/advertising/gpp-gpc-and-us-opt-outs#turning-signals-into-a-decision)).
- **Lowest unit charge:** the federal rule that a broadcast station charge a legally qualified candidate no more than its lowest rate for the same class and amount of time in the 45 days before a primary and 60 days before a general election ([DSA, DMA & Political Ads](/books/advertising/dsa-dma-and-political-ads#us-political-ad-law)).
- **Made for Kids:** YouTube's per-video audience label, required after its FTC settlement, that decides whether viewers are treated as children for ads ([Children, Teens & Advertising](/books/advertising/children-and-teens#kids-apps-games-and-ctv)).
- **Maryland Online Data Privacy Act:** the state law, in force since October 2025, that bans selling sensitive data even with consent and allows processing it only when strictly necessary for a requested product ([US State Privacy Law for Advertising](/books/advertising/us-state-privacy-law#maryland)).
- **Mixed audience:** a COPPA service directed to children but not primarily, which must screen age neutrally before collecting personal information ([Children, Teens & Advertising](/books/advertising/children-and-teens#child-directed-and-mixed-audience-properties)).
- **Multi-State Privacy Agreement (MSPA):** IAB Privacy's springing contract among signatories that supplies the contract terms US state laws require when ad data flows between them ([GPP, GPC & US Opt-Outs](/books/advertising/gpp-gpc-and-us-opt-outs#the-us-national-section-and-the-mspa)).
- **My Health My Data Act (MHMDA):** Washington's consumer health data law, requiring consent to collect or share health data and covering inferences and geofences near health facilities ([Sensitive Data & Discriminatory Targeting](/books/advertising/sensitive-data-and-discriminatory-targeting#health-data)).
- **National Advertising Division (NAD):** BBB National Programs' self-regulatory forum where competitors challenge ad claims and advertisers defend their substantiation ([Advertising Content Law](/books/advertising/advertising-content-law#competitors-and-self-regulation)).
- **Net impression:** the FTC's rule that an ad is judged by the overall message it conveys to reasonable consumers, not by isolated words ([Advertising Content Law](/books/advertising/advertising-content-law#the-ftc-act)).
- **Pay or consent:** offering access without personalized ads for a fee as the alternative to consenting to them ([GDPR & ePrivacy for Ads](/books/advertising/gdpr-and-eprivacy-for-ads#pay-or-consent)).
- **Persistent opt-out:** an opt-out attached to a browser, device or account that outlives one request and must be applied and passed on to partners ([GPP, GPC & US Opt-Outs](/books/advertising/gpp-gpc-and-us-opt-outs#propagating-opt-outs-through-the-chain)).
- **Political file:** a broadcast licensee's online record of political time requests and sales, kept two years under 47 CFR 73.1943 ([DSA, DMA & Political Ads](/books/advertising/dsa-dma-and-political-ads#us-political-ad-law)).
- **Publisher restriction:** a publisher's TCF setting that forbids a vendor a purpose or forces it onto consent or legitimate interest ([Consent Management & the TCF](/books/advertising/consent-and-the-tcf#turning-a-string-into-a-decision)).
- **Purpose (TCF):** one of the TCF's numbered uses of data (store and access information on a device, create a personalized ads profile, measure ad performance and others) that users consent or object to per vendor ([Consent Management & the TCF](/books/advertising/consent-and-the-tcf#purposes-features-and-legal-bases)).
- **Reasonable basis:** the substantiation an advertiser must have and rely on for an objective claim before making it ([Advertising Content Law](/books/advertising/advertising-content-law#the-ftc-act)).
- **Restricted data processing (RDP):** Google's publisher setting for US state law under which it limits its data use and serves only non-personalized ads ([GPP, GPC & US Opt-Outs](/books/advertising/gpp-gpc-and-us-opt-outs#turning-signals-into-a-decision)).
- **Risk assessment (CPPA):** a documented assessment California requires before selling or sharing personal information or processing sensitive data ([US State Privacy Law for Advertising](/books/advertising/us-state-privacy-law#risk-assessments-and-automated-decisions)).
- **Sale (CCPA):** disclosing personal information to a third party for money or other valuable consideration ([US State Privacy Law for Advertising](/books/advertising/us-state-privacy-law#sale-sharing-and-targeted-advertising)).
- **Sensitive personal information:** a statutory list (in California, precise geolocation within 1,850 feet, health, sexual orientation, citizenship and more) subject to limits, opt-in or sale bans by state ([US State Privacy Law for Advertising](/books/advertising/us-state-privacy-law#sensitive-data-under-state-law)).
- **Service provider (CCPA):** a party processing personal information for a business under a written contract with the required terms, so disclosure to it is not a sale ([US State Privacy Law for Advertising](/books/advertising/us-state-privacy-law#contracts-and-roles)).
- **Sharing (CCPA):** disclosing personal information to a third party for cross-context behavioral advertising, paid or not ([US State Privacy Law for Advertising](/books/advertising/us-state-privacy-law#sale-sharing-and-targeted-advertising)).
- **Special ad category:** a campaign-level declaration (housing, employment, financial products, political) that restricts targeting and triggers delivery monitoring ([Sensitive Data & Discriminatory Targeting](/books/advertising/sensitive-data-and-discriminatory-targeting#special-ad-categories)).
- **Special categories (GDPR):** the GDPR Article 9 data (health, religion, sexual orientation, ethnicity, politics and others) that the DSA forbids using for profiling-based ads ([Sensitive Data & Discriminatory Targeting](/books/advertising/sensitive-data-and-discriminatory-targeting#the-eu-special-categories-in-ads)).
- **Support for internal operations:** COPPA's exception allowing persistent identifiers without parental consent for uses such as contextual ads and frequency capping, never behavioral advertising ([Children, Teens & Advertising](/books/advertising/children-and-teens#coppa-for-ad-systems)).
- **Targeted advertising (state laws):** ads based on a person's activity over time across unaffiliated sites or apps; most state laws give a right to opt out of it ([US State Privacy Law for Advertising](/books/advertising/us-state-privacy-law#sale-sharing-and-targeted-advertising)).
- **TC string:** the base64url-encoded TCF record of a user's purpose, vendor, special feature and disclosure choices, carried in `user.consent` and ad tag macros ([Consent Management & the TCF](/books/advertising/consent-and-the-tcf#the-tc-string)).
- **Transparency and Consent Framework (TCF):** IAB Europe's standard for recording EU and UK users' choices about purposes and vendors in a TC string every ad tech vendor can read ([Consent Management & the TCF](/books/advertising/consent-and-the-tcf#what-a-cmp-does)).
- **Transparency notice:** the EU political ads regulation's per-ad disclosure of sponsor, amounts, period and methodology, kept seven years ([DSA, DMA & Political Ads](/books/advertising/dsa-dma-and-political-ads#eu-political-advertising)).
- **Triggering term:** a credit ad term (down payment, number or amount of payments, finance charge) that requires further Regulation Z disclosures ([Advertising Content Law](/books/advertising/advertising-content-law#consumer-credit-and-deposits)).
- **US National section (usnat):** GPP section 7, the MSPA's signal: MSPA version, notice, opt-outs and the first party's signatory status, plus GPC ([GPP, GPC & US Opt-Outs](/books/advertising/gpp-gpc-and-us-opt-outs#the-us-national-section-and-the-mspa)).
- **Variance Reduction System (VRS):** Meta's settlement-required system that measures and reduces demographic differences between a housing, employment or credit ad's eligible and actual audiences ([Sensitive Data & Discriminatory Targeting](/books/advertising/sensitive-data-and-discriminatory-targeting#special-ad-categories)).

## Contracts and Money

- **10% rule (discrepancy rule):** under the IAB/4A's terms, a gap over 10% where the controlling count is lower triggers reconciliation, then under-delivery or payment on the controlling count plus 10% ([Reconciliation & Discrepancies](/books/advertising/reconciliation-and-discrepancies#the-10-rule)).
- **Billing profile:** who is billed for an account or IO, in which currency and calendar, how invoices are grouped and which references they must carry ([Billing & Invoicing](/books/advertising/billing-and-invoicing#what-an-invoice-must-carry)).
- **Billing threshold:** the accrued amount at which a self-serve card account is charged automatically, raised as the account builds payment history ([Advertiser Payments & Credit](/books/advertising/advertiser-payments-and-credit#billing-thresholds)).
- **Broadcast calendar:** the billing calendar in which weeks run Monday to Sunday and each month ends on its last Sunday, giving four- or five-week months ([Billing & Invoicing](/books/advertising/billing-and-invoicing#billing-periods-and-the-broadcast-calendar)).
- **Consideration payable to a customer:** payments such as rebates to a customer, treated as a reduction of revenue unless they are for a distinct service ([Advertising Revenue Accounting](/books/advertising/advertising-revenue-accounting#rebates-consideration-payable-to-a-customer)).
- **Consolidated billing:** one invoice covering many ad accounts under a paying manager or agency, without changing which advertiser each line belongs to ([Billing & Invoicing](/books/advertising/billing-and-invoicing#agency-and-consolidated-billing)).
- **Contract liability:** prepaid consideration for ads not yet delivered, recognized as revenue as they deliver ([Advertising Revenue Accounting](/books/advertising/advertising-revenue-accounting#recognizing-revenue-as-ads-deliver)).
- **Controlling measurement:** the count an insertion order names as the one invoices are based on, usually the third-party ad server's if it complies and reports daily ([Insertion Orders & Standard Terms](/books/advertising/insertion-orders-and-terms#third-party-ad-serving-and-the-controlling-measurement)).
- **Credit memo:** a separate document reducing what a buyer owes, applied to an outstanding invoice and numbered in its own series ([Billing & Invoicing](/books/advertising/billing-and-invoicing#credits-corrections-and-credit-memos)).
- **Custom material:** custom content or development for an advertiser, invoiced as its own IO line and owed even when the media is canceled ([Insertion Orders & Standard Terms](/books/advertising/insertion-orders-and-terms#cancellation-and-short-rates)).
- **Discrepancy:** the gap between the controlling count and another party's count over the invoice period, with a direction and a denominator ([Reconciliation & Discrepancies](/books/advertising/reconciliation-and-discrepancies#the-10-rule)).
- **First-party and third-party counts:** the seller's own ad server count and the buyer's independent ad server or verification count of the same delivery ([Reconciliation & Discrepancies](/books/advertising/reconciliation-and-discrepancies#first-party-and-third-party-counts)).
- **Force majeure:** the IO clause excusing performance for events outside a party's control, with the right to cancel after a stated period ([Insertion Orders & Standard Terms](/books/advertising/insertion-orders-and-terms#force-majeure-and-the-rest)).
- **Gross spend:** what a buyer's clients spend through a platform, including the platform's fee; for an agent reporting net, larger than revenue ([Advertising Revenue Accounting](/books/advertising/advertising-revenue-accounting#gross-spend-revenue-and-contribution-ex-tac)).
- **IAB General Terms and Direct Buy Addendum:** the modular IAB framework replacing 3.0, final May 20, 2026: a common core plus addenda by type of relationship ([Insertion Orders & Standard Terms](/books/advertising/insertion-orders-and-terms#the-2026-iab-framework)).
- **IAB/4A's Standard Terms and Conditions 3.0:** the 2010 standard terms for US digital media buys of one year or less, incorporated into most direct IOs ([Insertion Orders & Standard Terms](/books/advertising/insertion-orders-and-terms#what-an-io-contains)).
- **Insertion order (DV360, Microsoft Advertising):** on those platforms, a budget and spend cap over line items rather than a contract with a seller ([Insertion Orders & Standard Terms](/books/advertising/insertion-orders-and-terms#what-an-io-contains)).
- **Invalid activity deduction:** publisher earnings taken back for traffic later found invalid ([Publisher Payouts & Revenue Share](/books/advertising/publisher-payouts#invalid-activity-deductions-and-clawbacks)).
- **Invoice run:** the period-close job that turns billable counts and fee terms into invoices and lines, once per period and repeatable without duplicates ([Billing & Invoicing](/books/advertising/billing-and-invoicing#the-invoice-run)).
- **Monthly invoicing:** a platform credit line billed monthly on net terms ([Advertiser Payments & Credit](/books/advertising/advertiser-payments-and-credit#monthly-invoicing-and-credit-lines)).
- **Net terms (publisher):** the days after the earnings month ends until payment is due ([Publisher Payouts & Revenue Share](/books/advertising/publisher-payouts#net-terms-and-paying-before-collecting)).
- **Payment fraud (self-serve):** stolen cards, card testing and chargebacks on a self-serve platform's own billing, which turn ad spend into a way to spend money that was never there ([Advertiser Payments & Credit](/books/advertising/advertiser-payments-and-credit#fraud-on-the-platform-s-own-billing)).
- **Payment hold:** a recorded reason a due payout is not released ([Publisher Payouts & Revenue Share](/books/advertising/publisher-payouts#payment-holds)).
- **Payment threshold:** the minimum balance a network pays out; below it the balance rolls over ([Publisher Payouts & Revenue Share](/books/advertising/publisher-payouts#thresholds-and-rollover)).
- **Prepay:** funds added before delivery and drawn down by spend ([Advertiser Payments & Credit](/books/advertising/advertiser-payments-and-credit#prepaid-balances)).
- **Principal versus agent:** whether an entity controls the ad space before it transfers (gross revenue) or arranges it for others (net revenue, its fee) ([Advertising Revenue Accounting](/books/advertising/advertising-revenue-accounting#principal-or-agent)).
- **Proof of performance:** the delivery evidence a publisher provides for an invoiced period on the agency's request ([Billing & Invoicing](/books/advertising/billing-and-invoicing#what-an-invoice-must-carry)).
- **Publisher statement:** the period's record of a publisher's earnings, deductions, holds and payout, the publisher-side counterpart to an invoice ([Publisher Payouts & Revenue Share](/books/advertising/publisher-payouts#the-statement)).
- **Reconciliation run:** one recorded comparison of two sources' counts for a contract and period ([Reconciliation & Discrepancies](/books/advertising/reconciliation-and-discrepancies#how-to-build-it)).
- **Sequential liability:** the IAB/4A's term making an agency liable to a publisher only to the extent the advertiser has paid the agency, the advertiser liable for the rest ([Insertion Orders & Standard Terms](/books/advertising/insertion-orders-and-terms#payment-and-sequential-liability)).
- **Short rate:** a repricing of a canceled or short buy whose discount depended on the full volume, applied to the degree the IO states ([Insertion Orders & Standard Terms](/books/advertising/insertion-orders-and-terms#cancellation-and-short-rates)).

## Running the Platform

- **ACoS (advertising cost of sales):** cost divided by attributed sales, the inverse of ROAS, the retail media convention ([Advertising Metrics](/books/advertising/advertising-metrics#engagement-and-outcomes)).
- **Ad Context Protocol (AdCP):** an open protocol from AgenticAdvertising.org, launched October 15, 2025, for AI agents to discover, plan, buy and measure media over MCP and A2A ([AI in Advertising](/books/advertising/ai-in-advertising#agentic-buying)).
- **Agent mandate:** the recorded authority an agent acts under: principal, accounts, operations, spend limits, approval threshold and the person's grant behind it ([AI in Advertising](/books/advertising/ai-in-advertising#agentic-buying)).
- **Agentic Real Time Framework (ARTF):** an IAB Tech Lab specification for vendor agent services running as containers inside a host platform and acting on the bidstream ([AI in Advertising](/books/advertising/ai-in-advertising#agentic-buying)).
- **Automated campaign type:** a goal-based campaign (Performance Max, AI Max, Advantage+) that hands audience, placement, bids and creative assembly to the platform ([AI in Advertising](/books/advertising/ai-in-advertising#automated-campaigns)).
- **Bid rate:** callouts answered with at least one bid divided by callouts sent ([Advertising Metrics](/books/advertising/advertising-metrics#auction-and-bidding)).
- **Calibration (of a prediction):** the ratio of expected to observed outcomes (1.0 is calibrated), measured per slice because predictions are spent as prices ([Machine Learning for Ads](/books/advertising/machine-learning-for-ads#calibration)).
- **Callout quota (QPS quota):** the maximum callouts per second an exchange sends to a bidder endpoint, per region, set by configuration, spend and error throttling ([Real-Time Bidding at Scale](/books/advertising/bidding-at-scale#qps-quotas-and-who-pays-for-a-callout)).
- **Cost per query:** a bidder's or exchange's request-path cost per thousand callouts, compared with the revenue per thousand callouts it produces ([Real-Time Bidding at Scale](/books/advertising/bidding-at-scale#cost-per-query)).
- **CTR (click-through rate):** valid clicks divided by impressions, from the same source ([Advertising Metrics](/books/advertising/advertising-metrics#engagement-and-outcomes)).
- **CVR (conversion rate):** attributed conversions divided by the interactions that can be tracked to a conversion, for a named action and attribution setting ([Advertising Metrics](/books/advertising/advertising-metrics#engagement-and-outcomes)).
- **Data finality:** the last day a report states won't change again ([Reporting & Data Pipelines](/books/advertising/reporting-and-data-pipelines#late-data-and-restatement)).
- **Data path:** everything that happens because the request path happened (logs, streams, late beacons, aggregation, IVT, attribution, billing, training), on event time with restatement ([Ad Platform Architecture](/books/advertising/ad-platform-architecture#request-path-and-data-path)).
- **Delayed feedback:** conversion labels arriving after training, so recent clicks look like non-conversions ([Machine Learning for Ads](/books/advertising/machine-learning-for-ads#delayed-feedback)).
- **Error throttling:** an exchange lowering a bidder's traffic when its late or invalid responses pass a threshold ([Real-Time Bidding at Scale](/books/advertising/bidding-at-scale#qps-quotas-and-who-pays-for-a-callout)).
- **Exploration:** serving some ads the model is unsure about so it learns from them, since a model trained only on what it chose to show can't see its own mistakes ([Machine Learning for Ads](/books/advertising/machine-learning-for-ads#feedback-loops-and-exploration)).
- **Generative creative:** ad copy, images or video a platform's models generate or vary from an advertiser's assets, reviewed under the same policies as any ad ([AI in Advertising](/books/advertising/ai-in-advertising#generative-creative)).
- **IVT rate:** invalid impressions divided by gross (valid plus invalid) impressions, by class and measurer ([Advertising Metrics](/books/advertising/advertising-metrics#quality)).
- **Kill switch:** a small, separately distributed block list (creatives, line items, advertisers, domains) checked on every request ahead of the snapshot, for blocks that can't wait for propagation ([Ad Platform Architecture](/books/advertising/ad-platform-architecture#serving-snapshots-and-configuration-propagation)).
- **Log-level data (event-level data):** one row per request, auction, bid, impression, click or conversion as a system logged it ([Reporting & Data Pipelines](/books/advertising/reporting-and-data-pipelines#three-kinds-of-data)).
- **Log-level data feed:** a contracted, recurring delivery of event-level data to a partner, with scope, fields, privacy transforms, cadence, retention, price and permitted use ([Reporting & Data Pipelines](/books/advertising/reporting-and-data-pipelines#log-level-data-feeds-and-deals)).
- **Negative downsampling:** training on a fraction of non-click examples, with predictions mapped back by q = p / (p + (1 - p) / w) ([Machine Learning for Ads](/books/advertising/machine-learning-for-ads#training-data)).
- **Normalized entropy (NE):** average log loss divided by the log loss of always predicting the background rate ([Machine Learning for Ads](/books/advertising/machine-learning-for-ads#evaluation)).
- **Overspend (budget):** spend past a budget because regions serve from slightly stale counters, bounded by how often they sync and how much each may spend between syncs ([Ad Platform Architecture](/books/advertising/ad-platform-architecture#budgets-caps-and-state-across-regions)).
- **pCTR and pCVR:** a model's predicted probability of a click given an impression, and of a conversion given a click ([Machine Learning for Ads](/books/advertising/machine-learning-for-ads#what-the-models-predict)).
- **Reach and average frequency:** the unique audience with a viewable impression, as a count or a share of the universe, and viewable impressions divided by that audience ([Advertising Metrics](/books/advertising/advertising-metrics#audience)).
- **Request path:** everything between an ad request arriving and the response leaving (consent parsing, decision, auction, bidding, scoring), served from memory and snapshots within the deadline ([Ad Platform Architecture](/books/advertising/ad-platform-architecture#request-path-and-data-path)).
- **Restatement:** recomputing a reported or billable period after late events or IVT findings, producing a new version ([Reporting & Data Pipelines](/books/advertising/reporting-and-data-pipelines#late-data-and-restatement)).
- **Retrieval (candidate generation):** the cheap first stage of ad ranking that cuts millions of eligible ads to thousands for the ranking models, such as Meta's Andromeda ([Machine Learning for Ads](/books/advertising/machine-learning-for-ads#retrieval-and-ranking)).
- **ROAS (return on ad spend):** attributed conversion value divided by cost, as a ratio; it inherits the attribution's assumptions ([Advertising Metrics](/books/advertising/advertising-metrics#engagement-and-outcomes)).
- **Serving snapshot:** an immutable, versioned, compiled copy of configuration that request-path nodes load and swap atomically; every decision records its version ([Ad Platform Architecture](/books/advertising/ad-platform-architecture#serving-snapshots-and-configuration-propagation)).
- **Synthetic performer:** under New York General Business Law 396-b, a digital asset meant to look like a human performer who is not an identifiable person, requiring a conspicuous disclosure in ads ([AI in Advertising](/books/advertising/ai-in-advertising#disclosure-rules)).
- **TACoS (total advertising cost of sales):** ad cost divided by all sales of the advertised items, attributed or not, checkable against the order book ([Advertising Metrics](/books/advertising/advertising-metrics#engagement-and-outcomes)).
- **Unique reach (platform):** distinct users or devices reached as one platform deduplicates them, which can't be summed across days or platforms ([Reporting & Data Pipelines](/books/advertising/reporting-and-data-pipelines#unique-metrics)).
- **Video completion rate (VCR):** completed views divided by video starts ([Advertising Metrics](/books/advertising/advertising-metrics#engagement-and-outcomes)).
- **Win rate:** auctions won divided by auctions bid in, not by bid requests ([Advertising Metrics](/books/advertising/advertising-metrics#auction-and-bidding)).
