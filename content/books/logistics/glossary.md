Logistics borrows its words from trucking, railroads, shipping law, customs law, warehousing and the factory floor, and the same word shifts with context: a shipment is goods under one bill of lading to a shipper and a parcel with a tracking number to a merchant, a load is a vehicle's trip to a broker and the cargo itself to a driver, a carrier is a regulated motor carrier to FMCSA and any company that moves freight to everyone else, and a tender is an offer of a load here and a way a sale was paid in a store. Entries are grouped by the part of the volume that explains them, alphabetized within each group, and each is one line and a link to the chapter that owns the idea; a word with two meanings appears once per meaning, qualified. Terms the [Commerce](/books/commerce/glossary), [Banking & Lending](/books/banking-and-lending/glossary), [Insurance](/books/insurance/glossary) and [Field Operations](/books/field-operations/glossary) glossaries already define keep their definitions. The volume's own names for the core records (party, party role, carrier, facility, item, lot, order, order line, shipment, shipment line, handling unit, load, stop, tender, rate agreement, lane rate, freight charge, shipment event, equipment unit, driver, document, freight invoice) are the ones in [The Core Data Model](/books/logistics/logistics-business#the-core-data-model).

## The Business

- **3PL (third-party logistics provider):** a company that runs warehouses for brands, receiving, storing, picking, packing and shipping their stock for fees per receipt, unit stored and order, as ShipBob and ShipMonk do ([The Logistics Business](/books/logistics/logistics-business#who-s-who); defined in [Warehouses, 3PLs & Fulfillment Networks](/books/commerce/warehouses-and-3pls#3pls)).
- **Advance ship notice (ASN):** a notice of what an inbound truck, pallet or carton contains, sent ahead so the receiver can check it ([The Logistics Business](/books/logistics/logistics-business#a-manufactured-lot-from-a-supplier); defined in [Warehouses, 3PLs & Fulfillment Networks](/books/commerce/warehouses-and-3pls#receiving-and-inbound)).
- **Agent (commission sales agent):** an independent company that sells freight and arranges capacity under another company's authority, insurance and credit, paid a contractual percentage of a load's revenue or spread; Landstar runs about 960 of them ([How Carriers, Brokers & 3PLs Make Money](/books/logistics/logistics-economics#the-agent-model)).
- **Asset carrier:** a carrier that owns or leases the vehicles that move the freight, as opposed to a broker or forwarder that arranges them ([The Logistics Business](/books/logistics/logistics-business#who-s-who)).
- **Asset-light:** a logistics business that buys capacity from carriers rather than owning tractors, trailers or terminals, so its costs per load are on carriers' invoices and scale with volume ([How Carriers, Brokers & 3PLs Make Money](/books/logistics/logistics-economics#asset-based-and-asset-light)).
- **Beneficial cargo owner (BCO):** the party that owns the goods and the commercial interest in them, whoever appears as shipper or consignee on a bill; not Landstar's "BCO Independent Contractor," an owner-operator ([The Logistics Business](/books/logistics/logistics-business#who-s-who)).
- **Buy side, sell side:** the payable lines owed to carriers and vendors and the receivable lines owed by customers; each movement a broker or forwarder handles has both ([The Logistics Business](/books/logistics/logistics-business#how-the-money-splits)).
- **Cass Freight Index:** Cass Information Systems' monthly indexes of North American for-hire freight shipments and expenditures, built from the freight invoices it processes, with January 1990 as 1.00 ([Freight Markets & Cycles](/books/logistics/freight-markets#how-the-indexes-do-it)).
- **Consignee:** the party freight is delivered to, named on the bill of lading ([The Logistics Business](/books/logistics/logistics-business#who-s-who)).
- **Contract rate:** a freight price agreed in a bid for a lane's volume over a term, usually a year, and tendered through the routing guide; it changes only at the next bid unless it is index-linked ([Freight Markets & Cycles](/books/logistics/freight-markets#spot-and-contract), [Rating, Tariffs & Accessorials](/books/logistics/rating-and-accessorials#contract-and-spot-rates)).
- **Corporate event:** an acquisition, merger, spin-off, rename, bankruptcy or cessation that changes a party's identity or ownership from an effective date, and with it invoices, identifiers and integrations ([The Logistics Landscape](/books/logistics/logistics-landscape#consolidation-2023-to-2026)).
- **Cost-plus (open book):** a contract logistics pricing structure in which the client pays the provider's allowable costs plus a fee or markup and can audit the costs ([How Carriers, Brokers & 3PLs Make Money](/books/logistics/logistics-economics#contract-warehousing), [Contract Logistics & 3PL Billing](/books/logistics/contract-logistics#contract-models)).
- **Document (logistics):** a bill of lading, rate confirmation, proof of delivery, waybill, invoice, entry or EDI message, kept as the bytes exchanged with a hash ([The Logistics Business](/books/logistics/logistics-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **Equipment unit:** a tractor, trailer, container, chassis, railcar or ULD, identified by a number its owner or a registry assigns ([The Logistics Business](/books/logistics/logistics-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **Event time, received time:** when a milestone happened according to its reporter, and when the system learned it; kept as two columns on every shipment event ([The Logistics Business](/books/logistics/logistics-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **Facility:** a place freight moves to, from or through (a plant, DC, store, terminal, port, ramp or airport), with an operator, a time zone and appointment rules; distinct from its address ([The Logistics Business](/books/logistics/logistics-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **Freight charge:** one priced line on a shipment or load, with its side, payer, payee, kind, code, quantity, rate and source ([The Logistics Business](/books/logistics/logistics-business#one-rule-for-all-of-them)).
- **Freight cycle:** the recurring swing between a tight market (spot above contract, rising tender rejections) and a soft one (spot below contract, rebids), which ran from a 2021 to 2022 boom through a 2022 to 2025 recession into a 2026 tightening ([Freight Markets & Cycles](/books/logistics/freight-markets#the-2021-to-2026-cycle)).
- **Freight invoice:** a bill for freight, sent to a customer or received from a carrier or vendor, with its payee and its lines as billed ([The Logistics Business](/books/logistics/logistics-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **Freight payment provider:** a company that audits and pays freight invoices on a shipper's behalf, as Cass does ([The Logistics Business](/books/logistics/logistics-business#who-s-who)).
- **Handling unit:** what is physically handled (a pallet, carton, crate or drum) with its dimensions, weight and SSCC; distinct from the lines of goods on it ([The Logistics Business](/books/logistics/logistics-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **Index-linked rate:** a contract rate that resets with a published freight index, by a stated share of the index's change and within a floor and ceiling ([Freight Markets & Cycles](/books/logistics/freight-markets#what-a-cycle-does-to-software)).
- **Lane rate:** one priced origin and destination pair inside a rate agreement, with its basis (flat, per mile, per hundredweight, per container, a tariff discount), minimum and fuel schedule ([The Logistics Business](/books/logistics/logistics-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **Load:** one vehicle's or container's trip with its stops, carrying one or more shipments ([The Logistics Business](/books/logistics/logistics-business#the-life-of-a-shipment)).
- **Lot:** a batch of an item made together and traced as one, identified by its manufacturer's lot code ([The Logistics Business](/books/logistics/logistics-business#a-manufactured-lot-from-a-supplier)).
- **Mini-bid:** a shipper's small, off-cycle procurement event for a subset of lanes, used when the market has moved away from the annual bid's rates ([Freight Markets & Cycles](/books/logistics/freight-markets#what-a-cycle-does-to-software), [Freight Procurement & Routing Guides](/books/logistics/procurement-and-routing-guides#mini-bids)).
- **Order (logistics):** the commercial demand behind freight: a sales, purchase, transfer or return order, owned by the party whose number it carries ([The Logistics Business](/books/logistics/logistics-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **Party (logistics):** an organization or person appearing in any role; one row however many roles it plays ([The Logistics Business](/books/logistics/logistics-business#who-s-who)).
- **Party role:** a role a party plays (shipper, carrier, broker, factor, customs broker and the rest), with the dates it applies ([The Logistics Business](/books/logistics/logistics-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **Planned versus actual:** an appointment or requested window against the arrival or delivery that happened, kept side by side for detention, on-time measures and claims ([The Logistics Business](/books/logistics/logistics-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **Rate agreement:** a contract, tariff, service contract, quote or rate confirmation that prices freight between an owner and a counterparty for a period ([The Logistics Business](/books/logistics/logistics-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **Retention contract:** an agent commission basis in which the principal first keeps a retained percentage of revenue and the agent's commission is computed on what remains after purchased transportation ([How Carriers, Brokers & 3PLs Make Money](/books/logistics/logistics-economics#the-agent-model)).
- **Revenue per hundredweight:** revenue divided by weight in pounds times 100, the LTL industry's yield measure ([How Carriers, Brokers & 3PLs Make Money](/books/logistics/logistics-economics#unit-metrics)).
- **Shipment (logistics):** goods under one bill of lading from one origin to one destination, with its shipper, consignee, bill-to party and identifiers ([The Logistics Business](/books/logistics/logistics-business#the-life-of-a-shipment)).
- **Shipment event:** a milestone or position as reported (by EDI, API, ELD, app or geofence), with its source code, event time and received time ([The Logistics Business](/books/logistics/logistics-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **Shipper:** the party that tenders goods to a carrier and is named as consignor on the bill of lading ([The Logistics Business](/books/logistics/logistics-business#who-s-who)).
- **Spot rate:** a price for a single load agreed when it needs a truck, outside any contract; brokers buy most capacity at spot rates even when they sell on contract ([Freight Markets & Cycles](/books/logistics/freight-markets#spot-and-contract), [Rating, Tariffs & Accessorials](/books/logistics/rating-and-accessorials#contract-and-spot-rates)).
- **Spot-contract spread:** the difference between contract and spot rates on the same equipment, the gauge of the freight cycle and of a broker's margin pressure ([Freight Markets & Cycles](/books/logistics/freight-markets#spot-and-contract)).
- **Standard cost:** a predetermined cost per mile, hour or stop that an asset carrier allocates to each load for contribution reporting and trues up to actuals at month end ([How Carriers, Brokers & 3PLs Make Money](/books/logistics/logistics-economics#how-to-build-it)).
- **Stop:** a facility visit on a load, with its appointment, its actual arrival and departure, and the shipments it picks up or drops ([The Logistics Business](/books/logistics/logistics-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **Tender rejection rate:** the share of contract tenders a carrier rejects or lets expire, the leading sign of a tightening market and a failing routing guide ([Freight Markets & Cycles](/books/logistics/freight-markets#what-a-cycle-does-to-software), [Freight Procurement & Routing Guides](/books/logistics/procurement-and-routing-guides#compliance)).

## Road and Rail

- **Absolute minimum charge:** the lowest linehaul charge an LTL carrier accepts for a shipment after the customer's discount, set in its rules tariff ([Freight Class, Density & LTL Pricing](/books/logistics/freight-classification#base-rates-weight-breaks-and-discounts)).
- **Breakbulk:** an LTL hub that unloads, sorts and reloads freight between service centers that lack volume for a direct trailer ([LTL Networks](/books/logistics/ltl-networks#the-network)).
- **Bridge formula:** the federal limit on the weight any group of consecutive axles may carry, by the group's length and axle count (23 CFR 658.17) ([Truckload](/books/logistics/truckload#equipment)).
- **Car hire:** what a railroad pays a car's owner for using its car, by the hour and by the mile, loaded and empty, at rates negotiated monthly and published in CHARM ([Rail Freight](/books/logistics/rail-freight#car-hire)).
- **Car reporting marks:** the reporting marks and number that identify a railcar, which a Class I railroad's demurrage invoice must show for each car ([Rail Freight](/books/logistics/rail-freight#cars-reporting-marks-and-umler)).
- **CHARM (Car Hire Accounting Rate Master):** the industry's monthly file of car hire rates per car, owner and user, the official source for car hire settlement ([Rail Freight](/books/logistics/rail-freight#car-hire)).
- **Chassis pool:** a shared fleet of container chassis that dray carriers draw from and return to any of the pool's locations, billed per day ([Intermodal & Drayage](/books/logistics/intermodal-and-drayage#containers-and-chassis)).
- **Constructive placement:** a railroad holding a car for a customer whose track can't take it, with notice to the customer; it can start demurrage as if the car were placed ([Rail Freight](/books/logistics/rail-freight#demurrage-under-49-cfr-1333)).
- **Cubic capacity rule:** an LTL tariff rule that rates light, bulky shipments at an assumed density (6 pounds a cubic foot at XPO and TForce) instead of their actual weight ([Freight Class, Density & LTL Pricing](/books/logistics/freight-classification#cube-length-and-capacity-rules)).
- **Deadhead:** miles driven without cargo, paid for by the carrier and billed to nobody; 13.9% of Knight-Swift's truckload miles in 2025 ([Truckload](/books/logistics/truckload#deadhead)).
- **Dedicated (truckload):** trucks and drivers assigned to one customer's network under a multi-year contract, usually priced per truck per week ([Truckload](/books/logistics/truckload#one-way-and-dedicated)).
- **Deficit weight:** rating an LTL shipment at the minimum weight of a heavier weight group when that charge is lower than rating its actual weight ([Freight Class, Density & LTL Pricing](/books/logistics/freight-classification#base-rates-weight-breaks-and-discounts)).
- **Delivery receipt:** the document the consignee signs at delivery, noting any shortage or damage; the proof of delivery that releases the freight bill ([LTL Networks](/books/logistics/ltl-networks#pickup-delivery-and-exceptions)).
- **Demurrage (rail):** the serving railroad's charge for holding a car past free time, both compensation and "a penalty for undue car detention" under 49 CFR 1333 ([Rail Freight](/books/logistics/rail-freight#demurrage-under-49-cfr-1333)).
- **Density (freight):** a handling unit's weight in pounds over its cube in cubic feet; under NMFTA's 13-step scale, 10 to under 12 pounds per cubic foot is class 92.5 ([Freight Class, Density & LTL Pricing](/books/logistics/freight-classification#density), [The Logistics Business](/books/logistics/logistics-business#an-ltl-shipment)).
- **Domestic container:** a 53-foot container owned by an intermodal company or railroad program that moves only inside North America ([Intermodal & Drayage](/books/logistics/intermodal-and-drayage#containers-and-chassis)).
- **Drayage:** short-haul trucking of containers and chassis between terminals, ramps and customer docks ([Intermodal & Drayage](/books/logistics/intermodal-and-drayage#drayage)).
- **Drop and hook:** leaving one trailer at a facility and taking another already loaded or emptied, so the driver doesn't wait at the dock ([Truckload](/books/logistics/truckload#live-drop-and-preloaded)).
- **Dual transaction:** a dray visit that drops one container and picks up another at the same terminal ([Intermodal & Drayage](/books/logistics/intermodal-and-drayage#drayage)).
- **eBOL (electronic bill of lading, LTL):** the Digital LTL Council's standard API for submitting a bill of lading to an LTL carrier, version 2.1 since March 2024 ([LTL Networks](/books/logistics/ltl-networks#the-bill-of-lading-and-the-pro-number), [Logistics APIs & Standards](/books/logistics/logistics-apis#carrier-apis)).
- **Equipment interchange receipt (EIR):** the record of a container or chassis handed to or from a trucker at a gate, with time, condition and seal ([Intermodal & Drayage](/books/logistics/intermodal-and-drayage#drayage)).
- **Escrow (owner-operator lease):** money a carrier holds from a leased owner-operator under 49 CFR 376.12(k), with quarterly interest and return within 45 days of termination ([Truckload](/books/logistics/truckload#owner-operators-and-leases)).
- **FAK (freight all kinds):** a pricing agreement provision that rates a range of actual classes as one class; not part of the NMFC ([Freight Class, Density & LTL Pricing](/books/logistics/freight-classification#fak-and-class-exceptions)).
- **Freight class:** the NMFC rating that, with weight and lane, sets an LTL base rate; for density-based items it follows from the density ([Freight Class, Density & LTL Pricing](/books/logistics/freight-classification#the-nmfc)).
- **Hub and spoke:** a network where local facilities feed hubs that sort everything to every destination region ([Parcel Networks From the Carrier Side](/books/logistics/parcel-networks#hub-and-spoke)).
- **Hundredweight (cwt):** 100 pounds, the unit LTL rates and revenue are quoted in; Old Dominion earned $33.31 per cwt in 2025 ([How Carriers, Brokers & 3PLs Make Money](/books/logistics/logistics-economics#unit-metrics)).
- **Interline:** handing an LTL shipment to a partner carrier to reach points outside the origin carrier's direct service area ([LTL Networks](/books/logistics/ltl-networks#service-areas-and-interline)).
- **Intermodal marketing company (IMC):** a company that buys rail line haul wholesale and sells door-to-door intermodal service to shippers ([Intermodal & Drayage](/books/logistics/intermodal-and-drayage#how-the-carriers-do-it)).
- **Leased owner-operator:** an owner-operator whose equipment is leased to a carrier and runs under its authority, under a written lease meeting 49 CFR 376 ([Truckload](/books/logistics/truckload#owner-operators-and-leases)).
- **Lineal foot rule:** an LTL tariff rule that charges by the feet of trailer length a shipment occupies once it passes a size or weight threshold ([Freight Class, Density & LTL Pricing](/books/logistics/freight-classification#cube-length-and-capacity-rules)).
- **Linehaul (LTL):** scheduled trips that carry trailers between an LTL carrier's terminals ([LTL Networks](/books/logistics/ltl-networks#the-network)).
- **LTL (less than truckload):** freight in handling units sharing trailers through a carrier's terminal network, priced from a tariff or base rate by class or density and weight, less a discount ([LTL Networks](/books/logistics/ltl-networks#the-network)).
- **NMFC (National Motor Freight Classification):** NMFTA's voluntary classification of commodities into items, subs and classes for LTL rating ([Freight Class, Density & LTL Pricing](/books/logistics/freight-classification#the-nmfc)).
- **One-way (truckload):** irregular-route truckload where each load is accepted on its own and the next one is found where the last ended ([Truckload](/books/logistics/truckload#one-way-and-dedicated)).
- **Operating ratio:** operating expenses as a percentage of revenue, the inverse of operating margin; 75.2% for Old Dominion and 97.0% for Knight-Swift's truckload segment in 2025 ([How Carriers, Brokers & 3PLs Make Money](/books/logistics/logistics-economics#operating-ratio)).
- **OS&D (over, short and damaged):** the exceptions an LTL carrier records when freight on hand doesn't match its bill or arrives damaged ([LTL Networks](/books/logistics/ltl-networks#pickup-delivery-and-exceptions)).
- **Owner-operator:** a driver who owns or leases a tractor and runs under their own authority or leases on to a carrier under 49 CFR 376 ([Truckload](/books/logistics/truckload#owner-operators-and-leases)).
- **Per diem (intermodal):** the daily charge for keeping an equipment provider's container or chassis outside its terminal or depot past free time ([Intermodal & Drayage](/books/logistics/intermodal-and-drayage#per-diem-detention-and-storage)).
- **Pickup and delivery (P&D):** local multi-stop routes from an LTL service center that deliver in the morning and pick up in the afternoon ([LTL Networks](/books/logistics/ltl-networks#the-network)).
- **Power-only:** a carrier supplying only the tractor and driver to pull a trailer owned by a broker, shipper or another carrier ([Truckload](/books/logistics/truckload#power-only)).
- **Private mark:** a reporting mark of a non-railroad car owner, ending in X for railcars ([Rail Freight](/books/logistics/rail-freight#cars-reporting-marks-and-umler)).
- **PRO number:** an LTL carrier's tracking number for a shipment ([LTL Networks](/books/logistics/ltl-networks#the-bill-of-lading-and-the-pro-number)).
- **Ramp:** a rail intermodal terminal where containers and trailers are lifted on and off trains ([Intermodal & Drayage](/books/logistics/intermodal-and-drayage#ramps-and-rail-legs)).
- **Reefer:** a refrigerated trailer, and the freight rules it brings: the shipper's written temperature specification, pre-cooling and proof of temperature ([Truckload](/books/logistics/truckload#equipment)).
- **Relay (LTL):** a site where linehaul drivers swap trailers so each can return home, with no freight handling ([LTL Networks](/books/logistics/ltl-networks#the-network)).
- **Reporting mark:** the alphabetic code identifying a railcar's owner, stenciled on the car with its number ([Rail Freight](/books/logistics/rail-freight#cars-reporting-marks-and-umler)).
- **Reweigh:** a carrier weighing a shipment in its custody and re-rating it, usually with a fee, when the weight differs from the bill of lading ([Freight Class, Density & LTL Pricing](/books/logistics/freight-classification#reweighs-inspections-and-reclassification)).
- **Rules tariff:** an LTL carrier's published rules and accessorial charges, which adopt the NMFC with exceptions and govern every shipment ([Freight Class, Density & LTL Pricing](/books/logistics/freight-classification#how-the-carriers-do-it)).
- **Service center:** an LTL end-of-line terminal that picks up and delivers in its local area and consolidates freight for linehaul ([LTL Networks](/books/logistics/ltl-networks#the-network)).
- **Sort plan:** the versioned rules that tell each parcel facility, for each sort window, where every destination goes next ([Parcel Networks From the Carrier Side](/books/logistics/parcel-networks#sortation-and-the-sort-plan)).
- **Street turn:** reusing an import's empty container for a nearby export load instead of returning it to the depot ([Intermodal & Drayage](/books/logistics/intermodal-and-drayage#drayage)).
- **Traffic Mitigation Fee (TMF):** the per-container fee the Los Angeles and Long Beach terminals collect through PierPass for extended gate hours ([Intermodal & Drayage](/books/logistics/intermodal-and-drayage#drayage)).
- **Truckload:** a whole trailer for one shipper's freight, priced per load or per mile on a contract lane or a spot quote, plus fuel surcharge ([Truckload](/books/logistics/truckload)).
- **UIIA:** the Uniform Intermodal Interchange and Facilities Access Agreement, the standard contract between intermodal truckers and equipment providers ([Intermodal & Drayage](/books/logistics/intermodal-and-drayage#containers-and-chassis)).
- **Umler:** the industry registry of North American rail equipment that Railinc maintains ([Rail Freight](/books/logistics/rail-freight#cars-reporting-marks-and-umler)).
- **Waybill (rail):** the railroad's contract of carriage and routing for a car or block of cars ([Rail Freight](/books/logistics/rail-freight#waybills-interchange-and-car-events)).
- **Weight group:** an LTL base rate bracket by shipment weight, named by its floor (L5C, M5C, M1M through M20M) ([Freight Class, Density & LTL Pricing](/books/logistics/freight-classification#base-rates-weight-breaks-and-discounts)).
- **Zone (parcel):** a distance band from origin to destination that carriers price by; USPS uses zones 1 to 9, based on 3-digit ZIP centroid distance ([Parcel Networks From the Carrier Side](/books/logistics/parcel-networks#zones-and-dimensional-weight-as-the-carrier-sets-them); defined in [Shipping & Carriers](/books/commerce/shipping-and-carriers#rate-shopping)).

## Ocean and Air

- **ACAS referral, Do-Not-Load:** CBP's request for information or enhanced screening that must be resolved before departure, and its instruction that cargo posing a potential bomb threat may not be carried ([Air Cargo](/books/logistics/air-cargo#air-cargo-advance-screening)).
- **Air waybill (AWB):** the airline's transport document for an air consignment; a forwarder's consolidation travels on a master AWB with house AWBs under it ([Air Cargo](/books/logistics/air-cargo#air-waybills-and-the-e-awb)).
- **Alliance (ocean):** a vessel sharing agreement among carriers on the major East-West trades; since February 2025 the Gemini Cooperation (Maersk, Hapag-Lloyd), the Ocean Alliance and the Premier Alliance ([Ocean Shipping](/books/logistics/ocean-shipping#carriers-alliances-and-services)).
- **Billed party, billing party:** under 46 CFR 541.3, the person receiving and responsible for a D&D invoice, and the ocean carrier, terminal operator or NVOCC issuing it ([Detention & Demurrage](/books/logistics/detention-and-demurrage#the-billing-rule)).
- **Carrier haulage, merchant haulage:** the inland move arranged by the ocean carrier (store-door service) or by the shipper or consignee (container-yard service), which changes who holds the container and who is billed for its time ([Ocean Shipping](/books/logistics/ocean-shipping#booking)).
- **CCSP (Certified Cargo Screening Program):** TSA's program for screening air cargo at certified facilities under chain of custody until it is loaded ([Air Cargo](/books/logistics/air-cargo#security-screening)).
- **Charge complaint:** information about a common carrier's charge submitted to the FMC under 46 U.S.C. 41310, which the carrier must show was reasonable ([Detention & Demurrage](/books/logistics/detention-and-demurrage#disputes-and-charge-complaints)).
- **Chargeable weight:** the greater of an air shipment's actual weight and its volumetric weight, on which it is priced ([Air Cargo](/books/logistics/air-cargo#chargeable-weight)).
- **Co-loading:** an NVOCC tendering its LCL cargo to another NVOCC's consolidation, becoming that NVOCC's shipper ([Freight Forwarders & NVOCCs](/books/logistics/forwarders-and-nvoccs#consolidation)).
- **Consolidation (forwarding):** many house shipments built into one container or air master under one master bill, with costs allocated back to each house ([Freight Forwarders & NVOCCs](/books/logistics/forwarders-and-nvoccs#consolidation)).
- **Container availability date:** the date an import container can be picked up after discharge, which starts import demurrage and must appear on the invoice ([Detention & Demurrage](/books/logistics/detention-and-demurrage#free-time)).
- **Container number:** an ISO 6346 identifier of a three-letter owner code, an equipment category letter, a six-digit serial and a check digit ([Ocean Shipping](/books/logistics/ocean-shipping#containers-and-vgm)).
- **Cut-off (ocean):** the latest time before a sailing to submit documentation, the VGM or the full container, coded `DCO`, `VCO`, `FCO`, `LCO` and `EFC` in DCSA's booking standard ([Ocean Shipping](/books/logistics/ocean-shipping#schedules)).
- **DCSA (Digital Container Shipping Association):** the carriers' standards body for booking, bill of lading, track and trace and schedule APIs; its newest releases are open to members and partners ([Ocean Shipping](/books/logistics/ocean-shipping#the-dcsa-standards)).
- **Demurrage (ocean):** the charge for a container left at the terminal past free time ([Detention & Demurrage](/books/logistics/detention-and-demurrage#free-time)).
- **Detention (ocean), per diem:** the charge for keeping a carrier's container out past free time, billed within 30 days of the last day under 46 CFR 541.7 ([Detention & Demurrage](/books/logistics/detention-and-demurrage#the-billing-rule)).
- **e-AWB:** an air waybill exchanged as data under IATA's Multilateral e-AWB Agreement (Resolution 672), activated per airline and station ([Air Cargo](/books/logistics/air-cargo#air-waybills-and-the-e-awb)).
- **Electronic bill of lading (eBL):** a bill of lading held on a platform that gives one party exclusive control, the electronic equivalent of possessing the paper original ([Bills of Lading & eBL](/books/logistics/ocean-documents#electronic-bills-of-lading)).
- **FCL, LCL:** full container load, booked by one shipper, and less than container load, a share of a container an NVOCC consolidates ([Ocean Shipping](/books/logistics/ocean-shipping#booking), [Freight Forwarders & NVOCCs](/books/logistics/forwarders-and-nvoccs#consolidation)).
- **Free time:** the days a container or terminal space is free before demurrage or detention starts, set by the tariff or service contract ([Detention & Demurrage](/books/logistics/detention-and-demurrage#free-time)).
- **FWB, FHL, FSU:** the air waybill, house manifest and status messages of Cargo-IMP (XFWB, XFHL, XFSU in Cargo-XML) ([Air Cargo](/books/logistics/air-cargo#air-waybills-and-the-e-awb)).
- **House bill, master bill:** an NVOCC's or forwarder's own bill of lading or air waybill to its customer, and the carrier's bill to the NVOCC or forwarder underneath it ([Bills of Lading & eBL](/books/logistics/ocean-documents#house-and-master-bills), [Air Cargo](/books/logistics/air-cargo#air-waybills-and-the-e-awb)).
- **Incentive principle:** the FMC's test of whether a D&D charge still works as an incentive to move cargo and equipment (46 CFR 545.5) ([Detention & Demurrage](/books/logistics/detention-and-demurrage#the-incentive-principle)).
- **Indirect air carrier (IAC):** a forwarder that tenders air cargo under a TSA-approved security program renewed each year ([Air Cargo](/books/logistics/air-cargo#security-screening)).
- **Negotiable bill, to order:** a bill of lading that delivers to the order of a consignee, so the holder of an endorsed original can claim or sell the goods ([Bills of Lading & eBL](/books/logistics/ocean-documents#negotiable-and-straight-bills)).
- **Non-conforming D&D invoice:** an invoice missing a required element or issued more than 30 days after the charge was last incurred; the billed party need not pay it ([Detention & Demurrage](/books/logistics/detention-and-demurrage#the-billing-rule)).
- **NRA (negotiated rate arrangement):** a written NVOCC rate agreed with the shipper before the cargo is received, exempt from tariff rate publication (46 CFR 532) ([Freight Forwarders & NVOCCs](/books/logistics/forwarders-and-nvoccs#how-an-nvocc-prices-freight)).
- **NSA (NVOCC service arrangement):** an NVOCC's contract with a minimum quantity or revenue commitment, its counterpart to the line's service contract (46 CFR 531) ([Freight Forwarders & NVOCCs](/books/logistics/forwarders-and-nvoccs#how-an-nvocc-prices-freight)).
- **NVOCC (non-vessel-operating common carrier):** an FMC-licensed intermediary that issues its own bills over an ocean carrier's, with $75,000 of financial responsibility ($150,000 if registered rather than licensed) ([Freight Forwarders & NVOCCs](/books/logistics/forwarders-and-nvoccs#fmc-licensing-and-financial-responsibility)).
- **Ocean freight forwarder:** an FMC-licensed US intermediary that books space and handles documents for shippers without issuing its own bills, with $50,000 of financial responsibility ([Freight Forwarders & NVOCCs](/books/logistics/forwarders-and-nvoccs#who-is-who)).
- **ONE Record:** IATA's API and linked-data standard for a single shared record of an air shipment, the preferred standard from January 1, 2026 ([Air Cargo](/books/logistics/air-cargo#status-messages-and-one-record)).
- **OTI (ocean transportation intermediary):** an ocean freight forwarder or an NVOCC, licensed by the FMC under 46 CFR 515 ([Freight Forwarders & NVOCCs](/books/logistics/forwarders-and-nvoccs#fmc-licensing-and-financial-responsibility)).
- **Port call:** a vessel's visit to one terminal on a voyage, with planned, estimated and actual arrival and departure ([Ocean Shipping](/books/logistics/ocean-shipping#carriers-alliances-and-services)).
- **Qualifying individual:** the person whose three years of US OTI experience and character an FMC license rests on (46 CFR 515.11) ([Freight Forwarders & NVOCCs](/books/logistics/forwarders-and-nvoccs#fmc-licensing-and-financial-responsibility)).
- **Rolled booking:** a confirmed booking moved to a later sailing, seen as a change of first-leg vessel and voyage between booking versions ([Ocean Shipping](/books/logistics/ocean-shipping#booking)).
- **Sea waybill:** a non-negotiable ocean transport document; the carrier delivers to the named consignee without an original ([Bills of Lading & eBL](/books/logistics/ocean-documents#bills-of-lading-and-sea-waybills)).
- **Service contract (ocean):** a written contract with an ocean carrier in which the shipper commits to a minimum quantity or revenue over a period for a rate and service level, filed with the FMC (46 CFR 530.3) ([Ocean Shipping](/books/logistics/ocean-shipping#carriers-alliances-and-services)).
- **Shipped on board, received for shipment:** a bill stating the goods were loaded on a named date, or only that the carrier received them ([Bills of Lading & eBL](/books/logistics/ocean-documents#bills-of-lading-and-sea-waybills)).
- **Shipper-owned container (SOC):** a container the shipper supplies rather than the carrier, flagged on the booking's equipment line ([Ocean Shipping](/books/logistics/ocean-shipping#booking)).
- **Shipping instructions:** the shipper's data for the bill of lading (parties, cargo, containers, seals, document type, originals), due by the documentation cut-off ([Bills of Lading & eBL](/books/logistics/ocean-documents#the-life-of-a-transport-document)).
- **Shutout day:** an unplanned terminal or depot closure that a carrier excludes from free time and charges ([Detention & Demurrage](/books/logistics/detention-and-demurrage#free-time)).
- **Straight bill:** a bill of lading to a named consignee, marked nonnegotiable under 49 U.S.C. 80103(b) ([Bills of Lading & eBL](/books/logistics/ocean-documents#bills-of-lading-and-sea-waybills)).
- **Surrender (bill of lading):** handing an original back to the carrier, for delivery of the goods or so it can be amended ([Bills of Lading & eBL](/books/logistics/ocean-documents#negotiable-and-straight-bills)).
- **ULD (unit load device):** an aircraft pallet with its net, or an aircraft container, regulated as an aircraft part ([Air Cargo](/books/logistics/air-cargo#unit-load-devices)).
- **VGM (verified gross mass):** the packed container's weight, by weighing it or by summing cargo, packing and tare, that SOLAS VI/2 makes a condition of loading since July 1, 2016 ([Ocean Shipping](/books/logistics/ocean-shipping#containers-and-vgm)).

## Freight Brokerage

- **Bona fide agent:** a person who is part of a motor carrier's normal organization and books its freight under a continuing agreement with no discretion to send loads to other carriers; not a broker (49 CFR 371.2) ([How Freight Brokerage Works](/books/logistics/freight-brokerage#broker-carrier-forwarder-dispatcher)).
- **Book now:** a load board or broker app feature that lets a qualified carrier accept a posted load at the posted price in one action, creating the tender and rate confirmation ([Load Boards & Digital Freight Matching](/books/logistics/load-boards-and-matching#book-now-and-auto-booking)).
- **Broker-carrier agreement:** the master contract a broker signs with a carrier at onboarding (status, insurance, safety, no re-brokering, cargo liability, payment, indemnity, non-solicitation), under which each rate confirmation is issued ([How Freight Brokerage Works](/books/logistics/freight-brokerage#the-broker-carrier-agreement)).
- **Carrier identity theft:** posing as an established, registered carrier (through lookalike contacts, a hijacked registry account or a copied packet) to obtain loads or payments ([Freight Fraud & Cargo Theft](/books/logistics/freight-fraud#the-schemes)).
- **Carrier qualification:** one broker's or shipper's recorded decision that a carrier may (or may not) be tendered loads, with its restrictions, the policy version and the checks behind it ([Carrier Onboarding & Vetting](/books/logistics/carrier-onboarding#how-to-build-it)).
- **Digital freight matching:** software that ranks carriers for a load (or loads for a truck) from position, lanes, fit, performance and price, and offers or books loads without a phone call ([Load Boards & Digital Freight Matching](/books/logistics/load-boards-and-matching#matching)).
- **Dispatch service:** a company that books loads for carriers; FMCSA treats one that serves several carriers and chooses which gets each load as a broker that needs broker authority ([How Freight Brokerage Works](/books/logistics/freight-brokerage#broker-carrier-forwarder-dispatcher)).
- **Double brokering:** a carrier, or someone posing as one, re-tendering a load to another carrier without the broker's consent, collecting from the broker and often leaving the real hauler unpaid ([Freight Fraud & Cargo Theft](/books/logistics/freight-fraud#the-schemes)).
- **FAAAA preemption:** the bar on state laws "related to a price, route, or service of any motor carrier" with respect to property (49 U.S.C. 14501(c)(1)), subject to a safety exception for state authority "with respect to motor vehicles" ([How Freight Brokerage Works](/books/logistics/freight-brokerage#negligent-selection-after-montgomery)).
- **Fictitious pickup:** a theft in which deception is used to obtain a shipment, typically a criminal booking a load and sending a driver who signs for it ([Freight Fraud & Cargo Theft](/books/logistics/freight-fraud#the-schemes)).
- **Freight broker:** a person other than a motor carrier that sells, negotiates for or holds itself out as arranging motor carrier transportation for compensation (49 U.S.C. 13102(2)), with a $75,000 bond or trust fund ([How Freight Brokerage Works](/books/logistics/freight-brokerage#broker-carrier-forwarder-dispatcher)).
- **Freight forwarder:** a person that assembles and consolidates shipments and assumes responsibility for them from receipt to destination, using other carriers (49 U.S.C. 13102(8)) ([How Freight Brokerage Works](/books/logistics/freight-brokerage#broker-carrier-forwarder-dispatcher)).
- **Gross margin (brokerage), adjusted gross profit:** revenue less purchased transportation; C.H. Robinson's adjusted gross profit margin was 16.8% in 2025 ([How Carriers, Brokers & 3PLs Make Money](/books/logistics/logistics-economics#how-the-companies-do-it)).
- **Load board:** a marketplace where brokers post loads and carriers find them, sold by subscription to both sides (DAT, Truckstop) or run by a broker for its own carriers ([Load Boards & Digital Freight Matching](/books/logistics/load-boards-and-matching#how-the-platforms-do-it)).
- **Load posting:** one advertisement of a load on one channel, with its own identifier, visible rate and status, withdrawn everywhere when the load is covered ([Load Boards & Digital Freight Matching](/books/logistics/load-boards-and-matching#posting)).
- **Negligent selection:** a state tort claim that a broker or shipper failed to use reasonable care in choosing a carrier; after *Montgomery v. Caribe Transport II* (May 14, 2026) it is not preempted by the FAAAA ([How Freight Brokerage Works](/books/logistics/freight-brokerage#negligent-selection-after-montgomery)).
- **Payment diversion (freight):** fraud that redirects a carrier's payment by a forged remit-to change, bank change or notice of assignment while the freight itself moves normally ([Freight Fraud & Cargo Theft](/books/logistics/freight-fraud#the-schemes)).
- **Pickup verification:** the recorded check, at the gate or by app, that the carrier, driver, tractor and trailer arriving for a load match what was sent ahead ([Freight Fraud & Cargo Theft](/books/logistics/freight-fraud#controls)).
- **Rate confirmation:** the broker's load-level contract with a carrier, setting the rate, accessorials and terms on top of the broker-carrier agreement ([How Freight Brokerage Works](/books/logistics/freight-brokerage#the-rate-confirmation)).
- **Transaction record (broker):** the record 49 CFR 371.3 requires a broker to keep for three years for each brokered load, open to every party to the transaction; a 2024 proposal would add electronic format and 48-hour delivery ([How Freight Brokerage Works](/books/logistics/freight-brokerage#the-broker-s-record-and-transparency)).

## Carrier Regulation

- **11-hour, 14-hour and 30-minute limits:** the property-carrying driver's daily clocks: 11 hours of driving inside a 14-hour window after 10 hours off, and a 30-minute interruption after 8 hours of driving ([Hours of Service](/books/logistics/hours-of-service#the-limits)).
- **34-hour restart:** an off-duty period of 34 or more consecutive hours that ends the 60/7 or 70/8 cycle and starts the sum again from zero ([Hours of Service](/books/logistics/hours-of-service#the-cycle-and-the-restart)).
- **60/70-hour cycle:** the limit of 60 on-duty hours in 7 consecutive days, or 70 in 8 for carriers that operate every day, summed over days that start at the carrier's designated hour ([Hours of Service](/books/logistics/hours-of-service#the-cycle-and-the-restart)).
- **Accident register:** the carrier's three-year list of every crash meeting the 390.5 definition (a fatality, an injury treated away from the scene, or a tow-away), with its date, place, driver, injuries, fatalities and any hazmat release ([Safety Ratings, CSA & Inspections](/books/logistics/safety-and-csa#crashes)).
- **Additional Cargo Liability (excess value):** a carrier's charge for raising its liability above the released value in its tariff, requested before shipping and shown on the bill of lading; not insurance ([Cargo Liability & Claims](/books/logistics/cargo-claims#released-values-in-ltl)).
- **AOBRD (automatic onboard recording device):** the pre-ELD recorder carriers could keep using through December 16, 2019 if installed before the ELD compliance date ([ELDs & Telematics](/books/logistics/electronic-logging-devices)).
- **BASIC:** one of the seven Behavior Analysis and Safety Improvement Categories in FMCSA's Safety Measurement System, from Unsafe Driving to Driver Fitness ([Safety Ratings, CSA & Inspections](/books/logistics/safety-and-csa#the-safety-measurement-system)).
- **Basic description:** a hazardous material's identification number, proper shipping name, hazard class and packing group, in that order, on a shipping paper ([Hazardous Materials in Transport](/books/logistics/hazmat-in-transport#shipping-papers-and-emergency-response-information)).
- **BMC-35, BMC-36:** the insurer's and surety's notices cancelling a filing, effective 30 days after FMCSA receives them ([Bonds & Insurance Minimums](/books/logistics/financial-security-and-insurance#filings-and-cancellations)).
- **BMC-84, BMC-85:** the surety bond and the trust fund agreement a broker or freight forwarder files for its $75,000 financial security ([Bonds & Insurance Minimums](/books/logistics/financial-security-and-insurance#broker-and-forwarder-security)).
- **BMC-91, BMC-91X:** the insurer's certificate of public liability filed with FMCSA; the 91X represents full coverage or a layer where several insurers aggregate to the limit ([Bonds & Insurance Minimums](/books/logistics/financial-security-and-insurance#filings-and-cancellations)).
- **BOC-3:** the form designating process agents in each state, usually a blanket designation filed by a process agent company ([Operating Authority & Registration](/books/logistics/operating-authority#process-agents-and-the-boc-3)).
- **Carmack Amendment:** 49 U.S.C. 14706, which makes interstate motor carriers and freight forwarders liable for the actual loss or injury to property they carry, with floors of nine months to file a claim and two years to sue ([Cargo Liability & Claims](/books/logistics/cargo-claims#the-carmack-amendment)).
- **CDL endorsement:** a code on a commercial driver's license authorizing a vehicle or cargo type: T doubles and triples, P passenger, N tank, H hazmat, X tank and hazmat, S school bus ([Drivers: CDLs, Qualification & the Clearinghouse](/books/logistics/drivers-and-qualification#the-license)).
- **CDLIS (Commercial Driver's License Information System):** the national system of CDL records that states check before issuing a license and that has held CDL drivers' medical certification since June 23, 2025 ([Drivers: CDLs, Qualification & the Clearinghouse](/books/logistics/drivers-and-qualification#medical-certification)).
- **COGSA:** the U.S. Carriage of Goods by Sea Act, limiting an ocean carrier's liability to $500 per package unless value is declared ([Cargo Liability & Claims](/books/logistics/cargo-claims#ocean-and-air)).
- **Compliance categories:** the enhanced Safety Measurement System's replacement for the BASICs, approved in November 2024 and not launched as of November 2026 ([Safety Ratings, CSA & Inspections](/books/logistics/safety-and-csa#the-enhanced-sms)).
- **Concealed damage:** damage discovered after the consignee signed a clean delivery receipt; carriers commonly ask for a report within five business days ([Cargo Liability & Claims](/books/logistics/cargo-claims#concealed-damage-salvage-and-subrogation)).
- **Crash Preventability Determination Program (CPDP):** FMCSA's review of eligible crashes; a not-preventable finding removes the crash from the Crash Indicator calculation ([Safety Ratings, CSA & Inspections](/books/logistics/safety-and-csa#crashes)).
- **CVSA decal:** a decal showing a vehicle passed a North American Standard inspection without a critical defect, honored by other jurisdictions for the month issued and the following two ([Safety Ratings, CSA & Inspections](/books/logistics/safety-and-csa#roadside-inspections)).
- **DataQs:** FMCSA's system for requesting review of inspection and crash data, decided by states in three stages ([Safety Ratings, CSA & Inspections](/books/logistics/safety-and-csa#dataqs)).
- **Drawdown (broker bond):** a payment from a broker's or forwarder's bond or trust that takes it below $75,000; since January 16, 2026 it suspends the broker's authority within 7 business days of notice unless cured ([Bonds & Insurance Minimums](/books/logistics/financial-security-and-insurance#broker-and-forwarder-security)).
- **Driver qualification file:** the file a carrier keeps for each driver under 49 CFR 391.51: application, motor vehicle records, road test, annual review, medical certification and registry verification ([Drivers: CDLs, Qualification & the Clearinghouse](/books/logistics/drivers-and-qualification#the-driver-qualification-file)).
- **Drug and Alcohol Clearinghouse:** FMCSA's database of CDL drivers' drug and alcohol program violations and return-to-duty status, queried by employers and states ([Drivers: CDLs, Qualification & the Clearinghouse](/books/logistics/drivers-and-qualification#the-drug-and-alcohol-clearinghouse)).
- **ELD (electronic logging device):** the engine-synchronized, self-certified recorder of a driver's duty status that drivers keeping records of duty status must use ([ELDs & Telematics](/books/logistics/electronic-logging-devices#what-an-eld-records)).
- **ELD malfunction:** a power, engine synchronization, timing, positioning, recording or transfer failure the device detects; the driver notifies the carrier within 24 hours and the carrier fixes it within 8 days ([ELDs & Telematics](/books/logistics/electronic-logging-devices#malfunctions)).
- **ELD output file:** the single comma-delimited file an ELD transfers at the roadside, with a file data check value to detect modification ([ELDs & Telematics](/books/logistics/electronic-logging-devices#roadside-transfer)).
- **Emergency response information:** the hazards, precautions, firefighting, spill and first aid information that travels with a hazmat shipping paper under 172.602 ([Hazardous Materials in Transport](/books/logistics/hazmat-in-transport#shipping-papers-and-emergency-response-information)).
- **Entry-level driver training (ELDT):** training from a provider on FMCSA's Training Provider Registry, required since February 7, 2022 before a first Class A or B CDL or a first P, S or H endorsement ([Drivers: CDLs, Qualification & the Clearinghouse](/books/logistics/drivers-and-qualification#entry-level-driver-training)).
- **Full query, limited query:** the two Clearinghouse queries: a full query returns records and needs the driver's consent in the Clearinghouse; a limited query says only whether records exist and satisfies the annual requirement ([Drivers: CDLs, Qualification & the Clearinghouse](/books/logistics/drivers-and-qualification#the-drug-and-alcohol-clearinghouse)).
- **Hazardous materials table:** the 172.101 table listing each proper shipping name with its class, identification number, packing group, labels, special provisions, packaging and quantity limits ([Hazardous Materials in Transport](/books/logistics/hazmat-in-transport#the-hazardous-materials-table)).
- **Hazmat employee, hazmat employer:** anyone who loads, handles, prepares papers for or drives hazardous materials, and anyone who employs or uses at least one such person ([Hazardous Materials in Transport](/books/logistics/hazmat-in-transport#who-the-rules-bind)).
- **Hazmat safety permit:** an FMCSA permit a motor carrier needs before hauling the highest-risk materials, such as highway route-controlled radioactive material or Zone A inhalation hazards ([Hazardous Materials in Transport](/books/logistics/hazmat-in-transport#registration-and-permits)).
- **MC number, FF number:** FMCSA docket numbers for motor carriers and freight forwarders, kept alongside the USDOT number as of 2026 because the Motus notice leaves their elimination out of phase II ([Operating Authority & Registration](/books/logistics/operating-authority#the-docket-number-isn-t-gone)).
- **MCS-90:** the FMCSA-prescribed endorsement on a carrier's liability policy that serves as proof of the federal minimum ([Bonds & Insurance Minimums](/books/logistics/financial-security-and-insurance#public-liability-minimums)).
- **MCS-150:** the motor carrier identification report filed before operations and updated every 24 months on a schedule set by the USDOT number's last two digits ([Operating Authority & Registration](/books/logistics/operating-authority#keeping-registration-current)).
- **Montreal Convention:** the 1999 treaty on international air carriage, limiting cargo liability to 26 SDRs per kilogram since December 28, 2024 ([Cargo Liability & Claims](/books/logistics/cargo-claims#ocean-and-air)).
- **Motus:** FMCSA's registration system replacing the Unified Registration System and the FMCSA Portal, phase I released December 8, 2025 ([Operating Authority & Registration](/books/logistics/operating-authority#motus-and-the-rules-in-flux)).
- **National Registry of Certified Medical Examiners:** FMCSA's list of examiners allowed to certify drivers, who transmit results to it by midnight of the next day ([Drivers: CDLs, Qualification & the Clearinghouse](/books/logistics/drivers-and-qualification#medical-certification)).
- **New entrant:** a carrier with a new USDOT number, monitored for 18 months and audited within 12 months of starting operations if it carries property ([Safety Ratings, CSA & Inspections](/books/logistics/safety-and-csa#new-entrants)).
- **Non-domiciled CDL:** a CDL a state issues to a person domiciled outside the United States, limited since March 16, 2026 to H-2A, H-2B and E-2 status holders and expiring with their I-94 or in one year ([Drivers: CDLs, Qualification & the Clearinghouse](/books/logistics/drivers-and-qualification#non-domiciled-cdls)).
- **North American Standard inspection levels:** CVSA's eight roadside inspection types, from Level I (full driver and vehicle) to Level VIII (electronic, in motion) ([Safety Ratings, CSA & Inspections](/books/logistics/safety-and-csa#roadside-inspections)).
- **On-duty time:** all time from beginning work or being required to be ready until released, including waiting at a shipper to be dispatched, loading, inspecting, fueling and driving ([Hours of Service](/books/logistics/hours-of-service#the-limits)).
- **Operating authority:** FMCSA's registration of a for-hire motor carrier, broker or freight forwarder to sell transportation, granted under a docket number on top of the USDOT number ([Operating Authority & Registration](/books/logistics/operating-authority)).
- **Out-of-service order:** an inspector's order taking a driver or vehicle off the road under CVSA's out-of-service criteria until the violation is corrected ([Safety Ratings, CSA & Inspections](/books/logistics/safety-and-csa#roadside-inspections)).
- **Personal conveyance:** moving a CMV for personal reasons while off duty and relieved of all work, logged as off duty under FMCSA's 2018 guidance, laden or not ([Hours of Service](/books/logistics/hours-of-service#exceptions)).
- **PHMSA registration:** the annual registration and fee owed by anyone who offers or transports placarded or other listed quantities of hazardous materials ([Hazardous Materials in Transport](/books/logistics/hazmat-in-transport#registration-and-permits)).
- **Placard:** the diamond sign on a vehicle naming the hazard class it carries, required for Table 1 materials in any amount and Table 2 materials at 454 kilograms aggregate ([Hazardous Materials in Transport](/books/logistics/hazmat-in-transport#placarding-and-marking)).
- **Preliminary grant:** the FMCSA Register publication of an authority application, after which the applicant has 20 days to file insurance and the BOC-3 ([Operating Authority & Registration](/books/logistics/operating-authority#applying-the-preliminary-grant-and-the-new-entrant-period)).
- **Process agent:** the person in a state on whom court papers for a carrier or broker can be served ([Operating Authority & Registration](/books/logistics/operating-authority#process-agents-and-the-boc-3)).
- **Record of duty status (RODS):** the driver's log of off duty, sleeper berth, driving and on duty time, kept six months by the carrier ([Hours of Service](/books/logistics/hours-of-service#auditing)).
- **Registered ELD list:** FMCSA's list of self-certified devices; a removed device must be replaced within 60 days or its logs count as no record of duty status ([ELDs & Telematics](/books/logistics/electronic-logging-devices#self-certification-the-registered-list-and-revocations)).
- **Released value (released rate):** a lower liability limit, usually per pound, that a shipper accepts in writing in exchange for the carrier's rate ([Cargo Liability & Claims](/books/logistics/cargo-claims#released-values-in-ltl)).
- **Safety Measurement System (SMS):** FMCSA's monthly ranking of carriers by BASIC over 24 months of inspections and crashes, used to prioritize interventions ([Safety Ratings, CSA & Inspections](/books/logistics/safety-and-csa#the-safety-measurement-system)).
- **Safety rating:** FMCSA's Satisfactory, Conditional or Unsatisfactory determination after a compliance review; a carrier never reviewed is Unrated ([Safety Ratings, CSA & Inspections](/books/logistics/safety-and-csa#safety-ratings-and-investigations)).
- **SCAC (Standard Carrier Alpha Code):** NMFTA's carrier identifier, issued only by NMFTA ([The Logistics Business](/books/logistics/logistics-business#modes)).
- **Segregation table:** the 177.848 table of hazard classes that may not share a vehicle (X) or may only if separated (O) ([Hazardous Materials in Transport](/books/logistics/hazmat-in-transport#segregation-loading-and-attendance)).
- **Shipping paper:** the document, usually the bill of lading, that carries a hazardous material's basic description, quantity and emergency response number ([Hazardous Materials in Transport](/books/logistics/hazmat-in-transport#shipping-papers-and-emergency-response-information)).
- **Short-haul exception:** relief from logs for drivers within 150 air miles of their reporting location who are released within 14 hours ([Hours of Service](/books/logistics/hours-of-service#exceptions)).
- **Sleeper berth split:** meeting the 10 hours off with two periods of at least 2 hours, one at least 7 in the sleeper berth, neither counting against the 14-hour window ([Hours of Service](/books/logistics/hours-of-service#the-sleeper-berth-split)).
- **Special Drawing Right (SDR):** the IMF unit of account in which the Montreal Convention and Hague-Visby Rules state liability limits ([Cargo Liability & Claims](/books/logistics/cargo-claims#ocean-and-air)).
- **Supporting documents:** the bills of lading, dispatch records, receipts, fleet messages and pay records a carrier keeps, up to eight per driver per day, to verify the log ([Hours of Service](/books/logistics/hours-of-service#auditing)).
- **TSA security threat assessment:** the background check TSA completes before a state issues or renews a hazmat endorsement ([Hazardous Materials in Transport](/books/logistics/hazmat-in-transport#the-endorsement-and-the-threat-assessment)).
- **Unidentified driving:** driving an ELD records with no driver logged in, which the driver must claim or reject and the carrier must assign or annotate ([ELDs & Telematics](/books/logistics/electronic-logging-devices#edits-certification-and-unidentified-driving)).
- **Unified Carrier Registration (UCR):** the annual interstate registration and fee, by fleet bracket, for carriers, brokers, forwarders and leasing companies ([Operating Authority & Registration](/books/logistics/operating-authority#keeping-registration-current)).
- **Uniform Straight Bill of Lading:** the bill of lading terms in the NMFC that most LTL carriers apply, setting common-law liability, claim periods and per-article released value ([Cargo Liability & Claims](/books/logistics/cargo-claims#how-the-carriers-do-it)).
- **USDOT number:** FMCSA's unique identifier for every regulated entity ([Operating Authority & Registration](/books/logistics/operating-authority#who-needs-what)).
- **Yard move:** driving inside a yard or terminal logged as on duty rather than driving, the subject of FMCSA's 2021 proposed guidance ([Hours of Service](/books/logistics/hours-of-service#exceptions)).

## Planning and Execution

- **Accessorial:** a charge beyond linehaul and fuel for extra service or delay (detention, liftgate, layover, lumper, stop-off) ([Rating, Tariffs & Accessorials](/books/logistics/rating-and-accessorials#accessorials)).
- **Broadcast tendering:** tendering to several carriers at once, taking the best or the first acceptable answer ([Tendering & Acceptance](/books/logistics/tendering#the-waterfall)).
- **Business share:** the target percentage of a lane's volume awarded to a carrier, with a tolerance before carrier selection penalizes deviation ([Freight Procurement & Routing Guides](/books/logistics/procurement-and-routing-guides#responses-rounds-and-award)).
- **Conditional acceptance:** a carrier's acceptance of a tender with conditions, code B in the X12 990's reservation action codes ([Tendering & Acceptance](/books/logistics/tendering#the-tender-state-machine)).
- **Consolidation (load planning):** combining shipments with compatible origins, destinations, windows and freight onto one load ([Load Planning & Optimization](/books/logistics/load-planning#consolidation)).
- **Continuous move:** two loads linked so one carrier's truck delivers the first and picks up the second nearby, priced together ([Load Planning & Optimization](/books/logistics/load-planning#continuous-moves)).
- **Detention (trucking):** pay for a truck held at a facility past free time; the DOT Inspector General estimated it cost truckload drivers $1.1 billion to $1.3 billion a year ([Rating, Tariffs & Accessorials](/books/logistics/rating-and-accessorials#detention)).
- **Dispatch:** the carrier's assignment of a load or leg to a driver, tractor and trailer ([Carrier Dispatch & Asset Management](/books/logistics/carrier-operations#dispatch)).
- **Driver settlement:** a statement of what a driver or owner-operator earned for a period and what was deducted, computed from the loads they ran ([Carrier Dispatch & Asset Management](/books/logistics/carrier-operations#settlements)).
- **First-tender acceptance:** the share of loads accepted by the first carrier tendered ([Tendering & Acceptance](/books/logistics/tendering#measuring-acceptance)).
- **Floor position:** the trailer floor space one standard 48-by-40-inch pallet occupies; a 53-foot dry van has about 26 loaded straight ([Load Planning & Optimization](/books/logistics/load-planning#cube-weight-and-floor-space)).
- **Freight RFP:** a shipper's bid event inviting carriers and brokers to price its lanes for a period; SAP calls it a freight agreement RFQ ([Freight Procurement & Routing Guides](/books/logistics/procurement-and-routing-guides#the-annual-rfp)).
- **Fuel surcharge:** a per-mile or percentage line indexed to a published diesel price, such as the EIA's weekly on-highway average ([Rating, Tariffs & Accessorials](/books/logistics/rating-and-accessorials#fuel-surcharges)).
- **Layover:** a per-day charge when a truck can't load or unload until a later day ([Rating, Tariffs & Accessorials](/books/logistics/rating-and-accessorials#accessorials)).
- **Linehaul:** the base transportation charge for moving freight between origin and destination, before fuel and accessorials ([The Logistics Business](/books/logistics/logistics-business#a-brokered-truckload)).
- **Mileage engine:** a commercial routing product (such as PC\*Miler) whose named version and routing type produce the miles a per-mile rate is applied to ([Rating, Tariffs & Accessorials](/books/logistics/rating-and-accessorials#mileage)).
- **Multi-stop truckload:** one truckload with several pickups or deliveries, priced with stop-off charges ([Load Planning & Optimization](/books/logistics/load-planning#multi-stop-truckloads)).
- **Order-to-invoice pipeline:** the steps every TMS runs, from order intake and shipment building through planning, rating, tendering, dispatch, execution and proof of delivery to invoicing, audit and payment ([Transportation Management Systems](/books/logistics/transportation-management#the-order-to-invoice-pipeline)).
- **Peer-to-peer tendering:** sequential tendering to one carrier at a time, each with a response window ([Tendering & Acceptance](/books/logistics/tendering#the-waterfall)).
- **Periodic (annual) inspection:** the inspection every commercial motor vehicle must pass at least once in each 12 months under 49 CFR 396.17 ([Carrier Dispatch & Asset Management](/books/logistics/carrier-operations#equipment-maintenance-and-inspections)).
- **Pool distribution:** consolidating many small shipments into a truckload to a pool point in the destination region, then delivering locally ([Load Planning & Optimization](/books/logistics/load-planning#pool-distribution)).
- **Pool point:** the cross-dock or terminal where pooled freight is broken down for local delivery, with a cutoff time ([Load Planning & Optimization](/books/logistics/load-planning#pool-distribution)).
- **Relay (driver):** a handoff of a trailer from one driver to another partway through a load ([Carrier Dispatch & Asset Management](/books/logistics/carrier-operations#trips-legs-relays-and-repowers)).
- **Repower:** replacing a load's tractor and driver mid-trip after a breakdown or hours running out ([Carrier Dispatch & Asset Management](/books/logistics/carrier-operations#trips-legs-relays-and-repowers)).
- **Routing guide (carrier):** the per-lane ordered list of contracted carriers a shipper tenders down, ending in a spot step; distinct from a retailer's inbound routing guide for vendors ([Freight Procurement & Routing Guides](/books/logistics/procurement-and-routing-guides#routing-guides)).
- **Routing guide compliance:** whether awarded carriers accept the tenders they were awarded, and whether the shipper's planners tender to the guide ([Freight Procurement & Routing Guides](/books/logistics/procurement-and-routing-guides#compliance)).
- **Stop-off charge:** a charge for each pickup or delivery beyond the first and last on a truckload, often plus out-of-route miles ([Load Planning & Optimization](/books/logistics/load-planning#multi-stop-truckloads)).
- **Tender (freight):** an offer of a load to a carrier, by EDI 204, API, portal or load board, which the carrier accepts or rejects before it expires ([Tendering & Acceptance](/books/logistics/tendering#the-tender-state-machine)).
- **Transportation management system (TMS):** software that turns orders into executed, paid freight: building shipments and loads, rating, tendering, tracking, and auditing and paying freight bills; shipper, broker and carrier TMSs center on different records ([Transportation Management Systems](/books/logistics/transportation-management#three-kinds-of-tms)).
- **Truck ordered not used (TONU):** a charge owed to a carrier when a load it accepted and dispatched is cancelled ([Tendering & Acceptance](/books/logistics/tendering#changes-and-cancellations)).
- **Waterfall (tender):** the process of tendering a load down its routing guide, sequentially or by broadcast, until a carrier accepts or the guide is exhausted ([Tendering & Acceptance](/books/logistics/tendering#the-waterfall)).

## Data and Visibility

- **204 (Motor Carrier Load Tender):** the X12 transaction set that offers a load to a carrier, with stops, dates, equipment and payment terms; its B2A purpose code marks an original, a cancellation or a change ([Transportation EDI](/books/logistics/transportation-edi#the-204-load-tender)).
- **210 (Motor Carrier Freight Details and Invoice):** the X12 freight bill for one shipment, with B3 header, L1 charge lines and L3 totals, amounts in implied-decimal N2 elements ([Transportation EDI](/books/logistics/transportation-edi#the-210-freight-invoice)).
- **214 (Transportation Carrier Shipment Status Message):** the X12 status message a carrier sends for a shipment, one AT7 status or appointment per loop, with a time code that is often local time with no offset ([Transportation EDI](/books/logistics/transportation-edi#the-214-shipment-status)).
- **990 (Response to a Load Tender):** the X12 answer to a 204, whose B1-04 reservation action code accepts (A), declines (D, "Reservation Cancelled") or conditions the tender, read per partner ([Transportation EDI](/books/logistics/transportation-edi#the-990-response)).
- **Arrival basis:** the kind of evidence (facility system, geofence, driver app, carrier report, manual) that set a stop's arrival, recorded with the event chosen and the precedence rule version ([Visibility & Tracking](/books/logistics/visibility-and-tracking#one-timeline-across-sources)).
- **AT7:** the 214 segment that carries a shipment status (AT701) and its reason (AT702), or an appointment status (AT703) and its reason (AT704), with the date, time and time code ([Transportation EDI](/books/logistics/transportation-edi#the-214-shipment-status)).
- **B2A purpose code:** the 204 element saying whether a tender is an original (00), a cancellation (01) or a change, sent as 04 (update) or 05 (replace) depending on the partner ([Transportation EDI](/books/logistics/transportation-edi#the-204-load-tender)).
- **Calibration (ETA ranges):** whether arrivals fall inside a source's stated ETA ranges as often as the ranges claim, such as about 80% of the time for an 80% range ([ETAs & Exception Management](/books/logistics/etas-and-exceptions#confidence)).
- **Check call:** a report of where a truck is between stops, by phone to a dispatcher or as an X6 En Route to Location in a 214; a partner's statement, kept as a `position` event ([Visibility & Tracking](/books/logistics/visibility-and-tracking#where-positions-come-from)).
- **DSDC (Digital Standards Development Council):** NMFTA's body for freight API standards, publishing LTL eBOL, pickup, preliminary charges and visibility standards and truckload booking, appointment, invoice, rating and visibility standards ([Logistics APIs & Standards](/books/logistics/logistics-apis#standards-bodies)).
- **Dwell (geofence):** the time a vehicle must stay inside a fence before its entry counts as an arrival ([Visibility & Tracking](/books/logistics/visibility-and-tracking#arrival-and-departure-at-facilities)).
- **ETA (freight):** a prediction of when freight reaches a stop, from a carrier, a visibility network, an ocean carrier or the system's own model, stored with when it was computed and often a range ([ETAs & Exception Management](/books/logistics/etas-and-exceptions#what-an-eta-is-made-of)).
- **ETA horizon:** how far ahead of the actual arrival a prediction was made; errors and ranges are measured per horizon ([ETAs & Exception Management](/books/logistics/etas-and-exceptions#confidence)).
- **Event classifier:** whether an event is actual, planned or estimated (DCSA's ACT, PLN, EST); only actual events move a stop's state ([Visibility & Tracking](/books/logistics/visibility-and-tracking#milestones)).
- **Exception (shipment):** a condition on a load, stop or shipment someone has to act on (late pickup, late delivery risk, missed appointment, no or stale tracking, off route, dwell, temperature, hold, OS&D), worked as a record from open to resolved ([ETAs & Exception Management](/books/logistics/etas-and-exceptions#exceptions)).
- **Exception rule:** a versioned rule, kept as data, that says when an exception opens for a scope (customer, lane, mode, facility, carrier), its thresholds, severity, owner and recipients ([ETAs & Exception Management](/books/logistics/etas-and-exceptions#exceptions)).
- **Feed cursor:** the consumer's saved position in a provider's polling feed (Geotab's version token), committed only after the batch it follows is processed ([Logistics APIs & Standards](/books/logistics/logistics-apis#polling-feeds)).
- **Geofence:** a boundary around a property whose entry and exit mark arrival and departure; at a freight facility, a versioned polygon around the gate, yard and dock apron, with a larger exit fence ([Visibility & Tracking](/books/logistics/visibility-and-tracking#arrival-and-departure-at-facilities); defined in [Dispatch & the Day of Service](/books/field-operations/dispatch#arrival-and-departure-detection)).
- **Hysteresis (geofence):** using a larger exit fence than entry fence, so a truck parked on the boundary doesn't flap between arrived and departed ([Visibility & Tracking](/books/logistics/visibility-and-tracking#arrival-and-departure-at-facilities)).
- **Implementation guide (EDI):** a trading partner's published specification of which segments, elements, codes and version it sends and accepts for one transaction set; the contract a map is built from ([Transportation EDI](/books/logistics/transportation-edi#how-the-partners-do-it)).
- **Late reason code:** the carrier's stated reason for a delayed status or appointment, sent in a 214's AT702 (NS for normal status); mapped to an exception's cause and kept as the carrier's claim ([ETAs & Exception Management](/books/logistics/etas-and-exceptions#exceptions)).
- **Letter of authority:** a shipper's written authorization for a third party to see or act on its freight in a carrier's systems, as Union Pacific requires for third-party API access ([Logistics APIs & Standards](/books/logistics/logistics-apis#connections-and-credentials)).
- **Milestone (freight):** a business fact at a place (arrived at pickup, picked up, arrived at delivery, delivered, gated out, loaded, discharged) reported by many channels and mapped to one event code ([Visibility & Tracking](/books/logistics/visibility-and-tracking#milestones)).
- **No tracking, stale tracking:** a load whose tracking never started by its lead time before pickup, and one whose session went quiet while in transit; the two most common visibility exceptions ([Visibility & Tracking](/books/logistics/visibility-and-tracking#tracking-sessions)).
- **Partner code map:** a per-partner, dated mapping from a partner's code in one field (a B1-04 answer, an AT701 status, an L1 charge code) to its meaning in this system, inbound and outbound ([Transportation EDI](/books/logistics/transportation-edi#partner-code-maps)).
- **Partner connection:** one party's link to one carrier, network or telematics account, with its credentials, environment, standard version, rate limit and the authority to see the data ([Logistics APIs & Standards](/books/logistics/logistics-apis#connections-and-credentials)).
- **Reporting latency:** a partner's received time minus event time for its status messages, the measure behind "within 15 minutes of the event" ([Visibility & Tracking](/books/logistics/visibility-and-tracking#one-timeline-across-sources)).
- **Reservation action code:** the 990's B1-04 element; seven X12 codes (A, B, C, D, N, R, U) that partners narrow and reinterpret ([Transportation EDI](/books/logistics/transportation-edi#the-990-response)).
- **Shipment identification number:** the tenderer's load or shipment number sent in the 204's B2-04 and echoed in the 990, 214 and 210; the join key across a load's messages ([Transportation EDI](/books/logistics/transportation-edi#the-204-load-tender)).
- **Shown ETA:** the one prediction a stop displays, chosen by rule from all sources (actuals first, stale ones out, the best-measured fresh source) ([ETAs & Exception Management](/books/logistics/etas-and-exceptions#choosing-the-eta-to-show)).
- **SSCC (Serial Shipping Container Code):** GS1's 18-digit ID for a carton or pallet, printed as a GS1-128 barcode and linked to the ASN ([The Logistics Business](/books/logistics/logistics-business#a-manufactured-lot-from-a-supplier); defined in [Warehouses, 3PLs & Fulfillment Networks](/books/commerce/warehouses-and-3pls#receiving-and-inbound)).
- **Tracking session:** one source tracking one load for a window (an ELD integration, a driver app, a visibility provider, a 214 feed, a carrier API or manual check calls), with its consent and the reason it ended ([Visibility & Tracking](/books/logistics/visibility-and-tracking#tracking-sessions)).
- **Visibility network:** a platform that aggregates carriers' ELD, app, EDI and API data into one tracking feed per shipment, such as project44, FourKites, MacroPoint or Trucker Tools ([Visibility & Tracking](/books/logistics/visibility-and-tracking#how-the-platforms-do-it)).

## Warehouses and Yards

- **Active location (pick face):** a location that holds loose units for picking and is refilled from reserve ([Warehouse Management Systems](/books/logistics/warehouse-management#the-building-as-data)).
- **Allocation (inventory):** reserving specific balances for an order line, by FEFO, FIFO, shelf-life and unit-fit rules, never across owners ([Warehouse Management Systems](/books/logistics/warehouse-management#allocation)).
- **AMR (autonomous mobile robot):** a robot that navigates freely to carry pallets, totes, carts or shelves, unlike an AGV that follows fixed paths ([Warehouse Automation](/books/logistics/warehouse-automation#the-kinds-of-automation)).
- **Appointment type (load type):** a facility's definition of an appointment's duration, direction, equipment and who may book it ([Yard Management & Dock Scheduling](/books/logistics/yard-and-dock#dock-appointments)).
- **AS/RS (automated storage and retrieval system):** a structure with cranes, shuttles or robots that stores pallets, totes or cases and brings them to a station ([Warehouse Automation](/books/logistics/warehouse-automation#the-kinds-of-automation)).
- **Blind receiving:** counting a delivery without seeing the expected quantities, reconciled against the notice afterward ([Warehouse Management Systems](/books/logistics/warehouse-management#receiving)).
- **Closed book (fixed plus variable):** a contract with a fixed fee for the building, systems and management and variable rates for volume and labor ([Contract Logistics & 3PL Billing](/books/logistics/contract-logistics#contract-models)).
- **Contract logistics:** running warehouses and related transportation for a client under a multi-year contract ([Contract Logistics & 3PL Billing](/books/logistics/contract-logistics)).
- **Cube storage:** a grid of stacked bins with robots on top that dig out and deliver bins to ports, as AutoStore and Ocado build ([Warehouse Automation](/books/logistics/warehouse-automation#the-kinds-of-automation)).
- **Depositor:** the party that stores goods with a warehouse; a 3PL's client ([Contract Logistics & 3PL Billing](/books/logistics/contract-logistics#warehouse-receipts-liability-and-liens)).
- **Directed putaway:** the WMS choosing each received plate's location by ordered strategies (consolidate, then an empty location) within zone and capacity rules ([Warehouse Management Systems](/books/logistics/warehouse-management#putaway)).
- **Dock appointment:** a facility's reservation of a door's time for one delivery or pickup, by appointment type, with a confirmation number and a recorded outcome ([Yard Management & Dock Scheduling](/books/logistics/yard-and-dock#dock-appointments)).
- **Engineered labor standard:** a task's goal time built from fixed, per-line and per-unit elements, travel at the equipment's speed, and a personal, fatigue and delay allowance ([Picking, Packing & Labor](/books/logistics/warehouse-operations#labor-standards)).
- **FEFO (first expired, first out):** allocating the lot with the earliest expiry first, subject to the customer's minimum remaining shelf life ([Warehouse Management Systems](/books/logistics/warehouse-management#allocation)).
- **Golden zone:** the shelf heights between waist and shoulder, where fast movers and heavy items are slotted ([Picking, Packing & Labor](/books/logistics/warehouse-operations#slotting)).
- **Goods-to-person:** automation that brings the location to a stationary picker at a port instead of sending the picker to the location ([Warehouse Automation](/books/logistics/warehouse-automation#the-kinds-of-automation)).
- **Handling in and out:** per-pallet, case or unit charges for receiving and putaway, and for picking and loading ([Contract Logistics & 3PL Billing](/books/logistics/contract-logistics#the-rate-card)).
- **High-security seal:** a bolt or cable seal meeting ISO 17712, applied after loading and verified at the gate ([Yard Management & Dock Scheduling](/books/logistics/yard-and-dock#the-gate)).
- **Inbound and outbound LPN:** the plate on received stock and the plate on a packed shipping unit; a full pallet picked whole ships under the plate it arrived with ([Warehouse Management Systems](/books/logistics/warehouse-management#license-plates)).
- **Initial storage:** storage charged on a receipt for its first month, with a split day set by the operator deciding whether the full or half month rate is billed (split-month billing) ([Contract Logistics & 3PL Billing](/books/logistics/contract-logistics#the-rate-card)).
- **Interleaving:** giving a worker the next task near where the last one ended, such as a replenishment after a putaway, so no trip runs empty ([Picking, Packing & Labor](/books/logistics/warehouse-operations#pick-methods-and-the-task-queue)).
- **Inventory status:** the condition dimension of a balance (available, quality hold, quarantine, damaged, customs hold, expired, recall hold); only available stock allocates ([Warehouse Management Systems](/books/logistics/warehouse-management#inventory-where-what-whose-and-in-what-condition)).
- **Minimum storage guarantee:** a commitment to pay for a floor of storage whether used or not; Lineage had one on about 46.1% of its 2025 storage revenue ([Contract Logistics & 3PL Billing](/books/logistics/contract-logistics#how-the-operators-do-it)).
- **Recurring storage:** storage billed each period on the stock counted at the previous period's cutoff ([Contract Logistics & 3PL Billing](/books/logistics/contract-logistics#the-rate-card)).
- **Replenishment (warehouse):** moving stock from reserve to pick faces, triggered by a minimum, a share of capacity, a picker's scan or a wave's demand ([Warehouse Management Systems](/books/logistics/warehouse-management#replenishment)).
- **Reserve location:** a racking location that holds full license plates as stock behind the pick faces ([Warehouse Management Systems](/books/logistics/warehouse-management#the-building-as-data)).
- **Slotting:** assigning items to pick faces by velocity, cube, weight and travel, and re-slotting as demand shifts ([Picking, Packing & Labor](/books/logistics/warehouse-operations#slotting)).
- **Unit ladder:** an item's units of measure (each, inner, case, layer, pallet) with the quantity, dimensions and GTIN of each; the pallet's ti and hi are its cases per layer and layers ([Warehouse Management Systems](/books/logistics/warehouse-management#items-units-and-packaging)).
- **VVTT:** CTPAT's seal check: view the seal and locks, verify the number against the documents, tug the seal, twist and turn it ([Yard Management & Dock Scheduling](/books/logistics/yard-and-dock#the-gate)).
- **Warehouse lien:** the warehouse's lien on stored goods for its charges under UCC 7-209, lost when it voluntarily delivers the goods ([Contract Logistics & 3PL Billing](/books/logistics/contract-logistics#warehouse-receipts-liability-and-liens)).
- **Warehouse quota:** in state law, a work standard requiring a specified productivity speed; covered employers must disclose it in writing and produce workers' work speed data on request ([Picking, Packing & Labor](/books/logistics/warehouse-operations#quotas-and-the-law)).
- **Warehouse receipt:** the document a warehouse issues for stored goods, whose required terms UCC 7-202 lists ([Contract Logistics & 3PL Billing](/books/logistics/contract-logistics#warehouse-receipts-liability-and-liens)).
- **Wave:** a set of order lines released to the floor together, allocated and turned into tasks as a group ([Picking, Packing & Labor](/books/logistics/warehouse-operations#waves-waveless-and-order-streaming)).
- **Waveless release (order streaming):** releasing each order or small batch as soon as it can be allocated and worked, prioritized by ship-by time ([Picking, Packing & Labor](/books/logistics/warehouse-operations#waves-waveless-and-order-streaming)).
- **WCS (warehouse control system):** the layer that directly controls conveyors, sorters, shuttles and AS/RS through their logic controllers ([Warehouse Automation](/books/logistics/warehouse-automation#the-control-stack)).
- **WES (warehouse execution system):** the layer that releases work continuously to people and machines, balancing zones between the WMS and the controls ([Warehouse Automation](/books/logistics/warehouse-automation#the-control-stack)).
- **Work speed data:** the quantities, rates and time-on-task records a worker can request under the warehouse quota laws, for 90 days or six months depending on the state ([Picking, Packing & Labor](/books/logistics/warehouse-operations#quotas-and-the-law)).
- **Yard check:** a physical audit of every yard spot's trailer against the system's record ([Yard Management & Dock Scheduling](/books/logistics/yard-and-dock#yard-checks)).
- **Yard driver (hostler, spotter, shunter):** the person who moves trailers between yard spots and doors with a yard tractor ([Yard Management & Dock Scheduling](/books/logistics/yard-and-dock#yard-moves)).

## Freight Money

- **Account debtor:** the business's customer who owes a receivable; once notified of an assignment it can discharge the debt only by paying the assignee; for a carrier's sold invoice, usually the broker or shipper (UCC 9-406) ([Paying Carriers, Quick Pay & Factoring](/books/logistics/paying-carriers#factoring-from-the-payer-s-side); defined in [Small Business Lending](/books/banking-and-lending/small-business-lending#invoice-factoring-and-financing)).
- **Accrued purchased transportation:** the estimated cost of freight that moved but hasn't been invoiced or approved at a period's close ([Logistics Accounting](/books/logistics/logistics-accounting#accrued-purchased-transportation)).
- **Apportioned registration:** registration of an interstate fleet in one IRP base jurisdiction, with each member jurisdiction paid a share of its fee in proportion to the fleet's distance there ([Fuel, Tolls & Fuel Cards](/books/logistics/fuel-and-fuel-cards#irp-apportioned-registration)).
- **Audit tolerance:** the accepted difference between a billed and a rated charge, an amount or a percentage per charge kind and carrier, inside which a line is paid as billed ([Freight Audit & Payment](/books/logistics/freight-audit-and-payment#tolerances-and-exceptions)).
- **Average per-vehicle distance:** the IRP chart a base jurisdiction uses to set fees for a fleet that accrued no actual distance in the reporting period ([Fuel, Tolls & Fuel Cards](/books/logistics/fuel-and-fuel-cards#irp-apportioned-registration)).
- **Balance-due bill:** a separate freight bill for charges beyond those on a bill already paid, such as a reweigh or a late-approved accessorial; a regulated carrier may extend 30 days of credit on it (49 CFR 377.209) ([Freight Invoicing & Settlement](/books/logistics/freight-invoicing#corrections-rebills-balance-due-bills-and-credits)).
- **Base jurisdiction (IFTA):** the member jurisdiction where a licensee's qualified vehicles are registered, its operational records are kept and some of its travel happens; it issues the license and receives the single quarterly return ([Fuel, Tolls & Fuel Cards](/books/logistics/fuel-and-fuel-cards#who-licenses-and-where)).
- **Cab card:** the IRP registration document carried in each apportioned vehicle, listing the jurisdictions and weights it is registered for ([Fuel, Tolls & Fuel Cards](/books/logistics/fuel-and-fuel-cards#irp-apportioned-registration)).
- **Credit period:** the time a carrier gives the payer to pay a presented freight bill; 15 days by default for regulated motor carriers and never more than 30 by tariff (49 CFR 377.203) ([Freight Invoicing & Settlement](/books/logistics/freight-invoicing#presentment-credit-and-the-clocks)).
- **Days in transit method:** recognizing a load's revenue and cost in proportion to the share of its transit time completed by a cutoff ([Logistics Accounting](/books/logistics/logistics-accounting#revenue-over-the-transit)).
- **Duplicate payment:** two or more payments for transporting the same shipment; refundable as an overcharge (49 CFR 378.2) ([Freight Invoicing & Settlement](/books/logistics/freight-invoicing#overcharges-and-duplicate-payments)).
- **Factoring:** buying a business's invoices at a discount, with an advance, a reserve and a fee; with or without recourse, with or without notice to the customer ([Paying Carriers, Quick Pay & Factoring](/books/logistics/paying-carriers#factoring-from-the-payer-s-side); defined in [Small Business Lending](/books/banking-and-lending/small-business-lending#invoice-factoring-and-financing)).
- **Fleet MPG:** on an IFTA return, the average fuel consumption factor: total miles in all jurisdictions divided by total fuel placed in the fleet, to two decimal places ([Fuel, Tolls & Fuel Cards](/books/logistics/fuel-and-fuel-cards#the-quarterly-return)).
- **Freight audit and payment (FAP):** a service, or a provider, that audits carriers' bills against agreed rates and events, codes them to the payer's ledger, and pays them ([Freight Audit & Payment](/books/logistics/freight-audit-and-payment)).
- **Heavy vehicle use tax (HVUT):** the federal annual tax on highway vehicles of 55,000 pounds or more taxable gross weight, filed on IRS Form 2290 for a July-to-June period ([Fuel, Tolls & Fuel Cards](/books/logistics/fuel-and-fuel-cards#the-heavy-vehicle-use-tax)).
- **IFTA decal:** one of the two serialized annual decals the base jurisdiction issues per qualified vehicle, displayed on both sides of the cab ([Fuel, Tolls & Fuel Cards](/books/logistics/fuel-and-fuel-cards#who-licenses-and-where)).
- **In-transit revenue:** revenue recognized at a period end for loads still moving, for the share of the transit already performed ([Logistics Accounting](/books/logistics/logistics-accounting#revenue-over-the-transit)).
- **Incurred but not reported (IBNR):** in the broad sense, all unpaid claims other than case reserves; for a carrier or broker, the part of a cargo or liability claims reserve for losses that have happened and haven't been reported yet ([Logistics Accounting](/books/logistics/logistics-accounting#claims-and-insurance-reserves); defined in [Loss Reserving & Development](/books/insurance/loss-reserving#what-a-reserve-contains)).
- **International Fuel Tax Agreement (IFTA):** the agreement among US states, Canadian provinces and Mexican states under which an interstate carrier licenses in one base jurisdiction and files one quarterly fuel use tax return for all of them ([Fuel, Tolls & Fuel Cards](/books/logistics/fuel-and-fuel-cards#ifta)).
- **International Registration Plan (IRP):** the registration reciprocity agreement under which an interstate fleet gets one plate and cab card per vehicle from its base jurisdiction and pays fees apportioned by distance ([Fuel, Tolls & Fuel Cards](/books/logistics/fuel-and-fuel-cards#irp-apportioned-registration)).
- **Money code:** a code a fleet issues through its fuel card program that a driver redeems for cash or a payment on the road, deducted later as an advance (EFS MoneyCodes, Comdata Comchek and Express Codes) ([Fuel, Tolls & Fuel Cards](/books/logistics/fuel-and-fuel-cards#fuel-on-the-books-and-the-settlement)).
- **Net taxable gallons:** on an IFTA return, a jurisdiction's taxable gallons minus the tax-paid gallons bought there; negative is a credit ([Fuel, Tolls & Fuel Cards](/books/logistics/fuel-and-fuel-cards#the-quarterly-return)).
- **Notice of assignment (NOA):** a factor's notice that a carrier's invoice was sold to it; after receiving one, a broker discharges the debt only by paying the factor (UCC 9-406(a)) ([Paying Carriers, Quick Pay & Factoring](/books/logistics/paying-carriers#factoring-from-the-payer-s-side)).
- **Overcharge claim:** a payer's written claim to the collecting carrier for charges above the applicable rate, including duplicate payments, processed under 49 CFR 378 ([Freight Invoicing & Settlement](/books/logistics/freight-invoicing#overcharges-and-duplicate-payments)).
- **Pass-through charge:** a charge billed to a customer and paid on in full to someone else, such as an owner-operator's fuel surcharge or a duty advanced to CBP, which a policy may exclude from revenue ([Logistics Accounting](/books/logistics/logistics-accounting#principal-or-agent)).
- **Payee (freight):** the party a freight invoice is paid to, which a notice of assignment can change from the carrier to its factor ([Paying Carriers, Quick Pay & Factoring](/books/logistics/paying-carriers#resolving-the-payee)).
- **Post-audit:** review of freight bills already paid, to find overcharges and duplicates and recover them as claims ([Freight Audit & Payment](/books/logistics/freight-audit-and-payment#pre-audit-post-audit-and-recovery)).
- **Pre-audit:** checking a freight bill against agreed rates, events and documents before it is approved for payment ([Freight Audit & Payment](/books/logistics/freight-audit-and-payment#pre-audit-post-audit-and-recovery)).
- **Presentment (freight bill):** delivering a freight bill to the party that owes it; a regulated motor carrier presents within 7 days, excluding weekends and holidays, of receiving prepaid freight or delivering collect freight (49 CFR 377.205) ([Freight Invoicing & Settlement](/books/logistics/freight-invoicing#presentment-credit-and-the-clocks)).
- **Principal versus agent:** whether a company controls a service before it transfers to the customer and books gross revenue, or arranges it and books its fee ([Logistics Accounting](/books/logistics/logistics-accounting#principal-or-agent)).
- **Proof of delivery (POD):** the signed delivery receipt or photo that shows freight arrived, and that gates the carrier's invoice ([Freight Invoicing & Settlement](/books/logistics/freight-invoicing#the-proof-that-gates-the-bill)).
- **Qualified motor vehicle:** under IFTA, a vehicle for persons or property with two axles over 26,000 pounds, three or more axles regardless of weight, or a combination over 26,000 pounds ([Fuel, Tolls & Fuel Cards](/books/logistics/fuel-and-fuel-cards#who-licenses-and-where)).
- **Quick pay:** a carrier's option to be paid in days instead of on standard terms, for a fee taken off the payable ([Paying Carriers, Quick Pay & Factoring](/books/logistics/paying-carriers#terms-quick-pay-and-offsets)).
- **Rebill (corrected invoice):** a new freight bill that replaces an issued one in full, pointing at the original, which is voided with a reason ([Freight Invoicing & Settlement](/books/logistics/freight-invoicing#corrections-rebills-balance-due-bills-and-credits)).
- **Release letter:** a factor's notice that it no longer holds a carrier's receivables, after which payment goes to the carrier or a new factor ([Paying Carriers, Quick Pay & Factoring](/books/logistics/paying-carriers#factoring-from-the-payer-s-side)).
- **Remit account:** a bank account or other payment destination that belongs to a carrier or factor, kept as history with how it was validated and verified ([Paying Carriers, Quick Pay & Factoring](/books/logistics/paying-carriers#payment-diversion-controls)).
- **Reporting period (IRP):** the 12 months ending June 30 before the registration year begins, whose distance by jurisdiction sets the year's apportioned fees ([Fuel, Tolls & Fuel Cards](/books/logistics/fuel-and-fuel-cards#irp-apportioned-registration)).
- **Schedule 1 (Form 2290):** the IRS-stamped schedule that proves the heavy vehicle use tax was paid, required when registering the vehicle with a state ([Fuel, Tolls & Fuel Cards](/books/logistics/fuel-and-fuel-cards#the-heavy-vehicle-use-tax)).
- **Self-insured retention:** the amount of each claim a carrier or broker pays itself before its insurance responds ([Logistics Accounting](/books/logistics/logistics-accounting#claims-and-insurance-reserves)).
- **Short pay:** a payment for less than a freight bill, usually with a reason for each reduced line ([Freight Invoicing & Settlement](/books/logistics/freight-invoicing#short-pays-and-disputes)).
- **Surcharge jurisdiction:** an IFTA member that levies a fuel tax surcharge reported on its own row of the return and rate matrix (Indiana, Kentucky and Virginia in 4Q2026) ([Fuel, Tolls & Fuel Cards](/books/logistics/fuel-and-fuel-cards#the-quarterly-return)).
- **Tax-paid gallons:** fuel bought at retail (or withdrawn from a taxed bulk tank) whose price included a jurisdiction's fuel tax, credited on the IFTA return with a qualifying receipt ([Fuel, Tolls & Fuel Cards](/books/logistics/fuel-and-fuel-cards#records)).
- **Toll-by-plate:** a toll billed to a vehicle's registered owner from a photographed license plate when no transponder is read ([Fuel, Tolls & Fuel Cards](/books/logistics/fuel-and-fuel-cards#tolls)).
- **Transponder:** a vehicle-mounted toll device (E-ZPass or an aggregator's device) read at a plaza and billed to an account ([Fuel, Tolls & Fuel Cards](/books/logistics/fuel-and-fuel-cards#tolls)).
- **Unidentified payment:** money a carrier received but can't match to an open receivable or a service (49 CFR 378.2) ([Freight Invoicing & Settlement](/books/logistics/freight-invoicing#overcharges-and-duplicate-payments)).

## Customs and Trade

- **ABI (Automated Broker Interface):** the channel brokers and self-filing importers use to transmit entries to ACE, in the formats CATAIR defines ([Import Entry & ACE](/books/logistics/import-entry#how-the-platforms-do-it)).
- **ACAS (Air Cargo Advance Screening):** pre-loading cargo data for inbound air shipments, filed by the carrier or an electing forwarder; a do-not-load instruction bars carriage (19 CFR 122.48b) ([Import Entry & ACE](/books/logistics/import-entry#manifests-and-air-cargo)).
- **Accelerated payment (drawback):** payment of a drawback claim before it liquidates, for an approved claimant with a bond (19 CFR 190.92) ([Drawback, FTZs & Trade Agreements](/books/logistics/drawback-ftz-and-trade-agreements#claims-liquidation-and-accelerated-payment)).
- **ACE (Automated Commercial Environment):** CBP's system of record for imports and exports and the US Single Window to partner agencies ([Import Entry & ACE](/books/logistics/import-entry#how-the-platforms-do-it)).
- **ACI eManifest:** Canada's advance commercial information program requiring carriers and forwarders to transmit cargo, conveyance and house bill data before arrival ([EU, UK & Canada Customs](/books/logistics/customs-outside-the-us#aci-emanifest)).
- **Activity code:** the category of obligation a customs bond secures, such as 1 for basic importation and entry, 1a for drawback, 4 for zone operators and 16 for ISF ([Customs Brokers & Bonds](/books/logistics/customs-brokers-and-bonds#bond-types-and-codes)).
- **AEO (Authorised Economic Operator):** EU trusted-trader status for customs simplifications (AEOC), security and safety (AEOS), or both ([EU, UK & Canada Customs](/books/logistics/customs-outside-the-us#authorised-economic-operators)).
- **AES (Automated Export System):** the export component of ACE where EEI is filed, directly or through the free ACE AESDirect portal ([Export Compliance & Screening](/books/logistics/export-compliance#the-export-filing-eei-in-aes)).
- **Antidumping and countervailing duties (AD/CVD):** duties offsetting sales below fair value or foreign subsidies, set by product, country and exporter, with liquidation suspended until review ([Tariff Programs](/books/logistics/tariff-programs#antidumping-and-countervailing-duties)).
- **Applicability review:** an importer's request showing the UFLPA doesn't apply because nothing in the goods came from Xinjiang or a listed entity, supported by tracing to raw materials ([Forced Labor & Partner Agencies](/books/logistics/forced-labor-and-partner-agencies#potential-input-direct-input)).
- **Assist:** materials, tooling, consumables or foreign design work a buyer supplies free or at reduced cost for production, added to transaction value (19 CFR 152.102) ([Classification, Valuation & Origin](/books/logistics/classification-valuation-origin#transaction-value)).
- **Binding ruling:** a written CBP decision applying customs law to a described transaction, binding for identical goods or like facts (19 CFR 177) ([Classification, Valuation & Origin](/books/logistics/classification-valuation-origin#binding-rulings)).
- **BIS Affiliates Rule:** a 2025 BIS rule extending Entity List, MEU and certain SDN restrictions to entities 50 percent or more owned by listed parties; stayed until January 10, 2027 ([Export Compliance & Screening](/books/logistics/export-compliance#the-bis-affiliates-rule)).
- **Bond sufficiency:** whether a continuous bond covers 10% of the principal's prior 12 months of duties, taxes and fees (at least $50,000) plus additions for unpaid bills, per CBP's formula ([Customs Brokers & Bonds](/books/logistics/customs-brokers-and-bonds#how-cbp-sizes-a-bond)).
- **CAD (Commercial Accounting Declaration):** the CARM declaration that replaced Canada's B3 accounting document ([EU, UK & Canada Customs](/books/logistics/customs-outside-the-us#carm-and-the-commercial-accounting-declaration)).
- **CAPE:** Consolidated Administration and Processing of Entries, CBP's ACE process for refunding IEEPA duties, in phases since April 20, 2026 ([Tariff Programs](/books/logistics/tariff-programs#refunds-through-cape)).
- **Cargo release:** the entry filing that asks CBP to release goods against a bond, answered per bill of lading; on paper, CBP Form 3461 ([Import Entry & ACE](/books/logistics/import-entry#cargo-release)).
- **CARM:** the CBSA Assessment and Revenue Management system, Canada's system of record for duties and taxes since October 21, 2024 ([EU, UK & Canada Customs](/books/logistics/customs-outside-the-us#carm-and-the-commercial-accounting-declaration)).
- **CATAIR:** the CBP and Trade Automated Interface Requirements, the record formats for transmitting data to ACE, by chapter ([Import Entry & ACE](/books/logistics/import-entry#how-the-platforms-do-it)).
- **CBP Form 5106:** the Create/Update Importer Identity form that registers an importer or consignee number with CBP with its first formal entry (19 CFR 24.5) ([Customs Brokers & Bonds](/books/logistics/customs-brokers-and-bonds#the-importer-of-record)).
- **CDS (Customs Declaration Service):** HMRC's customs declaration system, used for all UK declarations since 30 March 2024 after CHIEF closed ([EU, UK & Canada Customs](/books/logistics/customs-outside-the-us#cds-and-eori)).
- **Chapter 99:** the HTSUS chapter whose numbers carry additional duties (Sections 301, 232, 338, 122) and are reported on the entry line in CBP's order ([Tariff Programs](/books/logistics/tariff-programs#the-reporting-order)).
- **Commerce Control List (CCL):** the EAR's list of controlled items by ECCN; items subject to the EAR but not on it are EAR99 ([Export Compliance & Screening](/books/logistics/export-compliance#scope-and-the-commerce-control-list)).
- **Country Chart:** the EAR table crossing reasons for control with destinations; an X means a license is needed unless an exception applies ([Export Compliance & Screening](/books/logistics/export-compliance#determining-whether-a-license-is-needed)).
- **Country of origin marking:** the legible, permanent English marking of an imported article's origin; unmarked goods owe an extra 10% duty (19 U.S.C. 1304) ([Classification, Valuation & Origin](/books/logistics/classification-valuation-origin#marking)).
- **CROSS:** the Customs Rulings Online Search System, CBP's public database of rulings ([Classification, Valuation & Origin](/books/logistics/classification-valuation-origin#binding-rulings)).
- **Customs bond:** a contract between a principal and a Treasury-listed surety, with CBP as beneficiary, guaranteeing duties and compliance; continuous for a year or single for one transaction ([Customs Brokers & Bonds](/books/logistics/customs-brokers-and-bonds#customs-bonds)).
- **Customs broker:** a person or company licensed by CBP to transact customs business for others, under a national permit (19 U.S.C. 1641) ([Customs Brokers & Bonds](/books/logistics/customs-brokers-and-bonds#who-may-transact-customs-business)).
- **Customs power of attorney:** the importer's written grant letting a broker act in its name, executed directly with the importer, not through a forwarder (19 CFR 111.36, 141.46) ([Customs Brokers & Bonds](/books/logistics/customs-brokers-and-bonds#the-power-of-attorney)).
- **Direct and indirect representation:** in the EU, a customs representative acting in the importer's name (direct) or in its own name on the importer's behalf (indirect) ([EU, UK & Canada Customs](/books/logistics/customs-outside-the-us#declarations-mrns-and-representation)).
- **Drawback:** the refund of 99% of duties, taxes and fees on imports that are exported or destroyed, or used to make exports, claimed within five years (19 U.S.C. 1313) ([Drawback, FTZs & Trade Agreements](/books/logistics/drawback-ftz-and-trade-agreements#drawback)).
- **EAPA:** the Enforce and Protect Act process under which CBP investigates evasion of AD/CVD orders (19 U.S.C. 1517) ([Tariff Programs](/books/logistics/tariff-programs#antidumping-and-countervailing-duties)).
- **ECCN (Export Control Classification Number):** the five-character code of an item on the Commerce Control List, giving its category, product group and reasons for control ([Export Compliance & Screening](/books/logistics/export-compliance#scope-and-the-commerce-control-list)).
- **ENS (entry summary declaration):** the pre-arrival security declaration for goods entering the EU or Great Britain, normally the carrier's responsibility ([EU, UK & Canada Customs](/books/logistics/customs-outside-the-us#ics2-entry-summary-declarations)).
- **Entry line:** a commodity from one country on an entry summary, with its net quantity, entered value, tariff numbers, charges and duty ([Import Entry & ACE](/books/logistics/import-entry#the-entry-summary)).
- **Entry number:** the 11-character identifier of an entry: a three-character filer code, seven digits and a check digit ([Import Entry & ACE](/books/logistics/import-entry#the-entry-number)).
- **Entry summary:** the filing, with estimated duties, due within 10 working days after the time of entry (19 CFR 142.12) ([Import Entry & ACE](/books/logistics/import-entry#the-clocks-on-an-import)).
- **Entry type:** the two-digit code on an entry summary whose first digit is the category (consumption, informal, warehouse) and second the processing type ([Import Entry & ACE](/books/logistics/import-entry#entry-types)).
- **Entry Type 86:** the voluntary electronic entry CBP created in 2019 for de minimis shipments, ended with the 2025 and 2026 de minimis suspensions ([Import Entry & ACE](/books/logistics/import-entry#de-minimis-and-low-value-shipments)).
- **EORI number:** the EU's customs identifier for economic operators, a country code plus up to 15 characters; the UK issues its own GB and XI EORIs ([EU, UK & Canada Customs](/books/logistics/customs-outside-the-us#the-union-customs-code-and-eori)).
- **Essential character:** the GRI 3(b) test classifying mixtures, composite goods and retail sets by the material or component that gives them their character ([Classification, Valuation & Origin](/books/logistics/classification-valuation-origin#the-general-rules-of-interpretation)).
- **EU Customs Data Hub:** the single EU customs data environment created by the 2026 reform, mandatory for e-commerce from 1 July 2028 and for all importers from 1 March 2034 ([EU, UK & Canada Customs](/books/logistics/customs-outside-the-us#the-2026-customs-reform)).
- **Exception review (UFLPA):** an importer's request conceding a Xinjiang or Entity List link but showing by clear and convincing evidence that the goods weren't made with forced labor; CBP reports granted exceptions to Congress ([Forced Labor & Partner Agencies](/books/logistics/forced-labor-and-partner-agencies#potential-input-direct-input)).
- **FD flag:** FDA's indicator (FD1 to FD4) on an HTS number saying whether a line must be reported to FDA, needs prior notice, or may be disclaimed ([Forced Labor & Partner Agencies](/books/logistics/forced-labor-and-partner-agencies#fda-prior-notice-and-entry-review)).
- **Filer code:** the three-character code CBP assigns to a broker or self-filing importer, the first part of every entry number it files ([Import Entry & ACE](/books/logistics/import-entry#the-entry-number)).
- **Finding (forced labor):** a CBP determination, published in the Customs Bulletin and Federal Register, that covered goods are made with forced labor; goods are then seized and forfeited unless the importer proves otherwise ([Forced Labor & Partner Agencies](/books/logistics/forced-labor-and-partner-agencies#forced-labor-the-statute-and-its-tools)).
- **Foreign trade zone (FTZ):** an area treated as outside the customs territory for duty, where goods can be stored and manufactured without duty until entered for consumption ([Drawback, FTZs & Trade Agreements](/books/logistics/drawback-ftz-and-trade-agreements#how-a-zone-defers-and-reduces-duty)).
- **General Rules of Interpretation (GRIs):** the six rules, applied in order, that govern classification in the HS and HTSUS ([Classification, Valuation & Origin](/books/logistics/classification-valuation-origin#the-general-rules-of-interpretation)).
- **Harbor maintenance fee (HMF):** a port use fee of 0.125% of the cargo's value (19 CFR 24.24) ([The Logistics Business](/books/logistics/logistics-business#an-ocean-import)).
- **HTSUS:** the Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States, the HS extended to eight-digit rate lines and ten-digit statistical reporting numbers, published by USITC ([Classification, Valuation & Origin](/books/logistics/classification-valuation-origin#the-tariff-schedule)).
- **ICS2 (Import Control System 2):** the EU's pre-arrival safety and security system receiving entry summary declarations from carriers and house-level filers, fully operational for all modes from 1 September 2025 ([EU, UK & Canada Customs](/books/logistics/customs-outside-the-us#ics2-entry-summary-declarations)).
- **IEEPA tariffs:** the 2025 emergency-powers duties the Supreme Court held unauthorized in Learning Resources, Inc. v. Trump on February 20, 2026 ([Tariff Programs](/books/logistics/tariff-programs#ieepa-and-learning-resources)).
- **Importer of record:** the owner, purchaser or designated licensed broker responsible for an entry and its duties, identified to CBP by an EIN, SSN or CBP-assigned number ([Customs Brokers & Bonds](/books/logistics/customs-brokers-and-bonds#the-importer-of-record)).
- **Importer security filing (ISF):** the importer's data on an ocean shipment, due 24 hours before the cargo is laden at the foreign port (19 CFR 149.2) ([Import Entry & ACE](/books/logistics/import-entry#the-importer-security-filing)).
- **Informal entry:** the simplified entry (type 11) for shipments valued at $2,500 or less, unavailable for articles over $250 reported in Chapter 99 subchapters III and IV (19 CFR 143.21) ([Import Entry & ACE](/books/logistics/import-entry#entry-types)).
- **ISF-5:** the shorter importer security filing for cargo remaining on board or moving in-bond for export (19 CFR 149.2(b)(4) and (5)) ([Import Entry & ACE](/books/logistics/import-entry#the-importer-security-filing)).
- **ITAR (International Traffic in Arms Regulations):** State Department rules controlling defense articles on the US Munitions List, with DDTC registration and earlier export filing deadlines ([Export Compliance & Screening](/books/logistics/export-compliance#itar-briefly)).
- **ITN (Internal Transaction Number):** the number AES returns when it accepts an EEI filing, which the filer must have before the carrier's deadline ([Export Compliance & Screening](/books/logistics/export-compliance#the-export-filing-eei-in-aes)).
- **Lacey Act declaration:** the import declaration for covered plants and plant products giving genus and species, value, quantity and country of harvest, filed in ACE or LAWGS since paper PPQ 505 ended on January 1, 2026 ([Forced Labor & Partner Agencies](/books/logistics/forced-labor-and-partner-agencies#usda-aphis-and-the-lacey-act)).
- **Landed cost:** a unit's supplier price plus freight, insurance, duty, brokerage and handling, allocated to received units by quantity, value, weight or volume ([The Logistics Business](/books/logistics/logistics-business#an-ocean-import); defined in [Purchasing & Replenishment](/books/commerce/purchasing-and-replenishment#landed-cost)).
- **License exception:** an EAR authorization in part 740, such as LVS, GBS or TMP, that allows an export otherwise needing a license when its conditions are met ([Export Compliance & Screening](/books/logistics/export-compliance#license-exceptions)).
- **Liquidation (customs):** CBP's final computation of duty on an entry; an entry is deemed liquidated one year after entry unless extended or suspended (19 U.S.C. 1504) ([Import Entry & ACE](/books/logistics/import-entry#liquidation)).
- **Manufacturer identification code (MID):** the code identifying the manufacturer on each entry line; one per line ([Import Entry & ACE](/books/logistics/import-entry#the-entry-summary)).
- **May Proceed:** FDA's notice that an entry line may proceed into commerce without further FDA review, which doesn't preclude later action ([Forced Labor & Partner Agencies](/books/logistics/forced-labor-and-partner-agencies#fda-prior-notice-and-entry-review)).
- **Merchandise processing fee (MPF):** CBP's ad valorem fee on formal entries, 0.3464% within a minimum and maximum adjusted each fiscal year (19 CFR 24.23) ([The Logistics Business](/books/logistics/logistics-business#an-ocean-import)).
- **MRN (Movement Reference Number):** the 18-character reference an EU or UK customs system assigns to a declaration ([EU, UK & Canada Customs](/books/logistics/customs-outside-the-us#declarations-mrns-and-representation)).
- **National permit:** the permit a licensed broker needs to transact customs business anywhere in the customs territory, since broker districts ended in 2022 (19 CFR 111.19) ([Customs Brokers & Bonds](/books/logistics/customs-brokers-and-bonds#who-may-transact-customs-business)).
- **Periodic monthly statement (PMS):** the statement on which an importer pays a month's entries in the following month, by ACH ([Import Entry & ACE](/books/logistics/import-entry#statements-and-payment)).
- **PGA Message Set:** the part of an ACE entry that carries partner government agency data per line, defined in CBP's CATAIR with a supplemental guide per agency ([Forced Labor & Partner Agencies](/books/logistics/forced-labor-and-partner-agencies#the-pga-message-set)).
- **Post-summary correction (PSC):** the importer's correction of an entry summary before liquidation, within 300 days of entry and 15 days before scheduled liquidation ([Import Entry & ACE](/books/logistics/import-entry#post-summary-corrections)).
- **Prior disclosure:** an importer's disclosure of an entry violation before it knows of an investigation, which caps the 19 U.S.C. 1592 penalty ([Import Entry & ACE](/books/logistics/import-entry#penalties-and-prior-disclosure)).
- **Prior notice (FDA):** advance notice of imported food to FDA, confirmed 2 to 8 hours before arrival depending on mode, without which the food is refused and held at the port ([Forced Labor & Partner Agencies](/books/logistics/forced-labor-and-partner-agencies#fda-prior-notice-and-entry-review)).
- **Privileged foreign status:** a zone status that fixes foreign merchandise's classification at admission and can't be abandoned; required for goods under several 2026 tariff programs (19 CFR 146.41) ([Drawback, FTZs & Trade Agreements](/books/logistics/drawback-ftz-and-trade-agreements#zone-status)).
- **Protest:** the importer's administrative challenge to a liquidation or other CBP decision, due within 180 days after liquidation (19 U.S.C. 1514) ([Import Entry & ACE](/books/logistics/import-entry#protests)).
- **Reasonable care:** the standard the importer of record must meet in declaring value, classification and rate of duty, against which entry penalties are judged (19 U.S.C. 1484) ([Customs Brokers & Bonds](/books/logistics/customs-brokers-and-bonds#reasonable-care-and-the-broker-s-duties)).
- **Reference PGA Message Set (CPSC):** the CPSC eFiling option in which the importer stores certificate data in CPSC's Product Registry and the broker files only the Certifier ID, Product ID and Version ID ([Forced Labor & Partner Agencies](/books/logistics/forced-labor-and-partner-agencies#cpsc-efiling)).
- **Related parties (customs):** buyer and seller linked by family, office, partnership, employment, 5% ownership or common control, whose price must pass a test to serve as transaction value ([Classification, Valuation & Origin](/books/logistics/classification-valuation-origin#related-parties)).
- **Release prior to payment (RPP):** CBSA's program releasing goods before duties and taxes are paid, against financial security posted through CARM ([EU, UK & Canada Customs](/books/logistics/customs-outside-the-us#carm-and-the-commercial-accounting-declaration)).
- **Responsible supervision and control:** the degree of oversight a broker must exercise over its customs business, judged by CBP on factors such as training, reject rates and audits (19 CFR 111.28) ([Customs Brokers & Bonds](/books/logistics/customs-brokers-and-bonds#responsible-supervision-and-control)).
- **Restricted party screening:** checking every party on an export against government lists, with fuzzy matching, review of potential matches and rescreening when lists change ([Export Compliance & Screening](/books/logistics/export-compliance#restricted-party-screening)).
- **Routed export transaction:** an export in which the foreign buyer authorizes a US agent to facilitate it and file the EEI, with the USPPI supplying the data ([Export Compliance & Screening](/books/logistics/export-compliance#routed-export-transactions)).
- **Section 122 surcharge:** the 10% import surcharge of February 24 to July 24, 2026 under the Trade Act's balance-of-payments authority ([Tariff Programs](/books/logistics/tariff-programs#the-section-122-surcharge)).
- **Section 232:** the Trade Expansion Act authority for duties on imports that threaten national security, such as metals, charged on full customs value since April 6, 2026 at 50% for most aluminum, steel and copper articles ([Tariff Programs](/books/logistics/tariff-programs#section-232)).
- **Section 301:** the Trade Act authority under which USTR imposes duties against a country's unfair practices: China, the 2026 forced labor actions on 60 economies, Brazil and Nicaragua ([Tariff Programs](/books/logistics/tariff-programs#section-301)).
- **Section 338:** the Tariff Act of 1930 authority to offset another country's discrimination, first used for 50% duties on Canadian goods in 2026 ([Tariff Programs](/books/logistics/tariff-programs#section-338)).
- **Statistical reporting number:** the full ten-digit HTSUS number reported on an entry line; the first eight digits carry the rate ([Classification, Valuation & Origin](/books/logistics/classification-valuation-origin#the-tariff-schedule)).
- **Substantial transformation:** processing that changes an article's origin to the country where it occurred (19 CFR 134.1) ([Classification, Valuation & Origin](/books/logistics/classification-valuation-origin#substantial-transformation)).
- **Substitution drawback:** drawback on exports of goods interchangeable with the imports, matched at the eight-digit HTS level and capped by the lesser-of rule ([Drawback, FTZs & Trade Agreements](/books/logistics/drawback-ftz-and-trade-agreements#substitution-and-its-limits)).
- **Supply chain tracing:** the chain of ordinary business records (orders, invoices, production and transport records) linking imported goods to their raw materials, tier by tier, that CBP requires in a forced labor review ([Forced Labor & Partner Agencies](/books/logistics/forced-labor-and-partner-agencies#the-package-tracing-to-raw-materials)).
- **Transaction value:** the price actually paid or payable for goods sold for export to the US, plus statutory additions; the primary basis of customs value (19 U.S.C. 1401a) ([Classification, Valuation & Origin](/books/logistics/classification-valuation-origin#transaction-value)).
- **Triennial status report (TSR):** the report every licensed broker files every three years, with continuing education certified, on pain of revocation by operation of law ([Customs Brokers & Bonds](/books/logistics/customs-brokers-and-bonds#who-may-transact-customs-business)).
- **TSCA certification:** the importer's positive or negative certification at entry that chemical substances comply with, or aren't subject to, the Toxic Substances Control Act (19 CFR 12.121) ([Forced Labor & Partner Agencies](/books/logistics/forced-labor-and-partner-agencies#epa-tsca-certification)).
- **UFLPA (Uyghur Forced Labor Prevention Act):** the law (Public Law 117-78) presuming goods made wholly or in part in Xinjiang or by an Entity List entity are prohibited as made with forced labor, effective June 21, 2022 ([Forced Labor & Partner Agencies](/books/logistics/forced-labor-and-partner-agencies#forced-labor-the-statute-and-its-tools), [UFLPA Enforcement](/books/logistics/forced-labor-and-partner-agencies#uflpa-enforcement)).
- **UFLPA Entity List:** DHS's list of entities whose goods fall under the UFLPA presumption, in sections tied to the statute's clauses; not part of the Consolidated Screening List ([Forced Labor & Partner Agencies](/books/logistics/forced-labor-and-partner-agencies#the-entity-list)).
- **UK Internal Market Scheme (UKIMS):** the Windsor Framework authorization letting traders declare goods moving from Great Britain to Northern Ireland as not at risk of entering the EU ([EU, UK & Canada Customs](/books/logistics/customs-outside-the-us#northern-ireland-and-the-windsor-framework)).
- **Union Customs Code (UCC):** the EU's customs law, Regulation (EU) No 952/2013, in force since 1 May 2016 ([EU, UK & Canada Customs](/books/logistics/customs-outside-the-us#the-union-customs-code-and-eori)).
- **USMCA certification of origin:** the importer's, exporter's or producer's statement with minimum data elements supporting a USMCA claim, valid four years, in hand when the claim is made (19 CFR 182.12) ([Drawback, FTZs & Trade Agreements](/books/logistics/drawback-ftz-and-trade-agreements#usmca-claims-and-certifications)).
- **USMCA joint review:** the parties' review of the agreement; after the US declined a 16-year extension on July 1, 2026, held annually until extension or the July 1, 2036 end ([Drawback, FTZs & Trade Agreements](/books/logistics/drawback-ftz-and-trade-agreements#the-joint-review-and-the-tariff-programs)).
- **USPPI (US principal party in interest):** the person in the United States that receives the primary benefit of an export, usually the seller or manufacturer ([Export Compliance & Screening](/books/logistics/export-compliance#the-export-filing-eei-in-aes)).
- **Weekly entry:** a single zone consumption entry for a calendar week's estimated removals (19 CFR 146.63(c)) ([Drawback, FTZs & Trade Agreements](/books/logistics/drawback-ftz-and-trade-agreements#how-a-zone-defers-and-reduces-duty)).
- **Withhold Release Order (WRO):** a CBP order, issued when information reasonably but not conclusively indicates goods are made with forced labor, that directs ports to detain them until the importer proves admissibility or exports them ([Forced Labor & Partner Agencies](/books/logistics/forced-labor-and-partner-agencies#forced-labor-the-statute-and-its-tools)).

## Manufacturing

- **8D (Eight Disciplines):** Ford's team problem-solving method, D0 to D8, from containment through root cause and escape point to permanent corrective action and closure, the usual answer to an automotive corrective action request ([Quality Systems](/books/logistics/quality-management#corrective-action-capa-and-8d)).
- **830 (Planning Schedule with Release Capability):** the X12 transaction a buyer sends a supplier with forecast and firm requirements over a horizon, resource authorizations and cumulative quantities; it can serve as the order release itself ([Supplier Schedules, JIT & Automotive EDI](/books/logistics/supplier-schedules#the-planning-schedule)).
- **862 (Shipping Schedule):** the X12 transaction that supplements an 830 with near-term shipping detail for JIT; it supersedes the 830's shipping detail in its horizon but can't authorize resources ([Supplier Schedules, JIT & Automotive EDI](/books/logistics/supplier-schedules#the-shipping-schedule)).
- **866 (Production Sequence):** the X12 transaction that tells a supplier the order in which goods must arrive, be unloaded or be consumed, used for just-in-sequence delivery ([Supplier Schedules, JIT & Automotive EDI](/books/logistics/supplier-schedules#sequencing-and-jis)).
- **Acceptable quality level (AQL):** the sampling parameter that, with lot size and inspection level, sets how many units an inspection samples and how many defects it accepts ([Quality Systems](/books/logistics/quality-management#inspection-and-lot-release)).
- **Action message:** an MRP suggestion to create, resize, reschedule or cancel a supply order, which a planner accepts or rejects ([MRP, Scheduling & Work Orders](/books/logistics/production-planning#action-messages-and-a-stable-plan)).
- **As-built record:** the serial-level genealogy of an assembly: which components, by serial or lot, were installed in it, where, and for which period ([Lot & Serial Traceability](/books/logistics/lot-traceability#genealogy)).
- **Authorised CBAM declarant:** the status an EU importer, or its indirect customs representative, must hold to import CBAM goods above the 50-tonne threshold in the definitive regime ([Emissions & Product Passports](/books/logistics/supply-chain-sustainability#cbam)).
- **Authorization (ATH):** a buyer's commitment, on an 830, to pay for a supplier's finished goods or raw material up to a cumulative quantity through a date ([Supplier Schedules, JIT & Automotive EDI](/books/logistics/supplier-schedules#authorizations-and-cumulative-quantities)).
- **Backflush:** posting component consumption when output is reported, at BOM quantities, instead of issuing components as they are used ([MRP, Scheduling & Work Orders](/books/logistics/production-planning#consumption-and-backflush)).
- **Battery passport:** the digital record, reached by a data carrier on the battery, required for EV, LMT and industrial batteries above 2 kWh placed on the EU market from 18 February 2027 ([Emissions & Product Passports](/books/logistics/supply-chain-sustainability#the-battery-passport)).
- **Bill of materials (BOM):** the list of components and quantities that make one parent item, one level at a time; nested BOMs form the product's structure ([BOMs, PLM & Engineering Change](/books/logistics/boms-and-engineering-change#what-a-bom-line-carries)).
- **Bullwhip effect:** the growth of order variability as demand moves upstream, from forecast updating, order batching, price swings and shortage gaming ([Demand & Supply Planning](/books/logistics/supply-chain-planning#the-bullwhip-effect)).
- **CAPA (corrective and preventive action):** the life sciences term for investigating a quality problem's cause and changing what produced it, with an effectiveness check before closure ([Quality Systems](/books/logistics/quality-management#corrective-action-capa-and-8d)).
- **CBAM (Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism):** the EU charge on emissions embedded in imported cement, iron and steel, aluminium, fertilisers, electricity and hydrogen, declared yearly and settled with certificates ([Emissions & Product Passports](/books/logistics/supply-chain-sustainability#cbam)).
- **Certificate of analysis (CoA):** a supplier's document of test results for a lot, checked at receiving against the buyer's limits; a certificate of conformance states compliance without results ([Quality Systems](/books/logistics/quality-management#inspection-and-lot-release)).
- **CMRT (Conflict Minerals Reporting Template):** the Responsible Minerals Initiative's free standardized spreadsheet that passes mineral country of origin and smelters up a supply chain ([Emissions & Product Passports](/books/logistics/supply-chain-sustainability#conflict-minerals)).
- **Conflict minerals (3TG):** tin, tantalum, tungsten and gold, from cassiterite, columbite-tantalite, wolframite and gold, reported on SEC Form SD each May 31 ([Emissions & Product Passports](/books/logistics/supply-chain-sustainability#conflict-minerals)).
- **Constrained plan:** a supply plan that sizes supply to the capacity of plants and suppliers within lead times, rather than reporting overloads ([Demand & Supply Planning](/books/logistics/supply-chain-planning#supply-planning)).
- **Core Business Vocabulary (CBV):** GS1's standard values for EPCIS business steps and dispositions, such as shipping, receiving, recalled and destroyed ([Lot & Serial Traceability](/books/logistics/lot-traceability#epcis-2-0)).
- **Critical tracking event (CTE):** under FSMA 204, a harvesting, cooling, initial packing, first land-based receiving, shipping, receiving or transformation event that requires key data elements ([FSMA 204, DSCSA & UDI](/books/logistics/regulated-traceability#fsma-204-food-traceability)).
- **Cum (cumulative quantity):** a quantity counted from an agreed start date, such as the model year, used in schedules so that missed or extra shipments correct themselves ([Supplier Schedules, JIT & Automotive EDI](/books/logistics/supplier-schedules#authorizations-and-cumulative-quantities)).
- **Device identifier (DI):** the fixed part of a UDI identifying the labeler and the device version or model, one per package level, published in GUDID ([FSMA 204, DSCSA & UDI](/books/logistics/regulated-traceability#udi)).
- **Digital product passport (DPP):** under the EU's ESPR, a product's digital record with a unique identifier registered in the EU DPP Registry and a data carrier on the product ([Emissions & Product Passports](/books/logistics/supply-chain-sustainability#the-espr-passport)).
- **Disposition (nonconformance):** the decision about nonconforming material: use as is, rework, repair, regrade, return to supplier or scrap, line by line with a named decision maker ([Quality Systems](/books/logistics/quality-management#nonconformance-and-disposition)).
- **DSCSA (Drug Supply Chain Security Act):** the 2013 law requiring package-level product identifiers and interoperable electronic transaction data for prescription drugs between trading partners ([FSMA 204, DSCSA & UDI](/books/logistics/regulated-traceability#dscsa)).
- **Due diligence statement (EUDR):** the statement an operator files in the EUDR Information System, with the geolocation of the plots of harvest, before placing covered commodities on the EU market ([Emissions & Product Passports](/books/logistics/supply-chain-sustainability#eudr)).
- **EBOM (engineering BOM):** the structure as engineering designs it, by function ([BOMs, PLM & Engineering Change](/books/logistics/boms-and-engineering-change#engineering-and-manufacturing-boms)).
- **Effectiveness check:** the recalling firm's verification that consignees received a recall notice and acted, at FDA levels A (100 percent) to E (none) ([Recalls From the Manufacturer's Side](/books/logistics/manufacturing-recalls#the-recall-strategy)).
- **Effectivity:** the date, quantity, plant, serial or lot from which a BOM version or change applies ([BOMs, PLM & Engineering Change](/books/logistics/boms-and-engineering-change#versions-and-effectivity)).
- **Embedded emissions:** the greenhouse gases released producing an imported CBAM good, from the installation's actual data or a default value ([Emissions & Product Passports](/books/logistics/supply-chain-sustainability#cbam)).
- **Engineering change order (ECO):** the approved document that changes a product's design or structure, naming the affected items, effectivity, dispositions and approvals ([BOMs, PLM & Engineering Change](/books/logistics/boms-and-engineering-change#engineering-change)).
- **EPCIS:** GS1's standard for creating and sharing visibility events (what, when, where, why, how) across companies; version 2.0 (June 2022) added JSON-LD and REST bindings ([Lot & Serial Traceability](/books/logistics/lot-traceability#epcis-2-0)).
- **EPCIS error declaration:** an event that declares an earlier EPCIS event wrong, by its event ID, instead of editing it ([Lot & Serial Traceability](/books/logistics/lot-traceability#epcis-2-0)).
- **ERP (enterprise resource planning):** the system holding the general ledger, item master and costs, purchasing, receivables on terms and the close; in a manufacturer, the level-4 system of items, BOMs, plans, orders, inventory value and cost ([ERP, MES & ISA-95](/books/logistics/manufacturing-systems#what-the-erp-owns-in-a-plant); defined in [ERP & Integrations](/books/commerce/erp-and-integrations#what-an-erp-owns)).
- **EUDR:** the EU deforestation regulation, (EU) 2023/1115, covering cattle, cocoa, coffee, oil palm, rubber, soya and wood deforested after 31 December 2020 ([Emissions & Product Passports](/books/logistics/supply-chain-sustainability#eudr)).
- **Firm requirement:** a schedule quantity the buyer is committed to, as opposed to a planning (forecast) quantity ([Supplier Schedules, JIT & Automotive EDI](/books/logistics/supplier-schedules#firm-and-forecast)).
- **Food Traceability List:** FDA's list of foods subject to FSMA 204's additional traceability records, including foods that contain a listed food in its listed form ([FSMA 204, DSCSA & UDI](/books/logistics/regulated-traceability#fsma-204-food-traceability)).
- **GLEC Framework:** the Smart Freight Centre's implementation guide for calculating logistics emissions in line with ISO 14083 ([Emissions & Product Passports](/books/logistics/supply-chain-sustainability#transport-emissions-iso-14083-and-glec)).
- **GS1 Digital Link:** GS1's standard for encoding GS1 keys such as a GTIN with lot and serial in a web URI, so one barcode carries identifiers and a resolvable link ([Lot & Serial Traceability](/books/logistics/lot-traceability#identifiers-lots-serials-and-gs1-keys)).
- **GUDID (Global Unique Device Identification Database):** FDA's database of device identifier records and attributes; it holds no production identifiers ([FSMA 204, DSCSA & UDI](/books/logistics/regulated-traceability#udi)).
- **IATF 16949:** the automotive quality management standard, used with ISO 9001 and each automaker's customer-specific requirements; the 2016 edition is under revision ([Quality Systems](/books/logistics/quality-management#the-standards)).
- **Illegitimate product:** under DSCSA, product that credible evidence shows is counterfeit, diverted, stolen, intentionally adulterated or otherwise unfit, which triggers notice to FDA within 24 hours ([FSMA 204, DSCSA & UDI](/books/logistics/regulated-traceability#dscsa)).
- **ISA-95 (IEC 62264):** the standard for integrating business systems with manufacturing control systems, which names levels 0 to 4 and models the exchange between levels 3 and 4 ([ERP, MES & ISA-95](/books/logistics/manufacturing-systems#the-isa-95-levels)).
- **ISO 14083:** the 2023 international standard for quantifying and reporting greenhouse gas emissions from transport chains, by legs and hubs ([Emissions & Product Passports](/books/logistics/supply-chain-sustainability#transport-emissions-iso-14083-and-glec)).
- **JIS (just-in-sequence):** delivery of parts in the exact order the buyer's line will consume them, on a fixed cadence ([Supplier Schedules, JIT & Automotive EDI](/books/logistics/supplier-schedules#sequencing-and-jis)).
- **Just-in-time (JIT):** making only what is needed, when it is needed, in the amount needed, by pulling replenishment from the next process ([Supplier Schedules, JIT & Automotive EDI](/books/logistics/supplier-schedules#kanban)).
- **Kanban:** the signal in a pull loop that a container has been used and one is to be made or delivered ([Supplier Schedules, JIT & Automotive EDI](/books/logistics/supplier-schedules#kanban)).
- **Key data element (KDE):** under FSMA 204, a piece of information tied to a critical tracking event that must be kept or provided, such as the traceability lot code or the reference document number ([FSMA 204, DSCSA & UDI](/books/logistics/regulated-traceability#fsma-204-food-traceability)).
- **Lot code:** the manufacturer's identifier for a lot, carried in GS1 application identifier (10) up to 20 characters ([Lot & Serial Traceability](/books/logistics/lot-traceability#identifiers-lots-serials-and-gs1-keys)).
- **Lot genealogy:** the record of which input lots went into which finished lots, used to find every affected shipment when one input is defective ([Lot & Serial Traceability](/books/logistics/lot-traceability#genealogy)).
- **Lot sizing:** the rule that turns a net requirement into an order quantity: lot-for-lot, per period, per order, fixed, up to a maximum, with minimum, maximum and multiple modifiers ([MRP, Scheduling & Work Orders](/books/logistics/production-planning#lot-sizing)).
- **Low-level code:** the deepest BOM level an item appears at, which orders the MRP run so every parent is planned before its components ([MRP, Scheduling & Work Orders](/books/logistics/production-planning#the-mrp-explosion)).
- **Market withdrawal:** FDA's term for removing or correcting a distributed product with a minor violation, or none, that wouldn't warrant legal action; not reported as a recall ([Recalls From the Manufacturer's Side](/books/logistics/manufacturing-recalls#recall-correction-withdrawal)).
- **Master production schedule (MPS):** the plan of how many of each end item to make in each period, which MRP explodes ([MRP, Scheduling & Work Orders](/books/logistics/production-planning#the-master-production-schedule)).
- **Material review board (MRB):** the cross-functional group that decides dispositions the line can't, often with the customer's signed deviation ([Quality Systems](/books/logistics/quality-management#nonconformance-and-disposition)).
- **MBOM (manufacturing BOM):** the structure as the plant builds it, with process materials and packaging engineering doesn't list ([BOMs, PLM & Engineering Change](/books/logistics/boms-and-engineering-change#engineering-and-manufacturing-boms)).
- **MES (manufacturing execution system):** the level-3 system that dispatches work orders, tracks material and production on the floor, and records how each lot was made ([ERP, MES & ISA-95](/books/logistics/manufacturing-systems#what-the-mes-owns)).
- **MMOG/LE:** the Materials Management Operations Guideline/Logistics Evaluation, AIAG's and Odette's supplier self-assessment for logistics ([Supplier Schedules, JIT & Automotive EDI](/books/logistics/supplier-schedules#supplier-performance)).
- **Mock recall:** a drill that traces a chosen lot forward and backward to every consignee and accounts for the quantity produced, timed and scored ([Recalls From the Manufacturer's Side](/books/logistics/manufacturing-recalls#mock-recalls)).
- **MOM (manufacturing operations management):** level 3 as a whole: execution plus quality, materials handling and maintenance, the scope MES vendors now sell ([ERP, MES & ISA-95](/books/logistics/manufacturing-systems#how-the-platforms-do-it)).
- **MRP (material requirements planning):** netting gross requirements against stock and receipts, level by level, to produce planned make and buy orders offset by lead time ([MRP, Scheduling & Work Orders](/books/logistics/production-planning#the-mrp-explosion)).
- **Nonconformance (NCR):** a record that a quantity of material doesn't meet its requirement, where it was found and what will be done with it; separate from the corrective action that addresses the cause ([Quality Systems](/books/logistics/quality-management#nonconformance-and-disposition)).
- **OFTP2:** Odette's file transfer protocol (RFC 5024) for EDI over the internet, with session and file encryption and signed receipts ([Supplier Schedules, JIT & Automotive EDI](/books/logistics/supplier-schedules#transport-and-partners)).
- **One up, one down:** the baseline traceability duty to record the immediate source of what a company receives and the immediate recipient of what it releases ([Lot & Serial Traceability](/books/logistics/lot-traceability#one-up-one-down)).
- **OPC UA:** the platform-independent, service-oriented protocol through which machines expose typed information models, with certificate-based security ([ERP, MES & ISA-95](/books/logistics/manufacturing-systems#plant-floor-protocols)).
- **Part 573 report:** a vehicle or equipment manufacturer's defect or noncompliance report to NHTSA, due within 5 working days of the determination ([Recalls From the Manufacturer's Side](/books/logistics/manufacturing-recalls#the-part-573-report)).
- **Pegging:** the link from each supply order to the demand it covers, up and down the BOM ([MRP, Scheduling & Work Orders](/books/logistics/production-planning#pegging)).
- **Phantom:** a BOM level that is built and consumed immediately and never stocked; orders flatten it into its components ([BOMs, PLM & Engineering Change](/books/logistics/boms-and-engineering-change#phantoms-and-kits)).
- **Planned order:** MRP's suggestion to make, buy or transfer a quantity by a date, until a planner firms it into a work order, purchase order or release ([MRP, Scheduling & Work Orders](/books/logistics/production-planning#how-to-build-it)).
- **PLM (product lifecycle management):** the system where engineering authors and releases items, structures and changes ([BOMs, PLM & Engineering Change](/books/logistics/boms-and-engineering-change#from-plm-to-the-erp)).
- **PPAP (Production Part Approval Process):** the automotive approval of a supplier's part and process, submitted at one of five levels with up to 18 elements and the Part Submission Warrant ([Quality Systems](/books/logistics/quality-management#supplier-quality-and-ppap)).
- **Product identifier (DSCSA):** the human-readable and machine-readable graphic on a drug package carrying the NDC, serial, lot and expiry ([FSMA 204, DSCSA & UDI](/books/logistics/regulated-traceability#dscsa)).
- **Production identifier (PI):** the variable part of a UDI: lot, serial, expiration date, manufacturing date, or a distinct code for certain HCT/Ps ([FSMA 204, DSCSA & UDI](/books/logistics/regulated-traceability#udi)).
- **QMSR (Quality Management System Regulation):** FDA's device quality regulation at 21 CFR 820, effective February 2, 2026, incorporating ISO 13485:2016 by reference ([Quality Systems](/books/logistics/quality-management#the-standards)).
- **Recall classification:** FDA's Class I, II or III designation of a recall by the degree of health hazard ([Recalls From the Manufacturer's Side](/books/logistics/manufacturing-recalls#recall-correction-withdrawal)).
- **Recall depth:** how far down distribution a recall reaches: wholesale, retail, or consumer or user level ([Recalls From the Manufacturer's Side](/books/logistics/manufacturing-recalls#the-recall-strategy)).
- **Recall strategy:** the planned course of action for one recall, covering depth, public warning and effectiveness checks, which FDA reviews ([Recalls From the Manufacturer's Side](/books/logistics/manufacturing-recalls#the-recall-strategy)).
- **Report of correction or removal:** a device manufacturer's or importer's written report to FDA within 10 working days of starting a correction or removal to reduce a risk to health (21 CFR 806) ([Recalls From the Manufacturer's Side](/books/logistics/manufacturing-recalls#devices-corrections-and-removals)).
- **S&OP (sales and operations planning):** the recurring cycle of product, demand, supply, financial and executive reviews that agrees one aggregate plan ([Demand & Supply Planning](/books/logistics/supply-chain-planning#sales-and-operations-planning)).
- **SCAR (supplier corrective action request):** a corrective action a buyer opens and the supplier owns, usually answered as an 8D ([Quality Systems](/books/logistics/quality-management#corrective-action-capa-and-8d)).
- **Scheduling agreement:** a long-running purchase agreement for a part with no fixed quantities, against which schedules release requirements ([Supplier Schedules, JIT & Automotive EDI](/books/logistics/supplier-schedules#scheduling-agreements)).
- **Scope 3 Category 4:** upstream transportation and distribution: purchased products moved from tier 1 suppliers and transport services the company pays for, including outbound ([Emissions & Product Passports](/books/logistics/supply-chain-sustainability#scope-3-categories-4-and-9)).
- **Scope 3 Category 9:** downstream transportation and distribution of sold products that the reporting company doesn't pay for ([Emissions & Product Passports](/books/logistics/supply-chain-sustainability#scope-3-categories-4-and-9)).
- **Sparkplug:** the Eclipse specification on top of MQTT that defines industrial payloads and birth and death certificates for state awareness ([ERP, MES & ISA-95](/books/logistics/manufacturing-systems#plant-floor-protocols)).
- **Stock recovery:** removing or correcting product that never left the firm's direct control; a hold, not a recall ([Recalls From the Manufacturer's Side](/books/logistics/manufacturing-recalls#recall-correction-withdrawal)).
- **Suspect product:** under DSCSA, product with reason to believe it is counterfeit, diverted, stolen, adulterated or otherwise unfit, to be quarantined and investigated ([FSMA 204, DSCSA & UDI](/books/logistics/regulated-traceability#dscsa)).
- **Time fence:** a point in the planning horizon inside which the plan isn't changed automatically, such as a planning or frozen fence ([MRP, Scheduling & Work Orders](/books/logistics/production-planning#the-master-production-schedule)).
- **Traceability lot code (TLC):** under FSMA 204, the code that uniquely identifies a traceability lot, assigned at initial packing, first land-based receiving or transformation and not changed on shipping ([FSMA 204, DSCSA & UDI](/books/logistics/regulated-traceability#fsma-204-food-traceability)).
- **Traceability lot code source:** the place where a food's traceability lot code was assigned, or a reference such as an FDA facility registration number that leads to it ([FSMA 204, DSCSA & UDI](/books/logistics/regulated-traceability#fsma-204-food-traceability)).
- **Transaction information and statement (DSCSA):** the product, lot, quantity, dates and parties of each drug sale, and the seller's attestation, exchanged electronically and kept 6 years ([FSMA 204, DSCSA & UDI](/books/logistics/regulated-traceability#dscsa)).
- **TransformationEvent:** the EPCIS event in which input objects are fully or partially consumed and output objects are produced; the shared form of a lot genealogy edge ([Lot & Serial Traceability](/books/logistics/lot-traceability#epcis-2-0)).
- **UDI (unique device identifier):** the identifier on every medical device label and package, a device identifier plus production identifiers, issued under an FDA-accredited agency's system ([FSMA 204, DSCSA & UDI](/books/logistics/regulated-traceability#udi)).
- **Usage decision:** the recorded accept, reject, deviation or rework decision that ends an inspection and sets the lot's stock status ([Quality Systems](/books/logistics/quality-management#inspection-and-lot-release)).
- **Where-used trace:** a forward walk of lot genealogy from an input lot to every lot made from it and every shipment that carried one; a where-from trace walks backward ([Lot & Serial Traceability](/books/logistics/lot-traceability#how-to-build-it)).
- **Work order (manufacturing):** the instruction to make a quantity of an item by a date with a fixed BOM and routing version; also a production or manufacturing order ([MRP, Scheduling & Work Orders](/books/logistics/production-planning#work-orders)).

## Running the Platform

- **Autonomy level:** how far an AI agent may go on one action (suggest, draft, act within limits, act and report), set per action rather than per agent ([AI in Logistics](/books/logistics/ai-in-logistics#agents-that-book-negotiate-and-tender)).
- **Document extraction:** reading a BOL, rate confirmation, POD, invoice or customs document into fields, each with a confidence, a check against the system's records and a decision ([AI in Logistics](/books/logistics/ai-in-logistics#document-extraction)).
- **Draining loads:** letting loads tendered in the old TMS finish there (events, POD, invoices, carrier pay) while new loads start in the new system ([Replacing a TMS, WMS or ERP](/books/logistics/replacing-logistics-systems#tms-cutover)).
- **Dual run (EDI):** the new system parses inbound and generates outbound documents without transmitting, and the results are compared field by field with the old system's ([Replacing a TMS, WMS or ERP](/books/logistics/replacing-logistics-systems#edi-cutover-and-parallel-runs)).
- **Dwell time:** the time a truck spends at a facility, from arrival to departure as the definition's sources record them; detention is the part beyond the agreement's free time ([Logistics Metrics](/books/logistics/logistics-metrics#dwell-and-detention)).
- **Facts reported in, decisions published out:** the two event streams of a logistics platform: partner-reported events, kept with event and received time and possibly wrong, and the system's own conclusions, published through the outbox ([Logistics Platform Architecture](/books/logistics/logistics-architecture#the-event-pipeline)).
- **Inventory accuracy:** by location, the share of counted locations with no variance; by unit, one minus the absolute variance over the system quantity, from planned cycle counts ([Logistics Metrics](/books/logistics/logistics-metrics#inventory-accuracy-and-dock-to-stock)).
- **Metric exclusion:** one record left out of a metric's denominator (a shipper-caused late arrival, a cancelled load), with its reason, evidence and approver ([Logistics Metrics](/books/logistics/logistics-metrics#how-to-build-it)).
- **Mock cutover:** a timed rehearsal of the cutover with real data, kept as a migration run ([Replacing a TMS, WMS or ERP](/books/logistics/replacing-logistics-systems#choosing-a-cutover-strategy)).
- **Model version (AI):** a released model and prompt for one feature, with its owner and the evaluation it passed, recorded on every output it produces ([AI in Logistics](/books/logistics/ai-in-logistics#governance)).
- **Movement link:** the record connecting two tenants' own copies of one load or shipment, created when a tender is accepted, so events cross with the receiver's identifiers and never the publisher's ([Logistics Platform Architecture](/books/logistics/logistics-architecture#how-to-build-it)).
- **Network identity:** a legal entity (a carrier, a broker, an importer) as the platform verified it once by a registry identifier, which each tenant's party row may point at without sharing its own data ([Logistics Platform Architecture](/books/logistics/logistics-architecture#multi-tenancy-when-everyone-is-a-counterparty)).
- **Network platform:** a logistics platform whose tenants trade with each other on it (a shipper tenders to a broker, the broker covers with a carrier), so each tenant keeps its own copy of a movement and projected events cross between them ([Logistics Platform Architecture](/books/logistics/logistics-architecture#multi-tenancy-when-everyone-is-a-counterparty)).
- **OEE (overall equipment effectiveness):** availability times performance times quality for a work center, or good count times ideal cycle time over planned production time ([Logistics Metrics](/books/logistics/logistics-metrics#oee)).
- **On-time pickup, on-time delivery:** stops whose arrival (or departure, as the definition says) falls inside the appointment plus grace, over stops with an appointment; early arrival counts or fails by the definition ([Logistics Metrics](/books/logistics/logistics-metrics#on-time-pickup-and-delivery)).
- **Partner cutover:** one trading partner's switch to the new system, set by set and direction by direction, with certification, an agreed date and a rollback window ([Replacing a TMS, WMS or ERP](/books/logistics/replacing-logistics-systems#partner-re-onboarding)).
- **Review queue (extraction):** where a person confirms, corrects or rejects extracted fields that fell below their threshold, failed a match or must always be reviewed ([AI in Logistics](/books/logistics/ai-in-logistics#document-extraction)).
- **Tenant link:** an agreement between two tenants of a platform to exchange events for their shared movements, with the topics, an allow list of fields per topic, a validity and the authority document behind it ([Logistics Platform Architecture](/books/logistics/logistics-architecture#multi-tenancy-when-everyone-is-a-counterparty)).
- **Terminal dwell (rail):** the STB's measure, "the average time a car resides at a specified terminal location expressed in hours," reported weekly by each Class I railroad ([Logistics Metrics](/books/logistics/logistics-metrics#dwell-and-detention)).
- **Transportation OTIF:** shipments delivered inside their delivery appointment (plus the definition's grace) with no shortage, overage, damage or refusal at delivery, over shipments delivered; distinct from a retailer's OTIF by PO ([Logistics Metrics](/books/logistics/logistics-metrics#otif)).
- **Wall-to-wall count:** counting every location and license plate in a building, here so that a new WMS opens on counted balances ([Replacing a TMS, WMS or ERP](/books/logistics/replacing-logistics-systems#wms-cutover)).
