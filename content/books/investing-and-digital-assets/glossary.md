Investing borrows its words from securities law, accounting and a century of back-office operations, and crypto adds a vocabulary of its own from cryptography and chain design, so the same word shifts with context: a settlement is a clearing house obligation on one screen and a chain transaction reaching finality on another, a position is a ledger claim to a broker and an address balance to a block explorer, a reserve is a broker's customer protection deposit and a stablecoin issuer's backing assets, and a confirmation is a Rule 10b-10 notice to a customer and a block on top of a deposit to an exchange. Entries are grouped by the part of the volume that explains them, alphabetized within each group, and each is one line and a link to the chapter that owns the idea; a word with two meanings appears once per meaning, qualified. Terms the [Payments](/books/payments/glossary) and [Banking & Lending](/books/banking-and-lending/glossary) glossaries already define keep their definitions. The volume's own names for the core records (legal entity, party, party identity, account, account party, instrument, chain, asset network, venue, order, order event, execution, journal entry, journal line, position, tax lot, lot closing, settlement, transfer, wallet, address, on-chain transaction, asset event, fee schedule, fee, disclosure, reconciliation, recon break, case) are the ones in [The Core Data Model](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/investing-business#the-core-data-model).


## The Business

- **Account:** one customer account at one legal entity, with its type (individual, joint, IRA, custodial, trust, entity, institutional), capabilities and status ([The Investing & Digital Assets Business](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/investing-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **Account party:** a party's role on an account (owner, joint owner, custodian, trustee, beneficiary, trusted contact, authorized trader), effective-dated ([The Investing & Digital Assets Business](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/investing-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **Activity license map:** the table of which registrations an activity needs in each jurisdiction and from when, checked before an account, product or order is enabled ([Regulators & Licenses](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/regulators-and-licenses#how-to-build-it)).
- **Asset event:** a corporate action or chain event that changes what a holder owns (dividend, split, merger, spinoff, tender offer, fork, airdrop, token migration, redenomination, delisting), with any holder elections ([The Investing & Digital Assets Business](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/investing-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **Asset network:** one instrument deployed on one chain, native or at a contract address, with its token standard, on-chain decimals and whether deposits and withdrawals are enabled ([The Investing & Digital Assets Business](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/investing-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **Assets on platform:** a crypto firm's reported value of customer and own assets it holds or administers; Coinbase reported $377 billion, 12.5% of crypto market capitalization, in the third quarter of 2026 ([The Investing & Crypto Landscape](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/investing-landscape#crypto-exchanges)).
- **Broker-dealer:** a firm registered with the SEC under Exchange Act §15 to buy and sell securities for customers or itself, which must also join FINRA and is a member of SIPC by registering ([Regulators & Licenses](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/regulators-and-licenses#the-regulators)).
- **Capabilities:** what an account is approved for beyond cash trading: margin, options (with the firm's level), crypto, staking, futures and event contracts ([The Investing & Digital Assets Business](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/investing-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **Case:** a complaint, surveillance alert, AML alert, dispute or regulatory inquiry, with the time notice arrived, the rule's deadline and the outcome ([The Investing & Digital Assets Business](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/investing-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **Chain:** a blockchain network as the ledger sees it: its chain ID, native asset and the finality rule deposits wait for ([The Investing & Digital Assets Business](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/investing-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **Corporate transaction:** a dated acquisition, listing, charter approval, license grant, bankruptcy or name change affecting a counterparty, kept with its source ([The Investing & Crypto Landscape](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/investing-landscape#how-to-build-it)).
- **Custodial staking commission:** the share of protocol staking rewards a platform keeps before passing the rest to customers ([How Brokers & Exchanges Make Money](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/revenue-models#staking-and-custody-fees)).
- **Customer credits:** cash customers leave at a broker, a liability the broker may use only within the customer protection rule; $186.2 billion at Interactive Brokers at September 30, 2026 ([How Brokers & Exchanges Make Money](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/revenue-models#net-interest-on-cash-and-margin)).
- **Disclosure:** a record of what was shown or sent to a customer: the template version, the rendered document, the channel and the time ([The Investing & Digital Assets Business](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/investing-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **Entity registration:** one license, registration or membership held by one of the platform's legal entities, with its regulator, jurisdiction, identifier, status, conditions and dates ([Regulators & Licenses](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/regulators-and-licenses#how-to-build-it)).
- **Entity relationship:** an ownership, introducing, clearing, custody, service or revenue-share link between two of the platform's legal entities, effective-dated with its agreement ([Regulators & Licenses](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/regulators-and-licenses#how-to-build-it)).
- **Execution:** one fill of an order at a venue, with price, quantity, liquidity flag, capacity and time ([The Investing & Digital Assets Business](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/investing-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **Fee schedule:** a versioned set of fee rules (maker and taker tiers, withdrawal fees, advisory rates); every fee names the version that produced it ([The Investing & Digital Assets Business](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/investing-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **FINRA:** the self-regulatory organization every broker-dealer dealing with the public must join, which writes conduct rules and examines and fines its members ([Regulators & Licenses](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/regulators-and-licenses#the-regulators)).
- **Gross versus net revenue:** whether a firm reports the full value of trades it executes as principal (BitGo's $15.6 billion of 2025 digital asset sales) or only its fees; the two don't compare ([How Brokers & Exchanges Make Money](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/revenue-models#how-the-platforms-do-it)).
- **Instrument:** anything that can be held or traded, cash included: equity, ETF, fund, option, future, bond, crypto asset, stablecoin, tokenized security or event contract, with its identifiers and the precision of its smallest unit ([The Investing & Digital Assets Business](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/investing-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **Interest program:** a sweep, margin rate, cash rate, stock loan or reserve arrangement, versioned with the rate the firm earns, the rate it pays and the balances they apply to ([How Brokers & Exchanges Make Money](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/revenue-models#how-to-build-it)).
- **Journal entry:** one balanced movement in the firm's books, in any instruments, with a legal entity, an effective date, a source and an idempotency key ([The Investing & Digital Assets Business](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/investing-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **Journal line:** one signed amount of one instrument in one ledger account, with the customer account or wallet it concerns and the date it settles; each instrument's lines in an entry sum to zero ([The Investing & Digital Assets Business](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/investing-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **Ledger account:** the role a journal line plays: customer, firm, omnibus, settlement, fees or suspense ([The Investing & Digital Assets Business](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/investing-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **Legal entity:** one of the platform's own regulated companies (broker-dealer, RIA, FCM, trust company, money transmitter, stablecoin issuer, EU CASP); every account, wallet and journal entry names one ([The Investing & Digital Assets Business](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/investing-business#who-s-who)).
- **Lot closing:** a disposal matched to a tax lot, with proceeds, realized gain and the relief method used ([The Investing & Digital Assets Business](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/investing-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **Maker and taker fees:** exchange fees that charge an order adding liquidity to the book (maker) less than one removing it (taker), tiered by 30-day volume ([The Investing & Digital Assets Business](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/investing-business#trading-revenue-and-payment-for-order-flow)).
- **Market participant:** an outside firm the platform deals with or measures (broker, wholesaler, exchange, custodian, issuer, API provider), keyed by LEI, CRD and CIK ([The Investing & Crypto Landscape](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/investing-landscape#how-to-build-it)).
- **Order:** an instruction to buy or sell an instrument, with type, time in force, quantity or notional, and status, keyed by the client's idempotency key ([The Investing & Digital Assets Business](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/investing-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **Order flow agreement:** the versioned terms on which a venue pays a broker for routed orders (a share of the spread, per share, or per contract), by asset class and session ([How Brokers & Exchanges Make Money](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/revenue-models#how-to-build-it)).
- **Participant metric:** a figure a firm reports about itself (funded customers, client assets, circulating supply) stored with the source's definition, date and document ([The Investing & Crypto Landscape](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/investing-landscape#how-to-build-it)).
- **Party:** a person or entity, independent of any account ([The Investing & Digital Assets Business](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/investing-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **Party identity:** a verified identifier for a party, with the method, the vendor's result and when it was verified ([The Investing & Digital Assets Business](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/investing-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **Payment for order flow (PFOF):** what a wholesaler pays a broker for routing it retail orders; at Robinhood Securities in the second quarter of 2026, 12.35% of the NBBO spread on equities in regular hours ([The Investing & Digital Assets Business](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/investing-business#trading-revenue-and-payment-for-order-flow)).
- **Position:** an account's settled and unsettled quantity of an instrument, rebuilt from the ledger and never written directly ([The Investing & Digital Assets Business](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/investing-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **Reconciliation:** a comparison of the ledger with an outside record (DTC, a clearing firm, a custodian, a bank, a chain at a block height, an issuer's supply), with each difference opened as a break ([The Investing & Digital Assets Business](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/investing-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **Revenue share agreement:** a split of revenue between two of the platform's entities or with a partner, such as a clearing broker passing 80% of order flow revenue to its introducing affiliate or an issuer's reserve-income waterfall ([How Brokers & Exchanges Make Money](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/revenue-models#how-to-build-it)).
- **SIPC assessment:** what SIPC charges its members, 0.15% of net operating revenues, continuing for 2027 ([Regulators & Licenses](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/regulators-and-licenses#the-regulators)).
- **Smallest unit:** the indivisible unit an instrument's quantities are stored in as integers (cents, satoshis, wei, a fraction of a share), with the exponent kept on the instrument ([The Investing & Digital Assets Business](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/investing-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **Tax lot:** one acquisition of an instrument in an account, with its cost basis, wash-sale adjustment, holding-period start and covered status ([The Investing & Digital Assets Business](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/investing-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **Transaction rebates:** what crypto market makers pay a broker for routed crypto orders, the crypto counterpart of payment for order flow ([How Brokers & Exchanges Make Money](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/revenue-models#crypto-spreads-fees-and-rebates)).
- **Transfer:** money or assets moving into or out of an account by ACH, wire, card, ACATS, on-chain transaction, internal move or journal, with its status, hold and journal entry ([The Investing & Digital Assets Business](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/investing-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **Venue:** where an order is filled or a trade settles: an exchange, ATS, wholesaler, crypto exchange, DEX, OTC desk or clearing house ([The Investing & Digital Assets Business](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/investing-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **Wallet:** a custody wallet the firm controls or uses, with its chain, purpose (hot, warm, cold, deposit, omnibus, staking, gas), key scheme and owning legal entity ([The Investing & Digital Assets Business](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/investing-business#the-core-data-model)).

## Accounts and Positions

- **ACATS:** DTCC's Automated Customer Account Transfer Service, which moves a customer's securities and cash in kind between NSCC members under FINRA Rule 11870 ([Funding & Account Transfers](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/funding-and-transfers#acats-transfers)).
- **Adequate identification:** naming the lots a sale relieves to the broker by the time of the sale, confirmed by the broker in writing; a standing order counts ([Cost Basis, Tax Lots & Wash Sales](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/cost-basis-and-tax-lots#lot-relief-methods)).
- **Airdrop:** new tokens distributed to holders' addresses; for an exchange customer, ordinary income at fair market value when the exchange credits them (Rev. Rul. 2019-24) ([Corporate Actions & Asset Events](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/corporate-actions#forks-and-airdrops)).
- **Average basis:** a cost basis method that averages the basis of identical shares in an account, allowed only for mutual fund and dividend reinvestment plan shares ([Cost Basis, Tax Lots & Wash Sales](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/cost-basis-and-tax-lots#lot-relief-methods)).
- **Bank sweep program:** a broker's service that moves customers' free credit balances into deposits at program banks, insured by pass-through coverage and no longer the broker's liability ([Cash, Sweeps & Yield](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/cash-and-sweeps#bank-sweep-programs)).
- **Buying power:** the largest purchase an account may make now, computed from settled and unsettled cash, margin equity, holds and instant credit; a formula over the ledger, not a balance in it ([Ledgers, Positions & Buying Power](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/ledgers-and-positions#buying-power)).
- **Carrying firm:** in an ACATS transfer, the broker that holds the account and must validate or take exception within one business day ([Funding & Account Transfers](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/funding-and-transfers#acats-transfers)).
- **Cash account:** a brokerage account that pays for purchases in full within the Regulation T payment period, without borrowing ([Ledgers, Positions & Buying Power](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/ledgers-and-positions#buying-power)).
- **Cash in lieu:** cash paid instead of a fractional share in a reverse split, merger or spinoff; treated as a sale of the fraction ([Corporate Actions & Asset Events](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/corporate-actions#splits-and-reverse-splits)).
- **Cash liquidation violation:** in a cash account, covering a purchase by selling other securities after the purchase date, so the covering sale settles after the purchase does ([Ledgers, Positions & Buying Power](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/ledgers-and-positions#buying-power)).
- **Composite (consolidated) 1099:** one year-end document carrying a brokerage account's 1099-B, 1099-DIV, 1099-INT and 1099-MISC, furnished by February 15 ([Confirms, Statements & Tax Forms](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/statements-and-tax-forms#the-1099-composite)).
- **Confirmation (Rule 10b-10):** the written notice of a securities trade, sent at or before completion, with price, quantity, capacity and order flow disclosures ([Confirms, Statements & Tax Forms](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/statements-and-tax-forms#rule-10b-10-confirmations)).
- **Core position:** the cash vehicle a brokerage account's uninvested cash sits in by default, chosen at opening: a money market fund, a bank sweep or a free credit balance ([Cash, Sweeps & Yield](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/cash-and-sweeps#how-the-platforms-do-it)).
- **Covered security:** a security the broker must report basis for: acquired after the applicable date (2011 for most stock, 2026 for digital assets in custody) in the account, or transferred in with a statement ([Cost Basis, Tax Lots & Wash Sales](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/cost-basis-and-tax-lots#covered-and-noncovered-securities)).
- **Custodial account (UTMA/UGMA):** an account a minor owns from the first deposit, managed by an adult custodian, who is the CIP customer, until the age the state's act sets ([Opening Accounts](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/opening-accounts#minors-and-custodial-accounts)).
- **De minimis error safe harbor:** the rule that a reported amount within $100 (or $25 of withholding) of correct need not be corrected unless the recipient elects corrections ([Confirms, Statements & Tax Forms](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/statements-and-tax-forms#corrections-and-their-timing)).
- **Deficit (instant deposit):** a negative cash balance left when a deposit the customer already spent through instant credit is returned; due immediately ([Funding & Account Transfers](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/funding-and-transfers#instant-deposits-are-credit)).
- **Deposit beta:** the share of a change in market rates a cash program passes to customers; a low beta earns more as rates rise and loses all of a cut ([Cash, Sweeps & Yield](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/cash-and-sweeps#the-sweep-spread)).
- **Dollar-based (notional) order:** an order stating the amount to spend, converted to a quantity at the fill price and the instrument's precision ([Fractional Shares & Recurring Investing](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/fractional-and-recurring#dollar-based-orders)).
- **DRIP (dividend reinvestment):** a standing instruction to buy more of a security with its dividend, carried out as an order after the pay date, each purchase its own lot ([Fractional Shares & Recurring Investing](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/fractional-and-recurring#drip-is-an-order-not-an-event)).
- **Ex-date:** the first day a buyer no longer receives a distribution; the record date for distributions under 25% of value, the day after the payable date for 25% or more ([Corporate Actions & Asset Events](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/corporate-actions#record-ex-and-pay-dates)).
- **Excess bank:** the bank in a sweep program that takes every dollar above the per-bank placements, without limit and uninsured above $250,000 ([Cash, Sweeps & Yield](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/cash-and-sweeps#bank-sweep-programs)).
- **FIFO (first in, first out):** the default lot relief method: without identification, the earliest acquired shares are sold first ([Cost Basis, Tax Lots & Wash Sales](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/cost-basis-and-tax-lots#lot-relief-methods)).
- **Form 1042-S:** the year-end form for US-source income paid to a foreign person and the tax withheld, furnished by March 15 ([Confirms, Statements & Tax Forms](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/statements-and-tax-forms#the-1042-s-for-foreign-accounts)).
- **Form 1099-B:** the broker's report of proceeds from securities sales, with adjusted basis, term and disallowed wash sale loss for covered lots ([Confirms, Statements & Tax Forms](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/statements-and-tax-forms#the-1099-composite)).
- **Form 1099-DA:** the broker's report of digital asset sales: gross proceeds from 2025, basis for covered units from 2026 ([Confirms, Statements & Tax Forms](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/statements-and-tax-forms#form-1099-da-and-its-timeline)).
- **Form 8937:** an issuer's report of an organizational action that affects holders' basis, due by the 45th day after the action ([Cost Basis, Tax Lots & Wash Sales](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/cost-basis-and-tax-lots#corporate-actions-and-basis)).
- **Fractional inventory:** the firm's own holding of fractional shares left over from batches, a firm position entitled to dividends and corporate actions like any holder ([Fractional Shares & Recurring Investing](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/fractional-and-recurring#how-brokers-create-fractions)).
- **Free credit balance:** cash a broker owes a customer, payable on demand, carried on the broker's books, usable in its business within Rule 15c3-3 and protected by SIPC up to $250,000 ([Cash, Sweeps & Yield](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/cash-and-sweeps#free-credit-balances)).
- **Free riding:** buying securities and paying for them with the proceeds of selling the same securities; under Regulation T it freezes a cash account's credit for 90 days ([Ledgers, Positions & Buying Power](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/ledgers-and-positions#buying-power)).
- **Global allocation:** under Rev. Proc. 2024-28, a rule written before 2025 for assigning unused digital asset basis to wallets and accounts ([Cost Basis, Tax Lots & Wash Sales](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/cost-basis-and-tax-lots#crypto-basis-per-wallet-and-per-account)).
- **Good faith violation:** in a cash account, selling a security bought with unsettled proceeds before those proceeds settle ([Ledgers, Positions & Buying Power](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/ledgers-and-positions#buying-power)).
- **Hard fork:** a permanent split of a chain into two; no income without new units, income when new units are received ([Corporate Actions & Asset Events](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/corporate-actions#forks-and-airdrops)).
- **Holding period:** the time an asset is held, counted from the day after acquisition; more than one year is long-term ([Cost Basis, Tax Lots & Wash Sales](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/cost-basis-and-tax-lots#basis-and-holding-period)).
- **Instant deposit:** a platform's advance of part of a pending bank deposit so the customer can trade at once; credit the firm extends against a reversible payment ([Funding & Account Transfers](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/funding-and-transfers#instant-deposits-are-credit)).
- **Investment profile:** the customer's income, net worth, liquid net worth, objectives, experience and affiliations, collected under FINRA 2090 and 4512 and Rule 17a-3(a)(17), versioned and confirmed every 36 months ([Opening Accounts](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/opening-accounts#the-investment-profile)).
- **Location (ledger):** where an asset sits (a wallet, a bank account, DTC, a custodian), carried on the ledger's omnibus lines opposite the owners' lines so each instrument sums to zero ([Ledgers, Positions & Buying Power](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/ledgers-and-positions#double-entry-in-many-units)).
- **Lot engine:** the processor that opens, relieves, adjusts and restates tax lots from executions, asset events, transfers and elections ([Cost Basis, Tax Lots & Wash Sales](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/cost-basis-and-tax-lots#the-lot-engine)).
- **Margin disclosure statement:** the FINRA Rule 2264 statement given to non-institutional customers at or before opening a margin account and annually after ([Opening Accounts](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/opening-accounts#capabilities-and-approvals)).
- **Nontransferable asset:** a position the receiving firm won't accept in an ACATS transfer, such as a proprietary fund, which the customer must instruct the carrying firm to liquidate, retain or transfer otherwise ([Funding & Account Transfers](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/funding-and-transfers#acats-transfers)).
- **Omnibus wallet:** an on-chain wallet holding many customers' crypto in the platform's name, whose ownership only the internal ledger records ([Ledgers, Positions & Buying Power](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/ledgers-and-positions#omnibus-and-segregated)).
- **Options approval level:** a firm's own tier of permitted option strategies, granted in writing by a principal under FINRA Rule 2360(b)(16), which defines no tiers itself ([Opening Accounts](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/opening-accounts#capabilities-and-approvals)).
- **Pass-through voting:** letting the holders of a fund or a fractional position instruct how the shares held for them are voted ([Corporate Actions & Asset Events](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/corporate-actions#proxy-and-pass-through-voting)).
- **Payment period:** under Regulation T, the standard settlement cycle plus two business days, the time a cash account customer has to pay for a purchase ([Ledgers, Positions & Buying Power](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/ledgers-and-positions#buying-power)).
- **Periodic plan statement:** the Rule 10b-10(b) quarterly statement that replaces per-trade confirmations for plan transactions, with prior written notice ([Confirms, Statements & Tax Forms](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/statements-and-tax-forms#rule-10b-10-confirmations)).
- **Receiving firm:** in an ACATS transfer, the broker the customer is moving to, which submits the transfer instruction ([Funding & Account Transfers](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/funding-and-transfers#acats-transfers)).
- **Record date:** the date on which holders on the issuer's books are entitled to a distribution or a vote ([Corporate Actions & Asset Events](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/corporate-actions#record-ex-and-pay-dates)).
- **Residual (ACATS):** a dividend, interest payment or other credit that reaches the carrying firm after a full transfer, forwarded within ten business days for at least six months ([Funding & Account Transfers](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/funding-and-transfers#acats-transfers)).
- **Residual (fractional):** the part of a whole share bought for a batch that customers didn't take, held in the firm's fractional inventory ([Fractional Shares & Recurring Investing](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/fractional-and-recurring#rounding-and-residuals)).
- **Reverse split:** a consolidation of shares (1 for 10, say) that keeps total basis, with fractions paid as cash in lieu ([Corporate Actions & Asset Events](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/corporate-actions#splits-and-reverse-splits)).
- **Riskless principal:** buying in the market to fill a customer order at the same price, acting as principal; one way brokers fill fractional orders ([Fractional Shares & Recurring Investing](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/fractional-and-recurring#how-brokers-create-fractions)).
- **Settlement-date position:** the quantity an account holds counting only lines whose settlement date has arrived; what is at the depository and what the stock record reflects ([Ledgers, Positions & Buying Power](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/ledgers-and-positions#trade-date-and-settlement-date)).
- **Specific identification:** choosing which lots a sale relieves, by instruction at the time of sale or by a standing order such as highest cost ([Cost Basis, Tax Lots & Wash Sales](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/cost-basis-and-tax-lots#lot-relief-methods)).
- **Specific unit allocation:** under Rev. Proc. 2024-28, assigning identified units of unused digital asset basis to particular wallets or accounts ([Cost Basis, Tax Lots & Wash Sales](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/cost-basis-and-tax-lots#crypto-basis-per-wallet-and-per-account)).
- **Specified adult:** under FINRA Rule 2165, a customer 65 or older, or an adult the firm reasonably believes can't protect their own interests, whose account a firm may hold for suspected exploitation ([Opening Accounts](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/opening-accounts#trusted-contacts-and-senior-holds)).
- **Spinoff:** a distribution of a subsidiary's shares to the parent's holders; in a tax-free spinoff, basis is split by relative fair market value and holding periods carry over ([Corporate Actions & Asset Events](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/corporate-actions#spinoffs)).
- **Stock record:** the broker-dealer's securities record under Rule 17a-3(a)(5), listing for each security who owns it and where it is, as of settlement dates, long equal to short ([Ledgers, Positions & Buying Power](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/ledgers-and-positions#the-stock-record)).
- **Sweep spread:** the difference between what program banks pay for swept cash and what the customer receives, kept by the broker or its affiliated bank ([Cash, Sweeps & Yield](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/cash-and-sweeps#the-sweep-spread)).
- **Temporary hold (FINRA 2165):** a hold on disbursements or transactions in a specified adult's account for suspected exploitation, up to 15 business days, extendable by 10 and then 30 ([Opening Accounts](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/opening-accounts#trusted-contacts-and-senior-holds)).
- **Tender offer:** an offer to buy holders' shares at a stated price, open at least 20 business days under Rule 14e-1 ([Corporate Actions & Asset Events](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/corporate-actions#tender-offers)).
- **TIF (transfer instruction):** the customer-signed instruction a receiving firm submits to start an ACATS transfer ([Funding & Account Transfers](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/funding-and-transfers#acats-transfers)).
- **Token migration:** a move of a token to a new contract or native asset at a fixed ratio, applied as a conversion of every holder's balance ([Corporate Actions & Asset Events](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/corporate-actions#token-migrations-and-redenominations)).
- **Trade-date position:** the quantity an account holds counting every executed trade, settled or not; the basis for market value, margin and tax lots ([Ledgers, Positions & Buying Power](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/ledgers-and-positions#trade-date-and-settlement-date)).
- **Transfer statement (6045A):** the statement of lots, basis and acquisition dates a delivering broker sends within 15 days after a transfer settles ([Cost Basis, Tax Lots & Wash Sales](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/cost-basis-and-tax-lots#transferred-basis)).
- **Value hold:** a withdrawal restriction equal to a reversible deposit's amount, applied to cash or crypto alike so ACH-funded assets can't leave before the deposit is safe ([Funding & Account Transfers](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/funding-and-transfers#crypto-in-and-out)).
- **Wash sale:** a loss sale with substantially identical stock acquired within 30 days before or after; the loss is disallowed and added to the replacement's basis, with the holding period carried over ([Cost Basis, Tax Lots & Wash Sales](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/cost-basis-and-tax-lots#wash-sales)).
- **Withdrawable cash:** settled cash less holds, instant credit outstanding, reserved orders and margin requirements; what may leave the firm today ([Ledgers, Positions & Buying Power](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/ledgers-and-positions#withdrawable-cash)).

## Trading and Markets

- **0DTE (zero days to expiration):** an option bought or sold on its expiration day; a majority of US index option volume by 2026 ([Options](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/options-trading#expiration-day-and-0dte)).
- **23/5 trading:** exchange trading of NMS stocks 23 hours a day, five days a week, with an overnight session from 8:00 pm to 4:00 am ET and a one-hour pause from 8:00 to 9:00 pm Monday to Thursday, planned from December 6, 2026 ([Extended Hours & 24-Hour Trading](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/extended-and-overnight-trading#23-5-on-exchanges)).
- **30.7 secured amount:** the separate pool an FCM holds for customers trading on foreign futures exchanges ([Futures & FCMs](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/futures-and-fcms#customer-segregation)).
- **Access fee cap:** the most an exchange may charge to execute against its protected quotations under Rule 610(c): $0.003 a share today, $0.001 a share (0.1% of the quote below $1.00) once the 2024 amendments take effect in November 2027 ([US Equity Market Structure](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/equity-market-structure#access-fees-and-tick-sizes)).
- **After-hours session:** exchange and ATS trading from 4:00 pm to 8:00 pm ET, limit orders only on most venues ([Extended Hours & 24-Hour Trading](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/extended-and-overnight-trading#the-sessions-today)).
- **Alternative trading system (ATS):** a trading system operated by a broker-dealer under Regulation ATS rather than registered as an exchange; an NMS Stock ATS needs an effective Form ATS-N ([US Equity Market Structure](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/equity-market-structure#the-venues)).
- **American-style option:** an option that can be exercised on any business day up to expiration, so a short position can be assigned early ([Options](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/options-trading#contracts-series-and-symbols)).
- **Assignment:** OCC's notice that an exercise has been matched to a short position, allocated by the clearing firm to a customer first in first out or at random ([Options](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/options-trading#exercise-and-assignment)).
- **Best execution:** a broker's duty under FINRA Rule 5310 to use reasonable diligence to find the best market for a customer's order so the price is as favorable as possible under prevailing conditions ([Order Routing, PFOF & Best Execution](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/routing-and-best-execution#the-duty)).
- **Best odd-lot order:** the highest-priced odd-lot bid above the national best bid, or lowest-priced odd-lot offer below the national best offer, added to odd-lot information by the 2024 amendments ([US Equity Market Structure](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/equity-market-structure#the-nbbo-worked)).
- **Book checksum:** a hash of a venue's top book levels sent with its feed (Kraken's CRC32 of the top 10 levels) so a client can prove its rebuilt book matches the venue's ([Market Data](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/market-data#crypto-websocket-books)).
- **Borrow fee:** the annualized rate a short seller pays to borrow shares, charged daily on the short's value (Alpaca and Robinhood divide by 360) ([Margin & Short Selling](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/margin-and-short-selling#short-selling-and-regulation-sho)).
- **Cancel/replace race:** a cancel or replace request that crosses a fill in flight, so the venue applies it to a partly or fully filled order; FIX replaces state the total quantity to avoid doubling a position ([Orders & Order Types](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/orders-and-order-types#cancel-replace-races)).
- **Candle:** the open, high, low, close and volume of trades in an interval, identified by its open time and recomputable when late, busted or corrected trades change it ([Market Data](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/market-data#candles-and-aggregation)).
- **ClOrdID chain:** the FIX client order IDs linking an order to its replacements; each replace carries a new ClOrdID and OrigClOrdID naming the previous non-rejected order ([Orders & Order Types](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/orders-and-order-types#fix-basics)).
- **Closing auction:** the single-price auction that sets a listed stock's official close, with entry and cancel cutoffs (3:50 p.m. ET at NYSE) and imbalance messages before it ([US Equity Market Structure](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/equity-market-structure#auctions)).
- **Collar (market order):** a broker's conversion of a market order into a limit order some distance from the quote so it can't fill through a thin book; a material term the customer must be told ([Orders & Order Types](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/orders-and-order-types#order-types)).
- **Conflation:** sending each client only the latest state per symbol at the client's rate rather than every update, correct for quotes and charts, wrong for consumers that need every event ([Market Data](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/market-data#fan-out-to-millions-of-clients)).
- **Contrary Exercise Advice:** the form a member submits by 7:30 p.m. ET on expiration day to exercise or not exercise a customer's expiring option against the exercise-by-exception default ([Options](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/options-trading#exercise-and-assignment)).
- **Covered call:** a short call against 100 shares held per contract; income now, upside capped at the strike ([Options](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/options-trading#payoffs-and-requirements)).
- **CT Plan:** the single consolidated equity data plan, effective November 20, 2024, that on implementation replaces the CTA, CQ and UTP plans ([US Equity Market Structure](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/equity-market-structure#sips-and-direct-feeds)).
- **Day margin:** an FCM's lower margin for a futures position opened and closed within a session, ending at a set time before the close ([Futures & FCMs](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/futures-and-fcms#margin-and-daily-settlement)).
- **Derivatives clearing organization (DCO):** a CFTC-registered clearing house that becomes the counterparty to every cleared futures trade, sets margin and settles with clearing members at least once each business day ([Futures & FCMs](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/futures-and-fcms#the-parties)).
- **Designated contract market (DCM):** a CFTC-registered futures exchange, which lists contracts by self-certification or approval ([Futures & FCMs](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/futures-and-fcms#the-parties)).
- **Direct feed:** an exchange's own market data product, with depth or order-by-order detail, that reaches a subscriber without the hop through the SIP ([US Equity Market Structure](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/equity-market-structure#sips-and-direct-feeds)).
- **Early assignment:** assignment of an American-style short option before expiration, most often on a call the day before an ex-dividend date when the dividend exceeds its time value ([Options](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/options-trading#early-assignment-and-dividends)).
- **Effective spread:** twice the distance between a fill price and the NBBO midpoint at order receipt; divided by the quoted spread it gives E/Q, the share of the spread the customer paid ([Order Routing, PFOF & Best Execution](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/routing-and-best-execution#one-order-worked)).
- **Event contract:** a derivative that pays a fixed amount, usually $1, if a stated event happens and nothing if it doesn't, priced between one cent and 99 cents ([Event Contracts & Prediction Markets](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/prediction-markets#pricing-price-as-probability)).
- **Exercise by exception (Ex-by-Ex):** OCC Rule 805's automatic exercise of expiring equity options in the money by a specified amount unless instructed otherwise; brokers publish $0.01 ([Options](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/options-trading#exercise-and-assignment)).
- **Futures commission merchant (FCM):** a firm registered with the CFTC and a member of NFA that takes customers' futures orders and money and must segregate customer funds ([Futures & FCMs](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/futures-and-fcms#the-parties)).
- **Hard to borrow:** a security whose shares are scarce in the lending market, needing an approved locate before a short sale and carrying a borrow fee ([Margin & Short Selling](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/margin-and-short-selling#hard-to-borrow-and-securities-lending)).
- **House requirement:** a firm's own margin requirement above the Reg T and FINRA minimums, set by security and changeable without notice ([Margin & Short Selling](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/margin-and-short-selling#maintenance-and-house-requirements)).
- **IML (intraday margin level):** under FINRA Rule 4210, the cash a margin account could withdraw while still meeting maintenance margin, negative when it is below ([Margin & Short Selling](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/margin-and-short-selling#intraday-margin-after-the-pattern-day-trader-rule)).
- **Initial margin (futures):** the collateral a new futures position requires, set by the clearing house and often raised by the FCM ([Futures & FCMs](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/futures-and-fcms#margin-and-daily-settlement)).
- **Initial margin (securities):** the equity required to open a position: 50% of market value for a margin equity security and 150% for a short sale under Regulation T ([Margin & Short Selling](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/margin-and-short-selling#reg-t-initial-margin)).
- **Intermarket sweep order (ISO):** a limit order marked as one of a set sent at once to take the full displayed size of every better protected quotation, letting the receiving venue execute without checking for trade-throughs ([US Equity Market Structure](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/equity-market-structure#order-protection)).
- **Intraday margin deficit:** for a day with an IML-reducing transaction, at least the largest negative IML that day; satisfied by later net deposits or IML increases, outstanding for 15 business days ([Margin & Short Selling](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/margin-and-short-selling#intraday-margin-after-the-pattern-day-trader-rule)).
- **Introducing broker (futures):** a futures firm that takes orders but holds no customer money and clears through an FCM, with $45,000 of capital or an FCM's guarantee ([Futures & FCMs](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/futures-and-fcms#the-parties)).
- **Kill switch:** a pre-built action that stops order flow and cancels resting orders at a scope (session, account, strategy, venue connection or firm) ([Pre-Trade Risk, Halts & Volatility](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/pre-trade-risk-and-halts#kill-switches)).
- **L1, L2, L3 data:** top of book (best bid and offer, last trade), depth aggregated by price level, and every resting order with every event on it ([Market Data](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/market-data#l1-l2-and-l3)).
- **Limit state:** under LULD, a stock whose best offer is at the lower band or best bid at the upper; a pause follows if it doesn't exit within 15 seconds ([Pre-Trade Risk, Halts & Volatility](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/pre-trade-risk-and-halts#limit-up-limit-down)).
- **Limit Up-Limit Down (LULD):** the NMS plan that keeps each stock's trades within percentage bands around a five-minute average price and pauses it for five minutes when it can't ([Pre-Trade Risk, Halts & Volatility](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/pre-trade-risk-and-halts#limit-up-limit-down)).
- **Locate:** under Regulation SHO Rule 203(b), the documented borrow, arrangement or reasonable grounds a broker must have before accepting a short sale order ([Margin & Short Selling](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/margin-and-short-selling#short-selling-and-regulation-sho)).
- **Maintenance margin (futures):** the equity floor below which a futures account is called ([Futures & FCMs](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/futures-and-fcms#margin-and-daily-settlement)).
- **Maintenance margin (securities):** the equity a margin account must keep: at least 25% of long market value under FINRA Rule 4210, more for shorts and under house rules ([Margin & Short Selling](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/margin-and-short-selling#maintenance-and-house-requirements)).
- **Margin call:** a demand for deposit when an account breaks a Reg T, maintenance or house requirement; the firm may sell instead of waiting ([Margin & Short Selling](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/margin-and-short-selling#margin-calls-and-liquidations)).
- **Margin level:** a crypto venue's ratio of equity to used margin (Kraken) or collateral to liabilities (Binance), with call and liquidation thresholds ([Margin & Short Selling](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/margin-and-short-selling#crypto-margin-and-borrowing)).
- **Market access rule (Rule 15c3-5):** the SEC rule requiring brokers with market access to keep pre-trade credit, capital, erroneous-order and regulatory controls under their direct and exclusive control ([Pre-Trade Risk, Halts & Volatility](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/pre-trade-risk-and-halts#the-market-access-rule)).
- **Market-wide circuit breaker:** a halt of all US equity trading after the S&P 500 falls 7% or 13% (15 minutes, before 3:25 p.m.) or 20% (the rest of the day) from the prior close ([Pre-Trade Risk, Halts & Volatility](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/pre-trade-risk-and-halts#market-wide-circuit-breakers)).
- **Marketable limit order:** a buy limit at or above the national best offer (or a sell at or below the national best bid) when received, which can trade at once and is treated like a market order in routing and reporting ([Orders & Order Types](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/orders-and-order-types#order-types)).
- **Mention market:** an event contract on whether a speaker says a given word, which the CFTC's staff warned presents a heightened risk of manipulation ([Event Contracts & Prediction Markets](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/prediction-markets#settlement-and-resolution)).
- **Minimum pricing increment (tick size):** the smallest price step for quotes and orders under Rule 612: $0.01 for stocks at $1.00 or more, and $0.005 for stocks with a time-weighted average quoted spread of $0.015 or less once the 2024 amendments take effect ([US Equity Market Structure](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/equity-market-structure#access-fees-and-tick-sizes)).
- **National best bid and offer (NBBO):** the best bid and best offer for an NMS stock across all exchanges and FINRA, calculated and disseminated under an NMS plan, ties ranked by size then time ([US Equity Market Structure](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/equity-market-structure#the-nbbo-worked)).
- **NFA:** the National Futures Association, the futures industry's self-regulatory organization, of which every registered FCM is a member ([Futures & FCMs](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/futures-and-fcms#the-parties)).
- **Non-display use:** market data consumed by machines (routing, risk, matching, derived calculations) rather than shown to people, licensed by category ([Market Data](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/market-data#entitlements-delayed-data-and-display-rules)).
- **Not held order:** an order where the customer gives the broker discretion over price and time; Rule 606's quarterly statistics exclude them, and brokers often treat fractional and dollar-based orders this way ([Orders & Order Types](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/orders-and-order-types#extended-hours-and-notional-orders)).
- **Options Disclosure Document (ODD):** "Characteristics and Risks of Standardized Options," which Rule 9b-1 requires a broker to deliver before approving an account for options or accepting an order ([Options](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/options-trading#approval-and-the-disclosure-document)).
- **Order management system (OMS):** the system that accepts orders, checks them against the account, sends them to venues, applies fills and cancels, and keeps each order's state and history ([Orders & Order Types](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/orders-and-order-types#the-oms-lifecycle-and-state-machine)).
- **Order protection rule (Rule 611):** the requirement that trading centers prevent trade-throughs of protected quotations, with listed exceptions; its rescission was proposed in June 2026 ([US Equity Market Structure](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/equity-market-structure#order-protection)).
- **Overnight ATS:** an alternative trading system that matches NMS stocks between 8:00 pm and 4:00 am ET, such as Blue Ocean, Bruce, IBKR Eos and MOON ([Extended Hours & 24-Hour Trading](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/extended-and-overnight-trading#overnight-atss-and-the-2024-halt)).
- **Overnight LULD bands:** static price bands for the overnight session, 20% around the official close and the 7:45 pm last round-lot sale, with minimum widths of $1.00 or $3.00 and no automatic pause ([Extended Hours & 24-Hour Trading](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/extended-and-overnight-trading#what-had-to-change-around-the-exchanges)).
- **Part 40.11:** the CFTC rule barring event contracts that involve terrorism, assassination, war, gaming or unlawful activity, with a 90-day review ([Event Contracts & Prediction Markets](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/prediction-markets#the-rules)).
- **Pattern day trader:** the former FINRA designation for four or more day trades in five business days, with a $25,000 minimum; replaced by the intraday margin standard from June 4, 2026 ([Margin & Short Selling](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/margin-and-short-selling#intraday-margin-after-the-pattern-day-trader-rule)).
- **Portfolio margin:** a risk-based margin method under FINRA Rule 4210(g) that sets the requirement from the worst loss across price scenarios ([Margin & Short Selling](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/margin-and-short-selling#portfolio-margin)).
- **Position closing only:** a firm's restriction that lets customers sell or close positions in a security but not open or add to them, as brokers imposed in January 2021 ([Pre-Trade Risk, Halts & Volatility](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/pre-trade-risk-and-halts#clearing-deposits-and-the-january-2021-restrictions)).
- **Pre-market session:** exchange and ATS trading from 4:00 am to 9:30 am ET ([Extended Hours & 24-Hour Trading](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/extended-and-overnight-trading#the-sessions-today)).
- **Prediction market:** a market in event contracts, in the US a CFTC-registered DCM and DCO reached through an FCM ([Event Contracts & Prediction Markets](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/prediction-markets#how-the-platforms-do-it)).
- **Price collar:** a pre-trade check rejecting a limit price too far through the current market, the control that catches a mistyped price ([Pre-Trade Risk, Halts & Volatility](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/pre-trade-risk-and-halts#checks-before-an-order-leaves)).
- **Price improvement:** the amount by which a fill beats the NBBO at receipt (below the offer for a buy, above the bid for a sell), per share and in total; a worse price is disimprovement ([Order Routing, PFOF & Best Execution](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/routing-and-best-execution#one-order-worked)).
- **Price-time priority:** the matching rule in which the best price trades first and, at one price, the earliest order; trades happen at the resting order's price ([Order Books & Matching Engines](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/order-books-and-matching#price-time)).
- **Pro-rata allocation:** a matching rule that splits fills at a price level in proportion to resting size rather than by arrival time, used in some futures and options markets ([Order Books & Matching Engines](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/order-books-and-matching#pro-rata)).
- **Professional subscriber:** a market data user whose use is by or for an entity or serves third parties for compensation, licensed at professional rates; anyone else is non-professional ([Market Data](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/market-data#entitlements-delayed-data-and-display-rules)).
- **Protected quotation:** an exchange's or FINRA's best bid or offer, displayed by an automated trading center as a round lot and disseminated under an NMS plan, which other venues may not trade through ([US Equity Market Structure](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/equity-market-structure#the-nbbo-worked)).
- **Realized spread:** the effective spread measured against the midpoint some time after execution (15 seconds to 5 minutes in Rule 605), showing how much the filler kept once prices moved ([Order Routing, PFOF & Best Execution](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/routing-and-best-execution#reading-a-rule-605-report)).
- **Regular and rigorous review:** the at-least-quarterly review, by security and order type, of execution quality against competing markets that FINRA 5310 requires of firms that don't review order by order ([Order Routing, PFOF & Best Execution](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/routing-and-best-execution#the-duty)).
- **Regulation SHO:** the SEC's short sale rules: order marking, the locate, the close-out of fails, threshold securities and the Rule 201 price test ([Margin & Short Selling](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/margin-and-short-selling#short-selling-and-regulation-sho)).
- **Regulation T:** the Federal Reserve's rule (12 CFR 220) on broker credit: initial margin, cash account payment and calls within one payment period ([Margin & Short Selling](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/margin-and-short-selling#reg-t-initial-margin)).
- **Residual interest:** an FCM's own money in a customer segregated account, which must cover undermargined customers by 6:00 pm ET on the settlement date ([Futures & FCMs](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/futures-and-fcms#customer-segregation)).
- **Round lot:** the order size whose quotes count for the NBBO and protection, set by a stock's average closing price: 100 shares at $250 or less, 40 to $1,000, 10 to $10,000 and 1 above ([US Equity Market Structure](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/equity-market-structure#round-lots-and-odd-lots)).
- **Rule 201 (short sale price test):** after a 10% decline from the prior close, short sales may not be priced at or below the national best bid for the rest of the day and the next ([Pre-Trade Risk, Halts & Volatility](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/pre-trade-risk-and-halts#trading-halts)).
- **Rule 2265 disclosure:** FINRA's required extended-hours risk disclosure naming lower liquidity, higher volatility, changing prices, unlinked markets, news announcements and wider spreads ([Extended Hours & 24-Hour Trading](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/extended-and-overnight-trading#the-sessions-today)).
- **Rule 605 report:** a monthly public report of execution quality statistics by order type and size by market centers and larger brokers, amended in 2024 with compliance from August 1, 2026 ([Order Routing, PFOF & Best Execution](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/routing-and-best-execution#reading-a-rule-605-report)).
- **Rule 606 report:** a broker's quarterly public report of where its non-directed orders went, by month and order type, with payments received and the material terms of each venue relationship ([Order Routing, PFOF & Best Execution](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/routing-and-best-execution#reading-a-rule-606-report)).
- **Securities information processor (SIP):** the processor under an equity data plan that consolidates exchanges' quotes and trades and publishes the NBBO and the consolidated tape ([US Equity Market Structure](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/equity-market-structure#sips-and-direct-feeds)).
- **Segregation computation:** the FCM's daily statement, by currency, of customer funds held, the amount required and its residual interest, filed by noon the next business day ([Futures & FCMs](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/futures-and-fcms#customer-segregation)).
- **Self-certification:** listing a new contract by filing it and certifying it complies with the Act by the open of business the business day before listing (17 CFR 40.2) ([Event Contracts & Prediction Markets](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/prediction-markets#listing)).
- **Self-trade prevention:** matching engine rules that stop an account's order from trading with its own resting order, by canceling or decrementing one or both under the mode chosen ([Order Books & Matching Engines](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/order-books-and-matching#self-trade-prevention)).
- **Sequencer (matching engine):** the component that assigns every input to a matching engine its place in one order and makes the log durable, so the book is a deterministic function of the log ([Order Books & Matching Engines](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/order-books-and-matching#determinism-and-sequencers)).
- **Series (option):** one option type, strike and expiration on one underlying, within the class of all options on it ([Options](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/options-trading#contracts-series-and-symbols)).
- **Smart order router:** software that splits and sends an order across venues by price, fees, fill probability and order protection, recording each decision for best execution review ([Order Routing, PFOF & Best Execution](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/routing-and-best-execution#smart-order-routers)).
- **Snapshot and replay:** recovering a matching engine by restoring its state at a sequence number and reapplying every later input; snapshot frequency sets recovery time ([Order Books & Matching Engines](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/order-books-and-matching#replay-and-recovery)).
- **Source agency:** the named source an event contract's rules use to determine the outcome ([Event Contracts & Prediction Markets](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/prediction-markets#settlement-and-resolution)).
- **Stop and stop limit orders:** orders dormant until the market reaches a stop price, then sent as a market order or a limit order; what "reaches" means (a trade or a quote) is each platform's rule ([Orders & Order Types](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/orders-and-order-types#order-types)).
- **Threshold security:** a security with fails of 10,000 shares or more and 0.5% of shares outstanding for five settlement days, on an SRO list; fails in it must be closed out after 13 days ([Margin & Short Selling](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/margin-and-short-selling#short-selling-and-regulation-sho)).
- **Time in force:** how long an order stays live: day, good till canceled, good till date, immediate or cancel, fill or kill, or for the opening or closing auction only ([Orders & Order Types](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/orders-and-order-types#time-in-force)).
- **Trade date roll:** the 8:00 pm ET boundary after which an execution belongs to the next trade date for CAT, trade reporting and settlement, from December 6, 2026 ([Extended Hours & 24-Hour Trading](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/extended-and-overnight-trading#what-had-to-change-around-the-exchanges)).
- **Trading pause:** the five-minute LULD halt in a single stock after a limit state lasts 15 seconds, ended by a reopening auction ([Pre-Trade Risk, Halts & Volatility](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/pre-trade-risk-and-halts#limit-up-limit-down)).
- **Trailing stop:** a stop whose price follows the market by a fixed amount or percentage from its best level since entry, which the platform must persist ([Orders & Order Types](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/orders-and-order-types#order-types)).
- **Variation margin:** the day's gain or loss on a futures position, paid in cash from the losers to the winners through the clearing house at each daily settlement ([Futures & FCMs](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/futures-and-fcms#margin-and-daily-settlement)).
- **Vertical spread:** a long and a short option of the same type and expiration at different strikes; its requirement equals its maximum loss ([Options](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/options-trading#payoffs-and-requirements)).
- **Wholesaler:** a broker-dealer that fills retail orders as principal off-exchange, usually at or inside the NBBO, and pays brokers for the flow ([US Equity Market Structure](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/equity-market-structure#the-venues)).

## Clearing and Settlement

- **Affirmation:** an institutional customer's custodian agreeing to a trade's allocation and confirmation details, which Rule 15c6-2 has brokers complete as soon as technologically practicable and no later than the end of trade date under T+1 ([Clearing & Settlement](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/clearing-and-settlement#t-1-and-the-affirmation-deadline)).
- **Aggregate debit items:** the total of the debits in a broker's Rule 15c3-3 reserve formula, mostly customer margin loans; the base of the alternative net capital standard ([Customer Protection, Net Capital & SIPC](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/customer-protection#net-capital)).
- **Alert disposition:** the recorded decision that ends a surveillance alert, with its reason, reviewer and time: closed with no action, closed by a principal or opened as a case ([Market Surveillance & Manipulation](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/market-surveillance#from-alerts-to-cases)).
- **Audit-trail alternative:** the Rule 17a-4(f)(2) option, since 2023, to keep electronic records with a complete time-stamped trail of every change that permits re-creating the original, instead of WORM storage ([Books, Records & the Audit Trail](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/books-records-and-cat#how-long-and-how-rule-17a-4)).
- **Book entry:** moving ownership of securities by debiting and crediting participants' accounts at a depository, with no certificate moving ([Clearing & Settlement](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/clearing-and-settlement#the-players-nscc-dtc-and-occ)).
- **Buy-In Intent:** an NSCC member's demand for immediate delivery of a long CNS position, which gives it allocation priority and leads to Retransmittal Notices and, if unfilled, a buy-in ([Clearing & Settlement](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/clearing-and-settlement#fails-and-buy-ins)).
- **Carrying agreement:** the FINRA-approved contract under Rule 4311 between a clearing firm and an introducing broker that allocates account opening, orders, credit, custody, confirms, statements and records ([Brokerage as a Service](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/brokerage-as-a-service#who-owns-which-obligation)).
- **Clearing Fund:** the collateral NSCC collects from each member, sized mainly by a 99th percentile volatility charge on its unsettled positions, to cover the cost of closing out a defaulting member ([Clearing & Settlement](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/clearing-and-settlement#the-clearing-fund)).
- **Consolidated Audit Trail (CAT):** the regulatory database that receives every order event in NMS stocks and listed options from exchanges and broker-dealers by 8:00 a.m. ET the next trading day ([Books, Records & the Audit Trail](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/books-records-and-cat#the-consolidated-audit-trail)).
- **Continuous Net Settlement (CNS):** NSCC's system that nets each member's eligible trades in a security to one position per day and becomes counterparty to each accepted trade ([Clearing & Settlement](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/clearing-and-settlement#continuous-net-settlement)).
- **Control location:** a place where a broker may hold customers' fully paid and excess margin securities under Rule 15c3-3(c), such as DTC or a bank that has confirmed in writing it holds them free of liens ([Customer Protection, Net Capital & SIPC](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/customer-protection#possession-or-control)).
- **Crypto as a service:** a licensed provider that executes, custodies and holds the licenses for crypto offered inside another firm's app ([Brokerage as a Service](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/brokerage-as-a-service#crypto-as-a-service)).
- **Daily reserve computation:** the Rule 15c3-3 reserve formula run every business day, required since June 30, 2026 for firms with average total credits of $500 million or more ([Customer Protection, Net Capital & SIPC](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/customer-protection#the-daily-computation)).
- **DTC (The Depository Trust Company):** the US securities depository, where settlement moves securities and cash between participants by book entry ([Clearing & Settlement](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/clearing-and-settlement#the-players-nscc-dtc-and-occ)).
- **Early warning:** a Rule 17a-11 notice a broker owes its regulators when net capital nears its limits, such as below 120% of the minimum or 5% of aggregate debit items ([Customer Protection, Net Capital & SIPC](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/customer-protection#net-capital)).
- **Excess Capital Premium (ECP):** an NSCC charge when a member's volatility charge exceeds its net capital, equal to the excess times the ratio of charge to capital, capped at 2.0 ([Clearing & Settlement](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/clearing-and-settlement#the-clearing-fund)).
- **Excess margin securities:** a margin customer's securities worth more than 140% of their debit balance, which the broker must keep in its possession or control ([Customer Protection, Net Capital & SIPC](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/customer-protection#possession-or-control)).
- **Fail to deliver:** a short settlement obligation not delivered on settlement date, marked to market and re-netted daily in CNS and subject to Regulation SHO close-out ([Clearing & Settlement](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/clearing-and-settlement#fails-and-buy-ins)).
- **Fully disclosed:** a clearing arrangement in which the clearing firm carries each end customer's account in the customer's own name ([Brokerage as a Service](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/brokerage-as-a-service#fully-disclosed-and-omnibus)).
- **Haircut:** the percentage of a position's value deducted in computing net capital, such as 15% for most equities ([Customer Protection, Net Capital & SIPC](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/customer-protection#net-capital)).
- **Introducing broker (securities):** a broker-dealer that owns the customer relationship and sends trades to a clearing firm that carries the accounts and holds the assets ([Clearing & Settlement](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/clearing-and-settlement#self-clearing-and-introducing-brokers)).
- **Layering:** spoofing with several orders at stepped prices on one side of the book, canceled once the trader's real order on the other side fills ([Market Surveillance & Manipulation](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/market-surveillance#the-patterns)).
- **Marking the close:** trading near the close to move the closing price, which settles derivatives, sets fund values and triggers margin ([Market Surveillance & Manipulation](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/market-surveillance#the-patterns)).
- **Net capital:** a broker-dealer's net worth less nonallowable assets and haircuts, which Rule 15c3-1 requires to stay above a minimum and a ratio ([Customer Protection, Net Capital & SIPC](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/customer-protection#net-capital)).
- **Net equity:** a customer's claim in a SIPA liquidation, valued as of the filing date, paid from customer property and, for a shortfall, SIPC advances ([Customer Protection, Net Capital & SIPC](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/customer-protection#sipc)).
- **Novation:** a clearing house replacing the original counterparties to a trade, becoming buyer to every seller and seller to every buyer and guaranteeing settlement ([Clearing & Settlement](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/clearing-and-settlement#the-players-nscc-dtc-and-occ)).
- **NSCC (National Securities Clearing Corporation):** the DTCC clearing agency that clears and nets US equity trades and collects margin from its members ([Clearing & Settlement](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/clearing-and-settlement#the-players-nscc-dtc-and-occ)).
- **Omnibus account (brokerage):** one account at a clearing firm or provider holding many customers' assets, whose owner's own books say who owns what ([Brokerage as a Service](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/brokerage-as-a-service#fully-disclosed-and-omnibus)).
- **Possession or control:** the Rule 15c3-3(b) duty to keep customers' fully paid and excess margin securities in good control locations, checked daily security by security ([Customer Protection, Net Capital & SIPC](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/customer-protection#possession-or-control)).
- **Reference Database:** CAT's customer and account data store, formerly CAIS, which no longer holds customers' names, addresses or years of birth after a January 2026 amendment ([Books, Records & the Audit Trail](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/books-records-and-cat#the-consolidated-audit-trail)).
- **Reserve formula:** Exhibit A to Rule 15c3-3: credits a broker owes customers less debits customers owe it, with any excess deposited in a reserve bank account for their exclusive benefit ([Customer Protection, Net Capital & SIPC](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/customer-protection#the-reserve-formula)).
- **Self-clearing:** a broker-dealer that is itself a member of NSCC, DTC and OCC, carries its customers' accounts and posts its own clearing fund ([Clearing & Settlement](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/clearing-and-settlement#self-clearing-and-introducing-brokers)).
- **Separate capacity:** an account category SIPC treats as a distinct customer with its own $500,000 limit, such as individual, joint, IRA and Roth IRA ([Customer Protection, Net Capital & SIPC](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/customer-protection#sipc)).
- **SIPC protection:** coverage for missing customer assets when a member broker-dealer fails, up to $500,000 per customer including $250,000 of cash; not for losses in value, non-security digital assets or stablecoins ([Customer Protection, Net Capital & SIPC](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/customer-protection#sipc)).
- **Spoofing:** placing bids or offers with the intent to cancel them before execution, to move other traders' prices toward an order on the other side ([Market Surveillance & Manipulation](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/market-surveillance#the-patterns)).
- **Surveillance-sharing agreement:** an agreement between markets to share trading data for manipulation investigations, the SEC's test for listing commodity-based ETPs ([Market Surveillance & Manipulation](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/market-surveillance#crypto-wash-trading-and-venue-surveillance)).
- **T+1:** the standard US settlement cycle of one business day after trade date, in force since May 28, 2024 ([Clearing & Settlement](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/clearing-and-settlement#t-1-and-the-affirmation-deadline)).
- **Wash trade:** a trade with no change in beneficial ownership, made to print volume or a price ([Market Surveillance & Manipulation](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/market-surveillance#the-patterns)).
- **WORM:** write once, read many: storage in a non-rewriteable, non-erasable format, one of the two Rule 17a-4(f)(2) options for electronic records ([Books, Records & the Audit Trail](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/books-records-and-cat#how-long-and-how-rule-17a-4)).

## Wealth and Advice

- **Applicable age (RMD):** the age at which traditional IRA required minimum distributions begin: 73 for someone who reaches 72 after 2022 and 73 before 2033, 75 for someone who reaches 74 after 2032 ([IRAs & Retirement Accounts](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/retirement-accounts#required-minimum-distributions)).
- **Block order:** one market order for the combined quantity of many client accounts, filled and then allocated back to them at the average price by a rule fixed before the order is sent ([RIA Custodians & Advisor Platforms](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/advisor-platforms#block-trading-and-allocation)).
- **Cash drag:** the return a portfolio gives up by holding cash instead of its invested assets, about the cash weight times the difference between the invested return and the cash yield ([Robo-Advisors & Model Portfolios](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/robo-advisors#cash-drag-and-the-schwab-order)).
- **Cherry-picking:** allocating a block's better-priced fills to favored accounts after prices are known; prevented by locking the allocation before the order goes out ([RIA Custodians & Advisor Platforms](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/advisor-platforms#block-trading-and-allocation)).
- **Composite (GIPS):** a group of portfolios managed to one strategy whose asset-weighted return a firm presents; every actual, fee-paying, discretionary segregated account must be in at least one ([Portfolio Accounting & Performance](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/portfolio-accounting#gips)).
- **Direct indexing:** owning an index's constituent stocks in the account instead of a fund, so individual stocks can be harvested, excluded or tilted at the cost of tracking error ([Tax-Loss Harvesting & Direct Indexing](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/tax-loss-harvesting-and-direct-indexing#direct-indexing)).
- **Direct rollover:** a distribution from a retirement plan paid straight to the receiving IRA's custodian, with no withholding and no 60-day clock; Form 1099-R code G ([IRAs & Retirement Accounts](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/retirement-accounts#rollovers-and-the-60-day-rule)).
- **Drift:** the distance between an account's current weights and its model's targets, measured per asset class or for the whole portfolio ([Robo-Advisors & Model Portfolios](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/robo-advisors#rebalancing-and-drift-bands)).
- **Drift band (tolerance):** the drift beyond which a threshold rebalance runs, for example 3% or 5% ([Robo-Advisors & Model Portfolios](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/robo-advisors#rebalancing-and-drift-bands)).
- **Dual registrant:** a firm registered as both a broker-dealer and an investment adviser, which must say in which capacity it acts ([Advice, Reg BI & Conduct](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/advice-and-conduct#broker-or-adviser)).
- **External cash flow:** money or assets crossing the boundary of the portfolio being measured (deposits, withdrawals, transfers), as opposed to the portfolio's own income and trades ([Portfolio Accounting & Performance](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/portfolio-accounting#external-flows-and-valuation-points)).
- **Fiduciary duty (adviser):** the duty of care and loyalty an investment adviser owes a client under the Advisers Act for the whole relationship, shaped by agreement but never waived ([Advice, Reg BI & Conduct](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/advice-and-conduct#the-adviser-s-fiduciary-duty)).
- **Form 1099-R:** the report of each distribution from a retirement account, with its taxable amount, withholding and a distribution code ([IRAs & Retirement Accounts](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/retirement-accounts#forms-5498-and-1099-r)).
- **Form 5498:** the IRA custodian's annual report of contributions by kind and tax year, year-end value and whether an RMD is due ([IRAs & Retirement Accounts](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/retirement-accounts#forms-5498-and-1099-r)).
- **Form ADV:** an adviser's registration and disclosure form: Part 1 (registration data), Part 2A (the brochure) and 2B (supplements), and Part 3 (Form CRS) ([Advice, Reg BI & Conduct](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/advice-and-conduct#form-adv)).
- **Form CRS (relationship summary):** the short document a broker-dealer or adviser delivers to retail investors describing services, fees, conflicts and its standard of conduct, at most two pages (four for a combined dual registrant) ([Advice, Reg BI & Conduct](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/advice-and-conduct#form-crs)).
- **Hypothetical performance:** performance not actually achieved by any of the adviser's portfolios (model, backtested, targeted or projected), usable in advertising only under the marketing rule's conditions ([Advice, Reg BI & Conduct](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/advice-and-conduct#the-marketing-rule)).
- **Indirect (60-day) rollover:** a retirement distribution paid to the owner and redeposited in an IRA within 60 days of receipt; IRA-to-IRA rollovers are limited to one per 12 months ([IRAs & Retirement Accounts](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/retirement-accounts#rollovers-and-the-60-day-rule)).
- **IRA match:** a broker's contribution to a customer's IRA as a percentage of their own contribution, kept only if conditions such as a holding period are met ([IRAs & Retirement Accounts](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/retirement-accounts#matches)).
- **Marketing rule:** Advisers Act Rule 206(4)-1, governing adviser advertisements: general prohibitions, testimonials and endorsements, third-party ratings and performance ([Advice, Reg BI & Conduct](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/advice-and-conduct#the-marketing-rule)).
- **Model portfolio:** a versioned set of target weights by asset class, with the tickers that represent each class, its drift band and its cash weight ([Robo-Advisors & Model Portfolios](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/robo-advisors#model-portfolios)).
- **Modified Dietz:** an approximation of the money-weighted return: the gain divided by the starting value plus time-weighted flows ([Portfolio Accounting & Performance](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/portfolio-accounting#two-returns-on-the-same-cash-flows)).
- **Money-weighted return (MWR):** the internal rate of return on an account's starting value and cash flows; measures the investor's outcome ([Portfolio Accounting & Performance](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/portfolio-accounting#two-returns-on-the-same-cash-flows)).
- **Pure gross-of-fees return:** a wrap fee portfolio's return before the whole bundled fee, shown under GIPS only as labeled supplemental information ([Portfolio Accounting & Performance](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/portfolio-accounting#gips)).
- **Regulation Best Interest (Reg BI):** Rule 15l-1, requiring a broker-dealer recommending a security, strategy or account to a retail customer to act in the customer's best interest, through disclosure, care, conflict of interest and compliance obligations ([Advice, Reg BI & Conduct](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/advice-and-conduct#regulation-best-interest)).
- **Required beginning date:** April 1 of the year after the IRA owner reaches the applicable age, the deadline for the first RMD ([IRAs & Retirement Accounts](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/retirement-accounts#required-minimum-distributions)).
- **Required minimum distribution (RMD):** the annual withdrawal from a traditional IRA after the applicable age: the prior December 31 balance divided by the distribution period from the IRS tables ([IRAs & Retirement Accounts](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/retirement-accounts#required-minimum-distributions)).
- **Retail communication (FINRA 2210):** a broker-dealer communication distributed or made available to more than 25 retail investors within any 30 calendar-day period, approved by a principal before use ([Advice, Reg BI & Conduct](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/advice-and-conduct#finra-rule-2210-for-broker-communications)).
- **RIA custodian:** a broker-dealer or bank that holds an independent adviser's client accounts in the clients' names, sends their statements and executes and bills on the adviser's instructions ([RIA Custodians & Advisor Platforms](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/advisor-platforms)).
- **Robo-adviser:** an investment adviser that gives software-generated advice and manages portfolios through automated jobs, owing the same fiduciary duty as any adviser ([Robo-Advisors & Model Portfolios](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/robo-advisors)).
- **Substitute security:** a fund bought after a harvest that keeps the asset class's exposure but tracks a different index, so it isn't substantially identical to the one sold ([Tax-Loss Harvesting & Direct Indexing](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/tax-loss-harvesting-and-direct-indexing#substitute-securities)).
- **TAMP (turnkey asset management program):** an outsourced provider of models or managed sleeves, rebalancing, trading and often billing for advisers ([RIA Custodians & Advisor Platforms](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/advisor-platforms#tamps-and-model-marketplaces)).
- **Tax alpha:** the after-tax return a harvesting program claims to add; mostly deferral, decaying over time, and often hypothetical ([Tax-Loss Harvesting & Direct Indexing](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/tax-loss-harvesting-and-direct-indexing#tax-alpha-and-its-limits)).
- **Tax-loss harvesting:** selling a lot below its basis to realize a loss and buying a substitute that keeps the exposure without washing the loss ([Tax-Loss Harvesting & Direct Indexing](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/tax-loss-harvesting-and-direct-indexing)).
- **Time-weighted return (TWR):** the return from chaining sub-period returns split at every external flow, unaffected by flows; measures the manager ([Portfolio Accounting & Performance](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/portfolio-accounting#two-returns-on-the-same-cash-flows)).
- **Tracking error:** the standard deviation of the difference between an account's return and its benchmark's ([Tax-Loss Harvesting & Direct Indexing](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/tax-loss-harvesting-and-direct-indexing#direct-indexing)).
- **Unified managed account (UMA):** one account split into sleeves following different models or managers, with an overlay that nets trades and manages taxes across them ([RIA Custodians & Advisor Platforms](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/advisor-platforms#tamps-and-model-marketplaces)).
- **Uniform Lifetime Table:** the IRS table of distribution periods by age used for most IRA owners' RMDs (Table III in Publication 590-B) ([IRAs & Retirement Accounts](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/retirement-accounts#required-minimum-distributions)).
- **Wrap fee:** one asset-based fee bundling advice, trading and custody ([Robo-Advisors & Model Portfolios](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/robo-advisors#fees)).

## Chains and Wallets

- **Account model:** a chain that keeps a balance and a transaction counter (nonce) per account, as Ethereum and Solana do, so an account's transactions execute strictly in order ([Blockchains for Platform Engineers](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/blockchains-for-engineers#accounts-and-nonces)).
- **Address poisoning:** putting a lookalike address into a victim's transaction history with a zero-value or dust transfer so the victim later copies it by mistake ([Wallets](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/wallets#approval-phishing-drainers-and-address-poisoning)).
- **Allowance (approval):** the amount an ERC-20 holder has authorized a spender to move with `transferFrom`, set by `approve` and kept until spent or reset ([Tokens & Token Standards](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/tokens-and-standards#approvals-worked)).
- **Associated token account:** a Solana holder's token account for one mint, at an address derived from the wallet and the mint ([Tokens & Token Standards](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/tokens-and-standards#spl-tokens-and-token-extensions)).
- **Base fee:** the per-gas price every transaction in an Ethereum block pays under EIP-1559, moving at most one-eighth per block toward the gas target and burned ([Blockchains for Platform Engineers](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/blockchains-for-engineers#eip-1559-on-ethereum)).
- **Blob:** a 128 KiB data payload rollups post to Ethereum under EIP-4844, priced by its own fee market and kept by nodes for about 18 days ([Layer 2s, Bridges & Cross-Chain](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/layer-2s-and-bridges#data-on-ethereum-blobs)).
- **Bridge:** a system that moves an asset between chains that don't share a ledger, by locking and minting, burning and minting, paying from inventory or relaying verified messages ([Layer 2s, Bridges & Cross-Chain](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/layer-2s-and-bridges#designs)).
- **Bridged token:** a token on one chain backed by collateral locked by a bridge on another, a claim on the bridge rather than on the original issuer ([Layer 2s, Bridges & Cross-Chain](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/layer-2s-and-bridges#designs)).
- **Challenge period:** the time an optimistic rollup's state claim can be disputed before withdrawals to Ethereum can be finalized, seven days on the OP Stack's mainnet configuration ([Layer 2s, Bridges & Cross-Chain](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/layer-2s-and-bridges#withdrawal-delays-and-what-final-means-on-a-rollup)).
- **Child pays for parent (CPFP):** pulling a stuck Bitcoin transaction into a block by spending its output in a child with a fee high enough for the pair ([Blockchains for Platform Engineers](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/blockchains-for-engineers#child-pays-for-parent)).
- **Commitment level:** how settled a Solana block is (processed, confirmed, finalized); under Alpenglow, confirmed and finalized are the same ([Blockchains for Platform Engineers](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/blockchains-for-engineers#solana-commitment-levels)).
- **Delegatecall:** an EVM call that runs another contract's code with the caller's storage and balance; from a multisig to an untrusted contract it hands over the wallet ([Keys, Signing & Key Management](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/keys-and-signing#safe-on-evm-chains)).
- **Drainer:** a phishing kit that gets a wallet user to sign an approval, permit or authorization that lets the attacker take the user's assets ([Wallets](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/wallets#approval-phishing-drainers-and-address-poisoning)).
- **DVN (decentralized verifier network):** a verifier an application configures in LayerZero to attest cross-chain messages, set per pathway as X-of-Y-of-N ([Layer 2s, Bridges & Cross-Chain](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/layer-2s-and-bridges#messaging-networks)).
- **Effective gas price:** what an Ethereum transaction pays per gas, the base fee plus the lesser of the priority fee cap and the room under the max fee ([Blockchains for Platform Engineers](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/blockchains-for-engineers#eip-1559-on-ethereum)).
- **EIP-7702 authorization:** a signed tuple that sets an EOA's code to point at a delegate contract; with chain ID 0 it is valid on every chain ([Wallets](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/wallets#eip-7702)).
- **Embedded wallet:** a wallet an app creates for a user at sign-up, with the key held in shares or enclaves and recovered through the app's login rather than a seed phrase ([Wallets](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/wallets#passkey-and-embedded-wallets)).
- **EOA (externally owned account):** an Ethereum account controlled by a private key rather than by contract code ([Wallets](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/wallets#browser-and-mobile-wallets)).
- **ERC-20:** the standard interface for fungible tokens on EVM chains: balances, transfers, approvals and the `Transfer` and `Approval` events ([Tokens & Token Standards](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/tokens-and-standards#erc-20)).
- **ERC-4337 (account abstraction):** a standard for contract wallets that submit user operations through bundlers to a shared EntryPoint contract, with optional paymasters for gas ([Wallets](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/wallets#erc-4337)).
- **ERC-721 and ERC-1155:** the standards for non-fungible tokens and for contracts holding many token types, identified by contract and token ID ([Tokens & Token Standards](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/tokens-and-standards#nfts-erc-721-and-erc-1155)).
- **Event log:** a record a contract emits during execution, with topics (the event signature hash and indexed fields) and data, which indexers filter on ([Chain Data & Indexing](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/chain-data-and-indexing#event-logs)).
- **Fee-on-transfer token:** a token that delivers less than the amount sent because the contract withholds a fee, so deposits credit by balance change ([Tokens & Token Standards](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/tokens-and-standards#fee-on-transfer-tokens)).
- **Finality:** the point after which a chain's block can't be reverted short of a prohibitive cost; on Ethereum, a finalized checkpoint, which an attacker would need to sacrifice at least one-third of staked ETH to revert ([Blockchains for Platform Engineers](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/blockchains-for-engineers#finality-per-chain)).
- **Fork point:** the last block two competing branches share, where reorg-safe ingestion rewinds to ([Chain Data & Indexing](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/chain-data-and-indexing#reorg-safe-ingestion)).
- **Gap limit:** the number of consecutive unused addresses (20 under BIP-44) after which recovery software stops scanning an HD account ([Keys, Signing & Key Management](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/keys-and-signing#bip-44-a-worked-path)).
- **Hardened derivation:** BIP-32 child derivation at indexes 2^31 and above, which needs the parent private key, so a leaked child key and xpub can't expose keys above it ([Keys, Signing & Key Management](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/keys-and-signing#bip-32-derivation-and-the-hardened-boundary)).
- **HD wallet:** a hierarchical deterministic wallet that derives every key from one seed along numbered paths (BIP-32, BIP-39, BIP-44) ([Keys, Signing & Key Management](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/keys-and-signing#hd-wallets)).
- **Key ceremony:** the scripted, witnessed event in which custody keys or shares are generated, backed up, refreshed or destroyed ([Keys, Signing & Key Management](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/keys-and-signing#key-ceremonies)).
- **Mempool:** a node's set of broadcast transactions not yet in a block; each node keeps its own under its own policy ([Blockchains for Platform Engineers](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/blockchains-for-engineers#mempools)).
- **Mnemonic (seed phrase):** 12 to 24 words encoding a wallet's entropy and checksum under BIP-39, from which the HD seed is derived ([Keys, Signing & Key Management](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/keys-and-signing#bip-39-the-seed-phrase)).
- **MPC (threshold signatures):** signing in which parties holding key shares jointly produce one ordinary signature without ever assembling the key ([Keys, Signing & Key Management](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/keys-and-signing#mpc-and-threshold-signatures)).
- **Multisig:** a wallet whose spending needs signatures from m of n keys, enforced by Bitcoin script or by a contract such as Safe ([Keys, Signing & Key Management](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/keys-and-signing#multisig)).
- **Native asset:** a chain's own coin (BTC, ETH, SOL), kept by the protocol and used to pay fees, as opposed to a token kept by a contract ([Tokens & Token Standards](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/tokens-and-standards#native-assets-and-tokens)).
- **Nonce (account):** the sequential transaction number an account signs into each transaction; one stuck nonce blocks every later one ([Blockchains for Platform Engineers](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/blockchains-for-engineers#accounts-and-nonces)).
- **Paymaster:** an ERC-4337 contract that pays a user operation's gas, sponsored or in exchange for a token ([Wallets](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/wallets#erc-4337)).
- **Permit:** an off-chain signature that sets an ERC-20 allowance (EIP-2612), or a Permit2 signature authorizing transfers through Uniswap's Permit2 contract ([Tokens & Token Standards](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/tokens-and-standards#permits)).
- **Policy engine:** the service before a signer that evaluates the decoded transaction against allowlists, limits and approval rules, where an explicit deny wins ([Keys, Signing & Key Management](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/keys-and-signing#policy-engines-and-quorum-approvals)).
- **Price mark:** the price a platform values a position at, from a documented methodology over several sources with staleness limits ([Chain Data & Indexing](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/chain-data-and-indexing#a-mark-methodology)).
- **Proxy (upgradeable contract):** a contract that forwards calls to a replaceable implementation, with the implementation and admin at EIP-1967 storage slots ([Tokens & Token Standards](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/tokens-and-standards#upgradeable-proxies-and-admin-keys)).
- **PSBT (partially signed Bitcoin transaction):** the BIP-174 format that carries an unsigned transaction and its inputs between signers, including offline ones ([Keys, Signing & Key Management](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/keys-and-signing#bitcoin-script)).
- **Rebasing token:** a token whose balances change without transfers as the protocol reports rewards or penalties, as stETH does ([Tokens & Token Standards](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/tokens-and-standards#rebasing-tokens)).
- **Reorg (reorganization):** a chain replacing its most recent blocks with a different sequence, which can remove a transaction that had been seen in a block ([Blockchains for Platform Engineers](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/blockchains-for-engineers#reorgs)).
- **Replace-by-fee (RBF):** replacing an unconfirmed Bitcoin transaction with one spending the same inputs at a higher fee; default for any transaction since Bitcoin Core 28.0 ([Blockchains for Platform Engineers](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/blockchains-for-engineers#replace-by-fee)).
- **Rollup:** a chain that executes transactions itself and posts its data and a proof or challengeable claim to Ethereum, optimistic or ZK ([Layer 2s, Bridges & Cross-Chain](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/layer-2s-and-bridges#optimistic-and-zk)).
- **Rollup stage:** ETHBEAT's (formerly L2BEAT's) 0 to 2 rating of how far a rollup depends on its operators rather than code ([Layer 2s, Bridges & Cross-Chain](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/layer-2s-and-bridges#stages)).
- **Sequencer (rollup):** the operator that orders a rollup's transactions and gives quick confirmations that are final only once the batch is final on Ethereum ([Layer 2s, Bridges & Cross-Chain](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/layer-2s-and-bridges#sequencers)).
- **Smart account:** a wallet whose rules are contract code (ERC-4337, or an EOA delegated under EIP-7702) ([Wallets](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/wallets#smart-accounts)).
- **SPL token and Token-2022:** Solana's token program and its Token Extensions successor, whose optional extensions include transfer fees, default-frozen accounts, transfer hooks and a permanent delegate ([Tokens & Token Standards](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/tokens-and-standards#spl-tokens-and-token-extensions)).
- **Trace:** the call frames an EVM transaction executed, the only place native transfers made by contracts appear ([Chain Data & Indexing](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/chain-data-and-indexing#traces-for-native-transfers)).
- **UTXO (unspent transaction output):** an output a Bitcoin transaction created and no later transaction has spent; a wallet's balance is the sum of the UTXOs its keys can spend ([Blockchains for Platform Engineers](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/blockchains-for-engineers#unspent-outputs)).
- **Virtual size (vsize):** a Bitcoin transaction's weight divided by four, the unit fee rates are quoted in (sat/vB) ([Blockchains for Platform Engineers](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/blockchains-for-engineers#fee-rates-on-bitcoin)).
- **WalletConnect:** a protocol that pairs an app with a wallet by URI or QR code and relays encrypted signing requests over a session ([Wallets](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/wallets#wallet-connection)).
- **xpub (extended public key):** a BIP-32 public key with its chain code, from which non-hardened child addresses can be derived without spend ability ([Keys, Signing & Key Management](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/keys-and-signing#bip-32-derivation-and-the-hardened-boundary)).

## Crypto Exchanges

- **Address allowlist:** a customer's list of verified withdrawal addresses, confirmed out of band and usable only after a cooling period, to which withdrawals can be restricted ([Deposits & Withdrawals](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/crypto-deposits-and-withdrawals#the-checks)).
- **Address ownership proof:** a signature over a fresh challenge from the key of each address an exchange counts as reserves, proving control (not exclusive control or freedom from claims) at the snapshot ([Proof of Reserves & Liabilities](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/proof-of-reserves#proof-of-assets)).
- **`allow_negative`:** the FTX.com account setting, set only for Alameda, that let an account withdraw while net negative and exempted it from automatic liquidation ([FTX, Celsius & the 2022 Collapse](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/exchange-collapses#the-alameda-exception)).
- **Auto-deleveraging (ADL):** the last step of a perpetuals liquidation waterfall, closing profitable opposing positions at the bankrupt trader's bankruptcy price, ranked by profit and leverage, when the insurance fund can't cover the loss ([Perpetuals & Crypto Derivatives](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/perpetuals-and-crypto-derivatives#auto-deleveraging)).
- **Backstop liquidation:** taking over a position the book couldn't close, by an insurance fund or a liquidator vault, once equity falls below a threshold beneath maintenance margin ([Perpetuals & Crypto Derivatives](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/perpetuals-and-crypto-derivatives#liquidation)).
- **Bankruptcy price:** the price at which a leveraged position's margin is exactly used up; fills below it are losses the trader can't pay ([Perpetuals & Crypto Derivatives](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/perpetuals-and-crypto-derivatives#a-worked-liquidation-price-under-isolated-margin)).
- **Cancel-only mode:** a market state in which the matching engine accepts cancels but no new orders, used to recover from incidents and to wind down delisted assets ([How a Crypto Exchange Works](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/crypto-exchange-architecture#the-matching-engine-briefly)).
- **Ceremony (cold storage):** a scripted, witnessed procedure for generating keys, signing from cold storage, rotating, testing recovery or retiring a wallet, recorded with people, devices, seals and software hashes ([Hot, Warm & Cold Wallets](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/hot-and-cold-wallets#cold-storage-ceremonies)).
- **Churn limit:** Ethereum's cap on stake that can activate or exit per epoch (256 ETH since Electra at current stake levels), which sets the length of the entry and exit queues ([Staking as a Product](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/staking#getting-out-of-ethereum-worked)).
- **Coin-listing policy:** a New York-regulated entity's Department-approved policy for self-certifying coins, with governance, an eight-area risk assessment and a required coin-delisting policy ([Listing & Delisting Assets](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/listing-assets#new-york-coin-listing-and-delisting-policies)).
- **Cold wallet:** a wallet whose keys stay offline and sign only through people with air-gapped devices under quorum, taking hours to a day to move coins out ([Hot, Warm & Cold Wallets](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/hot-and-cold-wallets#the-tiers)).
- **Confirmation (chain):** a block added after the block holding a transaction; exchanges credit some chains' deposits after a set count (Kraken: three for bitcoin) ([Deposits & Withdrawals](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/crypto-deposits-and-withdrawals#confirmation-policies-per-chain)).
- **Confirmation policy:** an exchange's rule per asset, network and amount band for how many confirmations (or which finality tag) a deposit needs before provisional credit and before it posts and can be withdrawn ([Deposits & Withdrawals](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/crypto-deposits-and-withdrawals#confirmation-policies-per-chain)).
- **Convert:** a simple-buy-style quote that exchanges one crypto asset for another at one displayed rate, through a quote currency or as a direct cross ([Crypto Trading Products](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/crypto-trading-products#how-a-quote-is-built)).
- **Cross margin:** a margin mode in which all cross positions share the account's collateral, so liquidation can take the whole account ([Perpetuals & Crypto Derivatives](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/perpetuals-and-crypto-derivatives#margin-modes)).
- **Deposit address:** a chain address, or a shared address plus a memo or tag, assigned to one customer account so incoming transfers can be credited to it ([Deposits & Withdrawals](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/crypto-deposits-and-withdrawals#deposit-addresses)).
- **Destination tag:** a 32-bit number in an XRP Ledger payment identifying the customer at a shared exchange address; memos play the same role on other chains ([Deposits & Withdrawals](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/crypto-deposits-and-withdrawals#memo-and-tag-chains)).
- **Early unbonding:** a platform paying out unstaked assets before the chain's unbonding period ends, from an unstaked buffer; a liquidity risk the platform carries ([Staking as a Product](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/staking#faster-than-the-chain)).
- **Earn program:** a platform product paying interest on deposited crypto by lending or using it; under Celsius's terms the deposits became the platform's property ([FTX, Celsius & the 2022 Collapse](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/exchange-collapses#celsius-the-terms-decided)).
- **Fee bump:** replacing a stuck outbound transaction with one paying a higher fee, by the same nonce on account chains or by replace-by-fee or child-pays-for-parent on Bitcoin ([Deposits & Withdrawals](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/crypto-deposits-and-withdrawals#stuck-transactions-and-fee-bumping)).
- **Fee tier:** the maker and taker rates an account pays, set by its rolling 30-day volume (or, at some exchanges, assets held or a token balance) and frozen on each fee when charged ([Crypto Trading Products](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/crypto-trading-products#the-order-book-maker-taker-and-tiers)).
- **Forwarder address:** a per-customer contract address on an account-model chain that forwards native coin to the main wallet and lets it pull tokens out, so deposit addresses never need gas ([Deposits & Withdrawals](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/crypto-deposits-and-withdrawals#one-address-per-customer-per-network)).
- **Funding rate:** the periodic payment rate between longs and shorts of a perpetual, from an average premium index plus a clamped interest component, that pulls the perpetual toward its index ([Perpetuals & Crypto Derivatives](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/perpetuals-and-crypto-derivatives#funding-rates)).
- **Gas tank:** the wallet that funds network fees for sweeps and top-ups on account-model chains; gas sent to customer addresses is tracked as customer funds ([Deposits & Withdrawals](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/crypto-deposits-and-withdrawals#the-gas-problem-on-account-chains)).
- **Greenlist:** the New York Department of Financial Services' list of coins its regulated entities may offer after notice, without self-certification ([Listing & Delisting Assets](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/listing-assets#new-york-coin-listing-and-delisting-policies)).
- **Hot wallet:** a wallet signed automatically by an online signer under a policy engine, holding the next hours of withdrawals within a floor, target and ceiling ([Hot, Warm & Cold Wallets](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/hot-and-cold-wallets#hot-wallet-limits-and-sizing)).
- **Hot wallet floor, target and ceiling:** the balance that triggers a refill (peak outflow times refill lead time times a safety factor), the level a refill restores, and the most a hot wallet may hold ([Hot, Warm & Cold Wallets](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/hot-and-cold-wallets#floor-target-ceiling)).
- **Impact price:** the average price to fill a set notional on one side of the book, used in the premium index so funding reflects depth ([Perpetuals & Crypto Derivatives](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/perpetuals-and-crypto-derivatives#the-formula)).
- **Inclusion proof:** the sibling hashes and sums from a customer's leaf to the root of a liability tree, from which the customer recomputes the published root ([Proof of Reserves & Liabilities](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/proof-of-reserves#a-worked-inclusion-proof)).
- **Index price:** a weighted average of the underlying asset's spot price on other venues, the anchor for a perpetual's funding ([Perpetuals & Crypto Derivatives](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/perpetuals-and-crypto-derivatives#index-mark-and-last-price)).
- **Insurance fund:** a venue's own pool, per collateral asset, that pays losses when liquidations fill below the bankruptcy price ([Perpetuals & Crypto Derivatives](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/perpetuals-and-crypto-derivatives#the-insurance-fund)).
- **Isolated margin:** a margin mode in which a position has its own margin, so liquidating it takes only that margin ([Perpetuals & Crypto Derivatives](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/perpetuals-and-crypto-derivatives#margin-modes)).
- **Ledger wallet proof:** a record per wallet and asset network at a block comparing the ledger's omnibus lines with the chain's balance, adjusted for withdrawals posted but not yet in a block and deposits not yet final ([How a Crypto Exchange Works](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/crypto-exchange-architecture#how-to-build-it)).
- **Liquid staking token (LST):** a transferable, usually rebasing, receipt for staked coins, such as stETH, whose market price can leave par ([Staking as a Product](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/staking#liquid-staking-tokens)).
- **Liquidation price:** the mark price at which a position's equity falls to maintenance margin and the venue starts closing it ([Perpetuals & Crypto Derivatives](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/perpetuals-and-crypto-derivatives#a-worked-liquidation-price-under-isolated-margin)).
- **Liquidity provider program:** an exchange's terms paying market makers lower fees or rebates for quoting, often by their share of maker volume, sometimes with uptime, spread and depth obligations ([Crypto Trading Products](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/crypto-trading-products#where-the-price-comes-from)).
- **Maker rebate:** a negative maker fee: the exchange pays the resting side of a trade, at top fee tiers or under a liquidity program ([Crypto Trading Products](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/crypto-trading-products#maker-and-taker)).
- **Mark price:** the price a derivatives venue computes margin, unrealized profit and liquidations from, built from the index plus a damped premium, never the last trade ([Perpetuals & Crypto Derivatives](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/perpetuals-and-crypto-derivatives#index-mark-and-last-price)).
- **Merkle-sum tree:** a Merkle tree whose nodes carry a sum as well as a hash, committing to every customer balance and their total ([Proof of Reserves & Liabilities](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/proof-of-reserves#proof-of-liabilities-merkle-sum-trees)).
- **Misdirected deposit:** coins received that can't be credited as sent: a missing tag, the wrong network, an unsupported token or a privacy feature, handled under a written recovery policy ([Deposits & Withdrawals](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/crypto-deposits-and-withdrawals#wrong-networks-and-recovery)).
- **Negative-balance attack:** hiding a shortfall in a liability tree by adding an account with a negative balance; closed by proving every leaf non-negative ([Proof of Reserves & Liabilities](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/proof-of-reserves#the-negative-balance-attack)).
- **Nonce reservation:** allocating each outbound transaction's nonce for a sending address inside a database transaction, so two workers never sign with the same nonce and gaps are visible ([Deposits & Withdrawals](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/crypto-deposits-and-withdrawals#nonce-management)).
- **OTC desk:** an exchange or broker desk that quotes large trades all-in, off the order book, often with settlement deferred by up to a day ([Crypto Trading Products](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/crypto-trading-products#rfq-and-otc-desks)).
- **Perpetual future:** a futures contract with no expiry, settled in cash against the margin asset and kept near spot by funding payments ([Perpetuals & Crypto Derivatives](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/perpetuals-and-crypto-derivatives#what-a-perpetual-is)).
- **Post-only:** an order instruction (or a whole market's mode) that rejects an order rather than let it take liquidity, guaranteeing the maker fee ([Crypto Trading Products](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/crypto-trading-products#maker-and-taker)).
- **Premium index:** the perpetual's measured premium or discount to the index from impact bid and ask prices, sampled every few seconds ([Perpetuals & Crypto Derivatives](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/perpetuals-and-crypto-derivatives#the-formula)).
- **Proof of liabilities:** a commitment to every customer balance, usually a Merkle-sum tree, that each customer can check their own balance against ([Proof of Reserves & Liabilities](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/proof-of-reserves#proof-of-liabilities-merkle-sum-trees)).
- **Proof of reserves:** a point-in-time comparison, per asset, of the assets an exchange proves it controls with the customer balances it commits to; it doesn't show other liabilities or encumbrances ([Proof of Reserves & Liabilities](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/proof-of-reserves#what-a-proof-of-reserves-claims)).
- **Protocol staking (SEC staff view):** solo, self-custodial and custodial staking in which the provider's role is administrative or ministerial, which Corporation Finance staff said in May 2025 does not involve offers of securities ([Staking as a Product](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/staking#the-sec-record)).
- **Provisional buying power (crypto):** trading credit extended against a deposit that has reached a banded confirmation threshold but not finality; nothing can leave the exchange until it posts ([Deposits & Withdrawals](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/crypto-deposits-and-withdrawals#confirmation-policies-per-chain)).
- **Rebalancing (wallets):** moving coins between wallet tiers or chains; downhill moves to fixed cold addresses can be automated, uphill moves toward the hot path need quorum, decoding and simulation ([Hot, Warm & Cold Wallets](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/hot-and-cold-wallets#rebalancing-between-tiers)).
- **Request for quote (RFQ):** asking one or more liquidity providers for a firm price on a whole size, valid for seconds, executed only by explicit acceptance ([Crypto Trading Products](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/crypto-trading-products#rfq-and-otc-desks)).
- **SAFU:** Binance's Secure Asset Fund for Users, an emergency reserve established in July 2018 from a share of trading fees ([Exchange Security & Hacks](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/exchange-security#customer-reimbursement)).
- **Signing service compromise:** an attacker gaining the ability to request signatures from an exchange's internal signer, so the policy at the signer, not key protection, decides the loss ([Exchange Security & Hacks](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/exchange-security#binance-october-29-2026)).
- **Simple buy:** a crypto purchase priced from a reference price plus a spread and a fee, filled by the platform from its book, a liquidity provider or inventory ([Crypto Trading Products](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/crypto-trading-products#the-simple-buy-and-convert)).
- **Slashing coverage:** a staking provider reimbursing customers for slashing penalties, rather than passing them through pro rata ([Staking as a Product](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/staking#slashing-pass-through)).
- **Sweep (crypto):** an on-chain transaction moving funds from deposit addresses into the exchange's hot or cold wallets; it changes where an asset is, not whose ([Deposits & Withdrawals](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/crypto-deposits-and-withdrawals#sweeping-and-gas-funding)).
- **Transfer-only:** the first live stage of a listing, with deposits and withdrawals open and no trading, so inventory arrives before the book forms ([Listing & Delisting Assets](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/listing-assets#the-launch-sequence)).
- **Treasury reconciliation:** the daily itemized check, per instrument at a block, that customer wallets on chain, adjusted for in-transit, staked and outside-venue items, equal customer balances in the ledger ([Hot, Warm & Cold Wallets](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/hot-and-cold-wallets#treasury-reconciliation)).
- **Unbonding period:** the time between a request to unstake and the assets becoming transferable, set by the chain and, on Ethereum, by the exit queue ([Staking as a Product](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/staking#unbonding-and-liquidity)).
- **Unified margin:** cross margin extended across collateral assets, each with a haircut, and sometimes across products; efficient until a weak collateral asset's price falls ([Perpetuals & Crypto Derivatives](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/perpetuals-and-crypto-derivatives#margin-modes)).
- **Velocity limit (hot wallet):** a ceiling on a wallet's outflow per window, enforced at the signer, that caps the loss from a compromised signing path ([Exchange Security & Hacks](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/exchange-security#key-and-signing-controls)).
- **Warm wallet:** a wallet signed by people approving on networked devices under quorum, sized to refill the hot wallet through a bad day ([Hot, Warm & Cold Wallets](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/hot-and-cold-wallets#the-tiers)).
- **White paper (MiCA):** the disclosure document a crypto-asset needs before admission to trading in the EU, notified to the home authority at least 20 working days before publication ([Listing & Delisting Assets](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/listing-assets#the-eu-admission-to-trading-under-mica)).
- **Withdraw-only:** the stage of a delisting after trading ends, when customers can only move the asset out before conversion or final disposition ([Listing & Delisting Assets](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/listing-assets#wind-down-mechanics)).
- **Withdrawability delay:** on Ethereum, the 256 epochs (about 27 hours) after a validator exits before its balance can be swept ([Staking as a Product](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/staking#getting-out-of-ethereum-worked)).

## Institutional Crypto

- **Admin quorum:** the minimum number of a custody client's administrators who must approve a sensitive change, such as adding a user or allowlisting an address ([Custody Platforms & Policy Engines](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/custody-platforms#governance-and-quorum-policies)).
- **ASU 2023-08:** the FASB standard (Subtopic 350-60) that measures in-scope crypto assets at fair value with changes in net income, adopted through a cumulative-effect adjustment to retained earnings ([Accounting for Crypto & Treasury Companies](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/crypto-accounting#asu-2023-08)).
- **Authorized participant (AP):** a broker-dealer with an agreement to create and redeem an ETP's shares in baskets; everyone else trades the shares on the exchange ([Crypto ETPs](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/crypto-etps#creation-and-redemption)).
- **Basket:** the fixed block of shares an ETP creates or redeems in one unit (40,000 shares for the iShares Bitcoin Trust), priced at NAV times the block ([Crypto ETPs](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/crypto-etps#creation-and-redemption)).
- **BTC Yield:** Strategy's KPI, the percentage change over a period in bitcoin per assumed diluted share ([Accounting for Crypto & Treasury Companies](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/crypto-accounting#digital-asset-treasury-companies)).
- **Cash creation:** an ETP creation in which the AP delivers cash, the trust buys the asset, and the AP pays any gap between the trust's execution price and the NAV price ([Crypto ETPs](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/crypto-etps#creation-and-redemption)).
- **Control (safekeeping):** the bank regulators' test for crypto custody: no other party, the customer included, has information sufficient to move the asset alone ([Qualified Custody](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/qualified-custody#bank-custody-after-sab-121)).
- **Custody (Advisers Act):** holding client funds or securities directly or indirectly, or having any authority to obtain possession of them, including authority to withdraw ([Qualified Custody](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/qualified-custody#the-custody-rule)).
- **Digital asset treasury company:** a listed company whose main asset is a crypto position funded by issuing stock, convertible notes and preferred stock ([Accounting for Crypto & Treasury Companies](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/crypto-accounting#digital-asset-treasury-companies)).
- **F/B/O titling:** titling a sub-custodian's account or wallet "for the benefit of" the custodian's customers, which NYDFS requires for customer assets in sub-custody ([Qualified Custody](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/qualified-custody#nydfs-trust-companies)).
- **Generic listing standards:** exchange rules approved in September 2025 that let a commodity-based trust meeting set tests list without its own 19(b) rule filing ([Crypto ETPs](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/crypto-etps#generic-listing-standards)).
- **Impairment model (crypto):** the pre-2025 treatment of crypto as indefinite-lived intangibles, written down to the lowest price since acquisition and never written back up ([Accounting for Crypto & Treasury Companies](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/crypto-accounting#the-old-model)).
- **In-kind creation:** an ETP creation in which the asset itself, not cash, is delivered for shares; allowed for US crypto ETPs since July 29, 2025 ([Crypto ETPs](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/crypto-etps#creation-and-redemption)).
- **Liquidation LTV:** the loan-to-value at which a crypto lender sells collateral without waiting for a margin call to be cured ([Prime Brokerage, OTC & Crypto Lending](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/crypto-prime-and-lending#institutional-credit-and-margin-lending)).
- **Loan-to-value (LTV, crypto loan):** a loan's accrued debt divided by its collateral's marked value, recomputed on every mark change ([Prime Brokerage, OTC & Crypto Lending](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/crypto-prime-and-lending#institutional-credit-and-margin-lending)).
- **mNAV:** what the market pays for a treasury company relative to the crypto it holds, on a market capitalization or enterprise value basis; definitions vary ([Accounting for Crypto & Treasury Companies](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/crypto-accounting#worked-numbers-mnav)).
- **NAV strike:** an ETP's once-daily net asset value, computed after 4 pm Eastern from a named benchmark rate, holdings and accrued liabilities ([Crypto ETPs](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/crypto-etps#nav-at-4-pm)).
- **Off-exchange settlement:** trading on an exchange against collateral that stays locked at a custodian, with net results moved between custody accounts each cycle ([Custody Platforms & Policy Engines](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/custody-platforms#off-exchange-settlement)).
- **Prime broker (crypto):** a firm that combines custody, multi-venue execution, settlement credit and financing for an institution ([Prime Brokerage, OTC & Crypto Lending](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/crypto-prime-and-lending#what-a-crypto-prime-broker-does)).
- **Qualified custodian:** an institution an adviser may use under the custody rule: a bank or savings association, a broker-dealer, an FCM for certain assets, or a qualifying foreign financial institution ([Qualified Custody](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/qualified-custody#the-custody-rule)).
- **Rehypothecation:** a lender reusing collateral pledged to it, by lending or repledging it; the large crypto lenders that survived 2022 disclaim it ([Prime Brokerage, OTC & Crypto Lending](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/crypto-prime-and-lending#tri-party-arrangements)).
- **SAB 121:** the SEC staff's 2022 bulletin that had safeguarding entities record a liability and asset at fair value for crypto held for others; rescinded in January 2025 ([Accounting for Crypto & Treasury Companies](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/crypto-accounting#sab-121-and-sab-122)).
- **SAB 122:** the January 2025 bulletin that rescinded SAB 121 and left the risk of loss on safeguarded crypto to the ordinary contingency test (ASC 450-20, IAS 37) ([Accounting for Crypto & Treasury Companies](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/crypto-accounting#sab-121-and-sab-122)).
- **Sub-custodian:** a custodian holding assets on another custodian's behalf; the primary custodian stays responsible and needs its records to reconcile ([Custody Platforms & Policy Engines](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/custody-platforms#sub-custody)).
- **Surprise examination:** the annual unannounced verification of client assets by an independent accountant that the custody rule requires ([Qualified Custody](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/qualified-custody#the-custody-rule)).
- **Trade Credit:** short-term credit a prime broker's lending affiliate gives an ETP to settle trades before its assets move; interest-free and due the next business day for the iShares Bitcoin Trust ([Crypto ETPs](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/crypto-etps#the-custody-stack)).
- **Trading Balance and Vault Balance:** an ETP's omnibus claim at its prime execution agent, used to trade, and its segregated cold-storage holdings, swept together daily ([Crypto ETPs](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/crypto-etps#the-custody-stack)).
- **Tri-party collateral:** collateral held by a custodian that is neither borrower nor lender, released to the lender only on a default notice under a control agreement ([Prime Brokerage, OTC & Crypto Lending](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/crypto-prime-and-lending#tri-party-arrangements)).

## Stablecoins

- **Access denied tokens:** Circle's term for USDC at addresses restricted to comply with a law, regulation or legal order; still a liability with reserves held in a segregated account, excluded from circulation ([Freezes, Blacklists & Seizures](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/freezes-and-onchain-compliance#the-contract-controls)).
- **Algorithmic stablecoin:** a token held at a dollar only by a promise to swap it for a dollar's worth of a sister token the protocol mints, with no reserve behind it; Terra's UST, which collapsed in May 2022 ([Depegs, Runs & Synthetic Dollars](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/depegs-and-synthetic-dollars#terra-s-ust)).
- **Asset-referenced token (ART):** MiCA's term for a stablecoin that references anything other than one official currency, such as a basket, a commodity or other crypto-assets ([Stablecoin Law](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/stablecoin-law#the-eu-mica)).
- **Attestation (stablecoin):** an accountant's examination of an issuer's assertions about its reserve and tokens outstanding at the dates tested, narrower than an audit of financial statements ([Issuing a Stablecoin](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/issuing-a-stablecoin#attestations-and-reports)).
- **Blacklist (token):** a contract function that stops an address from sending (and in USDC, receiving) a token; `blacklist` in FiatToken, `addBlackList` in Tether's contract ([Freezes, Blacklists & Seizures](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/freezes-and-onchain-compliance#the-contract-controls)).
- **Break the buck:** a stablecoin trading below its dollar peg; the New York Fed's staff estimate puts the threshold at which redemptions accelerate at $1 ([Depegs, Runs & Synthetic Dollars](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/depegs-and-synthetic-dollars#run-dynamics)).
- **Burn:** destroying stablecoin tokens on redemption or cross-chain transfer, reducing supply on that chain; in FiatToken only a minter can burn, and burning doesn't restore its allowance ([Issuing a Stablecoin](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/issuing-a-stablecoin#redemption-operations)).
- **CCTP (Cross-Chain Transfer Protocol):** Circle's burn-and-mint protocol for moving USDC between chains: burn on the source, attestation by Circle's Iris service, mint on the destination ([Issuing a Stablecoin](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/issuing-a-stablecoin#cross-chain-transfers-cctp)).
- **Depeg:** a stablecoin's market price moving away from its reference value; a reserve run, a design failure or a single venue's mispricing ([Depegs, Runs & Synthetic Dollars](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/depegs-and-synthetic-dollars)).
- **Destroy and reissue:** burning a frozen balance and minting the same amount to a victim or the government, the way Tether returns and forfeits USDT ([Freezes, Blacklists & Seizures](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/freezes-and-onchain-compliance#seizure-burn-and-reissue)).
- **Distribution agreement:** a stablecoin issuer's contract to share reserve income with an exchange, wallet or other distributor, on balances it holds or more broadly; Circle's with Coinbase and Binance ([The Stablecoin Business](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/stablecoin-business#distribution-costs)).
- **Distribution costs:** what a stablecoin issuer pays exchanges and other partners out of its reserve income for holding and distributing its token; Circle's 2025 distribution and transaction costs were $1,661.5 million, 63% of its reserve income and about 60% of its total revenue and reserve income ([The Stablecoin Business](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/stablecoin-business#distribution-costs)).
- **Fast Transfer:** CCTP's mode that attests before source-chain finality, in seconds for a fee, backed by Circle's Fast Transfer allowance; Standard Transfer waits for finality ([Issuing a Stablecoin](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/issuing-a-stablecoin#cross-chain-transfers-cctp)).
- **Federal qualified payment stablecoin issuer:** a nonbank, uninsured national bank or federal branch approved by the OCC to issue payment stablecoins and supervised exclusively by it ([Stablecoin Law](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/stablecoin-law#who-may-issue)).
- **Issuer portion:** the share of net reserve income a stablecoin issuer takes before splitting the rest with distributors under an agreement such as Circle's with Coinbase ([The Stablecoin Business](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/stablecoin-business#the-coinbase-agreement)).
- **Lawful order (GENIUS Act):** a final federal court or agency order requiring an issuer to seize, freeze, burn or prevent transfer of its payment stablecoins, naming them with reasonable particularity and subject to review ([Freezes, Blacklists & Seizures](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/freezes-and-onchain-compliance#what-the-genius-act-requires)).
- **Master minter:** the token contract role that authorizes minters and sets each one's allowance (`configureMinter`, `removeMinter`) ([Issuing a Stablecoin](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/issuing-a-stablecoin#minter-allowances)).
- **Mint:** creating stablecoin tokens against dollars received and matched, increasing the issuer's supply and liability ([Issuing a Stablecoin](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/issuing-a-stablecoin#mint-on-deposit)).
- **Minter allowance:** the cap on how many tokens a minter may create, reduced by each mint, not restored by burning and reset by the master minter, so a compromised minter key is bounded ([Issuing a Stablecoin](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/issuing-a-stablecoin#minter-allowances)).
- **On-platform balance:** stablecoins held in a distributor's or the issuer's own custody, which earn that party the income on them under a distribution agreement ([The Stablecoin Business](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/stablecoin-business#the-coinbase-agreement)).
- **Operational backstop:** liquid assets equal to 12 months of an issuer's total expenses, held apart from the reserve under the OCC's GENIUS rule ([Stablecoin Law](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/stablecoin-law#capital-and-the-operational-backstop)).
- **Pause (token):** a contract function that halts every transfer, mint and burn at once, leaving blacklisting, role changes and upgrades available ([Freezes, Blacklists & Seizures](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/freezes-and-onchain-compliance#usdc-blacklist-and-pause)).
- **Payment stablecoin reserve:** the assets backing a payment stablecoin at least one to one under the GENIUS Act: currency, Fed balances, insured deposits, Treasuries of 93 days or less, overnight Treasury repo, government money market funds and tokenized forms of them ([Issuing a Stablecoin](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/issuing-a-stablecoin#the-reserve)).
- **Peg stability module (PSM):** a contract that swaps a crypto-collateralized stablecoin one to one with another stablecoin, such as Sky's LitePSM with USDC ([Depegs, Runs & Synthetic Dollars](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/depegs-and-synthetic-dollars#crypto-collateralized-dai-and-usds)).
- **Permitted payment stablecoin issuer:** an issuer allowed to issue payment stablecoins in the US: a subsidiary of an insured depository institution, a federal qualified issuer or a state qualified issuer ([Stablecoin Law](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/stablecoin-law#who-may-issue)).
- **Redemption:** exchanging stablecoins for dollars with the issuer at par; the OCC's final rule makes timely no later than two business days after a request from an onboarded customer ([Issuing a Stablecoin](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/issuing-a-stablecoin#redemption-operations)).
- **Reserve income:** the yield a stablecoin issuer earns on the assets backing its tokens, which it may not pass to holders as interest ([The Stablecoin Business](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/stablecoin-business#how-reserve-income-is-earned)).
- **Reserve return rate:** an issuer's reserve income divided by the simple daily average of reserves held for holders; Circle's was 4.1% for 2025 ([The Stablecoin Business](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/stablecoin-business#how-reserve-income-is-earned)).
- **RLDC (revenue less distribution costs):** Circle's measure of total revenue and reserve income minus distribution, transaction and other costs, 39% of revenue in 2025 ([The Stablecoin Business](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/stablecoin-business#distribution-costs)).
- **Significant e-money token:** an EMT classified as significant under MiCA, whose issuer takes on reserve, custody and own funds rules from the ART regime and a six-monthly reserve audit ([Stablecoin Law](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/stablecoin-law#the-eu-mica)).
- **State qualified payment stablecoin issuer:** an issuer with $10 billion or less outstanding regulated under a state regime certified as substantially similar to the federal one ([Stablecoin Law](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/stablecoin-law#the-state-path-and-the-10-billion-line)).
- **Supply by network:** a stablecoin's total supply read from its contract on each chain at a recorded block, summed, net of issuer inventory and plus burns in flight, for reconciliation with the liability ([Issuing a Stablecoin](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/issuing-a-stablecoin#supply-across-chains)).
- **Synthetic dollar:** a token held near a dollar by crypto assets hedged with short futures of the same size rather than by a cash reserve; Ethena's USDe ([Depegs, Runs & Synthetic Dollars](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/depegs-and-synthetic-dollars#synthetic-dollars-ethena-s-usde)).
- **Tokenized deposit:** a bank deposit represented on a blockchain, legally still a deposit and eligible for deposit insurance; JPMorgan's JPMD ([Depegs, Runs & Synthetic Dollars](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/depegs-and-synthetic-dollars#tokenized-deposits)).
- **Vault (Sky):** a position that locks collateral and mints DAI or USDS against it up to a collateralization ratio, liquidated by Dutch auction below it ([Depegs, Runs & Synthetic Dollars](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/depegs-and-synthetic-dollars#crypto-collateralized-dai-and-usds)).
- **Yield ban (GENIUS Act):** the prohibition on a permitted or foreign issuer paying holders interest or yield solely for holding, using or retaining a payment stablecoin ([Stablecoin Law](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/stablecoin-law#the-yield-ban)).

## Decentralized Finance

- **Aggregator (DEX):** a service that reads many pools and liquidity sources, splits a trade across routes and returns one transaction with an expected and minimum output, such as 0x or LI.FI ([DEXs & AMMs](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/dexs-and-amms#aggregators-and-routers)).
- **Allowance target:** the contract an aggregator's quote says the user must approve to spend the sell token, pinned per provider and chain because a wrong one is a drain ([DEXs & AMMs](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/dexs-and-amms#aggregators-and-routers)).
- **Automated market maker (AMM):** a contract that holds two tokens and prices trades against them by formula, with no order book or operator ([DEXs & AMMs](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/dexs-and-amms#the-constant-product-formula)).
- **Bad debt (DeFi):** loan value in a lending pool that no collateral covers, left when a price gaps or collateral can't be sold before liquidation ([DeFi Lending & Liquidations](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/defi-lending#bad-debt)).
- **Close factor:** the share of a borrower's debt a liquidator may repay in one call; 50% in Aave V3 above a health factor of 0.95, 100% at or below it ([DeFi Lending & Liquidations](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/defi-lending#the-rules-in-aave-v3)).
- **Concentrated liquidity:** liquidity provided on a chosen price range, active and earning fees only while the price is inside it, as in Uniswap v3 ([DEXs & AMMs](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/dexs-and-amms#concentrated-liquidity)).
- **Constant product formula:** the AMM rule x × y = k, under which the product of a pool's reserves can't fall, so each unit bought costs more than the last ([DEXs & AMMs](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/dexs-and-amms#the-constant-product-formula)).
- **DeFi broker rule:** T.D. 10021 (December 2024), which would have made trading front-end service providers report gross proceeds; disapproved by Public Law 119-5 on April 10, 2025 and removed from the CFR ([DeFi from a Regulated Platform](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/defi-from-a-regulated-platform#the-defi-broker-rule-and-its-repeal)).
- **Flash loan:** a loan borrowed and repaid inside one transaction, so it needs no collateral; used by liquidators and in governance and oracle attacks ([DeFi Lending & Liquidations](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/defi-lending#bots-and-the-race)).
- **Formal verification:** machine-checked proof that contract code satisfies written properties for every execution, limited by which properties were written ([Smart Contract Risk & Exploits](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/smart-contract-risk#formal-verification)).
- **Health factor:** a borrower's collateral value weighted by liquidation thresholds, divided by debt; below 1.0 the position can be liquidated ([DeFi Lending & Liquidations](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/defi-lending#the-health-factor)).
- **Hook (Uniswap v4):** a contract a v4 pool names at creation that runs custom code around swaps and liquidity changes, with its permissions encoded in its address's low bits ([DEXs & AMMs](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/dexs-and-amms#hooks-and-the-v4-singleton)).
- **Impermanent loss:** what an AMM liquidity provider loses against simply holding the deposited tokens when the price moves; 2√r / (1 + r) − 1 for a price ratio r, 5.72% at a 2x move ([DEXs & AMMs](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/dexs-and-amms#impermanent-loss)).
- **Intent:** a signed order stating what a user wants, settled later by competing fillers or solvers instead of submitted as a transaction, as in UniswapX and CoW Protocol ([DEXs & AMMs](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/dexs-and-amms#intents-and-batch-auctions)).
- **Liquidation bonus:** the extra collateral, as a percentage of the debt repaid, a liquidator receives for repaying an unhealthy loan; part may go to the protocol as a fee ([DeFi Lending & Liquidations](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/defi-lending#worked-numbers-a-liquidation-with-bonus)).
- **Liquidation threshold:** the share of a collateral asset's value counted toward the health factor, set above the borrowing limit (LTV) to leave a buffer ([DeFi Lending & Liquidations](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/defi-lending#two-thresholds)).
- **Liquidity provider (LP):** someone who deposits tokens into a pool to earn its trading fees, carrying impermanent loss ([DEXs & AMMs](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/dexs-and-amms#impermanent-loss)).
- **Loan-to-value (LTV, DeFi):** the share of a collateral asset's value an account may borrow against it ([DeFi Lending & Liquidations](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/defi-lending#two-thresholds)).
- **Maximal extractable value (MEV):** the value that can be extracted from block production by including, excluding or reordering transactions ([Oracles & MEV](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/oracles-and-mev#what-it-is)).
- **MEV-Boost:** validator middleware that sources blocks from external builders through relays, an out-of-protocol form of proposer-builder separation ([Oracles & MEV](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/oracles-and-mev#flashbots-mev-boost-and-pbs)).
- **Oracle:** a contract that publishes off-chain data, usually prices, on chain for other contracts to read ([Oracles & MEV](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/oracles-and-mev#price-oracles)).
- **Oracle manipulation:** moving the price an oracle reports, for long enough that a protocol lends, liquidates or settles against it ([Oracles & MEV](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/oracles-and-mev#oracle-manipulation)).
- **Price impact:** how much a trade moves a pool's price by its own size, computed from the pool's state at quote time ([DEXs & AMMs](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/dexs-and-amms#price-impact-and-slippage)).
- **Private order flow:** transactions sent to builders through private channels instead of the public mempool, to avoid sandwiches and sometimes earn refunds ([Oracles & MEV](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/oracles-and-mev#private-order-flow)).
- **Proposer-builder separation (PBS):** splitting block building from block proposing, today through MEV-Boost and proposed in-protocol by EIP-7732 ([Oracles & MEV](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/oracles-and-mev#flashbots-mev-boost-and-pbs)).
- **Safe Harbor (SEAL):** an on-chain agreement under which a protocol pre-authorizes white hats to rescue funds during an exploit for a bounty of 10% capped at $1 million ([Smart Contract Risk & Exploits](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/smart-contract-risk#incident-response-and-white-hat-recoveries)).
- **Sandwich attack:** buying just before a victim's swap and selling just after, so the victim trades at a price the attacker moved, up to its slippage limit ([Oracles & MEV](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/oracles-and-mev#sandwiches)).
- **Slippage:** the difference between a swap's quoted output and what it actually gets when it lands; the slippage tolerance is the minimum output the transaction enforces ([DEXs & AMMs](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/dexs-and-amms#price-impact-and-slippage)).
- **Timelock:** a contract that delays execution of a governance decision, giving users notice before code or parameters change ([Smart Contract Risk & Exploits](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/smart-contract-risk#governance-and-timelocks)).
- **TWAP (time-weighted average price):** a price averaged over a window from a pool's accumulator; in Uniswap v3 a geometric mean of the tick ([Oracles & MEV](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/oracles-and-mev#time-weighted-prices-from-pools)).
- **Utilization:** the share of a lending pool's supplied funds that is borrowed, which sets its interest rates along a kinked curve ([DeFi Lending & Liquidations](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/defi-lending#interest-rate-curves)).

## Tokenization

- **Accumulating NAV:** a tokenized fund's income model in which no units are distributed and the price per token rises with income less fees, as at Superstate's USTB ([Tokenized Treasuries & Funds](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/tokenized-funds#nav-and-income-on-chain)).
- **Agent (ERC-3643):** an address the token's owner appoints to write the identity registry and run operational functions such as freezes, forced transfers and recovery ([Transfer Agents & Permissioned Tokens](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/transfer-agents-and-permissioned-tokens#erc-3643-and-allowlisted-transfers)).
- **Allowlist:** the on-chain list of addresses an issuer or transfer agent has approved to hold a permissioned token; a transfer to any other address reverts ([Tokenized Treasuries & Funds](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/tokenized-funds#allowlists)).
- **Backed wrapper:** a stock token issued by a special-purpose issuer that holds the underlying shares and owes their value to token holders, without votes or a legal claim on the shares, such as xStocks ([Tokenized Stocks & 24/7 Securities](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/tokenized-stocks#four-kinds-of-stock-token)).
- **Control book:** the transfer agent's record of the authorized and issued count of each issue, changed only on the issuer's written authorization, that the holder file must sum to (Rule 17Ad-10(e)) ([Transfer Agents & Permissioned Tokens](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/transfer-agents-and-permissioned-tokens#the-sec-s-transfer-agent-rules)).
- **Covered Firm (tokenized NMS stock exemption):** a liquidity provider supplying tokenized NMS stock to a Tokenized Securities Venue's pools from proprietary capital, exempt from the dealer definition under the SEC's September 2026 order ([Tokenized Stocks & 24/7 Securities](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/tokenized-stocks#the-us-rules-as-of-november-2026)).
- **DTCC Tokenization Service:** DTC's service, live October 28, 2026, that mints tokenized forms of securities held at DTC with one CUSIP, movable between DTC-registered wallets ([Tokenized Stocks & 24/7 Securities](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/tokenized-stocks#the-us-rules-as-of-november-2026)).
- **ERC-3643 (T-REX):** a final Ethereum token standard for permissioned securities with an identity registry, a compliance contract checked on every transfer, freezes, forced transfers and wallet recovery ([Transfer Agents & Permissioned Tokens](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/transfer-agents-and-permissioned-tokens#erc-3643-and-allowlisted-transfers)).
- **Forced transfer:** an agent's transfer of tokens between holders without the sender's signature, for a court order, estate or correction; under ERC-3643 it needs a verified receiver and bypasses compliance rules ([Transfer Agents & Permissioned Tokens](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/transfer-agents-and-permissioned-tokens#forced-transfers-and-recovery)).
- **Identity registry:** the ERC-3643 contract linking each wallet to an investor identity and country, whose `isVerified` check gates who can receive the token ([Transfer Agents & Permissioned Tokens](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/transfer-agents-and-permissioned-tokens#erc-3643-and-allowlisted-transfers)).
- **Issuer-native token:** a company's or fund's own security issued and recorded on chain by its transfer agent, carrying the security's full rights ([Tokenized Stocks & 24/7 Securities](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/tokenized-stocks#four-kinds-of-stock-token)).
- **Master securityholder file:** the transfer agent's official list of holders and their positions, posted promptly with every transfer, issuance and redemption under Rule 17Ad-10 ([Transfer Agents & Permissioned Tokens](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/transfer-agents-and-permissioned-tokens#the-sec-s-transfer-agent-rules)).
- **Multiplier (stock token):** the factor of shares per token a wrapper issuer publishes and changes on dividends and splits, applied to balances on EVM chains and to displayed amounts on Solana ([Tokenized Stocks & 24/7 Securities](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/tokenized-stocks#corporate-actions-through-a-wrapper)).
- **Recovery (token):** an agent moving a holder's tokens from a lost wallet to a new wallet of the same verified investor, an ownership non-event; `recoveryAddress` in ERC-3643 ([Transfer Agents & Permissioned Tokens](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/transfer-agents-and-permissioned-tokens#forced-transfers-and-recovery)).
- **Stock token:** any token whose value follows a listed share: issuer-native, a tokenized DTC entitlement, a backed wrapper or a derivative, each with different rights ([Tokenized Stocks & 24/7 Securities](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/tokenized-stocks#four-kinds-of-stock-token)).
- **Tokenized fund:** a fund whose shares are recorded or represented as tokens on a public chain, with eligibility enforced by an allowlist, such as BUIDL or FOBXX ([Tokenized Treasuries & Funds](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/tokenized-funds#what-a-tokenized-fund-is)).
- **Tokenized NMS stock:** under the SEC's September 2026 order, an NMS stock tokenized by or for its issuer or by an unaffiliated third party with the class's full rights, excluding synthetic tokens ([Tokenized Stocks & 24/7 Securities](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/tokenized-stocks#the-us-rules-as-of-november-2026)).
- **Tokenized Securities Venue:** a venue running AMM pools for permissioned participants in tokenized NMS stock on a public chain, exempt from the exchange definition from September 17, 2026 to September 17, 2031 ([Tokenized Stocks & 24/7 Securities](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/tokenized-stocks#the-us-rules-as-of-november-2026)).
- **Transfer agent:** the SEC-registered firm that keeps a security's official holder record, processes transfers, issuances and redemptions, and pays holders of record ([Transfer Agents & Permissioned Tokens](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/transfer-agents-and-permissioned-tokens#the-sec-s-transfer-agent-rules)).

## The Rules

- **Accredited investor (Singapore, crypto):** a customer outside most of MAS's retail crypto restrictions, tested on net personal assets, net financial assets or income, with digital payment tokens counted after at least a 50% haircut and capped at S$200,000 ([Beyond the US](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/beyond-the-us#singapore)).
- **Airdrop (tax):** new units received without payment, ordinary income at fair market value when the holder gains dominion and control, with that value as basis (Rev. Rul. 2019-24) ([Crypto Tax](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/crypto-tax#airdrops-and-forks)).
- **Anonymizing services provider:** in FinCEN's 2019 guidance, a mixer that accepts and retransmits value and is therefore a money transmitter, unlike a supplier of anonymizing software ([Licensing a Crypto Business](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/crypto-licensing#fincen-who-is-a-money-services-business)).
- **Attribution label:** a name or category attached to an address or cluster, with its source, date and confidence ([On-Chain Analytics & Investigations](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/blockchain-analytics#attribution)).
- **BitLicense:** New York's license for virtual currency business activity with New York or New Yorkers under 23 NYCRR Part 200; a New York limited purpose trust charter is the alternative ([Licensing a Crypto Business](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/crypto-licensing#new-york-the-bitlicense)).
- **CASP (crypto-asset service provider):** a firm authorized under MiCA to provide one or more of the ten crypto-asset services in the EU, passportable across the EEA ([Licensing a Crypto Business](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/crypto-licensing#the-eu-mica-casp-authorization)).
- **Change address:** the output of a UTXO transaction that returns the unspent remainder to the sender, which analytics tries to identify to grow the sender's cluster ([On-Chain Analytics & Investigations](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/blockchain-analytics#change-addresses)).
- **CLARITY Act:** the House-passed 2025 market structure bill that would have given the CFTC supervision of digital commodity spot markets; stalled after a failed Senate cloture vote on September 15, 2026 ([Securities, Commodities & Crypto Market Structure](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/securities-or-commodities#congress-the-clarity-act)).
- **Clustering:** grouping addresses one party controls, by heuristics such as common-input ownership and change detection ([On-Chain Analytics & Investigations](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/blockchain-analytics#clustering)).
- **CoinJoin:** a transaction combining many users' inputs into equal-value outputs, built to break common-input clustering ([On-Chain Analytics & Investigations](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/blockchain-analytics#worked-example-a-small-graph)).
- **Common-input ownership:** the heuristic that addresses spent together as inputs to one transaction are controlled by one party ([On-Chain Analytics & Investigations](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/blockchain-analytics#common-input-ownership)).
- **DFAL (Digital Financial Assets Law):** California's Digital Financial Assets Law, requiring a DFPI license for digital financial asset business with California residents from July 1, 2026 ([Licensing a Crypto Business](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/crypto-licensing#california-the-dfal)).
- **Digital commodity:** in the SEC's 2026 taxonomy, a crypto asset whose value comes from the programmatic operation of a functional crypto system and that is not itself a security ([Securities, Commodities & Crypto Market Structure](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/securities-or-commodities#the-2026-taxonomy)).
- **Digital security:** in the SEC's 2026 taxonomy, a tokenized financial instrument, a security whether on chain or off ([Securities, Commodities & Crypto Market Structure](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/securities-or-commodities#the-2026-taxonomy)).
- **Disposal (crypto tax):** giving up units for dollars, goods, services or another digital asset, including a swap or a fee paid in crypto, which realizes gain or loss ([Crypto Tax](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/crypto-tax#disposals)).
- **Dominion and control:** the point at which a taxpayer can sell, transfer or otherwise dispose of received units, which sets the time and value of staking and airdrop income ([Crypto Tax](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/crypto-tax#income-at-receipt)).
- **Exposure (direct, indirect):** the share of a transfer's value that comes from, or traces back within some number of hops to, a labeled category of counterparty ([On-Chain Analytics & Investigations](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/blockchain-analytics#exposure-and-risk-scores)).
- **Hosted wallet provider:** in FinCEN's 2019 guidance, an account-based money transmitter that receives, stores and transmits crypto for accountholders ([Licensing a Crypto Business](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/crypto-licensing#fincen-who-is-a-money-services-business)).
- **Howey test:** the Supreme Court's 1946 definition of an investment contract: an investment of money in a common enterprise with an expectation of profits from the efforts of others ([Securities, Commodities & Crypto Market Structure](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/securities-or-commodities#the-test)).
- **Income event:** a receipt of units as income (staking reward, airdrop, mining, lending interest) recorded with its time and value, which opens a tax lot at that value ([Crypto Tax](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/crypto-tax#how-to-build-it)).
- **Investment contract separation:** in the SEC's 2026 interpretation, the point at which a non-security crypto asset stops being subject to an investment contract, by fulfillment or public abandonment of the issuer's promised efforts ([Securities, Commodities & Crypto Market Structure](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/securities-or-commodities#the-2026-taxonomy)).
- **IVMS101:** the interVASP Messaging Standard data model for originator and beneficiary information carried by Travel Rule protocols ([AML & the Travel Rule for Crypto](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/crypto-aml-and-travel-rule#how-the-platforms-do-it)).
- **Jarrett v. United States:** the Tezos staking refund suit the Sixth Circuit dismissed as moot in August 2023 after the IRS paid the refund, leaving the taxation of created tokens undecided ([Crypto Tax](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/crypto-tax#jarrett)).
- **KYT (know your transaction):** screening a crypto transfer's source or destination for exposure to risky categories, before withdrawals, on deposits and again as labels change ([On-Chain Analytics & Investigations](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/blockchain-analytics#kyt-on-deposits-and-withdrawals)).
- **MiCA passport:** a CASP's notification to its home authority to serve another member state, effective from the authority's confirmation or the 15th calendar day after notifying ([Licensing a Crypto Business](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/crypto-licensing#the-eu-mica-casp-authorization)).
- **Money Transmission Modernization Act (MTMA):** CSBS's model law setting one standard for state money transmitter net worth, bonds and permissible investments, enacted in full or part by 31 states ([Licensing a Crypto Business](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/crypto-licensing#state-money-transmission-and-the-mtma)).
- **Notice 2014-21:** the IRS notice treating virtual currency as property, with income at fair market value on receipt and gain or loss on exchange ([Crypto Tax](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/crypto-tax#property-not-currency)).
- **One-time change address:** an output appearing on chain for the first time, the only such output in a transaction without self-change, which the change heuristic attributes to the sender ([On-Chain Analytics & Investigations](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/blockchain-analytics#change-addresses)).
- **PFOF ban (EU):** MiFIR Article 39a's prohibition on investment firms receiving payment for routing retail orders, with national exemptions ended June 30, 2026 ([Beyond the US](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/beyond-the-us#mifid-ii-for-brokers)).
- **Programmatic sales:** in *SEC v. Ripple*, the issuer's blind bid/ask sales on exchanges, held not to be investment contract offers ([Securities, Commodities & Crypto Market Structure](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/securities-or-commodities#ripple-2020-to-2025)).
- **Regulation Crypto Assets:** the SEC's August 2026 proposal of startup and fundraising exemptions and a safe harbor for investment contracts involving crypto assets ([Securities, Commodities & Crypto Market Structure](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/securities-or-commodities#regulation-crypto-assets-proposed)).
- **Retail commodity transaction:** a leveraged, margined or financed commodity transaction with a non-eligible contract participant, treated as a futures contract unless actual delivery occurs within 28 days ([Securities, Commodities & Crypto Market Structure](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/securities-or-commodities#the-cftc-and-the-spot-market)).
- **Rev. Rul. 2023-14:** the IRS ruling that staking rewards are income at fair market value when the taxpayer gains dominion and control, including staking through an exchange ([Crypto Tax](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/crypto-tax#staking)).
- **Sunrise problem:** the gap when a counterparty VASP sits in a jurisdiction that hasn't implemented the Travel Rule and can't send or receive compliant messages ([AML & the Travel Rule for Crypto](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/crypto-aml-and-travel-rule#counterparty-vasps)).
- **Travel Address:** in TRP, a base58check-encoded URL with a "ta" prefix that tells the originating VASP where to send a transfer inquiry ([AML & the Travel Rule for Crypto](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/crypto-aml-and-travel-rule#worked-example-a-5-000-withdrawal-two-destinations)).
- **Travel Rule (crypto):** FinCEN's funds transfer rule applied to crypto transmittals of $3,000 or more, met by sending originator and beneficiary data to the counterparty VASP out of band ([AML & the Travel Rule for Crypto](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/crypto-aml-and-travel-rule#the-rule)).
- **TRISA:** an open-source Travel Rule protocol using gRPC, a certificate-issuing directory and mutual TLS ([AML & the Travel Rule for Crypto](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/crypto-aml-and-travel-rule#how-the-platforms-do-it)).
- **TRP (Travel Rule Protocol):** the OpenVASP Association's open protocol of inquiry, resolution and confirmation calls between VASPs over mutual TLS ([AML & the Travel Rule for Crypto](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/crypto-aml-and-travel-rule#how-the-platforms-do-it)).
- **Unhosted wallet:** a self-custody wallet with no institution behind it; a transfer to one has no counterparty to receive Travel Rule data ([AML & the Travel Rule for Crypto](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/crypto-aml-and-travel-rule#worked-example-a-5-000-withdrawal-two-destinations)).
- **VARA:** Dubai's Virtual Assets Regulatory Authority, which licenses virtual asset activities by category ([Beyond the US](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/beyond-the-us#the-uae-dubai-s-vara)).
- **VASP (virtual asset service provider):** FATF's term for a business that exchanges, transfers or safekeeps virtual assets for others, the counterparty a Travel Rule message goes to ([AML & the Travel Rule for Crypto](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/crypto-aml-and-travel-rule#counterparty-vasps)).

## Running the Platform

- **Account shard:** one slice of a broker's or exchange's accounts with its own application servers, database and deploy pipeline, so a failure or a release touches only that slice ([Trading Platform Architecture](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/trading-platform-architecture#sharding-by-account)).
- **Account shard mapping:** the stored record of which shard holds an account, read by the router and cached, so an account can move without rehashing every other ([Trading Platform Architecture](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/trading-platform-architecture#sharding-by-account)).
- **Active brokerage account:** Schwab's count of brokerage accounts with activity in the preceding 270 days ([Investing & Digital Assets Metrics](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/investing-metrics#how-the-companies-report)).
- **Agent mandate:** the customer's stored authorization for a software agent trading an account: instruments, order types, per-order and daily limits, hours and expiry, checked by the platform on every order ([AI in Investing](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/ai-in-investing#agents-that-trade-and-hold-wallets)).
- **AI washing:** claiming in marketing or filings to use AI that a firm doesn't use as described; the SEC's 2024 actions against Delphia and Global Predictions ([AI in Investing](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/ai-in-investing#ai-washing)).
- **ARPU (broker):** total revenue over the average of funded customers at the end of the period and the prior period, annualized in quarterly releases ([Investing & Digital Assets Metrics](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/investing-metrics#revenue-per-customer)).
- **Brokerage conversion:** moving accounts in bulk from one platform or firm to another without each customer's transfer instruction, in batches with a freeze, load, reconciliation and go decision ([Conversions, Shutdowns & Distributions](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/conversions-and-shutdowns#brokerage-conversions)).
- **Business continuity plan (FINRA 4370):** a broker-dealer's written plan for meeting obligations to customers through a significant business disruption, covering at least ten listed areas including mission critical systems and alternate customer communications ([Peak Days, Outages & Resilience](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/peak-days-and-outages#finra-rule-4370-the-continuity-plan)).
- **Control total:** a sum or count taken from the source and the target of a migration at one level (accounts, positions, shares, cash, lots, adjusted basis) and compared before going live ([Conversions, Shutdowns & Distributions](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/conversions-and-shutdowns#worked-numbers-reconciling-one-batch)).
- **Core net new assets:** Schwab's net new client assets before significant one-time flows such as acquisitions ([Investing & Digital Assets Metrics](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/investing-metrics#flows)).
- **Covered technology:** in the SEC's withdrawn 2023 proposal, any analytical function that optimizes for, predicts, guides, forecasts or directs investment-related behaviors or outcomes in an investor interaction ([AI in Investing](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/ai-in-investing#the-withdrawn-predictive-data-analytics-proposal)).
- **DARTs:** daily average revenue trades, customer orders that generate revenue per trading day, as Interactive Brokers reports them ([Investing & Digital Assets Metrics](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/investing-metrics#trading-activity-and-take-rate)).
- **Degraded mode:** a deliberate, scoped state (cancel-only, close-only, read-only, withdrawals paused, a feature off) that the platform enters during an incident, with a runbook and a customer message ([Peak Days, Outages & Resilience](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/peak-days-and-outages#load-shedding-and-degraded-modes)).
- **Device binding:** a record that a customer proved a device with a passkey or device key, used as evidence that later instructions from it come from the customer ([Account Security & Fraud](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/account-security#device-binding-and-step-up)).
- **Distribution agent:** a firm a bankruptcy estate uses to pay creditors in cash or crypto, such as PayPal and Coinbase in the Celsius case ([Conversions, Shutdowns & Distributions](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/conversions-and-shutdowns#bankruptcies-and-distributions)).
- **Entity partition:** the region, database cluster and shards that hold one legal entity's data, recorded as a row so the entity can be moved, isolated or wound down ([Trading Platform Architecture](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/trading-platform-architecture#multi-entity-design)).
- **Escheatment:** turning unclaimed money over to the state after a dormancy period ([Conversions, Shutdowns & Distributions](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/conversions-and-shutdowns#unclaimed-property)).
- **Funded customer:** Robinhood's count of unique people with an account at any of its entities that had a positive balance (excluding firm-granted promotions) or a transaction in the past 45 days ([Investing & Digital Assets Metrics](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/investing-metrics#customers-and-accounts)).
- **Indication of interest (unclaimed property):** an owner's act that restarts the dormancy clock, such as a communication about the property or owner-directed activity in the account, as Delaware's law lists them ([Conversions, Shutdowns & Distributions](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/conversions-and-shutdowns#unclaimed-property)).
- **Invariant check:** a stored query that must return no rows (an entry that doesn't balance, a ledger-to-chain difference, a negative balance without credit), run on a schedule with its result kept ([Trading Platform Architecture](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/trading-platform-architecture#invariants-checked-continuously)).
- **Market exit:** a platform stopping service to a country or state, run as a sequence of states from notice to close-only, withdraw-only and final disposition ([Conversions, Shutdowns & Distributions](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/conversions-and-shutdowns#exiting-a-market)).
- **Meaningful wallet:** Circle's count of on-chain wallets holding at least $10 of USDC ([Investing & Digital Assets Metrics](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/investing-metrics#stablecoin-supply-and-reserve-income)).
- **Mission critical system:** under FINRA 4370, any system needed for prompt and accurate processing of securities transactions, from order taking and execution to access to customer accounts and delivery of funds and securities ([Peak Days, Outages & Resilience](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/peak-days-and-outages#finra-rule-4370-the-continuity-plan)).
- **Monthly transacting user (MTU):** Coinbase's count of consumers who actively or passively transacted at least once in a rolling 28 days ([Investing & Digital Assets Metrics](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/investing-metrics#how-the-companies-report)).
- **Negative response letter:** a notice telling customers their accounts will move in a bulk transfer unless they object within at least 30 days, accepted by FINRA in listed circumstances ([Conversions, Shutdowns & Distributions](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/conversions-and-shutdowns#brokerage-conversions)).
- **Net deposits:** money and assets customers brought in, plus dividends, interest, staking rewards and promotions, less withdrawals, transfers out, interest and subscription fees charged; distinct from performance external flows ([Investing & Digital Assets Metrics](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/investing-metrics#flows)).
- **Outage compensation:** a payment to a customer for a trade an outage prevented, computed from a logged attempt, the price at the attempt and the first reasonable opportunity after recovery ([Peak Days, Outages & Resilience](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/peak-days-and-outages#paying-for-outages)).
- **Posting service:** the one writer of journal entries, holds and positions; every other service asks it to move money or assets ([Trading Platform Architecture](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/trading-platform-architecture#the-services)).
- **Restitution payment:** money or assets a firm pays a customer for a loss outside a platform security incident, by firm error, voluntary policy, a regulator's order, an award or a settlement ([Account Security & Fraud](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/account-security#restitution)).
- **SCI entity:** a self-regulatory organization, an ATS over Regulation SCI's volume thresholds, a plan processor, certain exempt clearing agencies or a competing consolidator, subject to the SEC's systems resilience rule; not a broker-dealer as such ([Peak Days, Outages & Resilience](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/peak-days-and-outages#regulation-sci)).
- **SCI event:** a systems disruption, systems compliance issue or systems intrusion at an SCI entity, with corrective action and notice to the SEC immediately and in writing within 24 hours ([Peak Days, Outages & Resilience](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/peak-days-and-outages#regulation-sci)).
- **Security change hold:** a withdrawal hold placed when value is sent to a destination added after a password, contact, 2FA or device change, with notice to the contact points that existed before the change ([Account Security & Fraud](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/account-security#withdrawal-holds-and-allowlists)).
- **Sequenced input:** an order, fill, deposit or other instruction given a gapless per-account sequence number before it's applied, so the account's state can be replayed in order ([Trading Platform Architecture](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/trading-platform-architecture#sequencing-and-the-event-log)).
- **SIM swap:** moving a customer's phone number to an attacker's SIM or carrier to receive their SMS codes; carriers must authenticate and notify under the FCC's 2023 order ([Account Security & Fraud](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/account-security#the-attack-paths)).
- **Surge factor:** the multiple of a normal peak a capacity plan is built for, set from the platform's history and public peaks such as Robinhood's 7x in 2020 ([Peak Days, Outages & Resilience](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/peak-days-and-outages#capacity-for-a-meme-stock-day)).
- **Take rate:** net revenue over gross volume, in basis points, read with its mix ([Investing & Digital Assets Metrics](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/investing-metrics#trading-activity-and-take-rate)).
- **Withdrawal hold:** a stop on one outbound transfer for a stated reason (a new destination, a security change, risk review, a customer lock, a senior investor hold, a legal order), released by a timer, the customer or a reviewer ([Account Security & Fraud](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/account-security#withdrawal-holds-and-allowlists)).
