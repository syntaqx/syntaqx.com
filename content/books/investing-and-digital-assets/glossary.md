Investing borrows its words from securities law, accounting and a century of back-office operations, and crypto adds a vocabulary of its own from cryptography and chain design, so the same word shifts with context: a settlement is a clearing house obligation on one screen and a chain transaction reaching finality on another, a position is a ledger claim to a broker and an address balance to a block explorer, a reserve is a broker's customer protection deposit and a stablecoin issuer's backing assets, and a confirmation is a Rule 10b-10 notice to a customer and a block on top of a deposit to an exchange. Entries are grouped by the part of the volume that explains them, alphabetized within each group, and each is one line and a link to the chapter that owns the idea; a word with two meanings appears once per meaning, qualified. Terms the [Payments](/books/payments/glossary) and [Banking & Lending](/books/banking-and-lending/glossary) glossaries already define keep their definitions. The volume's own names for the core records (legal entity, party, party identity, account, account party, instrument, chain, asset network, venue, order, order event, execution, journal entry, journal line, position, tax lot, lot closing, settlement, transfer, wallet, address, on-chain transaction, asset event, fee schedule, fee, disclosure, reconciliation, recon break, case) are the ones in [The Core Data Model](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/investing-business#the-core-data-model).

## The Business

- **Account:** one customer account at one legal entity, with its type (individual, joint, IRA, custodial, trust, entity, institutional), capabilities and status ([The Investing & Digital Assets Business](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/investing-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **Account party:** a party's role on an account (owner, joint owner, custodian, trustee, beneficiary, trusted contact, authorized trader), effective-dated ([The Investing & Digital Assets Business](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/investing-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **Asset event:** a corporate action or chain event that changes what a holder owns (dividend, split, merger, spinoff, tender offer, fork, airdrop, token migration, redenomination, delisting), with any holder elections ([The Investing & Digital Assets Business](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/investing-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **Asset network:** one instrument deployed on one chain, native or at a contract address, with its token standard, on-chain decimals and whether deposits and withdrawals are enabled ([The Investing & Digital Assets Business](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/investing-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **Capabilities:** what an account is approved for beyond cash trading: margin, options (with the firm's level), crypto, staking, futures and event contracts ([The Investing & Digital Assets Business](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/investing-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **Case:** a complaint, surveillance alert, AML alert, dispute or regulatory inquiry, with the time notice arrived, the rule's deadline and the outcome ([The Investing & Digital Assets Business](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/investing-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **Chain:** a blockchain network as the ledger sees it: its chain ID, native asset and the finality rule deposits wait for ([The Investing & Digital Assets Business](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/investing-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **Disclosure:** a record of what was shown or sent to a customer: the template version, the rendered document, the channel and the time ([The Investing & Digital Assets Business](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/investing-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **Distribution costs:** what a stablecoin issuer pays exchanges and other partners out of its reserve income for holding and distributing its token; about 60% of Circle's 2025 revenue ([The Investing & Digital Assets Business](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/investing-business#reserve-income)).
- **Execution:** one fill of an order at a venue, with price, quantity, liquidity flag, capacity and time ([The Investing & Digital Assets Business](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/investing-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **Fee schedule:** a versioned set of fee rules (maker and taker tiers, withdrawal fees, advisory rates); every fee names the version that produced it ([The Investing & Digital Assets Business](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/investing-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **Instrument:** anything that can be held or traded, cash included: equity, ETF, fund, option, future, bond, crypto asset, stablecoin, tokenized security or event contract, with its identifiers and the precision of its smallest unit ([The Investing & Digital Assets Business](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/investing-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **Journal entry:** one balanced movement in the firm's books, in any instruments, with a legal entity, an effective date, a source and an idempotency key ([The Investing & Digital Assets Business](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/investing-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **Journal line:** one signed amount of one instrument in one ledger account, with the customer account or wallet it concerns and the date it settles; each instrument's lines in an entry sum to zero ([The Investing & Digital Assets Business](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/investing-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **Ledger account:** the role a journal line plays: customer, firm, omnibus, settlement, fees or suspense ([The Investing & Digital Assets Business](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/investing-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **Legal entity:** one of the platform's own regulated companies (broker-dealer, RIA, FCM, trust company, money transmitter, stablecoin issuer, EU CASP); every account, wallet and journal entry names one ([The Investing & Digital Assets Business](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/investing-business#who-s-who)).
- **Lot closing:** a disposal matched to a tax lot, with proceeds, realized gain and the relief method used ([The Investing & Digital Assets Business](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/investing-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **Maker and taker fees:** exchange fees that charge an order adding liquidity to the book (maker) less than one removing it (taker), tiered by 30-day volume ([The Investing & Digital Assets Business](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/investing-business#trading-revenue-and-payment-for-order-flow)).
- **Order:** an instruction to buy or sell an instrument, with type, time in force, quantity or notional, and status, keyed by the client's idempotency key ([The Investing & Digital Assets Business](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/investing-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **Party:** a person or entity, independent of any account ([The Investing & Digital Assets Business](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/investing-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **Party identity:** a verified identifier for a party, with the method, the vendor's result and when it was verified ([The Investing & Digital Assets Business](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/investing-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **Payment for order flow (PFOF):** what a wholesaler pays a broker for routing it retail orders; at Robinhood Securities in the second quarter of 2026, 12.35% of the NBBO spread on equities in regular hours ([The Investing & Digital Assets Business](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/investing-business#trading-revenue-and-payment-for-order-flow)).
- **Position:** an account's settled and unsettled quantity of an instrument, rebuilt from the ledger and never written directly ([The Investing & Digital Assets Business](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/investing-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **Reconciliation:** a comparison of the ledger with an outside record (DTC, a clearing firm, a custodian, a bank, a chain at a block height, an issuer's supply), with each difference opened as a break ([The Investing & Digital Assets Business](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/investing-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **Reserve income:** the yield a stablecoin issuer earns on the assets backing its tokens, which it may not pass to holders as interest ([The Investing & Digital Assets Business](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/investing-business#reserve-income)).
- **Reserve return rate:** an issuer's reserve income over its average reserve; Circle reported 4.1% for 2025 and 3.5% for the third quarter of 2026 ([The Investing & Digital Assets Business](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/investing-business#reserve-income)).
- **Smallest unit:** the indivisible unit an instrument's quantities are stored in as integers (cents, satoshis, wei, a fraction of a share), with the exponent kept on the instrument ([The Investing & Digital Assets Business](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/investing-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **Tax lot:** one acquisition of an instrument in an account, with its cost basis, wash-sale adjustment, holding-period start and covered status ([The Investing & Digital Assets Business](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/investing-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **Transfer:** money or assets moving into or out of an account by ACH, wire, card, ACATS, on-chain transaction, internal move or journal, with its status, hold and journal entry ([The Investing & Digital Assets Business](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/investing-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **Venue:** where an order is filled or a trade settles: an exchange, ATS, wholesaler, crypto exchange, DEX, OTC desk or clearing house ([The Investing & Digital Assets Business](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/investing-business#the-core-data-model)).
- **Wallet:** a custody wallet the firm controls or uses, with its chain, purpose (hot, warm, cold, deposit, omnibus, staking, gas), key scheme and owning legal entity ([The Investing & Digital Assets Business](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/investing-business#the-core-data-model)).

## Clearing and Settlement

- **Affirmation:** an institutional customer's agreement to a trade's allocation and confirmation details, required as soon as technologically practicable and no later than the end of trade date under T+1 ([The Investing & Digital Assets Business](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/investing-business#the-life-of-a-stock-trade)).
- **Book entry:** moving ownership of securities by debiting and crediting participants' accounts at a depository, with no certificate moving ([The Investing & Digital Assets Business](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/investing-business#the-life-of-a-stock-trade)).
- **Continuous Net Settlement (CNS):** NSCC's system that nets each member's eligible trades in a security to one position per day and becomes counterparty to each accepted trade ([The Investing & Digital Assets Business](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/investing-business#the-life-of-a-stock-trade)).
- **DTC (The Depository Trust Company):** the US securities depository, where settlement moves securities and cash between participants by book entry ([The Investing & Digital Assets Business](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/investing-business#the-life-of-a-stock-trade)).
- **Novation:** a clearing house replacing the original counterparties to a trade, becoming buyer to every seller and seller to every buyer and guaranteeing settlement ([The Investing & Digital Assets Business](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/investing-business#the-life-of-a-stock-trade)).
- **NSCC (National Securities Clearing Corporation):** the DTCC clearing agency that clears and nets US equity trades and collects margin from its members ([The Investing & Digital Assets Business](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/investing-business#the-life-of-a-stock-trade)).
- **SIPC protection:** coverage for missing customer assets when a member broker-dealer fails, up to $500,000 per customer including $250,000 of cash; not for losses in value, non-security digital assets or stablecoins ([The Investing & Digital Assets Business](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/investing-business#the-life-of-a-stock-trade)).
- **Stock record:** a broker-dealer's securities record of every long and short position by security as of clearance dates, and where each long position is held ([The Investing & Digital Assets Business](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/investing-business#the-life-of-a-stock-trade)).
- **T+1:** the standard US settlement cycle of one business day after trade date, in force since May 28, 2024 ([The Investing & Digital Assets Business](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/investing-business#the-life-of-a-stock-trade)).
- **Trade confirmation (Rule 10b-10):** the written notice a broker-dealer gives at or before completion of a transaction, with date, time, price, quantity, capacity and payment for order flow ([The Investing & Digital Assets Business](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/investing-business#the-life-of-a-stock-trade)).

## Crypto Exchanges

- **Confirmation (chain):** a block added after the block holding a transaction; exchanges credit some chains' deposits after a set count (Kraken: three for bitcoin) ([The Investing & Digital Assets Business](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/investing-business#the-life-of-a-crypto-trade)).
- **Deposit address:** a chain address, or a shared address plus a memo or tag, assigned to one customer account so incoming transfers can be credited to it ([The Investing & Digital Assets Business](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/investing-business#the-life-of-a-crypto-trade)).
- **Finality:** the point after which a chain's block can't be reverted short of a prohibitive cost; on Ethereum, a finalized checkpoint, which an attacker would need to sacrifice at least one-third of staked ETH to revert ([The Investing & Digital Assets Business](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/investing-business#the-life-of-a-crypto-trade)).
- **Omnibus wallet:** one wallet holding many customers' crypto in the custodian's name, with the internal ledger recording each customer's share ([The Investing & Digital Assets Business](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/investing-business#the-life-of-a-crypto-trade)).
- **Reorg (reorganization):** a chain replacing its most recent blocks with a different sequence, which can remove a transaction that had been seen in a block ([The Investing & Digital Assets Business](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/investing-business#the-life-of-a-crypto-trade)).
- **Sweep (crypto):** an on-chain transaction moving funds from deposit addresses into the exchange's hot or cold wallets; it changes where an asset is, not whose ([The Investing & Digital Assets Business](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/investing-business#the-life-of-a-crypto-trade)).

## Stablecoins

- **Burn:** destroying stablecoin tokens on redemption, reducing the issuer's outstanding supply and liability ([The Investing & Digital Assets Business](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/investing-business#the-life-of-a-stablecoin-dollar)).
- **CCTP (Cross-Chain Transfer Protocol):** Circle's burn-and-mint protocol for moving USDC between chains without liquidity pools ([The Investing & Digital Assets Business](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/investing-business#the-life-of-a-stablecoin-dollar)).
- **Mint:** creating stablecoin tokens against dollars received, increasing the issuer's outstanding supply and liability ([The Investing & Digital Assets Business](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/investing-business#the-life-of-a-stablecoin-dollar)).
- **Minter allowance:** the cap on how many tokens a minter role may create, reduced by each mint and not restored by burning, so a compromised minter key is bounded ([The Investing & Digital Assets Business](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/investing-business#the-life-of-a-stablecoin-dollar)).
- **Payment stablecoin reserve:** the assets backing a payment stablecoin at least one to one under the GENIUS Act: currency, Fed balances, insured deposits, Treasuries of 93 days or less, overnight repo and government money market funds ([The Investing & Digital Assets Business](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/investing-business#the-life-of-a-stablecoin-dollar)).
- **Redemption:** exchanging stablecoins for dollars with the issuer at par; two business days is the OCC's standard for timely redemption ([The Investing & Digital Assets Business](/books/investing-and-digital-assets/investing-business#the-life-of-a-stablecoin-dollar)).
